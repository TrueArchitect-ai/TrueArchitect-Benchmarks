**Runners the server actually starts** (all wiring is in `server/server.go:151-176`, `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`):

- **S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — one immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup** (`server/server.go:159-160`), synchronous, before the loop begins.
- **S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner.Run`) — a `time.NewTicker` loop, fixed 12-hour interval** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-40`, `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`); runs in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG`, stopped via its own `context.CancelFunc` stored in `backgroundRunnerCancels` (`server/server.go:153-172`, `stopBackgroundRunners`). Each tick re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose last presign is older than 4 days.

That is the entire content of `startBackgroundRunners` — no other runner is registered.

**Adjacent long-lived goroutines started indirectly** (not registered as background runners, so not shut down by `stopBackgroundRunners`):

Trigger is store construction in `cmd/memos/main.go:63`, not the server.

The hub itself (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) has no background goroutine; it's only closed on shutdown (`server/server.go:180-185`).
- **Fire-and-forget goroutines**, event-driven per call, not scheduled: PAT last-used writeback (`server/auth/authenticator.go:178-184`), webhook dispatch (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:44`), email send (`internal/email/email.go:18`).
