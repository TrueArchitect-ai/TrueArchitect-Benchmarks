**Server-Sent Events (SSE) over `GET /api/v1/sse`; hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub`); broadcasting handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction` (plus `SetMemoRelations` / `SetMemoAttachments` via the shared `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper).**

Details:

**Mechanism** — SSE, not WebSockets. `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `data: {...}\n\n` frames with explicit `http.Flusher` flushes, sends a `: connected` comment on open and `: heartbeat` every 30s (`sseHeartbeatInterval`). The route is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`; auth is a Bearer token via `auth.Authenticator`. Client side is `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (fetch-based stream against `/api/v1/sse`, line 203).

**Hub** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`: `SSEHub` holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`; `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`/`Broadcast`. Each client gets a 32-buffered channel and events are dropped rather than blocking on a slow client. `SSEClient.canReceive` filters delivery: private-visibility events go only to the creator or an admin. Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. Wired into the server at `server/server.go:38,77` and constructed at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`.

**Broadcast call sites**
- `CreateMemo` → `memo.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`, skipped when `isSSESuppressed(ctx)` — i.e. when invoked from `CreateMemoComment`)
- `DeleteMemo` → `memo.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`)
- `CreateMemoComment` → `memo.comment.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` → `reaction.upserted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` → `reaction.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161`)
- `memo.updated` is emitted indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`), called by `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `memo_relation_service.go:48`, and `memo_attachment_service.go:48`.
