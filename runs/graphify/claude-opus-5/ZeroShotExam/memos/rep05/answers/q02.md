**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` contains exactly three dialect directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/` — each with per-version incremental migrations (e.g. `0.2` … `0.30`) plus a `LATEST.sql` full schema.
- `store/migrator.go:44` embeds that tree (`//go:embed migration`), and `store/migrator.go:39` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`.
- Matching driver implementations live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/` (dispatched from `store/db/db.go`).

Note: sqlite has the deepest history (from `0.2`), while mysql starts at `0.17` and postgres at `0.19`.
