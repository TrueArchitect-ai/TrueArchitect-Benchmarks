All ConnectRPC service clients are created there via `createClient(...)` against a shared `createConnectTransport` instance:

- `web/src/connect.ts:184` — the main transport (`baseUrl: window.location.origin`, binary format, `authInterceptor`)
- `web/src/connect.ts:192-203` — exported clients: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`
