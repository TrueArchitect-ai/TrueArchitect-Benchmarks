**Runners the server actually starts** (all in `server/server.go:154-176`, `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`):

- **s3presign runner — one immediate synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup** (`server/server.go:163`), then a `time.NewTicker` loop every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = 12h`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-40`); exits on context cancel. Its per-item work is also time-gated: an attachment is re-presigned only if its `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (5-day URL expiry), `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:83-88`. Shutdown is via the stored `context.CancelFunc` + `WaitGroup` (`server/server.go:157-176`, `stopBackgroundRunners`/`waitBackgroundRunners`).
- **Store cache cleanup loops (3 of them) — implicitly started, not by `startBackgroundRunners`**: `cache.New` spawns `go c.cleanupLoop()` (`store/cache/cache.go:83`) with a `CleanupInterval` ticker (`store/cache/cache.go:201`). `store.New` creates three caches — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — each with a **5-minute** cleanup interval (`store/store.go:42,51-53`). These are goroutines the server process runs, though they're a side effect of constructing the store, not registered background runners.

That's it — no other long-lived scheduled worker exists. Other goroutines in the codebase are per-request/fire-and-forget, not scheduled workers: SSE heartbeat ticker is per connection (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`), webhook dispatch (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:44`), email send (`internal/email/email.go:18`), PAT last-used update (`server/auth/authenticator.go:180`).

**Unused infrastructure**

- **`internal/scheduler/` — a full cron scheduler package that the server never uses.** It provides 5/6-field cron parsing, timezone-aware scheduling, middleware (logging/recovery/timeout), and graceful shutdown (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`, `README.md`, `doc.go`), but the only non-test importers are its own `example_test.go` and `integration_test.go`. Nothing in `cmd/memos/main.go`, `server/server.go`, or anywhere else calls `scheduler.New()`/`Register`/`Start` — the s3presign runner rolls its own `time.Ticker` instead.
- **`server/runner/memopayload`** lives under `server/runner/` but is *not* a background runner: `RebuildMemoPayload` is called inline from the memo service on create/update (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`). It is never started as a goroutine.
