**Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP; hub: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub`); handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`**

Details:

**Mechanism** — plain SSE (`text/event-stream`), not WebSockets. The endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` and served by `handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25-110`: Bearer-token auth via `auth.Authenticator`, headers `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, an initial `: connected` comment, `data: {json}\n\n` frames, and a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s (`sseHeartbeatInterval`). The browser side consumes it with a `fetch`-based stream in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203`.

**Hub** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`. `SSEHub` holds a `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` behind a `sync.RWMutex` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`/`Broadcast`. Each client has a 32-slot buffered channel; events are dropped rather than blocking on slow clients (`sse_hub.go:115-132`). `canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:134-144`) filters private-memo events to the creator or an admin. Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. The hub is owned by `APIV1Service` and closed on shutdown (`server/server.go:38,77,205-206`).
