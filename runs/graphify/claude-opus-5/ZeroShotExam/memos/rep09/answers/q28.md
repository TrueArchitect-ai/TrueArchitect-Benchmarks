**Runners the server actually starts**

- **s3presign runner** — `time.NewTicker(12h)` loop (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,29-41`). Not tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` — they stop via `Store.Close()`.
- **Shutdown waiter** — `go s.backgroundRunnerWG.Wait()` in `waitBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:184-201`); one-shot, triggered by `Shutdown`.
- **Signal-handler goroutine** — `go func(){ <-c; s.Shutdown(ctx) }()` in `cmd/memos/main.go:99-103`; driven by SIGINT/SIGTERM (`cmd/memos/main.go:83-87`).
