**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub`); CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

Details:

**Mechanism — SSE, not WebSockets.** The endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` and served by `handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25,34`. It sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, and sends a heartbeat every 30s (`sseHeartbeatInterval`, sse_handler.go:17). Auth is a Bearer token in the `Authorization` header. The browser side is `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:205`, which consumes the stream with `Accept: text/event-stream`.

**Hub — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`.** `SSEHub` (line 56) holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Each client gets a 32-event buffered channel; `Broadcast` (line 115) drops events for slow clients rather than blocking, and filters per-client via `canReceive` so `Private` memos only reach the creator or an admin. The hub is constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` and closed on shutdown in `server/server.go:205`. Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (sse_hub.go:15-21).
