**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/` — the path convention is documented at `store/migration.go`… specifically `store/migrator.go:39` (`store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`), and the whole tree is embedded via `//go:embed migration` at `store/migrator.go:44`.

The three driver directories are:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versions 0.2 through 0.30, plus `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — versions 0.17 through 0.30, plus `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versions 0.19 through 0.30, plus `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`

These match the driver implementations in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
