`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

For a fresh (uninitialized) database, `preMigrate` in `store/migrator.go:211-235` reads `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` — i.e. `migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` from the embedded `migrationFS` — and executes the whole file in a single transaction, then stamps the current schema version. The versioned directories (`store/migration/sqlite/0.2` … `0.30`) are only applied incrementally to already-initialized databases; they aren't used on a fresh install. The comment at `store/migrator.go:42` states this directly: "LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)".
