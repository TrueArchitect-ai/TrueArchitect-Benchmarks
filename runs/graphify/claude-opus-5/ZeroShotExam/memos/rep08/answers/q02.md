**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` contains exactly three dialect subdirectories: `mysql/`, `postgres/`, `sqlite/` — each with versioned migration folders (e.g. `0.17`–`0.30`) plus a `LATEST.sql`.
- These are compiled into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`.
- The same three dialects have driver implementations under `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`.

Note: seed data (`store/seed/`, embedded at `store/migrator.go:47-48`) is SQLite-only — the three dialects apply to migrations, not seeds.
