`web/src/connect.ts`

All ConnectRPC service clients are created there. It builds a shared `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) with auth/error interceptors, then exports the clients via `createClient(...)` — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`). It also creates a separate `refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient` pair for token refresh (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`).
