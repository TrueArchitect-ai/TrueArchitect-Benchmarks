**Convergence for delivery:**

The first three funnel through the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator and loads their webhooks; the comment event builds its payload itself (targeting the *related* memo owner's webhooks, not the commenter's). All four then converge on `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (128 slots, dropping on overflow) drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Those workers call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the single place the HTTP POST, HMAC-SHA256 Standard Webhooks signing, and SSRF-guarded `safeClient` delivery happen.
