**`APIV1Service.SignIn` — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go:51`**

This is where the actual sign-in logic lives: it handles both password credentials (bcrypt comparison against `user.PasswordHash`, plus a `DisallowPasswordAuth` check) and SSO/OAuth2 authorization-code flow, then issues an access token.
