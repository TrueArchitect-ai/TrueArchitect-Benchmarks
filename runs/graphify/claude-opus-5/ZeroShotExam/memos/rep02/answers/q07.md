`web/src/connect.ts`

All ConnectRPC service clients are created there via `createClient(...)` over a shared `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184-203`):

- Transport: `createConnectTransport({ baseUrl: window.location.origin, useBinaryFormat: true, ... })` with an auth interceptor (`web/src/connect.ts:156`)
- Exported clients: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`
- Plus a separate `refreshAuthClient` on an interceptor-free transport to avoid refresh recursion (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`)
