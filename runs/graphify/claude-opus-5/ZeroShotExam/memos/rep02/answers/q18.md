The interface is `store.Driver`, declared in `store/driver.go`. Its three implementations (each a `DB` type in its own package, entry file):

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory that selects between them at startup:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`), calling the corresponding package's `NewDB(profile)` and returning `store.Driver`; unknown values yield `errors.New("unknown db driver")`. It's invoked from `cmd/memos/main.go:21` (which imports `store/db`), plus test helpers in `store/test/store.go`.
