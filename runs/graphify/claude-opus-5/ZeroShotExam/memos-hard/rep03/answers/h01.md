**`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — once at startup, then every 12 hours — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

How it works:

- **What goes stale:** S3 presigned GET URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77-79`). Attachments stored in S3 keep that URL in their `Reference` field (`server/router/api/v1/attachment_service_storage.go:111-124`), so without refresh the links would break after 5 days.
- **The component:** `s3presign.Runner.CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with storage type `S3` in batches of 100, skips any whose `LastPresignedTime` is under 4 days old (`runner.go:85-89`), re-presigns the rest via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` (`runner.go:108`), and stamps a new `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:115`). The 4-day skip window against a 5-day expiry gives a one-day safety margin.
- **The schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Runner.Run` (`runner.go:29-41`).
- **The wiring:** `startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously so links are fresh immediately at boot (`server.go:163`), then launches `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `s.backgroundRunnerWG`. The cancel func for `s3Context` is stored in `s.backgroundRunnerCancels` (`server.go:156-159`) so `stopBackgroundRunners` (`server.go:176`) can shut it down cleanly on server stop.
