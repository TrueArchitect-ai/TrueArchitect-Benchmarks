### The interface
`markdown.Service` — `internal/markdown/markdown.go:31-54`. Eight methods: `ExtractAll`, `ExtractTags`, `ExtractProperties`, `RenderMarkdown`, `RenderHTML`, `GenerateSnippet`, `ValidateContent`, `RenameTag`.

### Concrete implementation — exactly one
`type service struct { md goldmark.Markdown }` at `internal/markdown/markdown.go:57-59`, wrapping goldmark. There are no mocks, fakes, or alternative implementations anywhere in the Go tree — every holder resolves to this type at runtime.

Its behavior is varied by functional options rather than by swapping types (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:62-83`): `WithTagExtension()` and `WithMentionExtension()` append `extensions.TagExtension` / `extensions.MentionExtension` on top of `extension.GFM`. Internally it delegates markdown-to-markdown rendering to a separate concrete helper, `renderer.NewMarkdownRenderer()` (`internal/markdown/renderer/markdown_renderer.go:15,20`), used by `RenderMarkdown` and `RenameTag` — that one is a plain struct, not behind an interface.
