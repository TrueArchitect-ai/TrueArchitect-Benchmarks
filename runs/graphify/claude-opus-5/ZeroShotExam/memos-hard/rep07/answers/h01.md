- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`); `Run` drives a `time.Ticker` at that interval and exits on `ctx.Done()` (`runner.go:29-41`). - **Refresh policy**: `CheckAndPresign` pages through S3-backed attachments in batches of 100, skips any whose `LastPresignedTime` is under 4 days old (presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry), otherwise calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and writes back the new `Reference` plus `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:47-127`).
- **Wiring/lifecycle**: it gets its own cancellable context (`s3Context, s3Cancel`) registered in `s.backgroundRunnerCancels` (`server/server.go:156-159`) and is tracked by `s.backgroundRunnerWG`, so `Shutdown` cancels and waits for it (`server/server.go:140-143`, `176-189`).
