Notes worth flagging:

- **The bytes never leave the database.** `user.AvatarURL` (store/user.go:43) holds a `data:image/...;base64,...` URI; `parseDataURI` base64-decodes it in-process and writes it straight to the response. Unlike attachments, there is no local-file or S3 branch for avatars — `getAttachmentBlob`/`getAttachmentReader` are not on this path.
- **Authorization is instance-wide, not per-user.** The only gate is `s.Profile.AllowAnonymous()` (fileserver.go:173); once any valid viewer exists, *any* user's avatar is served by username. There's no equivalent of `checkAttachmentPermission`.
- Response is capped to `avatarAllowedTypes` (fileserver.go:75) and gets `nosniff`/`DENY`/restrictive CSP via `setSecurityHeaders` plus `max-age=3600`.
- The service is wired up at server/server.go:81-82.
