How it works:

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner`, defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`. Its `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:47`) pages through attachments whose `StorageType` is `AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, calls `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` for each, and writes the fresh URL back via `store.UpdateAttachment` (setting `Reference` and stamping `LastPresignedTime`).
- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). There's a second, inner staleness rule: an object is skipped if its last presign was under 4 days ago (`runner.go:88`), since presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry — so each URL is effectively rotated in its 5th day.
- **Wiring**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) constructs the runner at `server/server.go:162`, does a synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163` so links are refreshed immediately on boot, then launches the ticker loop in a goroutine at `server/server.go:167-171` under its own cancellable context (`server/server.go:156`) and a `WaitGroup`. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`; shutdown cancels and waits at `server/server.go:140,143`.
