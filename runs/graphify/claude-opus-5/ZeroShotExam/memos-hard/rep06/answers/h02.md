## Where delivery executes

`webhook.Post()` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` — is the function that actually performs the outbound HTTP request. It marshals the payload to JSON, builds a `POST`, optionally adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers (`webhook-id` / `webhook-timestamp` / `webhook-signature`), and issues the call via `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`, then reads a size-limited response body (1 MiB) and checks the status code and the `{code, message}` envelope.

Callers never invoke `Post` directly — they call `webhook.PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`); overflow is dropped with a warning. The producers are the memo lifecycle dispatchers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71` (created/updated/deleted/comment-created).

## What protects it from internal addresses

Two layers, both keyed off `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`:

1. **Dial-time SSRF guard (the enforcing layer).** `safeClient`'s `Transport.DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`). It splits the address, resolves the host itself, and refuses to dial if *any* resolved IP is reserved. Because the check happens at connect time rather than at save time, it also defeats DNS rebinding and redirect-based bypasses.
2. **Admission-time validation.** `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects non-absolute URLs, non-http(s) schemes, unresolvable hosts, and reserved-IP resolutions with a gRPC `InvalidArgument`. It runs when webhooks are created/updated, at `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74`, `:161`, and `:182`.

The blocklist itself is `reservedCIDRs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`): IPv4 loopback, all three RFC-1918 ranges, link-local `169.254.0.0/16` (explicitly covering cloud IMDS at 169.254.169.254), plus IPv6 `::1/128`, `fc00::/7`, and `fe80::/10`. These are parsed once at startup in `init()` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:28`).

One thing worth flagging: the escape hatch `webhook.AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP` and disables *both* layers when true. It defaults to `false` and I found no non-test assignment anywhere in the repo — no flag, env var, or profile field wires it up — so in practice it is currently always off. The blocklist also does not cover `0.0.0.0/8`, carrier-grade NAT `100.64.0.0/10`, or IPv4-mapped IPv6 forms such as `::ffff:127.0.0.1`.
