**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

The embedded migration tree at `store/migration/` has exactly three dialect directories — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` — each containing a `LATEST.sql` plus versioned incremental migration folders (sqlite goes back to `0.2`, mysql to `0.17`, postgres to `0.19`; all three currently top out at `0.30`).

They're embedded and selected at runtime in `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`) via `getMigrationBasePath()`, and matched by the driver packages `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/` (dispatched in `store/db/db.go`).

Note: seed data ships for sqlite only (`store/seed/sqlite/`).
