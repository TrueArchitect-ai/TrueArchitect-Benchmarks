**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) — a long-lived `text/event-stream` GET at `/api/v1/sse`, Bearer-token authenticated, with a 30s heartbeat (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:15-50`; route registered in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` via `RegisterSSERoutes`).

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, line 56) plus `SSEClient` and the `SSEEvent` payload/event-type constants (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`). Events carry `Visibility`/`CreatorID` as non-serialized fields used for per-client delivery filtering. Helpers for building events are in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
