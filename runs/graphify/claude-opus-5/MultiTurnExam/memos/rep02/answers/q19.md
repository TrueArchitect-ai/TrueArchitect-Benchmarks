**Connect:** `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21-28`) and mounts the generated handler with `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` at `connect_handler.go:40`. The per-method wrappers that adapt `connect.Request`/`connect.Response` to the plain gRPC-style signatures live in `connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at :306, `SignIn` at :78 for the header-carrier cases). It's wired onto the mux in `v1.go:140-141`.

**REST gateway:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `v1.go:108` registers `APIV1Service` directly against a `grpc-gateway` `runtime.ServeMux` (in-process — no network hop), using the `google.api.http` annotations from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. The mux is mounted on the Echo server for `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:126-129`), with auth applied by `gatewayAuthMiddleware`.

Both protocols share one implementation: the `APIV1Service` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`. There is no separate native gRPC listener — `grpc.NewServer` appears nowhere in the server code.
