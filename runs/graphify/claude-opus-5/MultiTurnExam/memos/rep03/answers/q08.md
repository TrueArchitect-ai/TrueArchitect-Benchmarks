**store.Driver — store/driver.go**

Declared at `store/driver.go:10`, documented as "an interface for store driver... contains all methods that store database driver should implement." It covers connection lifecycle (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus per-model CRUD methods (attachment, memo, user, etc.).

The three implementations are the `DB` structs in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, each returned as a `store.Driver` from its package's `NewDB`.
