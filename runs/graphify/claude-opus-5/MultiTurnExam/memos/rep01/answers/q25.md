**Engine: Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by the in-repo `internal/filter` package** — `internal/filter/engine.go:24` (`NewEngine` builds a `cel.Env` from a `Schema`; `cel.AstToParsedExpr` at line 57), with `internal/filter/parser.go` lowering the CEL AST into a dialect-neutral IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), `internal/filter/schema.go` declaring the allowed fields/functions, and `internal/filter/render.go` emitting SQL.

**Mechanism:** the filter string is CEL-parsed into an AST, converted to an intermediate `Condition` tree, then rendered per dialect by `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect{SQLite|Postgres|MySQL}, &where, &args)` — each driver calls it at the top of `ListMemos` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:61`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:58`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:69`), appending parameterized `WHERE` fragments plus bind args, with `render.go` switching on `DialectName` for the dialect-specific bits (placeholder style, JSON access, string length, etc.).

The RPC-side entry point is `ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`, which passes the request's `filter` through to `store.FindMemo.Filters` (`store/memo.go:72`). Saved shortcuts store the same CEL syntax (`proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto:15-17`).
