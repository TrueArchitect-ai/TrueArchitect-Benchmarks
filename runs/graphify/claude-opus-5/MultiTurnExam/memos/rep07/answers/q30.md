**Convergence: `webhook.PostAsync` → `webhook.Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (:184 and :120).**

The first three funnel through the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:47`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their hooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, stamps the activity type onto the payload, and calls `webhook.PostAsync` (:69). `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` bypasses that helper — it targets the *parent* memo's owner (`relatedMemoCreatorID`) instead of the comment author — but reaches the same `webhook.PostAsync` at :42.
