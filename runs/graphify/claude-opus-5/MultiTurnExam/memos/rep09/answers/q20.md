**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism** — SSE over plain HTTP. The stream endpoint sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48`; there's no WebSocket path.

**Hub** — `SSEHub` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`, with `NewSSEHub` (:63), `Subscribe(userID, role)` (:71), `Unsubscribe` (:89), `Close` (:99), and `Broadcast(event)` (:115). Subscribe takes the user ID and role because `Broadcast` filters per client on the event's `Visibility` and `CreatorID`, so private memos don't leak to other subscribers. Helpers for building events are in `sse_event_helpers.go`.

One wrinkle worth knowing: `CreateMemo`'s broadcast is guarded by `if !isSSESuppressed(ctx)` (`memo_service.go:162`). `CreateMemoComment` calls `CreateMemo` with `withSuppressSSE(ctx)` and then emits its own comment-specific event instead, so a comment produces one event rather than two.
