**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

- Migration files live under `store/migration/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/` — e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/mysql/0.30/`, `store/migration/postgres/0.30/`.
- They're embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, with the dialect selected from the driver at runtime.
- The same three dialects have driver implementations under `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/`.

Note the dialects differ in migration history depth: sqlite goes back to 0.2, mysql starts at 0.17, and postgres at 0.19.
