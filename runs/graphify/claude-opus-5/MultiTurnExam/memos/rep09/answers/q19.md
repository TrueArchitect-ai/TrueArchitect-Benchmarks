**ConnectRPC:** `RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` on the `http.ServeMux` (`connect_handler.go:31-50`). The `s` there is `ConnectServiceHandler`, which embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21-23`); its per-RPC wrappers live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at line 306) and mostly just unwrap `req.Msg` and delegate.

**REST:** `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` binds the same `APIV1Service` into a grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux` configured with `gatewayAuthMiddleware` (`v1.go:96-98`), using the `google.api.http` annotations from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. This is the in-process `...HandlerServer` variant, so it calls the service directly rather than dialing a gRPC port.

Both protocols share one implementation — the `APIV1Service` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
