**REST/gRPC-Gateway:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` binds `APIV1Service` directly into a `runtime.NewServeMux` carrying the gateway auth middleware; the mux is mounted on `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:126-127`). Routes come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` via the generated `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

**Connect:** `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, registered by `(*ConnectServiceHandler).RegisterConnectHandlers`. The per-RPC wrapping — unwrapping `req.Msg` and delegating to `APIV1Service` — lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at line 306); interceptors are configured back in `v1.go:131+`. This is the protocol the web client uses via `memoServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:197`).

Both protocols share one implementation: the `APIV1Service` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
