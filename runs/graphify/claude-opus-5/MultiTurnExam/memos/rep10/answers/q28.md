```
s3presign.Runner — time.Ticker, every 12h, plus one immediate RunOnce at startup
webhook async workers (4 goroutines) — package init(), consume a buffered channel; triggered per event, not on a schedule
SSE hub — event-driven; goroutine per subscribed client, no timer
```

Details:

- **`s3presign.Runner`** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) is the only entry in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:151-167`). It calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously at startup, then runs `Run(ctx)` in a goroutine driven by `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` with `runnerInterval = 12 * time.Hour` (`runner.go:27,30`). It re-presigns expiring S3 attachment URLs. Cancellation is via a per-runner `context.CancelFunc` tracked in `backgroundRunnerCancels`, with a `WaitGroup` join on shutdown (`server.go:135-138`, `:169-197`).
- **Webhook delivery workers** — four goroutines started in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` (package `init`, so implicitly running, not started by `Server`), draining the 128-slot `asyncPostQueue` that `PostAsync` feeds (`:184`).
- **SSE hub** (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) is purely event-driven off `Broadcast`, with a goroutine per connected client — not a scheduled runner.

**Unused infrastructure — two items:**

1. **`internal/scheduler`** — a full cron scheduler (cron parsing, timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown; `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, documented in `doc.go` and `README.md`) with **zero non-test importers** anywhere in the repo. Nothing in `server/` registers a job with it.
2. **`server/runner/memopayload`** — despite living under `server/runner/`, it is not a runner at all: it has no `Run` loop and is only called inline from the API handlers, `RebuildMemoPayload` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` (create) and `:447` (update).
