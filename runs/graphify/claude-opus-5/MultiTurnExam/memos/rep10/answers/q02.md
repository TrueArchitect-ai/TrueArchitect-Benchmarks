**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/` with one directory per dialect:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (e.g. `0.30/`, `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

The same three dialects have driver implementations in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`. Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is SQLite-only.
