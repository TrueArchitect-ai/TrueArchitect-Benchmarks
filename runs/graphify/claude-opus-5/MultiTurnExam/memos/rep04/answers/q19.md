Details:

- **Connect:** `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21`) and `RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` on the `http.ServeMux` (`connect_handler.go:40`). The per-RPC adapters that unwrap `connect.Request` and call the underlying `APIV1Service` methods are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at `:307`).
- **grpc-gateway REST:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` registers the service directly (in-process, no gRPC dial) against a `runtime.NewServeMux` with auth middleware; the mux is mounted on `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:127-129`). Routes come from the `google.api.http` options in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

Both protocols share the same business logic — the single `APIV1Service` implementation in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
