**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

- Migration files live in `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each with versioned dirs plus a `LATEST.sql`), embedded and selected by driver in `store/migrator.go`.
- The same three dialects have driver implementations under `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres` (`store/db/db.go`).
