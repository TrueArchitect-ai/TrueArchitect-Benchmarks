**store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

On a fresh install `Store.preMigrate` finds the DB uninitialized and executes `migration/<driver>/LATEST.sql` from the embedded FS in one transaction (`store/migrator.go:211-235`, path built by `getMigrationBasePath` at `store/migrator.go:254` and the `LatestSchemaFileName` const at line 56) — for `driver=sqlite` that resolves to `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`. The versioned dirs under `store/migration/sqlite/0.x/` are only replayed for existing databases.
