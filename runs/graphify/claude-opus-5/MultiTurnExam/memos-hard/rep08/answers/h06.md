**Handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`; convergence function: `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`** (all six enqueue through `webhook.PostAsync`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`, and the queue's four workers call `Post`, which issues the actual `POST` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`).

| `DeleteMemo` | `memo_service.go:518` | `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` at `memo_service.go:562` |
| `CreateMemoComment` | `memo_service_comments.go:20` | `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` at `memo_service_comments.go:119` |

The last three are the non-obvious ones: `SetMemoAttachments` and `SetMemoRelations` don't look like content-change RPCs, but both call `touchMemoUpdatedTimestamp` then `buildUpdatedMemoState` + `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go:27,66`), which fires a `memos.memo.updated` webhook exactly as `UpdateMemo` does.

The funnel below the handlers:

1. `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go:66`) → `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`.
2. The three `DispatchMemo{Created,Updated,Deleted}Webhook` wrappers (`memo_service_webhooks.go:15,20,25`) all delegate to `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync` per hook (line 71). `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:30`) takes a parallel path — it targets the *parent* memo owner's hooks — and calls `PostAsync` at line 43.
3. `PostAsync` drops the payload on a 128-slot channel; four `init`-started goroutines (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) drain it into **`Post`**, the sole place the request is built, HMAC-signed, and sent via `safeClient.Do`.

The URL is user-configured (`hook.Url` from `GetUserWebhooks`), which is why `Post` goes through the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`/`safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32,59`).
