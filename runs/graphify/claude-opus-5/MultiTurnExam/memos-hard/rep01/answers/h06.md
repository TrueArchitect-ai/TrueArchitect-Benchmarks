The three `Set*`/`Update*` handlers share the helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66-78`), which calls `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`.

All four `Dispatch*Webhook` methods (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15-46`) load the creator's configured hooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks`, copy `hook.Url` and `hook.SigningSecret` into the payload, and call **`webhook.PostAsync`** — the two call sites at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71` are the only entries into the webhook package from the RPC layer.

`PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) is the convergence point: it enqueues onto the bounded 128-slot `asyncPostQueue`, dropping with a warning if full. Four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) drain it and call `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), where the actual `safeClient.Do(req)` to the user-configured URL executes at line 152.

Two consequences worth noting: because dispatch is asynchronous and every handler only logs the returned error at `slog.Warn` level, a failed or dropped delivery never affects the RPC's response; and the URL is user-configured but constrained at save time by `webhook.ValidateURL` and at dial time by `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`).
