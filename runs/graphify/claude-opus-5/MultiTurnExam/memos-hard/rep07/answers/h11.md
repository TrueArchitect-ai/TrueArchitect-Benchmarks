**1st: `store.User` — 7 packages outside the data layer; 2nd: `store.Memo` — 6 packages.**

Method: entity types are the structs declared in the `store` package (`store/user.go:*`, `store/memo.go:*`, `store/attachment.go:*`, etc. — excluding the `Find*`/`Update*`/`Delete*` parameter structs). For each, I counted the distinct Go packages referencing `store.<Type>` with all of `store/` (including `store/db/*` and `store/test`) excluded as "the data layer."

**`store.User` — 7 packages** (19 files in the largest):
- `server/router/api/v1` (19 files), `server/router/api/v1/test` (3), `server/router/rss` (2), `server/router/fileserver` (2 — e.g. `getUserByUsername` returning `*store.User`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:715`), `server/auth` (2), `server/router/frontend` (1), `server/notification` (1)

**`store.Memo` — 6 packages** (13 files in the largest):
- `server/router/api/v1` (13), `server/router/api/v1/test` (3), `server/router/rss` (2), `server/runner/memopayload` (1 — `RebuildMemoPayload`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:73`), `server/router/frontend` (1), `server/notification` (1)

Runners-up: `store.Attachment` — 4 packages (`server/router/api/v1`, `.../v1/test`, `server/router/rss`, `server/router/fileserver`); then `store.MemoRelation`, `store.MemoShare`, `store.Inbox` at 3 each; `store.Reaction` and `store.UserIdentity` at 2; `store.UserSetting`, `store.InstanceSetting`, `store.IdentityProvider` at 1 (confined to `server/router/api/v1`).

`store.User` leads because it appears in the auth/session layer (`server/auth`) and in file serving in addition to every place `store.Memo` shows up — memos are always reached through an API or renderer, while users are also resolved during authentication.
