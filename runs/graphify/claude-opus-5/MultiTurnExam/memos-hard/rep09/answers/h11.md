**1st: `store.User` — ~7 packages outside the data layer; 2nd: `store.Memo` — ~6 packages.**

Method: I enumerated the persistent entity structs declared in `store/*.go` (`User` `store/user.go:29`, `Memo` `store/memo.go:35`, `Attachment` `store/attachment.go:16`, `Reaction` `store/reaction.go:7`, `Inbox` `store/inbox.go:24`, `IdentityProvider` `store/idp.go:12`, `MemoRelation` `store/memo_relation.go:16`, `MemoShare` `store/memo_share.go:6`, `UserSetting` `store/user_setting.go:13`, `UserIdentity` `store/user_identity.go:8`), then counted distinct directories referencing each as `store.<Type>` with everything under `store/` excluded — so the drivers (`store/db/*`) and `store/test` don't count as "outside."

**`store.User` — 7:**
- `server/router/api/v1` (19 files — the bulk)
- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/auth`
- `server/router/fileserver`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/router/rss`
- `server/notification`

**`store.Memo` — 6:**
- `server/router/api/v1` (13 files)
- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/router/rss`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/notification`
- `server/runner/memopayload`

The gap is the `server/auth` + `server/router/fileserver` pair: authentication resolves a `*store.User` from the session/PAT, and the avatar handler loads one directly (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:185`), neither of which touches memos. `Memo` picks up `server/runner/memopayload` in exchange, netting one behind.

If you discount `_test`-only packages, it's `User` 6 vs `Memo` 5 — same ordering. Third place is `store.Attachment` at 4.

Word-boundary matching (`\bstore\.User\b`) keeps `store.UserSetting` / `store.UserIdentity` from inflating the `User` count, and likewise `store.MemoRelation` / `store.MemoShare` out of `Memo`.
