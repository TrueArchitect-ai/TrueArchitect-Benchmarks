**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job, and the cron scheduler is not running in the server at all — so there is no scheduled time to report.**

Two separate corrections:

**1. No cleanup job exists.** Searching for `cleanup`/`vacuum`/`prune`/`purge`/`nightly` across `server/`, `store/`, and `internal/` turns up no scheduled database maintenance. Everything matching is either request-scoped or unrelated:
- `attachmentCleanupErr` — inline S3/local blob deletion during `DeleteUser` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:421-450`), triggered by the RPC, not a timer
- `store.DeleteMemo` relation/attachment cleanup (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:579`)
- `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute` (`store/store.go:42`, `store/cache/cache.go:60`) — the **in-memory cache's** expired-entry sweeper (`store/cache/cache.go:83`), not a database job

**2. The cron scheduler is dead code.** `internal/scheduler/` is a full implementation — cron parser (`internal/scheduler/parser.go`), job model (`job.go`), middleware (`middleware.go`), scheduler (`scheduler.go`) — but `grep -rn "internal/scheduler"` shows its only importers are its own tests: `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13` and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`. Nothing in `server/` or `cmd/` imports it, so no cron expression is ever registered or parsed at runtime.

**What actually runs on a timer:** exactly one background runner, and it isn't cron-scheduled. `s3presign.Runner` refreshes presigned S3 attachment URLs on a `time.Ticker` with `runnerInterval = 12 * time.Hour` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), started from `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:162-171`) with one immediate synchronous pass at `server/server.go:163`. Because it's a plain interval ticker anchored to process start, it has no wall-clock time of day — it fires 12 and 24 hours after boot, whenever that was.
