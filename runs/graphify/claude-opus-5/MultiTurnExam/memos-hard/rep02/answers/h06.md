**Handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoRelations`, `SetMemoAttachments` — convergence: `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`**

The user-configured URL is the per-user webhook registered via `UpdateUser` (validated by `webhook.ValidateURL` at `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74`) and read back through `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks`.

The last three of those "updated" paths share a helper: `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66`) calls `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` at `memo_update_helpers.go:67`. So `SetMemoRelations` and `SetMemoAttachments` fire memo-updated webhooks even though neither is named "update".

**Convergence.** All four `Dispatch*Webhook` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15-51` loop over the owner's registered webhooks and call `webhook.PostAsync` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:43`, `:71`). `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) only enqueues onto a 128-slot channel; the four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`) are what call **`webhook.Post`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which is the single function that builds and issues the outbound request — `safeClient.Do(req)` at `webhook.go:152`. Every path above passes through it.

One consequence of the async hop worth noting: because delivery happens on a worker goroutine, the `err` returned by the `Dispatch*` calls only reflects enqueue-time failures, which is why each call site merely logs a warning rather than failing the RPC.
