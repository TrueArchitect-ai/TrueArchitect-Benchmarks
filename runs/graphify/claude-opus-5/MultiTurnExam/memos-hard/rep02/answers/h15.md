Two structural notes:

- The rules differ by surface in ways that matter. `checkMemoReadAccess` (`memo_service.go:39-67`) additionally hides *archived* memos from non-creators, and returns `Unauthenticated` for anonymous callers on non-public memos. The fileserver variant is the only one with a **share-token bypass** — a valid, unexpired `share_token` matching the memo grants access to private/protected attachments (`fileserver.go:683-690`) — and it's also gated on `Profile.AllowAnonymous()` for public memos (`fileserver.go:677`). The SSE filter is coarser: it only special-cases `Private`, letting `Protected` events reach any subscriber (`sse_hub.go:135-139`).
- The MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) is **not** a separate enforcement site — it exposes `MemoService_ListMemos` (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12`) by dispatching into the same v1 handlers, so it inherits the API checks above.
