**`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — every 12 hours, plus one synchronous pass at startup — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

Details:

- **What it does** — `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType == S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-issues a presigned GET URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` (`runner.go:108`), writing the fresh URL back to the attachment's `Reference` field along with an updated `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:114-123`). So the DB-stored link to the S3 object never expires out from under clients.
- **Refresh policy** — presigned URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry, and an attachment is skipped if its `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old (`runner.go:85-91`), giving a 1-day safety margin. The per-attachment S3 config falls back to the instance storage setting when the attachment doesn't carry its own (`runner.go:93-100`).
- **Schedule** — `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`); `Run` drives a `time.Ticker` and exits on `ctx.Done()` (`runner.go:29-41`).
- **Wiring** — `NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`; an immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163` (blocking, before the goroutine starts); then the ticker loop in a tracked goroutine at `server/server.go:167-171` using a dedicated cancellable `s3Context` (`server/server.go:156-159`) so `Shutdown` can stop and wait for it (`server/server.go:140`, `143`).
