Premise is wrong — **there is no message broker at all; neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor AMQP/Kafka) appears in the repo or `go.mod`, and no queue is declared anywhere.**
Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — is drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`). Producers use `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the delivery with a warning if the buffer is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:189-195`). There is no persistence, no retry, and no redelivery — deliveries in flight are lost on restart.
