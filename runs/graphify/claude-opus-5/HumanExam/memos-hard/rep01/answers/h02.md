- **Delivery function:** `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (actual `safeClient.Do(req)` at `:152`). Callers enqueue via `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`) into a 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:142-158`).
- **Protection (two layers, both in `internal/webhook`):**
  1. **Dial-time SSRF guard** — `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:162-180`) is the `Transport.DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` (`:135-140`); it resolves the host and refuses any reserved/private IP, which also defeats DNS rebinding.
  2. **Validation at configuration time** — `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) requires http/https and rejects hostnames resolving into `reservedCIDRs` (`validate.go:14-23`: loopback, RFC-1918, 169.254.0.0/16 IMDS, IPv6 equivalents).
- Both are bypassable by the opt-in `webhook.AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`validate.go:41`, set from `--allow-private-webhooks` at `cmd/memos/main.go:49`).
