**Premise is wrong — there is no message broker; neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka) appears in `go.mod`/`go.sum` or any source file, and no queue is declared anywhere.** Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) does a non-blocking send into a package-level buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:142`), drained by **4 goroutines started in `init()`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:145-158`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Consequences worth noting: the queue is unpersisted, and on overflow the event is dropped with a warning rather than retried (`webhook.go:189-195`).
