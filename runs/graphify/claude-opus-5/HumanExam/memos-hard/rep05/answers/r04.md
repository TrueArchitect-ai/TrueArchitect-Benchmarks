— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka anywhere in the repo (no such imports in `go.mod` or any Go file), and no queue is declared. Webhook delivery is fully in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` of capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:38`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). `PostAsync` (`:184`) does a non-blocking send and **drops** the webhook with a warning when the buffer is full (`:190-195`); nothing is persisted, so pending deliveries are lost on restart.
