The cache layer is an in-process, in-memory map with a background cleanup goroutine: `store/cache/cache.go` (`Set` at `:93`, `SetWithTTL` at `:98`, `cleanupLoop` at `:200`). Three instances are created in `store.New` — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — all sharing one `cache.Config` at `store/store.go:40-45`: `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000`. User entries go in via plain `Set` (`store/user.go:98,122,132,143,167`), so they take the 10-minute default TTL; they're also evicted explicitly on user delete (`store/user_delete.go:40`).
