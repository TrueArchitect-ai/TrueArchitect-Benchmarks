— Premise is wrong: neither. There is no message broker of any kind — no NATS, RabbitMQ, or AMQP client in `go.mod` and no match anywhere in the Go sources, and no queue is declared. Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`webhook.go:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the channel is full (`webhook.go:189-195`). The queue is not durable — it dies with the process.
