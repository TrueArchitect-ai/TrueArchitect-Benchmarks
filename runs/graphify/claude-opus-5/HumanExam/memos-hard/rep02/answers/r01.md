The actual cache layer is a **process-local in-memory cache** built on `sync.Map` — `store/cache/cache.go:65-72`, constructed by `cache.New` (`store/cache/cache.go:74`). Three instances are created in `store.New`: `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — `store/store.go:38-50`. The **user entry TTL is 10 minutes** (`DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `store/store.go:39`; same value as `cache.DefaultConfig()`, `store/cache/cache.go:59`), with a 5-minute cleanup sweep and a 1000-item cap. Entries are also invalidated explicitly on write/delete (`store/user.go:167`, `store/user_delete.go:40`). Being in-process, the cache is not shared across instances.
