— **Premise is wrong: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used — there is no external message broker at all, and no queue is declared anywhere.** No `nats`, `amqp`, `rabbitmq`, or `kafka` dependency appears in `go.mod`/`go.sum`. The actual mechanism is a purely in-process, in-memory queue: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184-196`), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the event with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). Consequences worth noting: no durability, no retries, and no cross-instance delivery — the queue dies with the process.
