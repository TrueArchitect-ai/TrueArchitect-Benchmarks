— Premise is wrong: there is no rate-limiting middleware, per-user or otherwise, so there are no request limits to report. `grep -rni "ratelimit|rate_limit|RateLimiter|golang.org/x/time/rate"` over the Go sources and `go.mod` returns zero hits. The only Echo middleware registered is `middleware.Recover()` and the CORS middleware (`server/server.go:51-52`), plus static/SPA middleware on the frontend service (`server/router/frontend/frontend.go:54,59`). Request-path gating is authentication/authorization only — `NewAuthorizer` + `CheckAccess` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:68-93`, ACL in `server/router/api/v1/acl_config.go`). The closest things to throttling are concurrency semaphores, not rate limits: thumbnail/image semaphores (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:61-62`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:107,117`) and the 128-slot webhook dispatch queue (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`).
