The authentication flow:
- **Frontend interceptor**: `authInterceptor` in `/bench/home/work/web/src/connect.ts` (lines 156-178) intercepts all Connect RPC requests
- **Tokens managed**: 
  1. Access tokens (JWT, 15 minutes, used in Authorization: Bearer header)
  2. Refresh tokens (JWT, 30 days, stored in HttpOnly memos_refresh cookie)
  3. Personal Access Tokens (PATs, programmatic access, prefix "memos_pat_")
- **Backend validation**: `/bench/home/work/server/auth` package handles token validation, including JWT verification for access/refresh tokens and PAT hashing/validation (see token.go and authenticator.go)
