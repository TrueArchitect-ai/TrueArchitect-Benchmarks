Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction

The SSE mechanism uses a hub at sse_hub.go with SSEHub and SSEClient types. The Broadcast() method delivers events to subscribed clients with visibility filtering (private events only to creator/admins). Handler methods that call Broadcast are: CreateMemo and DeleteMemo in memo_service.go, CreateMemoComment in memo_service_comments.go, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects in memo_update_helpers.go, and UpsertMemoReaction/DeleteMemoReaction in reaction_service.go.
