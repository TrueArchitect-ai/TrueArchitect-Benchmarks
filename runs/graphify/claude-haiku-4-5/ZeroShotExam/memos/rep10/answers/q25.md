The filter engine uses **CEL (Common Expression Language)** as parsed by the `cel-go` library. The engine lives in `/bench/home/work/internal/filter/`. The filter becomes SQL through a three-phase pipeline: (1) CEL expressions are parsed and validated by `cel-go` against the memo schema, (2) the CEL AST is converted to a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR) by `parser.go`, and (3) the renderer in `render.go` walks the IR and produces SQL fragments with dialect-specific syntax (SQLite, MySQL, or Postgres) using switch statements on the `DialectName` enum to handle differences like placeholder styles (`?` vs `$n`), JSON accessors, and boolean/time handling.
