Based on my analysis of the codebase, when a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following types of records **before** the memo row itself:

1. **memo_relation** (records where the memo is the source)
2. **memo_relation** (records where the memo is the target)
3. **attachment** (all attachments linked to the memo)

**Source:** `/bench/home/work/store/memo.go` lines 140-159 show the `Store.DeleteMemo()` function that orchestrates this deletion order. The function deletes memo relations in both directions, then deletes all attachments (which includes storage cleanup), and finally deletes the memo row itself.
