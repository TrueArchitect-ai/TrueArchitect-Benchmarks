**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`

**Handler names that broadcast through the SSEHub:**
- `CreateMemo`
- `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`)
- `DeleteMemo`
- `CreateMemoComment`
- `UpsertMemoReaction`
- `DeleteMemoReaction`

The `SSEHub` type in `sse_hub.go` manages client subscriptions and broadcasts events through a `Broadcast()` method. Events are dispatched after memo or reaction operations complete, with visibility controls ensuring clients only receive events they're authorized to see.
