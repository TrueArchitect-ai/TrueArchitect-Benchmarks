**memo_relation**

The `memo_relation` table stores links between one memo and another related memo. It has three columns: `memo_id` (foreign key to memo), `related_memo_id` (foreign key to another memo), and `type` (indicating the relationship type). This table maintains a unique constraint on the combination of (memo_id, related_memo_id, type) to prevent duplicate relationships.
