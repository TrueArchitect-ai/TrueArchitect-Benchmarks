MemoService is served over TWO protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/1.1)** — `/bench/home/work/server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108)
2. **Connect (HTTP/1.1 or HTTP/2)** — `/bench/home/work/server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40)

The gRPC-Gateway registration happens in `v1.go` where `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` is called, wrapping the gRPC service for HTTP/1.1 clients. The Connect protocol registration happens in `connect_handler.go` where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is called, providing native Connect RPC support for browser and client applications.
