**Engine + Path:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go` (Google's Go implementation at `github.com/google/cel-go`); lives at `/bench/home/work/internal/filter/` in `engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`, and `schema.go`.

**Mechanism in one sentence:** The filter is parsed by `cel-go` into an AST (lines 53-60 in `/bench/home/work/internal/filter/engine.go`), converted to a dialect-agnostic IR (intermediate representation) by `buildCondition()` in `parser.go`, then rendered to SQL fragments with dialect-specific handling (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`) by the renderer in `render.go`.
