Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks and their dispatch functions:

- memo created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`

**Convergence point for delivery:**

All dispatch functions converge through `webhook.PostAsync()` in `/bench/home/work/internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`, which enqueues webhook requests to an async queue (4 worker goroutines) that then calls `Post()` to perform the actual HTTP delivery with HMAC-SHA256 signing.

The internal routing is:
- `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`, `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`, `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` → `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook()` (line 48 in `memo_service_webhooks.go`)
- `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` → direct handling
