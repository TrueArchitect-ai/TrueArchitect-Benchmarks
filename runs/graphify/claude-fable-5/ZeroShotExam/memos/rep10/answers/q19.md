MemoService is exposed over two protocols, both wired up in `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/REST + JSON)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, where `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the service on the gateway mux, which is mounted at `/api/v1/*` (v1.go:128).

2. **Connect (connectrpc.com, replacing grpc-web for browser clients)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, where `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` registers the handler inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` (v1.go:144). The per-method adapter wrappers for MemoService live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService section starting at line 304).

Both protocols share the same underlying implementation: `APIV1Service` embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer` (v1.go:26), and `ConnectServiceHandler` wraps that same struct to adapt it to Connect's request/response types.
