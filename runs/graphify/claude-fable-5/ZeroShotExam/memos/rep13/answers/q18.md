The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go:10`) has three implementations, each with a `NewDB` constructor as its entry file:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL driver

**NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (line 14) is the factory that selects between them at startup, switching on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) and returning an error for any unknown driver.
