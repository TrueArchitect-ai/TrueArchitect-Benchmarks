**`memo_relation`**

The `memo_relation` table stores links between one memo and another related memo. Its schema is defined in the migration files (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46`, and correspondingly in the sqlite/postgres migrations) with columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type` (e.g. `COMMENT`). The store-layer CRUD for it lives in `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go` (with equivalents under `store/db/postgres/` and `store/db/mysql/`), and it's joined in queries like `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:141` to resolve parent/comment relationships between memos.
