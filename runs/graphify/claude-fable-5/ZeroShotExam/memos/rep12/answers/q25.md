**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by the in-repo filter package at `internal/filter/`.

**Mechanism:** `APIV1Service.ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`) passes the filter strings down to the store, where each driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, and the mysql/postgres counterparts) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect constant. The engine compiles the filter in a CEL environment whose variables (`content`, `tag`, `pinned`, `created_ts`, `now`, …) are declared by the schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`), then `internal/filter/parser.go` walks the parsed CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (IR in `internal/filter/ir.go`); `internal/filter/render.go` renders that tree into parameterized SQL per dialect, keyed by `DialectName` (`sqlite`/`mysql`/`postgres`, `internal/filter/schema.go:11-16`), with dialect-specific SQL templates (e.g. `('users/' || %s)` vs `CONCAT('users/', %s)`) and JSON-extraction/placeholder handling in the schema's per-dialect `Expressions` map.

In one sentence: the filter is a CEL expression compiled by cel-go into an AST, lowered by `internal/filter` into a dialect-neutral condition tree, and rendered to SQLite/MySQL/Postgres SQL with args by a per-dialect renderer that each `store/db/<dialect>` driver invokes.
