MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

The project ships SQL migrations under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/` (29 .sql files), `store/migration/postgres/` (23 .sql files), and `store/migration/sqlite/` (58 .sql files). These match the three database drivers in `store/db/` (`mysql`, `postgres`, `sqlite`), applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go`.
