`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

For a fresh (uninitialized) database, the migrator applies the full current schema from `LATEST.sql` instead of replaying incremental migrations — see `store/migrator.go:27` ("preMigrate: Check if DB is initialized. If not, apply LATEST.sql") and `store/migrator.go:42` ("LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations"), with the filename constant `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` at `store/migrator.go:56`. The SQLite copy lives at `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (MySQL and Postgres have their own equivalents alongside their versioned migration folders).
