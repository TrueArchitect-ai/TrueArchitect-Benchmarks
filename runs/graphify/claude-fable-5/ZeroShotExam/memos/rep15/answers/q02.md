**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The project ships versioned SQL migrations under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each containing versioned folders like `0.17`–`0.30` plus a `LATEST.sql`). These are applied by `store/migrator.go` and correspond to the three database drivers in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
