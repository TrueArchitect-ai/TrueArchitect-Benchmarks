**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with the `github.com/google/cel-go` library (`go.mod`). The filter engine lives in **`internal/filter/`**.

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-223`) validates the filter string and puts it on `memoFind.Filters`; each store driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, plus the mysql/postgres equivalents) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect, and the engine runs a three-phase pipeline — cel-go parses/validates the expression against the memo schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`, `parser.go`), the CEL AST is normalized into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks the IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args per dialect (SQLite/MySQL/Postgres), handling differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, JSON access (`JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`/`@>`), and boolean/timestamp coercions. Entry points `Compile`/`CompileToStatement`/`DefaultEngine` are in `internal/filter/engine.go`, with the pipeline documented in `internal/filter/README.md`.
