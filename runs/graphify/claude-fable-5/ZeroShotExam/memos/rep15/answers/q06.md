**`memo_relation`**

The table is defined in the database schemas, e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (also in `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql` and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, introduced by `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`). It has columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`, linking one memo to another related memo.
