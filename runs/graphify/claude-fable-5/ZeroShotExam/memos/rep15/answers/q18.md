The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`), each with its entry file:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver (`sqlite.NewDB`)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver (`mysql.NewDB`)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL driver (`postgres.NewDB`)

**NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (line 14). It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) at startup and returns the matching `store.Driver` implementation, erroring on any unknown driver name.
