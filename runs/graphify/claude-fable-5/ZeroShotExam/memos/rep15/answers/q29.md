**Answer:** `web/src/connect.ts`; short-lived JWT access tokens (with refresh via a cookie-based refresh token, plus support for long-lived Personal Access Tokens); `server/auth`.

Details:

- **Interceptor:** The frontend defines a Connect RPC `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` (the "Authentication Interceptor" section around line 153, with helpers `setAuthorizationHeader` at line 97, `getRequestToken` at line 124, and `shouldHandleUnauthenticatedRetry` at line 102). It attaches an `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header to every RPC, does a preflight refresh when the access token is near expiry, and reactively refreshes-and-retries on 401 responses.
- **Tokens it manages:** a short-lived **JWT access token** (kept in memory via `web/src/auth-state.ts` — `getAccessToken`/`setAccessToken`/`isTokenExpired`) which it renews by calling the `refreshToken` RPC through a separate interceptor-free transport; the **refresh token** itself lives in an HTTP-only cookie and is sent automatically (see `ExtractRefreshTokenFromCookie` in `server/auth/extract.go`). Refreshes are serialized through a `tokenRefreshManager` to avoid races with token rotation.
- **Backend validation:** the server-side `AuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go:208` reads the `Authorization` header and delegates to the `Authorizer` in `server/router/api/v1/authz.go`, which is backed by the **`server/auth` package** (`github.com/usememos/memos/server/auth`). Its `Authenticator` (`server/auth/authenticator.go`) validates short-lived JWT access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`/`ParseAccessTokenV2`, 15-minute lifetime), refresh tokens against the database (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), and long-lived Personal Access Tokens.
