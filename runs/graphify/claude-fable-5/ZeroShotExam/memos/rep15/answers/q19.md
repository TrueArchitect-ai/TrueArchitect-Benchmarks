MemoService is served over both the **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** protocol and the **Connect (connectrpc)** protocol, and both are wired up in `RegisterGateway`:

- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `RegisterGateway` creates the gateway mux and registers the service at line 108 with `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, then mounts it under `/api/v1/*`.
- **Connect (connectrpc)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `ConnectServiceHandler` wraps `APIV1Service`, and `RegisterConnectHandlers` registers the service at line 40 with `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)`; the per-method Connect adapter wrappers live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (the `// MemoService` section around line 304). The Connect mux is mounted from `v1.go` under `/memos.api.v1.*`.

The comment in `connect_handler.go` states this explicitly: the wrapper pattern lets the same `APIV1Service` implementation "Support both native gRPC and Connect protocols."
