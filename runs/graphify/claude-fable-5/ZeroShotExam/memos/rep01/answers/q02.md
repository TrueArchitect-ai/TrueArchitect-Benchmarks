**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The project ships versioned SQL migrations for three dialects, one directory per dialect under `store/migration/`: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each containing versioned subdirectories like `0.19`–`0.30` plus a `LATEST.sql`). These are applied by `store/migrator.go` (`.applyMigrations()` at `store/migrator.go:140`), with matching driver implementations under `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
