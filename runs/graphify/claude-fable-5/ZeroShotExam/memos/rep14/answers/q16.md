When `Store.DeleteMemo` runs (called from the API's memo deletion path), the store layer cleans up two other kinds of records before deleting the memo row itself, as implemented in `store/memo.go:140-159`:

- **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation`) — deleted in both directions: rows where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and rows where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142-147`)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — listed via `ListAttachments` with the memo's ID, then each deleted with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`)

Only after those succeed does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row itself (`store/memo.go:158`).
