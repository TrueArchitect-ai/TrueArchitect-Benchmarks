**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is the only frontend source file that creates ConnectRPC service clients. It sets up a `createConnectTransport` from `@connectrpc/connect-web` (with an auth interceptor and token-refresh logic) and creates all the service clients via `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` — for example `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` at `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`, plus a dedicated `refreshAuthClient` at `web/src/connect.ts:72`.
