**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The project ships versioned SQL migrations under `store/migration/` with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each containing versioned `*.sql` files plus `LATEST.sql`). This matches the driver layout in `store/db/` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`), and `store/migrator.go:255` selects the path as `migration/{driver}/` at runtime.
