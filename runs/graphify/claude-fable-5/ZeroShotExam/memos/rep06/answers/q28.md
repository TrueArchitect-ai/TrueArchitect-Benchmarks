**Background runners the server starts** (`server/server.go:153`, `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — the only one. It's run once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`, `server/server.go:163`), then continuously in a goroutine via `Run`, driven by a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` with `runnerInterval = 12 hours` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-30`). It refreshes presigned S3 URLs for attachments; shutdown is via per-runner `context.CancelFunc` plus a `sync.WaitGroup`.

**Not a background runner, despite living in `server/runner/`:**
