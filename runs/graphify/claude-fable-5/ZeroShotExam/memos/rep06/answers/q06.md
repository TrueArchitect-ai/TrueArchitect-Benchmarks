**`memo_relation`**

This table links one memo to another related memo via its `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` columns (plus a relation `type`). It is defined in the schema files `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, and `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, and was introduced in the migration `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`.
