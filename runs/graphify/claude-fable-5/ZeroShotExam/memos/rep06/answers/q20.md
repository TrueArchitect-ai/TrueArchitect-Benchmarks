**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoRelations and SetMemoAttachments via the shared update helper).**

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The hub (`SSEHub`, `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`) manages subscribed clients and its `Broadcast()` method (`sse_hub.go:115`) fans events out to them. The HTTP endpoint is wired in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`), registered from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`. On the browser side, `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` consumes the stream.
- **Memo handlers that broadcast through `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (emits `SSEEventMemoCreated`, suppressed when invoked from CreateMemoComment)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (`SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
  - `UpdateMemo` — via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71` (`SSEEventMemoUpdated`), called at `memo_service.go:513`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (`SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`)
  - `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) also broadcast the memo-updated event through the same helper; reaction handlers (`reaction_service.go:106,161`) broadcast reaction events as well, though those are reaction rather than memo handlers.
