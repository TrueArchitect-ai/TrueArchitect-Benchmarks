**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The project ships migration SQL files under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/`. These are embedded and applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go` (see `getMigrationBasePath()` at `store/migrator.go:254` and the `fs.Glob` over `*.sql` files at `store/migrator.go:141`), with corresponding drivers in `store/db/` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`).
