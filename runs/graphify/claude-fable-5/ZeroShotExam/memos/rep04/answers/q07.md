**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is the single frontend source file where the ConnectRPC service clients are created. It builds a `createConnectTransport` transport with an auth interceptor and then creates all the service clients with `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` — for example `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`), plus a separate `refreshAuthClient` on a dedicated refresh transport (`web/src/connect.ts:72`). A repo-wide grep confirms no other file under `web/src` calls `createClient`.
