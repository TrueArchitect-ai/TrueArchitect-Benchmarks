`web/src/connect.ts`

This is the only frontend source file that calls ConnectRPC's `createClient`. It builds a `createConnectTransport` transport with an auth interceptor and exports all the service clients — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:184-203`, plus an interceptor-free `refreshAuthClient` at line 72).
