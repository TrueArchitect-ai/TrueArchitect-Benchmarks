**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project ships migration SQL files under `store/migration/` with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/` (29 .sql files), `store/migration/postgres/` (23 .sql files), and `store/migration/sqlite/` (58 .sql files). These are applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go` (`.applyMigrations()`, `.getMigrationBasePath()`), and each dialect has a matching driver implementation under `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.
