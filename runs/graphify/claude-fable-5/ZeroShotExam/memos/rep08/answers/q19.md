MemoService (the `APIV1Service` implementation) is served over **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON HTTP)** and **Connect (ConnectRPC, for browser clients — replacing grpc-web)**. There is no native gRPC server registration in the codebase; both protocols are wired up inside `RegisterGateway`.

- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, with the gateway mux mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*`.
- **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` from `v1.go:144`). The per-method Connect request/response wrapping for MemoService lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService section starts at line 304).

Both reuse the same `APIV1Service` gRPC-style implementation as the single source of business logic.
