**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The project ships migration files in three per-dialect directories under `store/migration/` — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/`. The migrator selects among them at runtime via `getMigrationBasePath()` in `store/migrator.go:254`, which builds the path from the configured driver name, and the matching driver implementations live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
