The domain structs in the `store` package are the ones the DB drivers (in `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/`) read from and write to those tables — e.g. `INSERT INTO user` in `store/db/sqlite/user.go:17`, `INSERT INTO attachment` in `store/db/postgres/attachment.go:33`, and `INSERT INTO reaction` in `store/db/postgres/reaction.go:13`.

- `user`: `User` — `store/user.go` (struct at line 29)
- `attachment`: `Attachment` — `store/attachment.go` (struct at line 16)
- `reaction`: `Reaction` — `store/reaction.go` (struct at line 7)

Note the proto-generated types (e.g. `User` in `proto/gen/api/v1/user_service.pb.go`) are API/wire messages, not the table-mapped structs.
