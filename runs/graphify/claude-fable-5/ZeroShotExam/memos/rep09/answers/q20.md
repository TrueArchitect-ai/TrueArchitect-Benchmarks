**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, and CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoAttachments and SetMemoRelations via the shared update helper).**

Details, verified against the knowledge graph and source:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The hub type `SSEHub` (with `NewSSEHub()` and `Broadcast()`) lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (Broadcast at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:115`), with tests in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub_test.go` and the streaming endpoint covered by `server/router/api/v1/sse_service_test.go`. On the browser side, `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` consumes the stream.

- **Handlers that broadcast:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (emits `SSEEventMemoCreated`; suppressed when invoked from CreateMemoComment)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (`SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (`SSEEventMemoCommentCreated` for the parent memo)
  - `UpdateMemo` — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoUpdated` indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71` (called at `memo_service.go:513`). The same helper is also invoked by `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`).
  - Additionally, the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` broadcast memo reaction events (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161`), though these live in the reaction service rather than the memo service files.
