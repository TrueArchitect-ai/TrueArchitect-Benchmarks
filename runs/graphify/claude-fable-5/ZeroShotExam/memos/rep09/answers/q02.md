MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

The project ships versioned migration SQL for three dialects under `store/migration/`: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/`, each containing per-version migration directories (e.g. `0.19`, `0.20`, …). These are applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go`, with the dialect drivers living in `store/db/` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`).
