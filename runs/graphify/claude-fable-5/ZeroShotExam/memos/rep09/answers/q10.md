**`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`**

For a fresh (uninitialized) database, the migrator applies the full current schema from `LATEST.sql` instead of running incremental migrations. This is documented in `store/migrator.go` (lines 27 and 42: "LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)", with `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` at line 56). The SQLite-specific copy lives at `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, alongside equivalent files for MySQL and Postgres.
