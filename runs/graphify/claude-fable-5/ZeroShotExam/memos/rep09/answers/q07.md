The ConnectRPC service clients are created in **`web/src/connect.ts`**. It creates a transport with `createConnectTransport` from `@connectrpc/connect-web` and instantiates all the service clients via `createClient` — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:181-203`).
