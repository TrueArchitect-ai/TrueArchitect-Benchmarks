MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

The project ships versioned SQL migrations under `store/migration/` with one subdirectory per dialect — `store/migration/mysql`, `store/migration/postgres`, and `store/migration/sqlite` — each containing versioned migration folders (e.g. `0.17`–`0.30`) plus a `LATEST.sql`. These match the three database drivers in `store/db/` (`mysql`, `postgres`, `sqlite`), and migrations are applied by `store/migrator.go`.
