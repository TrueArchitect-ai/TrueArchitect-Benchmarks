**`memo_relation`**

This table stores links between one memo and another related memo. Its schema has a `memo_id` column, a `related_memo_id` column, and a `type` column, with a uniqueness constraint over all three. It is defined in the schema files for all three supported databases:

- `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47`
- `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`
- `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46`

The corresponding data-access code lives in `store/memo_relation.go` and the driver implementations `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go`, and `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`.
