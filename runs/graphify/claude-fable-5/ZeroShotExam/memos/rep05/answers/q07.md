**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is the only frontend source file that creates ConnectRPC clients. It imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect`, sets up the transport, and exports all service clients — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`, under the "Transport & Service Clients" section).
