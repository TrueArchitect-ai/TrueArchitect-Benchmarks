The call chain for creating a memo, verified in source:

- `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` — `rpc CreateMemo(CreateMemoRequest) returns (Memo)` (line 21)
- `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` — `func (s *APIV1Service) CreateMemo(...)` (line 70), which builds the store model and calls the store
- `store/memo.go` — `func (s *Store) CreateMemo(...)` (line 109), which validates the UID and delegates to `s.driver.CreateMemo`
- `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` — `func (d *DB) CreateMemo(...)` (line 16), which assembles and executes the `INSERT INTO memo (...)` statement
