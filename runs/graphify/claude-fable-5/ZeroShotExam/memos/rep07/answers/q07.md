**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is where all the ConnectRPC service clients are created in the frontend. It imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and exports a client per service — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` (web/src/connect.ts:192-203), plus an internal `refreshAuthClient` at web/src/connect.ts:72.
