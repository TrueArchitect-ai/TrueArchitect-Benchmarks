**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project ships versioned migration scripts under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (each containing versioned folders like `0.17`–`0.30` plus a `LATEST.sql`). These are applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go` (`.Migrate()`, `.getMigrationBasePath()`), and each dialect has a matching driver under `store/db/{mysql,postgres,sqlite}/`.
