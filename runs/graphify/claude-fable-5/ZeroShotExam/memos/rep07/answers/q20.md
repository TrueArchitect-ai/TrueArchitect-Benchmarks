**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoRelations and SetMemoAttachments via the shared update helper)**

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The HTTP endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes()` and served by `handleSSE()` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`; the browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `NewSSEHub()` (line 63), `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`, and `Broadcast()` (line 115). It's instantiated in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` and wired to routes at `v1.go:125`.
- **Memo handlers that broadcast** (all on `APIV1Service`):
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) all broadcast indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects()` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`.
  - Reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction` / `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (lines 106, 161) also broadcast memo-scoped events, if you count reaction handlers as memo-related.
