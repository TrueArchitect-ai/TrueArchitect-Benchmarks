The domain structs live in the `store` package; the SQL in the per-driver files under `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/` reads and writes the corresponding tables (e.g. `INSERT INTO user ...` in `store/db/sqlite/user.go:17`, `INSERT INTO attachment ...` in `store/db/postgres/attachment.go:33`, `INSERT INTO reaction ...` in `store/db/postgres/reaction.go:13`).

- `user`: **User** — `store/user.go:29`
- `attachment`: **Attachment** — `store/attachment.go:16`
- `reaction`: **Reaction** — `store/reaction.go:7`

Note there are also protobuf-generated `User`/`Attachment` structs under `proto/gen/`, but those are API/serialization types, not the ones scanned from the database tables.
