**`memo_relation`**

This table stores links between one memo and another related memo. Its schema — `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type` columns — is defined in the migration files such as `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, and it was introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`. The corresponding data-access code lives in `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`, and the postgres equivalent.
