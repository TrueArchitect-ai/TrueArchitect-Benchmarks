`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

For a fresh (uninitialized) database, the migrator applies the full current schema from a single file rather than replaying incremental migrations. This is confirmed in `store/migrator.go`, which defines `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` and documents that `LATEST.sql` is the "Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)" — applied during `preMigrate` when the DB isn't initialized. The SQLite variant lives at `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (alongside per-version incremental migrations like `store/migration/sqlite/0.28/...` used for upgrades).
