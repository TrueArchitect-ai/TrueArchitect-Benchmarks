When `Store.DeleteMemo` runs (called by the API's delete-memo handler), it removes two other kinds of records before deleting the memo row itself (`store/memo.go:140-159`):

- **Memo relation records** — deleted twice via `driver.DeleteMemoRelation`, once for relations where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and once where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142-147`)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — listed with `ListAttachments{MemoID}` and each removed via `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`)

Only after those cleanups does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row (`store/memo.go:158`).
