**mysql, postgres, sqlite**

The project embeds SQL migrations per database driver under `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql` (documented in `store/migrator.go:39` and embedded via `//go:embed migration` at `store/migrator.go:44`). The `store/migration/` directory contains exactly three dialect subdirectories — `mysql/`, `postgres/`, and `sqlite/` — matching the three driver implementations in `store/db/`.
