MemoService is served over both **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP JSON)** and **Connect RPC**:

- **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/REST)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`. In `RegisterGateway`, the service is registered on the gateway mux with `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`), and the mux is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*`.

- **Connect RPC** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`. In `RegisterConnectHandlers`, the service is registered via `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`). The per-method Connect wrapping (adapting `connect.Request`/`connect.Response` to the underlying gRPC-style `APIV1Service` methods like `CreateMemo`, `ListMemos`) lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. line 306 for `CreateMemo`).

Both paths share the same `APIV1Service` business logic — the `ConnectServiceHandler` struct in `connect_handler.go` explicitly documents this wrapper pattern ("Support both native gRPC and Connect protocols").
