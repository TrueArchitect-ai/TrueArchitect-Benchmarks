The premise is off: there is no nightly database-cleanup job registered in the cron scheduler. The repo contains a cron scheduler package (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `internal/scheduler/parser.go`), but nothing in the application actually registers jobs with it — the only importers of `internal/scheduler` are its own test files (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`).

What does exist, and may be what the question is reaching for:

- **Documentation examples only**: the scheduler's `README.md` (line 34), `doc.go` (line 15), and `example_test.go` (line 97) all show an illustrative `"daily-cleanup"` / `"cleanup"` job with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily). These are usage examples with placeholder handlers (`fmt.Println("Running cleanup...")`), not real jobs, and they clean "old data" generically — not the database.
- **The real periodic cleanup** in the codebase is the in-memory cache cleanup in `store/cache/cache.go:200` (`cleanupLoop`), which runs on a plain `time.Ticker` every 5 minutes (`CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, set at `store/cache/cache.go:60` and `store/store.go:42`) — it does not use the cron scheduler and does not touch the database.

So: no scheduled time exists, because no database-cleanup cron job is wired up anywhere in the code. If you were thinking of the `0 2 * * *` example, that's documentation, not a configured job.
