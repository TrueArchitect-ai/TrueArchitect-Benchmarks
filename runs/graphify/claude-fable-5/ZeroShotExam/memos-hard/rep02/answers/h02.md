The outbound webhook delivery executes in `Post()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (with `PostAsync()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` enqueueing payloads to a bounded queue that worker goroutines drain by calling `Post`). Content-change dispatch is wired in from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` and `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`.

The SSRF protection has two layers, both in the `internal/webhook` package:

- **Dial-time guard (the real protection):** all requests go through `safeClient`, whose transport uses `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`). It resolves the target hostname at connection time and refuses to dial any address matching `isReservedIP` — the reserved ranges (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS 169.254.0.0/16, and IPv6 equivalents) are defined in `reservedCIDRs` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14`. Doing the check at dial time also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks.
- **Creation-time validation:** `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects webhook URLs that aren't http/https or that resolve to a reserved/private IP when the webhook is configured.

An escape hatch exists for self-hosted setups: `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) disables the reserved-IP check when set.

**Answer format:** delivery function — `Post()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `safeDialContext`/`isReservedIP` blocking reserved/private CIDRs in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` + `internal/webhook/validate.go`.
