**Convergence function — `webhook.Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`.** Both the async workers (`PostAsync` → `asyncPostQueue` → `Post`, lines 42–55, 184–196) and any direct caller funnel through `Post`, which builds the request against `requestPayload.URL` and performs the actual delivery at `safeClient.Do(req)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`).
