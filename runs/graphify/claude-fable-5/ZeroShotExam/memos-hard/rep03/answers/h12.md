**Determination method:** I traced the driver-selection chain rather than assuming, because three implementations exist (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`).

1. The store's `CreateMemo` is a thin dispatcher — `store/memo.go:113` calls `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)` on whatever `store.Driver` was injected.
2. The driver is chosen in `store/db/db.go` (`NewDBDriver`), which switches on `profile.Driver`: `"sqlite"` → `sqlite.NewDB`, `"mysql"` → `mysql.NewDB`, `"postgres"` → `postgres.NewDB`.
3. With no database flags, that value defaults to `sqlite`, set in `cmd/memos/main.go:122` (`viper.SetDefault("driver", "sqlite")`) and mirrored on the flag default at line 130.
