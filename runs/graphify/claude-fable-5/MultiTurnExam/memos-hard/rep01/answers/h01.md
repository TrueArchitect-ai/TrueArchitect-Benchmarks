- The component is the `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method pages through S3-stored attachments (batches of 100) and re-generates presigned URLs so externally-stored attachment links don't expire/go stale.
- Schedule: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`); `Run` fires `RunOnce` on a 12-hour ticker until the context is cancelled.
- Wiring: `Server.Start` calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` at `server/server.go:129`. That function (`server/server.go:153`) creates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, does an immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then starts the continuous `Run` loop in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG`, with a dedicated cancel function for shutdown.
