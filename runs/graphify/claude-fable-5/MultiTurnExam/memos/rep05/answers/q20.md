**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub`, held on `APIV1Service` and created in `NewAPIV1Service` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go`). Event construction helpers live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

**Handlers that broadcast through it:**
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
- `UpdateMemo` — via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
- `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161`
