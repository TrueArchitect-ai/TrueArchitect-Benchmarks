MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **REST (HTTP/JSON via gRPC-Gateway)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108): `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the service on the gRPC-Gateway mux, exposing the `google.api.http` REST mappings like `POST /api/v1/memos`.

2. **Connect (ConnectRPC, used by the web frontend)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40): `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is mounted on an `http.ServeMux` in `RegisterConnectHandlers`, with the `ConnectServiceHandler` wrapper (also `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`) adapting the underlying `APIV1Service` implementation to Connect's request/response types.
