**Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`) — `internal/filter/`**

The filter expressions passed to ListMemos are CEL. The engine lives in the `internal/filter` package: `internal/filter/engine.go` (`DefaultEngine`, line 111) sets up the cel-go environment with the memo schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`), `internal/filter/parser.go` parses/typechecks the expression into an intermediate representation (`internal/filter/ir.go`).

**Mechanism:** each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, and the mysql/postgres equivalents) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` (`internal/filter/helpers.go:9`) with its dialect constant (`DialectSQLite` / `DialectMySQL` / `DialectPostgres`), and the renderer in `internal/filter/render.go` walks the parsed IR and emits dialect-specific WHERE-clause fragments and bound arguments (switching on dialect for things like JSON access and placeholders), which the driver appends to its SQL query.
