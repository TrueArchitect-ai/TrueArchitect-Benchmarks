**mysql, postgres, sqlite**

The project ships migrations under `store/migration/` with one subdirectory per SQL dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/`. These are applied by the migrator in `store/migrator.go`, with matching database drivers under `store/db/` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`).
