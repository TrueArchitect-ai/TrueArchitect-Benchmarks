**Mechanism:** the compiled frontend (`web` build output copied into `server/router/frontend/dist/`) is embedded into the Go binary with `go:embed` and served as static files by Echo's static middleware, with an SPA fallback to `index.html` for client-side routes.

**File:** `server/router/frontend/frontend.go` — `//go:embed dist/*` (line 20), `FrontendService.Serve` wires `middleware.StaticWithConfig` over the embedded FS plus `spaFallbackMiddleware`, and it's registered at startup in `server/server.go:72` via `frontend.NewFrontendService(profile, store).Serve(ctx, echoServer)`.
