**Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `cel-go` — `internal/filter/`** (schema/environment in `internal/filter/schema.go`, engine in `internal/filter/engine.go`).

**Mechanism:** The filter goes through a three-phase pipeline (documented in `internal/filter/README.md`): the CEL expression is parsed and validated against a memo-specific schema (`schema.go`), normalized into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation of conditions (`internal/filter/ir.go`, `parser.go`), and then rendered into a parameterized SQL WHERE fragment per dialect by the renderer in `internal/filter/render.go`, which switches on `DialectSQLite` / `DialectMySQL` / `DialectPostgres` to handle differences like JSON access, placeholders, and LIKE vs ILIKE. The dialect drivers invoke this via `filter.AppendConditions` — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` passes `filter.DialectSQLite` to append the rendered conditions to the query's WHERE clause and args.
