Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes:

1. **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation`) — both where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) of a relation (`store/memo.go:142-147`)
