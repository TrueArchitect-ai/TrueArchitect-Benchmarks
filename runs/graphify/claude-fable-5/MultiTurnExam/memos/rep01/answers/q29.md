**Interceptor:** `web/src/connect.ts` — it defines a ConnectRPC auth `Interceptor` attached to the transport that adds the access token as a `Authorization: Bearer` header, does a preflight refresh when the token is near expiry (`REQUEST_TOKEN_EXPIRY_BUFFER_MS`), and reactively refreshes on 401 via a dedicated `refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient` (no interceptor, to avoid recursion). Token storage/sharing across tabs lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts` (localStorage + BroadcastChannel).

**Token kinds:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT sent as a Bearer header, kept in localStorage) and a long-lived **refresh token** (held server-side as a session cookie, exchanged via the `AuthService.RefreshToken` RPC to mint new access tokens).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` — `server/auth/authenticator.go` (`Authenticator`, with `resolveBearer` for access tokens and `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` for refresh tokens), plus `server/auth/token.go` and `server/auth/extract.go`; it's applied to both the Connect handlers and the gRPC-gateway middleware in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
