**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `github.com/google/cel-go` — the engine lives in **`internal/filter/`** (`internal/filter/engine.go` builds a `cel.Env` and compiles filters; grammar/schema in `internal/filter/schema.go` and `internal/filter/parser.go`).

**Mechanism:** The engine compiles the CEL expression into a dialect-agnostic condition tree/IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), which each database driver then renders into parameterized SQL for its own dialect via `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectSQLite/DialectMySQL/DialectPostgres, &where, &args)` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`), with per-dialect SQL emission handled by the renderer's `DialectName` switches in `internal/filter/render.go`.
