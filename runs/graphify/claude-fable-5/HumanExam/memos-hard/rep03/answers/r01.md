— False premise: there is **no Redis**. The cache layer is an in-process, in-memory `cache.Cache` (`store/cache/cache.go`). Store creates three caches (instance-settings, users, user-settings) with a **DefaultTTL of 10 minutes** (`store/store.go:41`, `store/cache/cache.go:59`), 5-minute cleanup, max 1000 items. So the "user entries" TTL is 10 minutes, via the in-memory cache — not Redis.
