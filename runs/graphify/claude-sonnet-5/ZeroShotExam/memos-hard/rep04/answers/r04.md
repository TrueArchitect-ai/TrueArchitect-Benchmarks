**Premise correction:** Neither. This repository (usememos/memos) has no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency anywhere in the codebase (no `amqp`, `nats-io`, or broker imports in `go.mod` or source).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is not broker-backed at all. It uses an in-process, buffered Go channel as a worker queue:

- The queue is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, a channel with a capacity of 128 pending payloads.
- Four goroutine workers are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` to actually deliver each webhook via HTTP.
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no external broker, persistence, or redelivery semantics.
