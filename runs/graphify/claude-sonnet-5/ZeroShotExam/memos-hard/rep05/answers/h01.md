**Component:** the S3 presign background runner, `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), which re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs (via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject`, valid 5 days) before they expire, refreshing any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry and updating the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` in the store.

**Schedule:** runs once immediately, then on a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:28-40`).

**Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `(s *Server) startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` — which is itself called from `(s *Server) Start(ctx)` at `server/server.go:129`. It creates `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` once synchronously, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `s.backgroundRunnerWG`.
