**Premise correction:** Neither. This codebase (usememos/memos) doesn't use NATS or RabbitMQ (or any external message broker) for webhook delivery — there's no AMQP/NATS client dependency anywhere in `go.mod`, and no broker connection code exists.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is fanned out through an in-process, unbuffered-consumer Go channel. It's declared as a package-level buffered channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. A fixed pool of 4 goroutines, spawned in the package's `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), range over that channel and call `Post()` for each payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking channel send and drops (with a `slog.Warn`) if the queue of 128 is full — there's no persistence, retry, or redelivery semantics you'd expect from a real broker.
