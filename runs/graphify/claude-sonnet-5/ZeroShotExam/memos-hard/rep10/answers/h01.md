**Component — schedule — wiring site:**

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) re-signs S3 attachment URLs before they expire. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (runner.go:47) refreshes any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (5-day presign expiry, 1-day safety margin — runner.go:85-90), generating a new presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and persisting it with `Store.UpdateAttachment`.

- **Schedule:** every 12 hours via `time.Ticker` — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (runner.go:27), driven by `Run(ctx)` (runner.go:29-41). It also fires once immediately at startup via `RunOnce`.
- **Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:162-163` (`s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`; `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`) and `server/server.go:169` (`go func() { ... s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context) ... }()`), both inside `startBackgroundRunners` (server.go:153), which is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
