**Premise correction:** Neither. The webhook delivery workers don't consume from any external message broker at all — there's no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency anywhere in this repository (`grep` for `nats`, `amqp`, `rabbitmq` across `.go`/`.ts`/config files returns nothing).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process Go channel as a bounded queue, defined and consumed entirely in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:
- The "queue" is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, a buffered channel (capacity 128).
- Workers are spun up in the package `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` — 4 goroutines each `range`-loop over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post(payload)` to deliver the webhook via HTTP.
- Producers call `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking send into the channel and drops (with a warning log) if the queue is full — no broker, no persistence, no redelivery guarantees beyond the process's lifetime.
