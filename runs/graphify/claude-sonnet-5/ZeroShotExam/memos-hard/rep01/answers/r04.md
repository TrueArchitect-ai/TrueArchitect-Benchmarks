**Premise correction:** Neither. This repo (usememos/memos) doesn't use an external message broker for webhook delivery — there's no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency anywhere in the codebase (no `amqp`/RabbitMQ or `nats` imports in any `.go` file).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process Go channel as a bounded work queue, not a broker. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `asyncPostQueue` is a buffered channel (`make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`). At startup, `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over this channel and call `Post()` for each payload. `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues payloads with a non-blocking `select`/`default`, dropping and logging a warning if the queue of 128 is full — so delivery is fire-and-forget, in-memory, with no persistence or external broker involved.
