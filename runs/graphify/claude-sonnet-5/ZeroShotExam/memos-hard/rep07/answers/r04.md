Actual mechanism: webhook delivery isn't broker-based — it's an in-process buffered Go channel used as a worker pool, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

Four goroutines are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) to drain this channel and call `Post()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) for each payload.
