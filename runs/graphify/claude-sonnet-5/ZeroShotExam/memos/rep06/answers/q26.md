**Additional context (not hit by `DeleteMemo` code, but relevant):**
- **memo_share** has a schema-level `FOREIGN KEY (memo_id) REFERENCES memo(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` (store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:108, and sqlite/mysql equivalents). This is a DB-enforced cascade, not explicit Go cascade code, so rows there are removed automatically as a consequence of the direct `memo` deletion, even though `DeleteMemo` never references `memo_share`.
