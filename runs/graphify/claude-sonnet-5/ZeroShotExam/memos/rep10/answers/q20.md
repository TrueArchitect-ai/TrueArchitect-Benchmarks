q20. mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) via an `SSEHub` that maintains client subscriptions and broadcasts JSON-encoded `SSEEvent`s to subscribed browser clients; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (struct `SSEHub`, methods `NewSSEHub()`, `.Subscribe()`, `.Unsubscribe()`, `.Broadcast()`, `.Close()`), wired up via `RegisterSSERoutes()`/`handleSSE()` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`; memo-related handlers that call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`: `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`), `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`), `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`), and `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`) — the latter is itself invoked from `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go`), `SetMemoRelations`-type handler in `memo_relation_service.go`, and the attachment-update handler in `memo_attachment_service.go`, so memo updates, relation changes, and attachment changes also broadcast through it indirectly.
