Before deleting the memo row, `Store.DeleteMemo` (store/memo.go:140-159) deletes:
1. **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation` table) — twice: once where this memo is the source (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}`) and once where it's the related/target memo (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}`) — store/memo.go:142-147.
2. **Attachment records** linked to the memo — it lists all attachments via `ListAttachments(&FindAttachment{MemoID: &delete.ID})` and calls `DeleteAttachment` for each one (which also removes the underlying attachment storage/blob) — store/memo.go:148-157.

Only after these two cleanups does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` to remove the memo row itself (store/memo.go:158).
