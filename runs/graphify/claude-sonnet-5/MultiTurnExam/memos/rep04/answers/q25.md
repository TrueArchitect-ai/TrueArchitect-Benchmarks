q25. Engine: **CEL (Common Expression Language, via `cel-go`)**, implemented in `internal/filter` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`, `ir.go`).

Mechanism: `ListMemos`'s filter strings are parsed by `cel-go` and validated against a memo-specific schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`), normalized into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (`internal/filter/ir.go`), then the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR to emit a dialect-specific SQL `WHERE` fragment plus placeholder args (`?` for SQLite/MySQL, `$n` for Postgres, with dialect-specific handling for JSON access, `LIKE`/`ILIKE`, regex, etc.) that each store driver appends to its query.
