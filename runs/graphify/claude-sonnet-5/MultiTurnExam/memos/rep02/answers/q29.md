**Interceptor:** `web/src/connect.ts` — `authInterceptor` (attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header, retries once on 401 by refreshing).

**Token kinds:** an in-memory/localStorage **access token** (short-lived, sent as a Bearer header, managed in `web/src/auth-state.ts`) and an HTTP-only **refresh token** (sent automatically via cookie, `credentials: "include"`, exchanged through `AuthService.RefreshToken`).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` (e.g. `server/auth/authenticator.go`, `token.go`, `extract.go`).
