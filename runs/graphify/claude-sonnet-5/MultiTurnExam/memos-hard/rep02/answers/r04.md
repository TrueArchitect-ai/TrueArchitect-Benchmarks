**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers don't consume from any external message broker — there's no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka dependency anywhere in this repository (`go.mod`/`go.sum` have no such module, and a repo-wide search for those terms is empty).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook dispatch uses an in-process, in-memory Go channel. `asyncPostQueue` is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four worker goroutines are spawned in the package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) to deliver. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking channel send and drops the payload (logging a warning) if the queue is full — there's no persistence, no broker, and no durable queue.
