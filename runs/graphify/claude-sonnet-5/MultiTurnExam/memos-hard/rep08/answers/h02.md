**Protection mechanism:** `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`), installed as the custom `DialContext` on `safeClient`'s `http.Transport` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). On every dial it resolves the target host via `net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost` and rejects the connection if any resolved IP is reserved/private, per `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44-54` (backed by the `reservedCIDRs`/`reservedNetworks` blocklist covering loopback, RFC-1918 ranges, link-local incl. `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents, `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-36`). Because resolution and checking happen at actual dial time (not just at webhook-registration time via `ValidateURL`), this also defeats DNS-rebinding TOCTOU attacks.
