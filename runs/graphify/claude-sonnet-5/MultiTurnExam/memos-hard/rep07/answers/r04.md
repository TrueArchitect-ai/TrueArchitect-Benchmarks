Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor any external message broker) is used anywhere in this codebase — confirmed via a repo-wide case-insensitive grep for "nats"/"rabbitmq"/"amqp" and a check of `go.mod`, both empty.

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery uses an in-process, buffered Go channel as its queue. It's declared as `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues onto it, and 4 worker goroutines spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) range over the channel, each calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) to perform delivery. This queue is entirely in-memory and process-local — it does not persist across restarts and has no external broker involved.
