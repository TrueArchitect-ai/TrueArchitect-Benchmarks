**Premise correction:** Neither. There is no message broker in this codebase — no NATS or RabbitMQ client, no queue declaration. A repo-wide search for "nats", "rabbitmq", "amqp" turns up nothing, and there's no such dependency in `go.mod`.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process, bounded Go channel as its "queue" — `asyncPostQueue := make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. A fixed pool of 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel's 128-item buffer is full — there's no persistence, no broker, no cross-process delivery; it's entirely in-memory and scoped to a single server process's lifetime.
