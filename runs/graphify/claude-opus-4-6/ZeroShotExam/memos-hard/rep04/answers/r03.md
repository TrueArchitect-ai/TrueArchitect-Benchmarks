The premise of the question is incorrect. There is no "nightly database-cleanup job" configured in this repository. The `internal/scheduler/` package (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `internal/scheduler/job.go`, `internal/scheduler/parser.go`) is a general-purpose cron scheduling library, but it does not define or register any specific jobs itself. The documentation and examples (in `internal/scheduler/README.md`, `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`) include illustrative cleanup jobs (e.g., `"daily-cleanup"` at `"0 2 * * *"` and `"cleanup"` at `"0 2 * * *"`), but these are purely example/documentation code, not an actual scheduled database-cleanup job wired into the application.
