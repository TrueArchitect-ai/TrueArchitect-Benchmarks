Here are the distinct memo visibility enforcement sites on the server:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (single-memo gate)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
2. **`ListMemos` query-level visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
4. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
5. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
6. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
7. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
8. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
9. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
10. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (gRPC attachment endpoint)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
11. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (HTTP file server)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
12. **`GetMemoByShare` share-token access** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go`
13. **SSE event delivery filtering (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
14. **`GetUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
15. **`ListAllUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
16. **RSS feed visibility filtering** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
17. **Sitemap visibility filtering** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
18. **`canViewerAccessMemo` for notification payloads** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go`
19. **`canViewerAccessMemo` in email dispatcher** — `server/notification/email.go`
20. **`canUserAccessMentionContext` for mentions** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go`
21. **Authorizer / ACL config (transport-level auth)** — `server/router/api/v1/authz.go` and `server/router/api/v1/acl_config.go`
22. **SQL-layer `VisibilityList` filter** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` (and MySQL/Postgres equivalents)
23. **SQL-layer `MemoFilter` on relations** — `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go` (and MySQL/Postgres equivalents)
