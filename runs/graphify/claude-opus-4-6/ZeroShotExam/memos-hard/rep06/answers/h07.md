5. `Store.ListUsers` — `store/user.go` (line 136, delegates to the database driver)
6. `DB.ListUsers` — `store/db/sqlite/user.go` (line 88, executes `SELECT … avatar_url … FROM user` — equivalent implementations in `store/db/postgres/user.go` and `store/db/mysql/user.go`)
7. `parseDataURI` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go` (line 737, decodes the base64 data URI from `user.AvatarURL` into MIME type + raw bytes)
