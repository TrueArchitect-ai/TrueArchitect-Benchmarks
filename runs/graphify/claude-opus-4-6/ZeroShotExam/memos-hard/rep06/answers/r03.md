The premise of this question is incorrect. There is no "nightly database-cleanup job" in this repository's cron scheduler. The `internal/scheduler/` package is a **general-purpose cron scheduling library** — it does not register any production jobs itself.

The closest things to a "cleanup" job are **illustrative examples** in documentation and test files:

- `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15–19` shows a `"daily-cleanup"` example with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily).
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97–106` shows a `"cleanup"` example (comment: "Cleanup old data every night at 2 AM") also with schedule `"0 2 * * *"`.

These are not real scheduled jobs — they are documentation/test examples demonstrating how to use the scheduler library. No actual database-cleanup job is registered or configured anywhere in the codebase.
