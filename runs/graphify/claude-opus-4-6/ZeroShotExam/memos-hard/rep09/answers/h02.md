**Protection mechanism** — SSRF protection is a two-layer defense in `internal/webhook/`:

1. **`ValidateURL`** in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (line 62) — called at webhook registration time (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`), it resolves the target hostname and rejects any URL whose IPs fall within reserved CIDR ranges (loopback, RFC-1918 private, link-local/cloud IMDS, IPv6 ULA/link-local), defined in `reservedCIDRs` (validate.go, line 14) and checked by `isReservedIP` (line 44).

2. **`safeDialContext`** in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line 59) — a custom `DialContext` wired into the HTTP transport of `safeClient`. It re-resolves DNS and re-checks IPs **at dial time**, defeating DNS-rebinding attacks where a hostname resolves to a public address during validation but a private one when the request is actually made.
