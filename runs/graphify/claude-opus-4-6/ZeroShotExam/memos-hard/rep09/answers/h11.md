The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/*.go`), which defines 11 persistent entity types: `User`, `UserSetting`, `UserIdentity`, `Memo`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `Attachment`, `IdentityProvider`, `Inbox`, `Reaction`, and `InstanceSetting`.

Counting distinct packages **outside** `store/` (i.e., excluding `store/db/*` and `store/test/`) that reference each `store.<Type>`:

1. **1st: `User`** — referenced from **~7 packages** (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`). Defined in `store/user.go`.

2. **2nd: `Memo`** — referenced from **~6 packages** (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`). Defined in `store/memo.go`.

`Attachment` comes in third at ~4 packages.
