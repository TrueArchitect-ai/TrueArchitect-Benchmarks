**S3 presign runner** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired in at `server/server.go:129` where `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` is called from `Start`, which instantiates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` and launches it at `server/server.go:162–169`.

The runner lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method iterates over all S3-type attachments and regenerates presigned URLs for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (the URLs themselves expire after 5 days, giving a 1-day safety margin). It runs once synchronously on startup (`server/server.go:163`) and then every 12 hours via a ticker in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:166–171`).
