Of the persistent entity types defined in the data layer (`store/` package), the two referenced from the largest number of packages outside that layer are:

1st: **User** — ~7 packages (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`)

2nd: **Memo** — ~7 packages (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)

Both are tied at 7 external packages, with `User` appearing in `server/auth` (where `Memo` does not) and `Memo` appearing in `server/runner/memopayload` (where `User` does not). The next closest is **Attachment** at 5 packages. The entity types are defined in `store/user.go` and `store/memo.go` respectively.
