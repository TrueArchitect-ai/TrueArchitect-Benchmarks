h10.
`GenUUID` — `internal/util/util.go` — approx 28 callers

This small wrapper around `uuid.New().String()` is used across both production code (session tokens, SSO usernames, shortcut/attachment IDs, personal access tokens) and extensively in tests (especially `auth_test.go`), giving it roughly 28 distinct call sites — the highest fan-in of any function declared under `internal/`.
