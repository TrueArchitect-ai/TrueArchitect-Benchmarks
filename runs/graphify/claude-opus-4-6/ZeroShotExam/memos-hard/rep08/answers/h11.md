The persistent entity types defined in the `store/` package (the data layer) are referenced across Go packages outside that layer as follows:

1. **Memo** and **User** are tied at **~7 packages** each (e.g., `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`).

**Top two ranked:**

1st: **Memo**; 2nd: **User** (~7 packages each, effectively tied)

Both are referenced from 7 distinct packages outside `store/`, far ahead of the next entity type (`Attachment` at 5 packages). `Memo` types (`store.Memo`, `store.FindMemo`, `store.UpdateMemo`, `store.Visibility`, etc. in `store/memo.go`) appear in packages like `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`, and others. `User` types (`store.User`, `store.FindUser`, `store.Role`, etc. in `store/user.go`) appear in `server/auth`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/frontend`, and others.
