**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired in at `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`), called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically scans all S3-stored attachments in batches of 100, re-generating presigned URLs for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days, giving a 1-day safety margin). It is created and launched in `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:162-171`): an immediate `RunOnce` pass runs at startup (line 163), then a goroutine starts the 12-hour ticker loop via `Run` (line 169).
