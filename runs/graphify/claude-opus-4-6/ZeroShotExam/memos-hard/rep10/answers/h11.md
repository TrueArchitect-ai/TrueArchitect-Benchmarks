The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/*.go`), which defines 11 persistent entity types. Counting references from packages **outside** that layer:

1. **`User`** — referenced from **~7 packages** outside the store layer (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`)
2. **`Memo`** — referenced from **~6 packages** outside the store layer (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)

The `User` type (defined in `store/user.go`) is the most widely referenced, appearing across authentication, notification, API, file-serving, frontend, and RSS packages. `Memo` (defined in `store/memo.go`) is second, referenced across notification, API, frontend, RSS, and the memo-payload runner. The next closest is `Attachment` at ~4 packages.
