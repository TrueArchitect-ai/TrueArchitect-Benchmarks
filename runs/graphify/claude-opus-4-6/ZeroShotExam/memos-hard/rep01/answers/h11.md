The persistent entity types are defined in the `store/` package (files like `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). Ranking by how many packages outside `store/` reference them:

1. **`User`** — referenced from **~8 packages** (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `proto/gen/store`)
2. **`Memo`** — referenced from **~6 packages** (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)
