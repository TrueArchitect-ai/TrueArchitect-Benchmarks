**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162` inside `startBackgroundRunners` (line 153), which is called from `Server.Start`.

The runner refreshes S3 presigned URLs for attachments before they expire. Presigned URLs are generated with a 5-day lifetime, and the runner skips any URL that still has more than 4 days of validity remaining (`runner.go:88`). At startup it runs once immediately (`server.go:163`), then launches a goroutine with a 12-hour ticker (`runner.go:30`) to keep checking.
