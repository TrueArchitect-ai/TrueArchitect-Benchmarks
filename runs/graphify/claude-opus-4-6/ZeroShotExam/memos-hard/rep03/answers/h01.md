**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired at `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`), which is called from `Server.Start`.

The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It periodically re-signs presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments so they don't expire (presigned URLs last 5 days; the runner re-signs any older than 4 days, giving a 1-day safety margin). The `runnerInterval` constant is set to `time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`.

At startup, `Server.Start` calls `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`), which instantiates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at line 162, runs an immediate `RunOnce` at line 163, then launches the ticker loop in a goroutine at line 169.
