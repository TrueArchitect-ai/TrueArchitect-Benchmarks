Of the 11 persistent entity types defined in the data layer (`store/` package), the two referenced from the most packages outside that layer are:

1. **User** — referenced from **~7 packages** (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`)
2. **Memo** — referenced from **~6 packages** (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)

The `store.User` struct (`store/user.go`) and `store.Memo` struct (`store/memo.go`) are the most widely consumed entity types outside the store layer. The next closest are `Attachment` (~4 packages) and `MemoRelation`/`Inbox`/`MemoShare` (~3 each).
