   Two cleanup steps run inside the delete-user transaction:
   - **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** — `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)` removes all reactions (by any user) on the deleted user's memos.
   - **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** — `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?` removes all reactions the deleted user placed on other people's content.

3. **Test confirmation** at `store/test/user_delete_test.go` (`TestDeleteUserCleansRelatedData`, lines ~97–220): creates reactions in both directions, deletes the user, then asserts only the unrelated peer-to-peer reaction survives.
