The outbound webhook delivery executes in the `Post` function in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (with `PostAsync` enqueuing work to background goroutines that call `Post`). The actual HTTP call goes through a `safeClient` whose transport uses `safeDialContext` (same file), which resolves the target hostname at dial time and checks every resolved IP against a blocklist of reserved/private CIDR ranges defined in `internal/webhook/validate.go`.

**Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; **protection mechanism** — SSRF guard via `isReservedIP` / `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` and `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go`. The protection is two-layered: `ValidateURL` (`validate.go`) rejects private-IP targets at webhook registration time, and `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go`) re-checks at TCP dial time to defeat DNS rebinding.
