The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and regenerates the presigned URL if it was created more than 4 days ago (presigned URLs expire after 5 days, giving a 1-day safety margin).
