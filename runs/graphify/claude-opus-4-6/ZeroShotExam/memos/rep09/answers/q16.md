When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-158`), the following kinds of records are deleted before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` records where the memo is the **source** (`MemoID`) are deleted (`store/memo.go:142`).
2. **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` records where the memo is the **target** (`RelatedMemoID`) are deleted (`store/memo.go:145`).
3. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo are listed and individually deleted (`store/memo.go:149-156`).
