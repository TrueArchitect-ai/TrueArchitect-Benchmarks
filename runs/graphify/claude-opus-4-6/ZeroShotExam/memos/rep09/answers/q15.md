- `user`: **User** — `store/user.go`
- `attachment`: **Attachment** — `store/attachment.go`
- `reaction`: **Reaction** — `store/reaction.go`

All three structs are in the `store` package. The project uses raw SQL (no ORM), so the mapping is established by hand-written queries in per-dialect driver files under `store/db/postgres/`, `store/db/sqlite/`, and `store/db/mysql/` (e.g., `INSERT INTO "user"`, `FROM attachment`, `DELETE FROM reaction`).
