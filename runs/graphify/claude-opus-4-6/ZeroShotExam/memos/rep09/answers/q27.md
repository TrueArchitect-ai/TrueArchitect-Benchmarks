The compiled React frontend is served via Go's **`//go:embed`** directive. The Vite build (`pnpm release` in `web/package.json`) outputs the frontend into `server/router/frontend/dist/`, and in `server/router/frontend/frontend.go` the directive `//go:embed dist/*` embeds those files into the Go binary. At runtime, Echo's `StaticWithConfig` middleware serves them, with a SPA fallback to `index.html`.
