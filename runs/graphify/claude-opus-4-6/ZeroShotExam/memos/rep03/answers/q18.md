The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. **`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`** — SQLite driver
2. **`store/db/mysql/mysql.go`** — MySQL driver
3. **`store/db/postgres/postgres.go`** — PostgreSQL driver

The factory that selects between them at startup:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (defaulting to `"sqlite"`) and calls the corresponding `NewDB` constructor for each implementation.
