When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes the following kinds of records before removing the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the source, i.e. `memo_id` matches) — via `DeleteMemoRelation`
2. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the target, i.e. `related_memo_id` matches) — via `DeleteMemoRelation`
3. **Attachments** linked to the memo — each attachment's storage files (including thumbnails and motion caches) are also removed before the attachment row is deleted

Only after all memo relations and attachments are cleaned up does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row itself.
