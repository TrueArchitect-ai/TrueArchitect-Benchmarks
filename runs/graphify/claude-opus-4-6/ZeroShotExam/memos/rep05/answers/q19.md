MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/REST)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`)
2. **Connect** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)`)

The gRPC-Gateway registration translates the gRPC service definition into RESTful HTTP endpoints (served under `/api/v1/*`), while the Connect registration provides the Connect protocol (served under `/memos.api.v1.*`). Both delegate to the same underlying `APIV1Service` implementation.
