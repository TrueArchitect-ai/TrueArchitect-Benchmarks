The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) to instantiate the appropriate backend.
