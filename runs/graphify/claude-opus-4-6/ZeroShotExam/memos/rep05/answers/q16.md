When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the source (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID}` at line 142) and where the memo is the target (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID}` at line 145)
2. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo are listed and individually deleted (lines 149–157)

After cleaning up those records, the actual memo row is deleted via `s.driver.DeleteMemo` (line 158).
