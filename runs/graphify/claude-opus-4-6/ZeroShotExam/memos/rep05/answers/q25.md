**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, living in `internal/filter/engine.go`.

**Mechanism:** `Engine.Compile()` parses the filter string through the CEL environment into an AST, then `buildCondition()` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) walks the parsed CEL expression tree to produce a dialect-agnostic IR (intermediate `Condition` tree), and `Render()` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR with a `renderer` struct that switches on `DialectName` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments (e.g. `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`/`->>`, `ILIKE` vs `LIKE`, `$N` vs `?` placeholders). Each DB driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` passing its dialect constant — `filter.DialectSQLite` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:61`), `filter.DialectPostgres` (`store/db/postgres/memo.go:58`), or `filter.DialectMySQL` (`store/db/mysql/memo.go:69`).
