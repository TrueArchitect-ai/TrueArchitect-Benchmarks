When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes these kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the source (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}`, line 142)
2. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the related/target (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}`, line 145)
3. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo (listed via `FindAttachment{MemoID: &delete.ID}`, then each deleted individually, lines 149–157)
