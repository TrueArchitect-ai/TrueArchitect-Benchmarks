**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE), served at `GET /api/v1/sse` with `Content-Type: text/event-stream`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct manages connected `SSEClient`s and provides `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, and `Broadcast` methods with per-client visibility filtering.

**Memo handlers that broadcast through it:**

- **`CreateMemo`** — broadcasts `memo.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`)
- **`UpdateMemo`** (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`) — broadcasts `memo.updated` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`, also triggered from `SetMemoAttachments` and `SetMemoRelations`)
- **`DeleteMemo`** — broadcasts `memo.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`)
- **`CreateMemoComment`** — broadcasts `memo.comment.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`)
- **`UpsertMemoReaction`** — broadcasts `reaction.upserted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`)
- **`DeleteMemoReaction`** — broadcasts `reaction.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`)
