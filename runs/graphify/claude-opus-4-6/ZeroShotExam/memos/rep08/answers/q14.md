The `ShortcutService` is defined in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto` and has **5 RPCs**:

1. **ListShortcuts**
2. **GetShortcut**
3. **CreateShortcut**
4. **UpdateShortcut**
5. **DeleteShortcut**

They are all implemented in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** as methods on `APIV1Service` (lines 49, 95, 135, 211, 284 respectively). A thin Connect-protocol adapter in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` delegates each RPC to the same `APIV1Service` methods.
