**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`; **path:** `internal/filter/` (entry point `internal/filter/engine.go`).

**Mechanism:** The engine compiles a CEL expression into a checked AST, converts it into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR) tree of conditions (`internal/filter/parser.go` → `internal/filter/ir.go`), then a renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits a SQL fragment with placeholder arguments tailored to the target dialect (`sqlite`, `mysql`, or `postgres`), handling differences like `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`/`->>`, `?` vs `$n` placeholders, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, and boolean coercion per dialect.
