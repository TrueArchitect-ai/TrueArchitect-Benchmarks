The compiled React frontend is served via Go's **`embed.FS`** (`//go:embed dist/*`). The Vite build outputs into `server/router/frontend/dist/`, and at compile time Go embeds those files into the binary. At runtime, they are served through Echo's static middleware and an SPA fallback handler.

**Mechanism:** `go:embed` (embedding the frontend dist into the Go binary, served via Echo `middleware.StaticWithConfig`); **File:** `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
