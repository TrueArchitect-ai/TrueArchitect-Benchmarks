The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite (pure-Go via `modernc.org/sqlite`)
2. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL (via `lib/pq`)
3. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL (via `go-sql-driver/mysql`)

The factory that selects between them at startup:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) and calls the corresponding `NewDB` constructor.
