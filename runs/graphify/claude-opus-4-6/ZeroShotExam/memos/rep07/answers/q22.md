When a memo is created, the `CreateMemo` handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` calls `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`, which delegates to `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`. That method fetches the creator's webhooks and calls `webhook.PostAsync` to enqueue each one.

**`DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`; delivery package **`internal/webhook`** (`webhook.go`); signing mechanism **HMAC-SHA256 following the Standard Webhooks spec** (message = `msgID.timestamp.body`, signature header `webhook-signature: v1,<base64-HMAC-SHA256>`, with keys optionally prefixed `whsec_` and base64-decoded).
