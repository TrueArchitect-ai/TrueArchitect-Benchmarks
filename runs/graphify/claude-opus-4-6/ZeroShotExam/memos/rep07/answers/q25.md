**Engine:** CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go/cel`); **Path:** `internal/filter/engine.go`.

**Mechanism:** The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string using CEL into an AST, converts it into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go` via `buildCondition`), and then `Program.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that tree and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) for each condition node—each store driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`) calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect constant to get the appropriate SQL.
