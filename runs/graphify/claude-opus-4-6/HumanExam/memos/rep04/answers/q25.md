CEL (Common Expression Language) via `google/cel-go`. The engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go` — it compiles CEL expressions into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (IR), then `internal/filter/render.go` renders that tree into dialect-specific SQL (SQLite/PostgreSQL/MySQL). Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` (`internal/filter/helpers.go:9`) with the appropriate `DialectName` to produce WHERE clause fragments with parameterized args.
