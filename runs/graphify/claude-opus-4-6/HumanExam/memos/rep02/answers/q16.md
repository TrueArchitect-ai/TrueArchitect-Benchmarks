Before deleting the memo row itself, the store layer deletes:
1. Memo relations (where the memo is the source — `memo_id`)
2. Memo relations (where the memo is the target — `related_memo_id`)
3. Attachments linked to the memo (including their storage files)

Additionally, the API handler (`memo_service.go:567-577`) first deletes all comment memos (via recursive `Store.DeleteMemo` calls). And `memo_share` rows are cascade-deleted via FK constraint.
