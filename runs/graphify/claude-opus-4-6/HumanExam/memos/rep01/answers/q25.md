CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`. The engine lives at `internal/filter/` (main entry: `engine.go`). CEL expressions are compiled to an AST, converted to a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`parser.go`/`ir.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL (`render.go`) — each driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect name (SQLite/MySQL/Postgres) to get the appropriate SQL fragment and args.
