Before the memo row itself, the store deletes:
1. Memo relations (where memo is source, `memo_relation.memo_id`)
2. Memo relations (where memo is target, `memo_relation.related_memo_id`)
3. Attachments linked to the memo (including physical storage files)

Additionally, `memo_share` rows are cascade-deleted by the DB schema (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), and at the API handler level, comment memos are recursively deleted first.
