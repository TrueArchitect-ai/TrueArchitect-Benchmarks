CEL (Common Expression Language) via `google/cel-go`; engine at `internal/filter/` (engine.go, parser.go, render.go); filters are compiled from CEL to a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree, then rendered to dialect-specific SQL (with placeholders) by `render.go` using `switch dialect` branches. Each store driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect name (DialectSQLite/DialectPostgres/DialectMySQL) to append WHERE clauses.
