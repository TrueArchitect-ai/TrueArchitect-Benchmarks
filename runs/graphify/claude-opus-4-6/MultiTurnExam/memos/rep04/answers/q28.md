The server starts **one** background runner:

**s3presign Runner** — driven by a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), started in `server/server.go:162-171`. It refreshes pre-signed S3 URLs for attachments.

**Unused infrastructure:** The `memopayload` runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) has a `RunOnce` method that rebuilds memo payloads, but it is **not started as a background worker** by the server. Its `RebuildMemoPayload` function is only called inline during `CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` — the runner's batch `RunOnce` method is never invoked by the server startup path.
