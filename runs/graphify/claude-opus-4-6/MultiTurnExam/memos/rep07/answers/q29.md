Interceptor: `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (line 156) attaches a `Bearer` access token to every request, handles 401 retries by refreshing the token, and redirects on auth failure.

Token kinds: **access tokens** (short-lived JWTs sent as `Authorization: Bearer` header), **refresh tokens** (sent via HTTP cookie with `credentials: "include"`), and **personal access tokens (PATs)** (hashed and validated separately).
