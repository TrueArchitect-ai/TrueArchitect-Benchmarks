Neither — the premise is incorrect. There is no message broker (NATS, RabbitMQ, or otherwise). Webhook delivery uses a plain in-memory Go buffered channel.

The "queue" is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go` as `asyncPostQueue`, a `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with a capacity of 128. Four worker goroutines are spawned in the package's `init()` function (same file) to drain this channel, each calling the synchronous `Post` function. `PostAsync` simply sends the payload onto this channel. There is no external broker, no durable queue, and no cross-process messaging — everything runs in-process.
