**`s3presign.Runner`** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`, where `s3presign.NewRunner` is instantiated and kicked off inside `startBackgroundRunners` (called from `Server.Start()` at `server/server.go:129`).

The runner refreshes pre-signed S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It runs once immediately on startup (`server/server.go:163`), then repeats on a 12-hour ticker. Each cycle it scans attachments with S3 storage type and re-signs any whose pre-signed URL is older than 4 days (URLs are valid for 5 days), preventing stale download links.
