**Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line ~120), which performs the synchronous HTTP POST; asynchronous dispatch goes through `PostAsync` (same file, line ~184), which enqueues onto a buffered channel drained by worker goroutines.

**Protection mechanism** — a dual-layer SSRF guard, both in `internal/webhook/`:

1. **Registration-time validation** — `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) resolves the hostname and checks every IP against `isReservedIP` (`validate.go:44`), which blocks RFC-1918, loopback, link-local, and IPv6 private CIDRs (`reservedCIDRs`, `validate.go:14`).
2. **Dial-time re-check** — `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) is wired as the `DialContext` on the HTTP transport (`safeClient`, `webhook.go:32`). It re-resolves the hostname at connection time and rejects any IP in a reserved range, defeating DNS rebinding attacks that could change resolution between registration and delivery.

**Summary format:**  
`Post` / `PostAsync` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; `isReservedIP` + `safeDialContext` (dual-layer SSRF protection) — `internal/webhook/validate.go` & `internal/webhook/webhook.go`
