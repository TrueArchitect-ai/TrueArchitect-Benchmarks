The major distinct enforcement sites for memo visibility:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39` (central gate for `GetMemo`, `ListMemoComments`)
2. **`ListMemos` query-level filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-227` (restricts visibility list based on auth state)
3. **`CreateMemoComment` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41` (blocks commenting on others' private memos)
4. **`ListMemoComments` filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:157-161` (filters visible comments by visibility)
5. **`ListMemoReactions` / `UpsertMemoReaction`** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:29-41, 82-85` (gates reaction access on private memos)
6. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (API)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422-455` (attachment access tied to memo visibility)
7. **`ListMemoAttachments`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:213-225`
8. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (fileserver)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650-703` (HTTP-level file serving with share-token fallback)
9. **RSS feeds** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90, 141` (only PUBLIC memos in feeds)
10. **SSE hub `canReceive`** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` (filters event delivery by visibility)
11. **`canViewerAccessMemo` (notifications)** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302-316` (gates notification payload construction)
12. **`canViewerAccessMemo` (email)** — `server/notification/email.go:306-320` (gates email notification delivery)
13. **`canUserAccessMentionContext`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:56-72` (suppresses mention inbox for private memos)
14. **Memo relation listing filters** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:108-112` and `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go:183-187`
15. **Stats query filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:83-91, 217-221`
16. **SQL-level `VisibilityList` WHERE clause** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:96`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:89`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:104` (bottom-layer DB enforcement)
