**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into startup at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` periodically re-signs S3 presigned URLs for attachments so they don't expire. Presigned URLs are valid for 5 days (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), and the runner skips any attachment whose URL was signed less than 4 days ago (`runner.go:88`), giving roughly a 1-day safety margin.

It is wired into the server in `server/server.go` inside `startBackgroundRunners` (line 153): the runner is instantiated at line 162 (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), run once immediately at line 163, then launched in a goroutine with its 12-hour ticker loop at line 167. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is called from `Server.Start` at line 129.
