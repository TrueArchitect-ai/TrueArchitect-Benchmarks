The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`), declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. The channel is initialized at line 40 (`var asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`), and four goroutines are started in `init()` (lines 42-55) to drain it. `PostAsync` (line 183) sends payloads into this channel; each worker goroutine calls `Post` (line 120) to execute the outbound HTTP request. Everything is in-process with no external messaging infrastructure.
