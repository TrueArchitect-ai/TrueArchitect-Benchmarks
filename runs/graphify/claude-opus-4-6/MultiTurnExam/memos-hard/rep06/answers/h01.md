**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (via `time.Ticker`) — wired in `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`)

The runner lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It regenerates presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire. Presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; the runner skips any attachment whose URL still has more than 4 days of validity, giving a 1-day safety margin.

At startup, `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) creates the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner`), calls `RunOnce` immediately (line 163), then launches `Run` in a goroutine (line 169) where it ticks every 12 hours.
