**Delivery function** — `Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`. It marshals the payload to JSON, attaches optional HMAC-SHA256 signatures (Standard Webhooks headers), and sends the request via `safeClient.Do(req)`. Async callers go through `PostAsync` (line 184), which enqueues into a buffered channel drained by 4 goroutines (line 42).

**Protection mechanism** — two-layer SSRF guard in `internal/webhook/`:

1. **Registration-time validation** — `ValidateURL` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`. When a webhook is created/updated, the URL's hostname is DNS-resolved and every resulting IP is checked against a list of reserved/private CIDR ranges (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS, IPv6 equivalents) defined at `validate.go:14-23` via `isReservedIP` (`validate.go:44`).

2. **Dial-time enforcement (anti-DNS-rebinding)** — `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`. The `safeClient` HTTP client (`webhook.go:32`) uses a custom `DialContext` that re-resolves the hostname and rejects reserved IPs at actual connection time, defeating TOCTOU/DNS-rebinding attacks that could bypass the registration check.
