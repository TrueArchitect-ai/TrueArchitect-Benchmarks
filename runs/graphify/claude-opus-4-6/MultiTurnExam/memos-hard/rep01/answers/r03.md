The question's premise is incorrect. There is no nightly database-cleanup cron job or cron scheduler in this codebase. The only scheduled background tasks are the S3 presign runner (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the cache cleanup timer (every 5 minutes, `store/cache/cache.go`), neither of which performs database cleanup. Cleanup of user-related data happens only on-demand during user deletion (`store/db/*/user_delete.go`), not on a schedule.
