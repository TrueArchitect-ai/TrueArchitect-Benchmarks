**Delivery function** — `Post()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line ~120) performs the synchronous HTTP POST; production callers go through `PostAsync()` (line ~184), which enqueues onto a buffered channel drained by 4 worker goroutines.

**Protection mechanism** — SSRF protection via reserved-IP filtering in `internal/webhook/validate.go`. Two layers:

1. **Registration-time**: `ValidateURL()` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) resolves the webhook URL's hostname and rejects any IP matching `reservedCIDRs` (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud-IMDS `169.254.0.0/16`, IPv6 ULA/link-local — defined at lines 14–23).
2. **Delivery-time**: `safeDialContext()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) re-resolves DNS and checks IPs against the same reserved ranges *at connection time*, defeating DNS-rebinding attacks that could pass the registration check but resolve differently later.

**delivery function** — `Post()` / `PostAsync()` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; **protection mechanism** — reserved-CIDR dial-time check (`safeDialContext`) + registration-time check (`ValidateURL`) — `internal/webhook/validate.go`
