The question's premise is incorrect. The webhook delivery workers do not consume from any message broker — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The mechanism is a plain Go buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`), declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. At init time (line ~42–55), 4 goroutine workers are started that drain this channel and call `Post()` for each payload. `PostAsync()` (line ~184) simply sends the payload onto this in-process channel. There is no external message broker dependency anywhere in the codebase.
