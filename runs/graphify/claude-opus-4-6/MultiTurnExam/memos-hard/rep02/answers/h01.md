**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired in at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically regenerates expiring S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments. The runner interval is 12 hours (`runner.go:27`, `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). It skips any attachment whose URL was presigned less than 4 days ago, and S3 presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day lifetime (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), so there's always a ~1-day buffer before a link would go stale.

- **Line 163**: `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` — immediate first pass at startup
