r03.

There is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. While an internal scheduler package exists at `internal/scheduler/`, it is not used to schedule any database cleanup task. The background runners that do exist (`s3presign` and `memopayload` under `server/runner/`) use simple `time.Ticker` loops, not cron schedules, and neither performs database cleanup. No scheduled database maintenance or cleanup job of any kind is configured in the server.
