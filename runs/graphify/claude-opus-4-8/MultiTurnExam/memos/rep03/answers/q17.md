**web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient**

`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` contains the memo CRUD queries/mutations (e.g. `useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo`, `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo`), and they call the generated ConnectRPC client `memoServiceClient` (created in `web/src/connect.ts` via `createClient(MemoService, transport)`).
