**Engine:** Google CEL (`cel-go`), wrapped by the project's own filter engine in `internal/filter/` (`internal/filter/engine.go`, with `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** The CEL filter string is compiled by `filter.Engine` (built on `cel-go`) into a dialect-agnostic condition tree/IR, and each dialect driver then calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, <dialect>, &where, &args)` — passing `filter.DialectSQLite`, `filter.DialectPostgres`, or `filter.DialectMySQL` — so `internal/filter/render.go` renders that IR into dialect-specific SQL WHERE clauses and bind args (see `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`).
