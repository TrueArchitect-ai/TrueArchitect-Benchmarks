- **Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (defined at `web/src/connect.ts:156`, registered in the transport at line 188). It sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, does proactive/reactive refresh, and dedups refreshes via a token-refresh manager.

- **Backend validation package:** `server/auth` — the `Authenticator` (`server/auth/authenticator.go`) validates access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`), refresh tokens (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), and PATs (`AuthenticateByPAT`), with token parsing in `server/auth/token.go`.
