- **Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped in the project's own filter engine at **`internal/filter/`** (`engine.go` builds the `cel.Env`; `parser.go`/`ir.go` produce an intermediate representation; `render.go` emits SQL).
- **Mechanism (one sentence):** Each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.DialectSQLite, ...)`, which compiles the CEL filter string to an IR and then the `renderer` in `internal/filter/render.go` walks it emitting dialect-specific SQL/placeholders selected by the `DialectName` (`DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`).
