**Delivery function — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (with the reserved-range definitions in `internal/webhook/validate.go`)**

Details:

- **Where outbound delivery executes**: `Post(requestPayload *WebhookRequestPayload)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`. It marshals the payload, builds the `POST` request, optionally HMAC-signs it (Standard Webhooks headers), and performs the actual network send via `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`. Webhooks are fired asynchronously through `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto `asyncPostQueue`; four worker goroutines (`webhook.go:42-55`) drain the queue and call `Post`.

- **What protects it from internal/private addresses (SSRF)**: The delivery goes through a dedicated `safeClient` whose transport uses a custom `DialContext = safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`) resolves the target host at **dial time** and refuses to connect if any resolved IP falls in a reserved/private range (`isReservedIP`), which also defeats DNS-rebinding since the check happens on the address actually being dialed. The reserved ranges (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents) and the `isReservedIP` helper live in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`. There's also an up-front `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) that rejects non-http(s) schemes and reserved IPs when a webhook is created/saved, but the delivery-time guarantee is the dialer. Both can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`validate.go:41`) for self-hosted setups targeting local services.
