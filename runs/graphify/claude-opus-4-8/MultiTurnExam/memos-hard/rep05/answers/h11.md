**1st: `store.User` (~5–6 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~4 packages).**

Method: I listed the persistent entity structs in the data layer (`store/*.go`: `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `IdentityProvider`, `MemoRelation`, `UserSetting`, `MemoShare`, `InstanceSetting`, …) and counted the distinct packages *outside* the data layer (excluding `store/` and its `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}` driver subpackages, and test files) that reference each via `store.<Type>`.

- **`store.User`** — referenced from these non-data-layer packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss` (≈5, or 6 counting the `server/router/api/v1/test` helper package). Highest fan-out across packages.
- **`store.Memo`** — referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload` (≈4).

The next contender, `store.Attachment`, appears in ~3 non-data-layer packages, below both. The ranking is stable whether or not you count the DB driver packages (raw distinct-package totals were User=9, Memo=7, Attachment=6).
