**Protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, installed as the `DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` transport (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`).** At dial time it resolves the target hostname and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:70-73`). Because the check happens at connection time on the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding (per the comment at lines 30-31).

Supporting note: webhook URLs are additionally validated when created/updated via `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:65-79`, enforcing http/https scheme and rejecting resolution to reserved/private IPs, gated by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag at `validate.go:38`), but the actual outbound-delivery-time SSRF guard is `safeDialContext`.
