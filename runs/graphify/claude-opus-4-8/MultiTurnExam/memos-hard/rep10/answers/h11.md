**1st: `store.User` (~5–6 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~4 packages).**

Method: the persistent entity types live in `store/` (e.g. `store/user.go:29` `User`, `store/memo.go:35` `Memo`, `store/attachment.go:16` `Attachment`, plus `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `IdentityProvider`, `MemoRelation`, `UserSetting`, `InstanceSetting`). I counted distinct non-test packages *outside the data layer* (excluding `store/` and its driver subpackages `store/db/*`) that reference each `store.<Type>`.

- **`store.User`** — referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss` (≈5 non-test external packages; 6 including `server/router/api/v1/test`).
- **`store.Memo`** — referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload` (≈4 external packages).

The next tier (`Attachment`, `Inbox`, etc.) sits lower. Whether or not the SQL driver packages (`store/db/mysql|postgres|sqlite`) are counted, the ranking is unchanged — `User` first (9 packages including drivers), `Memo` second (7 including drivers).
