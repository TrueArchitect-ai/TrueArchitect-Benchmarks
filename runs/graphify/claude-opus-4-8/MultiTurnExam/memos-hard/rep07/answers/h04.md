**No.** Outside the driver-selection factory, the MySQL driver is never constructed directly.

Evidence:
- The MySQL driver's only exported constructor is `mysql.NewDB` (`store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20`), returning a `store.Driver`.
- A repo-wide search for the package's construction sites (`mysql.NewDB`, struct literals like `mysql.DB{` / `&mysql.DB`) finds exactly one hit outside the `store/db/mysql/` directory: `store/db/db.go:22`, inside `NewDBDriver`'s `switch profile.Driver` — the driver-selection factory itself.
