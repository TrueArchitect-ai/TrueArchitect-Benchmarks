This runner walks all S3 attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, writing the fresh URL back via `UpdateAttachment`. That keeps the stored `Reference` (the externally-served link) from going stale. See `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`, with the 5-day/4-day window documented at lines 86-88.

- **Wiring into startup:** `server/server.go:162` constructs it (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), line 163 does an immediate `RunOnce`, and line 169 launches the continuous `Run(s3Context)` in a background goroutine. `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
