**Mechanism:** Removed by **explicit Go code** — reactions are deleted programmatically inside the `DeleteUser` transaction, not by a database cascade rule. In fact they're removed on two axes: reactions the user *made* (by `creator_id`) and reactions *on the deleted user's memos* (by `content_id`).

**Evidence locations (checked all three DB drivers):**

- MySQL `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go`:

  - `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` → `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)` for the user's memos (`store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:417-425`).
- Postgres `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:102`, `:383`, `:400-401` — same two deletes.
