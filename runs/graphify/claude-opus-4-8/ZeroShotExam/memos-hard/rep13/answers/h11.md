**1st: `store.User` — ~7 external packages; 2nd: `store.Memo` — ~6 external packages.**

The data layer is the `store` package, whose persistent entity structs are defined in `store/*.go` (e.g. `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, `store/inbox.go`, etc.). Counting distinct packages *outside* the `store/` tree that reference each type by its qualified name (`store.<Type>`), the two most widely referenced entities are:

1. **`User`** (`store/user.go`) — referenced from **7** packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
2. **`Memo`** (`store/memo.go`) — referenced from **6** packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

`Attachment` (`store/attachment.go`) is a distant third at ~4 packages. The ranking is stable whether or not `store`'s own DB drivers (`store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}`) and test helpers are counted — `User` leads `Memo` either way (11 vs 10 if those are included).
