Notes:
- Because the check runs at dial time on the resolved IPs, it also defeats DNS-rebinding, not just literal internal IPs.
- There's a second, earlier check at webhook-registration time — `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62` — but the guard on the actual delivery path is `safeDialContext`.
- Both checks can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) for self-hosted setups targeting local services.
