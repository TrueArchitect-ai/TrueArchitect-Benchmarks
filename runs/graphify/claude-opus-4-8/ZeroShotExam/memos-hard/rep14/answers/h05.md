h05.

**Mechanism: explicit Go code.** When a user is deleted, their emoji reactions are removed by hand-written `DELETE FROM reaction ...` statements inside the delete-user transaction — not by a database cascade, and they are not left behind. In fact reactions are cleaned up in *two* distinct ways:

1. **Reactions the deleted user made** (`creator_id` match) — `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` runs `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`.
2. **Reactions attached to the deleted user's memos** (`content_id` match, e.g. `memos/<uid>`) — `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` runs `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`, so reactions *others* left on this user's content also go.

Both are invoked explicitly in `deleteUserTargetsTx`, all within a single transaction (`BeginTx`/`Commit`).

**Evidence locations** (identical logic across all three DB drivers):
- `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go` — `deleteUserTargetsTx` calls at lines 96 (`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`) and 102 (`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`); the functions themselves at lines 380–388 and 400–403.
- `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go` — same calls at lines 96 and 102; functions at 380–388 and 400–403.
