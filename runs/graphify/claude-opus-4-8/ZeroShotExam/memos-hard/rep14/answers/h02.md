delivery function — `Post()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (the outbound request actually goes out at `safeClient.Do(req)`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`; `PostAsync` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` just enqueues onto the async worker pool that ultimately calls `Post`)

protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (defined in `internal/webhook/validate.go`), which also defeats DNS-rebinding since the check happens on the resolved IP right before connecting.
