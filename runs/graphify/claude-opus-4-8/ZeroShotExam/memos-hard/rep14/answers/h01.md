Details:

- **Component**: `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (`server/server.go:163` constructs it via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`). Memos stores S3 attachments by their **presigned GET URL** (`Reference`), which expires. The runner's `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) lists S3-backed attachments in batches and re-presigns any URL whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry — see `runner.go:88`), writing the fresh URL back via `UpdateAttachment`. That's what keeps the links from going stale.

- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). `Run` uses a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` loop (`runner.go:29-41`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at boot.

- **Wiring into server startup**: `Server.Start()` calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` at **`server/server.go:129`**. `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) then creates the runner, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` once immediately (`server/server.go:164`), and launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:167-172`).
