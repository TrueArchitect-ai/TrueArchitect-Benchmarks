**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers consume from *neither* NATS nor RabbitMQ. This repository (the Memos project, Go) has no external message broker at all — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, Kafka, Redis, SQS, or Pub/Sub anywhere in the source. (Apparent broker mentions exist only in `go.sum`, generated proto/locale files, and `graphify-out/`.)

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery runs off an **in-process, in-memory buffered Go channel** with a local goroutine worker pool — not a broker at all.

- **Queue declared:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, a buffered channel of capacity 128 held in process memory.
- **Consumer workers:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` — `init()` spawns a fixed pool of 4 goroutines, each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` (line 45) and calling `Post(payload)` to deliver the webhook over HTTP.
- **Producer/enqueue side:** `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) does a non-blocking send onto the channel, dropping the payload with a warning if the buffer is full. It's invoked from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71` on memo activities.

Consequence worth noting: because the "queue" is purely in-memory, queued webhooks are lost on process restart or when the 128-slot buffer overflows — there is no durable brokered delivery.
