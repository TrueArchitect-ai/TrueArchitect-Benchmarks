I now have a clear picture of the distinct enforcement sites for who may **view** a memo.

h15.
Single-memo read access check (`checkMemoReadAccess`) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go
List-memos visibility filtering (`ListMemos` builds the `visibility in [...]`/`VisibilityList` filter) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go
SSE event delivery filtering (`SSEClient.canReceive`) — server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go
Attachment/file serving access check (memo-linked attachment access) — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go
RSS feed generation (restricts to `store.Public` only) — server/router/rss/rss.go

Additional visibility gates guarding memo-derived read surfaces (comments, reactions, relations) live in:
- Comment listing/creation — server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go
- Reaction access — server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go
- Memo relations listing — server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go

The three primary "who may view a memo" enforcement sites are the first three (`checkMemoReadAccess`, `ListMemos` filter, and the SSE `canReceive` delivery filter); the fileserver and RSS surfaces enforce the same visibility rules for their respective content channels.
