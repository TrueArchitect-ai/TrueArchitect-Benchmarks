**S3 presign runner** — **every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup)** — **wired in `server/server.go:162-169`** (via `startBackgroundRunners`, itself called from `server.go:129`).

Details, with citations:

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (`runner.go:47`) walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates a fresh presigned GET URL for any whose presigned link is nearing expiry, storing it back via `UpdateAttachment`. This is what keeps links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from going stale.

- **Schedule:** The refresh interval is `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). Each attachment is only re-presigned if its `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of the 5-day expiration window (`runner.go:85-91`), so the 12-hour tick just checks; it re-signs roughly once per attachment near day 4–5.

- **Wiring into startup:** `startBackgroundRunners` constructs the runner and calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately, then launches `Run` in a background goroutine — `server/server.go:162-169`. That function is invoked during `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
