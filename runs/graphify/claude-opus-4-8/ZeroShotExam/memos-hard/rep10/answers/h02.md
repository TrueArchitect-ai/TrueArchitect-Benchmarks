**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, installed as the `DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` transport (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32`). It re-resolves the target host at *dial time* and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`), whose CIDR list (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents) is defined at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14`. Because the check runs at the moment of connection, it also defeats DNS-rebinding/TOCTOU attacks that would bypass the earlier config-time `ValidateURL` check (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`). The guard can be disabled for trusted self-hosted setups via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`).
