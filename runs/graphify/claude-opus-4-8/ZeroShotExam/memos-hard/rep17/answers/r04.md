**Premise correction:** Neither. The webhook delivery workers do not consume from a message broker at all — there is no NATS, RabbitMQ, or AMQP dependency anywhere in the codebase (a repo-wide grep for `nats`/`rabbitmq`/`amqp`/`streadway` in `.go` files returns nothing, and neither appears in `go.mod`).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process using a buffered Go channel as the "queue," drained by a fixed pool of goroutine workers.

- The "queue" is a package-level buffered channel declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:
  ```go
  asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
  ```
- The workers are 4 goroutines started in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`, each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)`.
- `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-190`) enqueues onto the channel; `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) does the actual delivery as an HTTP `POST` via a shared `http.Client` (`safeClient`, declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32`).

So the "message broker" is just an in-memory `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with capacity 128, and delivery is a direct signed HTTP POST to the subscriber's URL — no external broker is involved.
