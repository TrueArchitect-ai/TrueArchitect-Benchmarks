**S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign`) — every 12 hours (plus once at startup) — `server/server.go:163`**

- **Component:** The `s3presign.Runner` re-signs presigned URLs for attachments whose content lives in external S3 storage. In `CheckAndPresign` it batches through S3-backed attachments and regenerates the presigned URL for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, stamping a fresh `LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`, refresh check at `runner.go:88`, timestamp update at `runner.go:115`). This is what keeps the externally-stored attachment links from expiring/going stale.
- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` — a `time.NewTicker` fires `RunOnce` every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:25` and `runner.go:27-40`), after an initial immediate `RunOnce` at boot.
- **Wiring into startup:** Created and kicked off in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` then `RunOnce(ctx)` and the continuous `Run(s3Context)` goroutine at `server/server.go:163-171`. `startBackgroundRunners` is itself called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
