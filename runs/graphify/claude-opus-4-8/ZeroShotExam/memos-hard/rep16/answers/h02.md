**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into `safeClient`'s `Transport.DialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`. At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`, backed by the `reservedCIDRs` list (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents) at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14`. Because the check happens on the resolved IP at connection time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. A secondary up-front check, `ValidateURL` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`, blocks reserved IPs when the webhook URL is first configured, but the authoritative guard on the actual delivery is `safeDialContext`. Both can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) for self-hosted local-network use.
