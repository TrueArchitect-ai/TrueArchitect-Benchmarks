**s3presign Runner (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup) — wired in at `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, itself invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

Details:

- **What it does:** The runner re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs so the externally-stored blob links don't expire. It batches through all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments (batch size 100), and for each S3 object regenerates a presigned GET URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, then updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`. Presigned URLs are set to expire in 5 days, and it refreshes any whose last presign is older than 4 days (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-132`, esp. the 12-hour interval at line 27 and the 4-day/5-day logic at lines 85-91).

- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` drives a `time.Ticker` in `Run()` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`). Additionally it runs once synchronously at boot via `RunOnce` (`server/server.go:163`).

- **Wiring:** Constructed and started in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`, immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at `:163`, and the continuous goroutine `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at `:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
