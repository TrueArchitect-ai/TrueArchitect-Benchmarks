The premise is mistaken on two counts. This repository (the **memos** project) has **no cron scheduler at all**, and it has **no nightly database-cleanup job**.

What actually exists is a small set of background runners started in `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`), driven by Go `time.Ticker` loops rather than any cron library (no `robfig/cron`, `gocron`, or cron expressions appear anywhere in the Go sources):

- **S3 presign runner** — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-31`: scheduled on a fixed interval of `time.Hour * 12` (every 12 hours), not nightly, and it re-presigns S3 object URLs rather than cleaning up the database.
- **Memo payload runner** — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`.

Neither is a nightly job, neither runs at a wall-clock "time" (they fire on a rolling interval from process start), and neither performs database cleanup. So there is no scheduled time to report, because the job the question presupposes does not exist.
