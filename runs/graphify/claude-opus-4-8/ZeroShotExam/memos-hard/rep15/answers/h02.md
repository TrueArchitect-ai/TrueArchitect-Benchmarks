**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext` (lines 32–37). At dial time it resolves the target hostname and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` → `reservedCIDRs` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44` (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents). Because the check happens on the post-resolution IP at connect time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding.

Notes:
- There's a second, earlier layer: `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62` validates the scheme and resolved IP when a webhook is created/updated — but the enforcement that actually protects the outbound request is `safeDialContext`.
- The SSRF guard can be globally disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`), intended for self-hosted deployments targeting local services.
