Details:

- **Component.** `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (`runner.go:47`) lists attachments backed by external S3 storage and re-signs each object's URL (`PresignGetObject`, `runner.go:108`), writing the fresh URL and a new `LastPresignedTime` back to the attachment payload (`runner.go:115-118`).
