**delivery function** — `Post()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (the actual outbound send is `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`; async dispatch is queued through `PostAsync` → the worker goroutines in `init()` which call `Post`).

**protection mechanism** — dial-time SSRF guard: `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59` (wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `Transport.DialContext`, lines 32–37) resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` → `reservedCIDRs`/`reservedNetworks` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines 14–54). Because the check runs at connection dial time on the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. There is a second, earlier check at URL-registration time — `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62` — but the enforcement that protects the actual delivery is the `safeDialContext`/`isReservedIP` guard. (Both can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag for self-hosted local-network use, `internal/webhook/validate.go:41`.)

Note: the `isInternalIP()`/`validateURL()`/`resolveAllowedIPs()` symbols in `internal/httpgetter/html_meta.go` that the graph also surfaced are a *separate* SSRF guard for link-preview fetching, not for webhook delivery.
