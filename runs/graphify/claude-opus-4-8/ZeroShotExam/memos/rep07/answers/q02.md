The project ships migrations for **MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**.

This is evident from the migration directories under `store/migration/`:
- `store/migration/mysql`
- `store/migration/postgres`
- `store/migration/sqlite`

These are also mirrored by the dialect-specific driver directories in `store/db/` (`store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`).
