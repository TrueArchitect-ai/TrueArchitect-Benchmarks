MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

The project ships migrations under `store/migration/`, which contains three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g., `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/sqlite/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`).
