MemoService is served over two protocols: gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/JSON REST) registered in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, and Connect RPC registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (with handler delegation in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`).
