q08. Driver — store/driver.go

The `Driver` interface defined in `store/driver.go` is what all database backends (SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL) implement. It contains 47 methods covering lifecycle management (GetDB, Close, IsInitialized, GetDatabaseSize) and CRUD operations for all data models (Attachment, Memo, MemoRelation, User, IdentityProvider, Inbox, Reaction, MemoShare, UserIdentity, and related settings).
