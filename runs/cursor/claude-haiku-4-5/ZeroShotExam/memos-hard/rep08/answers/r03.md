Perfect! Based on my comprehensive search of the repository, I have found the answer. The nightly database-cleanup job (or just "cleanup" job) is scheduled in the cron scheduler.

r03.

The nightly cleanup job is scheduled to run at **2 AM** (02:00) daily. In cron format, this is represented as `"0 2 * * *"`, where the `2` represents the hour (2 AM in 24-hour format).

**Source:** `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/example_test.go` (line 99-100), where the cleanup job is registered with the schedule `"0 2 * * *"`. This schedule is also documented in `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/doc.go` (line 16) and `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/README.md` (line 35) as an example of daily cleanup running at 2 AM.
