**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery workers consume from an in-process Go channel named `asyncPostQueue`, which is declared at line 39 in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

```39:39:internal/webhook/webhook.go
	asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

The queue is a buffered channel with capacity 128. Four goroutines are spawned on application startup (in the `init()` function) that consume webhook payloads from this channel and dispatch them to the configured webhook endpoints via the `Post()` function.
