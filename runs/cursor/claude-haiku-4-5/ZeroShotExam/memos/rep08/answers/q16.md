When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** - where the memo is the source (lines 141-143)
2. **Memo relations** - where the memo is the target/related memo (lines 145-147)
3. **Attachments** - linked to the memo (lines 149-157)

After deleting all of these related records, the memo row itself is finally deleted (line 158).
