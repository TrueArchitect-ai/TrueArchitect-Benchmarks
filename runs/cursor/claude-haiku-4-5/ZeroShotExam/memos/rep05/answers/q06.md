The database table that stores links between one memo and another related memo is **`memo_relation`**.

This table is defined in `/bench/repo/store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and contains:
- `memo_id` - the ID of the first memo
- `related_memo_id` - the ID of the related memo
- `type` - the type of relationship between the memos
- A unique constraint on (memo_id, related_memo_id, type) to prevent duplicate relations
