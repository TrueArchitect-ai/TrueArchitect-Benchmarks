q25.

The ListMemos RPC accepts a CEL (Common Expression Language) filter expression. **CEL** is the parsing engine/syntax used; it lives at **`github.com/google/cel-go`** (Google's CEL-Go library). The filter is compiled and converted to dialect-specific SQL through the custom rendering layer in **`/bench/repo/internal/filter/`** (specifically `engine.go` for CEL compilation and `render.go` which inspects the `Dialect` field and generates appropriate SQL for SQLite, PostgreSQL, or MySQL by branch-switching on dialect-specific functions and operators at render time.
