**Engine + Path:** `CEL-go` (Common Expression Language engine); located at `internal/filter/engine.go`

**Mechanism in one sentence:** The filter expression is parsed by the `cel-go` library into an AST, then converted to a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR) in `parser.go`, and finally rendered to SQL for each dialect (SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL) via dialect-specific renderers in `render.go`, with the compilation and rendering orchestrated by `engine.CompileToStatement()` method which accepts dialect-specific `RenderOptions`, and the resulting SQL fragments are appended to the WHERE clause by `filter.AppendConditions()` helper in `helpers.go`.

**Key files:**
- `internal/filter/engine.go` - Engine definition and `CompileToStatement()` entry point
- `internal/filter/parser.go` - CEL AST to IR conversion
- `internal/filter/render.go` - IR to dialect-specific SQL rendering
- `internal/filter/helpers.go` - `AppendConditions()` integration helper for stores
- Used in store layer: `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
