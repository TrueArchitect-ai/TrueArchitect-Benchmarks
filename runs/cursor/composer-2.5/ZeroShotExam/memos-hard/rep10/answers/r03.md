r03. There is no nightly **database-cleanup** job scheduled in this repository’s cron scheduler.

The cron scheduler lives under `internal/scheduler/` (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `internal/scheduler/job.go`), but it is not used by the running Memos server. `server/server.go` starts only S3 presign background runners (`server/runner/s3presign/`), not the scheduler. Every `scheduler.Register(...)` call is in tests, examples, or docs — for example `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`, and `internal/scheduler/README.md`.

A repo-wide search finds no job named `database-cleanup` (or `database_cleanup` / `DatabaseCleanup`). The closest scheduler examples are generic cleanup jobs in documentation and tests, not a production database job:

- `"daily-cleanup"` with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2:00 AM daily) in `internal/scheduler/doc.go` and `internal/scheduler/README.md`
- `"cleanup"` with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` in `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`
- `"stale-session-cleanup"` with schedule `"0 3 * * *"` (3:00 AM daily) as a best-practices example in `internal/scheduler/README.md`

So the premise of the question is incorrect: this repo does not schedule a nightly database-cleanup job in the cron scheduler.
