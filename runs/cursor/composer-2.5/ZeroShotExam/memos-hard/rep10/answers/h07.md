For uploaded avatars, the browser requests `GET /file/users/{username}/avatar` (set by `convertUserFromStore` in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_converters.go` and rendered by `UserAvatar` in `web/src/components/UserAvatar.tsx`). The image bytes are not stored as a separate file; they live in the `user.avatar_url` column as a base64 data URI.

serveUserAvatar — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go
getUserByUsername — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go
GetUser — store/user.go
ListUsers — store/user.go
ListUsers — store/db/sqlite/user.go
avatar_url — store/db/sqlite/user.go (SQL `SELECT ... avatar_url ... FROM user`; also `store/db/postgres/user.go` or `store/db/mysql/user.go` depending on driver via `store/db/db.go`)
