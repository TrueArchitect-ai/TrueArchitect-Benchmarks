**Handlers (≥3):** `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo` (and also `CreateMemoComment`) — all on `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` and `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`.

**Convergence function:** `Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`

These memo RPC handlers can transitively POST to user-configured webhook URLs (`hook.Url` from `GetUserWebhooks`). The only outbound HTTP to such URLs in this repo is the webhook path; `webhook.Post` is where every path converges immediately before delivery (`http.NewRequest` + `safeClient.Do` at ```119:152:internal/webhook/webhook.go```).
