**s3presign.Runner** — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:129`

The `s3presign` runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` keeps S3 attachment links fresh by re-presigning URLs before they expire. Presigned URLs last 5 days (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go`); the runner skips attachments whose URL is still valid for at least 4 more days, and refreshes the rest by updating each attachment’s `Reference` field.
