**Premise correction:** Webhook delivery workers in this repository do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ. There are no references to either broker anywhere in the codebase.

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery is handled in-process via a buffered Go channel and goroutine workers in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. The queue is declared as `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (line 39). Four worker goroutines are started in `init()` (lines 42–54) and read from that channel in a `for payload := range asyncPostQueue` loop, calling `Post(payload)` for each item. Producers enqueue work through `PostAsync()` (lines 184–196), which sends to `asyncPostQueue` with a non-blocking `select` (drops when full). Call sites include `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`, which invokes `webhook.PostAsync(payload)`.
