sqlite, postgres, mysql

Migrations live under `store/migration/` in three dialect-specific directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/mysql/`. The migrator documents this layout in `store/migrator.go` (`store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`), and tests in `store/test/main_test.go` and `store/test/containers.go` use the same three drivers.
