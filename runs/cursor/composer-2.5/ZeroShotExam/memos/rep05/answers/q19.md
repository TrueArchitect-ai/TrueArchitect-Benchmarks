Connect — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`

gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`

MemoService is exposed over Connect and gRPC-Gateway. Both are wired up in `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (see the comment at line 64).

For **Connect**, registration is in `connect_handler.go` via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers` (line 40). Method wrapping is in `connect_services.go`, where `ConnectServiceHandler` delegates MemoService RPCs to the underlying `APIV1Service` (starting at line 304).

For **gRPC-Gateway**, registration is in `v1.go` via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (line 108). There is no separate wrapper; `APIV1Service` implements the gRPC service interface directly and is mounted on `/api/v1/*`.
