Connect — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`, `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`
gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`

`MemoService` is exposed through both protocols inside `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`:

- **Connect** — `ConnectServiceHandler` wraps `APIV1Service` in `connect_handler.go`; `RegisterConnectHandlers` registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` there; per-RPC Connect request/response adapters live in `connect_services.go`; `v1.go` mounts the Connect mux on `/memos.api.v1.*` via `echo.WrapHandler`.
- **gRPC-Gateway** — `v1.go` calls `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` and serves the gateway mux on `/api/v1/*` via `echo.WrapHandler(http.MaxBytesHandler(gwMux, ...))`.
