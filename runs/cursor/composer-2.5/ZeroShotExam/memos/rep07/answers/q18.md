NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go`

Each backend implements `store.Driver` via its own `NewDB` constructor (`sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, `postgres.NewDB`). At startup, `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) and returns the matching implementation. It is invoked from `cmd/memos/main.go` during application startup.
