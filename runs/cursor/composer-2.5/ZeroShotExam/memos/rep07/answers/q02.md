This project ships migrations for **SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL**. They live under `store/migration/` in three dialect-specific trees:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versioned `.sql` files plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned `.sql` files plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versioned `.sql` files plus `LATEST.sql`

The migrator loads them from `store/migration/{driver}/`, as documented in `store/migrator.go` (line 39). `AGENTS.md` (line 12) and `README.md` (line 21) list the same three supported database backends. There are no other dialect directories under `store/migration/`.
