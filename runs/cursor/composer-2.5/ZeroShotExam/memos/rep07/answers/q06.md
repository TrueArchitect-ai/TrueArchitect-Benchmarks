**memo_relation**

The `memo_relation` table stores links between a memo and a related memo. It is defined in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` and `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` with columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`. The store layer reads and writes this table in files such as `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go`.
