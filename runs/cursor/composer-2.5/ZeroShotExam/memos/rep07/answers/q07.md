`web/src/connect.ts`

That file creates the ConnectRPC transport and all exported service clients via `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` (lines 72 and 191–203), including `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`. Other frontend files import these clients from `@/connect` rather than creating their own.
