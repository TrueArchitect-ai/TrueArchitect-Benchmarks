`useMemos` (and `useInfiniteMemos` in the same file) calls `memoServiceClient.listMemos` in `web/src/connect.ts`, which hits the `ListMemos` RPC in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. The backend handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` builds a `store.FindMemo` and calls `s.Store.ListMemos`, which delegates from `store/memo.go` to the dialect-specific `ListMemos` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` (also implemented for Postgres and MySQL).
