These four events are defined in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`. Created, updated, and deleted share `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook`, which sets the activity type and calls `webhook.PostAsync`; comment-created uses `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`, which also ends at `webhook.PostAsync`.

Call sites:
- **created** — `CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
- **updated** — `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go` (used from `UpdateMemo` and attachment/relation updates)
- **deleted** — `DeleteMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
- **comment.created** — `CreateMemoComment` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`

Delivery convergence is in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`: every dispatch enqueues via `PostAsync`, background workers drain `asyncPostQueue`, and actual HTTP delivery happens in `Post`.
