APIV1Service.SignIn — server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go

The `SignIn` RPC is defined on `AuthService` in `proto/api/v1/auth_service.proto`. The backend handler is `APIV1Service.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`, where password and SSO sign-in logic runs. `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` is a thin Connect wrapper that delegates to that method.
