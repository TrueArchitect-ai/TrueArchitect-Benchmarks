**memo_relation**

Links between memos are stored in the `memo_relation` table, defined in `/bench/repo/store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and `/bench/repo/store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`. It has `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type` (e.g. `REFERENCE`, `COMMENT`). The store layer reads and writes it in `/bench/repo/store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go` (and the postgres/mysql equivalents).
