**memo_relation**

The `memo_relation` table stores links between one memo and another related memo. It has three columns: `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type` (for example `REFERENCE` or `COMMENT`).

Defined in:
- `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (lines 46–52)
- `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`

Used by the store layer in `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, with equivalent implementations for Postgres and MySQL under `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`.
