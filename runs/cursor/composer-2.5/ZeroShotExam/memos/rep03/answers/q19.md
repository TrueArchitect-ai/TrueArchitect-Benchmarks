gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`

MemoService is exposed on two transports from `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`:

1. **Connect RPC** — `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` is registered in `connect_handler.go`; `ConnectServiceHandler` methods in `connect_services.go` wrap the underlying `APIV1Service` gRPC implementation.
2. **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/JSON REST)** — `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` is called in `v1.go`; the generated reverse-proxy handlers live in `memo_service.pb.gw.go`.
