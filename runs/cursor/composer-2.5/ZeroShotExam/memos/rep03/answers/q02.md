SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL

Migrations live under `store/migration/` in three dialect-specific directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/`. Each contains versioned migration files (for example `store/migration/sqlite/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`) plus a `LATEST.sql` full schema. `store/migrator.go` embeds and applies them from `store/migration/{driver}/`. `AGENTS.md` also documents all three as supported storage backends.
