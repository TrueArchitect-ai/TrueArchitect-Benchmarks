**APIV1Service.SignIn** — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`

The `SignIn` RPC is defined on `AuthService` in `proto/api/v1/auth_service.proto`. The backend implementation is `APIV1Service.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go` (lines 51–108). It handles password and SSO sign-in, then calls `doSignIn` to create the session and tokens.

`ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` is a thin Connect adapter that delegates to `APIV1Service.SignIn`; the business logic lives in `auth_service.go`.
