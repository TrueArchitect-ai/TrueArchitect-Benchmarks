`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

Each of the three packages implements `store.Driver` (defined in `store/driver.go`) via a `NewDB` constructor that returns `store.Driver`. At startup, `cmd/memos/main.go` calls `db.NewDBDriver`, which switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) to pick the implementation.
