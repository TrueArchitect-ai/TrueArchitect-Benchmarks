`ShortcutService` defines five RPCs in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`:

- **ListShortcuts**
- **GetShortcut**
- **CreateShortcut**
- **UpdateShortcut**
- **DeleteShortcut**

All five are implemented in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** as methods on `APIV1Service`.

`server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` only wraps those methods for Connect RPC and delegates to `APIV1Service`; the business logic lives in `shortcut_service.go`.
