Go `embed.FS` with Echo `StaticWithConfig` middleware; `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`

In production, the Vite-built SPA is copied into `server/router/frontend/dist` (via `web/package.json`’s `release` script), then baked into the Go binary with `//go:embed dist/*` in `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`. That file’s `Serve` method wires Echo’s `middleware.StaticWithConfig` to serve those embedded assets, plus an SPA fallback middleware that returns `index.html` for client-side routes.

`server/server.go` registers that service on the Echo server (`frontend.NewFrontendService(profile, store).Serve(ctx, echoServer)`), but the embed + static-serving mechanism itself lives in `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`.
