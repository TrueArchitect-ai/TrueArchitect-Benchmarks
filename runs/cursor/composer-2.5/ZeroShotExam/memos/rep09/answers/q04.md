**APIV1Service.SignIn — server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go**

The `SignIn` RPC is defined on `AuthService` in `proto/api/v1/auth_service.proto`. On the backend, it is implemented by `APIV1Service.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`, which performs password and SSO authentication and returns tokens.

That method is registered as the gRPC-Gateway handler in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` via `RegisterAuthServiceHandlerServer`. Connect clients hit a thin wrapper, `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`, which delegates to `APIV1Service.SignIn`.
