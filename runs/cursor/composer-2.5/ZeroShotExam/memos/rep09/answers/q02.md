q02. sqlite, mysql, postgres

Migrations live under `store/migration/` in three dialect-specific directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/`. `store/migrator.go` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and `store/test/main_test.go` lists the supported drivers as `sqlite`, `mysql`, and `postgres`.
