NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go`

Each of those entry files defines a `DB` type that implements `store.Driver` (declared in `store/driver.go`) via its package’s `NewDB` constructor. At startup, `cmd/memos/main.go` calls `db.NewDBDriver`, which switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) to pick the implementation.
