sqlite, postgres, mysql

Migrations live under `store/migration/` in three driver-specific directories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/mysql/`. The migrator documents that layout in `store/migrator.go` (path pattern `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`), and `store/db/db.go` wires those same three drivers (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`). `AGENTS.md` also lists the supported storage backends as SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL.
