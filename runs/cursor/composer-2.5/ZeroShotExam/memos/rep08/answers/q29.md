**`web/src/connect.ts`**; **access token** (JWT, sent as `Authorization: Bearer …`, stored in `web/src/auth-state.ts`) and **refresh token** (HttpOnly cookie, sent with `credentials: "include"` when the interceptor calls `refreshAccessToken()`); **`server/auth`**

The Connect RPC `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` attaches the access token to every request, refreshes it proactively when near expiry or reactively on `Unauthenticated`, and retries once. Token persistence and expiry checks live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`. On the backend, `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go` delegates to `Authorizer` (`server/router/api/v1/authz.go`), which uses `auth.Authenticator` in **`server/auth`** (`authenticator.go`, `token.go`) to validate Bearer access tokens and refresh tokens.
