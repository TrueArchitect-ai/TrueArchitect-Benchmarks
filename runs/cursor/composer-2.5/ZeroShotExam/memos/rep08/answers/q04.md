**APIV1Service.SignIn** — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`

The `SignIn` RPC is declared on `AuthService` in `proto/api/v1/auth_service.proto`, and the backend implementation is the `SignIn` method on `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go` (starting at line 51). It handles password and SSO sign-in, then calls `doSignIn` to create the session and tokens.

`ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` is only a Connect RPC wrapper that forwards to `APIV1Service.SignIn`.
