This project ships migrations for **sqlite, postgres, mysql**.

Each dialect has its own tree under `store/migration/`:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (including `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (including `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (including `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`)

`store/migrator.go` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and `store/db/db.go` wires those three drivers (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`) to their migration paths.
