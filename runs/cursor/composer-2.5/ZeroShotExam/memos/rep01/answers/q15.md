- user: `User` — `store/user.go`
- attachment: `Attachment` — `store/attachment.go`
- reaction: `Reaction` — `store/reaction.go`

These are the domain structs in the `store` package. The SQLite/Postgres drivers use them for CRUD against the `user`, `attachment`, and `reaction` tables (for example, `CreateUser` inserts into `user` in `store/db/sqlite/user.go`, `CreateAttachment` into `attachment` in `store/db/sqlite/attachment.go`, and `UpsertReaction` into `reaction` in `store/db/sqlite/reaction.go`).
