MemoService is exposed over **Connect RPC** and **gRPC-Gateway** (HTTP/JSON REST). Registration and wiring live in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (`RegisterGateway`).

Connect — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`

gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`

**Connect:** `connect_handler.go` registers MemoService via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` in `RegisterConnectHandlers`. `connect_services.go` implements the Connect wrapper methods (e.g. `CreateMemo`, `ListMemos`) that delegate to `APIV1Service`.

**gRPC-Gateway:** `v1.go` registers MemoService with `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, mounting REST endpoints under `/api/v1/*` from the proto `google.api.http` annotations.
