**memo_relation**

Links between memos are stored in the `memo_relation` table. It was introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and is part of the current schema in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`. Each row has `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type` (for example `REFERENCE` or `COMMENT`, as defined in `store/memo_relation.go`).
