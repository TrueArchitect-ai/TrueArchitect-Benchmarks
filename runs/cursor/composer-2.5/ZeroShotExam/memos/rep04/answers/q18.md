NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go`

Each of those entry files defines a `DB` type with a `NewDB` constructor that returns `store.Driver` (the interface in `store/driver.go`). `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) and delegates to the matching `NewDB`. It is invoked at startup from `cmd/memos/main.go`.
