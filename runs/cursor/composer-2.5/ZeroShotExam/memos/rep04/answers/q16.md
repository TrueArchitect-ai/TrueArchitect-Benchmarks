When a memo is deleted through the API, the handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` calls `Store.DeleteMemo` (after deleting comment memos recursively). In `store/memo.go`, `Store.DeleteMemo` deletes these other record kinds before the memo row itself:

- **memo_relation** records — relations where the memo is either the source (`memo_id`) or the target (`related_memo_id`) (`store/memo.go`, via `DeleteMemoRelation`)
- **attachment** records — attachments linked to the memo (`store/memo.go`, via `ListAttachments` + `DeleteAttachment`)

The driver-level delete (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, and equivalents for other DB backends) only removes the `memo` row after that cleanup.
