`ShortcutService` defines five RPCs in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`:

- **ListShortcuts**
- **GetShortcut**
- **CreateShortcut**
- **UpdateShortcut**
- **DeleteShortcut**

All five are implemented in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** on `APIV1Service` (e.g. `ListShortcuts` at line 49, `GetShortcut` at line 95, `CreateShortcut` at line 135, `UpdateShortcut` at line 211, `DeleteShortcut` at line 284).

`server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` also defines matching `ConnectServiceHandler` methods for the same RPCs, but those only delegate to `APIV1Service`; the business logic lives in `shortcut_service.go`.
