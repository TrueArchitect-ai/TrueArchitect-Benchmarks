**DB** — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The PostgreSQL driver is the `DB` struct in that file. `NewDB` returns it as a `store.Driver`, and it wraps a `*sql.DB` opened with the `github.com/lib/pq` driver (`sql.Open("postgres", profile.DSN)`). The rest of the `store.Driver` methods are implemented on `*DB` in other files under `store/db/postgres/`.
