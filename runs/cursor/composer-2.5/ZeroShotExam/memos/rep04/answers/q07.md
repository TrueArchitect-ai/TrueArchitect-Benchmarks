web/src/connect.ts

ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. That file sets up the Connect transport and instantiates the clients with `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect`, including `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (lines 191–203). It also creates a separate `refreshAuthClient` for token refresh (line 72).
