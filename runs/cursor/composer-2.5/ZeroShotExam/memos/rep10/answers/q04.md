**APIV1Service.SignIn** — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`

The SignIn RPC is implemented on `APIV1Service` at line 51 of that file. That method contains the sign-in logic (password and SSO). For Connect, `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` is a thin wrapper that delegates to `APIV1Service.SignIn`; gRPC-Gateway registers `APIV1Service` directly via `RegisterAuthServiceHandlerServer` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
