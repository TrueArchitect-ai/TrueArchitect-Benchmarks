**Hop-by-hop:**

1. **`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`** — `useCreateMemo()` calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo({ memo: memoToCreate })` in its mutation function.
2. **`web/src/connect.ts`** — exports `memoServiceClient`, created with `createClient(MemoService, transport)`.
3. **`proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`** — defines `rpc CreateMemo(CreateMemoRequest) returns (Memo)` on `MemoService`.
4. **`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`** — `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` validates input, builds a `store.Memo`, and calls `s.Store.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
5. **`store/memo.go`** — `Store.CreateMemo` validates the UID and delegates to `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
6. **`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`** — `(d *DB) CreateMemo` runs the `INSERT INTO memo (...)` statement (postgres/mysql equivalents live under `store/db/postgres/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`).

**Note:** Connect requests also pass through a thin adapter in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (`ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` → `APIV1Service.CreateMemo`), but the substantive handler that performs business logic and reaches the store is `memo_service.go`.
