When a memo is deleted through the API, the handler calls `Store.DeleteMemo` (see `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`). In the store layer, `DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` removes these other record kinds **before** the memo row itself:

- **memo_relation** — deleted twice: once where the memo is the source (`MemoID`), and once where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
- **attachment** — attachments linked to the memo are listed and deleted via `DeleteAttachment` before the memo row is removed

The final step is `s.driver.DeleteMemo`, which deletes only the `memo` row (see `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, and the equivalent implementations under `store/db/postgres/` and `store/db/mysql/`).
