Three `store.Driver` implementations (entry file each):

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`

Startup factory (switches on `profile.Driver`):

NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go`

The interface is defined in `store/driver.go`. Each backend exposes `NewDB(profile *profile.Profile) (store.Driver, error)` from its entry file; `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` picks among them via a `switch` on `"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, and `"postgres"`. It is invoked from `cmd/memos/main.go` at startup.
