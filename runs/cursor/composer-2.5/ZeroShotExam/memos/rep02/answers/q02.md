sqlite, postgres, mysql

These are the three dialect directories under `store/migration/`:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` — SQLite migrations (e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, versioned files under `store/migration/sqlite/0.30/`, etc.)
- `store/migration/postgres/` — PostgreSQL migrations (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` — MySQL migrations (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`)

The migrator documents this layout in `store/migrator.go` as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and the matching DB drivers live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/mysql/`. No other migration dialect directories exist in the repository.
