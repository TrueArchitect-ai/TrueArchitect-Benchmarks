Connect — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`

gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`

MemoService is exposed over **Connect RPC** and **gRPC-Gateway** (HTTP/JSON REST), as noted in `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.

For **Connect**, `connect_handler.go` registers MemoService via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(...)`, and `connect_services.go` wraps the underlying gRPC-style `APIV1Service` methods into Connect request/response types.

For **gRPC-Gateway**, `v1.go` calls `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, and the generated gateway handlers live in `memo_service.pb.gw.go`.
