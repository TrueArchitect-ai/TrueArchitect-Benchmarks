memo_relation

Links between one memo and another related memo are stored in the `memo_relation` table. It is defined in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and in the current schema at `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, with columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`. The store layer reads and writes this table in files such as `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`.
