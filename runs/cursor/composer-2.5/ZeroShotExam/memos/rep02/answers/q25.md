**CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`** + **`internal/filter/`** (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`); the filter string is compiled by cel-go into an AST, lowered to a dialect-agnostic condition IR in `parser.go`, then walked by the renderer in `render.go` to emit parameterized SQL fragments (with dialect-specific JSON, date-part, and placeholder syntax) that each store driver appends via `filter.AppendConditions` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`.
