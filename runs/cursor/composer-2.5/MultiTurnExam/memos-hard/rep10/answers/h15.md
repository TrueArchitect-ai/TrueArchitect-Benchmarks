Memo visibility is enforced in multiple places, not only through `checkMemoReadAccess`. That helper covers single-memo reads such as `GetMemo` and `ListMemoComments` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-67`, `:356`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:148`).

List endpoints apply query-time filters instead: `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-226`), `ListMemoRelations` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:107-112`), `batchConvertMemoRelations` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go:182-187`), and `ListAllUserStats` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:83-91`).

HTTP surfaces outside gRPC/API v1 also filter or gate access: attachment serving (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:674-701`), RSS (`server/router/rss/rss.go:88-90`), and the public sitemap (`server/router/frontend/frontend.go:149-150`).

Live updates and notifications apply their own rules: SSE delivery (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-143`), in-app notifications (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302-316`), and email links (`server/notification/email.go:306-319`).
