**s3presign.Runner** — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:129`

The `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` refreshes presigned S3 URLs stored in attachment `Reference` fields so externally stored attachment links do not expire. It lists S3 attachments, skips those whose presigned URL is still valid for at least 4 more days (URLs are issued with a 5-day lifetime in `internal/storage/s3/s3.go`), and updates stale ones via `CheckAndPresign`.
