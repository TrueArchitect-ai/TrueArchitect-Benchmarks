**Why SQLite wins among the three candidates:** `store.Driver` is implemented by `sqlite.DB`, `mysql.DB`, and `postgres.DB`, each with its own `CreateMemo` containing an `INSERT INTO memo` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`). With default `driver=sqlite`, `db.NewDBDriver` constructs only `sqlite.NewDB`, so runtime dispatch through `s.driver.CreateMemo` resolves to the SQLite method.
