**Premise correction:** Webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ. There are no references to either broker in the codebase, and `go.mod` includes no NATS/RabbitMQ/AMQP dependencies.

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery is in-process via a buffered Go channel and fixed worker pool in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

- **Queue declaration:** `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at line 39 of `internal/webhook/webhook.go`
- **Workers:** four goroutines started in `init()` (lines 42–54), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)`
- **Enqueue:** memo handlers call `webhook.PostAsync(...)` from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`, which non-blockingly sends to `asyncPostQueue` (drops if full; lines 184–195 of `internal/webhook/webhook.go`)
- **HTTP dispatch:** workers invoke `Post`, which uses the shared `safeClient` with SSRF-safe dialing (`safeDialContext`) to POST to the webhook URL
