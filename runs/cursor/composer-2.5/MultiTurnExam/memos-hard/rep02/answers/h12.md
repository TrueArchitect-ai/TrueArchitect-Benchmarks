**Why not the other candidates:** `mysql.DB.CreateMemo` (`store/db/mysql/memo.go`) and `postgres.DB.CreateMemo` (`store/db/postgres/memo.go`) also implement INSERTs, but they are only constructed when `profile.Driver` is `"mysql"` or `"postgres"`. With default flags, that never happens — the factory switch and default `"sqlite"` config rule them out without needing runtime observation.
