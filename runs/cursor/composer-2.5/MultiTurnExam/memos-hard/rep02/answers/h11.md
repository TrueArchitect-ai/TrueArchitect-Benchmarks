- `server/auth`
- `server/notification`

- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/router/fileserver`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/router/rss`

**`Memo` (~6 packages)** — referenced from:
- `server/notification`
- `server/router/api/v1`
- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/router/rss`
- `server/runner/memopayload`

Excluding paths under `/test`, the counts are roughly **6** for `User` and **5** for `Memo`; the ranking is unchanged.

Next closest is **`Attachment`** at ~4 packages (`server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`). Other store entities (`IdentityProvider`, `InstanceSetting`, `UserSetting`, etc.) are defined under `store/` but are not referenced as `store.{Type}` outside that layer; callers tend to use protobuf types from `proto/gen/store` instead. No packages under `internal/` or `cmd/` reference these store entity types directly (only `store.New` in `cmd/memos/main.go`).
