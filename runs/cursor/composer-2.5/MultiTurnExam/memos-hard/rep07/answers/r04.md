**Premise correction:** Webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ. A repo-wide search finds no references to either broker (or AMQP/Kafka).

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. Four worker goroutines are started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`); they read from that channel and call `Post()` to perform the HTTP dispatch. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-195`), called from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`.
