**s3presign runner** — **every 12 hours** (plus one immediate run at startup via `RunOnce`) — **server/server.go:129**

The `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` keeps S3 attachment links fresh by re-presigning URLs whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry (presigned URLs expire after 5 days; see `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77-79`). It lists S3 attachments in batches and updates each `Reference` with a new presigned URL.

The schedule is defined as `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, and `Run()` ticks on that interval (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29-40`).

Startup wiring is in `Server.startBackgroundRunners()` (`server/server.go:153-174`): it creates the runner, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately (`server/server.go:163`), then starts `Run(s3Context)` in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:167-169`). That method is invoked from `Server.Start()` at `server/server.go:129`.
