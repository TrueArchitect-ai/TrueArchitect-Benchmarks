Stored data is the `avatar_url` column on the `user` table (`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:20`), typically a base64 data URI. After fetch, `serveUserAvatar` decodes it via `parseDataURI` (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:196-209`) and returns the bytes with `c.Blob`.
