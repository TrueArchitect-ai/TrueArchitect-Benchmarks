**Premise correction:** Webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ. A repo-wide search finds no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka client or dependency.

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery is in-process. Four background goroutines are started in `init()` and read from a buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128), declared in ```39:39:internal/webhook/webhook.go```. Workers loop on that channel and call `Post` (```42:54:internal/webhook/webhook.go```). Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (```184:195:internal/webhook/webhook.go```), invoked from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`. There is no external message broker and no separate queue declaration beyond the channel created with `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`.
