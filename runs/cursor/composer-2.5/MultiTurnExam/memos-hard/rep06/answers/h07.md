**Notes:**
- Route registration: `GET /file/users/:identifier/avatar` is wired in ```121:124:server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go``` and mounted at startup in ```81:82:server/server.go```.
- `getUserByUsername` calls `s.Store.GetUser(ctx, &store.FindUser{Username: &identifier})` at ```715:716:server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go```.
- `Store.GetUser` delegates to `ListUsers` when there is no ID cache hit (```148:168:store/user.go```).
