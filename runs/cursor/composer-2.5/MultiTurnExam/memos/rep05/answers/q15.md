Each struct is defined in the `store` package and used by the database drivers against the matching table. For example, SQLite queries use `user`, `attachment`, and `reaction` in `store/db/sqlite/user.go`, `store/db/sqlite/attachment.go`, and `store/db/sqlite/reaction.go`, and those tables are defined in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`.
