Connect RPC — server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go, server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go  
REST (gRPC-Gateway) — server/router/api/v1/v1.go

**Connect RPC:** MemoService is registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)`. Request/response wrapping lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` on `ConnectServiceHandler` (e.g. `CreateMemo`, `ListMemos`). Both are wired from `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (lines 139–141).

**REST (gRPC-Gateway):** MemoService is registered as HTTP/JSON in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` with `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (line 108), served under `/api/v1/*`. Route mapping is generated in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.
