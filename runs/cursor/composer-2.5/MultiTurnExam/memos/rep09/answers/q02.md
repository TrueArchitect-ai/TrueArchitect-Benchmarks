**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/mysql/`

The migrator embeds that tree and picks the path from the configured driver via `getMigrationBasePath()` in `store/migrator.go` (`migration/{driver}/`). The same three drivers are wired in `store/db/db.go` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`).
