**web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient**

Memo CRUD queries and mutations live in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`. It imports `memoServiceClient` from `@/connect` and uses it for list/read/create/update/delete:

- **Read:** `useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo` → `listMemos`, `getMemo`
- **Create:** `useCreateMemo` → `createMemo`
- **Update:** `useUpdateMemo` → `updateMemo`
- **Delete:** `useDeleteMemo` → `deleteMemo`

`memoServiceClient` is defined in `web/src/connect.ts` as `createClient(MemoService, transport)`, where `MemoService` comes from the generated proto at `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts`.
