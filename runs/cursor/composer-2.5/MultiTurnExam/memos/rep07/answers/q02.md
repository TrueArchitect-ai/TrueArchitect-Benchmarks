This project ships migrations for **MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite**.

They live under `store/migration/` in three dialect-specific directories:

- `store/migration/mysql/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

`store/migrator.go` documents that layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql` and loads migrations from `migration/{driver}/` based on the configured driver. `store/db/db.go` supports the same three drivers: `sqlite`, `mysql`, and `postgres`.
