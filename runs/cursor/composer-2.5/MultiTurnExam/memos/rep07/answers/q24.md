The chain:

1. **Frontend hook** — `useCreateMemo` calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo(...)` in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`.
2. **Client** — `memoServiceClient` is created from `MemoService` in `web/src/connect.ts`.
3. **Proto RPC** — `MemoService.CreateMemo` is defined in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.
4. **Handler** — `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` handles the request (Connect entry in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` delegates here).
5. **Store method** — `Store.CreateMemo` in `store/memo.go` delegates to the driver.
6. **Dialect driver** — `(d *DB) CreateMemo` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` runs the `INSERT INTO memo ...` SQL.
