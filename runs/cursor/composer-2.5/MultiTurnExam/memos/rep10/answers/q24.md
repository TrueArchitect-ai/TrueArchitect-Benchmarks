**Chain:**

1. **`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`** — `useCreateMemo` calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo(...)`.
2. **`web/src/connect.ts`** — `memoServiceClient` is a Connect client for `MemoService`.
3. **`proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`** — defines the `CreateMemo` RPC.
4. **`server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`** — `ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` receives the Connect request and delegates to `APIV1Service.CreateMemo`.
5. **`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`** — `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` builds the store memo and calls `s.Store.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
6. **`store/memo.go`** — `Store.CreateMemo` validates the UID and calls `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
7. **`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`** — `DB.CreateMemo` runs the SQL `INSERT INTO memo ...` (MySQL/PostgreSQL equivalents are in `store/db/mysql/memo.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`).
