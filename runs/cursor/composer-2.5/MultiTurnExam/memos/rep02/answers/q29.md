**Frontend interceptor** — `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` is registered on the Connect transport. It:

1. Gets a token via `getRequestToken()` from `web/src/auth-state.ts`
2. Sets `Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>` on each request
3. On `Unauthenticated`, refreshes via `AuthService.refreshToken` and retries once
4. Uses `fetch` with `credentials: "include"` so cookies are sent on refresh
