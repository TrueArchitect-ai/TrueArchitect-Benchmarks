`MemoService` is exposed on two stacks:

**1. Connect (ConnectRPC)**  
- Registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`.  
- RPC wrappers that delegate to `APIV1Service` are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `ConnectServiceHandler.ListMemos`).  
- Mounted in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` on `/memos.api.v1.*`.

**2. REST (gRPC-Gateway)**  
- Registered in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`.  
- Serves HTTP REST routes from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (e.g. `GET /api/v1/memos`).  
- Mounted on `/api/v1/*` in the same file.
