**Chain:**

1. **Frontend hook** — `useCreateMemo` in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo(...)`.
2. **Client** — `memoServiceClient` is created in `web/src/connect.ts` from the generated `MemoService`.
3. **Proto RPC** — `MemoService.CreateMemo` is defined in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.
4. **Handler** — `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` handles the request and calls `s.Store.CreateMemo(...)`. (The Connect wrapper in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` delegates here; registration is in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`.)
5. **Store method** — `Store.CreateMemo` in `store/memo.go` forwards to `s.driver.CreateMemo(...)`.
6. **Dialect driver** — `(d *DB) CreateMemo` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` runs the `INSERT INTO memo ...` statement (same pattern in `store/db/postgres/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`).
