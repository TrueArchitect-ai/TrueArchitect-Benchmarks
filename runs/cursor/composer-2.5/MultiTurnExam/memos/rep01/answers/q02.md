**mysql, postgres, sqlite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

`store/migrator.go` documents that layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql` and embeds the whole `migration` tree. Test code in `store/test/containers.go` also lists the supported drivers as `sqlite`, `mysql`, and `postgres`.
