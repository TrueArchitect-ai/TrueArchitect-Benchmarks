SSE (Server-Sent Events); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoAttachments, SetMemoRelations, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction

**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. Clients connect to `GET /api/v1/sse` with `Content-Type: text/event-stream` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, registered from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`).

**Hub:** `SSEHub` is defined in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Broadcast`).
