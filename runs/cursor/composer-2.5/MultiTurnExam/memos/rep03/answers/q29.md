The Connect transport’s `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` (lines 156–178) calls `getRequestToken()`, sets `Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>`, and on `Unauthenticated` retries after `refreshAccessToken()`.

**Access token:** held in memory/localStorage (`web/src/auth-state.ts`, keys `memos_access_token` / `memos_token_expires_at`) and sent as a Bearer token.

**Refresh token:** not stored in JS; refresh uses `fetchWithCredentials` (`credentials: "include"`) and `AuthService.refreshToken` via a separate transport without the auth interceptor (lines 57–72, 74–83).

**Backend validation:** `server/auth` (`Authenticator` in `server/auth/authenticator.go`) validates access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`), refresh tokens (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), and PATs. Connect/gRPC requests go through `Authorizer` in `server/router/api/v1/authz.go`, which delegates to `auth.Authenticator` via `NewAuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`.
