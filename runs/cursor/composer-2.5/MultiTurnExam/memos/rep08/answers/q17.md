web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient

Memo CRUD queries and mutations live in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`:

- **Read:** `useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo` → `listMemos`, `getMemo`
- **Create:** `useCreateMemo` → `createMemo`
- **Update:** `useUpdateMemo` → `updateMemo`
- **Delete:** `useDeleteMemo` → `deleteMemo`

All of these call `memoServiceClient`, imported from `@/connect` (defined in `web/src/connect.ts` as `createClient(MemoService, transport)`).
