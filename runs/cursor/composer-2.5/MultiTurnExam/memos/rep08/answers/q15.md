Database access uses these types in the dialect drivers under `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/mysql/` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/user.go`, `store/db/sqlite/attachment.go`, `store/db/sqlite/reaction.go`).
