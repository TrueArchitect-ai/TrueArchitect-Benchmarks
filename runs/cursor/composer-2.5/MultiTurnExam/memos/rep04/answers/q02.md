q02. sqlite, mysql, postgres

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/`
- `store/migration/mysql/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`

Each dialect has its own `LATEST.sql` and versioned migration files (for example `store/migration/sqlite/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`).

`store/migrator.go` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and `store/test/main_test.go` lists the three drivers as `sqlite`, `mysql`, and `postgres`.
