s3presign runner — runs once synchronously at server startup, then every 12 hours via a `time.Ticker` (`server/server.go`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`).

Unused infrastructure: `internal/scheduler` provides a cron scheduler, but the server never constructs, registers, or starts it. `memopayload.Runner` is also not started as a background worker; memo payload rebuilding is invoked synchronously during memo create/update requests (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`).
