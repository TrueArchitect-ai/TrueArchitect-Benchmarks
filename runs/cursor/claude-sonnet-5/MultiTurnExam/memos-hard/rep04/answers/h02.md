**Protection mechanism:** The shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`) uses a custom `http.Transport` whose `DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`). At dial time, `safeDialContext` resolves the target hostname via `net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost` and rejects the connection if any resolved IP is reserved/private, per `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44-54`, checked against `reservedNetworks`/`reservedCIDRs` covering loopback, RFC‑1918, link‑local/cloud IMDS, and IPv6 equivalents — `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-36`). This dial-time check (rather than just a one-time URL validation) is what also defeats DNS‑rebinding SSRF attacks. A separate, earlier check, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`), performs the same reserved-IP check at webhook-registration time (used by the webhook CRUD handlers in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`), but the actual delivery-time defense is the `safeDialContext`/`isReservedIP` pairing in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` and `internal/webhook/validate.go`.
