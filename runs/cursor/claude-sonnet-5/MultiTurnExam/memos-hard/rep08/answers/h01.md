**Component**: `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — it periodically re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs (`CheckAndPresign`) so the presigned S3 GET URLs stored in each attachment's `Payload.S3Object.Reference` don't expire. It re-signs any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs are issued with a 5‑day expiry), via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and persists the refreshed URL/timestamp with `Store.UpdateAttachment`.

**Schedule**: every 12 hours, per the ticker in `Run`:
