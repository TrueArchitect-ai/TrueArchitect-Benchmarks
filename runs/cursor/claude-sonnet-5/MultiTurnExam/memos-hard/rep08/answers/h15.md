Memo visibility (`store.Visibility`: Public / Protected / Private) is checked against the requester in numerous places across the server. Distinct enforcement sites include:

- **`checkMemoReadAccess`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68` — gates reading a memo (used by `GetMemo` at `memo_service.go:356` and its parent-memo lookup at `:367`, and reused by `ListMemoComments` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:148`). Enforces private/archived-memo restrictions.
- **`checkAttachmentAccess`** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422-455` — gates the Connect/gRPC `AttachmentService`'s access to an attachment based on its linked memo's visibility.
- **Inline visibility check in `ListMemoAttachments`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:213-225` — separately re-implements the same visibility gate for listing a memo's attachments.
- **Inline visibility checks in the reaction endpoints** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:29-41` (`ListMemoReactions`) and `:82-85` (`UpsertMemoReaction`).
- **`checkAttachmentPermission`** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:649-704` — a completely separate surface: the raw HTTP `/file/attachments/...` file-serving route, gating direct binary/streamed access based on the owning memo's visibility (and share-token fallback).
- **`SSEClient.canReceive`** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` — gates real-time push: decides whether a connected SSE client is allowed to receive a live memo-change event based on its visibility.
- **RSS feed generation** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:88-90` (and `:141`) — enforces visibility by construction, restricting the unauthenticated feed query to `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`.
- **Sitemap/frontend generation** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:149-151` — likewise restricts the public sitemap listing to `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`.
