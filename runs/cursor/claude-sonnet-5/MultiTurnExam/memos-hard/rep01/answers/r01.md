**Actual mechanism:** The cache layer is a purely in-memory, per-process cache, implemented in `store/cache/cache.go` as `Cache` (a `sync.Map`-backed structure with TTL expiration and a background cleanup goroutine — see `store/cache/cache.go:66-90`). It's instantiated directly in Go code, not connected to over the network:
