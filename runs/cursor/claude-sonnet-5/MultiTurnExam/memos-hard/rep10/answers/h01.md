h01.

The `s3presign.Runner` background job (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps externally-stored (S3) attachment links from going stale by re-presigning their download URLs before the presigned URL expires.

- **Component**: `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — its `CheckAndPresign`/`RunOnce` method lists all attachments with `StorageType == S3` and, for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration), generates a fresh presigned GET URL via the S3 client and updates the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` in the store (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-131`, especially the 12-hour ticker at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` and the staleness check at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).
- **Schedule**: runs once immediately, then on a repeating 12-hour interval (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by `time.NewTicker` in `Run` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29-41`).
- **Wiring into server startup**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs the runner with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce` synchronously once, then launches `Run` in a goroutine for the recurring schedule (`server/server.go:162-163` and `server/server.go:166-171`). `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
