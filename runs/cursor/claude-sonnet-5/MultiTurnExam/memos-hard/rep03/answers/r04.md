		}()

- The "queue" is the buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128), declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.
- Four worker goroutines are spawned in the package's `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) to range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload.
