**Component:** the S3 presign runner, `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (created via `s3presign.NewRunner`), which re-signs S3-backed attachment URLs before they expire — it walks all attachments with `StorageType == storepb.AttachmentStorageType_S3`, and for any whose `S3Object.LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (since presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration), it calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and persists the new URL/`LastPresignedTime` via `Store.UpdateAttachment` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:43-131`).

**Schedule:** runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`), then on a repeating `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-41`).

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162-170` inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which itself is invoked from server startup at `server/server.go:129`.
