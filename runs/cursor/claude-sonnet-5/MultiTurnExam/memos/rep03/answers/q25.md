**Engine:** [google/cel-go](https://github.com/google/cel-go) (Common Expression Language), wrapped by this project's own `filter.Engine` — `internal/filter/engine.go` (with parsing in `internal/filter/parser.go`, the IR in `internal/filter/ir.go`, and the schema in `internal/filter/schema.go`)

**Mechanism:** The `ListMemos` filter string is compiled by `filter.Engine` (built on `cel-go`'s `cel.NewEnv`) into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` intermediate representation (`internal/filter/ir.go`), which the `renderer` in `internal/filter/render.go` then walks to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments and positional/placeholder arguments (branching on a `DialectName` such as sqlite/postgres/mysql) that get appended to the `WHERE` clause of the generated query.
