**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE), via an in-process `SSEHub` that broadcasts JSON events to connected browser clients.

**Hub implementation:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (with route wiring in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` and `SSEHub` instantiated/held on `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`)

**Memo-related handlers that broadcast through it** (all in `server/router/api/v1/`, calling `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — `memo_service.go`
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` — `memo_service.go`
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` — `memo_service_comments.go`
- `APIV1Service.UpsertMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go`
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go`
- `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go`) — broadcasts memo-update events, invoked from `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go`), `SetMemoAttachments`-style attachment updates (`memo_attachment_service.go`), and memo-relation updates (`memo_relation_service.go`)
