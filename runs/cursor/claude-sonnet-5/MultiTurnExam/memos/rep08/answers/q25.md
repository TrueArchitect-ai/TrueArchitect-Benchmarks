**Engine + path:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed via `google/cel-go`, implemented in the memo filter engine at `repo/internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `repo/internal/filter/engine.go`; schema in `schema.go`, IR in `ir.go`, dialect rendering in `render.go`, parsing in `parser.go`).

**Mechanism:** The engine parses/validates the CEL `filter` expression against a memo-specific schema into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR), then a per-dialect renderer walks that IR to emit parameterized SQL fragments (with dialect-specific placeholders — `?` for SQLite/MySQL, `$n` for Postgres — and dialect-specific handling of JSON access, boolean semantics, and `LIKE`/`ILIKE`/`JSON_CONTAINS`/`@>` for tag membership) for `sqlite`, `mysql`, or `postgres`.
