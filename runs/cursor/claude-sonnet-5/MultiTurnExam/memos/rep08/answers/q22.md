**Signing mechanism:** [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/)-style HMAC-SHA256 signing. A per-request `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` are generated, an HMAC-SHA256 digest is computed over `msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body` using the webhook's signing secret, and the base64-encoded result is sent as `webhook-signature: v1,<signature>`:
