**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)
**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub` struct with `Broadcast` method)
**Handler names broadcasting through it (in `server/router/api/v1`):**
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` (`memo_service.go`)
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (`memo_service.go`)
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` (`memo_service_comments.go`)
- `APIV1Service.UpsertMemoReaction` (`reaction_service.go`)
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemoReaction` (`reaction_service.go`)
- `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go`) — a shared helper invoked by `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go`), and by the attachment/relation update paths in `memo_attachment_service.go` and `memo_relation_service.go`

The SSE endpoint registration/streaming itself lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, with event construction helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
