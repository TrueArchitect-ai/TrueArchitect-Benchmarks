Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks (all in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`):

- created: `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (called from `CreateMemo` in `memo_service.go`)
- updated: `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (called from `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go`)
- deleted: `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (called from `DeleteMemo` in `memo_service.go`)
- comment created: `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (called from `CreateMemoComment` in `memo_service_comments.go`)

**Delivery convergence:** `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`/`DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`/`DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` all funnel through the shared helper `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go`, line 48), while `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` builds its payload independently — but all four ultimately call `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`) for actual HTTP delivery.
