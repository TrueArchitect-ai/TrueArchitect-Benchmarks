- **Interceptor path**: `web/src/connect.ts` (`authInterceptor`, a Connect `Interceptor` that sets the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header and handles 401-triggered refresh/retry)
- **Token kinds**: JWT **access tokens** (short-lived, 15 minutes, held in memory/JS state) and JWT **refresh tokens** (long-lived, 30 days, delivered via an HTTP-only cookie and used through the `refreshToken` RPC); the backend also supports **Personal Access Tokens (PAT)** for programmatic access
- **Backend package that validates them**: `server/auth` (see `server/auth/token.go` and `server/auth/authenticator.go`)
