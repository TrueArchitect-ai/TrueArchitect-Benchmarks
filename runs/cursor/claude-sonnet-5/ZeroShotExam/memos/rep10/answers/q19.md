`MemoService` is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP+JSON)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108, inside `RegisterGateway`).
2. **Connect (Connect RPC, which also serves gRPC/gRPC-Web over the same handler)** — wired via `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40, inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the handler's per-method wrapping/delegation implemented in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (the `// MemoService` section, lines 304–464).

Both registrations are invoked together from `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
