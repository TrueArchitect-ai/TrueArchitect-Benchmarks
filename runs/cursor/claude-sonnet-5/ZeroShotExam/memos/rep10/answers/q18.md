The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, one per supported database:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` is the factory function; it switches on `profile.Driver` ("sqlite"/"mysql"/"postgres") at startup and calls the corresponding package's `NewDB(profile)` constructor to select the driver.
