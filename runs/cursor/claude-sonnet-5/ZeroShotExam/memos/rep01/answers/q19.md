Both protocols and their registration points are confirmed. MemoService is also used by the MCP router, but that's a consumer of the API, not a separate serving protocol for MemoService itself, so the two serving protocols are grpc-gateway (REST/JSON) and Connect-RPC.

q19.
- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON-over-HTTP)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108, inside `RegisterGateway`), and mounted on the Echo group at `/api/v1/*` (line 128 of the same file).
- **Connect protocol (Connect-RPC, used by browser clients in place of gRPC-Web)** — registered via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40, inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the actual method implementations (wrapping `APIV1Service`'s gRPC-style methods into Connect's `connect.Request`/`connect.Response` types) living in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`'s companion file `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (lines 304–464, under the `// MemoService` section).

So, in the requested "protocol — path" format:

- gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`
- Connect (Connect-RPC) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`

(with the Connect method adapters additionally in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`)
