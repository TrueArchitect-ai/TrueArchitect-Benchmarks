The project ships SQL migrations for: **SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL**

This is evident from the versioned migration directories under `store/migration/`:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.2 through 0.30)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (versions 0.19 through 0.30)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versions 0.21 through 0.30)
