MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108, inside `RegisterGateway`), which mounts the gateway mux at `/api/v1/*`.
2. **Connect protocol** (used by browser clients) — registered via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40), mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` back in `v1.go`. The `ConnectServiceHandler` wrapper that adapts the gRPC service methods to Connect's request/response types lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService methods at lines 304–464).

Answer format:
- gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`
- Connect — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`
