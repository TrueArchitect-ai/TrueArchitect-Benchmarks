q18.

The `store.Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`) has three concrete implementations, each with its constructor in an entry file:

- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`

Factory function: `NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

This function switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) to call the corresponding package's `NewDB(profile)` constructor and return a `store.Driver` at startup.
