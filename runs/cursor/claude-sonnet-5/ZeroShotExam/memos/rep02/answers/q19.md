MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **REST / JSON via gRPC-Gateway** — registered in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (`v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`), mounted on the Echo group at `/api/v1/*`.

2. **Connect protocol** (which itself transports gRPC, gRPC-Web, and Connect-JSON) — wrapped/registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (`apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`), mounted via `connectHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:141` on the Echo group matched by `/memos.api.v1.*`.

Answer format:
- REST/gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`
- Connect (gRPC/gRPC-Web/Connect) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`

(Note: a plain gRPC `RegisterMemoServiceServer` also exists in the generated `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service_grpc.pb.go`, but a search of the codebase shows it is never called, so it isn't one of the two actually-wired protocols.)
