q20. Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) — a long-lived `text/event-stream` HTTP connection served at `/api/v1/sse` (registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, with headers/heartbeat/streaming loop in `handleSSE`), with events defined in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

Hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (the `SSEHub` type, holding subscribed `SSEClient`s and exposing `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`; instantiated as `APIV1Service.SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` and referenced as `server.sseHub` in `server/server.go`).

Handlers that broadcast through it (all call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
- `CreateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`)
- `UpdateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:401`, via the shared `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`)
- `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`)
- `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161`)

Additionally, `SetMemoAttachments` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoRelations` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:48`) also broadcast indirectly, since they too call the shared `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper that performs the `SSEHub.Broadcast`.
