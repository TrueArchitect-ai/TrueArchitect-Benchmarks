MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON-over-HTTP)** — registration happens in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, inside `RegisterGateway`).
2. **Connect (Connect-RPC, for browser clients replacing grpc-web)** — the handler wrapping/registration happens in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the Connect-to-gRPC-type-conversion wrapper methods (e.g. `CreateMemo`, `ListMemos`, etc. on `ConnectServiceHandler`) implemented in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`.
