Enforcement sites for memo visibility (`store.Private` / `store.Protected` / `store.Public`), each independently gating who can see a memo's data:

- Core Memo API (gRPC/Connect `GetMemo`/`ListMemos`/comment reads) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (`checkMemoReadAccess`, lines 39-68, called from `GetMemo`/`ListMemoComments`; `ListMemos` also builds a `VisibilityList` filter, lines 219-225)
- Attachment/file serving — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go` (`checkAttachmentPermission`, lines 649-704: blocks Private attachments to non-creators/non-admins, gates Public/Protected on auth or a valid share token)
- RSS feeds — `server/router/rss/rss.go` (`GetExploreRSS`/`GetUserRSS`, lines 72-159: `store.FindMemo{VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}}` restricts feed contents to public memos only)
- Sitemap generation — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go` (`getSitemapXML`, lines 143-167: lists memos with `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}}`)
- In-app notifications/inbox payloads — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go` (`canViewerAccessMemo`, lines 302-316, used to suppress mention/comment notification content the viewer isn't allowed to see)
- Email notifications — `server/notification/email.go` (`canViewerAccessMemo`, lines 306-320, used before including memo/comment content in outgoing emails)

That's 6 distinct enforcement sites (more than the required 3), each independently reimplementing the Private/Protected/Public rules rather than sharing one central check.
