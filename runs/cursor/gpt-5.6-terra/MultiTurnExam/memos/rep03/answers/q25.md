CEL (`cel-go`) `filter.Engine` — `internal/filter/engine.go`
The engine compiles CEL into a dialect-agnostic condition tree, then renders parameterized SQL via `filter.AppendConditions` for SQLite, MySQL, or PostgreSQL in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`.
