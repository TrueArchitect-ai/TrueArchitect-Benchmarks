CEL-based memo filter `Engine` — `internal/filter/engine.go`

Each driver calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its SQLite, MySQL, or PostgreSQL dialect; the engine parses CEL into a dialect-neutral IR and renders parameterized, dialect-specific SQL fragments (`internal/filter/helpers.go`, `internal/filter/render.go`, `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`).
