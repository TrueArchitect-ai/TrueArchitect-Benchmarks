Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery workers consume an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`.

Actual mechanism: the queue is declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. Four goroutines created in that file’s `init()` consume from it; `PostAsync` enqueues delivery payloads.
