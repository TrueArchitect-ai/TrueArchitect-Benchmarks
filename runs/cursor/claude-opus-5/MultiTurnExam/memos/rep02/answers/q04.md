**APIV1Service.SignIn — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`**

The real implementation lives at line 51 of that file. It handles two credential types: password-based (bcrypt comparison against `user.PasswordHash`, plus a `DisallowPasswordAuth` check) and SSO/OAuth2 (via `resolveSSOIdentity` and `resolveSSOUser`). It rejects archived users, then calls `doSignIn` to mint the access token and set the refresh cookie.
