**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `repo/store/migration/`, which has exactly three dialect subdirectories: `mysql/`, `postgres/`, and `sqlite/` (each with versioned folders like `0.17` … `0.30` plus a `LATEST.sql`). This matches the three driver packages under `repo/store/db/` (`mysql`, `postgres`, `sqlite`), selected in `repo/store/db/db.go`.
