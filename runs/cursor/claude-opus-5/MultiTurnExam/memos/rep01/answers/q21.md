Two clarifications, since `GetUser` is an overloaded name in this repo. `APIV1Service.GetUser` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:171`) and its Connect wrapper are the *RPC* handler, unrelated to the store method — they resolve users through `ResolveUserByName`, which is already in the list. Separately, if you also change the `GetUser` method on the `store.Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`), you'd need to update the three implementations in `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/user.go`, plus `store/db/mysql/user.go:77`, which calls its own driver-level `d.GetUser` inside `UpdateUser`.
