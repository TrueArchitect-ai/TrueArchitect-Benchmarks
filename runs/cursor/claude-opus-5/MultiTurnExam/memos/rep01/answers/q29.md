Refresh itself goes through `AuthService.refreshToken` on a *separate* transport with no interceptors, to avoid recursion, and relies on the cookie because both transports use `fetch` with `credentials: "include"` (lines 57-72). Concurrent refreshes are deduplicated by `tokenRefreshManager` so proactive and reactive refreshes don't race server-side token rotation.

On the server, `auth.Authenticator` validates all three credential kinds — `AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` (JWT), `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` (cookie), and `AuthenticateByPAT` — with `resolveBearer` trying access token then PAT, and `AuthenticateToUser` falling back to the refresh cookie (`server/auth/authenticator.go`; JWT signing/parsing in `server/auth/token.go`, header/cookie parsing in `server/auth/extract.go`).
