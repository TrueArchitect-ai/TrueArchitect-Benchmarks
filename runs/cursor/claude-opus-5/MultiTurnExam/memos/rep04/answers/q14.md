It's a straightforward CRUD resource nested under a user, with no custom methods. As with the other services, `ConnectServiceHandler` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` carries thin Connect adapters that delegate to these methods rather than implementing anything themselves.

Worth noting for context: a `Shortcut` is a named, reusable CEL filter string. The proto comment at lines 15–17 spells out the coupling — a shortcut's `filter` field is passed directly as the `filter` argument to `ListMemos` (q12) to reproduce a saved view, so changes to the memo filter grammar affect stored shortcuts.
