**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, which has exactly three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/`. Each contains versioned folders of incremental `NN__description.sql` files plus a `LATEST.sql` full schema for fresh installs.

This matches the rest of the storage layer: `store/db/` has one driver package per dialect (`store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`), and `store/migrator.go` embeds the whole tree with `//go:embed migration`, resolving files by the path convention `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql` (documented in the comments around line 39).

Coverage differs by dialect, since SQLite goes back to `0.2` while Postgres starts at `0.19` and MySQL at `0.17`, but all three are shipped and currently go up to `0.30`.
