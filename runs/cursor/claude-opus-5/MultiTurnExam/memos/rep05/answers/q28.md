Runners the server actually starts:

**s3presign runner** — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`; triggered by a `time.Ticker` on a 12-hour interval (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup. Started in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go` (lines 162–171) under its own cancellable context, tracked by a `WaitGroup` for graceful shutdown.

**Webhook async dispatch workers** — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; four goroutines started from a package `init()` (line 42), event-driven off the buffered channel `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128) that `PostAsync` feeds. Not scheduled by time, and not registered with the server's runner lifecycle, so they aren't cancelled on shutdown and enqueues are dropped with a warning when the queue is full.

Scheduling infrastructure present but **not** started by the server:

**memopayload runner** — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` defines `Runner`, `NewRunner`, and a batched `RunOnce` that rebuilds every memo's payload, but nothing calls `memopayload.NewRunner` anywhere in the codebase. `server/server.go` doesn't even import the package. Only the standalone helper `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` is used, called inline per-memo by `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, lines 108 and 447).
