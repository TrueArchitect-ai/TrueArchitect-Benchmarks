Note the non-blocking `default` branch: if the queue is saturated the webhook is dropped with a warning rather than backing up the request. Queued payloads are delivered by `Post` (line 120), which JSON-encodes the body, signs it with HMAC-SHA256 into the Standard Webhooks `webhook-id`/`webhook-timestamp`/`webhook-signature` headers, and POSTs through the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.
