`ShortcutService` has **5 RPCs**, declared in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`:

- `ListShortcuts` — `GET /api/v1/{parent=users/*}/shortcuts`
- `GetShortcut` — `GET /api/v1/{name=users/*/shortcuts/*}`
- `CreateShortcut` — `POST /api/v1/{parent=users/*}/shortcuts`
- `UpdateShortcut` — `PATCH /api/v1/{shortcut.name=users/*/shortcuts/*}`
- `DeleteShortcut` — `DELETE /api/v1/{name=users/*/shortcuts/*}`

**Implementing file: `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`**

All five are methods on `APIV1Service` in that one file, at lines 49, 95, 135, 211, and 284 respectively. The Connect-protocol wrappers for them live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`, but those just delegate to these methods.

For context on what a shortcut is: each one is a named, reusable CEL filter string, and the proto comments note that the `filter` field can be passed straight to the `ListMemos` `filter` argument to reproduce a saved view. Shortcuts are modeled as a child resource of a user, which is why every path is nested under `users/*`.
