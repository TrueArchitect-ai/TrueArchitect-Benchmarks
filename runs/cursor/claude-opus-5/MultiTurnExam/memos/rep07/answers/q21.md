Two notes on scope. `APIV1Service.fetchCurrentUser` is the highest-leverage caller — it's the shared "who is the current user" helper invoked throughout the v1 handlers, so a behavioral change there propagates widely even though it's one call site. And be careful not to conflate this with the same-named but unrelated `APIV1Service.GetUser` RPC handler or the generated protobuf `GetUser()` accessors; the test files above contain many `ts.Service.GetUser(...)` and `currentUser.GetUser()` calls that are *not* affected by a `Store.GetUser` signature change. I excluded those, along with the distinct store methods `GetUserSetting`, `GetUserIdentity`, and `GetUserStats`.

Since every current caller passes a `*FindUser` struct, adding a field to `FindUser` would be source-compatible and require no call-site edits; changing the parameter list or return types is what forces the updates above.
