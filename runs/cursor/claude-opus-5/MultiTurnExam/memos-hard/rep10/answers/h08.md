Exactly two types declare `RunOnce`, and only one of them is reachable from production code.

`*s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:43`) — yes — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:163`, plus a self-call from `(*Runner).Run` on each ticker tick at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:36`
`*memopayload.Runner` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27`) — no — no caller anywhere in the repository

Evidence and detail:

**The invoked one.** `s3presign.Runner.RunOnce` is a thin wrapper that delegates to `CheckAndPresign`. It is called twice on the live path: synchronously at startup (`server/server.go:163`, right after `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` on line 162), and then every 12 hours from inside `Run`'s ticker loop.

**The dead one.** `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` rebuilds the payload (tags and properties) of every memo in batches of 100. Nothing constructs the runner: `memopayload.NewRunner` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:19`) has zero call sites — a repo-wide search for `NewRunner` returns only its own declaration plus the `s3presign` pair at `server/server.go:162` and `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:20`. The `memopayload` package *is* imported in production, but only for its package-level helper `RebuildMemoPayload`, used by `CreateMemo` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and by `UpdateMemo` at line 447. That helper is a plain function, not a method on `Runner`, so the import does not exercise `RunOnce`.

**Tests don't change the picture.** `server/runner/memopayload/` contains only `runner.go` with no test file, and the one test in the other package, `server/runner/s3presign/runner_test.go`, doesn't reference `RunOnce` or `CheckAndPresign` at all. So `memopayload.Runner` and its `RunOnce` are unreferenced from both production and test code — effectively a leftover backfill utility.
