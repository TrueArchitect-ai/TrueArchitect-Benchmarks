**h08.**

Two types declare `RunOnce`, and only the S3 presign one is actually called in production — the memo-payload runner is dead code.

```
*s3presign.Runner (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:43) — yes — server/server.go:163 (Server.startBackgroundRunners), plus its own ticker loop at server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:36
*memopayload.Runner (server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27) — no — no caller anywhere (production or test)
```

Evidence:

- A repo-wide search for `RunOnce` returns exactly five hits: the two method declarations above, plus three references. Two of the references belong to `s3presign` — the synchronous startup call `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163` inside `startBackgroundRunners`, and the self-call from the 12-hour ticker in `Run` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:36`.

- Nothing references `*memopayload.Runner` at all: searching for `memopayload.NewRunner` and `memopayload.Runner` yields no results. The package *is* imported by `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:18`, but only for the standalone function `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload`, used at `memo_service.go:108` (memo creation) and `memo_service.go:447` (memo update). Since `RunOnce` is a method on `*Runner` and the `Runner` value is never constructed, that method is unreachable.

So `memopayload.Runner`, its `NewRunner` constructor, and its `RunOnce` (a batched full-table payload rebuild, 100 memos at a time) are orphaned — presumably a leftover backfill job. The reusable part of that file, `RebuildMemoPayload`, survived and is still on the hot write path.
