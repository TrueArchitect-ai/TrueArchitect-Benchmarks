Two structural notes. The sites split into two kinds: *post-fetch gates* that reject a specific memo (the `checkMemoReadAccess` family, reaction service, file server) and *query scoping* that never loads invisible rows in the first place (`ListMemos`, stats, RSS, frontend). The scoping form is what protects list endpoints, so there is no per-item check to find there.

The canonical policy lives in `checkMemoReadAccess` (`memo_service.go:39-68`): archived memos are visible only to their creator; non-public memos require authentication; private memos require being the creator. Other surfaces re-implement rather than reuse it, and they differ deliberately — the file server additionally accepts a valid, non-expired `share_token` for the specific memo (`fileserver.go:684-689`) and treats admins as permitted (`:699`), whereas `checkMemoReadAccess` grants no admin bypass for private memos. `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:137` is the related share-token redemption path.
