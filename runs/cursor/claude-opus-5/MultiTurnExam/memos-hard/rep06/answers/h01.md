**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (plus one immediate pass at startup) — wired at `server/server.go:162-171`, invoked from `server/server.go:129`**

The component is the S3 presign runner defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its job is to keep presigned S3 URLs for attachments from expiring: it pages through attachments whose storage type is `AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-presigns the object via `s3.NewClient(...).PresignGetObject(...)`, then writes the fresh URL back through `store.UpdateAttachment` along with an updated `LastPresignedTime`.

The schedule has two parts:

- The interval is a `time.Ticker` at `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driving `RunOnce` in the loop at lines 29-41.
- Independently, each attachment is skipped unless its presigned URL is within a day of expiry — the check at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91` skips anything presigned less than 4 days ago, since URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration. So the 12-hour tick is just a polling cadence; actual re-signing happens roughly every 4 days per attachment.

Inside `startBackgroundRunners`, the runner is constructed at `server/server.go:162`, then run **synchronously once** via `RunOnce(ctx)` at line 163 so links are refreshed immediately on boot, and only afterward started in a goroutine at lines 166-171 for the recurring loop.
