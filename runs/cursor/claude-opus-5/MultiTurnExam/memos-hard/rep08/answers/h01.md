**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired at `server/server.go:163` (immediate `RunOnce`) and `server/server.go:169` (the recurring goroutine), inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which `Server.Start` calls at `server/server.go:129`.**

How it works:

- The component is `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (lines 16–24), constructed with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`.
- The schedule is a `time.Ticker` with `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, declared at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` and consumed by `Run` (lines 29–41). `Run` blocks on the ticker and calls `RunOnce` on each tick until its context is cancelled.
- The work itself is `CheckAndPresign` (line 47): it pages through attachments with `StorageType = AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, and for each S3 object re-issues a presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the new URL into the attachment's `Reference` along with an updated `LastPresignedTime`.
- The staleness margin is what keeps links alive: presigned URLs are created with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77`), and the runner skips any object whose `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`). So each URL gets refreshed in the 1-day window before it expires.

On the wiring side, `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`) gives the runner its own cancellable context, registers the cancel function in `s.backgroundRunnerCancels`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously so links are refreshed immediately at boot rather than 12 hours later, and then launches `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `s.backgroundRunnerWG`. `Shutdown` stops it through `stopBackgroundRunners`/`waitBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:140-143`).
