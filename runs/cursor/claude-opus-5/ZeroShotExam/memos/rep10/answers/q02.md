**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migration files are embedded from `store/migration`, which has exactly three driver subdirectories, each holding a `LATEST.sql` full schema plus per-version incremental scripts:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.2 through 0.30, the longest history)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (0.17 through 0.30)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (0.19 through 0.30)
