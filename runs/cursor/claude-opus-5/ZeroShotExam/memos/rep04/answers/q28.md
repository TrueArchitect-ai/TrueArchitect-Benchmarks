**Runners the server actually starts** — the only registered background runner is in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — one immediate synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup, then a goroutine running `Run(s3Context)` driven by `time.NewTicker(12 * time.Hour)` (`runnerInterval`, line 27). Its context is cancelled on shutdown via `backgroundRunnerCancels`, and `backgroundRunnerWG` waits for it.

Other long-lived goroutines exist in the process, but none are started by `startBackgroundRunners`:

- **HTTP serve goroutine** (`server/server.go:124`) — event-driven by the listener, not a scheduled worker.
- **Cache cleanup loops, 3 of them** (`store/cache/cache.go:83` `go c.cleanupLoop()`) — started implicitly by `cache.New` for `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` in `store.New` (`store/store.go:47-53`), driven by `time.NewTicker(CleanupInterval)` = 5 minutes; stopped by `Store.Close` (`store/store.go:72-79`).
- **Webhook dispatch pool, 4 goroutines** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`) — started by package `init()`, driven by the `asyncPostQueue` channel (queue-driven, not time-driven).
- **Email send pool, 2 goroutines** (`internal/email/email.go:14-30`) — also package `init()`, driven by the `asyncEmailQueue` channel.
- **SSE heartbeat** (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`) — per-connection ticker at 30s, lives for the request, not a shared worker. `SSEHub` itself (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) has no loop; the server only calls `Close()` on it at shutdown.
- **PAT last-used update** (`server/auth/authenticator.go:179-185`) — fire-and-forget goroutine per authenticated request.

**Unused scheduling infrastructure**

- `internal/scheduler` is a complete cron scheduler (cron parser, jobs, middleware, timezone support, graceful shutdown, plus `README.md` and `doc.go`), and it is **never used by the server**. The only files importing it are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`); nothing in `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/` references it. In particular, the s3presign runner rolls its own `time.Ticker` instead of registering a scheduler job.
- Related dead runner: `memopayload.Runner` / its `RunOnce` batch backfill (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) is never constructed or scheduled anywhere. Only the standalone helper `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` is called, inline during memo create/update (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`).
