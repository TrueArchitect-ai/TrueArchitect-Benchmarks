MemoService is served over **REST/JSON via gRPC-Gateway** and over **Connect RPC** (connectrpc.com/connect). There is no native gRPC listener — `grep` finds no `grpc.NewServer` or `RegisterMemoServiceServer` call anywhere outside generated code; the gRPC service implementation on `APIV1Service` is reused by both transports.

- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON, `/api/v1/*`) — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**: `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` binds the service directly to the gateway mux (in-process, no gRPC dial), and the mux is mounted on Echo.

The wrapping itself lives in two places: `ConnectServiceHandler` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` embeds `*APIV1Service`, and `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService block starts at line 304) adapts each gRPC method to Connect's `connect.Request`/`connect.Response` types, translating errors through `convertGRPCError`.
