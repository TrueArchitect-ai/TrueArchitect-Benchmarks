**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over plain HTTP — no WebSockets. The endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered on the Echo gateway group alongside the rest of `/api/v1` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`). It authenticates via a Bearer token in the `Authorization` header, sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` with `Cache-Control: no-cache` and `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes an initial `: connected` comment, and then sends `data: {...}\n\n` frames plus a `: heartbeat` comment every 30 seconds (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`). The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`, mounted once in `web/src/main.tsx`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` defines `SSEHub` (a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`) with `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Close`, and `Broadcast`. Each client gets a 32-event buffered channel and events are dropped rather than blocking a slow consumer. `Broadcast` filters per client via `canReceive`, so private memos only reach the creator or an admin. The hub is constructed as the `SSEHub` field of `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:36,57`.
