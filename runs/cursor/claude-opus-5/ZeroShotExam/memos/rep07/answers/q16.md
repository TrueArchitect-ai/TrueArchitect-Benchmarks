`Store.DeleteMemo` deletes two kinds of dependent records before removing the memo row:

- **Memo relation records (`memo_relation`)** — deleted twice, once for rows where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and once for rows where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`).
- **Attachments** — every attachment linked to the memo is listed and deleted one by one via `Store.DeleteAttachment`, which also removes the underlying stored file/object (local disk or S3) in addition to the DB row.
