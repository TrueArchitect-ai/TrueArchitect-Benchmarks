Delivery convergence: every one of them builds a `webhook.WebhookRequestPayload` via `convertMemoToWebhookPayload` and hands it to `webhook.PostAsync` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`. `PostAsync` enqueues onto the buffered `asyncPostQueue` channel (capacity 128, dropping with a warning when full), and four worker goroutines started in that file's `init` drain the queue and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which does the actual signed HTTP POST through the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.
