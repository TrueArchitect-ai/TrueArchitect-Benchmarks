q02. SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, which has exactly three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/`. Each contains a full-schema `LATEST.sql` for fresh installs plus per-version folders (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/0.30/`) of incremental `NN__description.sql` files, matching the layout documented in `store/migrator.go` (`store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, embedded via `//go:embed migration`).

The same three dialects are the only ones the driver factory accepts in `store/db/db.go`, which dispatches on `profile.Driver` to `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, or `store/db/postgres` and errors out otherwise. Note that seed data is SQLite-only (`store/seed/sqlite/`), but that's demo seeding rather than migrations.
