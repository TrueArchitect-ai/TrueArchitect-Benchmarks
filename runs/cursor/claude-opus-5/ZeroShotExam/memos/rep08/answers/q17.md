`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`; `memoServiceClient`

That hook module is the single place where memo CRUD lives: `useMemos`/`useInfiniteMemos`/`useMemo` for reads, plus `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, and `useDeleteMemo` for writes (along with `useMemoComments`, `useInfiniteMemoComments`, and `useLinkMetadata`). Every one of them calls `memoServiceClient` imported from `@/connect`.
