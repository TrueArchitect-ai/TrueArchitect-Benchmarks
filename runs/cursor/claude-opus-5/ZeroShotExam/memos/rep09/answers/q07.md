`web/src/connect.ts`

This file is the single place where the ConnectRPC transport and all service clients are constructed. It creates the main transport via `createConnectTransport` (with `window.location.origin` as the base URL, binary format, a credentialed fetch, and an auth interceptor), then exports one client per service with `createClient` — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`.
