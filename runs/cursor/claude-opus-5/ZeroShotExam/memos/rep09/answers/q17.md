`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`; `memoServiceClient`

That module holds the memo CRUD hooks — `useMemos`/`useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo`, `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo`, plus comment and link-metadata queries — all keyed through the `memoKeys` factory for cache management.

The client it calls is `memoServiceClient`, imported from `@/connect`. It's defined in `web/src/connect.ts` as `createClient(MemoService, transport)`, where `MemoService` comes from the generated `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb` and `transport` is the shared Connect-web transport with the auth/token-refresh interceptor.
