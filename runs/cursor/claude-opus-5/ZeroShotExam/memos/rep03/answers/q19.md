MemoService is exposed over **gRPC-Gateway REST/HTTP-JSON** and **Connect RPC**. Both are wired up inside `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` and mounted on the same Echo server, but through separate muxes:

- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP+JSON under `/api/v1/*`)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at line 108 registers the service directly on the grpc-gateway `ServeMux` (in-process, no gRPC dial), and that mux is served at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (lines 128–129). The generated gateway code lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

- **Connect RPC (under `/memos.api.v1.*`)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` at line 40 in `RegisterConnectHandlers`, invoked from `v1.go` lines 140–144 where the Connect mux is mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*`. The `ConnectServiceHandler` wrapper methods that adapt Connect request/response envelopes to the plain gRPC implementations are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService block starts at line 304), and the generated constructor is in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go` (line 461).

Both paths delegate to the same `APIV1Service` methods, so there is one business-logic implementation; note that native gRPC itself is *not* served — there is no `grpc.NewServer`/`RegisterMemoServiceServer` call anywhere outside the generated `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service_grpc.pb.go`.
