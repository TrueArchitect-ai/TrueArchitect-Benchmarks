`web/src/connect.ts`

That single file builds the ConnectRPC transport and exports every service client. Under the "Transport & Service Clients" section it creates the transport with `createConnectTransport` (pointed at `window.location.origin`, binary format, credential-aware fetch, plus an auth interceptor) and then instantiates the clients with `createClient`: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts` lines 184–203). There is also a separate `refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient` pair earlier in the same file (lines 65–72) used for token refresh, which avoids the interceptor loop.
