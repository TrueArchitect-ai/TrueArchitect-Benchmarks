**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — runs once at startup then every 12 hours — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

How it works:

- The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its interval is the package constant `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), and `Run` drives a `time.Ticker` on that interval until its context is cancelled.
- `CheckAndPresign` pages through S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 via `store.ListAttachments` filtered by `AttachmentStorageType_S3`. For each attachment it re-presigns the object key with `s3.Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the new URL into the attachment's `Reference` and stamps `LastPresignedTime` through `store.UpdateAttachment`.
- Staleness is avoided by the margin between two numbers: presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), and the runner skips any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:83-89`), so every URL is refreshed with roughly a day to spare.
- At startup the server calls `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously first (`server/server.go:163`) so links are fresh immediately, then launches `Run` on a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` with its own cancellable context, which `Shutdown` cancels via `stopBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:176`).
