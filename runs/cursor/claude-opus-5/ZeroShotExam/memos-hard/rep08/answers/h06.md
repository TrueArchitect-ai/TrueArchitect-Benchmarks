The URL is user-supplied through the webhook management RPCs `CreateUserWebhook` / `UpdateUserWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`), which validate it with `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`); those handlers store the URL but do not themselves make an outbound request. Egress is additionally constrained at dial time by `safeDialContext`, which rejects reserved/private IPs.
