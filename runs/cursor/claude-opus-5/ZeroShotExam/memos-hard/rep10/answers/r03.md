The premise doesn't hold: this repository has no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled in the cron scheduler, so there's no scheduled time to report.

What actually exists:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a standalone, self-contained cron scheduler library (parser, job, middleware, scheduler). Nothing in the application imports it — searching all Go files outside that directory for `internal/scheduler` returns no hits, and `server/server.go` never constructs a scheduler. So no jobs of any kind are registered with it at runtime.
- The "2 AM cleanup" you may be thinking of appears only in illustrative documentation and example code, not in a wired-up job. `internal/scheduler/README.md` (lines 33-40) shows a quick-start snippet with `Name: "daily-cleanup", Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`, `internal/scheduler/doc.go` (lines 14-18) repeats it, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go` (lines 97-106) registers a job named `cleanup` on `0 2 * * *` whose handler just prints "Cleaning up old data...". These are demos of the library's API; none touch the database.
- The only real recurring background work in the server is the S3 presign refresh runner, and it is interval-based rather than cron-scheduled: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` uses a `time.Ticker` with `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, started from `server/server.go` (lines 162-169) with one immediate `RunOnce` at startup. The other runner, `server/runner/memopayload`, is invoked on demand from the memo service, not on a schedule.
