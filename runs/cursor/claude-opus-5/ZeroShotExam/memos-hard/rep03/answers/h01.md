How it works:

- The runner refreshes presigned S3 URLs for attachments whose blobs live in S3 rather than the database. `CheckAndPresign` pages through `store.ListAttachments` in batches of 100, filtered to `AttachmentStorageType_S3`, and for each attachment re-presigns the object key and writes the new URL back into the attachment's `Reference` field along with an updated `LastPresignedTime`.
