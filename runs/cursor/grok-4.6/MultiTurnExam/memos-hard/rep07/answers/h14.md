`memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) via `markdown.Service.ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go`); `CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` when `content` is in the update mask (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); tags plus `MemoPayload.Property` (`has_link`, `has_task_list`, `has_code`, `has_incomplete_tasks`, `title`)

`RebuildMemoPayload` writes `memo.Payload.Tags` and `memo.Payload.Property` from a single goldmark pass (`ExtractAll`). Create always rebuilds; update rebuilds only on a content change. `ExtractAll` also collects mentions, but those are not stored on the payload (they are used separately for notifications). Location is user-set, not derived. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild all memos but is not started in production.
