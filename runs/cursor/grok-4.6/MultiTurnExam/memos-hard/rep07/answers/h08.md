h08.
`s3presign.Runner` — yes — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go`) and `Runner.Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)  
`memopayload.Runner` — no — none

Only those two types declare `RunOnce`. Production constructs `s3presign.Runner` in `startBackgroundRunners` and calls `RunOnce` at startup, then again from the 12-hour ticker in `Run`. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` is never called; production uses `RebuildMemoPayload` from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` instead.
