Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. Webhook workers read an in-process Go channel, not a message broker.

`asyncPostQueue` is `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. `init()` starts 4 goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post`. `PostAsync` enqueues; if the buffer is full the payload is dropped. There is no broker, queue declaration, or NATS/AMQP dependency.
