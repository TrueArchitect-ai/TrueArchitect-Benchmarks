Derived list/preview metadata is `storepb.MemoPayload` (`proto/store/memo.proto`): tags and computed `Property`, not user-set `Location`. `RebuildMemoPayload` parses content with `ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go`) and writes `Tags` and `Property`. It runs on create (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`) and on update when the mask includes `content` (`:447`). `CreateMemoComment` goes through `CreateMemo`. `Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild all memos but is not started with the server. `ExtractAll` also collects `@mentions`; those are not stored on the payload (they drive mention notifications).
