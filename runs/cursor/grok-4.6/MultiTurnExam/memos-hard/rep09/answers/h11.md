Persistent entities live in `store/` (`User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, …). Counting distinct Go packages **outside** `store/` (including `store/db/*`) that mention `store.<Type>`:

Including tests: **User** in 7 packages (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`); **Memo** in 6 (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`).

Production-only: **User** 5–6, **Memo** 4. Third is `Attachment` (~3). `IdentityProvider` / `InstanceSetting` / `UserSetting` are used outside store as `storepb.*`, not `store.*`.
