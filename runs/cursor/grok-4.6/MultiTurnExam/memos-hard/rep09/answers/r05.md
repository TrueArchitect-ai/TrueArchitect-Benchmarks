There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is Protobuf/Connect (plus gRPC-Gateway HTTP).

Service definitions live in `proto/api/v1/*.proto` (`memo_service.proto`, `user_service.proto`, `auth_service.proto`, and the rest). Generated Connect handlers are registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`; gRPC-Gateway is wired in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (`RegisterGateway`). No `.graphql` files or GraphQL libraries are in the repo.
