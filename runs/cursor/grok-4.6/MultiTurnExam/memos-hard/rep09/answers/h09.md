Eight `Close` methods exist:

- `SSEHub.Close` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
- `Store.Close` — `store/store.go`
- `Cache.Close` — `store/cache/cache.go`
- `sqlite.DB.Close` — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `mysql.DB.Close` — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `postgres.DB.Close` — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `MemoFilterTestContext.Close` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`
- `AttachmentFilterTestContext.Close` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`

Non-test server shutdown calls `s.sseHub.Close()` (`server/server.go`, `closeLongLivedConnections`) and `s.Store.Close()` (`server/server.go`, `Shutdown`). `Store.Close` then calls `Cache.Close` on the three caches and `s.driver.Close()`, which is the active driver’s `DB.Close`. The two test-context `Close` methods are not used by production server code. (`Driver` in `store/driver.go` only declares the interface method, not an implementation.)
