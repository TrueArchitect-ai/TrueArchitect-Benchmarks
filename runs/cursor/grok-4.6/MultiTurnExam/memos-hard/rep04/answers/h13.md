markdown.service — NewAPIV1Service via markdown.NewService (server/router/api/v1/v1.go); holders APIV1Service and RSSService

`markdown.Service` has one impl, the unexported `service` in `internal/markdown/markdown.go`. Production constructs it in `NewAPIV1Service` with `WithTagExtension` and `WithMentionExtension`. `Server.NewServer` passes that same instance into `rss.NewRSSService`. `memopayload.Runner` also holds `markdown.Service`, but `NewRunner` is unused at runtime; memo create/update call `RebuildMemoPayload` with `APIV1Service.MarkdownService`.
