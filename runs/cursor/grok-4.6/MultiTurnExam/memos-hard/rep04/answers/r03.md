No nightly database-cleanup job is scheduled. `internal/scheduler` is a library only—never constructed or started from `cmd/` or `server/`. Docs/examples mention `"0 2 * * *"` (`internal/scheduler/doc.go`) and `"0 3 * * *"` stale-session cleanup (`internal/scheduler/README.md`); those are not registered. The only production background runner is S3 presign on a 12-hour ticker (`server/server.go`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`).
