h14.
memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload (server/runner/memopayload/runner.go) via markdown.Service.ExtractAll; CreateMemo always, UpdateMemo when content changes; tags plus Property (has_link, has_task_list, has_code, has_incomplete_tasks, title)

Create and content-updating `UpdateMemo` call `RebuildMemoPayload`, which parses memo content once and writes `Payload.Tags` and `Payload.Property`. `ExtractAll` also collects `@mentions`, but those are not stored on the payload (mentions drive notifications in `memo_mention_helpers.go`). Location is user-set, not derived. `Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild all memos but is not started in production.
