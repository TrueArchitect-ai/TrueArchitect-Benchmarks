h03.
internal/idp; internal/idp/oauth2

The provider abstraction lives in `internal/idp` (`IdentityProviderUserInfo` in `internal/idp/idp.go`). The only concrete protocol that ships is OAuth2 in `internal/idp/oauth2` (`IdentityProvider` with `ExchangeToken` and `UserInfo`). Store/API config also only has `OAuth2Config`.
