memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload → markdown.ExtractAll; CreateMemo and UpdateMemo (content); tags plus Property (title, has_link, has_code, has_task_list, has_incomplete_tasks)

Derived list/preview metadata lives on `storepb.MemoPayload` (`proto/store/memo.proto`). It is rebuilt by `RebuildMemoPayload` in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`, which calls `markdown.Service.ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go`). Production triggers are `CreateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`) and `UpdateMemo` when the update mask includes `content` (`:447`). The persistable extract is `#tags` and `Property`: first H1 `title`, `has_link`, `has_code`, `has_task_list`, `has_incomplete_tasks`. `ExtractAll` also collects `@mentions`, but `RebuildMemoPayload` does not store them on the payload. `Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild all memos but is not started with the server.
