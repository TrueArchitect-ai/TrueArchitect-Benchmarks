Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used; there is no external message broker.

Webhook workers consume an in-process Go channel declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. `init()` starts 4 goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post`. `PostAsync` enqueues; if the buffer is full the payload is dropped. No NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or other broker appears in the backend.
