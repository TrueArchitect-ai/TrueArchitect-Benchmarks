`s3presign.Runner.RunOnce` is declared in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` and is called from production at `server/server.go` (startup) and again from that runner’s `Run` ticker. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` is declared in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` but nothing in production or tests calls it; callers use `RebuildMemoPayload` instead. No other `RunOnce` methods exist.
