r03.
There is no nightly database-cleanup job and no cron schedule for one in production.

`internal/scheduler` is a library only. It is never constructed or started from `server/` or `cmd/`. The “daily-cleanup” / `0 2 * * *` snippet in `internal/scheduler/doc.go` is example docs, not a registered job. The only background runner wired at startup is S3 URL re-presign on a 12-hour ticker (`server/server.go`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). Cache cleanup is an in-process interval (`CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute` in `store/store.go`), not a nightly DB job.
