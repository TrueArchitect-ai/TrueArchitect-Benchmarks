There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is defined in Protocol Buffers and served as Connect RPC plus gRPC-Gateway REST.

Service contracts live under `proto/api/v1/` (`memo_service.proto`, `user_service.proto`, `auth_service.proto`, and the other `*_service.proto` files). `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` mounts gRPC-Gateway on `/api/v1` and Connect handlers on `/memos.api.v1.*`. Generated OpenAPI is at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml`. There are no `.graphql` files or GraphQL libraries in the repo.
