Neither. There is no NATS or RabbitMQ (or any message broker). Webhook workers consume an in-process Go channel.

`asyncPostQueue` is a buffered `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` (size 128) declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. `init()` there starts 4 goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post`. `PostAsync` enqueues; if the channel is full the payload is dropped. No queue is declared in a broker.
