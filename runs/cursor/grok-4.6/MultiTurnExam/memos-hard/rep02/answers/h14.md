h14.
memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload (via markdown.Service.ExtractAll) — server/runner/memopayload/runner.go, internal/markdown/markdown.go; CreateMemo and UpdateMemo when content changes; tags plus Property (title, has_link, has_code, has_task_list, has_incomplete_tasks)

`RebuildMemoPayload` calls `ExtractAll` and writes `Memo.Payload.Tags` and `Memo.Payload.Property` (`proto/store/memo.proto`). `CreateMemo` always rebuilds before insert (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); `UpdateMemo` rebuilds only when the update mask includes `content`. `CreateMemoComment` goes through `CreateMemo`, so it rebuilds too. `Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild every memo but is not started with the server. Location is user-set and left alone. `ExtractAll` also finds `@mentions`; those are not stored on the payload (used later for notifications in `memo_mention_helpers.go`).
