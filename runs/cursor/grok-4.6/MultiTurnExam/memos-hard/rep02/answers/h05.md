On user delete, each driver runs `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` (reactions on the user’s memos) and `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = …`). Example: `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go` around the `deleteUserTargetsTx` calls and those two helpers. The `reaction` table in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (same shape in mysql/postgres) has `creator_id` but no `FOREIGN KEY` / `ON DELETE CASCADE` to `user`. `store/reaction.go` only deletes a single reaction by ID.
