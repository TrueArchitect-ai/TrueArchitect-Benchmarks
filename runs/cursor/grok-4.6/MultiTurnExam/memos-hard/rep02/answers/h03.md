h03.
internal/idp; internal/idp/oauth2

`internal/idp` is the provider abstraction (`IdentityProviderUserInfo` in `internal/idp/idp.go`). The only concrete protocol is OAuth2 in `internal/idp/oauth2` (`IdentityProvider` in `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`). The store proto (`proto/store/idp.proto`) likewise only defines `OAUTH2`.
