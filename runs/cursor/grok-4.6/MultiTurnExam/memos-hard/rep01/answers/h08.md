Two types declare `RunOnce`: `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` and `memopayload.Runner` in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`. Only the S3 presign runner is called in non-test code (once at startup, then from its 12-hour ticker). `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` is unused; production uses `RebuildMemoPayload` instead.
