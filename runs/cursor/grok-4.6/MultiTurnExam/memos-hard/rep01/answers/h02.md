webhook.Post — internal/webhook/webhook.go; reserved-CIDR SSRF check (isReservedIP / ValidateURL / safeDialContext) — internal/webhook/validate.go

Outbound HTTP delivery runs in `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`), which `PostAsync` workers call. Internal targets are blocked by `reservedCIDRs` / `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go`: `ValidateURL` rejects those hosts when a webhook is configured, and `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` rejects them again at dial time (including DNS rebinding).
