There is no nightly database-cleanup job and no cron schedule for one.

`internal/scheduler` can parse cron expressions, but `cmd/memos` and `server/server.go` never construct or start a `Scheduler`. The only production background runner is S3 URL refresh on a 12-hour `time.Ticker` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), not a DB cleanup. In-process cache expiry uses a 5-minute ticker (`store/cache/cache.go`), not cron.
