memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload → markdown.ExtractAll (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`, `internal/markdown/markdown.go`); CreateMemo and UpdateMemo when `content` is in the update mask (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); tags plus Property (title, has_link, has_task_list, has_code, has_incomplete_tasks)

`RebuildMemoPayload` parses memo content and writes `MemoPayload.Tags` and `MemoPayload.Property` (see `proto/store/memo.proto`). Those flags/tags are what list/filter/stats/preview paths read. Mentions are also parsed by `ExtractAll` but are not stored on the payload. Location is user-set, not derived. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` would rebuild every memo’s payload, but that runner is not started in production.
