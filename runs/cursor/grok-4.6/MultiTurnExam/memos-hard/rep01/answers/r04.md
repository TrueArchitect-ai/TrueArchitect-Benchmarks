Neither. There is no NATS or RabbitMQ broker, and no external queue is declared.

Webhook workers consume an in-process Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. `init()` starts 4 goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post`. Producers (`PostAsync`, used from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`) enqueue on the same channel; if it is full the dispatch is dropped.
