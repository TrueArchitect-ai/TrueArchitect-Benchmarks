memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload → markdown.ExtractAll (server/runner/memopayload/runner.go, internal/markdown/markdown.go); CreateMemo and UpdateMemo when content changes; tags plus Property (title, has_link, has_code, has_task_list, has_incomplete_tasks)

`RebuildMemoPayload` calls `ExtractAll` and writes `memo.Payload.Tags` and `memo.Payload.Property` (`proto/store/memo.proto`). Create always rebuilds (`memo_service.go`); update rebuilds only if the mask includes `content`. Comments go through `CreateMemo`. Extracted fields: `#tags`; first H1 as `title`; `has_link`, `has_code`, `has_task_list`, `has_incomplete_tasks`. Mentions are parsed but not stored on the payload. Location is user-set, not derived.
