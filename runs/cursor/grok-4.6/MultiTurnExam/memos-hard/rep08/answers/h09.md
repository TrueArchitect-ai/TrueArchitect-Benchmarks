Eight `Close` methods: `SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`), `Cache` (`store/cache/cache.go`), `Store` (`store/store.go`), `sqlite.DB` / `postgres.DB` / `mysql.DB` (`store/db/*/sqlite.go|postgres.go|mysql.go`), plus test-only `MemoFilterTestContext` and `AttachmentFilterTestContext` (`store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`). Server shutdown calls `Store.Close` and `sseHub.Close`; `Store.Close` then closes the three caches and the selected DB driver. The two test-context methods are not used by non-test server code.
