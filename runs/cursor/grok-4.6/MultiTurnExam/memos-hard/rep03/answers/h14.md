`RebuildMemoPayload` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) via `ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go`); `CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` when `content` is in the update mask (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); tags plus `Property` (`has_link`, `has_task_list`, `has_code`, `has_incomplete_tasks`, `title`)

Memo `Payload` (`proto/store/memo.proto`) is derived from markdown, not user-edited. `RebuildMemoPayload` parses content with goldmark and writes `Tags` and `Property`. That runs on create and on content updates. `ExtractAll` also finds `@mentions`, but those are not stored on the payload (they feed mention notifications). `Location` on the same payload is user-set, not computed. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` can rebuild all memos but is not started in production.
