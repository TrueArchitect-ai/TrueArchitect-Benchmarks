`CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`; `PostAsync` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`

Those RPC handlers dispatch memo webhooks (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, `memo_service_comments.go`, `memo_attachment_service.go`, `memo_relation_service.go`). All of them end in `webhook.PostAsync`, which queues the POST to the user-configured URL; workers then call `Post` for the actual request.
