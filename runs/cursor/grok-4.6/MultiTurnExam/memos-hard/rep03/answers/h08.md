`s3presign.Runner` — yes — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go`) and `s3presign.Runner.Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)  
`memopayload.Runner` — no — none

Two types define `RunOnce`: `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and `memopayload.Runner` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`). Only the S3 presign method is called in non-test code—once at startup and again from the 12-hour ticker in `Run`. `memopayload.NewRunner` is never used; production only calls `RebuildMemoPayload`.
