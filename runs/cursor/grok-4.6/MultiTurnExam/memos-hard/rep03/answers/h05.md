On user delete, each driver runs `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = …` (the user’s own reactions) and `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN …` (reactions on that user’s memos). The `reaction` table only has `creator_id INTEGER NOT NULL` plus a uniqueness constraint—no foreign key or `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
