h03.
`internal/idp`; `internal/idp/oauth2`

The shared provider types live in `internal/idp` (`IdentityProviderUserInfo` in `internal/idp/idp.go`). The only shipped protocol is OAuth2, in `internal/idp/oauth2` (`IdentityProvider` in `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`). Store/API protos only define `OAUTH2` as a provider type.
