The `markdown.Service` interface is implemented only by the unexported `service` in `internal/markdown/markdown.go`. Production builds it once with `WithTagExtension` and `WithMentionExtension`, stores it on `APIV1Service`, and hands that same value to RSS. Memo create/update call `RebuildMemoPayload` with `s.MarkdownService`; they do not construct a second impl.
