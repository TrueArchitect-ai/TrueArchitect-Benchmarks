There is no nightly database-cleanup cron job. `internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library with a `"daily-cleanup"` example in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`; nothing in `cmd/` or `server/` constructs a scheduler or registers jobs. The only background runner is S3 URL re-presign on a 12-hour ticker (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`, started from `server/server.go`). Cache expiry cleanup is in-process every 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go`), not a DB job.
