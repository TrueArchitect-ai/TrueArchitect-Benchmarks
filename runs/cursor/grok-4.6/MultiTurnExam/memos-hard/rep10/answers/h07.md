`DB.ListUsers` — `store/db/sqlite/user.go` (same method in `store/db/mysql/user.go`, `store/db/postgres/user.go`)

The browser hits `GET /file/users/:identifier/avatar`. The handler loads that user by username; `GetUser` does not cache username lookups, so it goes through `ListUsers` to the driver, which reads `avatar_url` from the `user` table. The image bytes themselves are the data-URI stored in that column, decoded in `parseDataURI` in the same fileserver file. There is no `GetUserAvatar` RPC in `proto/api/v1/user_service.proto` (only a leftover ACL entry).
