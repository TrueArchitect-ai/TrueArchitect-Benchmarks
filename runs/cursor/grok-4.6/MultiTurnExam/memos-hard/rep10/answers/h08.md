`s3presign.Runner` — yes — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go`) and `s3presign.Runner.Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)  
`memopayload.Runner` — no — none

Only those two types declare `RunOnce`. Production starts the S3 presign runner and calls `RunOnce` immediately, then again on the 12-hour ticker. `memopayload.RunOnce` is never called; live memo create/update uses `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` instead.
