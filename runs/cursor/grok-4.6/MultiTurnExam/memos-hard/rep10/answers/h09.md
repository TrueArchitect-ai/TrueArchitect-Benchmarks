Declarations: `SSEHub.Close` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`), `Cache.Close` (`store/cache/cache.go`), `Store.Close` (`store/store.go`), `sqlite.DB.Close` (`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`), `postgres.DB.Close` (`store/db/postgres/postgres.go`), `mysql.DB.Close` (`store/db/mysql/mysql.go`), plus test-only `MemoFilterTestContext.Close` and `AttachmentFilterTestContext.Close` (`store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`).
