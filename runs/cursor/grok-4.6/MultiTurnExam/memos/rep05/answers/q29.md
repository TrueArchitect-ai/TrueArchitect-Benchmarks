web/src/connect.ts; access JWT (Bearer) and refresh token (cookie); server/auth

The Connect `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` from `web/src/auth-state.ts` (localStorage) and, on expiry or 401, calls `RefreshToken` with `credentials: "include"` so the refresh cookie is sent. The backend validates those tokens in `server/auth` (`Authenticator` in `authenticator.go`); Connect wiring is `NewAuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`. PATs are also accepted by the authenticator but are not what the frontend interceptor issues.
