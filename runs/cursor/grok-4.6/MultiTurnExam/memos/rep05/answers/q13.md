`store.Memo` is defined in `store/memo.go` and appears on `store.Driver` in `store/driver.go`. Dialect drivers scan into it in `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`. API handlers convert and mutate it throughout `server/router/api/v1` (for example `memo_service.go`, `memo_service_converter.go`). Other consumers are `server/notification/email.go`, `server/router/rss/rss.go`, and `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`. `server/router/frontend/frontend.go` and `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go` use `ListMemos`/`GetMemo` results and read `Memo` fields. Tests that construct `&store.Memo{...}` live under `store/test`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub_test.go`, `server/router/frontend/frontend_test.go`, and `server/router/rss/rss_test.go`.
