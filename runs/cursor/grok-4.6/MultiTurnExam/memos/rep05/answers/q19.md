gRPC-Gateway — server/router/api/v1/v1.go
Connect — server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go

`RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` mounts MemoService on HTTP REST via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer`. Connect is registered in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` with `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler`; `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` wraps those Connect calls onto `APIV1Service`. There is no native `grpc.NewServer` registration.
