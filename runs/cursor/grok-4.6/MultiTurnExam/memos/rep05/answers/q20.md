The browser live channel is Server-Sent Events. `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` fans events out; `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` exposes the stream. `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`) and `CreateMemoComment` (`memo_service_comments.go`) call `SSEHub.Broadcast` directly. `UpdateMemo`, `SetMemoAttachments`, and `SetMemoRelations` go through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go`. `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `reaction_service.go` broadcast reaction events.
