`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` rows (source and target) and linked `attachment` rows, then deletes the `memo` row. `memo_share` is removed by `ON DELETE CASCADE` in the schemas (`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` and the Postgres/MySQL `LATEST.sql` files). The API handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` also deletes comment memos first (same tables). `reaction` and `inbox` are not cleaned up.
