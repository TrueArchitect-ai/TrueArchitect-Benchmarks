CEL (`cel-go`) — internal/filter; each dialect `ListMemos` calls `filter.AppendConditions` with `DialectSQLite` / `DialectPostgres` / `DialectMySQL`, which compiles the CEL AST to IR (`parser.go`) and renders a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholders (`render.go`).

`ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` puts `request.Filter` on `FindMemo.Filters`. The engine is documented in `internal/filter/README.md` and entered via `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`. SQLite, Postgres, and MySQL apply it in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`.
