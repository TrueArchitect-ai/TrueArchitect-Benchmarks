web/src/connect.ts; JWT access token (Bearer) and refresh token (cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` from `web/src/auth-state.ts`, and refreshes via `refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"`.
