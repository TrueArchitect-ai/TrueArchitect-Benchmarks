CEL (cel-go) — internal/filter; each dialect’s ListMemos calls filter.AppendConditions, which compiles the CEL filter to an IR then renders dialect-specific SQL (sqlite/mysql/postgres).

`ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` puts `request.Filter` on `FindMemo.Filters`. `internal/filter` parses CEL (`engine.go` / `parser.go`), normalizes to IR (`ir.go`), and `render.go` emits SQL. Drivers invoke that via `filter.DefaultEngine()` + `AppendConditions` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`.
