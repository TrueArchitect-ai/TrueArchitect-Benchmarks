web/src/connect.ts; access token (Bearer) and refresh token (cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` on the Connect transport attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` (from `web/src/auth-state.ts`) and, on 401 or expiry, calls `refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"` so the refresh cookie is sent. The backend validates those tokens in `server/auth` (`Authenticator` / JWT + stored refresh tokens); Connect/gRPC-Gateway wire that through `NewAuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`.
