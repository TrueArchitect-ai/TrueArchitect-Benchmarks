web/src/connect.ts; access JWT (Bearer) and refresh token (cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>` (from `web/src/auth-state.ts`) and, on 401 or expiry, calls `refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"` so the refresh cookie is sent. The backend validates those in `server/auth` (`Authenticator.Authenticate` / `AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` / `AuthenticateByRefreshToken`); Connect uses `NewAuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`. PATs are also accepted by `server/auth` but are not what this frontend interceptor issues.
