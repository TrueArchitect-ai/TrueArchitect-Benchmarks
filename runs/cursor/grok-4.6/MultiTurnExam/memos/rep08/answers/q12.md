`useInfiniteMemos` in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` calls `memoServiceClient.listMemos` from `web/src/connect.ts`. That is `MemoService.ListMemos` in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`, handled by `APIV1Service.ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, which uses `Store.ListMemos` in `store/memo.go` and a dialect `DB.ListMemos` such as `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` (MySQL/Postgres have the same method too).
