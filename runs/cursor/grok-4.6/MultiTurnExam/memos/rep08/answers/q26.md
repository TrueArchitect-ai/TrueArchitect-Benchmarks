memo, memo_relation, attachment, memo_share

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` (source and target) and `attachment` rows, then `DELETE FROM memo`. `memo_share` is removed by `FOREIGN KEY (memo_id) REFERENCES memo(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` in the LATEST schemas (`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` and the MySQL/Postgres copies). Reactions and inbox rows are not deleted.
