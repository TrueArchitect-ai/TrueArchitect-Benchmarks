The hub is `SSEHub` in `sse_hub.go`; clients connect via `GET /api/v1/sse` in `sse_handler.go`. Those memo handlers call `SSEHub.Broadcast` (UpdateMemo, SetMemoAttachments, and SetMemoRelations go through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go`). Reaction handlers also broadcast, but they are not memo RPCs.
