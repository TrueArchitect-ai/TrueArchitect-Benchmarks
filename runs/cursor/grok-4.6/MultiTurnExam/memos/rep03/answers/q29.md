web/src/connect.ts; access JWT (Bearer / localStorage) and refresh JWT (HttpOnly cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` in `connect.ts` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` and, on 401 or expiry, calls `refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"`. Access tokens live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`; refresh tokens are the `memos_refresh` cookie set by `auth_service_session.go`. The backend `server/auth` `Authenticator` validates Access Token V2, refresh tokens, and PATs.
