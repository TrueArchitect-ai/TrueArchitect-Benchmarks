gRPC-Gateway — server/router/api/v1/v1.go
Connect — server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go

`RegisterGateway` in `v1.go` registers MemoService on the grpc-gateway mux (`RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer`). Connect is registered in `connect_handler.go` via `RegisterConnectHandlers` / `NewMemoServiceHandler`; `connect_services.go` wraps each RPC onto `APIV1Service`. There is no standalone gRPC listener.
