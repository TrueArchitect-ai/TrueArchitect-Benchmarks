CEL (`cel-go`) — internal/filter; `Engine` compiles the CEL filter to a dialect-agnostic IR, then `AppendConditions` / `CompileToStatement` render SQL fragments with `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, or `DialectMySQL`.

The proto field is documented as a CEL expression in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. `internal/filter/engine.go` parses with `cel-go`; `internal/filter/README.md` describes parse → IR (`ir.go` / `parser.go`) → dialect render (`render.go`). Each driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`) calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect.
