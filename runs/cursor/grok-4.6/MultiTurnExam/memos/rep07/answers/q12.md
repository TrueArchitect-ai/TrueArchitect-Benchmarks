`useMemos` and `useInfiniteMemos` in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` call `memoServiceClient.listMemos`, which is created in `web/src/connect.ts`. That maps to `MemoService.ListMemos` in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. The RPC is implemented by `APIV1Service.ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, which calls `Store.ListMemos` in `store/memo.go`, then a dialect driver such as `DB.ListMemos` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`.
