web/src/connect.ts; access token (JWT Bearer) and refresh token (cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` from `web/src/auth-state.ts` and, on 401 or expiry, calls `AuthService.RefreshToken` with `credentials: "include"` so the refresh-token cookie is sent. The backend validates those tokens in `server/auth` (`Authenticator` in `authenticator.go`; JWT helpers in `token.go`).
