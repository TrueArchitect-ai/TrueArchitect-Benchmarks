`store.Memo` is defined in `store/memo.go` and appears on `store.Driver` in `store/driver.go`. Dialect constructors/scanners live in `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`. Callers that take or return `*store.Memo` (or call `CreateMemo` / `ListMemos` / `GetMemo` and use the result) are the API handlers under `server/router/api/v1`, plus `server/notification`, `server/runner/memopayload`, `server/router/rss`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/fileserver`. Tests that construct `store.Memo` are in `store/test` and `server/router/api/v1/test`.
