memo_relation (as source or target), attachments

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` removes `memo_relation` rows where the memo is `memo_id` or `related_memo_id`, then attachments with that `MemoID` (`ListAttachments` / `DeleteAttachment`), and only then calls `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the `memo` row. The API handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` also deletes comment memos first; that still goes through the same store cleanup.
