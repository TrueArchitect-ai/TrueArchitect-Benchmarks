`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` rows (memo as source or target), then `attachment` rows via `DeleteAttachment`, then the `memo` row (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`). `memo_share` is removed by `FOREIGN KEY (memo_id) REFERENCES memo(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (same in mysql/postgres). Reactions and inbox rows are not cleaned up.
