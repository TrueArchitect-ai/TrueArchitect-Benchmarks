The hub is `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; browsers connect at `/api/v1/sse` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`). Memo handlers broadcast via `s.SSEHub.Broadcast` or `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_service.go`, `memo_service_comments.go`, `memo_update_helpers.go`, `memo_attachment_service.go`, `memo_relation_service.go`). Reaction handlers also broadcast but are not memo RPCs.
