gRPC-Gateway — server/router/api/v1/v1.go
Connect — server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go

`RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` registers MemoService on the gRPC-Gateway mux via `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer`. Connect registration is `RegisterConnectHandlers` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (`apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler`); `ConnectServiceHandler` wrappers live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`. There is no separate native gRPC listener.
