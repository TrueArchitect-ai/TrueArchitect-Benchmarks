`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` rows (source and target) and linked `attachment` rows, then deletes the `memo` row. `memo_share` is removed by `FOREIGN KEY (memo_id) REFERENCES memo(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` in `store/migration/*/LATEST.sql`. The API handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` also deletes comment memos first (same tables). `reaction` and `inbox` are not deleted.
