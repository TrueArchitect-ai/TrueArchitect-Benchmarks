memo_relation rows (where the memo is source or target), then attachments

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` calls `DeleteMemoRelation` for `MemoID` and `RelatedMemoID`, then `DeleteAttachment` for each linked attachment, then `driver.DeleteMemo`. The API handler in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` deletes comment memos first; that is not store-layer cleanup of the memo row itself.
