web/src/connect.ts; access token (Bearer) and refresh token (HttpOnly cookie); server/auth

`authInterceptor` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>` (storage in `web/src/auth-state.ts`) and on 401/expiry calls `AuthService.refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"`. The backend validates tokens in `server/auth` (`authenticator.go`, `token.go`); Connect/gateway use `Authorizer` in `server/router/api/v1/authz.go`. PATs are validated there too but are not what the frontend interceptor manages.
