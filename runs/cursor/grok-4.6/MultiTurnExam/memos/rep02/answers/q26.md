memo, memo_relation, attachment, memo_share

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` (source and target) and `attachment` rows, then the `memo` row. `memo_share` is declared `ON DELETE CASCADE` in `store/migration/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/LATEST.sql`; SQLite opens with `foreign_keys(0)` in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, so that cascade applies on MySQL/Postgres only. The API also deletes comment memos first (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); those hit the same tables. Reactions and inboxes are not removed.
