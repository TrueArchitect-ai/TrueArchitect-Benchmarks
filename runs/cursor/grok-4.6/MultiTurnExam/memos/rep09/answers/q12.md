`useInfiniteMemos` (and unused-by-UI `useMemos`) in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` call `memoServiceClient.listMemos`, created in `web/src/connect.ts`. That maps to `rpc ListMemos` on `MemoService` in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`, handled by `APIV1Service.ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, which calls `Store.ListMemos` in `store/memo.go`, which delegates to the dialect driver — e.g. `DB.ListMemos` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`.
