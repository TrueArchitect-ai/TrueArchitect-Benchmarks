`CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` calls `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`, which loads the creator’s webhooks and posts via `webhook.PostAsync`. Delivery is `internal/webhook` (`Post` / `PostAsync` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`).
