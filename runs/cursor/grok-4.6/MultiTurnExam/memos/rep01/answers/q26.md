memo, memo_relation, attachment

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` (source and target), then `attachment` rows, then the `memo` row. The API `DeleteMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` also deletes comment memos first (same tables). `memo_share` has `ON DELETE CASCADE` in LATEST.sql, but SQLite opens with `foreign_keys(0)` in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, so that cascade is not reliable there. Reactions and inbox are not cleaned up.
