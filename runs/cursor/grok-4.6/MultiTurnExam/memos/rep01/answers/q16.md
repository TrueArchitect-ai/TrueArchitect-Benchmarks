q16.
memo_relation (source and target), attachments

`Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes `memo_relation` rows where the memo is `MemoID` or `RelatedMemoID`, then attachments for that memo, then the memo row. The API in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` deletes comment memos first; that is not done inside the store cleanup.
