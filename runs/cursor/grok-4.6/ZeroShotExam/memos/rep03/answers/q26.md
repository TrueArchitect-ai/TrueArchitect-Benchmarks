`memo`, `memo_relation`, `attachment`

`store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes the `memo` row, then explicitly cascades by calling `DeleteMemoRelation` (`memo_relation`) and `DeleteAttachment` (`attachment`). `memo_share` is not in that path: its `ON DELETE CASCADE` is schema-only, and SQLite runs with foreign keys off.
