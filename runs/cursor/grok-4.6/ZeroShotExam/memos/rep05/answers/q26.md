q26.
`memo`, `memo_relation`, `attachment`

`store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go` deletes those tables itself: it runs explicit cleanup on `memo_relation` (rows where the memo is source or target) and `attachment` (linked rows via `DeleteAttachment`), then `DELETE FROM memo` in the SQLite/MySQL/Postgres drivers (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`).

`memo_share` has schema `ON DELETE CASCADE` in the `LATEST.sql` files, but that is not explicit cascade code, and SQLite opens with `foreign_keys(0)` in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, so it is not part of this list. Reactions and inbox rows are not deleted.
