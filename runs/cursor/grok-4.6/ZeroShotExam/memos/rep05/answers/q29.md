web/src/connect.ts; access tokens and refresh tokens; server/auth

Frontend Connect RPC clients in `web/src/connect.ts` attach a Bearer **access token** via `authInterceptor`. On expiry or `Unauthenticated`, that interceptor refreshes through `AuthService.refreshToken` (cookie-backed **refresh token**, `credentials: "include"`). `server/auth` validates those JWTs (and PATs on the backend).
