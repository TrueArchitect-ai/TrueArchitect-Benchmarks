The question assumes a nightly database-cleanup job is registered on the cron scheduler. It is not.

`internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library. Nothing under `server/` or `cmd/` imports it or registers jobs. The only cleanup schedules are documentation/test examples: `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM) for sample jobs named `daily-cleanup` / `cleanup` in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, `internal/scheduler/README.md`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`. There is no production job named nightly database-cleanup and no scheduled run time.
