**1st: User (~7 packages); 2nd: Memo (~6 packages)**

The data-layer models live in `store/` (see `store/driver.go`: Attachment, Memo, MemoRelation, InstanceSetting, User, UserSetting, IdentityProvider, Inbox, Reaction, MemoShare, UserIdentity). Counting `store.User` / `store.Memo` mentions in Go packages outside `store/`:

- **User** (`store/user.go`): `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- **Memo** (`store/memo.go`): `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

If Find/Update/Delete companions are counted too, Memo also appears in `server/router/fileserver` and ties User at ~7; Attachment is next (~4–5).
