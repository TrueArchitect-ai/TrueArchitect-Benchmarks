Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ: webhook delivery workers consume an in-process Go channel, not an external broker. The queue is `asyncPostQueue` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (`make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`); `init()` starts four goroutines that range over it, and `PostAsync` enqueues payloads that `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` dispatches.
