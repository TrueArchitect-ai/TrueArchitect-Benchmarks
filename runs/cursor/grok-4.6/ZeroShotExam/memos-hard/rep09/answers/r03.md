The question assumes a nightly database-cleanup job is registered with the cron scheduler. That job does not exist.

`internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library. `server/server.go` never starts it; the only background runner is S3 presign refresh. The 2 AM cleanup examples in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, `internal/scheduler/README.md`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go` (`0 2 * * *`) are documentation only, not a production database-cleanup job.
