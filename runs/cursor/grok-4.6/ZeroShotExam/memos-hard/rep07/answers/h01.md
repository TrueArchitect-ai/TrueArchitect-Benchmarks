s3presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:129`

The runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` re-presigns S3 attachment URLs so stored `Reference` links do not expire. `runnerInterval` is `12 * time.Hour`. `Server.Start` wires it in by calling `startBackgroundRunners`, which constructs the runner, runs it once immediately, then starts `Run` in a background goroutine.
