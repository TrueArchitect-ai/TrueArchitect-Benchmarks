1st: User; 2nd: Memo (~7 vs ~6 packages)

Persistent entities live in `store/` (e.g. `User` in `store/user.go`, `Memo` in `store/memo.go`). Counting `store.User` / `store.Memo` in Go packages outside that layer:

- **User** — 7: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- **Memo** — 6: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

Next is Attachment (~4). Settings/IDP row types are mostly used as proto payloads, not as `store.*` structs outside the layer.
