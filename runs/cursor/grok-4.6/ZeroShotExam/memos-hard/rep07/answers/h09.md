The eight `func` method declarations are `*v1.SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`), `*cache.Cache` (`store/cache/cache.go`), `*store.Store` (`store/store.go`), `*sqlite.DB` / `*postgres.DB` / `*mysql.DB` (`store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}`), and the test-only `*MemoFilterTestContext` / `*AttachmentFilterTestContext` (`store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`).

Non-test `server/` code only calls two of those: `s.Store.Close()` in `Server.Shutdown` and `s.sseHub.Close()` in `Server.closeLongLivedConnections`, both in `server/server.go`. (`*http.Server.Close` and `net.Listener.Close` there are stdlib, not backend declarations. `Cache.Close` and driver `DB.Close` are invoked from `store/store.go`, not from server code.)
