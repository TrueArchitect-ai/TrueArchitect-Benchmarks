Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery workers consume from an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (`make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`). Four goroutines started in `init()` range over that channel; `PostAsync` enqueues payloads onto it. There is no external message broker.
