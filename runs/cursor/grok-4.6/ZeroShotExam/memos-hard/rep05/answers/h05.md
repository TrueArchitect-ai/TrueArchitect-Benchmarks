User deletion runs a Go transaction that issues `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN …` (reactions on the user’s memos) and `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?` (reactions the user created). The `reaction` table only has `creator_id INTEGER NOT NULL` plus a uniqueness constraint—no foreign key or `ON DELETE` rule. Tests in `store/test/user_delete_test.go` and `server/router/api/v1/test/user_service_delete_test.go` assert those rows are gone after delete.
