`*s3presign.Runner` — yes — `(*Server).startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go`), `(*s3presign.Runner).Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
`*memopayload.Runner` — no — n/a

Two types declare `RunOnce`: `*s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` and `*memopayload.Runner` in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`. Only the s3presign method is called from production: once at startup in `startBackgroundRunners`, and again on each tick from `Run`. `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` is used from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, but `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` itself has no callers.
