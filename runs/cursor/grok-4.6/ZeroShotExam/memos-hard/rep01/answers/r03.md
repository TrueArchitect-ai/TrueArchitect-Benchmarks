There is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository’s cron scheduler. `internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library; its only cleanup examples are documentation/tests (`daily-cleanup` / `cleanup` at `0 2 * * *` in `internal/scheduler/README.md`, `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`), and `server/server.go` never registers any scheduler job. Production background work is ticker-based runners, not a database-cleanup cron.
