1st: User; 2nd: Memo (~7 and ~6 packages)

Persistent models live in the `store/` data layer (`store/driver.go` lists them as Attachment, Memo, MemoRelation, InstanceSetting, User, UserSetting, IdentityProvider, Inbox, Reaction, MemoShare, UserIdentity). Counting `store.<Type>` uses in Go packages outside `store/`:

- **User** (`store/user.go`): 7 packages — `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- **Memo** (`store/memo.go`): 6 packages — `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

Attachment is next (~4 packages). Proto payload types in `proto/gen/store` do not outrank these two.
