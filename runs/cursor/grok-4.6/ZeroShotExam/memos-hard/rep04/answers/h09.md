The backend has **8** distinct `Close` method declarations (`func (recv) Close`):

- `*SSEHub` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
- `*cache.Cache` — `store/cache/cache.go`
- `*store.Store` — `store/store.go`
- `*sqlite.DB` — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `*postgres.DB` — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `*mysql.DB` — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `*MemoFilterTestContext` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`
- `*AttachmentFilterTestContext` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`

Only two of those are invoked by non-test `server/` code, both from `server/server.go`: `s.Store.Close()` in `Shutdown`, and `s.sseHub.Close()` in `closeLongLivedConnections`. Other `.Close()` calls in that package are stdlib (`net.Listener`, `http.Server`, `os.File`, `io.ReadCloser`), not these declarations.
