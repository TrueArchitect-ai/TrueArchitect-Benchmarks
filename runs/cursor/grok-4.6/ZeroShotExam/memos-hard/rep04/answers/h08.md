s3presign.Runner — yes — startBackgroundRunners (server/server.go); also Runner.Run (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go)
memopayload.Runner — no — none

Two types declare `RunOnce`: `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` and `memopayload.Runner` in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`. Only the s3presign method is called from production: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go` invokes it at startup, and `s3presign.Runner.Run` invokes it on each 12-hour tick. `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` is never called (production uses `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` instead).
