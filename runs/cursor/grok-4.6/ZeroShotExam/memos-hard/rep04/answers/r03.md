There is no nightly database-cleanup job in the cron scheduler. `internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library; its only “cleanup” entries are examples (`daily-cleanup` / `cleanup` at `0 2 * * *` in `internal/scheduler/README.md`, `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`). Nothing in `server/` registers that scheduler or a database-cleanup job—the only background runner is S3 presign in `server/server.go`.
