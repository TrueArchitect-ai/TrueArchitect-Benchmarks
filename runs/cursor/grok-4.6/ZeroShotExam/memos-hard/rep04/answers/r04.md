Webhook delivery workers consume from an in-process Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (`make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`). `init()` starts four goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post`; callers such as `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` enqueue via `webhook.PostAsync`.
