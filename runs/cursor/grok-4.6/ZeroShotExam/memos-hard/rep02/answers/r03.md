There is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository. `internal/scheduler` is only a reusable cron library; nothing in `server/` or `cmd/` registers it, and no job named database-cleanup exists. The only cleanup schedules are documentation examples (`daily-cleanup` / `cleanup` at `0 2 * * *` in `internal/scheduler/README.md`, `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`). Actual background work is the S3-presign and memo-payload runners in `server/runner/`, not a cron DB cleanup.
