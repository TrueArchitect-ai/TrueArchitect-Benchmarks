8; Store (`server/server.go`), SSEHub (`server/server.go`)

The eight `func (…) Close` declarations are `SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`), `Cache` (`store/cache/cache.go`), `MemoFilterTestContext` and `AttachmentFilterTestContext` (`store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`), `Store` (`store/store.go`), and the three driver `DB` types (`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`). Non-test `server/` code only calls `s.Store.Close()` and `s.sseHub.Close()` in `server/server.go` (`Shutdown` / `closeLongLivedConnections`). Other `.Close()` calls there are stdlib (`net.Listener`, `http.Server`, files/readers), not these declarations.
