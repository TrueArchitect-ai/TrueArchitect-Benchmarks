They consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ; there is no external message broker. Webhook delivery workers are four in-process goroutines started in `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` that range over the package-level buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue`, declared in that same file as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. `PostAsync` enqueues onto that channel (or drops the payload if it is full).
