1st: User; 2nd: Memo (~7 and ~6 packages)

The persistent entities live in the `store/` data layer (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, plus Attachment, Inbox, MemoRelation, MemoShare, Reaction, UserIdentity, and related types). Counting Go packages **outside** `store/` that mention the entity type itself (`store.User`, `store.Memo`):

- **User** (~7): `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- **Memo** (~6): `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

No other entity is close (Attachment is next at ~4 packages).
