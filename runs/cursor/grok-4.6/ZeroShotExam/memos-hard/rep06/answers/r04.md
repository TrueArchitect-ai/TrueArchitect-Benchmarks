Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery workers consume from an in-process Go channel, not an external message broker.

The queue is `asyncPostQueue` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`: a package-level buffered channel (`chan *WebhookRequestPayload`, capacity 128). `init()` starts four goroutines that `range` over it and call `Post`. `PostAsync` enqueues payloads (dropping them if the channel is full). Callers such as `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` dispatch via `webhook.PostAsync`.
