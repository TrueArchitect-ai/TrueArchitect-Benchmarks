8; `*store.Store` (`server/server.go`), `*v1.SSEHub` (`server/server.go`)

There are **8** distinct `func (recv) Close` declarations in the Go backend:

- `*v1.SSEHub` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
- `*cache.Cache` — `store/cache/cache.go`
- `*store.Store` — `store/store.go`
- `*sqlite.DB` — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `*postgres.DB` — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- `*mysql.DB` — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `*MemoFilterTestContext` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`
- `*AttachmentFilterTestContext` — `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go`

Non-test `server/` code only calls two of those:

- `s.Store.Close()` in `server/server.go` (`Server.Shutdown`)
- `s.sseHub.Close()` in `server/server.go` (`Server.closeLongLivedConnections`)

(`store.Store.Close` later calls `*cache.Cache.Close` and `Driver.Close`, which is one of the three `*DB.Close` methods, but those callers are in `store/store.go`, not server code.)
