There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is Protocol Buffers under `proto/api/v1/` (AIP-style services: MemoService, AuthService, UserService, AttachmentService, InstanceService, IdentityProviderService, ShortcutService, AIService), served as Connect RPC / gRPC-Gateway REST from `server/router/api/v1/`, with generated OpenAPI at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml`.
