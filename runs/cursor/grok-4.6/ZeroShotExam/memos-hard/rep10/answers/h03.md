h03.
`internal/idp`; `internal/idp/oauth2`

The shared provider types live in `internal/idp` (`IdentityProviderUserInfo` in `internal/idp/idp.go`). The only shipped protocol is OAuth2 in `internal/idp/oauth2` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`), used by SSO in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go`.
