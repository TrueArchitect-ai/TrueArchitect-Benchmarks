The premise is wrong: this repository does not schedule a nightly database-cleanup job in the cron scheduler.

`internal/scheduler` is a reusable cron library. Production never registers jobs on it (`server/server.go` starts ticker-based runners such as S3 presign, not the scheduler). The only cleanup-like cron expressions are documentation/examples: `daily-cleanup` / `cleanup` at `0 2 * * *` in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, `internal/scheduler/README.md`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, plus a `stale-session-cleanup` example at `0 3 * * *` in `internal/scheduler/README.md`. None of those is a real nightly database-cleanup job.
