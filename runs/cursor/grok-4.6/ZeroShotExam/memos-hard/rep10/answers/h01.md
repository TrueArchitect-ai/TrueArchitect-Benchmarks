s3presign.Runner — every 12 hours — server/server.go:129

The S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) re-presigns S3 attachment URLs so stored links do not expire. It ticks every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). `Server.Start` hooks it in by calling `startBackgroundRunners`, which constructs the runner, runs it once immediately, then starts `Run` in a background goroutine.
