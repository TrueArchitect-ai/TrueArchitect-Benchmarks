CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed by `cel-go`; engine: `internal/filter/` (syntax documented in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`).

Mechanism: CEL compiles to an AST, `internal/filter/parser.go` converts it to IR, and `internal/filter/render.go` emits dialect-specific SQL selected by `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, or `store/db/mysql/memo.go`.
