Premise correction: this repository has no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API.

The public API is defined by Protobuf services in `proto/api/v1/*.proto`, including REST annotations such as those in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. It is exposed through gRPC-Gateway at `/api/v1/*` and Connect RPC at `/memos.api.v1.*`, wired in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`. A generated OpenAPI specification lives at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml`.
