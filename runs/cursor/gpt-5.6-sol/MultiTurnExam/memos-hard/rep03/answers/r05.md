There is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API. The public API is defined with Protocol Buffers under `proto/api/v1/` (for example, `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-31`) and exposed through gRPC-Gateway REST and Connect RPC (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:95-144`). OpenAPI is generated at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml:1-9`.
