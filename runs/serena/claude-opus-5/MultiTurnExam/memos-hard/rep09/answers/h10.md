**`(*renderer).addArg` — `internal/filter/render.go:842` — ~28 call sites (from 14 distinct calling methods)**

`addArg` is the SQL placeholder allocator for the filter engine: it appends a value to `r.args`, bumps the counter, and returns `$N` for Postgres or `?` for SQLite/MySQL (`internal/filter/render.go:842-850`). Essentially every leaf of the filter-to-SQL renderer has to bind a parameter through it, which is why the fan-in is so concentrated.

How I measured, and the runners-up:
