Method: I enumerated the struct types declared in the top-level data-layer package (`store/*.go`), kept the persistent entities (dropping the `Find*`/`Update*`/`Delete*` parameter structs and `Store` itself), then counted distinct package directories outside the `store/` tree that reference each as `store.<Type>`, excluding `proto/gen/`.

Full ranking:

**`store.User`** is referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`. Restricting to non-test files it's 6 packages — the `server/router/frontend` hit is test-only.

**`store.Memo`** is referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`. Non-test only: 5 packages (again `frontend` drops out).

The gap between the two is the auth and file-serving layers: `store.User` is what `auth.Authenticator` resolves tokens to (`server/auth/`) and what avatar serving looks up (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:715`), neither of which touches `store.Memo`.

Worth noting the counts understate nothing but are shaped by one convention: `UserSetting`, `InstanceSetting`, and `IdentityProvider` score zero outside the data layer not because they're unused, but because callers consume the protobuf forms (`storepb.UserSetting`, `storepb.IdentityProvider`) rather than the store structs — see `server/router/api/v1/idp_service.go` and `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go`.
