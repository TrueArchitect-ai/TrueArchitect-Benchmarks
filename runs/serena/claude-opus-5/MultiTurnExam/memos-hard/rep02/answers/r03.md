Two separate corrections:

**1. No cleanup job exists.** Grepping the backend for `cleanup`, `vacuum`, `purge`, and `prune` surfaces no scheduled database maintenance. The hits are all unrelated: in-memory cache expiry sweeps (`store/cache/cache.go:47`, `:83`), best-effort attachment-storage deletion during user deletion (`server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:421-450`, `store/attachment.go:171`), and a test teardown helper (`server/router/api/v1/test/test_helper.go:65`).

**2. The scheduler package is unused by production code.** `internal/scheduler` does implement a full GitHub-Actions-style cron scheduler — 5-field expression parsing (`internal/scheduler/parser.go`), `Job` registration (`internal/scheduler/job.go`), and middleware for recovery/logging/timeout (`internal/scheduler/middleware.go`). But the only files importing it are its own tests: `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`. No `scheduler.New(...)` or `Register(...)` call exists anywhere in `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/`, so no jobs are ever registered and the scheduler never starts.

**What actually runs on a timer.** The server's only background schedule is the S3 presign refresher, and it uses a plain `time.Ticker` rather than cron — no wall-clock time of day is involved:

- `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`, interval `12 * time.Hour` (`:27`)
- Started at `server/server.go:162-171`, with one synchronous `RunOnce` at startup (`:163`)

So the honest answer to "what time" is: nothing is scheduled at any time of day. The one recurring task fires 12 hours after boot and every 12 hours thereafter, drifting with restart time.
