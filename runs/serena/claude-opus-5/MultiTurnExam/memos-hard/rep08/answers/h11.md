**1st: `store.User` — referenced from ~6 packages outside `store/`; 2nd: `store.Memo` — ~4 packages.**

Method: for each persistent entity struct declared in `store/*.go` (`User` `store/user.go:29`, `Memo` `store/memo.go:35`, `Attachment` `store/attachment.go:16`, `Reaction` `store/reaction.go:7`, `Inbox` `store/inbox.go:24`, `IdentityProvider` `store/idp.go:12`, `MemoRelation` `store/memo_relation.go:16`, `UserSetting` `store/user_setting.go:13`, plus `MemoShare`, `UserIdentity`, `InstanceSetting`), I counted the distinct non-test packages referencing `store.<Type>` outside the `store/` tree.

**`store.User` — 6 packages:**
- `server/auth`
- `server/notification`
- `server/router/api/v1` (16 files)
- `server/router/api/v1/test` (test-support package, non-`_test.go` helpers)
- `server/router/fileserver`
- `server/router/rss`

**`store.Memo` — 4 packages:**
- `server/notification`
- `server/router/api/v1` (12 files)
- `server/router/rss`
- `server/runner/memopayload`

Third is `store.Attachment` at 3 (`server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`); everything else sits at 1–2.

Two caveats on the counts:
- If you discount `server/router/api/v1/test` as test infrastructure, `User` is 5 and the gap narrows to 5 vs 4 — the ranking holds either way.
- If the three driver packages (`store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`) are counted as "outside" rather than as part of the data layer, the totals become `User` 9 and `Memo` 7 — again the same ordering.

`User` leads mainly because authentication and access control touch it everywhere: `server/auth` for session/token resolution, and the fileserver and RSS routers each resolve a viewer or owner independently of any memo.
