2. **Only one call site of the constructor.** `mysql.NewDB` is defined at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20` and called exactly once, in the factory's `case "mysql"` branch at `store/db/db.go:22`.
3. **No struct-literal bypass in production code.** The only `DB{...}` literals in the package are `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:29` (inside `NewDB` itself) and `store/db/mysql/auth_config_test.go:12` (`db := &DB{}`) — an in-package unit test that builds a zero-valued struct with a nil `*sql.DB` to exercise pure SQL-building logic, not a live driver.
