That `Reference` is the externally-stored link that would otherwise expire.

- **Staleness margin**: an attachment is skipped if its last presign was under 4 days ago, since presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry (`runner.go:76-83`) — so each URL gets refreshed with about a day of slack.
- **Lifecycle**: the runner gets its own cancellable context (`server/server.go:156-159`) and is tracked in `backgroundRunnerWG` so `Shutdown` can stop and wait for it (`server/server.go:140-143`).
