**Where it's computed.** `RebuildMemoPayload` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-87`) initializes `memo.Payload` if nil, calls `markdownService.ExtractAll([]byte(memo.Content))`, then assigns exactly two fields:

The real work is `ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:356-421`) — a single `gast.Walk` over the goldmark AST that collects everything in one pass. Note it deliberately does *not* touch `Payload.Location`, which is user-supplied and set separately (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:110-112`).

**Triggers** (all call sites of `RebuildMemoPayload`, non-test):
