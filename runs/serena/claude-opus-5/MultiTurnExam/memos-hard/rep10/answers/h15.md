Visibility (`PUBLIC` / `PROTECTED` / `PRIVATE`) is enforced at these distinct sites. They fall into three shapes: **per-memo gates** (deny an already-fetched memo), **query-scoping** (constrain the DB query so invisible memos are never read), and **fan-out filters** (decide who receives a push/notification).

Per-memo access gates:

- `APIV1Service.checkMemoReadAccess` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39` (the main gate; also handles archived-only-for-creator at `:44-53`, visibility at `:55-66`)
- `APIV1Service.checkAttachmentAccess` — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422` (visibility checks at `:445-452`)
- `APIV1Service.ListMemoAttachments` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:200` (inline check at `:214-224`)
- `APIV1Service.ListMemoReactions` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:15` (inline check at `:30-41`)
- `APIV1Service.UpsertMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:60` (check at `:83`)
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41` (private parent blocks commenting)
- `FileServerService.checkAttachmentPermission` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650` (the non-gRPC surface: public+anonymous shortcut at `:677`, `share_token` fallback at `:683-689`, private check at `:699`)
- `APIV1Service.GetMemoByShare` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:151` (deliberate *bypass* surface: a valid unexpired share token grants access regardless of visibility; archived/missing memos still 404 at `:163-165`)

Query-scoping (visibility applied to `FindMemo` before hitting the DB):

- `APIV1Service.ListMemos` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`, scoping at `:217-227` (anonymous → `PUBLIC` only; authenticated → CEL filter `creator_id == N || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`)
- User stats handlers — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:84-90` and `:218-220`
- `RSSService` feeds — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` and `:141` (`VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`)
- `FrontendService.getSitemapXML` — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:143`, scoping at `:150` (public memos only in the sitemap)

Fan-out / notification filters:

- `SSEClient.canReceive` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:135` (private events go only to the creator or an admin)
- `canViewerAccessMemo` (notification email bodies) — `server/notification/email.go:306`
- `canViewerAccessMemo` (user notification API) — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302` — a second, near-identical copy of the same predicate
- Mention-notification gating — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62-84` (skips notifying mentioned users who couldn't see the memo)

Two notes: the MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) adds no checks of its own — it routes `MemoService_ListMemos`/`GetMemo` (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-14`) through the same v1 handlers, inheriting their enforcement. And the two `canViewerAccessMemo` functions are duplicated logic in different packages that differ subtly: `server/notification/email.go:310` tests `viewer.Role == store.RoleAdmin` while `user_service_notifications.go:305` uses `isSuperUser(viewer)`.
