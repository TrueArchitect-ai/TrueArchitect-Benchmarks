Notes on the shape of the enforcement:

- Two distinct **strategies** are in play: post-fetch checks on a single memo (`checkMemoReadAccess`, `checkAttachmentPermission`) versus pre-query narrowing that never loads invisible rows (`ListMemos`, the stats handlers, RSS, sitemap — all setting `FindMemo.VisibilityList` or appending a CEL filter).
- The last two entries are near-identical copies of `canViewerAccessMemo` in different packages — they don't block access, they blank the memo snippet for PRIVATE (and PROTECTED for anonymous) memos in notification payloads.
- `GetMemoByShare` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:153`) is a deliberate **bypass**, not an enforcement site: a valid, unexpired share token grants read access regardless of visibility. `checkAttachmentPermission` honors the same token via the `share_token` query param (`fileserver.go:684-689`).
- The MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) has no visibility logic of its own — it dispatches to the same v1 handlers listed above and inherits their checks.
