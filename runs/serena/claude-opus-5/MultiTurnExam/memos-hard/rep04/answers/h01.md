**`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162-171`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.**

Details:

- **What it does** — `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:44`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, and for each one whose payload has an `S3Object`, re-generates a presigned GET URL via `s3.NewClient(...).PresignGetObject(ctx, key)` and writes it back as the attachment's `Reference`, stamping `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:99-115`). That's what keeps the stored link to the S3-hosted blob from going stale.
- **Freshness policy** — presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry, and an attachment is skipped if its last presign was under 4 days ago (`runner.go:80-86`), so each URL is refreshed with ~1 day of slack. The 5-day expiry itself is set in `internal/storage/s3/s3.go` (`PresignGetObject`).
- **Schedule** — `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29-42`), which exits on context cancellation.
- **Wiring** — `startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner at `server/server.go:162`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously at line 163 (so links are refreshed once at boot), then launches `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` (lines 166-171). Shutdown cancels `s3Cancel` via `stopBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:176`) and waits in `waitBackgroundRunners` (line 184).
