**Handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations` — all six converge on `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`.**

The call graph, all in `server/router/api/v1`:

| Handler | Definition | Dispatch call |
|---|---|---|
| `CreateMemo` | `memo_service.go:70` | `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` at `memo_service.go:157` |
| `DeleteMemo` | `memo_service.go:518` | `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` at `memo_service.go:562` |
| `UpdateMemo` | `memo_service.go:401` | `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` at `memo_service.go:513` |
| `SetMemoAttachments` | `memo_attachment_service.go:16` | `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` at `memo_attachment_service.go:48` |
| `SetMemoRelations` | `memo_relation_service.go:16` | `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` at `memo_relation_service.go:48` |
| `CreateMemoComment` | `memo_service_comments.go:20` | `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` at `memo_service_comments.go:119` |

The last three are the non-obvious ones: `SetMemoAttachments` and `SetMemoRelations` reach the network through the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go:66`), which calls `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` at `memo_update_helpers.go:67` — so editing a memo's attachments or relations fires a `memos.memo.updated` webhook just like `UpdateMemo` does.

Convergence:

- The four `Dispatch*Webhook` entry points (`memo_service_webhooks.go:15,20,25,30`) load the target user's webhooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks` and call `webhook.PostAsync` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:43,71`) — the URLs come from user-configured webhook settings.
- `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) only enqueues onto a bounded channel; the four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) then call **`webhook.Post`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which is the single place the outbound request is built, signed, and issued via `safeClient.Do` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`.

(`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59` guards that delivery against reserved/private IPs at dial time, as covered earlier. Separately, `GetLinkMetadata` and the AI service make outbound requests too, but those don't flow through `webhook.Post`.)
