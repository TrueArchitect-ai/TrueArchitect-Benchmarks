**1st: `store.User` (~7 packages, 30 files); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~6 packages, 21 files)**

Counting Go packages outside the data layer (everything under `store/`, including `store/db/*` and `store/test`, excluded; generated proto excluded) that name each persistent entity type:

**`store.User` — 7 packages** (defined in `store/user.go`):
- `server/router/api/v1` (19 files)
- `server/router/api/v1/test` (3)
- `server/router/rss` (2)
- `server/router/fileserver` (2) — e.g. the avatar lookup at `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:715`
- `server/auth` (2)
- `server/router/frontend` (1)
- `server/notification` (1)

**`store.Memo` — 6 packages** (defined in `store/memo.go`):
- `server/router/api/v1` (13 files)
- `server/router/api/v1/test` (3)
- `server/router/rss` (2)
- `server/runner/memopayload` (1)
- `server/router/frontend` (1)
- `server/notification` (1)

The gap between the two is one package: `store.User` additionally reaches `server/auth` and `server/router/fileserver`, while `store.Memo` reaches `server/runner/memopayload`. `User` is the only entity that appears in the auth layer, which is what puts it on top.

Rest of the ranking for context: `store.Attachment` 4 packages, then `store.MemoShare` / `store.MemoRelation` / `store.Inbox` at 3 each, `store.UserIdentity` / `store.Reaction` at 2, and `store.UserSetting`, `store.InstanceSetting`, `store.IdentityProvider` at 1 each — those last three are touched only by `server/router/api/v1`.

Method note: this is a textual count of `store.<Type>` references with a word boundary (so `store.User` doesn't absorb `store.UserSetting`/`store.UserIdentity`), not a type-checker-driven analysis. It also counts only the entity structs themselves, not their companion `Find*`/`Update*`/`Delete*` parameter types, which follow roughly the same distribution.
