Two observations. The rule is applied inline and duplicated rather than centralized — `checkMemoReadAccess` has only three callers (`memo_service.go:356`, `:367`, `memo_service_comments.go:148`), while reactions, attachments, and memo-attachments each re-implement the same PUBLIC/PROTECTED/PRIVATE ladder. Also, the two `canViewerAccessMemo` functions are name-identical but separately defined in different packages, and the fileserver is the only surface honoring `share_token` (`fileserver.go:684-690`), matching the API's `GetMemoByShare` path.

The MCP router is not a separate site: it proxies to the same v1 handlers (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12`) and contains no visibility logic of its own.
