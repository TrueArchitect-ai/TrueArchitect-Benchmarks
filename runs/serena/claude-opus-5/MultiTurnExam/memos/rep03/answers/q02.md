**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live in `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subdirectories — `mysql/`, `postgres/`, and `sqlite/` — each with versioned migration folders (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`) plus a `LATEST.sql` schema file. The corresponding driver implementations are in `/bench/repo/store/db/mysql`, `/bench/repo/store/db/postgres`, and `/bench/repo/store/db/sqlite`.
