**Server-Sent Events (SSE), on `GET /api/v1/sse`; server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments (plus reactions: UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction)**

- **Mechanism:** SSE. The endpoint is registered at `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25-30` (`GET /api/v1/sse`, Bearer-token authenticated) and wired into the API router at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`.
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`, with `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast` (`:115`) and per-client visibility filtering in `canReceive` (`:134`). Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:15-20`).
- **Memo handlers that broadcast:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (skipped when `isSSESuppressed`, i.e. when invoked from `CreateMemoComment`)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `UpdateMemo` — via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:513` → `memo_update_helpers.go:71`)
  - `SetMemoRelations` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:48` (same helper)
  - `SetMemoAttachments` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:48` (same helper)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - Reaction handlers also broadcast memo-scoped events: `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161`.
