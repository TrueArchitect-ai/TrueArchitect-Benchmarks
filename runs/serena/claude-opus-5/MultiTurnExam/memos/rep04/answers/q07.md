**web/src/connect.ts**

All eight clients are created there from a single `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) with an auth interceptor: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
