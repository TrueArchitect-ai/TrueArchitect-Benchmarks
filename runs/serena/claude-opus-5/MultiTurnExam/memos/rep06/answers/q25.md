**CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by the in-repo engine at `internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`)**

Mechanism: the filter string is parsed and type-checked by cel-go against a memo-specific CEL environment (`internal/filter/schema.go`), normalized into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/parser.go` → `internal/filter/ir.go`), then rendered into a SQL fragment plus placeholder args for the target dialect by `internal/filter/render.go` — each driver calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect<X>, &where, &args)` to splice that fragment into its `WHERE` clause (e.g. `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`, with sqlite/mysql equivalents in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`).

Dialect differences are confined to the renderer: `?` vs `$n` placeholders, JSON access (`json_extract` vs `->>`), tag membership (`LIKE` / `JSON_CONTAINS` / `@>`), and `LIKE` vs `ILIKE` — see `internal/filter/render.go:231-237, 365-402` and `internal/filter/README.md`. The API-layer glue that builds `find.Filters` from the RPC request is in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_filter.go`.
