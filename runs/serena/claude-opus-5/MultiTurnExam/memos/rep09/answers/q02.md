**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Each has its own migration tree under `store/migration/`: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` — every one containing versioned directories plus a `LATEST.sql`. Matching driver implementations live in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.
