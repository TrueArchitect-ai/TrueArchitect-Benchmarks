**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/` (`store/migrator.go:39,44-45,255` embeds the `migration` dir and selects the subdirectory by `profile.Driver`), and that directory contains exactly three dialect folders:

- `store/migration/mysql/` (with `LATEST.sql` + versioned dirs)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`. Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is SQLite-only.
