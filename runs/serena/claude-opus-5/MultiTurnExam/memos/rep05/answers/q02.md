**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each containing versioned migration folders plus a `LATEST.sql`). This matches the driver implementations in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
