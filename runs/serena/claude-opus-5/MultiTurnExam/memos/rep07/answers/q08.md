**Driver — store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` is defined at `store/driver.go:10`. It covers connection lifecycle (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus the per-model CRUD methods (attachments, memos, users, etc.).
