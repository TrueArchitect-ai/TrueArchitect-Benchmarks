**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

- **Mechanism** — SSE over the same `/api/v1` Echo group. The hub is a fan-out of per-client channels: `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close` (`sse_hub.go:56-133`), events typed `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (`sse_hub.go:15-20`). Per-client visibility filtering happens in `SSEClient.canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:134`). The HTTP endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (`sse_handler.go`, wired in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:123`).
- **Broadcasting handlers** (all `APIV1Service` methods):
  - `CreateMemo` — `memo_service.go:163` (suppressed when called internally from comment creation, via `withSuppressSSE`)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` / `DeleteMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go:106`, `:161`
  - `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) broadcast indirectly through the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go:66-71`).
