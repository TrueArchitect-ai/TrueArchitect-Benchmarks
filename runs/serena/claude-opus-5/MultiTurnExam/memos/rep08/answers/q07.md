**web/src/connect.ts**

All service clients are created there via `createClient(...)` over a shared transport (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`.
