`Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes, in order, before calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo`:

1. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`)
2. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the target** — `DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:145`)
3. **`attachment` rows linked to the memo** — listed via `FindAttachment{MemoID: ...}` and removed one by one with `Store.DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`), which also deletes the backing blob/file from storage (`store/attachment.go:132-149`, local-file removal at `store/attachment.go:210-224`).

Then the memo row itself: `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` (`store/memo.go:158`).

Two things the store layer does *not* do, handled by the API handler `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518`) instead: **comment memos** are deleted first by walking `MemoRelationComment` relations and calling `Store.DeleteMemo` on each (lines 567-577), and **reactions** are cleaned up separately by the handler (they're keyed by resource name in `content_id`, not by memo ID, so they aren't reachable from the store's memo-delete path).
