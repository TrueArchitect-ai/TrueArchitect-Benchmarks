**Convergence / delivery:** the three memo-lifecycle functions funnel into the private helper `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the creator, loads their hooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync(payload)`. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` builds its payload inline (it targets a different user) but ends at the same `webhook.PostAsync`. That enqueues onto a bounded channel drained by four worker goroutines (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` and the pool started in `init()` at :42), each calling **`webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`**, the single point of HTTP delivery and HMAC-SHA256 signing.
