Engine: CEL (google/cel-go) wrapped by the in-repo filter engine in internal/filter (internal/filter/engine.go:24,111 `NewEngine`/`DefaultEngine`, schema/variables in internal/filter/schema.go). Mechanism: the CEL expression is compiled to an AST, lowered into a dialect-agnostic condition/IR tree (internal/filter/ir.go), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL + args by internal/filter/render.go (switching on `DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL`), and each dialect driver appends the result to its WHERE clause via `filter.AppendConditions` (internal/filter/helpers.go:9; e.g. store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61).
