— Premise is wrong: **neither** — there is no message broker at all. No NATS, RabbitMQ, or AMQP dependency appears in `go.mod` or any `.go` file, and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by **4 goroutines** started in the package `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`webhook.go:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184-196`), which drops the payload with a warning if the channel is full. Consequence worth noting: the queue is unbounded-loss and non-durable — pending webhooks are lost on restart.
