— Premise is wrong: **neither**. There is no message broker, no queue declaration, and no NATS/RabbitMQ/AMQP dependency in `go.mod`. Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` of capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by **4 goroutines** started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-196`). Nothing is persisted, so queued deliveries are lost on restart.
