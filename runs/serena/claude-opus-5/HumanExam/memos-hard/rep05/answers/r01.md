— Premise is wrong: there is **no Redis** anywhere in the codebase (no client dependency, no configuration). Caching is a process-local, in-memory map with a janitor goroutine: `store/cache/cache.go` (`Cache`, `New` at `:76`, `Set`/`SetWithTTL` `:93-119`, `Close` `:187`). The store creates three of them — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — in `store.New`, `store/store.go:38-52`. The TTL for user entries is the shared `DefaultTTL` = **10 minutes** (`store/store.go:40`), with 5-minute cleanup sweeps and a 1000-item cap; entries are also invalidated explicitly on user delete (`store/user_delete.go:38-46`). Caches are per-process, so they are not shared between instances.
