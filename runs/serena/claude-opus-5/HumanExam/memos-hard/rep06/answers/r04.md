— False premise: **neither**. There is no message broker at all — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka in the Go sources or `go.mod`, and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery is in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` of capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Producers use `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which **drops** the event with a warning when the buffer is full (`:191-195`) — delivery is best-effort, with no persistence or retry.
