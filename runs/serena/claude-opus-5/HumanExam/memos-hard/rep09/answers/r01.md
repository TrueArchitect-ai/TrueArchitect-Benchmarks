— Premise is wrong: there is no Redis anywhere in this repo (no `redis` import, no `redis` entry in `go.mod`, no config key). Caching is an in-process, in-memory implementation: `store/cache/cache.go` (`cache.Cache`, `cache.Interface` at `:10-32`). The `Store` creates three such caches — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — at `store/store.go:51-53`, all sharing one `cache.Config` with `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000` (`store/store.go:40-45`). So user entries expire after **10 minutes** (they are also explicitly invalidated on user delete, `store/user_delete.go:40`). Caches are stopped in `Store.Close` (`store/store.go:74-76`).
