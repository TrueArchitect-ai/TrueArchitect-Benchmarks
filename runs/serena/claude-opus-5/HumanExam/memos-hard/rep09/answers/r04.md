— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker at all — no NATS, no RabbitMQ/AMQP, no queue declaration, and no such dependency in `go.mod`. Webhook delivery is in-process: `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` declares `asyncPostQueue`, a buffered Go channel of capacity 128, and the package `init()` (`:42-55`) starts 4 goroutine workers that range over it and call `Post`. Producers use `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the event with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). There is no persistence, no retry, and delivery does not survive a restart.
