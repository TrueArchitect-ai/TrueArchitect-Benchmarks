— Premise is wrong: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used; there is no message broker and no queue declaration in the codebase. Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` of capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by **4 goroutines** started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync`, which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the delivery with a warning if the buffer is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`). Each worker calls `Post` (`:120`), which POSTs via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient` with a 30 s timeout (`:31-38`, `:152`). Consequence: queued webhooks are lost on restart and there is no retry.
