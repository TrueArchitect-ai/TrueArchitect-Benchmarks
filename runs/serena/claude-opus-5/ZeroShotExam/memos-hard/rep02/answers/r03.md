The premise doesn't hold: **there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.** The `internal/scheduler` package is a standalone cron library that is never wired into the running application — nothing outside the package imports it.

What actually exists:

- `internal/scheduler/` (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`) is a self-contained cron scheduler. Its only importers are its own tests: `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`. No production code under `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/` registers a job with it.
- The `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily) cleanup you may have seen is documentation/example code, not a registered job:
  - `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-17` — a `"daily-cleanup"` job in the package doc comment
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-36` — the same snippet in the Quick Start
  - `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-99` — a `"cleanup"` job in `Example_multipleJobs`, alongside example `health-check` (`*/5 * * * *`) and `weekly-backup` (`0 1 * * 0`) jobs
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:241-245` also shows an illustrative `"stale-session-cleanup"` at `"0 3 * * *"`

The only real recurring background task in the server is the S3 presign refresher, and it's a `time.Ticker` loop, not cron: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` sets `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, driving the ticker at `runner.go:30`. (There's also an SSE heartbeat ticker at `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`, unrelated to maintenance.)
