The premise is incorrect: **there is no nightly database-cleanup job registered in this repository's cron scheduler.** No time can be reported because no such job exists.

What's actually there:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a self-contained, general-purpose cron scheduler library (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`). It is **not wired into the application at all** — the only files importing `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`). Nothing under `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/` registers any job with it.

- The `0 2 * * *` (2 AM daily) cleanup schedule you may be thinking of appears only in **documentation and example code**, never as a live registration:
  - `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` — package doc example, `Name: "daily-cleanup"`, `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`
  - `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-99` — `Example_multipleJobs`, `Name: "cleanup"`, `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-37` and `:241-245` — illustrative snippets (`daily-cleanup`, `stale-session-cleanup`)

The actual background work in the server is not cron-scheduled and not database cleanup:

- `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,30` — a ticker-based runner refreshing S3 presigned links every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`), started at `server/server.go:162-169`.
- `store/cache/cache.go:46-47,199-218` — an in-memory cache eviction loop running every 5 minutes, unrelated to the database.
- `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` — a one-shot payload rebuild, no schedule.
