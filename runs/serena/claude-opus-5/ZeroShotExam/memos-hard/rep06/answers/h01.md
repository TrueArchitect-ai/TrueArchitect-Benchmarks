- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:44`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-signs the object key via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the fresh URL back to the attachment's `Reference` along with a new `LastPresignedTime` in the payload (`runner.go:104-117`). This is what keeps the stored S3 links from expiring.
- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). An attachment is skipped unless its last presign is more than 4 days old — the presigned URLs themselves are issued with a 5-day expiry, so there's a 1-day safety margin (`runner.go:81-88`).
- **Wiring**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) constructs the runner at `server/server.go:162`, does a synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163` so links are fresh right after boot, then launches the ticker loop in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` (`server/server.go:166-171`). `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked at the end of `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:129`); shutdown cancels its dedicated context via `stopBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:140`, `176`).
