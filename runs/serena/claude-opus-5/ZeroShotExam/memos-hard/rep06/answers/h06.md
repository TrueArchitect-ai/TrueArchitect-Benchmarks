The single function where delivery actually happens is:

**`Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:118`** (it builds the `POST` to `requestPayload.URL`, adds the Standard-Webhooks HMAC headers, and issues `safeClient.Do(req)` at line 152). `PostAsync` (line 175) is only an enqueue onto `asyncPostQueue`; the four init-spawned workers all call `Post`.

The URL is user-configured through `CreateUserWebhook`/`UpdateUserWebhook` (`user_service_webhooks.go:74,161,182`), validated by `webhook.ValidateURL`, and SSRF-guarded at dial time by `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`), which rejects reserved/private IPs.

Two other outbound paths exist but don't share this convergence point: OAuth2 SSO user-info fetches to the admin-configured `UserInfoUrl` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go:85-91`, reached from `CreateSession` → `resolveSSOIdentity`, `auth_service_sso.go:104`), and link-metadata/image fetches in `internal/httpgetter/` (URL comes per-request, not from stored config).
