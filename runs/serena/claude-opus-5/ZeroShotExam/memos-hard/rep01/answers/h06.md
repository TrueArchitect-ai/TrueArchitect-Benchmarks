**Convergence function: `Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`**

Every one of the above funnels through `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`, called only at `memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`), which enqueues onto `asyncPostQueue`; four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:43-52`) drain that queue and call `Post`. `Post` is the sole place the request is built and sent — it marshals the payload, adds the Standard Webhooks HMAC headers when a signing secret is set, and issues `safeClient.Do(req)` against `requestPayload.URL`.

Two related notes:
- The URL comes from a per-user webhook record (`store.GetUserWebhooks`), registered/edited via `CreateUserWebhook`/`UpdateUserWebhook` in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`. Those handlers themselves only do a DNS resolution inside `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:64`) — no HTTP request.
- SSRF is guarded at two layers: `ValidateURL` at registration time, and `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:58`) on the shared `safeClient` transport at dial time, which re-resolves and rejects reserved/private IPs (defeating DNS rebinding). Both are bypassable via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`).
