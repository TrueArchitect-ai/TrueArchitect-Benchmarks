**MemoService is served over (1) HTTP/JSON REST via gRPC-Gateway and (2) Connect RPC** (connectrpc, the browser-facing replacement for grpc-web). There is no native gRPC listener — the generated `RegisterMemoServiceServer` in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service_grpc.pb.go:439` is never called; the same `APIV1Service` implementation backs both live protocols.

- **gRPC-Gateway REST (HTTP/JSON)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` (`v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, generated handlers in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`); the mux is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:126-128`.

- **Connect RPC** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (`wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the per-method Connect wrappers that adapt the gRPC methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304+` (the `// MemoService` section); the mux is mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:140-144`.
