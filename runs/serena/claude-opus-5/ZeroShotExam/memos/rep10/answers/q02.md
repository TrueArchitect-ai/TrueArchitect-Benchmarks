**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project embeds migrations for three SQL dialects, one directory per driver:

- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

These are embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`, and the corresponding driver implementations live in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`, selected in `store/db/db.go`.
