**`store.Driver` — `store/driver.go`** (declared at `store/driver.go:10`)

It's the interface "for store driver… contains all methods that store database driver should implement" — `GetDB()`, `Close()`, `IsInitialized()`, `GetDatabaseSize()`, plus the full CRUD surface for every model (Attachment, Memo, MemoRelation, InstanceSetting, User, UserSetting, IdentityProvider, Inbox, Reaction, MemoShare, UserIdentity).

Backends implementing it live under `store/db/`:
- `store/db/sqlite/`
- `store/db/mysql/`
- `store/db/postgres/`

`store/db/db.go:14` (`NewDBDriver`) is the factory that selects one based on `profile.Driver` and returns it as a `store.Driver`.
