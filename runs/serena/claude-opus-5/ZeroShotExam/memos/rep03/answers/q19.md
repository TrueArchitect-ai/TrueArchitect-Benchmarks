One nuance worth flagging: MemoService operations are *also* reachable as MCP tools (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-25`, served at `/mcp` by `server/router/mcp/service.go`), but that path isn't a third registration of the service — it replays the OpenAPI-described REST routes in-process through `server/router/mcp/adapter.go`, so it sits on top of the gRPC-Gateway protocol rather than beside it.
