`web/src/connect.ts`

This is the single module where all ConnectRPC service clients are created:

- `web/src/connect.ts:184` — builds the shared `createConnectTransport` (binary format, `window.location.origin`, credentialed fetch, auth interceptor).
- `web/src/connect.ts:192-203` — exports the clients via `createClient(...)`: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`.
- `web/src/connect.ts:65-72` — also creates a separate interceptor-free `refreshTransport` and `refreshAuthClient` used for token refresh, to avoid recursion.
