**Engine:** Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`, go.mod:13), wrapped by the in-repo filter engine at `internal/filter/` — entry point `internal/filter/engine.go` (`filter.DefaultEngine()` / `Engine.Compile` / `Engine.CompileToStatement`).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** `ListMemos` validates `request.Filter` and pushes it into `memoFind.Filters` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-215`, validation in `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336` `validateFilter`); the store driver then calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, <dialect>, &where, &args)` (`internal/filter/helpers.go:9`), which runs the three-phase pipeline — cel-go compiles/type-checks the expression against the memo schema env (`internal/filter/schema.go`, `NewSchema` + `cel.NewEnv`), `internal/filter/parser.go` lowers the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`, folding `now`/`timestamp()`/`duration()` to constants), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks the IR to emit a parameterized SQL fragment plus args for the target dialect.

**Dialect handling:**
- Dialects enumerated in `internal/filter/schema.go:14-16` (`DialectSQLite`, `DialectMySQL`, `DialectPostgres`); each `Field` carries per-dialect column expressions (`Expressions map[DialectName]string`, e.g. `('users/' || %s)` vs `CONCAT(...)`, `UNIX_TIMESTAMP(%s)` for MySQL).
- Call sites: `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`.
- Placeholders: `?` for SQLite/MySQL, `$n` for Postgres, with `PlaceholderOffset` threaded from existing arg count (`internal/filter/render.go:842-849`, `internal/filter/helpers.go:12-14`); booleans become 0/1 on SQLite (`render.go:851`).
- JSON/tag/text differences (e.g. `LIKE` vs `JSON_CONTAINS` vs `@>`, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, `LENGTH` vs `CHAR_LENGTH`) live in renderer helpers, documented in `internal/filter/README.md`.
