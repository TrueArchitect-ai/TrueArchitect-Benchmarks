**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations are embedded from `store/migration/` (`store/migrator.go:44-45`, `//go:embed migration`), with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

`store/migrator.go:141` globs `<basePath>*/*.sql`, where the base path is selected per configured driver, and `store/db/` holds the corresponding driver implementations.
