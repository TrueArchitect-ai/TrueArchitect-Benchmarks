**Runners the server actually starts**

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — started in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`). Two triggers: one synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup, then a `time.NewTicker(12h)` loop in `Run()` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`). Each pass re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose last presign is older than 4 days. It's the only entry in `backgroundRunnerCancels`/`backgroundRunnerWG`, cancelled on shutdown (`server/server.go:176-201`).
- **store cache cleanup goroutines ×3** (`store/cache/cache.go:83`, loop at `:199-213`) — started implicitly by `cache.New` in `store.New` for `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (`store/store.go:38-53`). Trigger: `time.NewTicker(CleanupInterval)` = 5 minutes; stopped via `Cache.Close()`.
- **HTTP serve goroutine** (`server/server.go:124-128`) — `httpServer.Serve(listener)`; trigger is incoming connections, not a schedule.
- **signal-watch goroutine** (`cmd/memos/main.go:99-103`) — blocks on SIGINT/SIGTERM, then calls `s.Shutdown`.

Per-connection / per-event goroutines (not schedulable runners, listed for completeness): SSE heartbeat ticker at 30s per connected client (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:17,64`), fire-and-forget webhook dispatch (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:44`), async email send (`internal/email/email.go:18`), async PAT last-used update (`server/auth/authenticator.go:180`).

**Unused infrastructure**

- **`internal/scheduler`** — a full cron scheduler (5/6-field cron parser, timezone support, middleware chain with Recovery/Logging, graceful shutdown; `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, `doc.go`) is **never used by the server**. The only files importing `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own `integration_test.go` and `example_test.go`. No cron job is registered anywhere; `server.go` hand-rolls its runner lifecycle with `context.CancelFunc` + `sync.WaitGroup` instead.
- **`server/runner/memopayload`** — despite living under `server/runner/`, it is not a background runner at all. `RebuildMemoPayload` is called synchronously from the memo service on create/update (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`); nothing starts it in a goroutine or on a schedule.
