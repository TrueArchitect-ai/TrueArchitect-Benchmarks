**Mechanism (one sentence):** `ListMemos` validates `request.Filter` via `s.validateFilter` (`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336`, called at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211`) and passes it through `store.FindMemo.Filters`, where each driver's `ListMemos` calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, <dialect>, &where, &args)` — which runs the three-phase pipeline `cel-go env.Compile` → schema-validated IR (`buildCondition`) → `newRenderer(...).Render` — emitting a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus bound args that get AND-ed into the `WHERE` clause.
