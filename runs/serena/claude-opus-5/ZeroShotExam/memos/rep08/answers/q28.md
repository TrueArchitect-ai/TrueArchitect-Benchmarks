**Runners the server explicitly starts** (`Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:151-176`, called from `Server.Start`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — one synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup, then a goroutine running `Run(ctx)` driven by a `time.Ticker` at a hard-coded `runnerInterval = 12h` (`runner.go:28-40`). It re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, in batches of 100. It's the *only* entry in `backgroundRunnerCancels`/`backgroundRunnerWG`, and is stopped via its own cancel func in `Shutdown` (`server/server.go:178-183`).

**Goroutines started indirectly by dependencies the server constructs** (not registered as "background runners", not covered by the WaitGroup):

- **3 × cache cleanup loops** (instanceSetting, user, userSetting) — started by `go c.cleanupLoop()` inside `cache.New` (`store/cache/cache.go:83,199-215`), driven by a `time.Ticker` at `CleanupInterval = 5m` set in `store.New` (`store/store.go:39-53`). Stopped by `Store.Close()` at shutdown (`store/store.go:72-79`).
- **4 × webhook dispatch workers** — package `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`, driven by receiving from the buffered `asyncPostQueue` channel (event-driven, no timer, no shutdown path).
- **2 × email send workers** — package `init()` in `internal/email/email.go:15-30`, driven by the `asyncEmailQueue` channel (same pattern).
- Request-scoped, not workers: SSE heartbeat `time.Ticker` at 30s per open connection (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:16-17,64`); PAT last-used write fire-and-forget goroutine (`server/auth/authenticator.go:180`); signal-wait goroutine in `cmd/memos/main.go:99`.

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:**

- **`internal/scheduler/` is entirely unused by the server.** It's a complete cron scheduler (5/6-field cron parser, timezone support, middleware chain with Recovery/Logging, graceful `Stop`) — `scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`, `doc.go` — but the only importers of `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`, `integration_test.go:13`). No production code registers a job or calls `Start()`. The s3presign runner rolls its own ticker instead.
- **`server/runner/memopayload`'s `Runner` is dead as a runner.** `NewRunner`/`RunOnce` (batch rebuild of all memo payloads, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:22-79`) has no callers anywhere; only the standalone `RebuildMemoPayload` function is used, synchronously on the create/update request paths (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`).
