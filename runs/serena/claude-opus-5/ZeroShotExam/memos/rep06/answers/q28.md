**Runners the server explicitly starts** — all from `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:158-181`), called at the end of `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:131`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — the only registered background runner.
