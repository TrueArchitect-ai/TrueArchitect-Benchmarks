**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go` (`go.mod:13`), wrapped by the project's own memo filter engine in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `schema.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `helpers.go`; docs in `internal/filter/README.md`).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** `ListMemos` validates `request.Filter` and appends it to `memoFind.Filters` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-215`, validation at `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336`); each store driver then calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` + `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its own `DialectName` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`), which runs a three-phase pipeline — cel-go compiles/type-checks the expression against the memo-specific CEL env and field whitelist in `internal/filter/schema.go` (`NewSchema`), `internal/filter/parser.go` normalizes the AST into a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a parameterized `WHERE` fragment plus args, encapsulating dialect differences there (`?` vs `$n` placeholders with offset tracking in `addArg`, `internal/filter/render.go:842-849`; JSON access `json_extract`/`JSON_EXTRACT`/`->>`; tag membership as `LIKE` for SQLite, `JSON_CONTAINS` for MySQL, `@>` for Postgres; `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`; boolean coercion).

Notable details:
- Time fields (`created_ts`, `updated_ts`) are CEL timestamps; `now`, `duration(...)`, and `timestamp(...)` fold to epoch seconds at compile time, with `now` frozen once per compile (`internal/filter/engine.go:17-21`, `Compile` at `:47`).
- Rendering has three outcomes (`internal/filter/render.go:31-57`): normal SQL, `""` for a trivially-true filter (dropped by `AppendConditions`, `internal/filter/helpers.go:17-22`), and `1 = 0` for an unsatisfiable one.
- The same machinery backs attachments via a separate schema/engine (`filter.DefaultAttachmentEngine`, `internal/filter/engine.go:117`).
