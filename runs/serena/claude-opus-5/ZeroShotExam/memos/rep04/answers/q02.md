- `store/migration/` contains exactly three dialect subtrees: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, `store/migration/mysql/` (each with per-version dirs of `.sql` files, e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.30/`).
- `store/migrator.go:39` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and `store/migrator.go:44-45` embeds the whole `migration` tree; `store/migrator.go:255` selects the path by `s.profile.Driver`.
- Matching driver implementations exist at `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, `store/db/mysql/`.
