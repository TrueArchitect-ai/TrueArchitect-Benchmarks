**Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over an authenticated `GET /api/v1/sse` stream; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`.**

### Mechanism
- SSE, not WebSockets. `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25` registers `GET /api/v1/sse`; `handleSSE` authenticates via a Bearer token (`auth.Authenticator`), sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `: connected`, then loops writing `data: <json>\n\n` plus a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s (`sseHeartbeatInterval`, sse_handler.go:17).
- Browser side: `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (fetch-based stream against `/api/v1/sse`, web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203).

### Hub
- `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (line 56) holds a `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` under an `RWMutex` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. `Broadcast` (line 115) marshals the event once and does a non-blocking send per client, dropping events for slow clients. `SSEClient.canReceive` (line 134) filters: `Private` memos go only to the creator or an admin; public/protected go to everyone.
- Event types (sse_hub.go:15-21): `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`.
- Wiring: constructed at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`, routes registered at v1.go:125, exposed on the server at `server/server.go:77`.
