All ConnectRPC service clients are created there via `createClient(...)` over a shared `createConnectTransport` instance:

- `web/src/connect.ts:184` — the main transport (binary format, `credentials: "include"` fetch, auth interceptor)
- `web/src/connect.ts:192-203` — the exported clients: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`
