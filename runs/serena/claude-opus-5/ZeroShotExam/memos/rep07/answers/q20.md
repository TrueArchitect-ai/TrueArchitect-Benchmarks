**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over plain HTTP — a `GET /api/v1/sse` endpoint that writes `Content-Type: text/event-stream` with `data: {...}` frames plus `: heartbeat` comments every 30s (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`). The browser consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (fetch-based stream with `Accept: text/event-stream`, since a Bearer token is required). No WebSockets are involved.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, per-client 32-event buffered channel, drops events for slow clients) with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`, and per-client visibility filtering in `SSEClient.canReceive`. It is constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`, wired to the route at `v1.go:125`, and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205`. Event types/payload struct also live in `sse_hub.go`; helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

**Handlers that broadcast:**
- `CreateMemo` — `memo.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`, skipped when suppressed via context by `CreateMemoComment`)
- `DeleteMemo` — `memo.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `memo.comment.created` on the parent memo (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` — `reaction.upserted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` — `reaction.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161`)
- `memo.updated` is emitted from the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`), called by `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`)
