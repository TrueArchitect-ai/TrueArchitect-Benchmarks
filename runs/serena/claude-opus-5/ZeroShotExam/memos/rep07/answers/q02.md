**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

They're embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the dialect-specific subdirectory is selected at runtime by `getMigrationBasePath()` (used in `store/migrator.go:141` and `store/migrator.go:300`).
