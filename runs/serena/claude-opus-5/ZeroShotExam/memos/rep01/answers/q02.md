**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

The whole tree is embedded into the binary at `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`), and the per-dialect path is selected at runtime via `getMigrationBasePath()`, used in the glob at `store/migrator.go:141` and `store/migrator.go:300`.
