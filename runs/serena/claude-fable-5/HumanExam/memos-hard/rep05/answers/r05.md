— Premise is false: there is no GraphQL anywhere (no schema, resolvers, or gqlgen/graphql dependency). The actual API surface is Protobuf-defined gRPC services with Connect + grpc-gateway REST/JSON transcoding: `.proto` definitions in `proto/api/v1/`, deps `connectrpc.com/connect`, `google.golang.org/grpc`, `grpc-gateway/v2` in `go.mod`, handlers in `server/router/api/v1/`, plus auxiliary RSS, MCP, and fileserver routers.
