— Premise is false: no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ, nor Kafka/Redis) is used. Webhook delivery is in-process via an asynchronous bounded goroutine queue: `asyncPostQueue`, a buffered channel of capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`) that call `Post`; producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), dropping with a warning on overflow.
