There is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled in the cron scheduler — the question's premise doesn't hold for this repo.

Details:

- The repo does contain a cron scheduler package at `internal/scheduler/` (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`), but it is not imported or used anywhere in the application code — no jobs are registered with it at all.
- The only "cleanup" job that appears in connection with the scheduler is a **documentation example**: a `"daily-cleanup"` job with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily) shown in `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` and `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-35`. That's illustrative sample code, not a real scheduled job.
- The server's actual background work doesn't go through the cron scheduler and isn't database cleanup: `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`) launches a memo-payload rebuild runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) and an S3 presign refresh runner that ticks every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). The only cleanup-like mechanism is the in-memory cache's 5-minute `CleanupInterval` in `store/store.go:42`, which is unrelated to a nightly database job.

So if you were told there's a nightly DB-cleanup cron job, the closest real artifacts are the 2 AM example in the scheduler docs and the 12-hour S3 presign runner — neither is a registered nightly database-cleanup job.
