The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) does this. Attachments stored in S3 have presigned GET URLs saved as their `Reference`, and presigned URLs expire (they're generated with a 5-day expiration). The runner periodically re-signs them so the links stay valid.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method pages through S3-backed attachments in batches of 100, skips any whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (of the 5-day expiry), and otherwise generates a fresh presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`.
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup.
- **Wiring:** `startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:153` — the runner is created and run once at `server/server.go:162-163`, then started as a continuous background goroutine at `server/server.go:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked during server startup at `server/server.go:129`.
