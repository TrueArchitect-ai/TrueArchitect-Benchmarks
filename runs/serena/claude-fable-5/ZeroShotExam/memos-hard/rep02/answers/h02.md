The outbound HTTP delivery for webhooks executes in `Post` (with `PostAsync` enqueueing to the same worker pool that calls `Post`) in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. It marshals the payload, builds the POST request, optionally adds an HMAC-SHA256 Standard Webhooks signature, and sends it via the shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180`).

The SSRF protection lives at two layers:

1. **Dial-time blocking (the real guard):** `safeClient`'s transport uses `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`), which resolves the target host at connection time and refuses to dial any address matching a reserved/private range — this also defeats DNS-rebinding, since the check happens on the actual connection, not just at registration.
2. **Registration-time validation:** `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83` rejects webhook URLs up front if they aren't http/https or resolve to a reserved IP. Both layers share `isReservedIP` and the `reservedCIDRs` list (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`), covering loopback, RFC-1918 ranges, link-local/IMDS (169.254.0.0/16), and IPv6 equivalents; the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag can disable this for self-hosted setups.
