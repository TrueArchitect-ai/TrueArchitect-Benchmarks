The premise doesn't hold: there is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled in the cron scheduler. The repo contains a cron scheduler package at `internal/scheduler/`, but nothing in the application ever registers a job with it — the package isn't imported anywhere outside its own directory. The "daily-cleanup" job with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily) that appears in `internal/scheduler/doc.go` and `internal/scheduler/README.md` is only a documentation/usage example, not a real configured job, and it isn't database-related.

The actual background work in the server doesn't use this cron scheduler at all: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` runs on a plain `time.Ticker` every 12 hours, and `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` exposes a `RunOnce` rebuild — neither is a nightly database cleanup.
