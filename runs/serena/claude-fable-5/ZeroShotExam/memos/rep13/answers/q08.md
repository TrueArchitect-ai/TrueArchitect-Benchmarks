**`Driver` — `store/driver.go`**

The `Driver` interface is defined at `store/driver.go:10`. All three database backends — SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL, located in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, and `store/db/postgres` — implement it, and `store/db/db.go` selects which implementation to instantiate based on the configured driver.
