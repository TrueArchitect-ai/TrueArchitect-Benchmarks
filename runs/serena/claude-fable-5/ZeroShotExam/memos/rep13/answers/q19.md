MemoService is exposed over two protocols:

1. **HTTP/JSON REST (gRPC-Gateway)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers it on the grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux`, which is then mounted at `/api/v1/*`).
2. **Connect RPC (connectrpc.com/connect, used by browser clients in place of grpc-web)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the Connect wrapper methods that adapt the gRPC-style implementation living in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (the `// MemoService` section around line 304).

There is no standalone `grpc.NewServer` registering `RegisterMemoServiceServer` outside generated code, so plain gRPC is not one of the two — it's REST via gRPC-Gateway plus Connect RPC.
