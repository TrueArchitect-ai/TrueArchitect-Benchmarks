The table is **`memo_relation`**. It stores `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and a `type` column linking one memo to another related memo. It's defined in the schema migrations for all three supported databases: `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql` (originally introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`).
