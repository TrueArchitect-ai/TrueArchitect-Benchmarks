Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting memo handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment` — plus `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` for reactions.

Details:

- **Mechanism**: HTTP Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` and streams `data:` frames plus heartbeat comments (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`). The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- **Hub**: `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast` and the `SSEEvent` types (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`) is implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`.
- **Broadcasting handlers** (all on `APIV1Service`):
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (skipped when invoked from comment creation)
  - `UpdateMemo` — broadcasts through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`, called at `memo_service.go:513`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - Reaction events on memos: `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161`
