**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoAttachments and SetMemoRelations via the shared update helper).**

Details:

- **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events. The hub (`SSEHub`) manages `SSEClient` subscribers and pushes JSON-encoded `SSEEvent`s to connected browsers; the HTTP endpoint lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, with event-building helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
- **Hub**: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Close`, and `Broadcast` (line 115), with per-client visibility filtering in `canReceive`.
- **Memo handlers that call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast`**:
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (suppressed via `withSuppressSSE` when invoked from comment creation)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo` — broadcasts through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`), called at `memo_service.go:513`
  - `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) also broadcast via that same helper.

Additionally, the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` broadcast memo reaction events through the hub (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161`), though those are reaction rather than memo handlers.
