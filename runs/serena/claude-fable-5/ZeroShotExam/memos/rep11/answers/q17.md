The memo CRUD mutations and queries live in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` — it defines the TanStack Query hooks for listing, getting, creating, updating, and deleting memos (e.g. `memoServiceClient.listMemos`, `getMemo`, `createMemo`, `updateMemo` calls are all in that file).

It uses the `memoServiceClient`, a Connect RPC client created in `web/src/connect.ts` via `createClient(MemoService, transport)`, where `MemoService` is the generated service definition from `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts`.

Answer: `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`; `memoServiceClient`
