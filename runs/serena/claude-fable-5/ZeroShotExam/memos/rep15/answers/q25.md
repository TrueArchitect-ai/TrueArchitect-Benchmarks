**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `google/cel-go`; the filter engine lives in `/bench/repo/internal/filter/` (this is the memos repository).

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` passes the request's filter string into the store's `FindMemo`, and each store driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, plus the `mysql` and `postgres` siblings in `store/db/`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect. The engine runs a three-phase pipeline documented in `internal/filter/README.md`: the CEL expression is parsed with cel-go and validated against the memo schema (`schema.go`), the CEL AST is normalized into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (`parser.go`, `ir.go`), and the renderer (`render.go`) walks that IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args per dialect — handling differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>` for the `memo.payload` JSON column, and tag membership via `LIKE` (SQLite), `JSON_CONTAINS` (MySQL), or `@>` (Postgres). `engine.go` glues the phases together (`Compile` / `CompileToStatement`), and `helpers.go` provides the `AppendConditions` helper the store drivers use to append the rendered condition to their WHERE clause.
