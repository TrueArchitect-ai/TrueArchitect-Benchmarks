The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and `createConnectTransport` from `@connectrpc/connect-web`, then creates and exports clients for all the services (e.g., `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`) around lines 192–199.
