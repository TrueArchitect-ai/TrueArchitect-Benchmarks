MemoService is exposed over two protocols, both mounted on the same Echo server in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (there is no plain gRPC listener — `RegisterMemoServiceServer`/`grpc.NewServer` are never called outside generated code):

1. **HTTP/JSON REST via gRPC-Gateway** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, where `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the service on the gateway mux (served under `/api/v1/*`).
2. **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is registered on the Connect mux (served under `/memos.api.v1.*`). The wrapping of the underlying `APIV1Service` methods into Connect handlers lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService section starting at line 304).
