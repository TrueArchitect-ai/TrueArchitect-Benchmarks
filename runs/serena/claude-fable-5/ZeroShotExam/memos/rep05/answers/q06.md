**`memo_relation`**

This table links one memo to another via `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` columns (plus a relation `type`). It's defined in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and appears in the latest schemas for all three backends: `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`.
