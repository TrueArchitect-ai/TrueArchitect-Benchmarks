**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). The hub delivers JSON change events (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`) to subscribed browser clients, with the HTTP endpoint in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`.

**Hub implementation:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — defines `SSEHub` (thread-safe client registry with `Broadcast`), `SSEClient`, `SSEEvent`, and the event-type constants.

**Memo handlers that broadcast** (all call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
- `UpdateMemo`, `SetMemoAttachments`, and `SetMemoRelations` broadcast indirectly via the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66,71`), called at `memo_service.go:513`, `memo_attachment_service.go:48`, and `memo_relation_service.go:48`.

(Related but not memo handlers: `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161` also broadcast memo-scoped reaction events.)
