The chain is: the React hook `useCreateMemo` (web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts:178) calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo`, where the client is created in web/src/connect.ts:197; that invokes the `CreateMemo` RPC declared in proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:21, handled server-side by `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` (server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70), which calls `Store.CreateMemo` (store/memo.go:109) delegating to the dialect driver — e.g. the SQLite driver's `CreateMemo` (store/db/sqlite/memo.go:16), which builds and executes the `INSERT INTO memo ...` statement.

- web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts
- web/src/connect.ts
- proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto
- server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go
- store/memo.go
- store/db/sqlite/memo.go

(Equivalent drivers exist at store/db/mysql/memo.go and store/db/postgres/memo.go. There is also a Connect-protocol wrapper `ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` in server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:306 that forwards to the same `APIV1Service` handler.)
