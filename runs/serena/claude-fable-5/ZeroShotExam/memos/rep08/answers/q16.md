When `Store.DeleteMemo` runs (called from the API's memo-delete handler), the store layer cleans up these records before deleting the memo row itself (`store/memo.go:140-159`):

- **Memo relation records** — `memo_relation` rows where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and rows where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`), via two `DeleteMemoRelation` calls (`store/memo.go:142-147`, implemented in `store/memo_relation.go`).
- **Attachment records** — all attachments linked to the memo, listed with `ListAttachments{MemoID}` and removed one-by-one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:148-157`).

Only after those cleanups does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row itself.
