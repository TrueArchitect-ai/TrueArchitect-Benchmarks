**`memo_relation`**

The table has columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`, linking one memo to another related memo. It's defined in the schema files for all three supported databases: `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (lines 47–52), `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, and was originally introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`.
