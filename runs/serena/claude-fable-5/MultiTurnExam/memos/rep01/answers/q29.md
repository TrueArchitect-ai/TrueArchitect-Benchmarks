**Interceptor:** web/src/connect.ts — the `authInterceptor` (line 156) attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` headers to every ConnectRPC request and reactively refreshes on 401/expiry (with in-memory token state and cross-tab sharing in web/src/auth-state.ts).

**Token kinds:** short-lived JWT **access tokens** (held in memory/localStorage, sent as a Bearer header) and long-lived (30-day) JWT **refresh tokens** (carried in the `memos_refresh` HttpOnly cookie, used by the interceptor's `refreshAuthClient.refreshToken` call over a separate no-interceptor transport to avoid recursion).

**Backend package:** server/auth — `server/auth/token.go` defines/generates and validates the JWT access & refresh token claims, and `server/auth/authenticator.go` (the `Authenticator`) validates incoming tokens (including `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` and bearer resolution) and loads the user, with request extraction helpers in `server/auth/extract.go`.
