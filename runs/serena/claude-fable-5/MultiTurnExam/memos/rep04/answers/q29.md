**Interceptor:** `web/src/connect.ts` — an `@connectrpc/connect` `Interceptor` that attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` to each request (helper `setAuthorizationHeader`, line 97), proactively refreshes tokens near expiry, and on 401 performs a deduplicated reactive refresh-and-retry (token storage/sharing lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts`).

**Token kinds:** two JWTs — a short-lived **access token** (~15 minutes, kept client-side in localStorage and sent as a Bearer header) and a long-lived **refresh token** (~30 days, delivered as an HTTP cookie via `Set-Cookie` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_session.go:44-47` and exchanged through the `RefreshToken` RPC).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — `server/auth/token.go` issues/parses the JWTs (using `github.com/golang-jwt/jwt/v5`) and `server/auth/authenticator.go` validates them and resolves the user for each request.
