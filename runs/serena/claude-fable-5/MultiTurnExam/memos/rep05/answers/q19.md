MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **REST (gRPC-Gateway / HTTP JSON)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at line 108 registers the service on the grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux`, which is mounted under `/api/v1/*`.
