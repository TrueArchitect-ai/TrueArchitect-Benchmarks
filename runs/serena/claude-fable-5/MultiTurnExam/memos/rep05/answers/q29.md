**Interceptor:** `web/src/connect.ts` — an `Interceptor` (`@connectrpc/connect`) attached to the Connect transport attaches `Authorization: Bearer <access token>` to every request, does a preflight refresh when the token is near expiry (`REQUEST_TOKEN_EXPIRY_BUFFER_MS`), and reactively refreshes-and-retries on 401. Token storage/sharing lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts` (localStorage plus a BroadcastChannel so tabs share refreshed tokens).

**Token kinds managed:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT sent in the Authorization header, kept in localStorage) and a long-lived **refresh token** (HTTP cookie; the interceptor calls the `AuthService.RefreshToken` RPC via a separate `refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient` in `web/src/connect.ts:65-90` to mint new access tokens).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` — `server/auth/authenticator.go` (`Authenticator.AuthenticateToUser` validates the Authorization header or refresh-token cookie; `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` checks the parsed claims against stored tokens, revocation, and expiry), with JWT parsing/generation in `server/auth/token.go` and header/cookie extraction in `server/auth/extract.go`.
