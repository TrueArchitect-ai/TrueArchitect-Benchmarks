**Interceptor:** `web/src/connect.ts` — `authInterceptor` (line 156), attached to the Connect transport (line 188). It attaches the access token as a Bearer header, does a preflight refresh when the token is near expiry, and reactively refreshes and retries on 401 (deduplicated via the token-refresh manager). Token storage/sharing across tabs lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts` (localStorage + BroadcastChannel).

**Token kinds:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT, sent as `Authorization: Bearer`, kept in localStorage by the frontend) and a long-lived **refresh token** (HTTP-only cookie, exchanged via the `AuthService.RefreshToken` RPC using a separate `refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient` in `connect.ts:65-83`).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — `Authenticator` in `server/auth/authenticator.go` validates the Bearer access token and falls back to the refresh-token cookie (`AuthenticateToUser` at line 190, `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` at line 61, with JWT parsing in `server/auth/token.go` and header/cookie extraction in `server/auth/extract.go`).
