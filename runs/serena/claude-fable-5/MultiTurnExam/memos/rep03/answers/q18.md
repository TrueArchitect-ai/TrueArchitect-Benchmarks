The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (`store/driver.go:10`) have their entry files at:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them at startup based on the configured driver name is:

**NewDBDriver — store/db/db.go** (defined at line 14, switching on the profile's driver value, e.g. `case "postgres"` at line 23).
