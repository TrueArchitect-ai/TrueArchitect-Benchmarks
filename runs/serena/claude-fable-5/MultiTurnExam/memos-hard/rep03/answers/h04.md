Other `mysql.*` references (e.g., `store/test/containers.go:101-105`) are the testcontainers-go MySQL container module, not this driver package — tests obtain the driver by going through the factory (`db.NewDBDriver(profile)` in `store/test/store.go:28,50` and `server/router/api/v1/test/memo_service_benchmark_test.go:70`).
