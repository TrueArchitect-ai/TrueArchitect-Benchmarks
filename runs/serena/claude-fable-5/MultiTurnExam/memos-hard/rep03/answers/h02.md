**Delivery function:** `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line 120) — it marshals the payload, optionally adds Standard-Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signature headers, and sends the POST via the shared `safeClient`. Asynchronous dispatch goes through `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded channel drained by four worker goroutines that call `Post`.

**Protection mechanism:** dial-time SSRF blocking via `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, which is installed as the `DialContext` of `safeClient`'s transport (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). At connection time it resolves the hostname and rejects any IP matching the reserved/private ranges in `reservedCIDRs` / `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54` (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS 169.254.0.0/16, and IPv6 equivalents). Checking at dial time rather than only at URL-save time also defeats DNS rebinding. A complementary early check, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`), enforces http/https scheme and the same IP restrictions when a webhook URL is registered, and the escape hatch `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) can disable the restriction for self-hosted deployments.
