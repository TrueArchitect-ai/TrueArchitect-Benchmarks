**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours — wired in `server/server.go:162`**

The component is the background runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Attachments stored in S3 are referenced via presigned GET URLs, which expire (5-day expiry); the runner's `CheckAndPresign` sweeps all S3-type attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates the presigned URL for any whose last presign is older than 4 days, updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`).

- **Schedule:** a ticker every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup.
- **Wiring site:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner at `server/server.go:162`, runs it once synchronously (`server/server.go:163`), then launches the continuous loop in a goroutine (`server/server.go:167-171`). `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
