**Premise correction: neither.** There is no message broker in this codebase — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka client appears anywhere in the Go source or in `go.mod`.

**Actual mechanism:** webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process with a buffered Go channel acting as the queue. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

- The "queue" is `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.
- The "workers" are four goroutines started in the package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over the channel and calling `Post` to execute the HTTP delivery.
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking send; if the 128-slot buffer is full the dispatch is dropped with a logged warning rather than persisted or retried.

So delivery is best-effort and in-memory only — there is no durable queue, no broker connection, and nothing survives a process restart.
