**Component — schedule — wiring site:** the S3 presign background runner (`s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — runs every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup — wired into server startup in `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` (called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`).

How it works: for attachments stored with `AttachmentStorageType_S3`, the runner's `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates the S3 presigned GET URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, updating each attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`. Presigned URLs expire after 5 days, so it re-signs any URL older than 4 days — keeping links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from going stale.
