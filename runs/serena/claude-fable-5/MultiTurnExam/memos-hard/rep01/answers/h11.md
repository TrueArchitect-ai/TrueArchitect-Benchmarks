**1st: `store.User` — referenced from ~6 packages outside the store layer; 2nd: `store.Memo` — ~4 packages.**

Method: the persistent entities are the structs in the `store` package (backed by DB tables — `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). I counted distinct non-test packages outside `store/` that reference each `store.<Type>`:

- **`store.User`** (declared in `store/user.go`) — 6 packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, plus the test-helper package `server/router/api/v1/test` (5 if you exclude that one).
- **`store.Memo`** (declared in `store/memo.go`) — 4 packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

Trailing candidates for context: `store.Attachment` at 3 packages, `store.Inbox` and `store.MemoShare` at 2 each; everything else (Reaction, IdentityProvider, MemoRelation, UserSetting, InstanceSetting, …) is referenced from at most 1 outside package (`server/router/api/v1`).
