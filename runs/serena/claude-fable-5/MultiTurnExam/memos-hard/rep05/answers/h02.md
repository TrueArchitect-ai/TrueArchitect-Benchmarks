Details:

- **Where delivery executes:** `webhook.Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180` builds the JSON payload, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC signature headers, and sends the POST through the shared `safeClient` (`webhook.go:152`). Asynchronous dispatch (`PostAsync`, `webhook.go:184`) just enqueues the payload; four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`) drain the queue and call the same `Post`.
- **SSRF protection:** the `safeClient`'s transport replaces the dialer with `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go:59-77`), which resolves the hostname and refuses to connect if any resulting IP falls in a reserved range — loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS, and IPv6 equivalents — as defined by `reservedCIDRs` and checked by `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`. Because the check happens at dial time on the resolved addresses, it also defeats DNS-rebinding tricks. There's an additional first line of defense at webhook-creation time: `ValidateURL` (`validate.go:62`) rejects URLs that resolve to reserved IPs, and the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`validate.go:41`) can disable the protection for self-hosted deployments.
