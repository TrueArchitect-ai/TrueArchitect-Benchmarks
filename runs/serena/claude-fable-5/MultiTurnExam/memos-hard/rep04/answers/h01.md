**s3presign background runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — **every 12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `runner.go:27`, plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup) — wired into startup in **`server/server.go:162-171`** inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

Details: the runner's `CheckAndPresign` pages through S3-stored attachments in batches of 100 and re-generates presigned GET URLs before they expire — URLs are valid 5 days, and any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days gets a fresh presigned URL written back to the attachment's `Reference` field (`runner.go:85-123`). This is what keeps links to externally (S3) stored attachment content from going stale.
