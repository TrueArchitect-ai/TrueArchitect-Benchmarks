2. **Factory dispatch:** driver selection happens only in `NewDBDriver` (`store/db/db.go:14`), whose `switch profile.Driver` takes the `case "sqlite"` branch and constructs `sqlite.NewDB(profile)` (`store/db/db.go:20`). 3. **Call chain to the INSERT:** saving a memo goes `Store.CreateMemo` (`store/memo.go:109`) → `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)` (`store/memo.go:113`) via the `store.Driver` interface — which, given step 1–2, is bound to the sqlite `*DB`, landing in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:16` where the INSERT executes.
