**s3presign runner — every 12 hours — wired at `server/server.go:162-169` (started from `startBackgroundRunners`, called at `server/server.go:129`)**

Details:

- **Component:** The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). Its `CheckAndPresign` re-generates presigned S3 URLs for attachments whose content lives in external S3 storage, so the links don't expire/go stale. Attachments carry a `LastPresignedTime`, and the runner re-presigns them in batches.

- **Schedule:** Every 12 hours, via a `time.Ticker`. See `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:25-26`:
  ```go
  // Schedule runner every 12 hours.
  const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12
  ```
  The `Run` loop ticks on that interval and calls `RunOnce` → `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:28-45`). An initial `RunOnce` also fires immediately at startup.

- **Wiring at startup:** In `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:162-169`:
