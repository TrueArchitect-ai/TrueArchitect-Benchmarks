The **S3 presign runner** keeps externally-stored attachment links from going stale. When attachments are stored in S3, their `Reference` is a presigned URL that expires (set to 5 days). This runner walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates any presigned URL that has less than 4 days of validity left, updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`.

- **Component:** `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (`Run` loop → `RunOnce`/`CheckAndPresign`, which calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject`).
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours via a `time.Ticker` (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate run at startup.
- **Wiring:** started from `startBackgroundRunners()` in `server/server.go` — instantiated at `server/server.go:162`, immediate run at `server/server.go:163`, and the continuous 12-hour goroutine launched at `server/server.go:169` (that function is invoked during startup from `server/server.go:129`).

**Answer:** `s3presign.Runner` (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go) — every 12 hours via time.Ticker — server/server.go:169 (continuous loop; also kicked off once at server/server.go:163, both within `startBackgroundRunners`).
