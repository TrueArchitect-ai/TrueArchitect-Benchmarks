**s3presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours — wired at `server/server.go:162`–`169`**

The memos server stores attachments externally in S3 using **presigned URLs**, which expire. The `s3presign.Runner` walks the attachments and re-presigns any URL that isn't valid for at least the next few days, keeping the links from going stale.

- **Component:** `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`, whose `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) lists S3-backed attachments and refreshes soon-to-expire presigned URLs (the 4-day validity check is at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85`–`86`).
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by a ticker in `Run` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29`–`41`.
- **Wiring into startup:** in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, the runner is constructed and started at `server/server.go:162` (`NewRunner`), does an immediate `RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`, and then the continuous ticker loop is launched in a goroutine at `server/server.go:166`–`169`. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
