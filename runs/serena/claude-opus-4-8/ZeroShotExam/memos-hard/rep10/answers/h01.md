Details:

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`. For attachments stored in S3 (external object storage), the pre-signed URLs used as the attachment's `Reference` link expire (set to a 5-day expiry). This runner walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-generates the pre-signed URL for any whose current one is within 4 days of expiring, then writes the fresh URL back via `UpdateAttachment` (`runner.go:47`–`132`). That's what keeps the links from going stale.

- **Schedule:** every 12 hours, defined by `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29`–`41`). It also runs once immediately at startup via `RunOnce` (`server/server.go:163`).

- **Wiring into startup:** constructed and launched in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`, immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`, and the continuous ticker goroutine `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at `server/server.go:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked during server start at `server/server.go:129`.
