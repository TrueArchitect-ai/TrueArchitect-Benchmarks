**`s3presign.Runner` (S3 presigned-URL refresh runner) — every 12 hours (re-presigning any S3 attachment whose 5-day presigned URL is within 4 days of the current time) — wired into startup at `server/server.go:162`.**

Details:

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. For every attachment with `AttachmentStorageType_S3`, it re-presigns the object's URL and writes it back via `UpdateAttachment` (`runner.go:47-132`). This keeps the `Reference` link to externally-stored (S3) attachment blobs from expiring/going stale. Presigned URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry, and the runner refreshes any that are within 4 days of `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:85-91`).
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours via a ticker — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:26-41`). It also runs once immediately at boot.
- **Wiring:** in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:129`). The runner is constructed at `server/server.go:162`, does an immediate `RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`, and its continuous `Run` loop is launched in a background goroutine at `server/server.go:166-170`.
