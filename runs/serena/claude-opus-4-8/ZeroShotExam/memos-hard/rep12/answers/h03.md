The provider abstraction is defined in the **`internal/idp`** package, and the concrete protocol implementation that ships is **`internal/idp/oauth2`** (OAuth2).

- `internal/idp/idp.go` (package `idp`) defines the provider-neutral abstraction — the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` result type (`Identifier`, `DisplayName`, `Email`, `AvatarURL`) that any provider returns. The provider *type* seam is the protobuf `proto/store/idp.proto`, where `IdentityProvider.Type` (currently `TYPE_UNSPECIFIED`, `OAUTH2`) and the `IdentityProviderConfig` `oneof config` mark the extension point.
- `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` (package `oauth2`) is documented as "implements the OAuth2 identity provider integration" and is the only concrete protocol shipped — providing `NewIdentityProvider`, `ExchangeToken` (with PKCE), and `UserInfo`.

The consumer `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go` dispatches on `IdentityProvider_OAUTH2` to this implementation, confirming OAuth2 is the sole shipped provider.

**Answer:** `internal/idp`; `internal/idp/oauth2`
