**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:162` (goroutine loop started at `server/server.go:169`)**

Memos stores attachments in external S3-compatible object storage, and each attachment's `Reference` holds a **presigned S3 URL** with a 5-day expiration. The `s3presign.Runner` re-presigns those URLs before they expire so the links to externally-stored attachment content don't go stale.
