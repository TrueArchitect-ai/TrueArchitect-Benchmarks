q02. SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL

The project ships migrations for three SQL dialects, organized as subdirectories under `store/migration/`: `store/migration/sqlite`, `store/migration/postgres`, and `store/migration/mysql` (mirrored by the driver packages in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/mysql`).
