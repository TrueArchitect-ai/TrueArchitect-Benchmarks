**1st: `User` (referenced from ~6 packages outside the data layer); 2nd: `Memo` (~4 packages).**

Method: I enumerated the persistent entity types declared in the `store` package (`User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `MemoShare`, `MemoRelation`, `UserSetting`, `UserIdentity`, `IdentityProvider`, `InstanceSetting`, …) and counted the distinct packages referencing each `store.<Type>` outside the data layer (excluding everything under `store/`, including the `store/db/*` drivers, and test-only dirs).

- **`store.User`** — referenced from the most non–data-layer packages (~6): `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, plus the `server/router/api/v1/test` helper package (5 if you exclude that test helper).
- **`store.Memo`** — next highest (~4): `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

Third place is `store.Attachment` at ~3 packages, confirming the top-two ranking.
