**No.** The MySQL driver (`store/db/mysql`, whose `NewDB` at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20` constructs the `store.Driver`) is only ever built inside the driver-selection factory.

Evidence:
- The factory `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go:22` calls `mysql.NewDB(profile)` in its `switch profile.Driver` block — this is the sole construction site.
- A repo-wide grep for direct constructions (`mysql.NewDB`, `mysql.Driver{`, `&mysql.`) returns exactly one hit: `store/db/db.go:22`. Excluding generated `proto/gen` code, no other match exists.
- The only other references to the `store/db/mysql` package come from that same `db.go` import; grepping `store/db/mysql` across all `.go` files (excluding `db.go`) yields nothing.
- The `mysql` identifiers appearing in `store/test/containers.go` and `store/test/store.go` are unrelated: they're the third-party `go-sql-driver/mysql` DSN parser and the `testcontainers-go` MySQL module for spinning up a test database. The test harness still obtains its driver through the factory (`store/test/store.go` builds a `profile.Profile{Driver: driver}` that flows into `NewDBDriver`), not by constructing `mysql.NewDB` directly.
