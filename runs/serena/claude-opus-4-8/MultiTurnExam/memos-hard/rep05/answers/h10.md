h10.

**`GenUUID` — `internal/util/uuid.go` — approx fan-in ≈ 28 call sites (≈7 in production/non-test code).**

Evidence and how I measured it:
- I enumerated exported functions/methods declared under `internal/` (packages `util`, `base`, `profile`, `version`, `filter`, `markdown`, etc.) and counted qualified references to each across the whole `.go` codebase, excluding the declaring file.
- `util.GenUUID` is the clear leader: **~28 total references** repo-wide (`grep -rn GenUUID` excluding its declaration). Of those, **~7 are in production (non-test) server code**, spread across 6 files:
  - `server/auth/token.go`
  - `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_session.go`
  - `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service_storage.go`
  - `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`
  - `server/router/api/v1/sso_username.go`
  - `server/router/api/v1/user_service_access_tokens.go`
- Nearest competitors were much lower: `util.ConvertStringToInt32` ~5, `Profile.AllowAnonymous` 3, `RandomString` 2, `GetCurrentVersion` 2; markdown/filter service methods are invoked through injected interfaces and don't accumulate comparable direct call counts.

So the exact number depends on whether test callers are counted (~28 including tests, ~7 production-only), but `GenUUID` has the highest inbound-call count of any `internal/`-declared symbol either way.
