**1st: `store.User` (≈9 packages, or ≈6 excluding the data-layer's own DB drivers); 2nd: `store.Memo` (≈7 packages, or ≈4 excluding drivers).**

Method: I enumerated the persistent entity types declared in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.) and counted, per type, the distinct packages referencing `store.<Type>` outside the `store` layer (with word boundaries so `store.User` doesn't catch `UserSetting`/`UserIdentity`, and `store.Memo` doesn't catch `MemoRelation`/`MemoShare`).

- **`store.User`** — referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss` (plus the three `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` driver packages, which are arguably still the data layer). ≈6 genuinely-outside packages.
- **`store.Memo`** — referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload` (plus the three DB driver packages). ≈4 genuinely-outside packages.

The next tier (`store.Attachment` ≈6 incl. drivers, `store.Inbox`/`store.MemoShare` ≈5) trails both. Either counting convention (including or excluding the DB driver packages) yields the same ranking: `User` first, `Memo` second.
