The shared `safeClient` (webhook.go:32-37) installs `safeDialContext` as its transport's `DialContext`. At **dial time** it resolves the target hostname (`net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost`) and rejects the connection if any resolved IP falls in a reserved/private range (`isReservedIP`). Because the check happens against the actually-resolved IPs at connection time — not against the URL string beforehand — it also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks that would otherwise slip past a pre-validation check.
