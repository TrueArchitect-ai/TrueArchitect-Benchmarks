**Mechanism: by explicit Go code** — each database driver's `DeleteUser` runs `DELETE FROM reaction` statements inside the deletion transaction. There is **no** database-level rule (no foreign key, `ON DELETE CASCADE`, or trigger) on the `reaction` table backing this up.

Evidence locations:

- **Explicit Go deletion (sqlite), verified:** `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:383` — `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN ...` (reactions on the user's memos) and `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:401` — `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?` (reactions made by the user).
