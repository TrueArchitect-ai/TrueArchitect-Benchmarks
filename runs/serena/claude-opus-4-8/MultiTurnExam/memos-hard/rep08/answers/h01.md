**s3presign Runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours — wired at `server/server.go:162-169`**

The externally-stored attachment content here is files kept in S3. S3 access is served via presigned URLs, which expire (set to a 5-day expiration in the code). To keep those links from going stale, memos runs a background presign runner:

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method lists all attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches, and for any whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry (or has never been signed), it re-presigns the S3 object and updates the attachment's `Reference` with the fresh URL (`runner.go:44-129`).

- **Schedule:** every 12 hours — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:25`), driven by a `time.NewTicker` in `Run` (`runner.go:28-39`).

- **Wiring at startup:** in `server/server.go`, the runner is created and run once immediately (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server.go:162-163`), then started as a continuous background goroutine (`go ... s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at `server.go:166-171`). The import is at `server/server.go:25`.
