Distinct memo-visibility enforcement sites (each a separate surface where "who may view a memo" is applied):

- Single-memo read gate (gRPC/Connect API) — `checkMemoReadAccess`, server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39 (invoked by `GetMemo` at memo_service.go:356/367 and comment listing at memo_service_comments.go:148); enforces archived-only-to-creator, non-public requires auth, private only to creator.
- List query visibility filter (gRPC `ListMemos`) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:222 (appends `creator_id == me || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`, or public-only when anonymous).
- Attachment access via linked-memo visibility (gRPC `AttachmentService`) — `checkAttachmentAccess`, server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422 (called at attachment_service.go:255).
- Attachment/media serving over HTTP (fileserver) — `checkAttachmentPermission`, server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650 (memo-visibility branch at ~674, plus share-token fallback).
- RSS feed generation — server/router/rss/rss.go:90 and rss.go:141 (feeds restrict to `store.Public` memos only).

- Comment listing filter — server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:158-160.
