**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (re-presigning any attachment whose 5-day URL is within 4 days of expiring) — wired in `server/server.go:162-169` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which `Server.Start` calls at `server/server.go:129`).**

Details:
- **Component:** `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` walks all S3-backed attachments in batches and, for any whose presigned URL is no longer valid for the next 4 days (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry), it re-presigns via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updates the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:47-132`). This is what keeps the externally-stored (S3) attachment links from going stale.
- **Schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` — a `time.Ticker` fires `RunOnce` every 12 hours (`runner.go:26-45`).
- **Wiring at startup:** In `startBackgroundRunners`, the runner is created and run once eagerly (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`, `server/server.go:163`), then started as a background goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`, `server/server.go:169`). `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
