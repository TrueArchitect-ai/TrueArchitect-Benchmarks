Distinct server-side visibility (who-may-view) enforcement sites:

- **`checkMemoReadAccess` — gRPC single-memo read** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39` (applied in `GetMemo` at `:356`, its parent memo at `:367`, and `CreateMemoComment` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:148`); denies non-public memos to anonymous users and private memos to non-owners.
- **`ListMemos` visibility filtering — gRPC list** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-227`; constrains results to `PUBLIC` for anonymous callers and to `creator_id == me || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]` (or `[PUBLIC, PROTECTED]`) for others.
- **`checkAttachmentAccess` — gRPC attachment read** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422` (comment/anchor at `:255`); gates attachment access by the linked memo's visibility.
- **`checkAttachmentPermission` — file server (blob/avatar/attachment HTTP)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650` (visibility checks at `:672`/`:694` region, e.g. `memo.Visibility == store.Public`/`store.Private`); also honors valid memo-share tokens for protected/private memos.
- **RSS feed — restricted to public memos** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` and `:141` (`VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`).
- **Frontend SSR / metadata rendering — restricted to public memos** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150` (`VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`).

(Related but adjacent: `server/notification/email.go:313-316` gates mention-notification emails on `Private`/`Protected` visibility — visibility-driven, though it governs notifications rather than direct viewing.)
