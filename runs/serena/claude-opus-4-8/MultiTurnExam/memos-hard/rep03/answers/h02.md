**Delivery function** — `webhook.Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`. This marshals the payload, builds the `POST` request, and actually executes the outbound HTTP call via `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`. (Async dispatch is `PostAsync`/the worker pool in `init()`, but they all funnel through `Post`.)

**Protection mechanism** — a custom `DialContext` that blocks reserved/private IPs at dial time. The shared `safeClient` is configured with `Transport.DialContext = safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32–37`). `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) resolves the target hostname and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:70–73`). Because the check runs on the *resolved* IP at connection time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding — an SSRF guard that URL-string validation alone can't provide.

There's also a pre-flight `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:65`) applied when webhooks are created/updated (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74`), but the authoritative internal-address protection at delivery time is `safeDialContext`.
