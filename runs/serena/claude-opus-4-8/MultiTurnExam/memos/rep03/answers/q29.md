**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the Connect auth `Interceptor` attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` and handles reactive refresh on 401 (token state stored in `web/src/auth-state.ts`).

**Token kinds:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT, sent as `Authorization: Bearer …`) and a **refresh token** (sent as an HTTP cookie via `credentials: "include"`, exchanged through the `refreshToken` RPC to mint a new access token).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` (JWT parsing/validation in `server/auth/token.go`, driven by `server/auth/authenticator.go`; the Connect `AuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go` invokes it).
