Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks (all in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`):

- created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

The first three converge on the internal helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`). All four ultimately converge for delivery at **`webhook.PostAsync`** in the `internal/webhook` package — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (which performs the actual HMAC-signed HTTP POST).
