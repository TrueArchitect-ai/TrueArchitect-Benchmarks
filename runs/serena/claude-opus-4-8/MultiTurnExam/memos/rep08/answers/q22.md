- **signing mechanism** — HMAC-SHA256 following the **Standard Webhooks** scheme: the key is derived from the `whsec_<base64>` signing secret, the MAC is computed over `msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body`, and the result is sent base64-encoded as the `webhook-signature: v1,<sig>` header (with `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` headers) — see `internal/webhook/webhook.go:133-149`.
