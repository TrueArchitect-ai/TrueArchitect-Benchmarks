**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language), via `github.com/google/cel-go` — wrapped by the project's `Engine` in `internal/filter/` (`internal/filter/engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** The filter string is parsed into a CEL AST by `NewEngine`/`cel.NewEnv` (`internal/filter/engine.go`), lowered to an intermediate representation (`ir.go`) and rendered to a dialect-specific SQL `WHERE` clause + args via `render.go` driven by the per-dialect column/function `Expressions` map in `schema.go` (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`), with `AppendConditions` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`) producing the final clauses the store passes to each dialect driver.
