Mechanism: The filter string is written in CEL syntax, compiled by the `filter.Engine` (`internal/filter/engine.go`) into a dialect-neutral intermediate representation/condition tree (`ir.go`, `parser.go`), which is then rendered to a parameterized SQL `WHERE` clause per dialect by `render.go` — switching on `DialectName` (`sqlite`/`mysql`/`postgres`, defined in `internal/filter/schema.go`) to emit dialect-specific column expressions and placeholders that the store dialect drivers splice into their `ListMemos` queries.
