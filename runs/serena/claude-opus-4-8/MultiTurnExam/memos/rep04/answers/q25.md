**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `cel-go` — lives in `internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`; CEL environment/schema in `internal/filter/schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** The CEL expression is parsed into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (`internal/filter/parser.go` → `internal/filter/ir.go`), then the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholder args for `sqlite`, `mysql`, or `postgres` (handling JSON access, boolean semantics, placeholders, and `LIKE`/`ILIKE` per dialect).
