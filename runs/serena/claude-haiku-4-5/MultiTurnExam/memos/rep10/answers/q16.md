memo_relation, attachment

When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`Store.DeleteMemo`), it explicitly deletes memo_relation records (both where the memo is the source and where it is the target) and attachments before deleting the memo row itself. The memo_share records are handled by database cascade delete (ON DELETE CASCADE defined in the schema at line 109 of LATEST.sql). Reactions are not explicitly deleted in the store layer.
