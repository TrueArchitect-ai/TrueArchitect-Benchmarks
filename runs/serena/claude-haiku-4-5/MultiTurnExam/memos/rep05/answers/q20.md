Server-Sent Events (SSE); /bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects

The server uses Server-Sent Events (SSE) to push live updates to connected browsers. The SSEHub is implemented in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`. Memo-related handlers that broadcast through it are: CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, and CreateMemoComment (which broadcast directly), plus dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects (which broadcasts update events).
