q07. web/src/connect.ts

The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. The file uses the `createClient` function from `@connectrpc/connect` to instantiate clients for all 8 services (InstanceService, AuthService, UserService, MemoService, AttachmentService, AIService, ShortcutService, and IdentityProviderService) with a shared transport that includes authentication interceptor logic.
