When a memo is created, user webhooks are notified through `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (defined in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`). The actual HTTP delivery is performed by the `internal/webhook` package. The request-signing mechanism uses HMAC-SHA256 with Standard Webhooks format, including headers: `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, and `webhook-signature` (with "v1," prefix).
