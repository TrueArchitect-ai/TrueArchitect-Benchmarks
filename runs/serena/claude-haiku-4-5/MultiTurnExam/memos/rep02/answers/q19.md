q19. gRPC-Gateway (REST) — server/router/api/v1/v1.go
Connect — server/router/api/v1/v1.go

MemoService is served over two protocols: gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP) and Connect. Both protocol registrations happen in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`. The gRPC-Gateway registration occurs at line 108 with `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer`, and the Connect protocol handlers are registered through `RegisterConnectHandlers` which is called at line 141, where MemoServiceHandler is registered in the `connect_handler.go` file (line 40: `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler`).
