q16. memo_relation, attachment

When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following records before the memo row itself:
1. **memo_relation** records (both where the memo is the source and where it is the target)
2. **attachment** records (all attachments linked to the memo)

The deletion order is: memo comments (as separate memos) → memo_relation records → attachment records → memo row.
