CEL (Common Expression Language) — `/bench/repo/internal/filter/engine.go`

The filter engine parses the CEL expression into a condition tree, then the renderer generates dialect-specific SQL by traversing the condition tree and calling `RenderOptions` with the appropriate dialect (SQLite, MySQL, or PostgreSQL), producing parameterized SQL statements that are appended to the WHERE clause by `AppendConditions` in `/bench/repo/internal/filter/helpers.go`.
