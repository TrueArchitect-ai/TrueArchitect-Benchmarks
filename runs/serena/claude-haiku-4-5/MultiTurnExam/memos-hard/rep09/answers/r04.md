Premise correction: Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. The webhook delivery workers consume from a Go channel-based queue, not a message broker.

Actual mechanism: The queue is declared as a buffered Go channel at `/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. Four concurrent goroutine workers are spawned in the `init()` function (webhook.go:42-55) that consume webhooks from this channel and dispatch them via the `Post()` function. Messages are enqueued by `PostAsync()` (webhook.go:182-196) from webhook dispatch calls in memo and comment services (memo_service_webhooks.go:43, 71). The queue has a capacity of 128 messages; if full, messages are dropped with a warning log.
