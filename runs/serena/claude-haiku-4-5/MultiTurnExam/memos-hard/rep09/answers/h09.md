There are 6 distinct Close method declarations: SSEHub, Store, Cache, and the three database driver implementations (SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL). In production server code, two are called directly: (1) `SSEHub.Close()` invoked by `closeLongLivedConnections()` at server/server.go:206; (2) `Store.Close()` invoked by `Shutdown()` at server/server.go:146. The other four (Cache and three DB drivers) are invoked indirectly through the Store.Close() call chain.
