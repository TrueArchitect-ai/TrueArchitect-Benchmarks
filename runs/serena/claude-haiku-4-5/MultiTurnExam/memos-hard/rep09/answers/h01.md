**S3Presign Runner** — Every 12 hours — server/server.go:162

The component that keeps S3-stored attachment presigned URLs from going stale is the **S3Presign Runner**. It operates on a **12-hour schedule**, refreshing presigned URLs that have less than 4 days remaining until expiration (URLs are valid for 5 days total). The runner is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:162**, where it's instantiated via `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` and then started as a background goroutine.
