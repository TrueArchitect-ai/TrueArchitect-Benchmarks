h11.

1st: User (~7 packages); 2nd: Memo (~7 packages)

The two most cross-package referenced entity types are tied: **User** (store/user.go) and **Memo** (store/memo.go), each referenced by approximately 7 distinct packages outside the store layer. User is imported by server/auth, server/notification, server/router/api/v1, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/rss, and others. Memo is similarly referenced across server/router/api/v1, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/rss, server/runner/memopayload, and other packages.
