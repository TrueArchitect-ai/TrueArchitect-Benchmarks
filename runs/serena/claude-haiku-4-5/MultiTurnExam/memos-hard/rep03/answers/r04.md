**Premise correction:** Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. The webhook delivery workers consume from an in-memory bounded Go channel, not an external message broker.

**Actual mechanism:** In-memory channel queue declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:
```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```
A bounded channel with capacity of 128 payloads. Four worker goroutines are initialized in the `init()` function at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–55`, each blocking on receives from the channel. When enqueued via `PostAsync()` (line 184), if the queue is full, payloads are dropped with a warning. Failed deliveries are logged but not retried—no persistence or external broker.
