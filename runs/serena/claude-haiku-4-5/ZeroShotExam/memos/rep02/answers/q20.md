**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (SSEHub struct)

**Memo handler names:**
- `CreateMemo()` (broadcasts `memo.created`)
- `UpdateMemo()` via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects()` (broadcasts `memo.updated`)
- `DeleteMemo()` (broadcasts `memo.deleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment()` (broadcasts `memo.comment.created`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction()` (broadcasts `reaction.upserted`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction()` (broadcasts `reaction.deleted`)

All located in `server/router/api/v1/` directory (memo_service.go, memo_service_comments.go, reaction_service.go, memo_update_helpers.go).
