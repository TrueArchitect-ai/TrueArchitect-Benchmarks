MemoService is served over two main protocols:

1. **gRPC** — `/bench/repo/proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service_grpc.pb.go`
2. **gRPC-Gateway** — `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/v1.go`

(Additionally, it's accessible via Connect RPC at `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` and has a curated subset exposed via MCP at `/bench/repo/server/router/mcp/service.go`, but the primary two protocols are gRPC and gRPC-Gateway.)
