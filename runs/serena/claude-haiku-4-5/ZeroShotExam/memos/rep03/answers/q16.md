q16.

When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **memo_relation** (where the memo is the source - MemoID)
2. **memo_relation** (where the memo is the target - RelatedMemoID)
3. **attachment** (all attachments linked to this memo)

These are deleted in that order before finally deleting the memo row itself via `s.driver.DeleteMemo()`.
