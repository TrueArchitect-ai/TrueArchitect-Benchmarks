When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **memo_relation** - Deleted in two passes:
   - MemoRelation records where the memo is the source (MemoID field)
   - MemoRelation records where the memo is the target (RelatedMemoID field)

2. **attachment** - All attachments linked to the memo

These deletions happen in `/bench/repo/store/memo.go:140-159` in the `DeleteMemo` method, which calls `DeleteMemoRelation` twice and then `DeleteAttachment` for each attachment before finally calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row itself.
