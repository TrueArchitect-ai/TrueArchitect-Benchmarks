**web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient**

The memo CRUD mutations and queries are contained in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`, which exports hooks like `useCreateMemo()`, `useUpdateMemo()`, `useDeleteMemo()`, and various query hooks. The generated service client used is `memoServiceClient`, created from `MemoService` (the generated protobuf definition) and imported via `@/connect` in `web/src/connect.ts`.
