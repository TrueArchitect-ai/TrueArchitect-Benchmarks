When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the source) - `/bench/repo/store/memo.go:145-148`
2. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the target) - `/bench/repo/store/memo.go:149-152`
3. **Attachments** (including their storage files and caches) - `/bench/repo/store/memo.go:153-159`

The deletion sequence is handled in `/bench/repo/store/memo.go` in the `DeleteMemo` function (lines 140-159). The API handler in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` also recursively deletes memo comments (which are themselves memos with comment relations) before calling the store's `DeleteMemo` function.
