When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following records before deleting the memo row itself:

1. **memo_relation** records (where the memo is the source)
2. **memo_relation** records (where the memo is the target/related memo)
3. **attachment** records (linked to the memo)

These deletions occur in lines 141-157 of `/bench/repo/store/memo.go`, before the actual memo row deletion at line 158.
