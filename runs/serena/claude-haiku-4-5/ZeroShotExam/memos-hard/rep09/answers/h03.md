h03.

**Abstraction package:** `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp` (`/bench/repo/internal/idp/idp.go`) — defines the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct as the provider abstraction.

**Concrete protocol implementation:** `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp/oauth2` (`/bench/repo/internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`) — implements OAuth2 as the concrete protocol with the `IdentityProvider` type that provides `ExchangeToken()` and `UserInfo()` methods.
