**Component:** S3 presign runner (`github.com/usememos/memos/server/runner/s3presign`)

**Schedule:** Every 12 hours (as indicated by `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`)

**Wiring site:** `/bench/repo/server/server.go:156-171` (specifically in the `startBackgroundRunners` method, where the runner is instantiated and started)

This component keeps S3-stored attachment links (presigned URLs) from going stale by refreshing them every 12 hours. It checks each S3 attachment and regenerates its presigned URL if the current one will expire within the next 4 days (given a 5-day expiration window). The runner is wired into server startup by being instantiated and launched as a background goroutine in the `startBackgroundRunners` method called from the `Start` method.
