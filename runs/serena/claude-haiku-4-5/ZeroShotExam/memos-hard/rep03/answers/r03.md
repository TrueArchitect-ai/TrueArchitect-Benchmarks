The nightly database-cleanup job is scheduled to run at **2 AM daily** using the cron expression **"0 2 * * *"**.

This can be found in:
- `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/example_test.go` (lines 98-100): A "cleanup" job with `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`
- `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/doc.go` (lines 15-16): Documentation example showing `Schedule: "0 2 * * *", // 2 AM daily` for a "daily-cleanup" job

The cron expression "0 2 * * *" means: minute 0, hour 2 (2 AM), every day of the month, every month, every day of the week.
