The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) involved in webhook delivery. The webhook workers consume from a plain **in-memory Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`, capacity 128), declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. Four goroutines are started in `init()` to drain the channel, each calling `Post()` directly. `PostAsync` enqueues payloads into this channel. There is no external queue or broker anywhere in the codebase.
