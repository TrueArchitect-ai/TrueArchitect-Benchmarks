**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (with an immediate `RunOnce` at startup) — wired in `startBackgroundRunners` at **`server/server.go:162`**

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) re-signs S3 presigned URLs for attachments. Presigned URLs expire after 5 days; the runner refreshes any that are older than 4 days, giving a 1-day safety margin. It is instantiated and kicked off at `server/server.go:162–169`, where `NewRunner` is called, `RunOnce` executes immediately, and then `Run` starts a 12-hour ticker loop in a background goroutine.
