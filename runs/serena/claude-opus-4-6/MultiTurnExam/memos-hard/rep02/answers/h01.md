**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`, where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is instantiated inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start()` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It runs once immediately at boot (`RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`), then ticks every 12 hours via a `time.Ticker` in its `Run` loop (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29-41`). On each tick it scans all S3-type attachments in batches and regenerates any presigned URL whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration, giving at least 1 day of margin).
