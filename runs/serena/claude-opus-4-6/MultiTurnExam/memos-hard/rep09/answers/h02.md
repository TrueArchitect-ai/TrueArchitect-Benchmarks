2. **Safe dialer at request time** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (~line 59) re-resolves DNS and re-checks every IP against the same reserved ranges before connecting, defeating DNS-rebinding attacks where a record changes between validation and delivery.
