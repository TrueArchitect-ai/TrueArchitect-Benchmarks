**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired in at `server/server.go` where `startBackgroundRunners` instantiates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` and launches it in a goroutine.

The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It iterates over all S3-backed attachments in batches, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and regenerates any presigned URL that was signed more than 4 days ago (presigned URLs are created with a 5-day expiry in `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`, giving a ~1-day safety margin). At startup, `RunOnce` is called immediately so URLs are fresh right away, then `Run` ticks every 12 hours thereafter.

**Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
**Schedule** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`)
**Wiring site** — `server/server.go` in `startBackgroundRunners` (around line 162, called from `Start` at ~line 129)
