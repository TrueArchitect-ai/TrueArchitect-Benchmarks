**`s3presign.Runner`** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at **`server/server.go:153`** in `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start`.

The runner re-signs S3 presigned URLs for attachments whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs are generated with a 5-day expiry, giving a 1-day safety margin). At startup it runs once immediately via `RunOnce`, then continues on its 12-hour ticker in a background goroutine.
