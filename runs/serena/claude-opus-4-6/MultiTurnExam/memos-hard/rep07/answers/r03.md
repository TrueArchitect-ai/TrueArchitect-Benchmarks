The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. The `internal/scheduler/` package exists but is not used for any scheduled database cleanup. The only background runners in the server are the `s3presign.Runner` (refreshes S3 presigned URLs every 12 hours, wired in `server/server.go`) and the `memopayload.Runner` (which isn't wired into production startup at all). No cron-based or scheduled database cleanup mechanism exists.
