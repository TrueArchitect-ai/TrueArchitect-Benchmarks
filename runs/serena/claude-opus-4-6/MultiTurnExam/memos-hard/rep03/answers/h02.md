**Delivery function** — `Post` (and its async wrapper `PostAsync`) in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines 120–179 for `Post`, lines 182–196 for `PostAsync`). `PostAsync` enqueues payloads onto a buffered channel drained by worker goroutines that call `Post`, which builds and sends the HTTP POST request.

**Protection mechanism** — A two-layer reserved-IP check prevents SSRF:

1. **Registration-time validation**: `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines 62–83) resolves the webhook URL's hostname and rejects it if any resolved IP falls within a reserved/private CIDR (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local, IPv6 ULA/link-local — defined at lines 14–23 of the same file, checked by `isReservedIP` at lines 44–54).

2. **Dial-time enforcement** (defense-in-depth against DNS rebinding): `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines 59–77) re-resolves the hostname at connection time and again checks every IP against `isReservedIP`, refusing to connect if any is private. The outbound `safeClient` (line 32) uses this custom dialer in its transport.
