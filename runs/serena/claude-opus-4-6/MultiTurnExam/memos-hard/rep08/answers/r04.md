The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **in-process Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`, a `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with capacity 128), declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutines are spawned in `init()` (line 42–49) to drain this channel and call `Post()` synchronously. `PostAsync` (line 184) sends payloads into the channel for asynchronous delivery.
