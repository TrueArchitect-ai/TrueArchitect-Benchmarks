The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`), declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. The channel has a capacity of 128 (`make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`), and four goroutines are started in the package's `init()` function to drain it and call `Post` for each payload. The entire mechanism is in-process with no external messaging infrastructure.
