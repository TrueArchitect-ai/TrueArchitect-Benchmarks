The browser hits `GET /file/users/:identifier/avatar`. Here is each hop from handler to stored data:

1. `FileServerService.serveUserAvatar` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:168` (HTTP handler; extracts username from URL)
2. `FileServerService.getUserByUsername` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:715` (builds `FindUser{Username}`, calls store)
3. `Store.GetUser` — `store/user.go:148` (cache check by ID; falls through to `ListUsers` for username lookups)
4. `Store.ListUsers` — `store/user.go:136` (delegates to the database driver)
5. `DB.ListUsers` — `store/db/sqlite/user.go:88` (SQL query on the `user` table, selects `avatar_url` column; equivalents in `store/db/postgres/user.go` and `store/db/mysql/user.go`)
6. `FileServerService.parseDataURI` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:737` (decodes the base64 data URI from `AvatarURL` into raw image bytes + MIME type, then served via `c.Blob`)

The route is registered by `FileServerService.RegisterRoutes` at `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:124`, wired during `Server.Setup` at `server/server.go:81–82`.
