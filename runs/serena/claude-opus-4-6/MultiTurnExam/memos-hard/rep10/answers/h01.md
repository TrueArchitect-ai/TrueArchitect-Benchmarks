**`s3presign.Runner`** — every **12 hours** (with an immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:129` where `startBackgroundRunners` is called from `Server.Start()`, which instantiates the runner and launches its ticker loop at `server/server.go:153–171`.

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It processes attachments in batches of 100, re-signing any URL whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days — providing a 1-day safety margin against the 5-day presigned URL lifetime configured in `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`. The 12-hour interval is defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`.
