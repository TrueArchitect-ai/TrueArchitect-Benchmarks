CEL (Common Expression Language) engine — `internal/filter/engine.go` (uses `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`); the filter expressions are parsed into a CEL AST by the engine, then converted into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and finally rendered into dialect-specific SQL (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) by `internal/filter/render.go`, which switches on the dialect passed via `AppendConditions` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`) from each driver (e.g., `filter.DialectSQLite` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`).
