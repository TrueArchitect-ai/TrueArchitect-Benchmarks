CEL (Common Expression Language) engine — `internal/filter/engine.go` (using `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`); the engine parses filter strings into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go`), which is then rendered to dialect-specific SQL by the renderer in `internal/filter/render.go`, with each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`) calling `filter.AppendConditions()` from `internal/filter/helpers.go` with its dialect name (`DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`) to produce the appropriate SQL WHERE clauses and parameter placeholders.
