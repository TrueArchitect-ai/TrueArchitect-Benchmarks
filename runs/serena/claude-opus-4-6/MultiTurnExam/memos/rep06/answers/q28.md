The server starts **one** background runner:

**s3presign.Runner** — driven by a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (plus an initial `RunOnce` at startup) — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`, started in `server/server.go` (line 162–170)

**Unused infrastructure:** The `memopayload` runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) has a `RunOnce` method but is **not** started as a background runner by the server. It is only called inline during `CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (lines 108, 447) to rebuild memo payloads synchronously — it is never scheduled or run in the background.
