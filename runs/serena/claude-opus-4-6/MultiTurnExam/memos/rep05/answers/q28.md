**s3presign runner** — driven by a `time.Ticker` with a 12-hour interval (plus an initial `RunOnce` at startup); started in `server/server.go`, implemented in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`.

**Unused infrastructure:** The `memopayload` runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) exists in the codebase and provides a `RunOnce` method for batch-rebuilding memo payloads, but it is **not started by the server** — `server/server.go` does not import or invoke it. It is only called inline from the API handler (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`) via its `RebuildMemoPayload` function, not as a background worker.
