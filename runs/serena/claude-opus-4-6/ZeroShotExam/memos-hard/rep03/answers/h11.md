The persistent entity types are defined in the `store` package (`store/*.go`). The two referenced from the largest number of outside packages are:

1st: **User** (`store/user.go`); ~7 packages (e.g. `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, and a test package)

2nd: **Memo** (`store/memo.go`); ~6 packages (e.g. `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`, and a test package)
