The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps externally-stored attachment links fresh by periodically re-generating pre-signed S3 URLs before they expire (URLs are valid for 5 days; the runner skips attachments presigned less than 4 days ago).

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`
- **Schedule:** Every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), with an immediate `RunOnce` sweep at startup
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162` — the runner is created and launched inside `startBackgroundRunners()`, which is called from `Start()` at `server/server.go:129`
