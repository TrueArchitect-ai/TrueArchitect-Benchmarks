Here are the distinct memo visibility enforcement sites:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (single-memo gatekeeper)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (called from `GetMemo`, and from `ListMemoComments`)
2. **`ListMemos` query-level filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
4. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
5. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
6. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
7. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
8. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
9. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
10. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (API-layer attachment access)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
11. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (file-server binary serving)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
12. **SSE delivery filtering (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
13. **RSS feed (public-only)** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
14. **Sitemap (public-only)** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
15. **`ListAllUserStats` / `GetUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
16. **`canUserAccessMentionContext` / `shouldSkipMentionInbox` (mention notifications)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go`
17. **`canViewerAccessMemo` (notification visibility)** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go`
18. **Database-layer `VisibilityList` SQL filtering** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
