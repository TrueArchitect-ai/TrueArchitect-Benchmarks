Of the persistent entity types defined in the data layer (`store/` package), the two referenced from the most packages outside that layer are:

1. **`User`** — referenced from **~7 packages** (across `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`)

2. **`Memo`** — referenced from **~6 packages** (across `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)

Both are defined in `store/user.go` and `store/memo.go` respectively. `User` edges ahead because it is additionally needed in `server/auth` and `server/router/fileserver`, while `Memo` is uniquely pulled into `server/runner/memopayload`.
