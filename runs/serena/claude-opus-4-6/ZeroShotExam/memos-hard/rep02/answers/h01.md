**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired in at **`server/server.go:162`** (inside `startBackgroundRunners`), where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is created and launched.

The runner lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates presigned URLs for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days, so there's a 1-day safety margin). On server start, `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) runs it once immediately (`RunOnce`), then kicks off a goroutine with a 12-hour ticker.
