The outbound delivery executes in the **`Post`** function at **`internal/webhook/webhook.go`** (line ~120), which builds and sends the HTTP POST request. For async dispatch, `PostAsync` (same file, line ~184) enqueues payloads onto a buffered channel drained by goroutines that call `Post`.

SSRF protection comes from two layers, both in **`internal/webhook/`**:

1. **Dial-time IP filtering** — `safeDialContext` in **`internal/webhook/webhook.go`** (line ~59). The custom HTTP client resolves the target hostname and checks every resulting IP against `isReservedIP()` before allowing the TCP connection, which also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks.

2. **URL validation at registration time** — `ValidateURL` in **`internal/webhook/validate.go`** (line ~62). When a webhook is created or updated, the URL is parsed, its scheme is restricted to `http`/`https`, and the resolved IPs are checked against the same `isReservedIP` function.

The reserved-IP blocklist is the `reservedCIDRs` variable in **`internal/webhook/validate.go`** (line ~14), covering RFC-1918 private ranges, loopback, link-local (including the cloud metadata `169.254.0.0/16`), and their IPv6 equivalents.
