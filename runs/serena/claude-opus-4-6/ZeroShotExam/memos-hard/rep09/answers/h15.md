Here are the distinct enforcement sites for memo visibility:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (gRPC GetMemo / GetMemoComments gate)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
2. **`ListMemos` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
4. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
5. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (attachment access via memo visibility)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
6. **`ListMemoReactions` / `UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
7. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
8. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
9. **`canViewerAccessMemo` in notification payload conversion** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go`
10. **`canViewerAccessMemo` in email notifications** — `server/notification/email.go`
11. **`canUserAccessMentionContext` / `shouldSkipMentionInbox`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go`
12. **SSE delivery filtering (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
13. **File server attachment access check** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
14. **RSS feed (public-only filtering)** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
15. **Sitemap generation (public-only filtering)** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
16. **User stats visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
17. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
18. **Database-level SQL `WHERE visibility IN (...)` clauses** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
