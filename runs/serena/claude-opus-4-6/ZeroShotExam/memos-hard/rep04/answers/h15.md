Here are the distinct memo visibility enforcement sites:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (per-memo guard)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (used by `GetMemo`, `ListMemoComments`)
2. **`ListMemos` query-level filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListAllUserStats` query-level filter** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
4. **`GetUserStats` query-level filter** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
5. **`CreateMemoComment` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
6. **`ListMemoComments` relation filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
7. **`ListMemoRelations` filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
8. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
9. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
10. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
11. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
12. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (API attachment access)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
13. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (file server)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
14. **SSE event delivery (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
15. **RSS feed (public-only filter)** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
16. **Sitemap (public-only filter)** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
17. **Store-layer SQL `VisibilityList` filtering** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
18. **Store-layer `MemoFilter` on relations** — `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`
