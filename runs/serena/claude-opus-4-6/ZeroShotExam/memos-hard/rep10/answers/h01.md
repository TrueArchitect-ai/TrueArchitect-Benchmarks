**`s3presign.Runner`** — every **12 hours** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at **`server/server.go:162`**, inside `startBackgroundRunners` (called from `Server.Start()`).

The runner refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It runs once immediately at startup (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`, line 163), then continuously via a 12-hour ticker in a background goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`, line 169). Re-signing is triggered when a URL has less than 1 day of validity remaining out of its 5-day lifetime.
