No; the MySQL driver is never constructed directly outside the factory. The constructor `mysql.NewDB` (defined in `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`) is called in exactly one place: the `NewDBDriver` factory in `store/db/db.go` (line 22). The `mysql` package is imported only by that factory file. All other callers — including test helpers in `store/test/store.go` and benchmark tests in `server/router/api/v1/test/` — obtain a driver exclusively through `db.NewDBDriver(profile)`. There are no direct struct literal constructions (`mysql.DB{}`) anywhere in the codebase either.
