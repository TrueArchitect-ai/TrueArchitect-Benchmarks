1. **`CreateMemo`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70`) — calls `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (line 157)

4. **`CreateMemoComment`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:20`) — calls `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (line 119), and also internally calls `CreateMemo` which fires `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
5. **`SetMemoRelations`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:16`) — calls `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (line 48)
6. **`SetMemoAttachments`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:16`) — calls `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (line 48)
