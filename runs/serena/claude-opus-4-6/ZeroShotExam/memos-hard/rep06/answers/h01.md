**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = 12h`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup via `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) regenerates presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire. Presigned URLs have a 5-day lifetime (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77-79`), and the runner skips any attachment presigned less than 4 days ago, so URLs are refreshed when they have roughly 1 day of validity remaining. At startup, `RunOnce` is called immediately for an initial sweep, then `Run` launches in a background goroutine ticking every 12 hours (`server/server.go:153-171`).
