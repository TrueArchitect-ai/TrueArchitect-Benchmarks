The outbound HTTP delivery executes in the **`Post`** function at **`internal/webhook/webhook.go`** (with `PostAsync` in the same file feeding payloads through a buffered channel to `Post` via background goroutines).

SSRF protection comes from two mechanisms, both in the `internal/webhook/` package:

1. **URL validation at registration time** — `ValidateURL` in **`internal/webhook/validate.go`** resolves the hostname and checks every IP against `isReservedIP` (same file), which blocks RFC-1918, loopback, link-local, and IPv6 private CIDRs defined in `reservedCIDRs`.
2. **Dial-time IP re-check at delivery time** — `safeDialContext` in **`internal/webhook/webhook.go`** re-resolves the hostname and re-checks against `isReservedIP` before allowing the TCP connection, defeating DNS-rebinding attacks that could bypass the registration-time check.

**Answer:** delivery function — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `isReservedIP`/`ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (registration-time check) and `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (dial-time re-check).
