**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup via `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:129`

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) re-signs S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire. Presigned URLs last 5 days; the runner refreshes any that are older than 4 days, giving a 1-day safety margin. It is created and kicked off (once synchronously, then continuously in a goroutine) inside `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`), which is called from `Server.Start()` at `server/server.go:129`.
