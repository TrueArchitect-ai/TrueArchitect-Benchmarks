When a memo is created, `CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` calls `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`, which delegates to the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`. This helper fetches the creator's webhooks and calls `webhook.PostAsync` to enqueue delivery.

**`DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`; delivery package **`github.com/usememos/memos/internal/webhook`** (uses Go's standard `net/http` client); signing mechanism is **HMAC-SHA256 following the Standard Webhooks spec** (`webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature` headers, where the signature is `v1,` + base64-encoded HMAC-SHA256 over `{msgID}.{timestamp}.{body}`, with keys optionally `whsec_`-prefixed base64).
