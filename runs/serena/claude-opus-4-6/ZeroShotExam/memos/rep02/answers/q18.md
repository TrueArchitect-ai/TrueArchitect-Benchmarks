The three implementations of the `Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. **`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`** — SQLite
2. **`store/db/postgres/postgres.go`** — PostgreSQL
3. **`store/db/mysql/mysql.go`** — MySQL

The factory function that selects between them at startup is:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` to dispatch to the appropriate `NewDB()` constructor for each backend.
