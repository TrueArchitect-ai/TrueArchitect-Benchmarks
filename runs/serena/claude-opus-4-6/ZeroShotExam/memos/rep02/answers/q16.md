When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`), the following kinds of records are deleted before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — all `memo_relation` records where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) are deleted (line 142), then all where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) are deleted (line 145).
2. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo are listed and individually deleted (lines 149–157).

After these are cleaned up, the memo row itself is deleted via `s.driver.DeleteMemo` (line 158).
