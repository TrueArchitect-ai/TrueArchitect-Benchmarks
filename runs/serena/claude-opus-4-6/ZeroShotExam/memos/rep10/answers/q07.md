The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. This file imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and uses it to instantiate clients for all services (e.g., `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`).
