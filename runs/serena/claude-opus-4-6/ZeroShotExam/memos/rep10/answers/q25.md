**Engine:** Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`); **path:** `internal/filter/engine.go` (compiles the CEL expression) and `internal/filter/parser.go` (walks the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`).

**Mechanism:** `Engine.Compile` parses the filter string via `cel.Env.Compile()` into a CEL AST, `buildCondition()` in `parser.go` converts that AST into an intermediate condition tree (IR), and then a `renderer` in `internal/filter/render.go` walks the IR and switches on `DialectName` (`sqlite` / `postgres` / `mysql`) to emit dialect-appropriate SQL fragments (e.g., `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`  for JSON access, `ILIKE` vs `LIKE` for case-insensitive text, `?` vs `$N` placeholders), which each store driver (`store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`) appends to its `WHERE` clause via `filter.AppendConditions()` in `internal/filter/helpers.go`.
