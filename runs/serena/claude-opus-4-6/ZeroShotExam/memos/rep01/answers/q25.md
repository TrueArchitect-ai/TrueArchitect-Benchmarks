**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, living in `internal/filter/` (engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`, parser at `internal/filter/parser.go`, renderer at `internal/filter/render.go`).

**Mechanism:** The `Engine.Compile` method uses `cel-go` to parse the filter string into a CEL AST, then `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) walks that AST to produce a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree of `Condition` interfaces defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`), and `Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks the IR with a `DialectName` switch (`DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments and placeholder args — wired together by `filter.AppendConditions` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`), which each store dialect (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`) calls with its own dialect constant.
