When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`), the following kinds of records are deleted before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` records where the memo is the **source** (`MemoID`) are deleted first, then `memo_relation` records where the memo is the **target** (`RelatedMemoID`).
2. **Attachments** — all `attachment` records linked to the memo are listed and individually deleted.
