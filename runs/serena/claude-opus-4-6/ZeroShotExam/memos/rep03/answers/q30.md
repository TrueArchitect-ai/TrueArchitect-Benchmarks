Four memo lifecycle events dispatch webhooks, all defined in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`:

- **memo created**: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- **memo updated**: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- **memo deleted**: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- **memo comment created**: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

The first three share a common internal helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (same file), while the comment-created variant has its own loop to target the parent memo's creator.

All four converge for delivery at `webhook.PostAsync` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`, which enqueues payloads onto a buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`). Worker goroutines drain that channel and call `webhook.Post` (same file, line ~120) to perform the actual HTTP POST with JSON body and optional HMAC signing.
