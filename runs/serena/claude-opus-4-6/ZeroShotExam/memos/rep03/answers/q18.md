The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. **`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`** — SQLite (pure-Go `modernc.org/sqlite`)
2. **`store/db/postgres/postgres.go`** — PostgreSQL (`lib/pq`)
3. **`store/db/mysql/mysql.go`** — MySQL (`go-sql-driver/mysql`)

The factory function that selects between them at startup is:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"`) and delegates to the corresponding package's `NewDB()` constructor.
