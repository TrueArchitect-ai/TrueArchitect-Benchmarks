**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by a custom `filter` package at **`internal/filter/`**.

**Mechanism:** The filter string is parsed by CEL into an AST, then `buildCondition()` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) walks that AST to produce a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR) tree of `Condition` nodes; each store driver (SQLite/Postgres/MySQL) calls `filter.AppendConditions()` passing its `DialectName`, and the `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks the IR tree emitting dialect-specific SQL fragments (e.g. `?` vs `$N` placeholders, `JSON_EXTRACT()` vs `@>`, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, `REGEXP` vs `~`) which are appended as parameterized WHERE clauses.
