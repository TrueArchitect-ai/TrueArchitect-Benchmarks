The `ShortcutService` defines five RPCs (in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`):

1. **ListShortcuts**
2. **GetShortcut**
3. **CreateShortcut**
4. **UpdateShortcut**
5. **DeleteShortcut**

They are all implemented in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** (on the `APIV1Service` struct, which embeds `UnimplementedShortcutServiceServer` from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`).
