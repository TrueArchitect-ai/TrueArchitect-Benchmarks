**Background workers/runners the server actually starts:**

- **S3 Presign Runner** — `time.NewTicker` every 12 hours (plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup); re-signs expiring S3 attachment URLs. Started in `server/server.go:startBackgroundRunners()`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`.
- **Webhook Async Workers (x4)** — channel-driven (`asyncPostQueue`, capacity 128); consume payloads enqueued by `PostAsync()` on memo create/update/delete/comment. Started via `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`.
- **Email Async Workers (x2)** — channel-driven (`asyncEmailQueue`, capacity 128); consume email requests enqueued by `SendAsync()` for inbox notifications. Started via `init()` in `internal/email/email.go`.
- **Cache Cleanup goroutines (x3)** — `time.NewTicker` every 5 minutes; evict expired entries from `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, and `userSettingCache`. Started via `cache.New()` in `store/cache/cache.go`, instantiated in `store/store.go`.

**Scheduling infrastructure present but NOT used by the server:**

- **`internal/scheduler/` package** — A complete cron-based job scheduler (with cron expression parser in `parser.go`, job registration, middleware chain with recovery/logging/timeout in `middleware.go`, `Start()`/`Stop()` lifecycle, and timezone support). Described in `doc.go` as a "GitHub Actions-inspired cron job scheduler." No code outside `internal/scheduler/` imports it; it is entirely unused.
- **`server/runner/memopayload/` runner** — Has a `Runner` struct with `RunOnce()` for batch-rebuilding memo payloads, but it is never instantiated as a background worker. Only the standalone `RebuildMemoPayload` function is called inline during memo create/update in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, not as a scheduled runner.
