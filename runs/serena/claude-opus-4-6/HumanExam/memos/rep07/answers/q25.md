- Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `google/cel-go`, implemented in `internal/filter/engine.go`
- The CEL expression is compiled into a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree) by `Engine.Compile`, then rendered to SQL per dialect by `internal/filter/render.go` using dialect constants (`DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`). Each database driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect to produce the WHERE clause.
