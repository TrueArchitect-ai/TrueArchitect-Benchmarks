CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go` (`Engine` struct, using `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`). The engine's `CompileToStatement` method parses the CEL expression into an AST, transforms it into a dialect-agnostic condition tree, then the `Render` method in `internal/filter/render.go` generates SQL with dialect-specific quoting/functions based on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (sqlite/postgres/mysql). Each database driver passes its own `filter.DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL` to `CompileToStatement`.
