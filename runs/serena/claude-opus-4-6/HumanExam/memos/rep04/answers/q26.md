Tables affected when DeleteMemo runs:
- `memo_relation` (explicit delete by store layer — both `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` sides)
- `attachment` (explicit delete by store layer for attachments linked to the memo)
- `memo` (the memo row itself, plus comment memos recursively at the handler level)
- `memo_share` (via FK `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `memo_id`)
