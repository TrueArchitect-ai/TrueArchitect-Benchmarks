CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; the CEL expression is compiled to an AST, converted to a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/parser.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL via `internal/filter/render.go`, with each dialect driver calling `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (SQLite/MySQL/Postgres) to produce the appropriate SQL fragment with correct placeholders and operators.
