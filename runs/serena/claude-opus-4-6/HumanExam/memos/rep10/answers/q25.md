CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`; engine lives in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go` compiles CEL to an IR, `parser.go` walks the AST, `render.go` emits dialect-specific SQL). Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (DialectSQLite/DialectPostgres/DialectMySQL), and the renderer produces appropriate SQL fragments (e.g., `strftime` for SQLite, `EXTRACT` for Postgres, `YEAR()` for MySQL).
