Tables affected when DeleteMemo runs:
- `memo_relation` (both as `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` — deleted by `Store.DeleteMemo`)
- `attachment` (attachments linked to the memo — deleted by `Store.DeleteMemo`)
- `memo` (the memo itself — deleted by the driver's `DeleteMemo`)
- At the API handler layer, comment memos (also `memo` rows) are recursively deleted, which triggers additional `memo_relation` and `attachment` deletions for each comment.
- `reaction` (listed before delete at the handler level, `memo_service.go:545`)

Note: reactions are listed at the handler level for webhook payload construction, but their deletion happens implicitly (the handler does not explicitly delete them — they are retrieved for the webhook payload only). The explicit deletes are: `memo_relation`, `attachment`, `memo`.
