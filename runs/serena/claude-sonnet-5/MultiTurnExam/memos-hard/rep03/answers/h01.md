**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — a background runner that re-generates presigned S3 URLs for attachments stored externally (S3). Defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately on startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each run scans S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of the 5-day expiration (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`:
- `server/server.go:162` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`
- `server/server.go:163` — `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (immediate run at boot)
- `server/server.go:169` — `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` launched in a goroutine to keep re-presigning on the 12-hour ticker for the life of the server
