**Premise correction:** Neither. There is no message broker (NATS, RabbitMQ, or otherwise) involved in webhook delivery — I confirmed a repo-wide search for "nats", "rabbitmq", and "amqp" returns zero matches, and there's no such dependency in `go.mod`.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses a simple in-process buffered Go channel as its queue, not an external broker. It's declared as `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four worker goroutines are spawned in the package `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) for every payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking send (`select`/`default`) and drops the payload with a warning log if the channel is full — all within a single process, with nothing external to declare a queue on.
