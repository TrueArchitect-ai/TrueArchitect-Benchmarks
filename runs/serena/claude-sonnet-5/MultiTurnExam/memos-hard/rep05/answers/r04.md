Premise correction — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used anywhere in this codebase; `go.mod` has no such dependency, and a repo-wide search for "nats"/"rabbitmq"/"amqp" turns up nothing. There is no message broker or externally-declared queue involved in webhook delivery.

Actual mechanism — the "queue" is an in-process, in-memory Go channel: `asyncPostQueue := make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four worker goroutines are started in that package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) for every payload enqueued via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`). If the channel's 128-item buffer is full, `PostAsync` drops the payload and logs a warning (`webhook.go:191-194`) rather than blocking — there's no persistence or broker-backed durability at all.
