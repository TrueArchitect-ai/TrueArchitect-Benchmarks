**Protection mechanism:** `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`, installed as the custom `DialContext` on `safeClient`'s `http.Transport` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). It resolves the target host via `net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost` at dial time (not just at URL-validation time) and rejects the connection if any resolved IP is reserved/private per `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44-54`, checking against `reservedCIDRs`/`reservedNetworks` covering loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, and link-local/cloud-metadata `169.254.0.0/16`, plus IPv6 equivalents). This dial-time check defeats DNS-rebinding bypasses of the separate registration-time `ValidateURL` check (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`).
