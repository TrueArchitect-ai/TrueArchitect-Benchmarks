q20. Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE). Hub implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (with helpers in `sse_event_helpers.go` and route registration in `sse_handler.go`).

Handlers that broadcast through it (`s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`), all in `server/router/api/v1/`:
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` (memo_service.go)
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (memo_service.go)
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` (memo_service_comments.go)
- `APIV1Service.UpsertMemoReaction` (reaction_service.go)
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemoReaction` (reaction_service.go)
- `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (memo_update_helpers.go) — invoked from `UpdateMemo` (memo_service.go), `SetMemoAttachments`-style attachment updates (memo_attachment_service.go), and memo relation updates (memo_relation_service.go)
