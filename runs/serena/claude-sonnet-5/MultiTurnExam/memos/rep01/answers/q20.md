q20. Mechanism: **Server-Sent Events (SSE)**

- Hub: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub` type, `Broadcast` method), with the HTTP endpoint in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`
- Handlers that broadcast through it:
  - `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — memo_service.go
  - `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` — memo_service.go
  - `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (used by `UpdateMemo`) — memo_update_helpers.go
  - `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` — memo_service_comments.go
  - Reaction upsert/delete handlers — reaction_service.go
