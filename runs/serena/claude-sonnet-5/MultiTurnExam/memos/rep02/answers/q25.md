Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`) — lives in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go` compiles the CEL expression, `schema.go` defines the field schema/CEL environment, `parser.go` converts the CEL AST into an internal IR of `Condition`/`ValueExpr` nodes, `render.go` renders that IR to SQL).

Mechanism: `Engine.CompileToStatement` (internal/filter/engine.go:74) parses the filter string with the CEL environment into an AST, `parser.go` walks that AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR, and `render.go`'s `renderer` (parameterized by `DialectName`) walks the IR to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments/placeholders and args — e.g. `qualifyColumn`/`jsonExtractExpr`/`buildPostgresJSONAccessor` in render.go branch on `DialectName` (sqlite/mysql/postgres) to produce the correct column references and JSON-path syntax for each database.
