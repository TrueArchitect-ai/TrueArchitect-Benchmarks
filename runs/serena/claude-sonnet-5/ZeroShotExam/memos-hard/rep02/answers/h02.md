  - `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59` — a custom `DialContext` installed on `safeClient`'s `http.Transport` that resolves the hostname and rejects any IP falling in a reserved/private range at actual dial time (defeats DNS rebinding).
  - `ValidateURL` / `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44` and `:62` — pre-validates a webhook URL's scheme and resolved IPs against `reservedCIDRs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14`, covering loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud IMDS 169.254.169.254, and IPv6 equivalents) before a webhook is even saved.

Both checks can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` package variable (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`), intended for self-hosted deployments targeting local-network services.
