**s3presign.Runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs before their existing presigned URL expires.

- **Component**: `Runner.CheckAndPresign` scans attachments with `StorageType == S3` in batches of 100 and re-generates the presigned URL (via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`) if `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry, so this refreshes them a day before they'd go stale) — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131` (threshold check at lines 85-91).
- **Schedule**: runs once immediately at startup, then on a `time.Ticker` every **12 hours** — `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by `Run()` at lines 29-41.
