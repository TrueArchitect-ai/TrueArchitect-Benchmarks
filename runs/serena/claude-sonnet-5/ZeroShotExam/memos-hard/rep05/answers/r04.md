**Actual mechanism:** Webhook dispatch uses an in-process, in-memory Go channel as a bounded work queue. The `init()` function (lines 42–55) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over this channel and call `Post()` for each payload, logging a warning on failure. `PostAsync()` (lines 184–196) enqueues onto the channel via a non-blocking `select`, dropping (and logging) the payload if the queue is full rather than blocking. So there's no broker or persistent queue declaration (no NATS subject, no RabbitMQ exchange/queue) — the "queue" is simply the Go channel declared at webhook.go:39, entirely in-memory and not durable across process restarts.
