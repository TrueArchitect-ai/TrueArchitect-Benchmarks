My exploration agent's findings confirm my answer and surface one addition worth noting: the question mentions "previews" specifically, and there's a preview-snippet computation I didn't include.

**Addendum:** Besides `Payload.Tags`/`Payload.Property` (computed in `RebuildMemoPayload`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-88`), memos also get a **content snippet** for previews — but it's computed on-the-fly at read time, not persisted:

- `GenerateSnippet(content []byte, maxLength int)` in `internal/markdown/markdown.go:263-346` walks the markdown AST, concatenates text (skipping code blocks, resolving `#tag`/autolink nodes to plain text), and truncates at a word boundary (appending `" ..."` if cut).
- Called via `getMemoContentSnippet` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:83-89`, and invoked from `convertMemoFromStoreWithCreators` in `memo_service_converter.go:76-80` — i.e., **every time a memo is converted to its API response** (Get/List/Create/Update, memo relations, notifications), not just on create/update.

So there are two distinct derived-metadata computations:
1. **Persisted** (`Tags`, `Property.{Title,HasLink,HasCode,HasTaskList,HasIncompleteTasks}`) — recomputed only on `CreateMemo` and on `UpdateMemo` when the update mask includes `content`.
2. **Ephemeral preview snippet** — recomputed on every read/serialization, never stored.

Also worth flagging: `Runner.RunOnce` (the batch-rebuild-all-memos job in the same `runner.go` file) isn't wired into server startup anywhere in the current codebase — it looks like unused/dead code, so the only real triggers are the two call sites in `memo_service.go` (create and content-update).
