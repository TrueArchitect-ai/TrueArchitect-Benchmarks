**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — periodically re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs so links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content don't go stale/expire. It skips attachments whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), generating a fresh presigned URL and updating `LastPresignedTime` otherwise (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).
