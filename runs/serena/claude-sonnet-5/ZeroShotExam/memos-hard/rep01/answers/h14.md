**Extracted properties:**

- `Property.title` — text of a leading H1 heading, if it's the document's first block.
- `Property.has_link`, `has_code`, `has_task_list`, `has_incomplete_tasks` — booleans from AST node presence.
- Snippet — plain-text preview (code stripped, truncated at word boundary, "..." suffix) — computed on demand, not stored.
- Mentions — transient `@username` extraction used only for notification dispatch, not persisted.
