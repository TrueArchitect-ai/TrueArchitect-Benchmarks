`internal/idp` (github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp); `internal/idp/oauth2` (github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp/oauth2)

The `idp` package (`/bench/repo/internal/idp/idp.go`) defines the shared identity-provider abstraction (`IdentityProviderUserInfo`), and the `oauth2` subpackage (`/bench/repo/internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`) ships the concrete OAuth2 protocol implementation, with its own `IdentityProvider` struct/`NewIdentityProvider` constructor that consumes `storepb.OAuth2Config` and returns `idp.IdentityProviderUserInfo`.
