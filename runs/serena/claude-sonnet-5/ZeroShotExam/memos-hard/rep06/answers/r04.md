Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. The webhook delivery workers don't consume from an external message broker at all — they read from an in-process, in-memory Go channel.

Actual mechanism: `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` declares `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, a buffered Go channel (capacity 128). `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` (the `init()` function) spawns 4 goroutines that range over this channel and call `Post(payload)` for each item, i.e., the "workers" are goroutines consuming an in-memory channel, not a broker queue. `PostAsync` (webhook.go:184-196) enqueues into that channel with a non-blocking `select`, dropping and logging a warning if the channel is full — so there's no persistence or external broker involved, just process-local concurrency.
