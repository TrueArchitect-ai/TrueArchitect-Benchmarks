**Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), a background runner that re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs. It queries all attachments with `StorageType == S3`, and for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of the 5-day presigned-URL expiry (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91), it calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updates the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` in the store (runner.go:108-123).

**Schedule** — runs once immediately, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, runner.go:27, used in `Run` at runner.go:30-40).

**Wiring site** — `server/server.go:162-171`, inside `Server.startBackgroundRunners`: `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` runs once synchronously (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`, line 163) and then is launched in a goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`, line 169) as part of server startup.
