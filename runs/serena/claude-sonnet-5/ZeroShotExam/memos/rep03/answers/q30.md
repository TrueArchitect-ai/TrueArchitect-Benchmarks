Addendum to q30: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` is invoked from a shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66-77`), which itself is called from three handlers — `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) — not just `UpdateMemo` alone. So archiving/pinning/visibility changes and attachment/relation edits all fire the same generic `memos.memo.updated` event through that one helper. The delivery convergence point (`webhook.Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`, reached via `PostAsync`'s async queue) is unchanged from my original answer.
