memos (Go server, `server/server.go`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — started in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:154-173`). It runs once immediately via `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup, then loops in a goroutine on a `time.Ticker` with `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-40`), re-scanning S3-backed attachments and refreshing presigned URLs that are within 4 days of their 5-day expiry.

That's the only background runner actually started by the server. `server.go`'s `startBackgroundRunners` only creates/launches the s3presign runner.

**Unused scheduling infra:** `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` defines a `Runner` type with a batch `RunOnce(ctx)` method (rebuilds every memo's payload/tags in batches of 100), but this `Runner`/`RunOnce` is never constructed or called anywhere — `grep` shows the `memopayload` package is only imported for its standalone `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload(...)` function, called synchronously inline inside memo create/update request handlers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and `:447`. So the memopayload package's `Runner` struct is dead/unwired background-job scaffolding — it has no ticker, no cron, and is never started from `server.go`. There's also no external cron/scheduler library in `go.mod` (no `robfig/cron`, `gocron`, etc.) — the only scheduling mechanism anywhere in the codebase is the plain `time.Ticker` used by s3presign.
