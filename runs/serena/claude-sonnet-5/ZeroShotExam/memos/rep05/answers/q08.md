q08. `Driver` — `store/driver.go` (defined at store/driver.go:10)

The `Driver` interface declares all the database methods (attachments, memos, users, settings, etc.) that each backend must implement. It's satisfied by the MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite drivers in `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`.
