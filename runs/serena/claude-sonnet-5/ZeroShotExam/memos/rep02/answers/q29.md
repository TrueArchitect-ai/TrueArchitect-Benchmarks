- **Interceptor**: `web/src/connect.ts` — defines `authInterceptor` for the Connect-RPC client (`@connectrpc/connect-web`, not axios). It attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` on outgoing requests and, on an `Unauthenticated` response, retries once after calling `refreshAccessToken()` (falling back to `redirectOnAuthFailure()` in `web/src/utils/auth-redirect.ts`).
- **Tokens managed**: An **access token** (JWT, short-lived, managed client-side in `web/src/auth-state.ts` — kept in memory + `localStorage` under `memos_access_token`/`memos_token_expires_at`, synced across tabs via `BroadcastChannel`), and a **refresh token** (`memos_refresh` httpOnly cookie, minted server-side, not touched by JS — refreshed via `AuthService.refreshToken()` with `credentials: "include"`). Personal Access Tokens (`memos_pat_` prefix) are also supported.
- **Backend validation package**: `server/auth/` (`token.go` for JWT/PAT constants and claims, `authenticator.go` for `AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` and `AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), used by the Connect/gRPC interceptors under `server/router/api/v1`.
