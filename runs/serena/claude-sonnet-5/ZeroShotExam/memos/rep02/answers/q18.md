Three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`):
- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory function: `NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`, which switches on `profile.Driver` to call `sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, or `postgres.NewDB` at startup.
