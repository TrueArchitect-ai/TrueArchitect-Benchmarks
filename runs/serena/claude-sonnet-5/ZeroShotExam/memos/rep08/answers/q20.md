- **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP (`text/event-stream`), not WebSockets — implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (the `/api/v1/...` SSE endpoint registered via `RegisterSSERoutes`).
- **Hub path**: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — defines `SSEHub`/`SSEClient` with `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, and `Broadcast` methods (visibility/creator-based filtering via `canReceive`). Event construction helpers live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`. The hub is instantiated as `APIV1Service.SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
- **Broadcasting handlers** (all call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
  - `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
  - `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`, also reused by `SetMemoAttachments` in `memo_attachment_service.go` and `SetMemoRelations` in `memo_relation_service.go`) in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`
  - `CreateMemoComment` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
