Details:

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs. For each `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachment it generates a fresh presigned GET URL, writes it back as the attachment's `Reference`, and stamps `LastPresignedTime`. It skips any object whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (URLs are minted with a 5-day expiration), so links never lapse before they're refreshed (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`–`132`).

- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29`–`41`). Additionally it runs once immediately at startup via `RunOnce` before the ticker loop begins.

- **Wiring into startup**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs the runner and launches it — `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`, an immediate `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`, then the recurring `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine at `server/server.go:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
