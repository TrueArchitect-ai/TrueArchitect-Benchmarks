The stored data is the `avatar_url` column of the `user` table — a `data:` URI whose bytes `serveUserAvatar` decodes via `parseDataURI` and returns with `c.Blob`. Storage backends (LOCAL/S3) are not involved for avatars; the image lives inline in the DB row.
