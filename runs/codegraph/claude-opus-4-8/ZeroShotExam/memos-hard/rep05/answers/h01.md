**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:162` (wired in `startBackgroundRunners`)**

The component is the S3 presign runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. For attachments stored externally in S3 (`AttachmentStorageType_S3`), the reference link is a presigned GET URL that expires (set to a 5-day lifetime). `CheckAndPresign` walks all S3-backed attachments in batches, and for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry it generates a fresh presigned URL and writes it back via `UpdateAttachment` (`runner.go:47-132`). This keeps the externally-stored content links from going stale.

- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` drives a `time.NewTicker` in `Run` — it re-presigns every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `29-41`).
- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.Start` → `startBackgroundRunners` constructs it and launches it. It runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`, `server/server.go:163`) and then continuously in a goroutine (`Run`, `server/server.go:169`). The construction/wiring site is `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at **`server/server.go:162`**, called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

(For contrast, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` is a sibling runner that rebuilds memo payloads, not attachment links.)
