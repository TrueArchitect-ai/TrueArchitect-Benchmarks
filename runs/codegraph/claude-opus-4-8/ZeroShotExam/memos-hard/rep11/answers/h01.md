**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (with one immediate run at startup) — `server/server.go:162` (goroutine started at `server/server.go:169`)**

The S3 presign runner keeps external attachment links from going stale. For attachments stored in S3, `SaveAttachmentBlob` records a presigned GET URL as the attachment's `Reference` along with a `LastPresignedTime` (`server/router/api/v1/attachment_service_storage.go:111-127`). Presigned URLs expire (set to 5 days), so the runner refreshes them before they lapse.

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.CheckAndPresign` (via `Run`/`RunOnce`) lists all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose URL isn't valid for at least the next 4 days, updating `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:47-132`).
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours, driven by a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` where `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27-41`). Additionally, `RunOnce` fires once synchronously at startup (`server/server.go:163`).
- **Wiring site:** constructed and kicked off in `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162`, with the recurring loop launched in a goroutine at `server/server.go:169`.
