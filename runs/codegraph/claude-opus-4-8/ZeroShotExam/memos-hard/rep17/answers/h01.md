The S3 presign runner regenerates presigned URLs for attachments stored in external S3 storage before they expire, keeping the `Reference` links from going stale.

**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method pages through all S3-backed attachments and re-presigns any whose presigned URL is not still valid for the next 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:47-132`).

**Schedule:** every 12 hours, via a `time.Ticker` in `Run` — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `Run` at `:29`). It also runs once immediately at startup via `RunOnce`.

**Wiring site:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:129`). The runner is constructed and kicked off there:
- `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` — `server/server.go:162`
- `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (immediate pass) — `server/server.go:163`
- `go ... s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` (continuous 12h loop) — `server/server.go:169`
