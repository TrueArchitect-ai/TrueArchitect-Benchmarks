The **S3 presign runner** keeps attachment links from going stale. Attachments stored in external S3 are served via presigned URLs (stored as the attachment's `Reference`) that expire after 5 days. This runner periodically re-presigns any S3 attachment whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry, writing back a fresh URL and `LastPresignedTime`.
