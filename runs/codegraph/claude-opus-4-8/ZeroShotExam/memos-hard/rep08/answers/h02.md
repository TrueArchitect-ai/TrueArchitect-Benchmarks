The outbound webhook delivery executes in the `Post` function, which builds the POST request and calls `safeClient.Do(req)` to actually send it over the network. `PostAsync` just enqueues onto a channel; four background workers (started in `init`) drain the queue and call `Post`. SSRF protection is enforced at dial time by `safeDialContext`, wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext`: it resolves the target host and rejects any resolved address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (also defeating DNS rebinding, since the check runs on the actual dialed IPs).

Answer:
- delivery function — `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (executes the send via `safeClient.Do` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`)
- protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59` (installed as `safeClient`'s `Transport.DialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32`, rejecting reserved/private IPs via `isReservedIP`)
