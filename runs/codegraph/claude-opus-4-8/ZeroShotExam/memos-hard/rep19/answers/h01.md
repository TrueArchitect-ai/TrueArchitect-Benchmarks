**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162` (started at `:169`)**

For attachments whose content lives in external S3 storage, the `Reference` field holds a **presigned S3 GET URL** that expires (set to 5 days). If it went stale, links would break. A background runner keeps them fresh:

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.CheckAndPresign` walks all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments in batches of 100, and for any whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry (`runner.go:85-91`), re-presigns via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and writes the new URL back with `UpdateAttachment`, stamping `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:108-123`).
- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Runner.Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). There is also an immediate `RunOnce` at startup.
- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.Start` → `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:129`), where the runner is constructed at `server/server.go:162` (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), run once synchronously at `:163`, then launched in its own goroutine with a cancelable context at `:169` (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`).
