**Premise correction:** There is no nightly *database-cleanup* job scheduled in the cron scheduler — the question's premise is mistaken.

The cron scheduler at `internal/scheduler/` is a **standalone, generic library package** (a "GitHub Actions-inspired cron job scheduler," per `internal/scheduler/doc.go:1`). A search of the whole repo turns up **no `scheduler.Register(...)` calls and no `scheduler.Job` instances registered anywhere in production code** — the scheduler is never wired up to run any real jobs, database-cleanup or otherwise.

What likely inspired the question:

- The only cron schedule literal in the entire codebase is inside a **doc-comment example** at `internal/scheduler/doc.go:14-21`: a job named `"daily-cleanup"` with `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily). This is illustrative documentation, not a running job, and it is a generic "cleanup," not a *database* cleanup.
- There is a separate, unrelated **in-memory cache cleanup** in `store/cache/cache.go` (`cleanupLoop`, line 200), but it is driven by a `time.Ticker` on a configurable `CleanupInterval` — not by the cron scheduler and not on a nightly schedule.

So: no nightly database-cleanup cron job exists. The closest match is the documentation example `"daily-cleanup"` at `0 2 * * *` (2 AM daily) in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, which is neither database-specific nor an actually-scheduled job.
