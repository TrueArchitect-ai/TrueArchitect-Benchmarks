**s3presign Runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162`**

Attachments stored in S3 are served via presigned URLs that expire (set to 5 days). If those URLs weren't refreshed, the links would go stale and stop resolving. The `s3presign.Runner` prevents this:

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. `CheckAndPresign` (line 47) lists all attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches, and for any whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry, it re-presigns via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the fresh URL back with `UpdateAttachment` and stamps `LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-123`).
- **Schedule:** every 12 hours — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (line 29). Note it also does one immediate `RunOnce` synchronously at startup.
- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs the runner and launches it. Called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`. The runner is created at `server/server.go:162` (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), invoked once immediately at line 163, and its continuous ticker loop is started in a goroutine at line 169.
