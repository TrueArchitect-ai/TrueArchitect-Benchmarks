**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (with one immediate run at startup) — `server/server.go:162-169` (wired via `startBackgroundRunners`, itself called at `server/server.go:129`)**

Details:

- **Component:** The S3 presign runner, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (`Runner` type, `NewRunner`/`Run`/`CheckAndPresign`). It scans S3-backed attachments and re-presigns their `Reference` URLs before they expire — presigned GET URLs are set to a 5-day expiry, and the runner refreshes any whose last presign is older than 4 days (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`). This is what keeps the externally-stored attachment links from going stale.

- **Schedule:** A `time.Ticker` firing every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,29-41`), plus one synchronous `RunOnce` at startup.

- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162-169`, inside `Server.startBackgroundRunners`. It's constructed at `server/server.go:162`, run once immediately at `:163`, and launched as a background goroutine at `:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` is invoked during server start at `server/server.go:129`.

Note: this repo also has a generic cron-style `internal/scheduler` package, but the attachment-link refresh does **not** go through it — it uses the dedicated `s3presign` background runner above.
