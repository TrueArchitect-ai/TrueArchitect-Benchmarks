**protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext` (lines 32–37). At dial time it resolves the target hostname with `net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost` and rejects the connection if any resolved IP satisfies `isReservedIP` (i.e. a reserved/private range). Because the check happens on the post-resolution IP at dial time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding attempts.
