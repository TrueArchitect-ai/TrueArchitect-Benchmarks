**Data layer & persistent entity types.** The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/store.go`, `module github.com/usememos/memos`). Its persistent entity structs are `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Inbox`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `Reaction`, `UserIdentity`, `IdentityProvider`, `InstanceSetting`, and `UserSetting` (defined in `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.).

**Cross-package usage (packages outside `store/` that reference `store.<Type>`):**

- `store.User` — **7 packages**: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- `store.Memo` — **6 packages**: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

Every other entity type is referenced from 4 or fewer outside packages (next is `Attachment` at 4). So `User` is the most widely referenced entity across packages, with `Memo` second.
