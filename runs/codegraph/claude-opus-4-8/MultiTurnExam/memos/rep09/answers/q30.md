Memo lifecycle events and their dispatch functions (all in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`):

- memo created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (activity `memos.memo.created`)
- memo updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (activity `memos.memo.updated`)
- memo deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (activity `memos.memo.deleted`)
- memo comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (activity `memos.memo.comment.created`)

Convergence for delivery: the first three funnel through the private helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`); that helper and `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` both call **`webhook.PostAsync`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which spawns `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) to perform the actual signed HTTP delivery.
