In `store/memo.go` `(*Store).DeleteMemo` (lines 140–158), before deleting the memo row itself the store layer deletes:

- `memo_relation` records where the memo is the source (`MemoID`)
- `memo_relation` records where the memo is the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
- attachments linked to the memo (via `DeleteAttachment` for each attachment found by `MemoID`)

Then it finally calls `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the `memo` row.
