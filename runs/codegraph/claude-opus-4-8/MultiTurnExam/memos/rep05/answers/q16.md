In `store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140`), before deleting the memo row itself (`s.driver.DeleteMemo`), the store layer deletes:

- **memo_relation records where the memo is the source** (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID}`)
- **memo_relation records where the memo is the target/related memo** (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID}`)
- **attachments linked to the memo** (lists via `ListAttachments{MemoID}`, then `DeleteAttachment` for each)

Additionally, at the API-handler level (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:567–577`), before deleting the memo the handler first deletes **comment memos** (child memos linked by a `comment`-type relation) — each of which recursively goes through `store.DeleteMemo` (cleaning up their own relations and attachments).
