The **S3 presign runner** keeps externally-stored (S3) attachment links from going stale by re-generating presigned URLs before they expire.

**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (plus once at startup) — wired at `server/server.go:162` (started at `server/server.go:169`)**

Details:
- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.CheckAndPresign` walks all `S3`-type attachments in batches of 100, skips any whose `LastPresignedTime` is still valid for the next 4 days (URLs expire at 5 days), and re-presigns the rest, updating each attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:47`).
- **Schedule**: a `time.Ticker` at `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by `Run` at line 29). It also runs immediately once on boot via `RunOnce` (`server/server.go:163`).
- **Wiring site**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs it with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at **`server/server.go:162`** and launches the recurring loop in a goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`) at `server/server.go:169`. That method is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
