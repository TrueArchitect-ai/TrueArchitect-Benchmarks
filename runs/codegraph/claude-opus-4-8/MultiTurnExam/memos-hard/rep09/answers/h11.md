**1st: `User` (store.User) — ~5 outside packages; 2nd: `Memo` (store.Memo) — ~4 outside packages.**

Method: the data layer is the `store` package (entity types declared in `store/*.go`; persistence drivers in `store/db/*`, `store/cache`). I counted distinct packages *outside* that layer (excluding `store/...` and test packages) that reference each entity type.

- **`store.User`** — 5 packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo`** — 4 packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.
- (Next: `store.Attachment` — 3 packages: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.)

The ranking is stable regardless of whether test/helper packages and the `store/db/*` drivers are folded in (including them, `User` ≈ 9 vs `Memo` ≈ 7) — `User` leads and `Memo` is second in both countings.
