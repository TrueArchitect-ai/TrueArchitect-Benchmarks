**Abstraction package:** `internal/idp` — `internal/idp/idp.go` defines the provider-neutral abstraction (`IdentityProviderUserInfo`, the normalized user shape every provider maps into).

**Implementation package:** `internal/idp/oauth2` — `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` (file header: *"Package oauth2 implements the OAuth2 identity provider integration"*), providing the concrete `IdentityProvider` with `NewIdentityProvider`, `ExchangeToken`, and `UserInfo`. OAuth2 is the only concrete protocol implementation that ships (it's the sole subpackage under `internal/idp/`), and it's what `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go` constructs for SSO sign-in.
