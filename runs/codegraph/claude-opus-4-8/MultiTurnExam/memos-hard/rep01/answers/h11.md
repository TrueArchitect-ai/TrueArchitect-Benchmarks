The data-layer entity types are the Go structs in `store/` (e.g. `store.User`, `store.Memo`, `store.Attachment`, `store.Reaction`, `store.Inbox`, `store.MemoRelation`, `store.MemoShare`, …). Counting distinct **packages outside the data layer** (excluding `store/` and its `store/db/*` drivers, and test packages) that reference each type:

**1st — `User`: ~5 packages** — `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss` (defined at `store/user.go:29`).

**2nd — `Memo`: ~4 packages** — `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload` (defined at `store/memo.go:35`).

(Next: `Attachment` ~3 — `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.) The ordering is stable regardless of whether the DB driver packages are counted as inside the layer: including them, `User` leads 9→7 over `Memo`; excluding them, 5→4.

Answer: **1st: `store.User` (~5 external packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~4 external packages).**
