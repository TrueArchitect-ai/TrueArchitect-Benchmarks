delivery function — `webhook.Post` at internal/webhook/webhook.go:120 (this is where the outbound HTTP POST actually executes via `safeClient.Do(req)` at internal/webhook/webhook.go:152; `PostAsync` at :184 enqueues onto a bounded queue whose workers call `Post`).

protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` at internal/webhook/webhook.go:59, installed as the `DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` transport (internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (internal/webhook/validate.go:44), whose blocklist of reserved CIDRs — loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS 169.254.169.254, and IPv6 equivalents — is defined at internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23. Because the check happens at connection time on the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. A second, earlier layer validates the URL when a webhook is configured: `ValidateURL` at internal/webhook/validate.go:62 (called from server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go). Both layers can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (internal/webhook/validate.go:41).
