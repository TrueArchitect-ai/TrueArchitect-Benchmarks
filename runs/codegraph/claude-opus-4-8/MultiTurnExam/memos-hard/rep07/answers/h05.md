mechanism — Explicit Go code, in the per-driver `DeleteUser` transaction. Emoji reactions are removed by two explicit `DELETE FROM reaction` statements executed inside the delete-user transaction: one deletes reactions on the deleted user's own content (by `content_id`), and one deletes reactions the user authored anywhere (by `creator_id`).

evidence locations —
- `deleteUserTargetsTx` orchestrates the deletes and calls both reaction helpers: store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:96 (`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`) and :102 (`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`).
- The helpers themselves: `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` — store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:380-388 (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`, over the user's memo content IDs built as `"memos/"+uid` in `memoContentIDsFromRefs`); `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` — store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:400-402 (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`).
- The same two helpers are mirrored in the other two drivers: store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:96,102,380,400 and store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96,102,417,437.

I checked all three driver implementations of `DeleteUser` (sqlite/postgres/mysql `user_delete.go`) and grepped each for "reaction". The reaction removal is done by explicit Go-issued SQL within the transaction, not left to a schema-level cascade (and not omitted).
