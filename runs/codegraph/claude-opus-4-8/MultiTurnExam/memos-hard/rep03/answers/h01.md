The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign`) keeps external attachment links fresh. Attachments stored in S3 are referenced by presigned URLs that expire after 5 days; this runner re-signs them before they go stale.

**component — schedule — wiring site**

`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker **every 12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`); each pass re-presigns any S3 attachment whose presigned URL has less than 4 days of validity left (`runner.go:85-91`) — wired in at **`server/server.go:162-169`** (created via `NewRunner`, `RunOnce(ctx)` for the initial pass on :163, and the continuous `Run(s3Context)` goroutine on :169), which is invoked from `Server.Start` → `startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` at `server/server.go:129`.
