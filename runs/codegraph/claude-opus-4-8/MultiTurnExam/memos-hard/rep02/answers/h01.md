**`s3presign.Runner` (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go) — every 12 hours — server/server.go:162 (within `startBackgroundRunners`, itself called from `Start` at server/server.go:129)**

Details:

- **What it does:** S3-stored attachments expose their content via a *presigned* S3 URL held in `Attachment.Reference` (surfaced to clients as `ExternalLink`, see `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service_storage.go:39-41`). Those presigned URLs are minted with a 5-day expiration. The `s3presign.Runner` re-signs them before they go stale: `CheckAndPresign` pages through all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments and, for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry, generates a fresh presigned URL and writes it back via `UpdateAttachment` (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132).

- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` — a ticker fires `RunOnce` every 12 hours (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-41). It also runs once synchronously at startup (`RunOnce` at server/server.go:163).

- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.Start` calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` (server/server.go:129); inside it the runner is constructed at server/server.go:162 (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), run once immediately (line 163), then launched in a goroutine on its own cancelable context at server/server.go:169 (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`).
