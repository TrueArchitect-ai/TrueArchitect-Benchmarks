SSRF protection is the custom `DialContext` on that client, `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`. At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`, ranges in `reservedCIDRs` at `:14`). Because it checks the resolved IP at connection time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. This is backstopped by `ValidateURL` at webhook-registration time (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`), and both can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`).
