The **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3-stored attachment links fresh by re-generating their presigned URLs before they expire.

- **Component** — `s3presign.Runner`, whose `CheckAndPresign` batches through all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments and re-presigns any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of the 5-day expiry (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`).
- **Schedule** — every **12 hours** via `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` in a ticker loop (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `Run` at line 29), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup.
- **Wiring site** — created and started in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start`. The runner is instantiated at `server/server.go:162`, run once at `server/server.go:163`, and launched as a background goroutine at `server/server.go:169`.
