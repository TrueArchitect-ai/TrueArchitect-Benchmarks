**s3presign Runner — every 12 hours (with one immediate `RunOnce` at startup) — `server/server.go:162` (started at `server/server.go:169`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`)**

Details:

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — the `Runner` type. Its `CheckAndPresign` method walks all attachments whose `StorageType` is S3 (in batches of 100) and re-generates a presigned GET URL for any whose last presign is older than the refresh threshold, then writes the fresh URL back via `UpdateAttachment` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`). This is what keeps the stored `Reference` links to externally-hosted (S3) attachment blobs from expiring. It skips any object still valid for the next 4 days, since presigned URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

- **Schedule:** every 12 hours, driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` using `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `29-41`). Additionally it runs once synchronously at startup (`RunOnce`) before the ticker loop begins.

- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs it and launches it — `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`, an immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`, and the continuous `Run(s3Context)` goroutine at `server/server.go:169`. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
