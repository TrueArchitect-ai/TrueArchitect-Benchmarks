The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3 attachment presigned URLs from going stale. It runs **every 12 hours** (line 27: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`), iterating over all S3-stored attachments in batches and re-presigning any whose URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; refresh triggers when fewer than 4 days remain).

It is wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162` inside `startBackgroundRunners`, where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is created, an initial `RunOnce` is called immediately, and then the continuous `Run` loop is launched in a goroutine.
