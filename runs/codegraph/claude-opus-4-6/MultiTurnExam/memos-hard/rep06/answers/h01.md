**S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — it iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether the presigned URL is older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days), and re-presigns any that are stale by calling `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the store.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule:** Every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`)
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:153` — `startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce` immediately, then launches `Run` in a goroutine. `startBackgroundRunners` is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
