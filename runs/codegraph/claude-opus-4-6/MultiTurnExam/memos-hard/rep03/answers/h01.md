**s3presign.Runner** — every **12 hours** (`time.Hour * 12` ticker, defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162` inside `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).

The runner iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; it re-signs any older than 4 days), generates a fresh presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, and writes the new URL back to the attachment's `Reference` field in the store.
