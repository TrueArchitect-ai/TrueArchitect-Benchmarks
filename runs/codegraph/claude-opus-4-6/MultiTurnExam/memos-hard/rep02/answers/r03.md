r03.

There is no nightly database-cleanup job. The cron scheduler (`internal/scheduler/`) exists as a general-purpose infrastructure component, but no database-cleanup job is registered in it. The server's background runners are the `s3presign.Runner` (runs every 12 hours to refresh presigned URLs, wired in `server/server.go`) and the `memopayload.Runner` (batch payload rebuilder, not scheduled at startup). Neither performs database cleanup, and neither uses the cron scheduler.
