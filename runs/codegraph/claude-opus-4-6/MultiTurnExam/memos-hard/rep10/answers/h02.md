**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, backed by `isReservedIP` and the `reservedCIDRs` / `reservedNetworks` list in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-36`

The SSRF protection works at two layers:
1. **At registration time**: `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) resolves the hostname and rejects URLs pointing at reserved/private IP ranges before the webhook is saved.
2. **At delivery time**: the `safeClient` HTTP client (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32`) uses a custom `Transport` whose `DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`). This re-resolves the hostname at connect time and blocks any IP in the reserved ranges (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS, IPv6 equivalents), defeating DNS rebinding attacks where a hostname resolves to a public IP at registration but a private IP at delivery time.
