**s3presign.Runner** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; it re-signs any older than 4 days), and updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the database. It runs once immediately at startup (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`) and then continuously on a 12-hour ticker via `s3presignRunner.Run(...)` launched in a goroutine at `server/server.go:169`.
