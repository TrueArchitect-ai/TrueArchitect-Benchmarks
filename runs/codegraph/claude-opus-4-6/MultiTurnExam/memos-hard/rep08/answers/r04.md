**Premise correction:** Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. There is no external message broker involved.

**Actual mechanism:** The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain in-process Go channel — `asyncPostQueue`, a buffered `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with capacity 128, declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutine workers are started in the package's `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each reading from this channel and calling `Post` to deliver the webhook. `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues payloads with a non-blocking send, dropping messages if the channel is full.
