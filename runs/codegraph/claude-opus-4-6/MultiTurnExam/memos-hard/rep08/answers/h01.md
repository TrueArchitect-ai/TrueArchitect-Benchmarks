**s3presign.Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`), which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL is older than 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), and re-presigns any that are due, updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the store. On startup it also runs once immediately (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`) before entering the 12-hour ticker loop.
