**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, backed by `isReservedIP` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`. The webhook package's shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32`) uses a custom `http.Transport` whose `DialContext` resolves the target hostname via DNS and then rejects any IP that falls within a reserved/private range (defeating both direct private-IP URLs and DNS rebinding). Additionally, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) performs an up-front check at webhook creation/update time, also calling `isReservedIP` on the resolved host.
