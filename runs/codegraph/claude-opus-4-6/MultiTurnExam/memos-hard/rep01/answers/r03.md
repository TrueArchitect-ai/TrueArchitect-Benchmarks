**Premise correction:** There is no nightly database-cleanup job in this codebase.

The cron scheduler framework exists at `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go` and `internal/scheduler/job.go`, but it is a general-purpose scheduling library — no database-cleanup job is registered anywhere. Searching the codebase for any scheduled job registrations or "cleanup" references reveals no such job.

The only background runners wired into the server at startup (`server/server.go:153`, `startBackgroundRunners`) are the **S3 presigned-URL refresh runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the **memo payload rebuild runner** (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) — neither performs database cleanup, and neither uses the cron scheduler. They run on simple `time.Ticker` intervals, not cron expressions.
