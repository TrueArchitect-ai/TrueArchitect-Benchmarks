**Premise correction:** There is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) anywhere in this codebase. Webhook delivery uses a simple **in-process Go channel** as its queue.

**Actual mechanism:** The async webhook queue is declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. `PostAsync` (line 184) sends a `postRequest` struct onto a buffered Go channel. A pool of goroutine workers (started by `StartAsyncDelivery`, line 160) reads from that channel and calls the synchronous `Post` function (line 120) to execute the outbound HTTP request. The "queue" is just a `chan postRequest` — a plain in-memory channel with no external broker, no persistence, and no delivery guarantees beyond the lifetime of the process.
