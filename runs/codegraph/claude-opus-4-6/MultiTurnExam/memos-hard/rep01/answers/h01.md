**s3presign.Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`.

The `startBackgroundRunners` method (called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`) creates an `s3presign.Runner`, runs it once immediately (`RunOnce`), then launches `runner.Run` in a goroutine which ticks every 12 hours. On each tick, `CheckAndPresign` iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether the presigned URL is older than 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), and re-presigns any that are stale by calling `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the store.
