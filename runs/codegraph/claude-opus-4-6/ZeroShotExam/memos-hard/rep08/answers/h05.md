Emoji reactions are removed by **explicit Go code**, not by a database-level rule. Two dedicated functions handle this within the `deleteUserTargetsTx` transaction, covering both dimensions of reaction ownership:

1. **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** — deletes all reactions on the user's memos (by `content_id`), removing reactions *other people* left on content the deleted user owned. (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`)

2. **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** — deletes all reactions the user themselves created on *any* memo (by `creator_id`). (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`)

Both are called from `deleteUserTargetsTx` (the orchestrating function inside the delete transaction) at lines 96–103 of each driver file.
