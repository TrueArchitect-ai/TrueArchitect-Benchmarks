The `internal/idp` package (`internal/idp/idp.go`) defines the provider abstraction — specifically the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct that all providers return. The `internal/idp/oauth2` package (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`) ships the concrete OAuth2 protocol implementation, providing `IdentityProvider` with `ExchangeToken` and `UserInfo` methods.
