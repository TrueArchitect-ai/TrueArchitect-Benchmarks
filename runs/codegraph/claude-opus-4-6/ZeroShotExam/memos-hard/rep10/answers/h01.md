**S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`.

The runner iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and regenerates the pre-signed URL (valid for 5 days) if less than 4 days remain. It also runs once immediately at startup (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`) before the periodic ticker loop begins in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:167–171`).
