**S3 Presign Runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, line 27). It re-signs any S3 attachment whose presigned URL will expire within the next 4 days (URLs are valid for 5 days).

It is wired into server startup at **`server/server.go:162`**, where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is created, `RunOnce` is called immediately for an initial pass, and then `Run` is launched in a goroutine (line 169) to tick every 12 hours.
