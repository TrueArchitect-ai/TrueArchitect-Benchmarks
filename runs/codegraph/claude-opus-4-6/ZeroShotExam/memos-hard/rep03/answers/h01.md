The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3 pre-signed attachment URLs from going stale. It iterates over all S3-stored attachments, checks whether each pre-signed URL will expire within the next day (URLs are valid for 5 days; it re-signs any older than 4 days), and generates fresh pre-signed URLs.

- **Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`)
- **Wiring site** — `server/server.go:162` inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`
