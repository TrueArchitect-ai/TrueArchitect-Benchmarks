The premise of this question is incorrect. There is no "nightly database-cleanup job" in this codebase. The `internal/scheduler/` package (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `internal/scheduler/job.go`, `internal/scheduler/parser.go`) is a **generic cron scheduling library** — it provides the `Scheduler`, `Job`, `Schedule`, and cron-parsing infrastructure but does not define any specific jobs itself. Jobs are defined by callers who create `Job` structs with their own `Name`, `Schedule`, and `Handler` fields at registration time. No pre-configured "database-cleanup" or similar nightly job exists anywhere in the scheduler code or its examples.
