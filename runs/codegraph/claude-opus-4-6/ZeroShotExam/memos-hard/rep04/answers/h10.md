`DefaultEngine` — `internal/filter/engine.go` — approx 7 callers

This function returns the process-wide memo filter engine and is called from all three database dialect implementations (SQLite, MySQL, Postgres) for both memo and memo-relation queries, plus `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`. It is tied with `util.GenUUID` (`internal/util/util.go`, also ~7 call sites) and the method `(*Engine).CompileToStatement` (`internal/filter/engine.go`, also ~7 call sites via method calls), but `DefaultEngine` edges ahead in unique caller files (7 files vs 6 for `GenUUID`).
