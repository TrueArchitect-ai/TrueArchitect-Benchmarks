Emoji reactions are removed by **explicit Go code**, not by a database-level rule. Two dedicated functions handle this within the `deleteUserTargetsTx` transaction:

1. **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** — deletes all reactions on the user's memos (by content ID), removing reactions left by *any* user on memos owned by the deleted user. SQL: `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`.

2. **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** — deletes all reactions *created by* the deleted user on any memo. SQL: `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`.

Both are called explicitly in `deleteUserTargetsTx` (lines 96–103 in each driver) before memos or the user row are deleted. This pattern is identical across all three database drivers:

- `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96-103` (calls), `:417` and `:437` (implementations)
- `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:96-103` (calls), `:380` and `:400` (implementations)
- `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:96-103` (calls), `:380` and `:400` (implementations)
