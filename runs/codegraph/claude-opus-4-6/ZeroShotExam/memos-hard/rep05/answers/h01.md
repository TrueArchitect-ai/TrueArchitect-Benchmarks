**s3presign.Runner** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` periodically iterates over all S3-stored attachments, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs have a 5-day lifetime; re-signing triggers when only 1 day remains), and generates a fresh presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`. It runs once immediately on startup (`RunOnce` at line 163) and then continuously via a goroutine (`Run` at line 169) that ticks every 12 hours.
