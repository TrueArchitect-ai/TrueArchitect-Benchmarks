**Protection mechanism** — Two layers of SSRF protection, both in `internal/webhook/validate.go`:

1. **Registration-time validation**: `ValidateURL` (line 62) resolves the webhook URL's hostname and checks every resolved IP against `isReservedIP` (line 44), which tests against a list of reserved CIDRs (`reservedCIDRs`, line 14) covering loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud IMDS (`169.254.0.0/16`), and IPv6 equivalents. URLs resolving to any of these are rejected with an `InvalidArgument` error.

2. **Delivery-time validation**: `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line 59) is wired into the `safeClient`'s Transport (line 34). It re-resolves the hostname at connection time and rejects reserved IPs via the same `isReservedIP` check, defeating DNS rebinding attacks where a hostname resolves to a public IP at registration but a private IP at delivery time.
