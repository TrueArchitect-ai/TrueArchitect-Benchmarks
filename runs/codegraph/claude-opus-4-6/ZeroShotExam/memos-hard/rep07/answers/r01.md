There is no Redis in this project. The caching layer uses two distinct **in-process, in-memory** mechanisms — no external cache service at all:

1. **`store/cache/cache.go`** — A generic in-memory cache implementing the `Interface` with `Set`, `SetWithTTL`, `Get`, `Delete`, and `Clear` methods. TTLs are per-item and stored as an `expiration` field on each `item` struct. There is no single hard-coded TTL for "user entries" — callers pass a `time.Duration` via `SetWithTTL`, or use `Set` for the instance's default. User settings are keyed via `getUserSettingCacheKey` in `store/cache.go:7`.

2. **`server/router/rss/rss.go`** — The RSS feed cache is a plain `map[string]*cacheEntry` protected by a `sync.RWMutex` (line 48–49). Its TTL is the `defaultCacheDuration` constant of **1 hour** (`server/router/rss/rss.go:25`), with a max of 50 entries (`maxCacheSize`, line 26). Per-user RSS feeds are cached under the key `"user:<username>"` (line 114).
