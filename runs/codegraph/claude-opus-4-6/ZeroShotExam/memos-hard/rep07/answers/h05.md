Emoji reactions are removed by **explicit Go code**, via two dedicated functions called during the user-deletion transaction. No database-level cascade rule is involved.

The function `deleteUserTargetsTx` (present in all three DB drivers) executes two reaction-deletion steps in sequence:

1. **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** — deletes all reactions on the user's memos (by `content_id`), removing reactions made by *any* user on content the deleted user owns.
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96`
   - `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:380–388`
   - `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:380–388`
   - SQL: `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`

2. **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** — deletes all reactions *created by* the deleted user on anyone's content.
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:102`
   - `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:400–403`
   - `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:400–403`
   - SQL: `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`

Both are called within a transaction in `deleteUserTargetsTx` (`store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:92–127`, and the equivalent in `postgres/user_delete.go` and `sqlite/user_delete.go`).
