Emoji reactions are removed by **explicit Go code**, not by a database-level rule. The deletion happens in two separate steps within the `deleteUserTargetsTx` function:

1. **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** — deletes all reactions on the user's memos (by content ID), removing reactions left by *any* user on the deleted user's content:
   - `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)` 
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go`, `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go`

2. **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** — deletes all reactions *created by* the deleted user on anyone else's content:
   - `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:102`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go`, `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go`

Both are called inside `deleteUserTargetsTx` (`store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:92–127`) within a single transaction, alongside deletion of attachments, memos, inbox entries, settings, and the user row itself. All three database drivers (MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite) implement the same two-step reaction cleanup.
