The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/Runner`) keeps externally-stored attachment links fresh. It iterates over all S3-backed attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; it re-signs any older than 4 days), and writes the new presigned URL back to the attachment record.

- **Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate run at startup (`RunOnce` at line 163)
- **Wiring site** — `server/server.go:162` (instantiated via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` and launched as a background goroutine at line 169)
