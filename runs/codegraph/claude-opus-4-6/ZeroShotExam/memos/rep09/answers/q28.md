**Scheduling infrastructure present but NOT used by the server:**

- **`internal/scheduler.Scheduler`** (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`) — a full cron-expression-based job scheduler with `Register`, `Start`, `Stop`, middleware support, timezone handling, and cron parsing (`internal/scheduler/parser.go`). It is **never imported or instantiated** by any production code; the only imports are from its own test files (`internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`).

- **`server/runner/memopayload.Runner`** (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) — has a `RunOnce` method that rebuilds all memo payloads in batches, but it is **not started as a background runner** by the server. Its `RebuildMemoPayload` function is called inline from the memo API service (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`) during individual memo create/update operations, but the runner itself is never instantiated or scheduled.
