The filter expression is parsed by a custom recursive-descent parser in `internal/filter/engine.go` (the `Engine` struct and its `parseContext` helper in `internal/filter/parser.go`). The engine parses the filter string into an AST of `Expression` nodes, then `internal/filter/render.go` walks that AST and renders it into a SQL `WHERE` clause, switching on `DialectName` (defined in `internal/filter/schema.go` — values like `SQLite`, `MySQL`, `Postgres`) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments (e.g., different date/time functions or quoting).
