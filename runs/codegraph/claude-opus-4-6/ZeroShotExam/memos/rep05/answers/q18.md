The three implementations of the `Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`):

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory function that selects between them at startup:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (lines 18–27) to call the appropriate `NewDB` constructor for sqlite, mysql, or postgres.
