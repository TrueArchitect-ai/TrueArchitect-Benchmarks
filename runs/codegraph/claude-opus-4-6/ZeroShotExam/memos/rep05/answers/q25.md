The filter expression is parsed by a custom recursive-descent parser (a CEL-like syntax) implemented in `internal/filter/engine.go` and `internal/filter/parser.go`. The `Engine` struct (`internal/filter/engine.go`) parses the filter string into an AST of `Expression` nodes, then `internal/filter/render.go` renders that AST into a SQL `WHERE` clause by switching on the `DialectName` (defined in `internal/filter/schema.go` — supporting SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL), adjusting operator syntax, quoting, and function calls (e.g., `INSTR` vs `POSITION`) per dialect.
