Four memo lifecycle events dispatch webhooks:

- **memos.memo.created**: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15`)
- **memos.memo.updated**: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:20`)
- **memos.memo.deleted**: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:25`)
- **memos.memo.comment.created**: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:30`)

The first three converge through the private method `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, fetches their webhooks, builds the payload via `convertMemoToWebhookPayload`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync`. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` skips `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (it receives the related memo creator ID directly) but also calls `webhook.PostAsync`.

All four ultimately converge at `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues the payload onto a buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`). Four goroutines (spawned in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`) drain that channel and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the synchronous HTTP delivery function that marshals the payload, optionally HMAC-signs it, and POSTs it to the webhook URL.
