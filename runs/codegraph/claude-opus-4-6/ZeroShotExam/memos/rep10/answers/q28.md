**Runners the server actually starts:**

- **s3presign.Runner** — `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = 12h`); also runs once synchronously at startup via `RunOnce`. Refreshes presigned URLs for S3 attachments. (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`, started at `server/server.go:162-171`)

That is the **only** background runner started. The `startBackgroundRunners` method (`server/server.go:153`) creates and launches just the S3 presign runner.
