The filter expression is parsed by a custom recursive-descent parser (the `Engine` struct) located at `internal/filter/engine.go`. The engine tokenizes the filter string, parses it into an AST of filter conditions (`parseContext` in `internal/filter/parser.go`), and then `render.go` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks the parsed conditions to emit dialect-specific SQL `WHERE` clauses, switching on `DialectName` (defined in `internal/filter/schema.go`) to handle syntax differences (e.g., SQLite vs. PostgreSQL) for operators like `CONTAINS` and time functions.
