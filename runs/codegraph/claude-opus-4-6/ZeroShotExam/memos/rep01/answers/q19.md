MemoService is served over two protocols: **gRPC** and **Connect (connectrpc.com)**.

1. **gRPC** — `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service_grpc.pb.go` (generated code; `RegisterMemoServiceServer` at line 439 registers the service with the gRPC server). The grpc-gateway HTTP wrapper is also generated in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go` (`RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` at line 961).

2. **Connect** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` registers the MemoService as a Connect handler via `RegisterConnectHandlers`).
