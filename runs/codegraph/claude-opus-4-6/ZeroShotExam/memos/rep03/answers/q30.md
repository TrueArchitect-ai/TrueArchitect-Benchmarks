Four memo lifecycle events dispatch webhooks:

- **memo created**: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15`)
- **memo updated**: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:20`)
- **memo deleted**: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:25`)
- **memo comment created**: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:30`)

All four converge through `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues payloads onto a buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`). Four goroutines (spawned in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`) drain that channel and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) for actual HTTP delivery.

The first three share a common private helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`) which resolves the memo creator's webhooks and calls `webhook.PostAsync` for each. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` builds its own loop (using the *related memo* creator's webhooks) but likewise calls `webhook.PostAsync` for delivery.

Delivery function/path: `webhook.Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`.
