`Driver` — `store/driver.go`

The `Driver` interface (defined at line 10 of `store/driver.go`) is implemented by all three database backends: SQLite (`store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`), MySQL (`store/db/mysql/mysql.go`), and PostgreSQL (`store/db/postgres/postgres.go`). Each backend's `DB` struct satisfies this interface, and `store/db/db.go:NewDBDriver` selects the appropriate one based on the configured driver name.
