When a memo is deleted, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140–158`) deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations (source)** — `DeleteMemoRelation` where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142`)
2. **Memo relations (target)** — `DeleteMemoRelation` where the memo is the related/target memo (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:145`)
3. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo, found via `ListAttachments` and deleted one-by-one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149–157`)
