The filter expression is parsed by a custom recursive-descent parser (the `Engine` struct) living in `internal/filter/engine.go`. The engine tokenizes the filter string, parses it into an AST of `Expression` nodes via `parseContext` (`internal/filter/parser.go`), and then `render.go` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that AST to emit a SQL `WHERE` clause, switching on `DialectName` (defined in `internal/filter/schema.go`) to produce dialect-specific SQL (e.g., quoting differences for SQLite vs. PostgreSQL).
