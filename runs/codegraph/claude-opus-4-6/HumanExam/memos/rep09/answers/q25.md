CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; the `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string into a CEL AST, `buildCondition` in `internal/filter/parser.go` walks it into a dialect-agnostic condition tree, and `Render` in `internal/filter/render.go` emits dialect-specific SQL WHERE fragments by switching on dialect name (sqlite/mysql/postgres), appended via `AppendConditions` in `internal/filter/helpers.go`.
