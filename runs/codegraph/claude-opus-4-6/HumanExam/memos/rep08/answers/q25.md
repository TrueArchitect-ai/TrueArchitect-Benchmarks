Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via google/cel-go, located at `internal/filter/`. The filter string is compiled by `engine.Compile()` (`internal/filter/engine.go:48`) which parses the CEL expression into an AST, then `buildCondition()` (`internal/filter/parser.go:18`) converts it to a dialect-agnostic IR. A `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`) then translates the IR to dialect-specific SQL (SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL) with parameterized placeholders, handling differences like `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`/`@>`, `?` vs `$N` placeholders, and `ILIKE` vs `memos_unicode_lower`.
