CEL (Common Expression Language) via `google/cel-go`; engine at `internal/filter/` (main types in `engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`). The CEL expression is parsed into an AST, converted to a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree, then rendered to SQL per dialect (SQLite/PostgreSQL/MySQL) via `filter.AppendConditions`, which each driver calls with its dialect constant (e.g., `filter.DialectSQLite` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:61`).
