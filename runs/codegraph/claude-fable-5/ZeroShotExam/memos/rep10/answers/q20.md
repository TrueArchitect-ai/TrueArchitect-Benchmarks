**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects (UpdateMemo path), UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

Details:

- **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` is registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`), which sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data: ...\n\n` frames, and sends `: heartbeat` comments every 30 seconds.
- **Hub**: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` implements `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Close`, and `Broadcast`, which fans events out to all connected clients (with per-client visibility filtering via `canReceive` and drop-on-full-buffer for slow clients). Event payloads (`SSEEvent`, types like `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`) are defined there too.
- **Handlers calling `SSEHub.Broadcast`**:
  - `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
  - `CreateMemoComment` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
  - `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go` (the UpdateMemo side-effect path)
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
