**Engine + path:** CEL (Google Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`), wrapped in the in-repo filter engine at `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`) validates the filter and appends it to `FindMemo.Filters`; each store driver's `ListMemos` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:54`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:62`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:51`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect constant, whereupon `Engine.Compile` (`internal/filter/engine.go:48`) compiles the string through a schema-configured `cel.Env`, `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go:18`) walks the CEL parsed-expression AST into a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR to emit a parameterized SQL fragment plus args specialized per `DialectName` (SQLite, MySQL, or Postgres — e.g. dialect-specific column quoting, placeholders, and JSON/date-part expressions).
