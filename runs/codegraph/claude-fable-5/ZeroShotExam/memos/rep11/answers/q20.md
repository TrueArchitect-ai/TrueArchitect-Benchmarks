**Mechanism; hub path; handler names:**

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). Browsers connect to `GET /api/v1/sse` (registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` by `RegisterSSERoutes`), which sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, sends `data: ...` frames plus `: heartbeat` comments every 30 seconds, and streams JSON `SSEEvent` payloads (event types like `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`).

- **Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`, holding a mutex-guarded set of `SSEClient`s (each with a buffered channel; events to slow clients are dropped, and private-memo events are filtered per client in `canReceive`).

- **Handlers that broadcast through it** (all in `server/router/api/v1/`):
  - `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` in `memo_service.go` (e.g. the `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)` call at `memo_service.go:585`)
  - `UpdateMemo` in `memo_service.go`, which broadcasts via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go`
  - `CreateMemoComment` in `memo_service_comments.go`
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `reaction_service.go`, which build their events with `buildMemoReactionSSEEvent` from `sse_event_helpers.go`
