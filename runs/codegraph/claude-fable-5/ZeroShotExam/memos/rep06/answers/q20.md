**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). The browser opens a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse` stream (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, consumed client-side by `useLiveMemoRefresh` in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`, which fetches `/api/v1/sse` with `Accept: text/event-stream` and invalidates React Query caches on each event).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct manages `SSEClient` connections (`Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`) and fans out events via `Broadcast`, with per-client visibility filtering in `canReceive`.

**Handlers that broadcast through it** (callers of `hub.Broadcast`):
- `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (the UpdateMemo path) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
- `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`

**Answer:** SSE (Server-Sent Events over `GET /api/v1/sse`); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects (UpdateMemo), CreateMemoComment, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction
