MemoService is served over two protocols, both wired up in `(*APIV1Service).RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go`):

1. **HTTP/JSON REST via gRPC-Gateway** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the generated gateway handlers from `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go` onto the mux mounted at `/api/v1/*`).

2. **Connect RPC (connectrpc.com, replacing grpc-web for browser clients)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, which wraps `APIV1Service` in `ConnectServiceHandler` and mounts the handlers; invoked from `v1.go:141` and served under `/memos.api.v1.*`).

Both protocols dispatch to the same `APIV1Service` implementation (which embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer`), so business logic exists once and is wrapped twice.
