In `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`), before deleting the memo row itself the store layer deletes:

- **Memo relation records** — two `DeleteMemoRelation` calls remove `memo_relation` rows where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142-147`).
- **Attachment records** — it lists all attachments linked to the memo via `ListAttachments` and deletes each one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`).

Only after those cleanups does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row.
