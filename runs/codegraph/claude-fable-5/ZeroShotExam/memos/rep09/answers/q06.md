**`memo_relation`**

This table stores links between one memo and another related memo. It's defined in the schema files for all three supported databases — e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`, and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46` — with columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`. It was originally introduced in the migration `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`.
