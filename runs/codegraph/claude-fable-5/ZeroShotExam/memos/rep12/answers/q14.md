ShortcutService defines five RPCs:

- **ListShortcuts**
- **GetShortcut**
- **CreateShortcut**
- **UpdateShortcut**
- **DeleteShortcut**

The backend implementation of all five lives in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** (e.g., `ListShortcuts` at `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:49`).

For completeness: the service is defined in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto` (generated stubs in `proto/gen/api/v1/shortcut_service_grpc.pb.go` and `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/shortcut_service.connect.go`), and `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:528-566` contains thin Connect-protocol wrappers that just delegate each RPC to the `APIV1Service` methods in `shortcut_service.go`.
