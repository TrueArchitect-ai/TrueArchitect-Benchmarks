**Mechanism; hub path; handler names:** Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; `CreateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (the update path), `CreateMemoComment`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`.

Details:

- **Mechanism:** SSE over HTTP. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` is registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`), which sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data: ...` frames, and sends `: heartbeat` comments every 30 seconds.
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — it tracks `SSEClient` connections (`Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`) and `Broadcast` fans an `SSEEvent` out to all clients, with per-client visibility filtering (`canReceive`) and drop-on-full-buffer for slow clients. It's tested in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub_test.go`.
- **Broadcasting handlers:** `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)` is called from:
  - `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (events `memo.created`, `memo.deleted`)
  - `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go` (`memo.updated`, fired from the memo update flow)
  - `CreateMemoComment` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go` (`memo.comment.created`)
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (`reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`, built via `buildMemoReactionSSEEvent` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`)
