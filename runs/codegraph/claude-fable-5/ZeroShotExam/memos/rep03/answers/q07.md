The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It builds a Connect transport via `createConnectTransport` (with an auth interceptor) and exports all the service clients created with `createClient` — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (web/src/connect.ts:192-203).
