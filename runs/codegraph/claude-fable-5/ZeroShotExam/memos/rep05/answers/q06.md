**`memo_relation`**

This table stores links between one memo and another related memo, with columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`. It is defined in the schema files `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (also in the postgres and mysql variants under `store/migration/`), and was introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`.
