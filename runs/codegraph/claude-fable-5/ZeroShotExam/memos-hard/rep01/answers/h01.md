The **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps attachment links fresh. Attachments stored in S3 carry a presigned GET URL as their reference; the runner's `CheckAndPresign` method pages through all S3-stored attachments and regenerates the presigned URL for any whose last presign is older than 4 days (the URLs themselves expire after 5 days), writing the new URL back via `Store.UpdateAttachment`.

- **Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`)
- **Schedule** — every 12 hours, via `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) driving a ticker in `Run` (`runner.go:29`); it also runs once immediately at startup
- **Wiring site** — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` (creates the runner at line 162, calls `RunOnce` at 163, then launches the continuous `Run` loop in a goroutine at 167-171), invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`
