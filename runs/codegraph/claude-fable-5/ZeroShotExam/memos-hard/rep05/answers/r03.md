The premise of the question is incorrect: **there is no nightly database-cleanup job actually scheduled in this repository.** The cron scheduler at `internal/scheduler/` is a self-contained library package, and nothing in the application imports or uses it — no `Register` call for any real job exists outside the package's own documentation and tests.

What the question is likely picking up on are illustrative examples, not real scheduled jobs:

- `internal/scheduler/README.md` (Quick Start) and `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15` show a sample job named `"daily-cleanup"` with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily).
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-99` registers an example `"cleanup"` job at `"0 2 * * *"` ("every night at 2 AM") inside a Go example test.

So if you take the examples at face value, the demo cleanup job runs at **2:00 AM**, but no such job is wired up in the running application, and the examples describe generic "old data" cleanup rather than a database-cleanup job specifically.
