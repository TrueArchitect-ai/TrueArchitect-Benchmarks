The S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) is what keeps externally-stored attachment links fresh: its `CheckAndPresign` method scans S3-backed attachments in batches and regenerates presigned GET URLs before they expire (URLs live 5 days; anything last presigned more than 4 days ago is refreshed).

**Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`)
**Schedule** — every 12 hours via a ticker (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` pass at startup
**Wiring site** — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` (runner created at line 162, immediate `RunOnce` at line 163, continuous `Run` goroutine started at line 167-171), which is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
