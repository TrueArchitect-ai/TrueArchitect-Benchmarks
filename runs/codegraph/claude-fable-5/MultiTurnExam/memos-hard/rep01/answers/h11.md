Method: the persistent entity types are the structs in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.), with drivers under `store/db/`. I grepped non-test Go files outside `store/` for references to each `store.<Type>` and counted distinct packages:

- **`store.User`** (`store/user.go:29`) — referenced from 6 packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, and the test-helper package `server/router/api/v1/test` (7 counting test files).
- **`store.Memo`** (`store/memo.go`) — referenced from 4 packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload` (6 counting test packages).
- Runners-up for context: `store.Attachment` — 3 packages (`server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`); `store.MemoShare` and `store.Inbox` — 2 each; everything else (`Reaction`, `MemoRelation`, `UserIdentity`, …) — 1 or fewer outside the store layer.

So the user entity is the most widely shared data-layer type across the backend, followed by the memo entity.
