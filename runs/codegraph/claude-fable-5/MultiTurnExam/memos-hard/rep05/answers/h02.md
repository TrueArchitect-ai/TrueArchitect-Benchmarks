**Delivery function:** `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (with `PostAsync` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` enqueuing to a bounded queue drained by 4 worker goroutines in `init`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`; the API layer triggers dispatch from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`).

**Protection mechanism:** dial-time SSRF blocking via `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`. All deliveries go through the shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`), whose `http.Transport.DialContext` resolves the target host at connect time and refuses any address in a reserved/private range (`isReservedIP` against the `reservedCIDRs` list of loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS, and IPv6 equivalents — `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`). Doing the check at dial time also defeats DNS-rebinding. A complementary registration-time check, `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`), rejects such URLs up front when webhooks are created/updated in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`, and the escape hatch `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) can disable the restriction for self-hosted LAN deployments.
