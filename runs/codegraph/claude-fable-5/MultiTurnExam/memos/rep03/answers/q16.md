Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes:

- **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation`) — deleted twice, once where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and once where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
- **Attachments** linked to the memo — it lists them via `ListAttachments` and deletes each with `DeleteAttachment`

Only after that does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row.
