**memo_relation**

The `memo_relation` table stores links between one memo and another (columns `memo_id` and `related_memo_id`). It's defined in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47` (introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`, with equivalents under the `mysql/` and `postgres/` migration directories), and accessed via `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go` and its sibling drivers.
