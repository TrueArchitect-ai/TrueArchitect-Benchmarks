Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting memo handlers: CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoAttachments, SetMemoRelations

Details:
- The SSE endpoint is registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`), with the handler in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` and event builders in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`. The frontend listens via `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- Direct broadcasts: `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` (`memo_service.go:163`), `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (`memo_service.go:585`), `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` (`memo_service_comments.go:126`).
- Indirect broadcasts via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go:71`): `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`).
- (Reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` in `reaction_service.go` also broadcast memo-reaction events, if you count them as memo-adjacent.)
