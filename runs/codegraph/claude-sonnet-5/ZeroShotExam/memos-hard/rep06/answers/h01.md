**s3presign.Runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — re-presigns S3-backed attachment content links before their presigned URLs expire.

- **Schedule:** runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each tick calls `CheckAndPresign`, which re-presigns any S3 attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of the 5-day expiry (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `(*Server).startBackgroundRunners`, called from `(*Server).Start` at `server/server.go:129`. It constructs the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, line 162), runs it once synchronously (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`, line 163), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine (line 169) for the recurring 12-hour cycle.
