**Premise correction:** Neither. This repository (usememos/memos) doesn't use an external message broker for webhook delivery — there's no NATS or RabbitMQ integration anywhere in the codebase.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook dispatch uses an in-process, in-memory Go channel as a bounded work queue:

- The queue is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:
  ```go
  asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
  ```
- A fixed pool of 4 goroutines is spun up in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) to drain the channel and call `Post()` for each payload.
- `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-195`) enqueues a payload with a non-blocking `select`/`default`, dropping (and logging a warning for) the dispatch if the channel is full — there's no persistence or redelivery.
- Callers like `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48-74`) and `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:30-46`) invoke `webhook.PostAsync(payload)` to trigger delivery.

The same pattern (unbuffered external broker replaced by a channel + goroutine pool) appears for email sending too, via `asyncEmailQueue` at `internal/email/email.go:14`.
