**`addArg`** — `internal/filter/render.go:842` (method on `*renderer`) — approx **14 callers**

This is the highest inbound fan-in among functions/methods declared under `internal/`. It's the helper that appends a bound SQL parameter and returns its placeholder, called from nearly every comparison/predicate renderer in the CEL-to-SQL filter engine: `renderAccessorComparison` (render.go:200), `renderFunctionComparison` (249), `renderScalarComparison` (304), `renderJSONBoolComparison` (357), `renderTagInList` (434), `renderElementInCondition` (483), `renderScalarInCondition` (516), `renderRegex` (558), `foldedLike` (578), `elementPredicateSQL` (695), `renderTagEquals` (711), `renderTagStartsWith` (727), `buildJSONArrayLike` (770), and its sibling `addBoolArg` (851), which itself wraps it.

I checked every other `internal/` subpackage (filter, markdown, scheduler, webhook, email, httpgetter, idp, ai, motionphoto, storage, profile, version, util, base) via `codegraph callers` on each declared function/method; nothing else exceeded single digits of non-test callers (the next closest in `internal/filter` were `qualifyColumn`/`expectLiteral`/`sqlOperator` at 5–6). Note: caller counts here are per distinct calling function, not per call-site, and test-only callers (e.g. `internal/filter/engine_test.go`) were excluded since the question is about production fan-in.
