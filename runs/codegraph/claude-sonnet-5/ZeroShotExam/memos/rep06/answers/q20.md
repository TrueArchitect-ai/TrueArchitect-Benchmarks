q20. mechanism; hub path; handler names

- **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events (SSE) via a custom in-process hub (`SSEHub`) that fans out JSON `SSEEvent`s to subscribed per-connection channels, with per-visibility filtering (`canReceive`) and slow-client event dropping.
- **Hub path**: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub` struct, `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`, and the `Broadcast` method at line 115). It's wired up in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` and referenced from `server/server.go` (`s.sseHub`) for shutdown via `closeLongLivedConnections`.
- **Handlers that call `Broadcast`**:
  - `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
  - `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go` (handles memo-update broadcasts)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
