MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/JSON REST)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `RegisterGateway` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` (using the generated handler in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`).

Note: there is no plain `grpc.NewServer` + `RegisterMemoServiceServer` (raw gRPC) call anywhere in `server/server.go` — only these two HTTP-mux-based registrations occur, both wired inside `RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
