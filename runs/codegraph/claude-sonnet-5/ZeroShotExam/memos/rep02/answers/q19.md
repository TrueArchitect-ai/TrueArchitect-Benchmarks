MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON-over-HTTP)** — `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` is called at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` (implementation in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go:961`), wired into the Echo server's `/api/v1/*` route group in `RegisterGateway`.

2. **Connect RPC (Connect/gRPC/gRPC-Web protocols for browser clients)** — `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` is wrapped at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, mounted on the `/memos.api.v1.*` route group (also set up in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, lines 139–144), with the handler generated in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go:461`.

Both are registered from the same entry point, `(*APIV1Service).RegisterGateway` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, called from `NewServer` in `server/server.go:88`. Note: there is no standalone native-gRPC server (e.g., via `grpc.NewServer`) in `server/server.go` — all traffic is served over HTTP via Echo, with gRPC-Gateway and Connect as the two protocol adapters over that MemoServiceServer/MemoServiceHandler implementation.
