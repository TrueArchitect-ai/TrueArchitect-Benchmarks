Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over `GET /api/v1/sse`, streamed to the browser and consumed client-side via `useLiveMemoRefresh` (`web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`).

Hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub` struct, with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`canReceive`), wired to the route in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`).

Handlers that broadcast through it (`hub.Broadcast(...)`, found in `server/router/api/v1/*.go`):
- `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` (`memo_service.go`)
- `CreateMemoComment` (`memo_service_comments.go`)
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_update_helpers.go`, used by `UpdateMemo`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` (`reaction_service.go`)
