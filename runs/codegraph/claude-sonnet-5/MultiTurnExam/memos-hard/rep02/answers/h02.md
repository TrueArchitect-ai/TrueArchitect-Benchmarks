**Protection mechanism:** a custom `http.Transport.DialContext` — `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`, wired into `safeClient` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`. It resolves the target host at dial time and rejects any resolved IP that falls in a reserved/private range, via `isReservedIP`/`reservedNetworks` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`. This guards against SSRF (including DNS-rebinding, since the check happens on the dial's actual resolved IP, not just at URL-validation time). A pre-flight check, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`), also runs at webhook creation/update time (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74`), covering the same reserved-CIDR list.
