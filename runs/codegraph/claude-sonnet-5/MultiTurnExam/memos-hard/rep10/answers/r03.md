**Premise correction:** There is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled anywhere in this codebase. A cron scheduler library does exist at `internal/scheduler/` (`scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`), and its doc comment (`internal/scheduler/doc.go:14-17`) shows an *illustrative example* of registering a `"daily-cleanup"` job at `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM) — but that's documentation only, not real wiring.

Searching for actual usages of `scheduler.New(...)` / `scheduler.Register(...)` outside the `internal/scheduler` package itself (including `server/server.go` and all of `server/`) turns up nothing — the only references are in the package's own tests, example test, and doc comment. The scheduler is never instantiated by `NewServer` or `(*Server).Start` (`server/server.go`), and the only background jobs actually started at server startup are the `s3presign` runner (every 12 hours) and a one-time `RunOnce` call — see `startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:153-174`. No database-cleanup job of any kind runs on it.
