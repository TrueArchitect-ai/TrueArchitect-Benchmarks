**Delivery function:** `Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180` (invoked synchronously, and via the bounded worker pool in `PostAsync`/the `asyncPostQueue` consumers, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` and `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). **Protection mechanism:** the shared `safeClient`'s custom `Transport.DialContext = safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37` and `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`), which re-resolves the hostname at dial time and rejects any resolved IP that `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44-54`, checked against `reservedNetworks`/`reservedCIDRs` covering loopback, RFC-1918, and link-local/cloud-IMDS ranges, `internal/webhook/validate.go:11-36`) flags as reserved/private — this defeats DNS-rebinding since it checks the IP actually being connected to, not just the URL's hostname at creation time (that earlier, weaker check is `webhook.ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`, used only when a webhook is created/updated in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`).
