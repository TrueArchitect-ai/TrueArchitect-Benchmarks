Engine — CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`), implemented in `internal/filter/` (parser.go, engine.go, ir.go, render.go, schema.go)

Mechanism: the CEL filter string is parsed and type-checked by `Engine` (`internal/filter/engine.go`) against a `Schema` of allowed fields into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), then `renderer.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits parameterized SQL fragments per target dialect (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`, `internal/filter/schema.go`), substituting dialect-specific function/operator templates (e.g. string concatenation, timestamp conversions) as it goes.
