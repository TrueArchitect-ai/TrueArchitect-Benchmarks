Actual mechanism: Webhooks use an in-memory buffered Go channel for asynchronous delivery in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:
```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

- Queue buffer size: 128 messages
- Worker pool: 4 concurrent goroutines
