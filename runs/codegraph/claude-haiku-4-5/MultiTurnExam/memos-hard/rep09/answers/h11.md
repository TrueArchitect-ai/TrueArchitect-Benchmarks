**1st: Memo (6 packages); 2nd: User (5 packages)**

The persistent entity types defined in the data layer show the following distribution of references from outside packages:

- **Memo**: Referenced from 6 distinct packages outside the data layer:
  - server/notification
  - server/router/api/v1
  - server/router/fileserver
  - server/router/frontend
  - server/router/rss
  - server/runner/memopayload

- **User**: Referenced from 5 distinct packages:
  - server/auth
  - server/notification
  - server/router/api/v1
  - server/router/fileserver
  - server/router/rss

(Other types like Attachment, Reaction, MemoShare, and UserIdentity have 4, 3, 2, and 2 packages respectively)
