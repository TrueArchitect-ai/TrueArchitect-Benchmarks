**s3presign.Runner** — **every 12 hours** — **server/server.go:162**

The `s3presign.Runner` component keeps S3 attachment presigned URLs from going stale. It runs on a 12-hour interval (defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` as `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) and is wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162-170` in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method. The runner regenerates presigned URLs for S3 attachments that haven't been refreshed in the last 4 days (before their 5-day expiration), keeping external links accessible.
