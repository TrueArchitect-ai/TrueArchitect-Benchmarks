The component that keeps links to externally-stored attachment content from going stale is **S3PresignRunner** (or `Runner` in the `s3presign` package), which refreshes S3 presigned URLs on a schedule of **every 12 hours**, and it is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:162-171**.

**Detailed breakdown:**
- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner` (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule**: Every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at line 27)
- **Wiring site**: `server/server.go:162-171` where it's instantiated and run as a background goroutine

The runner refreshes presigned URLs for S3-stored attachments, checking if the last presigned time is older than 4 days (since presigned URLs expire after 5 days) and regenerating them as needed.
