**Premise correction:** There is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled in the Memos codebase. The question assumes the existence of a scheduled database maintenance task that is not implemented.

The server's background runner infrastructure in `server/server.go:153-174` (`startBackgroundRunners`) only registers two background tasks:
1. **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`): runs every 12 hours on a ticker interval
2. **Memo payload runner** (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`): runs once on startup to rebuild memo metadata

No scheduler-based job queue or cron infrastructure is used for database maintenance. The codebase has a scheduler package (`internal/scheduler/`), but it is not instantiated or used in the running server. Database vacuuming, optimization, or other cleanup operations are not scheduled.
