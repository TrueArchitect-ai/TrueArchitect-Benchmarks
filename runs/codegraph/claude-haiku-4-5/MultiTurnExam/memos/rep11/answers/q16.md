q16.
Memo relations (as both source and target)
Attachments

When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following records before deleting the memo row itself:
1. Memo relations where the memo is the source (MemoID)
2. Memo relations where the memo is the target (RelatedMemoID)
3. Attachments linked to the memo

As shown in `store/memo.go:140-159`, the `DeleteMemo` function performs these cascading deletions before calling the driver's `DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row itself.
