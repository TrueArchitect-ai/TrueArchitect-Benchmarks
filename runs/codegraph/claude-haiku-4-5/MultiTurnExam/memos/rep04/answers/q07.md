**web/src/connect.ts**

The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. This file imports all the service definitions from the proto-generated types (AIService, AttachmentService, AuthService, IdentityProviderService, InstanceService, MemoService, ShortcutService, UserService) and exports the service clients created using `createClient()` with a configured transport that includes authentication interceptors and other necessary configuration.
