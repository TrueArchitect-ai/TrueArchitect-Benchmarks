**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment**

The mechanism used is **Server-Sent Events (SSE)**. The hub is implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`. The memo handlers that broadcast through the SSE hub are:
- `CreateMemo` (broadcasts `SSEEventMemoCreated`)
- `DeleteMemo` (broadcasts `SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment` (broadcasts `SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`)
