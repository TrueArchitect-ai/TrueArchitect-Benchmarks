**8 services: AIService, AttachmentService, AuthService, IdentityProviderService, InstanceService, MemoService, ShortcutService, UserService**

The project defines 8 gRPC/Connect services under `proto/api/v1/`, one in each of these proto files:
- `ai_service.proto` - AIService
- `attachment_service.proto` - AttachmentService
- `auth_service.proto` - AuthService
- `idp_service.proto` - IdentityProviderService
- `instance_service.proto` - InstanceService
- `memo_service.proto` - MemoService
- `shortcut_service.proto` - ShortcutService
- `user_service.proto` - UserService
