**NewDBDriver — store/db/db.go**

The three database driver implementations are in:
- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` (SQLite implementation)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` (MySQL implementation)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` (PostgreSQL implementation)

The factory function `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` selects between them at startup based on the driver type from the profile configuration.
