NewDBDriver — store/db/db.go

The three implementations of the database driver interface are:
1. SQLite: store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go
2. MySQL: store/db/mysql/mysql.go
3. PostgreSQL: store/db/postgres/postgres.go

The factory function `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` (lines 14-32) selects between them at startup based on the `profile.Driver` field, calling the appropriate `NewDB` constructor for each dialect.
