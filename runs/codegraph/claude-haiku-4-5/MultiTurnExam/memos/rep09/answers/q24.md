The complete call chain for creating a memo is:
1. **Frontend mutation hook** - `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` calls the mutation
2. **ConnectRPC client** - `web/src/connect.ts` (memoServiceClient) sends the request
3. **Proto RPC definition** - `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (CreateMemo RPC)
4. **Backend handler** - `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (APIV1Service.CreateMemo method)
5. **Store layer** - `store/memo.go` (Store.CreateMemo method)
6. **SQLite driver** - `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` (DB.CreateMemo method that executes INSERT)
