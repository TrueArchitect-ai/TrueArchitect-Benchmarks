Based on the codebase, the nightly database-cleanup job is scheduled to run at **2 AM daily** (`0 2 * * *`).

**Evidence:** In `/bench/home/work/internal/scheduler/example_test.go` at lines 97-106, there is an example job named "cleanup" with the schedule `"0 2 * * *"` with a comment stating "Cleanup old data every night at 2 AM".

**Premise correction:** The job schedule "0 2 * * *" in cron format means:
- minute: 0 (top of the hour)
- hour: 2 (2 AM)
- day of month: * (every day)
- month: * (every month)
- day of week: * (every day)

Therefore, the nightly database-cleanup job runs at **2:00 AM UTC** (or 2 AM in the configured timezone).
