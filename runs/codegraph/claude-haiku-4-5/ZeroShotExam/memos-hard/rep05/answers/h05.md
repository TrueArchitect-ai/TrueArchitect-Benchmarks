h05. Emoji reactions are removed by explicit Go code.

**Mechanism:** When a user is deleted, the deletion process explicitly executes two separate `DELETE` SQL statements to remove emoji reactions:

1. **`deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx`** (lines 417-425 in MySQL, 380-388 in SQLite, 380-388 in PostgreSQL) — deletes all reactions on the user's memos by deleting reactions where `content_id` matches the memo content IDs.
2. **`deleteReactionsByCreatorTx`** (lines 437-440 in MySQL, 400-403 in SQLite, 400-403 in PostgreSQL) — deletes all reactions created by the user by deleting reactions where `creator_id` matches the deleted user's ID.

These functions are called explicitly in `deleteUserTargetsTx` at lines 96 and 102 respectively (in all database implementations: `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go`, `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go`).

**Evidence locations:**
- `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96-104` (deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx and deleteReactionsByCreatorTx calls)
- `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:417-440` (both deletion function implementations)
- `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:96-104` and `:380-403` (same calls and implementations)
- `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:96-104` and `:380-403` (same calls and implementations)
