The ConnectRPC service clients are created in **web/src/connect.ts**.

This file:
- Creates the transport using `createConnectTransport()` (lines 184-189)
- Instantiates service clients using `createClient()` with the transport (lines 192-203)
- Exports multiple service clients including: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`
