**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Based on the migration directory structure at `store/migration/`, the project ships migrations for three SQL dialects:
- `store/migration/mysql/` 
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

This is further confirmed by the dialect constants defined in `internal/filter/schema.go:14-16`:
- `DialectSQLite`
- `DialectMySQL`
- `DialectPostgres`
