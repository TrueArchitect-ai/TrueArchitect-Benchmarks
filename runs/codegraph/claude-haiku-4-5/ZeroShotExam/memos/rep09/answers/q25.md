The **CEL (Common Expression Language)** engine parses the filter expressions, located in `internal/filter/engine.go`. The mechanism works in three steps: (1) the Engine compiles the CEL filter string using `cel.Compile()` from the `github.com/google/cel-go/cel` package, (2) converts it to an AST and then to an intermediate condition tree via `buildCondition()` in `internal/filter/parser.go`, and (3) the `Render()` method in `internal/filter/render.go` converts this dialect-agnostic condition tree to dialect-specific SQL via a renderer that switches on the `DialectName` (DialectMySQL, DialectSQLite, DialectPostgres) to generate appropriately formatted SQL fragments with parameterized placeholders.
