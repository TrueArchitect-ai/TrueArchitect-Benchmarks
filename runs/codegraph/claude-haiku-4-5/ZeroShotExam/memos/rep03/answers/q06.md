The database table that stores links between one memo and another related memo is **`memo_relation`**.

This is evident from multiple locations in the codebase:
- The MySQL implementation at `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go:13` shows the INSERT statement: `INSERT INTO `memo_relation` (`memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, `type`)`
- The table has three columns: `memo_id` (the source memo), `related_memo_id` (the related memo), and `type` (the relationship type, such as REFERENCE or COMMENT)
