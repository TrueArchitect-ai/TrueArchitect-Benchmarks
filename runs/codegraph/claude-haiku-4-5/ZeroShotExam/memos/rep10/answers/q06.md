**memo_relation**

This is the database table that stores links between one memo and another related memo. The table is referenced across all database implementations:
- `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go:13` - `INSERT INTO `memo_relation``
- `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go:14` - `INSERT INTO memo_relation`
- `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go:14` - `INSERT INTO memo_relation`

The table has three main columns: `memo_id` (the source memo), `related_memo_id` (the related memo), and `type` (the type of relation, either `REFERENCE` or `COMMENT`).
