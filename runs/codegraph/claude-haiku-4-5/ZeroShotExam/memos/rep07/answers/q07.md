The ConnectRPC service clients are created in **web/src/connect.ts**.

In this file, the service clients (like `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`, etc.) are instantiated using the `createClient` function from `@connectrpc/connect` with a shared transport (web/src/connect.ts:184-189) that has the authentication interceptor configured.
