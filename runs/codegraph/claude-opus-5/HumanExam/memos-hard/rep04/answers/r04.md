Premise wrong — neither. There is no message broker at all: no NATS, RabbitMQ, or AMQP references in the Go sources or `go.mod`, and no queue is declared. Webhook delivery is in-process: `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) does a non-blocking send onto a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`). Overflow is dropped with a warning (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:191-195`) — no durability.
