Actual mechanism: webhook delivery is in-process. `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) does a non-blocking send into a buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutine workers are started in the package `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) and each calls `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Overflow is dropped with a warning, not persisted (`:191-195`), so pending deliveries are lost on restart.
