Webhook delivery uses an in-process Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`).
