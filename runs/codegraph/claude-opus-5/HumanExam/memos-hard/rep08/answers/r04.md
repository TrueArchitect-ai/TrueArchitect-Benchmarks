Premise is wrong: there is no message broker — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (no such dependency in `go.mod`, no reference in any source file), and no queue is declared anywhere. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) is drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). `PostAsync` (`:184`) does a non-blocking send and drops the delivery with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). Nothing is persisted or retried.
