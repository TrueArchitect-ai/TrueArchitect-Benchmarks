Three caches are created in `store.New` — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (`store/store.go:47-51`) — all sharing one config with `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000` (`store/store.go:39-44`; same defaults at `store/cache/cache.go:57-64`). So user entries expire 10 minutes after write; they are also invalidated explicitly on user update/delete (`store/user.go:179`).
