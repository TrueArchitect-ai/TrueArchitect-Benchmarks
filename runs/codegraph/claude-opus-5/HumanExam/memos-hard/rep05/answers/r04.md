— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka/AMQP) appears in `go.mod` or any Go source. Webhook delivery is in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). `PostAsync` (`:184`) enqueues non-blockingly and **drops** the event with a warning when the queue is full (`:191-195`); nothing is persisted, so pending deliveries are lost on restart.
