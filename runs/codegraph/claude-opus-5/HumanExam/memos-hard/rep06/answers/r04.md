Premise is wrong: **no message broker at all** — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ/AMQP appears in the code or `go.mod`, and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery is in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), fed by `PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:191-195`). Delivery itself is `Post` (`:120`) over `safeClient`. Nothing survives a process restart.
