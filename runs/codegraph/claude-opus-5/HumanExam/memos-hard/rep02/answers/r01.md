— False premise: there is no Redis anywhere in this codebase (no `redis` import, dependency, or config key).

Actual mechanism: an in-process, in-memory cache — `store/cache/cache.go`, a `sync.Map`-backed `Cache` with per-item expiry and a background `cleanupLoop`. `Store` holds three of them (`instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`) at `store/store.go:27-29`, all constructed in `store.New` with one shared `cache.Config` (`store/store.go:40-53`): `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000`. User entries are written with `Set` (`store/user.go:98`, `:122`, `:132`, `:143`, `:167`), and `Set` applies `c.config.DefaultTTL` (`store/cache/cache.go:92-94`) — so **10 minutes** for user entries. They are also invalidated eagerly on delete (`store/user_delete.go:40`).
