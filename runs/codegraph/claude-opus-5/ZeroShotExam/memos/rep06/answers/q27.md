How it fits together:

- `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:20-21` — `//go:embed dist/*` embeds `server/router/frontend/dist` (the Vite output) into `var embeddedFiles embed.FS`.
- `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:115-121` — `getFileSystem("dist")` does `fs.Sub(embeddedFiles, "dist")` to get the served root.
