**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Migrations are shipped as embedded `.sql` files under `store/migration/`, with one subtree per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/0.30/`)
- `store/migration/mysql/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`

They're embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45` and selected at runtime by driver through `getMigrationBasePath()` (`store/migrator.go:141`, `:300`). The same three dialects are enumerated in code as `DialectSQLite`, `DialectMySQL`, `DialectPostgres` in `internal/filter/schema.go:14-16`.
