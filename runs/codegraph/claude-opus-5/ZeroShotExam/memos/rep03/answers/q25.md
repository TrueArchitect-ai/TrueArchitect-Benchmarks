**Mechanism (one sentence):** `Engine.Compile` compiles the filter string against a memo-specific CEL environment declared in `internal/filter/schema.go` (only whitelisted identifiers like `content`, `tags`, `visibility`, `creator_id`, `created_ts`, `has_task_list` are allowed, with `now`/`duration()`/`timestamp()` folded to epoch constants at compile time), `buildCondition` in `internal/filter/parser.go:19` normalizes the CEL AST into the dialect-agnostic IR of `internal/filter/ir.go`, and `renderer.Render` in `internal/filter/render.go:32` walks that IR emitting a parameterized SQL fragment plus args for the target `DialectName` (`sqlite`/`mysql`/`postgres`), switching on dialect for JSON access, timestamp extraction, `LIKE`/`ILIKE`, boolean coercion, and placeholders (`?` vs `$n` with an offset — `render.go:842`).

**Wiring:** `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`) validates `request.Filter` via `validateFilter` (`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336`, which compiles it against the driver's dialect) and appends it to `store.FindMemo.Filters`; each store driver then calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` + `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect and splices the fragment into the `WHERE` clause — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-63`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-71`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-60`.
