Notes on where review effort concentrates:
- `store/driver.go:29-30` is the interface both `CreateMemo`/`ListMemos` flow through — any field change must land in all three `store/db/*/memo.go` implementations (INSERT column lists, SELECT scan targets, RETURNING clauses) or one dialect silently diverges.
- `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go` is the store→proto boundary; a new field is invisible to the API until it's mapped there.
- The `Payload *storepb.MemoPayload` field (`store/memo.go:51`) is regenerated from `proto/gen/store/memo.pb.go`, so payload-shaped changes belong in the proto, not the Go struct.
- Web/TypeScript `Memo` (`web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts`) is the *API* message, not `store.Memo` — it only needs review if the change propagates through the converter into the proto.
