**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations are shipped under `store/migration/`, one subdirectory per dialect:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.28/00__user_identity.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`)

This matches the driver layout in `store/db/` (`store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`).
