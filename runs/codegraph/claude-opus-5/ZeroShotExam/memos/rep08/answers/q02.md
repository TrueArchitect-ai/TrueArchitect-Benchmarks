**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migrations live in `store/migration/`, with one subtree per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (same layout)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (same layout)

These correspond to the three driver implementations under `store/db/` — `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, selected in `store/db/db.go`.
