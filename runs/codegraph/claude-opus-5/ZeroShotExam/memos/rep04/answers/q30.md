**Where they converge**

The first three are thin wrappers over `(*APIV1Service).dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads that user's webhooks, and builds a payload per hook. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` bypasses that helper (it targets the *related* memo owner's webhooks rather than the comment creator's) and builds its payload inline.

All four then converge on `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto the buffered `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, dropping with a warning when full). Four goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`) drain that queue and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the actual HTTP delivery, including optional HMAC-SHA256 Standard Webhooks signing and the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.
