**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subtree per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (each containing versioned dirs like `0.17/`…`0.30/` plus a `LATEST.sql`). The whole tree is embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`, and the matching driver implementations sit in `store/db/{mysql,postgres,sqlite}`.

Note: seed data (`store/seed/`) is shipped for SQLite only.
