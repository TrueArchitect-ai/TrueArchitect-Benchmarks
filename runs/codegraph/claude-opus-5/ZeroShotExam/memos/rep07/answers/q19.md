MemoService is exposed over two wire protocols, both fronted by the same `APIV1Service` implementation and wired up in `RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:65`):

**gRPC-Gateway REST/HTTP-JSON — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**
`v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` registers MemoService on the grpc-gateway ServeMux (in-process, no gRPC dialing), and that mux is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:126-129`). The generated route table lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

**Connect RPC (connectrpc, used by the browser client in place of grpc-web) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`**
`wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` registers it on a `http.ServeMux`; the per-method Connect→gRPC wrapper methods (`CreateMemo`, `ListMemos`, …) are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304-464`. The mux is created and mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:139-144`, and the generated handler constructor is in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/`.

One caveat on naming: despite the comment at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:19` ("Support both native gRPC and Connect protocols"), there is no native gRPC server — `RegisterMemoServiceServer` is never called anywhere outside generated code. The two live protocols are REST/HTTP-JSON via grpc-gateway and Connect.
