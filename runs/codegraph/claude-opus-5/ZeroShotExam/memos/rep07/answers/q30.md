**Convergence for delivery**

- The three plain lifecycle events (created/updated/deleted) are thin wrappers over the shared helper `(*APIV1Service).dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks`, builds a payload with `convertMemoToWebhookPayload`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync`. The comment-created dispatcher skips that helper (it targets the *related* memo's owner, not the comment creator's) but ends at the same call.
- All four therefore converge on **`webhook.PostAsync`** — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` — which enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (dropping with a warning if full). Four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`) drain the queue and perform actual HTTP delivery in **`webhook.Post`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which marshals the payload, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC headers when a signing secret is set, and POSTs via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.

Callers of the dispatchers: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` from `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`, `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
