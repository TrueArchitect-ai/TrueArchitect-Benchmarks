**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subtree per dialect:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

They're embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the dialect subdirectory is selected by the configured driver. The same three-dialect split appears in the filter layer as well (`internal/filter/schema.go`, e.g. `DialectSQLite`).
