**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project ships one migration tree per dialect under `store/migration/`:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

These correspond to the three driver implementations in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`, selected in `store/db/db.go`.
