**APIV1Service.SignIn — server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go:51**

That's where the actual sign-in logic lives: it branches on `request.GetPasswordCredentials()` (bcrypt compare against the stored hash, plus a `DisallowPasswordAuth` check) vs. `request.GetSsoCredentials()` (`resolveSSOIdentity` → `resolveSSOUser`), rejects archived users, then calls `s.doSignIn` to mint the access token and set the refresh-token cookie.

The transport-layer entry point that wraps it is `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:78`, which just injects a header carrier (so cookies can be set) and delegates to `s.APIV1Service.SignIn`. Generated plumbing lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/auth_service.connect.go` and `proto/gen/api/v1/auth_service_grpc.pb.go`.
