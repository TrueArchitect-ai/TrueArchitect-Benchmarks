**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migration files live in `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versioned dirs `0.19`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versioned dirs `0.10`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`

This matches the driver implementations under `store/db/` (`mysql/`, `postgres/`, `sqlite/`, wired up in `store/db/db.go`).
