**s3presign Runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — once immediately at startup, then every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, runner.go:27) — wired at `server/server.go:162-171`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which `Server.Start` calls at `server/server.go:129`.**

How it works:

- `Runner.Run` (runner.go:29) drives a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` loop calling `RunOnce` → `CheckAndPresign` (runner.go:47).
- `CheckAndPresign` pages through attachments with `StorageType = AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100 (runner.go:55-65), and for each one with an `S3Object` payload re-presigns the object key via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` (runner.go:107), updating `LastPresignedTime`.
- The refresh is skipped while the existing URL still has slack: presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration, and an attachment is only re-signed once its `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (runner.go:85-90). The 12-hour tick is therefore just the polling cadence, not the URL lifetime.
- Startup does one synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` before launching the goroutine (server.go:163), so stale links get fixed at boot rather than waiting 12 hours. Shutdown is handled through the runner's dedicated cancel func (server.go:156-159) and the wait group (server.go:184-187).
