**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.**

The cron scheduler at `internal/scheduler/` is a standalone, unused library — it has no registered jobs at all. `Scheduler.Register` (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go:70`) is only ever called from the package's own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`); nothing under `server/`, `cmd/`, or `store/` imports `internal/scheduler`, so no job — database-cleanup or otherwise — is ever scheduled in the running application.

What likely prompted the question: two illustrative "cleanup" jobs using `0 2 * * *` (2 AM daily), neither of which touches a database:

- `internal/scheduler/doc.go:14-20` — package doc example named `daily-cleanup`, schedule `"0 2 * * *"`, with a `// Your cleanup logic here` placeholder handler.
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-107` — `Example_multipleJobs` registers a job named `cleanup` on `"0 2 * * *"` that prints `"Cleaning up old data..."`. That same example also defines a health check (`*/5 * * * *`) and a weekly backup (`0 1 * * 0`).

The only real periodic cleanup in the codebase is unrelated to cron: the in-memory cache's `cleanupLoop` (`store/cache/cache.go:200`), which runs on a `time.Ticker` at `Config.CleanupInterval`, not a cron schedule.
