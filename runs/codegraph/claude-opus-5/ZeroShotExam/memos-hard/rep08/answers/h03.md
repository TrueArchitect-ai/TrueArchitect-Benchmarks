**`internal/idp`; `internal/idp/oauth2`**

- **Abstraction package:** `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp` — `/bench/home/work/internal/idp/idp.go` defines the provider-agnostic contract, the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct (`Identifier`, `DisplayName`, `Email`, `AvatarURL`) that every provider must produce.
- **Implementation package:** `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp/oauth2` — `/bench/home/work/internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` ("Package oauth2 implements the OAuth2 identity provider integration") provides `IdentityProvider` backed by `storepb.OAuth2Config`, with `NewIdentityProvider`, `ExchangeToken` (including PKCE `code_verifier` support), and user-info retrieval mapped through the configured `FieldMapping`.

OAuth2 is the only concrete protocol that ships: the store enum in `/bench/home/work/proto/gen/store/idp.pb.go:24-38` has just `TYPE_UNSPECIFIED` and `OAUTH2`, and the sole consumer, `/bench/home/work/server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go:12-13`, imports exactly those two packages.
