**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — once at startup, then a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171`, itself called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.**

Details:

- **What it does** — `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, and for each S3 object whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry) it re-issues a presigned GET URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and writes it back as the attachment's `Reference`, stamping a new `LastPresignedTime`. That's what keeps the stored external links from expiring.
- **Schedule** — `Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29`) loops on a `time.Ticker` of 12 hours until its context is cancelled. Additionally `RunOnce(ctx)` is invoked synchronously at startup (`server/server.go:163`) before the goroutine starts, so a refresh happens immediately on boot.
- **Wiring/shutdown** — the runner goroutine gets its own cancellable context (`server/server.go:156-159`) tracked in `s.backgroundRunnerCancels` and `s.backgroundRunnerWG`, cancelled by `stopBackgroundRunners` during `Server.Shutdown` (`server/server.go:140-143`).
