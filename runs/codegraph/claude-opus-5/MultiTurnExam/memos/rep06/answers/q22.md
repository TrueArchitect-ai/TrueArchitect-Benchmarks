**Signing** — Standard Webhooks-style **HMAC-SHA256**: the key is derived from the hook's signing secret (`resolveSigningKey`), the MAC is computed over `msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body`, base64-encoded, and sent as headers `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, and `webhook-signature: v1,<sig>` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:133-150`). Signing is skipped when the hook has no secret.
