**Started by the server** (`Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:153-174`):

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — one immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a goroutine running `Run(s3Context)` on a `time.Ticker` with `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-39`); it re-presigns expiring S3 attachment URLs. Cancelled via the stored `context.CancelFunc` on shutdown (`server/server.go:176-182`).

That is the only long-running background worker. Two other goroutine-ish pieces for completeness:
- **HTTP server goroutine** — `s.httpServer.Serve(listener)` (`server/server.go:124`), not a scheduled worker.
- **SSE hub** (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`, constructed at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`) — event-driven, no timer; it fans out per-connection from `Broadcast` calls.

**Not a background runner:** `server/runner/memopayload` lives under `server/runner/` but has no `Run` loop — `RebuildMemoPayload` is called inline from the memo handlers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and `:447`).

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** `internal/scheduler` — a full cron scheduler (5/6-field cron parsing, timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown; `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `doc.go`) that **nothing in the server imports**. Its only importers are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`, `example_test.go`). The s3presign runner uses a plain `time.Ticker` instead.
