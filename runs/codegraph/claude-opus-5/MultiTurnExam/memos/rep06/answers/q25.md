**Engine + path** — Google **CEL** (`github.com/google/cel-go`), wrapped by the project's own `internal/filter` package: `internal/filter/engine.go` (`Engine`, `Compile` — builds a `cel.Env` from a `Schema` and compiles the expression), `internal/filter/parser.go` (walks the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` tree, folding `now` to a frozen instant), `internal/filter/ir.go` (condition types), `internal/filter/schema.go` (field/column definitions and the `DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres` names), and `internal/filter/render.go` (renders the tree to SQL).

**Mechanism** — The filter string is compiled by CEL into an AST, converted to a dialect-neutral condition tree, then rendered per dialect by `internal/filter/render.go` (dialect-specific column/JSON expressions and placeholder style — `?` vs `$n` via `PlaceholderOffset`) into a parameterized `WHERE` fragment plus args, which each driver splices into its query via `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.DialectSQLite, &where, &args)` — see `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` and the postgres/mysql equivalents in `store/db/postgres/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`.

Background notes on the design live in `internal/filter/README.md` and `internal/filter/MAINTENANCE.md`.
