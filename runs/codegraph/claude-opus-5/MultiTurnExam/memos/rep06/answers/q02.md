**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each containing versioned `LATEST.sql` plus `0.x/` upgrade scripts). This matches the driver set in `store/db/` and the dialect constants in `internal/filter/schema.go`.
