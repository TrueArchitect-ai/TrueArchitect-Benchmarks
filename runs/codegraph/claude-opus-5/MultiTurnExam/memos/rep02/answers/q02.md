MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

Migrations live in `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, `store/migration/sqlite/` (each holding versioned subdirs plus a `LATEST.sql`). These match the driver implementations under `store/db/` and the `DialectSQLite`/`Dialect*` constants in `internal/filter/schema.go`.
