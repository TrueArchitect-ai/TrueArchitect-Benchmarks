`web/src/connect.ts`

All eight clients are created there under the "Transport & Service Clients" section (`web/src/connect.ts:184-203`) from a shared `createConnectTransport` that carries the auth/token-refresh interceptor: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`. A separate `refreshAuthClient` (line 72) uses an interceptor-free transport to avoid recursion during token refresh.
