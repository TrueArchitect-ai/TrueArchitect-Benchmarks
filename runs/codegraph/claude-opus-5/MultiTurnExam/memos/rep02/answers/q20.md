**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) — an `text/event-stream` endpoint at `GET /api/v1/sse`, with a 30s heartbeat, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25` (wired from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (:56) with `NewSSEHub` (:63), `Subscribe` (:71), `Unsubscribe` (:89), `Broadcast` (:115), and per-client visibility filtering in `SSEClient.canReceive` (:134). Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (:15-20).
