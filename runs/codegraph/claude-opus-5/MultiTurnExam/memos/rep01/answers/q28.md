**Runners the server actually starts** — all from `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`), called during `Server.Start`:

- **s3presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)** — one immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a goroutine running `Run(s3Context)`, a `time.Ticker` loop on `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-39`); stopped by its own `context.CancelFunc` collected in `backgroundRunnerCancels`.

That is the only entry in `startBackgroundRunners`. Two other long-lived goroutine sets exist but are not "runners" registered there:

- **Webhook async dispatch pool (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`)** — 4 goroutines started in `init()`, event-driven off the buffered `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128) fed by `webhook.PostAsync`; no timer.
- **SSE hub (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`)** — created with the API service (`server/server.go:77`), event-driven per `Broadcast` call and per subscribed client connection; closed at shutdown (`server/server.go:205-206`).

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** `internal/scheduler/` — a self-contained, zero-dependency cron scheduler (5/6-field cron expressions, timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown; `doc.go`, `scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`) — **is not referenced anywhere outside its own package and tests.** Nothing in `server/` or `cmd/` imports it; the only periodic work in the running server is the 12-hour `time.Ticker` in the s3presign runner.

Also note `server/runner/memopayload/` is not a background runner despite its location: `RebuildMemoPayload` is called synchronously from the memo handlers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`, `:447`).
