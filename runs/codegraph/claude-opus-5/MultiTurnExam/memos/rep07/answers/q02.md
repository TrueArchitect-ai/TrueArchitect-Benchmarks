q02.
mysql, postgres, sqlite

Migration SQL lives under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (each containing versioned subdirs plus a `LATEST.sql`). The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.
