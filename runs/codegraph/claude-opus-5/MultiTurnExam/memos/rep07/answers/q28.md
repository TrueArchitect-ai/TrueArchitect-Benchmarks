**Runners the server actually starts** (all from `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:153-174`, invoked by `Server.Start` at line 129):

- **s3presign runner** — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`: one immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a goroutine running `Run(s3Context)` which is driven by a `time.Ticker` at a fixed 12-hour interval (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-40`). Cancelled via its own `context.CancelFunc` on shutdown (`server/server.go:156-159`, `:176`).

That is the only entry in `startBackgroundRunners`. Other long-lived goroutines the process starts, for completeness:

- **HTTP serve loop** — `go s.httpServer.Serve(listener)` (`server/server.go:124`), event-driven by requests.
- **Webhook delivery workers** — 4 goroutines draining a 128-slot channel, started from `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`; triggered per queued `PostAsync` call, not scheduled.
- **Async email workers** — 2 goroutines over a 128-slot queue, `init()` in `internal/email/email.go:16-30`; also demand-driven.
- **SSE hub** — no polling loop; `SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) fans out on `Broadcast` and holds one goroutine per connected client stream.
- Fire-and-forget one-shots (not runners): PAT last-used update (`server/auth/authenticator.go:180`).

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** `internal/scheduler` — a full cron-expression scheduler (`scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`, plus docs in `doc.go` and its own tests) is present but **not referenced anywhere outside its own package**; nothing in `server/` or `bin/` imports it, so no job is ever registered or started.

Also note `server/runner/memopayload` is not run as a background runner — it exposes `RunOnce` for a full payload rebuild, but the server only calls its `RebuildMemoPayload` helper inline from the memo create/update handlers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and `:447`).
