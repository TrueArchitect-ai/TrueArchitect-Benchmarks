q02.
**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` has exactly three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/` (each with per-version folders of `.sql` files, plus `LATEST__SCHEMA.sql`-style base schemas).
- These are embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` at `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the per-dialect path is selected by `getMigrationBasePath()` (used at `store/migrator.go:141`).
- Matching driver implementations exist under `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, dispatched by the `switch profile.Driver` in `store/db/db.go:18-23`.
