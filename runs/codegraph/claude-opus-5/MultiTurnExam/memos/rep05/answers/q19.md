How each side wires up:

- **ConnectRPC** — `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` on the `http.ServeMux` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`. `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21-27`), and the per-RPC adapters in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at :306) unwrap `connect.Request` and delegate to the same `APIV1Service` methods; `convertGRPCError` (`connect_handler.go:63`) maps gRPC status codes to Connect codes.
- **gRPC-Gateway** — `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` registers the REST routes directly against `APIV1Service` (in-process, no network hop), on a `runtime.NewServeMux` carrying the auth middleware; the mux is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` (`v1.go:129`). The route table itself is generated in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go` from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

Both protocols share one implementation — `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
