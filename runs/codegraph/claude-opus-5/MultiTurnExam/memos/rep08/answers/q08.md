**Driver — store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` is defined at `store/driver.go:10` ("It contains all methods that store database driver should implement") and covers `GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`, plus the per-model CRUD methods. The three backends implement it as `DB` structs in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, each returned as a `store.Driver` from its `NewDB` constructor.
