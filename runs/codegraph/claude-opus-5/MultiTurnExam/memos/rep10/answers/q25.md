**Engine:** Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`), wrapped by the in-repo filter engine at `internal/filter/` — `Engine`/`Compile` in `internal/filter/engine.go:14,48`, CEL env + field schema in `internal/filter/schema.go`, AST→IR in `internal/filter/parser.go` and `internal/filter/ir.go`, SQL rendering in `internal/filter/render.go`, entry helper `AppendConditions` in `internal/filter/helpers.go:9`.

**Mechanism:** The filter string is compiled by CEL into an AST, lowered into a dialect-agnostic condition tree, and then rendered per dialect by `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go:24-32`), which each `ListMemos` driver invokes with its own `DialectName` — `filter.DialectSQLite` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:61`), `filter.DialectPostgres` (`store/db/postgres/memo.go:58`), `filter.DialectMySQL` (`store/db/mysql/memo.go:69`) — appending the generated `WHERE` fragments and bound args (with `?` vs `$n` placeholder offsets, and dialect-specific JSON/LIKE/length expressions) to the query.

Design notes live in `internal/filter/README.md` and `internal/filter/MAINTENANCE.md`.
