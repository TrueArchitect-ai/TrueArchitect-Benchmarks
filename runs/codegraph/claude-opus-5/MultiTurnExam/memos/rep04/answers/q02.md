MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

The migrations live in `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (each containing versioned subdirectories plus a `LATEST.sql`). This matches the three driver implementations under `store/db/` and the `DialectSQLite`/dialect constants in `internal/filter/schema.go`.
