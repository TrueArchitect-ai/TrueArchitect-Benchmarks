Both are wired up in `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64`):

- **gRPC-Gateway**: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:109` registers against the runtime mux (generated glue in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`), mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` (`v1.go:128`). This is the path the `google.api.http` annotations (e.g. `POST /api/v1/memos`) serve.
- **Connect**: `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` inside `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (generated glue in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`), mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` (`v1.go:144`). The per-RPC wrappers that unwrap `connect.Request` and delegate to `APIV1Service` live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`.

There is no native gRPC listener — both protocols share the same `APIV1Service` implementation over HTTP.
