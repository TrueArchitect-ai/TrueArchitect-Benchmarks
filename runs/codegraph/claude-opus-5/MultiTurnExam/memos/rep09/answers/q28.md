Details:

- **s3presign** is the only entry in `startBackgroundRunners`. `NewRunner(s.Store)` → `RunOnce(ctx)` inline, then `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG`; the loop ticks on `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26`) and exits on `ctx.Done()`. It re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before expiry.
- **webhook pool** isn't wired through `startBackgroundRunners` — four consumer goroutines are spawned in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`, draining the 128-slot `asyncPostQueue` that `PostAsync` feeds (`:184`). Overflow is dropped with a warning, not queued.
- **SSE heartbeat** is per-request rather than a global worker, started inside `handleSSE`.

**Unused scheduling infrastructure — two items:**

1. **`internal/scheduler/`** is a complete, GitHub-Actions-style cron scheduler (5/6-field cron parsing, timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown — `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, documented in `doc.go`). **Nothing in the server imports it** — the only importers are its own `example_test.go` and `integration_test.go`. No cron jobs are registered anywhere in the running binary.
