MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subtree per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` — each containing versioned directories (0.17 … 0.30) plus a `LATEST.sql`. The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.
