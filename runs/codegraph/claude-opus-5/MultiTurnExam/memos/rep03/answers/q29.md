**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — `authInterceptor` (:155) is installed on the shared Connect transport (:187) used by every service client. Supporting state lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts` (in-memory/stored access token, `isTokenExpired`, `setAccessToken`), and the redirect-on-failure helper in `web/src/utils/auth-redirect.ts`.

**Token kinds it manages:**
- **Access token (JWT, "V2")** — sent as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`; refreshed preemptively when near expiry (`getRequestToken`, :123) and reactively once on a `Code.Unauthenticated` response, guarded by the `X-Retry` header to prevent loops (:155-176).
- **Refresh token** — an HttpOnly cookie, never read by JS; carried automatically because every request uses `credentials: "include"` (:56-61). Refreshes go through `AuthService.RefreshToken` on a separate interceptor-free transport (`refreshTransport`/`refreshAuthClient`, :64-72), deduplicated by `tokenRefreshManager` (:30-50).
- **Personal Access Tokens (PAT)** are also accepted as `Bearer` by the backend, but they're for programmatic clients, not issued by this interceptor.

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — `Authenticator` in `server/auth/authenticator.go` (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` :41, `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` :61, `AuthenticateByPAT` :102, `Authenticate` :212), with JWT parsing/minting in `server/auth/token.go`, header/cookie extraction in `server/auth/extract.go`, and context plumbing in `server/auth/context.go`. It's invoked from the Connect interceptors (`server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`), the grpc-gateway middleware (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go`), the per-method authorization table in `server/router/api/v1/authz.go`, and the SSE handler (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:36`).
