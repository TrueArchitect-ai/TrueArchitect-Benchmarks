**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job, and no cron job of any kind is registered in this codebase — so there is no scheduled time to report.**

Two separate things are wrong with the premise:

**1. No database-cleanup job exists.** Grepping for `cleanup`, `vacuum`, `purge`, and `prune` across `server/`, `store/`, and `cmd/` turns up only inline, request-triggered cleanup — attachment-storage cleanup during user deletion (`server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:421-450`, `store/attachment.go:171`), a best-effort rollback of a provisional SSO user row (`server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go:76`), and memo-relation/attachment cleanup on memo delete (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:579`). The only periodic "cleanup" in the process is the in-memory cache's expiry sweep every 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go:60`, `cleanupLoop` at line 199) — that's RAM, not the database.

**2. The cron scheduler is never used in production.** `internal/scheduler/` is a complete, well-tested cron package — `Job` with a 5-field `Schedule` and IANA `Timezone` (`internal/scheduler/job.go:14-36`), `ParseCronExpression` (`internal/scheduler/parser.go`), `Scheduler.Register`/`Start`/`Stop` (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`), plus Recovery/Logging/Timeout middleware (`internal/scheduler/middleware.go`). But grepping the import path `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` across the repo returns hits in only two files, both of them the package's own tests: `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13` and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`. Nothing in `server/`, `cmd/`, or `store/` imports it, and `scheduler.New(...)` / `Register(...)` is never called outside those tests. The cron examples you may have seen — `"0 0 * * *"` (daily at midnight), `"0 * * * *"` (hourly) — are doc-comment illustrations at `internal/scheduler/job.go:21-22`, not registered schedules.

**What actually runs on a timer:** exactly one background job, the S3 presign runner — a plain `time.Ticker` at 12-hour intervals, not a cron expression, started from `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:162-171`, ticker at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,30`). That's the whole of the server's scheduled work.
