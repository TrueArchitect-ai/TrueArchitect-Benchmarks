Enforcement falls into three kinds: per-object read checks, query-level visibility filters, and delivery/notification filters. Every distinct site:

**Per-object read checks (gRPC/Connect API)**
- `checkMemoReadAccess` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39` — the canonical check (also gates archived memos to their creator); used by `GetMemo` and friends
- `checkAttachmentPermission` — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:655` (visibility branches at `:677` and `:699`) — resolves the parent memo's visibility before serving attachment bytes, with a share-token fallback at `:684`
- `ListMemoAttachments` guard — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214-222`
- Attachment-service memo guard — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:445-451`
- Reaction read/write guards — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-38` and `:83`
- `CreateMemoComment` guard — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41`
- Share-link path — `getActiveMemoShare` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:199` — the deliberate bypass: a valid, non-expired token grants read access regardless of visibility (`isMemoShareExpired`, `:194`)

**Query-level filtering (visibility applied in the SQL predicate)**
- `ListMemos` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:217-226` — anonymous → `PUBLIC` only; authenticated → `creator_id == me || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`
- User stats — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:84-90` and `:218-220`
- RSS feeds — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` and `:141` — `PUBLIC` only
- Sitemap — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150` — `PUBLIC` only

**Push/notification filtering**
- SSE fan-out — `(*SSEClient).canReceive` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` — private events only to the creator or an admin
- Email notification gating — `server/notification/email.go:313-316` and `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:309-312`
- Mention-notification gating — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62`, `:67`, `:84`

Two things worth flagging: the per-object checks and the list filters are independent implementations of the same policy (a change to one doesn't propagate to the other), and the private-instance switch `Profile.AllowAnonymous()` (`internal/profile/profile.go:46`) layers on top of the fileserver check at `fileserver.go:677` — a `PUBLIC` memo's attachment is still withheld from anonymous callers when no `InstanceURL` is configured.
