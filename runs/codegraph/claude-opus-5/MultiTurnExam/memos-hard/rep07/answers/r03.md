**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job, and the cron scheduler isn't wired into the running server at all — so there's no scheduled time to report.**

Two separate corrections:

1. **No database-cleanup job exists.** Searching for `cleanup`/`vacuum`/`purge`/`prune` across `server/`, `store/`, and `cmd/` turns up nothing resembling a periodic DB maintenance task. The only `cleanup` machinery is the in-memory cache's expiry sweep — `Cache.cleanupLoop`, driven by a ticker at `store/cache/cache.go:199-211` on a 5-minute `CleanupInterval` (`store/store.go:42`) — which evicts expired map entries, not database rows. Row deletion in this codebase is all synchronous and request-driven (e.g. the `DeleteUser` transaction in `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:29`).

2. **The cron scheduler has no production callers.** `internal/scheduler` is a full cron implementation — `Scheduler` (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go:12`), `Job` (`internal/scheduler/job.go:14`), `ParseCronExpression` (`internal/scheduler/parser.go`), plus `Recovery`/`Logging`/`Timeout` middleware — but `grep -rn "usememos/memos/internal/scheduler"` shows it's imported by exactly two files, both its own tests: `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13` and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`. Nothing in `server/`, `store/`, or `cmd/` registers a job with it. The `"0 9 * * *"` expression you may have seen is test data (`internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:183`), not a configured job.

What actually runs periodically in the server is a single background task, and it uses a plain ticker rather than cron: the S3 presign runner, every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `:29-41`), started at `server/server.go:162-171`.
