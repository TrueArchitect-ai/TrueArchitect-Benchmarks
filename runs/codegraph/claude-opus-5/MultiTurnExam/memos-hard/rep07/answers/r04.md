**Premise correction: neither. There is no message broker anywhere in this repository — no NATS, no RabbitMQ, no AMQP or Kafka client in `go.mod`, and no queue declaration to point at.** A case-insensitive search for `nats|rabbitmq|amqp|kafka|streadway` across the whole tree returns a single file, `web/src/locales/ca.json`, and those are false positives — Catalan words containing the substring "nats" (e.g. "elimi**nats**", "seleccio**nats**").

**Actual mechanism — an in-process buffered Go channel with a fixed worker pool, all in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:**

- **The "queue"** is a package-level channel with a hard capacity of 128: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`). It is declared in the same `var` block as the HTTP client, not by any broker.
- **The "workers"** are four goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Each ranges over the channel and calls `Post(payload)`, logging a warning on failure.
- **Enqueue** happens in `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) via a non-blocking `select` with a `default` branch: when the buffer is full the event is **dropped** with a warning, not retried or persisted.
- **Producers** are the memo webhook dispatchers, which call `PostAsync` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`.
- **Delivery** is the plain `net/http` POST in `Post` — `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`, with Standard Webhooks HMAC headers and the SSRF-guarded dialer described earlier.

Practical consequences of it being a channel rather than a broker: the queue is per-process and in-memory, so it does not survive a restart, does not fan out across replicas, has no acknowledgement or redelivery, and offers no retry — a failed POST is logged and discarded (`webhook.go:46-51`).
