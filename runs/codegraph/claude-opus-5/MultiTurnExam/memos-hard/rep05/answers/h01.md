- **What it does** — `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, and for each one whose payload has an `S3Object`, regenerates a presigned GET URL via `s3.NewClient(...).PresignGetObject(ctx, key)` and writes it back as the attachment's `Reference` along with a fresh `LastPresignedTime`.
