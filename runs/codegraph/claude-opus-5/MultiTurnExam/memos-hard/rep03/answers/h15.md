Distinct enforcement sites (each independently decides who may see memo content or memo-derived data):

- `APIV1Service.checkMemoReadAccess` — shared per-memo gate: archived→creator only; non-public requires auth; private requires creator — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-67` (called from `GetMemo` `memo_service.go:356`, parent-memo check `memo_service.go:367`, and `ListMemoComments` `memo_service_comments.go:148`)
- `APIV1Service.ListMemos` — query-level scoping: anonymous → `VisibilityList = [PUBLIC]`; self → CEL filter `creator_id == N || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`; other creator → `[PUBLIC, PROTECTED]` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-227`
- `APIV1Service.ListMemoReactions` — its own inline copy of the visibility check (adds `isSuperUser` bypass) — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:29-41`
- `APIV1Service.ListMemoAttachments` — another inline copy of the same check — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:213-225`
- `APIV1Service.ListAllUserStats` / `GetUserStats` — stats scoped by viewer: `[PUBLIC]` anonymous, `[PUBLIC, PROTECTED]` for other users — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:84,90` and `:218,220`
- `APIV1Service.GetMemoByShare` / `getActiveMemoShare` — share-token bypass: a valid, non-expired share grants access regardless of visibility — `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:153,199-206` (expiry check `:195`)
- `FileServerService.checkAttachmentPermission` — HTTP attachment serving: unlinked→creator/admin; public+`AllowAnonymous()`→open; else share-token fallback; else authenticated, and private→creator/admin — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650-703`
- `SSEClient.canReceive` — live-event fan-out filter: private events only to creator or admin; public/protected/unset to all — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-143` (event visibility populated at `sse_event_helpers.go:29-31`)
- `RSSService.GetExploreRSS` / `GetUserRSS` — feeds hard-restricted to `[PUBLIC]` — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` and `:141`
- `FrontendService.getSitemapXML` — sitemap lists `[PUBLIC]` memos only — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150`

Two observations worth flagging: the checks in `checkMemoReadAccess`, `ListMemoReactions`, and `ListMemoAttachments` are three separate copies of the same logic that have already drifted — the latter two allow an `isSuperUser` bypass for private memos while `checkMemoReadAccess` does not. And `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:84` gates mention *notifications* on `memo.Visibility != store.Private`, which is visibility-driven behavior but a notification rule rather than a read-access gate, so I've listed it separately here rather than above.
