**Premise correction: neither — there is no message broker in this codebase at all.** Grepping the Go sources, `go.mod`, and `go.sum` for `nats`, `rabbitmq`, `amqp`, `kafka`, and `streadway` returns zero matches. There is no external queue, no exchange, and no queue declaration anywhere.

**Actual mechanism — an in-process buffered Go channel:**

- **The "queue"** is a package-level channel with a fixed capacity of 128, declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:38`:
  `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`
- **The "workers"** are four goroutines started in the package's `init()` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:41-54`. Each ranges over `asyncPostQueue` and calls `Post` for every payload, logging a warning on failure. They live for the process lifetime; there's no shutdown handshake.
- **Enqueue** happens in `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) via a non-blocking `select` with a `default` branch: if the buffer is full the payload is **dropped** and only a warning is logged (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:192-194`). A nil payload is likewise dropped at `:185-188`.
- **Producers** are the memo webhook dispatchers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:41,69`, reached from `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, and `SetMemoRelations`.
- **Delivery** is `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which signs the body with HMAC-SHA256 Standard Webhooks headers and POSTs it via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`.

Consequences worth knowing, since they're the practical difference from a broker: no durability (a restart loses queued deliveries), no retry or dead-lettering (a failed `Post` is logged and discarded), no cross-instance sharing, and silent shedding under burst load once 128 payloads are outstanding.
