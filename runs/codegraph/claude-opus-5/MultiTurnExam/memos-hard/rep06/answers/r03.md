**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job — and the cron scheduler that exists is never used by the server, so nothing is scheduled at any time.**

Two separate things are wrong with the premise:

1. **No database-cleanup job exists.** Grepping for cleanup/vacuum/prune/purge across the Go backend turns up only unrelated things: the in-memory cache's expiry loop (`store/cache/cache.go:199-218`, driven by a 5-minute `CleanupInterval`, not cron), attachment/storage cleanup performed inline during user or memo deletion (`store/attachment.go:171`, `server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:421-447`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:579`), and a test teardown helper (`server/router/api/v1/test/test_helper.go:65`). No job deletes stale rows, compacts the DB, or runs on a nightly schedule.

2. **The cron scheduler is dead code.** `internal/scheduler` is a complete implementation — `Scheduler` (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go:12`), `New` (`:54`), `Register` (`:70`), 5-field cron parsing in `ParseCronExpression` (`internal/scheduler/parser.go:30`), plus timezone, recovery, logging, and timeout middleware — but nothing outside the package instantiates it. Every `scheduler.New(...)` / `s.Register(...)` reference lives in `internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`, or the doc comments in `internal/scheduler/doc.go`. The `server/` tree never imports the package.

**What actually runs on a schedule:** exactly one background task, and it's a plain `time.Ticker`, not cron — the S3 presign refresher. `s3presign.Runner.Run` ticks every 12 hours (`runnerInterval` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), wired up in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` with an immediate `RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`. Because it's interval-based from process start, it has no fixed wall-clock time — it fires 12 and 24 hours after whenever the server last started.
