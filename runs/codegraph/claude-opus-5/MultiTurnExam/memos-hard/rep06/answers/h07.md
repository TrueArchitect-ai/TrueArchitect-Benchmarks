Notes:
- The service is constructed and its routes mounted at server startup: `server/server.go:81-82` (`fileserver.NewFileServerService` / `RegisterRoutes`).
- The image bytes are not a file on disk or in S3 — they live in the `user.avatar_url` column as a data URI, selected at `store/db/sqlite/user.go:152-165` and scanned into `store.User.AvatarURL` (`store/user.go:29`).
- Access control for "another user's" avatar is coarse: on a public instance (`Profile.AllowAnonymous()`) anyone may fetch it; otherwise any authenticated viewer may fetch any user's avatar (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:171-179`). The related gRPC method `UserService/GetUserAvatar` is likewise in the allowlist at `server/router/api/v1/acl_config.go:25`.
