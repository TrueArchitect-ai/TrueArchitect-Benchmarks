The three implementations of the `Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory function that selects between them at startup is:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` to instantiate the appropriate backend via each package's `NewDB` constructor.
