MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108), where `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers MemoService onto the gRPC-Gateway mux, translating HTTP/JSON requests to gRPC calls. The generated gateway code lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

2. **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40), where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` registers the Connect protocol handler. The `ConnectServiceHandler` wrapper (same file, line 21) adapts the gRPC service implementation to Connect's request/response types, with the generated Connect service definition in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`. The Connect handlers are mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `v1.go` (line 144).
