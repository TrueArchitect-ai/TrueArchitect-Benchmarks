The `ShortcutService` defines 5 RPCs in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`:

1. **ListShortcuts**
2. **GetShortcut**
3. **CreateShortcut**
4. **UpdateShortcut**
5. **DeleteShortcut**

All five are implemented in the single backend file: **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** (on the `APIV1Service` struct, which embeds `UnimplementedShortcutServiceServer` from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`).
