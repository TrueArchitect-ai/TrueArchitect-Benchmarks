The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. This file sets up the `createConnectTransport` and then uses `createClient` (from `@connectrpc/connect`) to instantiate all service clients: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (lines 192–203).
