**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; **path:** `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string into a CEL AST, then `buildCondition` (in `internal/filter/parser.go`) converts that AST into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`), and `Program.Render` (in `internal/filter/render.go`) walks that condition tree and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (`sqlite`, `postgres`, `mysql`) for every node—choosing the right placeholder style, JSON operators, case-folding functions, and date-extraction expressions per dialect.
