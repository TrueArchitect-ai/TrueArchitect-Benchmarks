MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108), where `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` registers MemoService into the gRPC-Gateway mux. The generated gateway code itself lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

2. **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40), where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` registers MemoService as a Connect handler. The Connect service wrapper methods that delegate to `APIV1Service` are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (line 304+), and the generated Connect definitions are in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`.
