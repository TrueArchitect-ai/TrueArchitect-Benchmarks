The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. This file imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and `createConnectTransport` from `@connectrpc/connect-web`, sets up a transport with an auth interceptor, and then creates and exports all service clients (lines 192–203): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`.
