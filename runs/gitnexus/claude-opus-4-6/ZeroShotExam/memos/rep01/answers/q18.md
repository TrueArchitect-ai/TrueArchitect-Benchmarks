The `Driver` interface is defined in `store/driver.go`. The three implementations (entry files):

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory function that selects between them at startup:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) and calls each implementation's `NewDB` constructor accordingly.
