**Server-Sent Events (SSE)**; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; handler names: **CreateMemo**, **DeleteMemo**, **CreateMemoComment**, **dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects** (called from `UpdateMemo`), **UpsertMemoReaction**, **DeleteMemoReaction**.

Specifically:
- `CreateMemo` broadcasts `memo.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`)
- `DeleteMemo` broadcasts `memo.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`)
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` broadcasts `memo.updated` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`), invoked by `UpdateMemo`
- `CreateMemoComment` broadcasts `memo.comment.created` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` broadcasts `reaction.upserted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` broadcasts `reaction.deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161`)

The `SSEHub` struct manages client connections with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast` methods, and the SSE HTTP endpoint is registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`.
