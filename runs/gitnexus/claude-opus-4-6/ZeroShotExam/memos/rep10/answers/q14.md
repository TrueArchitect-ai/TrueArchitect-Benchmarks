**ShortcutService** defines 5 RPCs (declared in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`):

1. **ListShortcuts**
2. **GetShortcut**
3. **CreateShortcut**
4. **UpdateShortcut**
5. **DeleteShortcut**

All five are implemented by `APIV1Service` in the backend file **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`**.
