**Server-Sent Events (SSE)**; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; handler names: `CreateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`.

The `SSEHub` struct in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` manages client connections and provides a `Broadcast` method that fans out `SSEEvent` messages to all subscribed browsers. The SSE endpoint is served by `handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`. Six memo/reaction handlers call `Broadcast`:

- `CreateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`)
- `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`)
- `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`)
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`)
