Three implementations of the `Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`):

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
3. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`

Factory function: `NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

This function switches on `profile.Driver` to instantiate the appropriate driver via `sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, or `postgres.NewDB`.
