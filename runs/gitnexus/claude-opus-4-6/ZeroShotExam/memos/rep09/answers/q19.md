MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC (via gRPC-Gateway)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108), where `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` registers the service on the gRPC-Gateway mux. The generated gateway code itself lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

2. **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40), where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` registers the service on an HTTP mux. The Connect protocol wrapper methods (delegating to the gRPC implementation) are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (line 304+).
