**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with `cel.Env.Compile()`, converts the resulting AST into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/parser.go:buildCondition`), and then `Program.Render` passes that IR to a `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`) which walks the condition tree and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` at each node.
