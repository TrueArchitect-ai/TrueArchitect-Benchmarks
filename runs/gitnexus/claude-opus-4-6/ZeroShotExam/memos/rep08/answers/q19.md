MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108), where `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` registers MemoService on the gRPC-Gateway mux, translating HTTP/JSON requests into gRPC calls. The generated gateway code lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

2. **Connect (Connect-RPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40), where `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` registers MemoService as a Connect-protocol handler. The Connect service wrapper methods are implemented in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (line 304+).
