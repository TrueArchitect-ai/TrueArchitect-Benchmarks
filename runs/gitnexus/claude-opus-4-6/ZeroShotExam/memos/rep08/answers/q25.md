**CEL** (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with `cel.Env.Compile`, converts the resulting AST into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go` + `internal/filter/parser.go` via `buildCondition`), and then `Program.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) for every condition/expression node.
