**`s3presign.Runner`** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired in `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks each one's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-generates a fresh presigned URL if the existing one will expire within 4 days (presigned URLs last 5 days). It updates the attachment's `Reference` field with the new URL.

It is wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162–171`: `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is created, `RunOnce` is called immediately for an initial pass, then `Run` is launched in a background goroutine on a 12-hour ticker.
