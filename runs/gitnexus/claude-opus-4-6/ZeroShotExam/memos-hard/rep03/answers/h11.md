Of the persistent entity types defined in the `store` package (the data layer), the two referenced from the largest number of packages outside that layer are:

1st: **User** (~7 packages) — referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/rss`.

2nd: **Memo** (~6 packages) — referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, and `server/runner/memopayload`.

Both are defined in `store/user.go` and `store/memo.go` respectively. `User` edges ahead because it is also consumed by the `server/auth` package (for authentication/session resolution) and `server/router/fileserver` (for access control checks), which don't need the `Memo` type.
