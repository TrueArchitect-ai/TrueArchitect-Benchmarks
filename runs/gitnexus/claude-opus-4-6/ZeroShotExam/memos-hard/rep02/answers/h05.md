**Evidence:**

**Explicit Go deletion in two steps** (inside `deleteUserTargetsTx`, within a transaction):

1. `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` — deletes all reactions **on the deleted user's memos** (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)`):
   - `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:380-388`
   - `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:380-388`
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:417-425`

2. `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` — deletes all reactions **made by the deleted user** (`DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?`):
   - `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:400-403`
   - `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:400-403`
   - `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:437-439`
