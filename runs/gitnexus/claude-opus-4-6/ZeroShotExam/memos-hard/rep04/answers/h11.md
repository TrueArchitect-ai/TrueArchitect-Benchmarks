The persistent entity types are defined in the `store/` package (e.g., `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`). Ranking by distinct packages outside `store/` that reference them:

1. **`Memo`** and **`User`** are very close, but **User** edges ahead:

   **1st: User** — referenced from ~7 packages (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`)

   **2nd: Memo** — referenced from ~6 packages (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)
