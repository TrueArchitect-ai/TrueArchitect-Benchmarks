The premise of the question is incorrect. There is no "nightly database-cleanup job" configured in this codebase. The only cleanup-related job appears in example/test files — `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-106` and `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-21` — where a `"cleanup"` / `"daily-cleanup"` job is shown as a documentation example with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily).
