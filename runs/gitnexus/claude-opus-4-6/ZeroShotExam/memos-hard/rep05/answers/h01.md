**S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, line 27) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162` inside the `startBackgroundRunners` method, where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is created and launched as a background goroutine.

The runner iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks each one's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-presigns any whose URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; it refreshes them once 4 days have passed). The fresh presigned URL is written back to the attachment's `Reference` field in the store, keeping the external links from going stale.
