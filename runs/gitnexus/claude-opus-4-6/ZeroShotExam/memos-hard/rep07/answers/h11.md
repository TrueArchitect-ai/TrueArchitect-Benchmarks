The persistent entity types are defined in the `store/` package (the data layer). Counting distinct Go packages outside `store/` that reference each entity type:

1. **Memo** and **User** are tied at the top, each referenced from **6 packages** outside the data layer:
   - **Memo** (6 packages): `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/notification`, `server/runner/memopayload`
   - **User** (6 packages): `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, `server/notification`, `server/auth`

2. **Attachment** comes in third with **4 packages**: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/s3presign`

**1st: Memo; 2nd: User** (both ~6 packages, effectively tied; Attachment follows at ~4).
