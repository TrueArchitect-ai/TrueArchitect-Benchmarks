**CEL (Common Expression Language)** engine at `internal/filter/engine.go` (using the `google/cel-go` library); the parser (`internal/filter/parser.go`) compiles CEL filter expressions into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) translates that IR into SQL with dialect-specific placeholders via `AppendConditions`, which each driver calls with its dialect constant — `filter.DialectPostgres` in `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `filter.DialectSQLite` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, and `filter.DialectMySQL` in `store/db/mysql/memo.go`.
