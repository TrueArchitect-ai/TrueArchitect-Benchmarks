The server starts only **one** background runner:

`s3presign.Runner` — driven by a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (plus an initial `RunOnce` at startup); re-signs expiring S3 presigned URLs for attachments (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`, started in `server/server.go:162–170`)

Unused infrastructure: The `memopayload.Runner` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) exists in the codebase and has a `RunOnce` method, but it is **not started** by the server — it is never referenced in `server/server.go`. Its `RebuildMemoPayload` function is only called inline during memo creation (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`), not as a background worker.
