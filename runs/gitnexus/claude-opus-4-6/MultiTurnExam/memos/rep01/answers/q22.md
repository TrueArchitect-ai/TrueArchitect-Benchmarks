The signature is computed as `HMAC-SHA256(key, msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body)` and sent via `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, and `webhook-signature` (`v1,<base64>`) headers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:132–149`).
