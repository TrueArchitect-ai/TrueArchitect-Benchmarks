Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140`) deletes:

1. **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` records where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) or the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
2. **Attachments** — `attachment` records linked to the memo

Additionally, at the API layer (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:567-577`), **comment memos** (child memos linked via `MemoRelationComment`) are recursively deleted before the parent memo itself, each triggering their own relation and attachment cleanup.
