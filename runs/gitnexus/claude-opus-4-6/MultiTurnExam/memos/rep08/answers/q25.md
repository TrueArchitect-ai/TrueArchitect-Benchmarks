CEL (Common Expression Language) engine — `internal/filter/` (parser in `internal/filter/parser.go`, engine in `internal/filter/engine.go`)

The filter string is parsed and compiled by a CEL environment (`cel-go`) in the `Engine` struct (`internal/filter/engine.go`). It produces a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), which is then rendered into dialect-specific SQL by a renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) that switches on the target dialect (`DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`, `DialectSQLite`). Each dialect's `ListMemos` driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` (`internal/filter/helpers.go:9`) with its dialect constant, which compiles the CEL filters and appends the resulting SQL WHERE clauses and bind arguments.
