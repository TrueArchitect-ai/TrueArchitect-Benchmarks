**S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/Runner`) — runs **every 12 hours** (defined as `runnerInterval` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; it re-signs if less than 4 days remain), and updates the attachment's `Reference` field with a fresh presigned URL. On startup it also runs once immediately (`RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`) before entering the ticker loop.
