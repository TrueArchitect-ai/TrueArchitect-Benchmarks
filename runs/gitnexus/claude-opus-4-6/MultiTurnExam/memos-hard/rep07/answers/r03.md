The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this codebase. While a general-purpose cron scheduler package exists at `internal/scheduler/` (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`), it is never imported or used by the server or any production code. No scheduled database cleanup, vacuum, or purge job is registered anywhere. The only periodic background tasks are the S3 presign runner (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the in-memory cache cleanup (every 5 minutes, `store/cache/cache.go`), neither of which performs database cleanup.
