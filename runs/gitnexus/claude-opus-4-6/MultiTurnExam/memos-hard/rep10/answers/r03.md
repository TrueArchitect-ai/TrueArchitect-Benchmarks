The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this codebase. While an `internal/scheduler` package exists (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`) with cron-expression parsing and job registration infrastructure, it is never wired into the server startup and no database-cleanup job is registered anywhere. The only recurring background tasks are the S3 presign runner (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and in-memory cache cleanup (every 5 minutes, `store/cache/cache.go`), neither of which is a database cleanup cron job.
