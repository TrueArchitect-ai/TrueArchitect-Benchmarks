**S3 Presign Runner** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3 presigned URLs from expiring. It iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-presigns any URL older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days). It updates the attachment's `Reference` field and `LastPresignedTime` in the store.

At server startup, `server/server.go:162` creates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, runs it once immediately (`RunOnce` at line 163), then launches the continuous 12-hour ticker loop in a background goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at line 169).
