**S3 presign runner** — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`.

The `s3presign.Runner` periodically re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before they expire. It iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks each one's `LastPresignedTime`, and regenerates the presigned URL if it will expire within the next day (URLs have a 5-day lifetime; refresh triggers when 4 days have elapsed). The runner is created and run once immediately at startup (`server/server.go:162-163`), then launched as a continuous background goroutine on a 12-hour ticker (`server/server.go:166-171`).
