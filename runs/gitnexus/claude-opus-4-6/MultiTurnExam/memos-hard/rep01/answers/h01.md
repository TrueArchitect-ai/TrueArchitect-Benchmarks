h01.
**s3presign.Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired at **`server/server.go:162`**

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) refreshes S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments. Its `CheckAndPresign` method iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches, regenerates presigned URLs for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (the URLs expire after 5 days), and writes the new URL back to the store.

It is wired into server startup in `server/server.go:162–171`: `NewRunner` is created, `RunOnce` is called immediately for an initial pass, and then `Run` is launched in a background goroutine on a 12-hour ticker.
