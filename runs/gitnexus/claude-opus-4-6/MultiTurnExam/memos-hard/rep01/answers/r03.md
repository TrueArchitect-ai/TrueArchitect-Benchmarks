The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. There is no cron scheduler for database maintenance at all. The only scheduled background runners are `s3presign.Runner` (runs every 12 hours to refresh presigned URLs, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the in-memory cache cleanup (runs every 5 minutes to evict expired entries, `store/cache/cache.go:201`). Neither performs database cleanup, and neither uses a cron scheduler — they use simple `time.Ticker` loops.
