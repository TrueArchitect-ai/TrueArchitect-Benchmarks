CEL (Common Expression Language) engine in `internal/filter/engine.go`; the handler passes filter strings to the engine, which compiles the CEL expression into an AST, converts it to a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR tree (`internal/filter/parser.go`), then renders it to SQL with dialect-specific expressions per field via `internal/filter/render.go` — each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (DialectSQLite, DialectPostgres, DialectMySQL) to produce parameterized WHERE clauses.
