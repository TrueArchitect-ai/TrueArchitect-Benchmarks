Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, at `internal/filter/engine.go`. The CEL expression is compiled, converted to a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL WHERE fragments by `internal/filter/render.go`. Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect constant (sqlite/mysql/postgres) to produce parameterized SQL.
