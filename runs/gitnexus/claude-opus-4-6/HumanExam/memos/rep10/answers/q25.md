Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`; lives in `internal/filter/` (engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`, parser at `internal/filter/parser.go`). The CEL expression is compiled to an AST, converted to a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL via `internal/filter/render.go` — each driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (DialectSQLite, DialectMySQL, or DialectPostgres) to produce parameterized WHERE clause fragments.
