Before deleting the memo row, the store layer deletes:
- memo_relation records where the memo is the source (memo_id)
- memo_relation records where the memo is the target (related_memo_id)
- attachment records linked to the memo (including physical files)

The API handler additionally deletes comment memos (via recursive `Store.DeleteMemo`) before calling `Store.DeleteMemo` on the memo itself. (`store/memo.go:140-158`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:567-580`)
