Three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`), each with its entry file:
- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory function that selects between them at startup:
`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver`, calling `sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, or `postgres.NewDB`)
