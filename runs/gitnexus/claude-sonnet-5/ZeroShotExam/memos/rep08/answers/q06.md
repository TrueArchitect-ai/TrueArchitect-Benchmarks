**memo_relation**

The table `memo_relation` stores links between memos (source `memo_id`, target `related_memo_id`, and a `type` column). Confirmed in `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go:14,101,149` (and mirrored in `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`), backing the store layer used by `store/memo.go`.
