q25. Engine: **CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by a local package at `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `ir.go`, `schema.go`, `parser.go`, `helpers.go`, `render.go`).

Mechanism: `internal/filter/engine.go` compiles the `ListMemos` request's `filter` string with a schema-driven `cel.Env` into a CEL AST, converts that AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` tree (defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`), and then `internal/filter/render.go`'s `renderer` walks that tree, switching on `DialectName` (`sqlite`/`mysql`/`postgres`) to emit dialect-specific WHERE-clause SQL fragments — invoked per-store in `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/memo.go` via `filter.AppendConditions(...)`.
