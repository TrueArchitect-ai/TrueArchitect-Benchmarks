MemoService is served over two protocols:

- **Connect (Connect-RPC, used for gRPC + Connect + gRPC-Web)** — wrapped via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`
- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`

Both registrations are wired together in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`'s `RegisterGateway` (server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64-141), which sets up the gRPC-Gateway mux and then calls `connectHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers(...)` for the Connect handlers.
