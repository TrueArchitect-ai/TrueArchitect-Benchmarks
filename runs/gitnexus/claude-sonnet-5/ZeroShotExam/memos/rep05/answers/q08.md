**Driver — store/driver.go**

The `Driver` interface (store/driver.go:10-86) defines all database-backend methods; it's implemented by the MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite drivers in `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, and referenced via `store.Driver` in `store/db/db.go`.
