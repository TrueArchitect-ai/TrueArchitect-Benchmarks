MemoService is served over **two protocols**:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, mounted at `/api/v1/*`.
