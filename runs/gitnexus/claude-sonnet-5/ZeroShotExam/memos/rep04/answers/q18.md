Three implementations of `store.Driver` (defined in `store/driver.go`), one entry file per backend:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory: `NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (selects the driver via a `switch` on `profile.Driver`, calling `sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, or `postgres.NewDB` respectively).
