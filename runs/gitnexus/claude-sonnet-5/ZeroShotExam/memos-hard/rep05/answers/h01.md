**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before they expire. It scans S3-stored attachments in batches of 100 (`CheckAndPresign`, runner.go:47-131), skipping any whose `LastPresignedTime` is still within 4 days of "fresh" (URLs are valid for 5 days), and regenerates + saves a new presigned URL (`s3Client.PresignGetObject`) plus updated `LastPresignedTime` otherwise.

**Schedule:** runs once immediately at startup, then on a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, runner.go:27, used in `Runner.Run`, runner.go:29-41).

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:153-171` in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, which is itself invoked from server startup at `server/server.go:129`. Specifically:
- `server/server.go:162` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`
- `server/server.go:163` — immediate `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`
- `server/server.go:167-171` — goroutine running `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` for the recurring 12h loop, with its own cancelable context (`s3Context`/`s3Cancel`) for clean shutdown.
