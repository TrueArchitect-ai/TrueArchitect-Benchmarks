r04. Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used — this repo (memos) has no external message broker at all for webhooks.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process via a buffered Go channel, not a message broker.

- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, an in-memory channel with a capacity of 128.
- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` (`init()`) — spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` for each payload, dispatching the actual HTTP POST to the webhook URL (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:119-180`).
- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` — `PostAsync()` is the producer side: it does a non-blocking `select` send into `asyncPostQueue`, dropping (and logging a warning for) the payload if the channel is full rather than blocking.

So "the queue" is declared as a Go channel literal in `webhook.go`, not as a NATS subject or RabbitMQ queue/exchange declaration — there's no AMQP or NATS client dependency anywhere in `go.mod` or the codebase.
