**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background runner that re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs before they expire.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each pass skips any attachment whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (5-day expiry, refreshed once <4 days of validity remain — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:153-174` (`startBackgroundRunners`) — specifically `server/server.go:162-163` creates the runner and calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately, and `server/server.go:166-171` launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine for the recurring 12-hour loop.
