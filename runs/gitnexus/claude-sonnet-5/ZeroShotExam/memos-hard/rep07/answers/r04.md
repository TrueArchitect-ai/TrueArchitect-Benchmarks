Premise correction: this repo (usememos/memos) doesn't use a message broker for webhook delivery at all — there is no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency anywhere in the codebase (`go.mod` has no such entries, and no `nats`/`rabbitmq`/`amqp` imports exist).

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery is handled in-process with a Go buffered channel acting as a lightweight work queue. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:38` the queue is declared as `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, and `init()` (webhook.go:41-53) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over that channel and call `Post()` to deliver each webhook via `safeClient` (an SSRF-guarded `http.Client`). Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (webhook.go:187-198), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no persistence or external broker involved.
