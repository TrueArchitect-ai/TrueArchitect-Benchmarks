- protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, the custom `DialContext` used by `safeClient`'s `http.Transport`. It resolves the target hostname at dial time and rejects any resolved IP that is reserved/private via `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`, checked against the CIDR list in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14` (loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, and link-local/cloud-IMDS `169.254.0.0/16`, plus IPv6 equivalents). This dial-time check (rather than a one-time pre-check of the URL) is what defeats DNS-rebinding SSRF. A separate pre-flight check, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`), performs the same IP check at webhook-creation time but is not what protects the actual delivery path.
