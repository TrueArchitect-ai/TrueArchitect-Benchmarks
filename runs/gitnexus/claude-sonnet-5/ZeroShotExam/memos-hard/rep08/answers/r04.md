**Premise correction:** Neither. This repo (usememos/memos) has no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency anywhere (`go.mod`/`go.sum` and `package.json` have no `nats-io`, `streadway/amqp`, or `amqp091` entries), and there is no separate "webhook delivery worker" service.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is done in-process with a Go buffered channel used as a tiny worker pool, not an external message broker:

- `asyncPostQueue := make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — the in-memory "queue," declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`
- 4 goroutines are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` to actually deliver the webhook HTTP request
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select`/`default` send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no persistence or redelivery guarantee, consistent with it being a local channel rather than a durable broker queue.
