**Interceptor:** `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts:156-178` (attaches to the Connect transport at line 188).

**Token kinds:**
- Short-lived **access token** (JWT), kept in memory/state via `web/src/auth-state.ts` and sent as an `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header on every request.
- **Refresh token**, delivered as an HttpOnly cookie (`credentials: "include"`) and exchanged via `AuthService.refreshToken` (through a separate `refreshTransport` with no interceptors, to avoid recursion) when the access token is expired or a request returns `Unauthenticated`.

**Backend validating package:** `server/auth/` — JWT parsing/verification in `server/auth/token.go` (`ParseAccessTokenV2`, `ParseRefreshToken`, `verifyJWTKeyFunc`) and request-level authentication in `server/auth/authenticator.go` (`Authenticator.AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`, `AuthenticateByRefreshToken`).
