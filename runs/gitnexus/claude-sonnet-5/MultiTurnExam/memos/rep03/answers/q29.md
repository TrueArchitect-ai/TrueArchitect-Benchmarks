q29. Interceptor: `web/src/connect.ts` (`authInterceptor`, lines 156-178, wired into the transport at line 188). Token kinds it manages: JWT **access token** (attached as `Authorization: Bearer` header) and the **refresh token** (an HTTP-only cookie, refreshed reactively on 401 or proactively via `refreshAccessToken`). Backend validation package: `server/auth` (`server/auth/token.go`), which issues/validates JWT access tokens (15 min), JWT refresh tokens (30 days), and Personal Access Tokens (PATs), used by the Connect/gRPC interceptors in `server/router/api/v1` and by `server/router/fileserver`.
