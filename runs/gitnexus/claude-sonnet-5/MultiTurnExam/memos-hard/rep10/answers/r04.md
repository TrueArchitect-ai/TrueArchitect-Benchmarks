**Premise correction:** Neither. There is no message broker in this codebase — no NATS or RabbitMQ/AMQP dependency exists anywhere (`go.mod` has no such entries, and no client library for either is imported).

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel, not an external broker. `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` declares `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over this channel and call `Post` for each payload. `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues onto it with a non-blocking `select`/`default` that drops and logs a warning if the channel is full — there's no persistence, no external queue, and no cross-process delivery.
