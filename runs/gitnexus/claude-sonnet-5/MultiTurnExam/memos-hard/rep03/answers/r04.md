**Premise correction:** Neither. There is no external message broker (no NATS, no RabbitMQ) involved in webhook delivery — a repo-wide search finds no references to either in this codebase.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook dispatch uses an in-process, buffered Go channel as the "queue," declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. Four worker goroutines are started in that same file's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`) to drain the channel and call `Post` for each payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which does a non-blocking send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no broker, no persistence, and no cross-process queueing involved.
