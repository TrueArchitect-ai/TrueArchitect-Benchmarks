**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before they expire. It lists attachments with `StorageType = S3`, and for each one whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (presigned URLs are set to expire after 5 days), it calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject` to generate a fresh URL and updates the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` via `store.UpdateAttachment` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`), then on a `time.NewTicker` firing every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`), refreshing any URL that's within 4 days of its 5-day expiry.

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` followed by an immediate `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` and a goroutine running `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` on its own cancelable context (`s3Context`, created at `server/server.go:156`).
