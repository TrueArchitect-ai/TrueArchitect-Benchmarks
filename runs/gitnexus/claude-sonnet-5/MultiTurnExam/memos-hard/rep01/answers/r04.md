**Premise correction** — neither. This codebase has no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency (no such imports in `go.mod` or anywhere in the Go source), and webhook delivery doesn't consume from an external message broker at all.

**Actual mechanism** — webhook delivery uses an **in-process, in-memory Go channel** as its queue: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four worker goroutines are started in that package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180`) to deliver. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a logged warning) if the channel is full — there's no persistence, acknowledgment, or broker involved.
