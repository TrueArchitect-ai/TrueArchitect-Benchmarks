**Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180` (with async fan-out via `PostAsync` at line 184, backed by 4 worker goroutines draining `asyncPostQueue`, defined at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). It marshals the payload, optionally HMAC-signs it (Standard Webhooks style), and sends it via the package's shared `safeClient`.

**Protection mechanism** — SSRF/private-network protection happens at two layers in `internal/webhook/validate.go` and `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:
- `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`) does an upfront check when a webhook is created/updated: scheme must be http/https, and the hostname's resolved IPs must not fall in `reservedCIDRs` (loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud IMDS `169.254.0.0/16`, and IPv6 equivalents), unless the `AllowPrivateIPs` escape hatch (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) is set for self-hosted use.
- `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-76`), wired in as the `DialContext` of `safeClient`'s `http.Transport` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`), re-resolves and re-checks the target IP against the same reserved ranges at actual connection time — this is what closes the DNS-rebinding TOCTOU gap that a one-time `ValidateURL` check alone would leave open.
