**`s3presign.Runner` (S3 presigned-URL refresh runner) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate `RunOnce` at boot) — wired at `server/server.go:162`** (invoked from `Server.Start` via `server/server.go:129`).

Details:

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:44`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = ATTACHMENT_STORAGE_TYPE_S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-signs the object key via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:70`), then writes the fresh URL back to the attachment's `Reference` field along with an updated `LastPresignedTime` in the payload. So the stored external link never has a chance to expire out from under clients.
- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Runner.Run` (`:29`). The refresh itself is skipped unless the existing URL is within ~1 day of expiry — it re-signs only when `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, since presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:76-83`).
- **Wiring**: `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) constructs it at line 162, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously at line 163 so links are current immediately at startup, then launches the ticker loop in a goroutine at line 169 under a dedicated cancellable context (`s3Context`, line 156) whose cancel func is tracked for graceful shutdown in `stopBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:176`).
