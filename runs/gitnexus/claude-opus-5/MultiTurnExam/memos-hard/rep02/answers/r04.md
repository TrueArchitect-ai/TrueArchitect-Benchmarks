**Premise correction: neither. There is no message broker in this repository at all** — no NATS, no RabbitMQ, no Kafka, no AMQP client. `go.mod` and `go.sum` contain zero matches for any of them, so there is no queue declaration to point to. (A grep for `nsq` does produce hits in `store/test/migrator_upgrade_test.go`, but those are false positives — the substring inside `openMigratio**nSQ**LDB` and `legacyInsert…**nSQL**` helper names.)

**Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel with a fixed worker pool**, all in `/bench/home/work/internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

- **The "queue"** is `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — declared as a package-level variable at `webhook.go:39`. Capacity 128, held in the server's own memory.
- **The workers** are four goroutines started in the package `init()` at `webhook.go:42-55`. Each loops with `for payload := range asyncPostQueue` and calls `Post(payload)`, logging a warning on failure.
- **The producer** is `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), which does a non-blocking `select` send: if the channel is full, it takes the `default` branch and **drops the payload** with a warning (`webhook.go:191-195`). It also drops a nil payload with a warning at `webhook.go:185-188`.
- **Delivery** happens in `Post` (`webhook.go:120`), which builds the request, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC headers when a signing secret is set, and executes via `safeClient.Do` (`webhook.go:152`).

Consequences that follow from it being a channel rather than a broker, worth being explicit about since they're the practical difference:

- **No durability** — anything sitting in the channel is lost on process exit or crash; there's no persistence or replay.
- **No retries** — a failed `Post` is logged and abandoned; there's no dead-letter path or backoff.
- **No cross-process fan-out** — each replica has its own independent 128-slot channel and its own four workers.
- **Backpressure is loss, not blocking** — the `default` case means a burst past 128 queued items silently discards webhooks rather than slowing the producing RPC.

The producers feeding this are the memo webhook dispatchers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`.
