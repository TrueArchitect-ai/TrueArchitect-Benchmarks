**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired at `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, itself called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

Details:

- **What it does:** `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-signs the object key via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:70`), then writes the new URL back to the attachment's `Reference` and stamps `LastPresignedTime` on the payload. This keeps the stored external link from expiring.
- **Refresh policy:** an attachment is skipped if its `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old — presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry, so each link is renewed with roughly a day of margin (`runner.go:85-91`).
- **Schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`); `Run` drives a `time.Ticker` at that interval and exits on context cancellation (`runner.go:29-41`).
- **Startup wiring:** `startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously so links are refreshed immediately at boot (`server/server.go:163`), then launches `Run` in a goroutine under a dedicated cancellable context tracked by `backgroundRunnerCancels`/`backgroundRunnerWG` for clean shutdown (`server/server.go:156-171`).
