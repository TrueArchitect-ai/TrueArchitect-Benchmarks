Two patterns worth distinguishing: the API/fileserver/SSE sites are *reject-after-fetch* checks (`Private` → creator/admin only; non-`Public` → authentication required), while RSS, the sitemap, and the stats endpoints are *filter-at-query* — they pass `VisibilityList` into `store.FindMemo` so restricted rows never leave the database.

One deliberate bypass: `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:686-691` accepts a valid, unexpired `share_token` in place of a visibility check, matching the share-link flow in `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go` (`getActiveMemoShare` at `:199`, expiry at `:195`). Also note `checkMemoReadAccess` enforces an orthogonal rule alongside visibility — archived memos are creator-only (`memo_service.go:44-52`).
