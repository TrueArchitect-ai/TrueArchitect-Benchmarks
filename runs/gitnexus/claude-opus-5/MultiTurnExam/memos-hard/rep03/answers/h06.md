**Convergence.** All four `Dispatch*Webhook` wrappers live in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`. Three of them (`created`/`updated`/`deleted`, lines 15-27) funnel into the shared `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`); the comment variant (`:30`) has its own loop because it targets the *parent memo owner's* webhooks rather than the actor's. Both loops end at the same call: `webhook.PostAsync(payload)` — `memo_service_webhooks.go:71` and `:43`. Grepping the repo, those two lines are the only `PostAsync` call sites, and `PostAsync` is the only entry into the delivery queue, so it is the single convergence point.

The user-configured URL comes from `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks(...)` → `hook.Url` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:67`, `:41`), registered via the user webhook RPCs in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`.

Two notes on the shape of this: dispatch is fire-and-forget — `PostAsync` enqueues onto a 128-slot channel drained by four workers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `:42-55`), so no handler blocks on the remote endpoint, and every call site logs-and-continues on error rather than failing the RPC. And the reaction RPCs (`UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction`) do *not* fire webhooks — there is no dispatcher for them.
