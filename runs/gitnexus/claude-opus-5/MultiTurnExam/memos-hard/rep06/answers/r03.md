**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job, and the cron scheduler is never used by the server at all.** There is no schedule to report.

Two separate things are false in the premise:

**1. No database-cleanup job exists.** Searching `server/`, `store/`, and `cmd/` for `cleanup`, `vacuum`, `purge`, `prune`, and `nightly` turns up nothing that deletes or compacts persisted data on a schedule. Every hit is unrelated: in-memory cache eviction (`store/cache/cache.go:199-260`, a 5-minute ticker over a `sync.Map` — no database involvement), a test teardown helper (`server/router/api/v1/test/test_helper.go:65`), and a comment about cascading deletes during memo deletion (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:579`).

**2. The cron scheduler is dead code in this tree.** `internal/scheduler` is a full GitHub-Actions-style cron implementation — `New`, `Register`, `Start`, `Stop`, a cron expression parser, timezone support, and recovery/logging middleware (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`, documented in `doc.go` and `README.md`). But the only files importing `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own tests: `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`. Nothing under `server/`, `store/`, or `cmd/` imports it, so `scheduler.Register` is never called with a real job and no cron expression is ever evaluated in production.

**What the server actually runs on a schedule** is a single hand-rolled ticker loop, not cron:

- The **s3presign runner** — `s3presign.Runner.Run` uses `time.NewTicker(12 * time.Hour)` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,29-41`), started from `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` with one immediate `RunOnce` at startup. It refreshes S3 presigned attachment URLs; it deletes nothing.

That is the entire set of recurring background work registered at startup (`server/server.go:153-173`). Note this is an interval, not a wall-clock time — it fires 12 hours after process start and every 12 hours thereafter, so it lands at a different time of day on every restart.
