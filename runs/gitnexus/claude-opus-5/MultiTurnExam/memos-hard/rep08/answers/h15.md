Two structural notes: enforcement splits into **row-level gates** (`checkMemoReadAccess` and its per-RPC equivalents, applied after fetching a memo) and **query-level filters** (`FindMemo.VisibilityList` / CEL filters, applied before fetching) — the latter are what protect list endpoints, and a missing filter there leaks silently rather than erroring. Also, the MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) contains no visibility logic of its own; `catalog.go:12` shows it dispatches to `MemoService_ListMemos`, so it inherits the `ListMemos` filter rather than being a distinct site.
