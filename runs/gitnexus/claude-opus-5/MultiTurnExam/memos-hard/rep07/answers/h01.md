**`s3presign.Runner` (S3 presigned-URL refresh runner) — once at startup, then every 12 hours — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, itself called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

Details:

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner` struct with `NewRunner(store)`, `Run(ctx)`, `RunOnce(ctx)`, and the working method `CheckAndPresign(ctx)` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`). It pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, and for each one whose `S3Object.LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days it calls `s3Client.PresignGetObject(ctx, key)` and writes the fresh URL back via `store.UpdateAttachment` (`Reference` + updated `LastPresignedTime`). The comment at `runner.go:86-87` states presigned URLs expire after 5 days, so refreshing at the 4-day mark leaves a 1-day safety margin.

- **Schedule**: `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`), which exits on `ctx.Done()`. Additionally, `startBackgroundRunners` calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously at `server/server.go:163` so a refresh pass happens immediately on boot before the periodic loop starts.

- **Wiring / lifecycle**: `Server.Start` → `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` (`server/server.go:129`). The runner gets its own cancellable context (`server/server.go:156`), whose cancel func is stored in `backgroundRunnerCancels` for shutdown (`server/server.go:159`, cancelled in `stopBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:176`), and the goroutine is tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` so `Shutdown` waits for it via `waitBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:143`, `184`).
