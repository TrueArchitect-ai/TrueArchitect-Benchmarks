## Which are invoked, and by whom

Everything hangs off `Server.Shutdown` (`server/server.go:134`), which drives two independent chains:

**Chain A — SSE.** `Server.Shutdown` calls `s.closeLongLivedConnections()` at `server/server.go:141`; that helper (`server/server.go:204`) calls `s.sseHub.Close()` at **`server/server.go:206`**, nil-guarded. This exists because long-lived SSE requests don't drain on their own during `http.Server.Shutdown`.

**Chain B — storage.** `Server.Shutdown` calls `s.Store.Close()` at **`server/server.go:146`**. `Store.Close` (`store/store.go:72`) then:
- calls `*cache.Cache.Close` three times — `store/store.go:74`, `:75`, `:76` — on `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, and `userSettingCache` (all `*cache.Cache`, declared at `store/store.go:27-29`), to stop their cleanup goroutines;
- calls `s.driver.Close()` at **`store/store.go:78`**. That's an interface call through `store.Driver`, so it dispatches to whichever of `mysql.DB.Close`, `postgres.DB.Close`, or `sqlite.DB.Close` the factory selected in `NewDBDriver` (`store/db/db.go:14`). Each simply closes the underlying `*sql.DB` (`store/db/mysql/mysql.go:48`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go:47`, `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go:68`).

So per-process only one of the three driver `Close` implementations executes, but all three are live production code reachable via that single call site — none is dead.

**Test-only callers**, listed for completeness and excluded from the above: `server/router/api/v1/test/test_helper.go:66` calls `ts.Store.Close()`, and the two `*TestContext.Close` methods in `store/test/filter_helpers_test.go` are test-harness cleanup.

Note that `s.httpServer.Close()` at `server/server.go:216` and `listener.Close()` at `:117` are calls into stdlib `net/http` types, not into any `Close` declared in this repo.
