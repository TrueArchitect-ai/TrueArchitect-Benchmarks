## What it does

`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` keeps S3 presigned URLs for attachments from expiring:

- `Runner.Run` (`runner.go:29`) ticks on `runnerInterval = 12 * time.Hour` (`runner.go:27`) and calls `RunOnce` → `CheckAndPresign`.
- `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType == S3` in batches of 100, and for each one re-presigns via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the new URL back to the attachment's `Reference` plus a fresh `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:108-123`).
- The refresh is skipped when the existing URL was signed less than 4 days ago (`runner.go:85-91`), because presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`). So each link is renewed roughly a day before it would go stale.
