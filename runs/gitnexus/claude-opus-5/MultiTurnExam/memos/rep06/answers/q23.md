Two things that matter if you're changing the shape:

- The SQL layer is defined three times, once per dialect — `store/migration/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/LATEST.sql` plus a versioned migration under each dialect's version directories. `sqlite` is cited above as the reference.
- The generated Go bindings sit between the proto and the handlers: `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.go`. The store↔proto translation happens in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`, and the row↔struct scanning in `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`.

The `payload` column is itself a nested proto — `MemoPayload` in `proto/store/memo.proto`, stored as JSON in that column.
