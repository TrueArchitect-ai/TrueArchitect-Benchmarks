**Driver — `store/driver.go`**

Declared at `store/driver.go:10` as `store.Driver`. It's the single interface the three backends satisfy — `sqlite.DB`, `mysql.DB`, and `postgres.DB` (e.g. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go:16`) — each returned as a `store.Driver` by its `NewDB` constructor and selected in the `switch profile.Driver` of `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go`.

It covers connection-level concerns (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize` — documented to return `-1` with a nil error when the backend can't report a size) plus the per-model CRUD methods (attachments, memos, users, etc.), which the driver packages implement across their respective files.
