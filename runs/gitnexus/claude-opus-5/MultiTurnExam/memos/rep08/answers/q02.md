q02.

**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` has exactly three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/` (each with per-version folders of `.sql` files, e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.30/`).
- `store/migrator.go:44-45` embeds that whole tree (`//go:embed migration`), and `store/migrator.go:255` resolves the path at runtime as `migration/{profile.Driver}/`.
- `store/db/db.go` confirms the driver names that key into those directories — the `switch profile.Driver` accepts only `"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, and `"postgres"`, erroring with `"unknown db driver"` otherwise.
