**ConnectRPC — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`** (plus `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`)
**gRPC-Gateway REST/JSON — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**

Both are wired up inside the single `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` function (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:65`), called once at startup from `server/server.go:88`.

**1. gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON transcoding).** `runtime.NewServeMux` is built at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:96`, and `MemoService` is registered at line 108 via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` — the `...HandlerServer` variant, so RPCs are invoked in-process rather than dialing a gRPC port. The mux is mounted on Echo for `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` behind `http.MaxBytesHandler(..., maxAPIRequestBytes)`. This is the protocol that honors the `google.api.http` annotations, e.g. `POST /api/v1/memos` from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

**2. ConnectRPC.** Registered at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` via `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, on its own `http.ServeMux` with interceptors (metadata, logging, recovery, auth) assembled at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:129-136`. The comment there notes it "replaces grpc-web" and is what browser clients use — matching `memoServiceClient` in `web/src/connect.ts:197`.

The bridge between them: `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:22`) and its per-RPC methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` unwrap `req.Msg` and delegate to the same handler the gateway calls — so business logic has one source of truth. `convertGRPCError`/`grpcCodeToConnectCode` (`connect_handler.go:63-79`) keep error semantics aligned, and both paths share one `Authorizer` instance (`v1.go:68`).

Strictly speaking the Connect handler also accepts native gRPC and gRPC-Web wire formats, since that's what `connect-go` handlers do — but in this codebase there is no separate `grpc.NewServer()`, so the two registration sites are the two above.
