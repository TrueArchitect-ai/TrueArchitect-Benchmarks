**APIV1Service.SignIn — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go`**

Defined at `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go:51`. It holds the actual logic: password-based auth (username lookup + `bcrypt.CompareHashAndPassword`, plus a `DisallowPasswordAuth` check) and SSO/OAuth2 authorization-code auth, then issues the token.

Worth knowing about the layer in front of it: `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:78` is the method that satisfies the generated Connect service interface, but it's a thin adapter — it wraps the call in `connectWithHeaderCarrier` (so the service can set response headers, e.g. the session cookie, protocol-agnostically) and delegates straight to `s.APIV1Service.SignIn(ctx, req.Msg)`. Session creation itself lives in `APIV1Service.doSignIn` at `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_session.go:24`.
