**Driver — store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` (`store/driver.go:10`) declares every method a backend must provide — connection handling (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus per-model CRUD for attachments, memos, memo relations, instance settings, users, etc. It's implemented by the `DB` structs in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/` (`store/db/postgres/postgres.go:16`), and `store/db/mysql/`.
