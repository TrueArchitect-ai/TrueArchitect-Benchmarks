**s3presign runner — one immediate `RunOnce` at startup, then a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`)**

That is the only background runner the server starts. `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-172`) constructs `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously, then launches `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine; the loop is `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29-41` with the interval constant at line 27. Its work is re-presigning expiring S3 attachment URLs (`CheckAndPresign`, line 47). Shutdown cancels its context and waits on the WaitGroup (`server/server.go:174-199`).

Two things that look like background infrastructure but are not scheduled:

- **`server/runner/memopayload`** — despite living under `server/runner/`, it is not a runner at all. It has no `Run` loop and is only called inline from the request path: `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` (CreateMemo) and `:447` (UpdateMemo).
- **`internal/scheduler`** — a complete GitHub-Actions-style cron scheduler package (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, plus `doc.go`/`README.md`), **entirely unused by the server**. The only references to `scheduler.New`/`Register` outside the package are in its own docs, examples, and tests; nothing in `server/` or `cmd/` imports it.
