**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect directories — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` — each with a `LATEST.sql` plus versioned subdirectories (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.24/00__memo.sql`). These correspond to the driver implementations in `store/db/` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`).
