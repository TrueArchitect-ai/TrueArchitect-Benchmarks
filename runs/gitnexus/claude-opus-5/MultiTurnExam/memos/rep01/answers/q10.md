**store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

On a fresh install, `preMigrate` detects an uninitialized DB and applies the full schema from `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` — i.e. `migration/<driver>/LATEST.sql` from the embedded FS (`store/migrator.go:211-223`, path construction at `store/migrator.go:254-256`, constant at `store/migrator.go:56`, `//go:embed migration` at line 44-45).
