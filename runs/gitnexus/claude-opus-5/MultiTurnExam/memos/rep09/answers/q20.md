**Server-Sent Events (SSE)**; hub at **server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go**; broadcasting handlers: **CreateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction** (plus the shared update helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`).

Details:

- **Mechanism** — SSE over `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` and served by `handleSSE` with Bearer-token auth (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25-37`); the route is mounted alongside the gateway in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`.
- **Hub** — `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close` and per-client visibility filtering via `SSEClient.canReceive` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56-140`). Event types and payload shaping live in `sse_hub.go:12-46` and `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
