**web/src/connect.ts; short-lived JWT access token (Bearer) + HTTP-only refresh-token cookie (plus PATs for programmatic clients); server/auth**

- **Interceptor** — `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts:155-177`, installed on the shared Connect transport (line 187). It fetches a token via `getRequestToken()`, sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, and on a `Code.Unauthenticated` reply refreshes once, retries with an `X-Retry` marker, and otherwise calls `redirectOnAuthFailure()`.
- **Tokens it manages**:
  - *Access token* — short-lived JWT held in `web/src/auth-state.ts` (localStorage + in-memory, `memos_access_token`), proactively refreshed when within `REQUEST_TOKEN_EXPIRY_BUFFER_MS` of expiry (`web/src/connect.ts:124-149`).
  - *Refresh token* — never touched by JS; carried as an HTTP-only cookie via `credentials: "include"` (`fetchWithCredentials`, `web/src/connect.ts:57`) and exchanged through `AuthService.RefreshToken` on a separate interceptor-free transport to avoid recursion (lines 64-81). Concurrent refreshes are deduped by `tokenRefreshManager`, and fresh tokens are shared across tabs over a `BroadcastChannel` (`web/src/auth-state.ts`).
- **Backend validation** — package **`server/auth`**: `Authenticator.AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` (`server/auth/authenticator.go:41`), `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` (line 61, checked against the DB), and `AuthenticateByPAT` for `Personal Access Tokens` (line 102); `resolveBearer` (line 149) picks the path by token prefix. Token minting/parsing lives in `server/auth/token.go`, header extraction in `extract.go`, context propagation in `context.go`. It's invoked from the Connect auth interceptor and the gateway middleware in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`, and by the SSE endpoint (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:34`).
