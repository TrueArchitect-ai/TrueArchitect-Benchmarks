**store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

On a fresh install `preMigrate` sees `IsInitialized == false` and applies `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` — i.e. `migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` from the embedded `migrationFS` — in a single transaction, instead of replaying the incremental `0.x/` migrations (`store/migrator.go:217-227`, constant at line 56, embed at line 44).
