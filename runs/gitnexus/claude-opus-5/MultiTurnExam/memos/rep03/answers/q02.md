**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Migrations live in `store/migration/`, which has exactly three dialect subdirectories — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/` — each with per-version folders plus a `LATEST.sql`. This matches the three driver packages under `store/db/` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`). Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is SQLite-only.
