**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoAttachments, SetMemoRelations, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism** — SSE over the HTTP endpoint registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (called from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:126`), with the streaming handler in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`. It's a separate Echo route, not part of the Connect/gateway RPC surface.

**Hub** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` defines `SSEEventType` and the six event names (lines 15-20: `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`), the `SSEEvent` payload struct (line 24), and the hub itself with client registration and `Broadcast`. `Visibility` and `CreatorID` are `json:"-"` — carried only for server-side delivery filtering. Helpers for building events live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
