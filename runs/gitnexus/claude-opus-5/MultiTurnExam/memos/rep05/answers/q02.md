**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations are shipped per-dialect under `store/migration/`, one directory each:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`

They're embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the matching driver implementations live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`.
