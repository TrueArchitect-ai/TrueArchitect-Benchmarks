**Driver — store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` is declared at `store/driver.go:10` and covers the whole persistence surface: connection lifecycle (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus per-model CRUD for attachments, memos, memo relations, instance settings, users, and so on.
