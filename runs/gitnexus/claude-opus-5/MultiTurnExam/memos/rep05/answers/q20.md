**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoAttachments, SetMemoRelations, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism.** SSE over a plain HTTP endpoint — `handleSSE` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48`) and the route is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (`sse_handler.go:25`), mounted on the same Echo group as `/api/v1/*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:123`. Not WebSockets.

**Hub.** `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`, with `NewSSEHub` (:63), `Subscribe` (:71), `Unsubscribe` (:89), `Close` (:99), and `Broadcast` (:115). Per-client delivery filtering happens in `SSEClient.canReceive` (:134), using the `Visibility`/`CreatorID` fields that `SSEEvent` (:24) carries but does not serialize. Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (`sse_hub.go:14-21`).
