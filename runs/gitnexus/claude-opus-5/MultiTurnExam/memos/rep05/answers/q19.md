**ConnectRPC.** `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is registered on the Connect mux at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, which is called from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:141` with the interceptors and a read-size limit. The `s` there is `ConnectServiceHandler` (`connect_handler.go:21`), which embeds `*APIV1Service`; the per-RPC wrappers that unwrap `req.Msg` and re-wrap the response — e.g. `CreateMemo` at `connect_services.go:307` — live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`. This is what the web client uses (`web/src/connect.ts`).

**REST/JSON.** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:107` registers the grpc-gateway handlers directly against `APIV1Service` (in-process, no network gRPC hop). The mux is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` (`v1.go:126`), and the routes come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`, with generated code in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

Both protocols share one implementation — the methods on `*APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`. Note there's no native gRPC listener: no `grpc.NewServer` exists in the codebase.
