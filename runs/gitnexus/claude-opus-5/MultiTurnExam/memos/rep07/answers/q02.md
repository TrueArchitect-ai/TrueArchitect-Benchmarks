**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` contains exactly three dialect subtrees: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/` — each with versioned migration dirs (0.17 … 0.30) plus a `LATEST.sql`.
- Matching driver implementations live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, dispatched from `store/db/db.go`.

Note: seed data (`store/seed/`) ships for SQLite only.
