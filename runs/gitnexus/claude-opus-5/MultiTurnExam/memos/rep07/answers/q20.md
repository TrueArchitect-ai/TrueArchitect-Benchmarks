**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over `GET /api/v1/sse`, Bearer-token authenticated.

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (`:57`) holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` and exposes `Broadcast`; `SSEEvent`/`SSEEventType` are declared at `:24` and `:12`, with per-client delivery filtering by `Visibility`/`CreatorID`. The endpoint and connection lifecycle (30s heartbeat) live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` at `:25`, `handleSSE` at `:34`); event construction helpers are in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

`dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` is the shared update path, called from `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`). `CreateMemo` can be called with SSE suppressed — `CreateMemoComment` does exactly that (`memo_service_comments.go:56`) so the parent's comment event fires instead of a generic `memo.created`.

On the browser side, the stream is consumed by `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
