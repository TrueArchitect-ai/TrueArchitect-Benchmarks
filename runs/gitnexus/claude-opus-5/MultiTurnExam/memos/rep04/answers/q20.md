Details:

- **Mechanism** — SSE. The endpoint sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` in `handleSSE` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:34`, header at `:48`), registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25`) and mounted alongside the rest of `/api/v1` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`.
- **Hub** — `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`: a mutex-guarded set of `SSEClient` connections, each with a buffered event channel plus `userID`/`role` used for delivery filtering; `Broadcast` at `sse_hub.go:115`. Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (`sse_hub.go:14-21`), with event construction helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
