**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/*.sql` — the three driver directories are `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` (each with per-version folders plus a `LATEST.sql`). They're embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44` and selected by driver at `store/migrator.go:255`, matching the driver implementations in `store/db/`.

Note: seed data (`store/seed/`) ships for sqlite only.
