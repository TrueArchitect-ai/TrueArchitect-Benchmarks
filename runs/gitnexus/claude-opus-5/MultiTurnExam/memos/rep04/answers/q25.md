**Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, wrapped by the in-repo filter engine at `internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` / `Engine.CompileToStatement` in `internal/filter/engine.go`); mechanism:** the CEL expression is parsed and type-checked against a memo-specific CEL environment (`internal/filter/schema.go:232+`, declaring `content`, `creator_id`, `created_ts`, `pinned`, `tag`/`tags`, `visibility`, `has_task_list`, …), normalized into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/parser.go` → `internal/filter/ir.go`), and then rendered by a dialect-aware renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) into a parameterized SQL fragment plus args, which the per-dialect memo drivers append to their WHERE clause (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:11`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:11`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:11`, via `internal/filter/helpers.go`).

Dialect differences handled in the renderer: `?` placeholders for SQLite/MySQL vs `$n` for Postgres, JSON payload access (`json_extract` vs `->`/`->>`), tag membership (`LIKE` / `JSON_CONTAINS` / `@>`), and `LIKE` vs `ILIKE` — documented in `internal/filter/README.md`. The same engine also backs attachment and shortcut filters (`store/db/*/attachment.go`, `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`).
