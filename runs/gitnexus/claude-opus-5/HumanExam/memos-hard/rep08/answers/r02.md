— Premise is wrong: there is no rate-limiting middleware, per-user or otherwise, and no rate-limit library in `go.mod`. The Echo middleware chain is only `middleware.Recover()` and the CORS middleware (`server/server.go:51-52`), plus static/SPA middleware for the frontend (`server/router/frontend/frontend.go:54,59`). The Connect interceptor chain is `MetadataInterceptor`, `LoggingInterceptor`, `RecoveryInterceptor`, `AuthInterceptor` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go:22,98,165,208`) — authentication/authorization only, no throttling. The only concurrency limits are semaphores on thumbnail/image processing (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:60-61`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:115`).
