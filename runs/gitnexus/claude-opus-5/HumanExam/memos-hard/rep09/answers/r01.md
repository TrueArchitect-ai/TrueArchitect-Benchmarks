— Premise incorrect: there is no Redis anywhere in this codebase (zero matches for `redis` in Go sources and in `go.sum`). Caching is an in-process, in-memory implementation: `store/cache` (`cache.Interface` at `store/cache/cache.go:11-32`, `Cache` with a background cleanup goroutine, `Close` at `:187`). The store creates three of them — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — with `cache.Config{DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute, CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute, MaxItems: 1000}` at `store/store.go:40-53`. So user entries expire after **10 minutes** (the shared default TTL; `cache.DefaultConfig()` at `store/cache/cache.go:56-60` uses the same values). User entries are also invalidated explicitly on delete (`store/user_delete.go:39-47`).
