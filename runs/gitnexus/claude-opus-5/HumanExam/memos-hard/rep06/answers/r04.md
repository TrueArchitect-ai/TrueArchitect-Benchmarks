Premise wrong: **neither — there is no message broker.** No NATS, RabbitMQ, or AMQP dependency exists (nothing in `go.mod`, no matching imports). Webhook delivery uses an **in-process buffered Go channel**: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:38`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that call `Post` (`:120`). Producers use `PostAsync` (`:184`), which **drops** the delivery with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). Consequently nothing is durable — queued webhooks are lost on restart, and there is no retry.
