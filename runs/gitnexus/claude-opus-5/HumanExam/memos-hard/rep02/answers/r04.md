— Premise is wrong on both options: there is no message broker at all — no NATS, no RabbitMQ, no AMQP client (nothing in `go.mod`, no imports), and consequently no queue declaration. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `package init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). This is process-local and non-durable: queued webhooks are lost on restart.
