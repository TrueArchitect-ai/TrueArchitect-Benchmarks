Premise wrong: there is no message broker at all — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka anywhere in the tree or `go.mod`, and no queue is declared. Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). Enqueue is non-blocking with drop-on-full and a warning log (`PostAsync`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`). Consequence: the "queue" is per-process and non-durable — pending webhooks are lost on restart, and there are no retries.
