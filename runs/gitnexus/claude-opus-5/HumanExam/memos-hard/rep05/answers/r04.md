**Premise is wrong — there is no message broker; neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka/AMQP) appears in `go.mod` or any source file.** Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which non-blockingly enqueues and **drops the event with a warning if the queue is full** (`:189-195`); consumers call `Post` (`:120`). Nothing is persisted, so pending deliveries are lost on restart.
