Premise is wrong: **there is no message broker at all** — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka/AMQP) appears in `go.mod` or any Go source, and no queue is declared anywhere. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue` is a `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with capacity 128 (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `package init` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). Producers use `PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the delivery with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). Consequence: deliveries are best-effort, in-memory, and lost on restart — no persistence, no retry.
