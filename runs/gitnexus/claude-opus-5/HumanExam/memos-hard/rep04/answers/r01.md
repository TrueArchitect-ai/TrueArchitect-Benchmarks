Premise is wrong on both counts: **there is no Redis anywhere in this repo** (no `redis` import, dependency, or config — repo-wide grep returns zero hits), and there is no per-entry TTL specific to users. Actual mechanism: a process-local in-memory cache, `store/cache/cache.go` (`Cache` type, `cache.New` at `:59`-region, `Config` at `:43`). `store.New` builds three of them — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — from one shared `cache.Config`: `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000` (`store/store.go:39-53`). So user entries live 10 minutes, swept every 5 minutes; `Cache.Set` applies `DefaultTTL` (`store/cache/cache.go:94`). Users are cached on read/list (`store/user.go:143`, `:167`) and evicted on delete (`store/user_delete.go:39-41`).
