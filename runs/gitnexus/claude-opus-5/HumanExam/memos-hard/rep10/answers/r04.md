— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker at all — no NATS, RabbitMQ, AMQP, or Kafka in the Go sources or in `go.mod`, and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery is entirely **in-process**: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). Producers call `PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`) — so delivery is best-effort and non-durable; nothing survives a restart.
