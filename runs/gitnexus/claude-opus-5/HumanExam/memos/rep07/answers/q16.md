`Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-158`) deletes, before the memo row:

2. `memo_relation` rows where the memo is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) — `store/memo.go:145`
3. every attachment linked to the memo, one by one via `Store.DeleteAttachment` (which also removes the stored blob/file) — `store/memo.go:149-157`
