**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, one subtree per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

They're embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`, and resolved per driver at the path `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql` (documented at `store/migrator.go:39`). The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`.
