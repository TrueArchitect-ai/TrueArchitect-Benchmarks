**Convergence for delivery:**

The first three are thin wrappers over the shared helper `(*APIV1Service).dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, builds a payload per hook, and calls `webhook.PostAsync`. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` does not go through that helper (it targets the *related* memo's owner, not the comment's creator) but performs the same loop inline.

All four converge on **`webhook.PostAsync`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, dropping with a warning when full). Four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`) drain the queue and call **`webhook.Post`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the single actual delivery function, which marshals the payload, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers when a signing secret is set, and POSTs via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.

Call sites: created at `memo_service.go:157`, deleted at `memo_service.go:562`, updated at `memo_update_helpers.go:67` (inside `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), comment-created at `memo_service_comments.go:119`. All four are best-effort — failures are logged at warn level, not returned to the caller.
