`web/src/connect.ts`

This is the only file in the frontend that creates ConnectRPC clients. It builds the transport with `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) and exports all service clients via `createClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`. It also creates a separate auth-interceptor-free transport and `refreshAuthClient` for token refresh (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`).
