**Engine:** Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go` v0.28.1) — the filter string is standard CEL syntax. The project's own wrapper engine lives in `internal/filter/` (see `internal/filter/README.md`), entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go:111`.

**Mechanism (one sentence):** `Engine.Compile` type-checks the CEL expression against a memo-specific `cel.Env` declared in `internal/filter/schema.go` (only whitelisted fields/ops/extensions are legal), `internal/filter/parser.go` lowers the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition-tree IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholder args (`Statement{SQL, Args}`) selected by `RenderOptions.Dialect` (`DialectSQLite` / `DialectMySQL` / `DialectPostgres`).

**Path through ListMemos:**
- `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-215` — validates the filter (`validateFilter`, defined at `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336`, which compiles it against the driver's dialect) and appends the raw string to `memoFind.Filters`; it also appends a generated visibility filter at line 222.
- The store driver compiles it for real: `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69` each call `filter.DefaultEngine()` then `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with their own `filter.Dialect*` constant.
- `internal/filter/helpers.go:9` — `AppendConditions` runs `CompileToStatement` per filter with a `PlaceholderOffset` (so `$n` numbering composes with pre-existing args) and appends `(sql)` to the `WHERE` list and the args to the arg slice.

Dialect differences (JSON access, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, `?` vs `$n` placeholders, tag containment via `LIKE`/`JSON_CONTAINS`/`@>`, regex via `REGEXP`/`~`, date-part extraction) are all encapsulated in `internal/filter/render.go` and the per-dialect `Expressions` maps in `internal/filter/schema.go`.
