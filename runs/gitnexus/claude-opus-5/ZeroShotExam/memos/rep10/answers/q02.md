**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project ships three parallel migration trees, one per SQL dialect, embedded into the binary:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs (`0.17` … `0.30`) plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

They're embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the corresponding driver implementations live in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/` (dispatched from `store/db/db.go`).
