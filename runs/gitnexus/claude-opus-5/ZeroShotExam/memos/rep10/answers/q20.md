**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse` stream (`text/event-stream`, Bearer-token auth, 30s heartbeat pings), consumed on the frontend by `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`) with `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast` (`sse_hub.go:115`, drops events for slow clients with full 32-event buffers), and `Close`. Per-client visibility filtering lives in `SSEClient.canReceive` (private memos only to their creator or admins). The endpoint itself is `RegisterSSERoutes`/`handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:24,33`; shutdown drains connections via `Server.closeLongLivedConnections` (`server/server.go:202`).

**Handlers that broadcast:**
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (`memo.created`; suppressed via the `suppressSSEKey` context flag when invoked from `CreateMemoComment`)
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (`memo.deleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (`memo.comment.created`, keyed to the parent memo)
- `UpsertMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` (`reaction.upserted`)
- `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161` (`reaction.deleted`)
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71` (`memo.updated`), the shared path called by `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `memo_attachment_service.go:48`, and `memo_relation_service.go:48`
