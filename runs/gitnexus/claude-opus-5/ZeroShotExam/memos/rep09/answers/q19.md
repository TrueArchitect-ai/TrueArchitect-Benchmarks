**REST/HTTP+JSON via gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**
`RegisterGateway` builds the grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux` and registers the service at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` (`v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`), then mounts the mux on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:128-129`). The generated glue lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

**Connect RPC (connectrpc, over HTTP) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`**
`RegisterConnectHandlers` wraps the same `APIV1Service` and registers the handler at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (`apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)`); the per-method Connect wrappers that adapt request/response types are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304+` ("// MemoService" section). The mux is mounted on Echo at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `v1.go:139-144`. Generated glue: `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`.

Both surfaces share one implementation (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`); `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` and just translates errors. Note there is **no** native gRPC listener — nothing calls `RegisterMemoServiceServer`, so `memo_service_grpc.pb.go` is only used for the `Unimplemented*` embeds and clients.

Separately, `server/router/mcp/` exposes `MemoService_*` operations as MCP tools (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-25`), but that is a proxy generated from the OpenAPI spec over the REST surface rather than a third registration of the service.
