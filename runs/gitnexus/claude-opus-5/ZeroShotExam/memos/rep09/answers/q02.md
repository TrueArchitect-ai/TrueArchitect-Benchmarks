**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/`, embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`. The three dialect directories are:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.10 … 0.30 + `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versions 0.17 … 0.30 + `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (versions 0.19 … 0.30 + `LATEST.sql`)

These match the three driver implementations in `store/db/` (`store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`), and the path convention is documented at `store/migrator.go:39` (`store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`).
