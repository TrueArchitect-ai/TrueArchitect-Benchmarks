There's no ORM tag mapping here — memos hand-writes SQL per driver, so each table has a plain Go struct in `store/` and per-driver SQL that names the table.

| table | Struct — path |
|---|---|
| `user` | `User` — `store/user.go:29` |
| `attachment` | `Attachment` — `store/attachment.go:16` |
| `reaction` | `Reaction` — `store/reaction.go:7` |

The mapping is confirmed by the driver SQL, e.g. `INSERT INTO "user"` at `store/db/postgres/user.go:16`, `INSERT INTO attachment` at `store/db/postgres/attachment.go:33`, and `INSERT INTO reaction` at `store/db/postgres/reaction.go:13` (parallel implementations exist under `store/db/mysql/` and `store/db/sqlite/`). Schemas are in `store/migration/{postgres,mysql,sqlite}/LATEST.sql`.
