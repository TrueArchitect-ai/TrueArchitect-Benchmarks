**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, one directory per dialect:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versions 0.2 through 0.30, plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — versions 0.17 through 0.30, plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versions 0.19 through 0.30, plus `LATEST.sql`

The whole tree is embedded into the binary at `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`), and the dialect subdirectory is selected at runtime via `getMigrationBasePath()`, used by the glob at `store/migrator.go:141` and `store/migrator.go:300`.
