**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by the in-repo filter engine at **`internal/filter/`** — `engine.go` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()`), `schema.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `helpers.go` (see `internal/filter/README.md`).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** `Engine.Compile` type-checks the CEL string against the memo-specific `cel.Env` declared in `internal/filter/schema.go`, `parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition-tree IR (`ir.go`), and `render.go` walks that IR to emit a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholder args (`Statement{SQL, Args}`) for `DialectSQLite` / `DialectMySQL` / `DialectPostgres`, which `filter.AppendConditions` (`internal/filter/helpers.go:9`) appends to the driver's `WHERE` clause.

**Path through ListMemos:**
- `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-216` — validates the request filter (`validateFilter`, `server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go:336`, which compiles it against the profile's driver dialect) and appends it to `memoFind.Filters`; the implicit visibility filter at line 222 is itself a CEL string.
- Store drivers call the engine per dialect: `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` (`DialectSQLite`), `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69` (`DialectMySQL`), `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58` (`DialectPostgres`).

Dialect differences (placeholders `?` vs `$n`, `json_extract`/`->>`/`JSON_EXTRACT`, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, `REGEXP` vs `~`, `json_each`/`JSON_TABLE`/`jsonb_array_elements_text`) are all encapsulated in `internal/filter/render.go`.
