**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, one directory per dialect:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versions 0.17–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (versions 0.19–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.2–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)

This matches the driver packages in `store/db/` (`store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`, wired up in `store/db/db.go`).
