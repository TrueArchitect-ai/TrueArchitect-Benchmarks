`web/src/connect.ts`

This is the single file where all ConnectRPC service clients are created. It:

- Builds the transport with `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`), pointing at `window.location.origin` with binary format, credentialed fetch, and an auth interceptor.
- Exports the clients via `createClient(...)` at `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`.
- Also creates a separate interceptor-free `refreshTransport` and `refreshAuthClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`) used solely for token refresh, to avoid recursion through the auth interceptor.
