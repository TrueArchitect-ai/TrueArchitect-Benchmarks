## Mechanism

Plain HTTP **SSE** (`text/event-stream`), not WebSockets. The endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25` (wired at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`). `handleSSE` (`sse_handler.go:34`) authenticates via a Bearer token, sets `Cache-Control: no-cache` / `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `data: {...}\n\n` frames, and emits a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s. The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.

## Hub

`SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Each client has a 32-slot buffered channel; events are dropped rather than blocking on slow clients (`sse_hub.go:126-130`). `SSEClient.canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:134`) filters private memos to the creator or admins. It's held on `APIV1Service.SSEHub` (`v1.go:36`) and closed at shutdown via `Server.closeLongLivedConnections` (`server/server.go:202-207`).
