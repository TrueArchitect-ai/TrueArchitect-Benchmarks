**`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`; `memoServiceClient`**

- `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` holds the memo CRUD layer: `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo` (mutations) and `useMemo`, `useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemoComments`, `useInfiniteMemoComments`, `useLinkMetadata` (queries), plus the `memoKeys` query-key factory.
- It imports `memoServiceClient` from `@/connect` (`web/src/connect.ts:...`), a Connect-RPC client created via `createClient(MemoService, transport)` where `MemoService` is generated into `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts`. Calls go through it as `memoServiceClient.createMemo/updateMemo/deleteMemo/getMemo/listMemos/listMemoComments/getLinkMetadata`.
