**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/` — one directory per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.28/00__user_identity.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (e.g. `LATEST.sql`)

These are embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and resolved at runtime by driver as documented in `store/migrator.go:39` (`store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`). The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, and `store/db/postgres`.
