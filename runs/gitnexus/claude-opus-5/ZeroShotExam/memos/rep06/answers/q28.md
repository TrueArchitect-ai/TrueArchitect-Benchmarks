**Background runners actually started by the server** (`server/server.go:153` `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`):

- **s3presign runner (`RunOnce`)** — one immediate synchronous pass at startup, driven by nothing but the server boot itself (`server/server.go:162-163`).
- **s3presign runner (`Run` goroutine)** — `time.NewTicker` on a fixed 12-hour interval (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-41`); it re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose last presign is older than 4 days. Runs under its own cancellable context tracked in `backgroundRunnerCancels` + `backgroundRunnerWG`, cancelled on shutdown (`server/server.go:156-171`, `176-201`).

That is the entire set — `startBackgroundRunners` starts exactly one long-lived runner.

**Other goroutines that exist but are not scheduled background workers** (listed to avoid confusion, each is request-/lifecycle-scoped, not periodic):
