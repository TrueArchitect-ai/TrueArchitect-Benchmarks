**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over plain HTTP — `GET /api/v1/sse`, `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, Bearer-token auth, `data: {json}\n\n` frames plus a `: connected` preamble and a 30s `: heartbeat` comment (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:15-110`). No WebSockets. The browser consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`) with `NewSSEHub`/`Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Each client has a 32-event buffered channel; events for a full buffer are dropped rather than blocking the broadcaster, and `SSEClient.canReceive` filters private-memo events to the creator or an admin. Wired up in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57,125` and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205-206`. Event payloads are built by helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
