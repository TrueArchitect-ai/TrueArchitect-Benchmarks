**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, one directory per dialect:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versions 0.2 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — versions 0.17 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versions 0.19 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`

They are embedded into the binary at `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`), and the per-dialect subdirectory is selected at runtime via `getMigrationBasePath()` (`store/migrator.go:141`). The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, and `store/db/postgres`.
