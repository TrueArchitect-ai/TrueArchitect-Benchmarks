**REST/HTTP+JSON via gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**
`APIV1Service` embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer` (`v1.go:26`) and is registered on the grpc-gateway mux by `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, served under `/api/v1/*` (`v1.go:128`). The REST paths come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (e.g. `get: "/api/v1/memos"` at line 30), with generated glue in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

**Connect RPC (connectrpc, `/memos.api.v1.MemoService/*`) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` + `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`**
`ConnectServiceHandler` wraps `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21-28`) and registers the handler via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`. The per-method Connect wrappers (unwrapping `connect.Request` → calling the gRPC impl → `connect.NewResponse`) are in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304+` (`// MemoService` section). It's mounted onto Echo at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:139-144`, with generated code in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`.

One note for completeness: `server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-25` exposes `MemoService_*` as MCP tools, but that isn't a third transport for the service — `server/router/mcp/adapter.go` replays them as internal HTTP requests against the Echo server, i.e. through the REST/gateway surface above.
