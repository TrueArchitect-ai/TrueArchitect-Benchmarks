**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP — a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse` stream with `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, a 30s `: heartbeat` comment ping, and Bearer-token auth (not WebSockets). Because auth uses an `Authorization` header, the browser side uses a `fetch`-based reader rather than `EventSource` (`web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203`).

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, per-client buffered channel of 32, drops events for slow clients rather than blocking). It's constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` and exposed as `APIV1Service.SSEHub`; the endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (`v1.go:125` → `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25`), and `server/server.go:38,77` holds it for shutdown (`Close()`).

The three `memo.updated` handlers share `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66`), which fires the webhook and the broadcast together.

Delivery is filtered server-side by `SSEClient.canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:129`): private-memo events reach only the creator or an admin; public/protected go to everyone connected.
