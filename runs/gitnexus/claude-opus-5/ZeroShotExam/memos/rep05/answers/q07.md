**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is the single module where the ConnectRPC transport and all service clients are created:

- `createConnectTransport(...)` builds the main transport (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) with an auth interceptor, plus a separate `refreshTransport` without the interceptor to avoid recursion (`web/src/connect.ts:65`).
- `createClient(...)` exports the clients (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` — plus the internal `refreshAuthClient` at line 72.
