`Store.DeleteMemo` (`/bench/home/work/store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes, in order, before calling the driver's `DeleteMemo` for the memo row itself:

1. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`)
2. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the target/related memo** — `DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:145`)
3. **Attachment records linked to the memo** — lists via `ListAttachments{MemoID: &delete.ID}` and deletes each with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`)

Then `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` removes the memo row (`store/memo.go:158`).
