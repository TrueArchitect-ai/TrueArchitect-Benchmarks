**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

They are embedded into the binary at `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration` → `migrationFS`) and selected at runtime via the driver-specific base path used in the glob at `store/migrator.go:141`. The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`.
