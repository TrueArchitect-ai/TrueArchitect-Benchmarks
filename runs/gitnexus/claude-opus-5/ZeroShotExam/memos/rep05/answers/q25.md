**Mechanism (one sentence):** `Engine.Compile` (`internal/filter/engine.go:47`) compiles the CEL string against a memo-specific CEL environment declared in `internal/filter/schema.go` (only schema-declared fields/types are allowed), `buildCondition` in `internal/filter/parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `Program.Render` (`engine.go:107`) hands that IR to the renderer in `internal/filter/render.go`, which emits a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholder args (`DialectSQLite` / `DialectMySQL` / `DialectPostgres`, `DialectName` declared at `internal/filter/schema.go:11`) handling JSON access, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, regex operator, and `?` vs `$n` placeholders.
