Details:

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner` with `NewRunner(store)`, `Run`, `RunOnce`, `CheckAndPresign`. It pages through attachments whose `StorageType` is `S3` (batches of 100, `runner.go:55-65`), regenerates the presigned GET URL via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` (`runner.go:108`), and writes it back to the attachment's `Reference` field along with a new `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:114-123`). That `Reference` is the externally-stored blob's URL, so this is what keeps it from expiring/going stale.
- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` loop in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). Additionally `RunOnce` is called synchronously once at startup (`server/server.go:163`). Each attachment is skipped unless its last presign is older than 4 days, since URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry (`runner.go:85-91`).
- **Wiring**: `startBackgroundRunners` creates a cancellable child context, constructs the runner, does the immediate `RunOnce`, then launches `Run` on a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` (`server/server.go:153-174`); shutdown cancels and waits at `server/server.go:140,143`.
