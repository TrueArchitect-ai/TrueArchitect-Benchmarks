**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.** The cron scheduler exists, but nothing registers a real cleanup job with it — no job of any kind is registered outside of the package's own tests and docs.

What actually exists:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a self-contained, GitHub-Actions-inspired cron scheduler library (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`). It is generic infrastructure — it has no knowledge of the database or of any specific job.
- Grepping for imports of `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` finds only `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13` and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`. The server startup path (`server/server.go`, `cmd/`) never constructs a scheduler, so no job runs in production.

The `0 2 * * *` ("2 AM daily") schedule you may be thinking of appears only in documentation and example code, not in wired-up application code:

- `internal/scheduler/doc.go:14-21` — a package-doc usage snippet naming a job `"daily-cleanup"` with `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`, whose handler body is the comment `// Your cleanup logic here`.
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-106` — an `Example_multipleJobs` test that registers a `"cleanup"` job at `"0 2 * * *"` whose handler just prints `"Cleaning up old data..."`.

Neither touches the database.

The only actual recurring cleanup in the codebase is unrelated to cron: the in-memory cache's expiry sweep at `store/cache/cache.go:199-218` (`cleanupLoop`), driven by a `time.Ticker` at `CleanupInterval`, which defaults to 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go:60`, and `store/store.go:42`).
