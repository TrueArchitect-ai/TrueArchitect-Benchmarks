How the paths merge: every handler above lands in one of the four `Dispatch*Webhook` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`, three of which funnel through the unexported `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`:48`). Each looks up the target URL from `Store.GetUserWebhooks` — i.e. the user-configured URL, registered via `CreateUserWebhook`/`UpdateUserWebhook` in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go` — sets `payload.URL = hook.Url`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`). `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:176`) only enqueues onto `asyncPostQueue`; the four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`:41-53`) drain it into `Post`, which is the sole place an outbound `http.Request` is constructed and executed (`safeClient.Do`, `:147`).

So `PostAsync` is the synchronous convergence point in the call graph, and `Post` is the single function that performs delivery.

Two things worth noting about that boundary: the URL is validated at registration time by `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go`, called at `user_service_webhooks.go:74`), and at dial time `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go:57`) re-resolves the host and rejects reserved/private IPs, so the SSRF surface is guarded on both ends rather than only at configuration.
