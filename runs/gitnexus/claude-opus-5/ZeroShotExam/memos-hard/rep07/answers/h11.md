## Method

The data layer is the Go package `store/` (`/bench/home/work/store/`), whose persistent entity types are declared as top-level structs backed by DB tables: `User` (`store/user.go:29`), `Memo` (`store/memo.go:35`), `Attachment` (`store/attachment.go:16`), `MemoRelation` (`store/memo_relation.go:16`), `MemoShare` (`store/memo_share.go:6`), `Reaction` (`store/reaction.go:7`), `Inbox` (`store/inbox.go:24`), `UserSetting` (`store/user_setting.go:13`), `UserIdentity` (`store/user_identity.go:8`), `IdentityProvider` (`store/idp.go:12`), `InstanceSetting` (`store/instance_setting.go:14`).

I counted distinct Go packages outside `store/…` (so excluding the driver sub-packages `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` and `store/test`) containing a qualified `store.<Type>` reference.
