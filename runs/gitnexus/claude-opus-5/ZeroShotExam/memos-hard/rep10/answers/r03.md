**The premise doesn't hold: there is no nightly database-cleanup job registered in the cron scheduler.** No time can be cited, because no such job exists in this repo.

What actually exists:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a **standalone, unwired library** — a GitHub Actions-inspired cron scheduler (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`). Grepping for imports of `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` outside the package returns only its own tests (`internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`, `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`). Nothing in `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/` constructs a `Scheduler` or calls `Register`/`Start`, so no job of any kind is scheduled in the running application.
- The "2 AM cleanup" you may be thinking of appears only in **documentation and example code**, and it doesn't touch the database:
  - `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-17` — package doc example, `Name: "daily-cleanup"`, `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"`.
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-35` — same quick-start snippet.
  - `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-100` — `Example_multipleJobs` registers `"cleanup"` at `"0 2 * * *"` whose handler just prints "Cleaning up old data...".
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:240-246` also shows a `stale-session-cleanup` at `"0 3 * * *"` as a best-practices illustration.

The real periodic work in this codebase is ticker-based, not cron-based:

- `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:30` — re-signs S3 URLs on a fixed `runnerInterval` ticker.
- `store/cache/cache.go:201` — evicts expired entries from the **in-memory** cache on `CleanupInterval`; this is not a database cleanup.
