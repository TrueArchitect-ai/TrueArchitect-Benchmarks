## The two leaders

**`store.User`** — `server/auth` (`authenticator.go`, `context.go`), `server/notification/email.go`, `server/router/api/v1` (largest concentration: `auth_service.go`, `user_service.go`, `user_service_converters.go`, `user_service_stats.go`, …), `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss/rss.go`.

**`store.Memo`** — `server/notification/email.go`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss/rss.go`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

`User` edges out `Memo` mainly because it also crosses into the auth layer (`server/auth`) and the file server, which handle identity but not memo bodies; `Memo` uniquely reaches `server/runner/memopayload`. Both are far ahead of every other entity.
