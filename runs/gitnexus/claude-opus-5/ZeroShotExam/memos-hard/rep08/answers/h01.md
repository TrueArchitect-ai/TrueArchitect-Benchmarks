**`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — every 12 hours, plus one synchronous pass at startup — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (inside `startBackgroundRunners`, called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`)**

Details:

- **What it does** — `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, re-signs each object key via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, and writes the new URL back to the attachment's `Reference` field along with `LastPresignedTime` in the payload (`runner.go:108-123`). That `Reference` is what serves as the attachment's external link, so re-signing on a schedule is what keeps it from expiring.

- **Schedule** — `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). Within each pass, an attachment is skipped unless its presigned URL is within 1 day of expiring: URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry and refreshed only once `LastPresignedTime + 4 days` has passed (`runner.go:85-91`).

- **Wiring** — `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) builds the runner with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` (`server.go:162`), calls `RunOnce(ctx)` inline so links are refreshed immediately on boot (`server.go:163`), then launches the ticker loop on a goroutine under its own cancelable context (`server.go:156`, `server.go:167-171`). Shutdown cancels that context and waits on the WaitGroup (`server.go:140`, `server.go:143`).
