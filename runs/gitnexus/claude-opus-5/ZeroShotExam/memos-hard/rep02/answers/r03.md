**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository's cron scheduler.** The scheduler is a standalone, unused library — no job of any kind is registered by the running application.

What's actually there:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a self-contained cron library (parser, job, middleware, scheduler). Its only importers are its own tests — `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`. Nothing in `cmd/`, `server/`, or `store/` imports it, so it never runs in production.
- The "cleanup at 2 AM" you may be thinking of exists only as **documentation/example code**, and it isn't database-specific:
  - `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` — `Name: "daily-cleanup", Schedule: "0 2 * * *", // 2 AM daily`, with a `// Your cleanup logic here` placeholder handler.
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:35` — the same `"0 2 * * *"` snippet.
  - `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-100` — an `Example_multipleJobs` job named `"cleanup"` on `"0 2 * * *"` whose handler just prints `"Cleaning up old data..."` (alongside example `health-check` every 5 min and `weekly-backup` at `0 1 * * 0`).

The only real background work in the server is interval-based, not cron-scheduled: `server/runner/s3presign` runs on a 12-hour `time.Ticker` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-36`, kicked off at `server/server.go:163`), and `server/runner/memopayload` exposes a one-shot `RunOnce` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27`). Neither is a database cleanup.
