**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (with one immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162-169`**

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and regenerates a fresh presigned `GetObject` URL (stored as the attachment's `Reference`) for any whose presigned URL is within ~1 day of its 5-day expiry (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-115`). This is what keeps the externally-stored (S3) attachment links from going stale.
- **Schedule**: a `time.NewTicker(time.Hour * 12)` loop — every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-41`).
- **Wiring at startup**: in `server.go`, the runner is created and run once synchronously (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`), then launched as a background goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at `server/server.go:169`, spawned via the `go func()` at line 167).
