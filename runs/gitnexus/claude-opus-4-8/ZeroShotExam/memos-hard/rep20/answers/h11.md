**1st: `User` (~7 external packages); 2nd: `Memo` (~6 external packages)**

The persistent entity types are defined in the `store/` data layer (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). Counting distinct packages *outside* `store/` that reference each type (`store.User`, `store.Memo`, …), the two most widely referenced are:

**`store.User`** (defined in `store/user.go`) — referenced from 7 external packages:
- `server/auth`
- `server/notification`
- `server/router/api/v1`
- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/router/fileserver`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/router/rss`

**`store.Memo`** (defined in `store/memo.go`) — referenced from 6 external packages:
- `server/notification`
- `server/router/api/v1`
- `server/router/api/v1/test`
- `server/router/frontend`
- `server/router/rss`
- `server/runner/memopayload`

The ordering is stable regardless of whether the data layer's own DB-driver subpackages (`store/db/mysql|postgres|sqlite`, `store/test`) are included — `User` leads `Memo` either way (11 vs 10 with them, 7 vs 6 without). `User` edges out `Memo` chiefly because it's additionally used by the auth and fileserver packages.
