The **S3 presign runner** re-signs the pre-signed URLs that memos stores for S3-hosted (externally-stored) attachments, so those links don't expire/go stale.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` re-generates pre-signed URLs for all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments (in batches of 100).
- **Schedule:** every **12 hours** (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (line 28). It also runs once immediately at startup via `RunOnce`.
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, then `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163` and the continuous goroutine launching `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` at `server/server.go:169` (imported at `server/server.go:25`).
