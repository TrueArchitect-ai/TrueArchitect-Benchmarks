**No.** The MySQL driver is constructed only inside the driver-selection factory.

Evidence:
- The factory `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go:14` switches on `profile.Driver` and calls `mysql.NewDB(profile)` at `store/db/db.go:22` (the `"mysql"` case).
- `mysql.NewDB` is defined at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20`. The concrete driver struct `mysql.DB` (defined at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:14`) is instantiated only within that constructor: `driver := DB{profile: profile}` at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:29`.
- Grepping the whole codebase for callers/constructors confirms this is the sole site:
  - `mysql.NewDB` appears only at `store/db/db.go:22` (plus its own definition).
  - No direct struct construction (`mysql.DB{…}`, `&mysql.DB`, `new(mysql.DB)`) exists anywhere.
- The other `mysql`-named references are unrelated: `store/test/containers.go` uses a MySQL *testcontainer* (spins up a DB server, not the Go driver struct), and `internal/filter/render.go` references a `mysql` SQL dialect for filter rendering — neither constructs the `mysql.DB` driver.

So outside the `NewDBDriver` factory, the MySQL driver is never constructed directly.
