**Protection mechanism** — SSRF protection against internal/private addresses, in two layers, both in `internal/webhook/`:

- **Dial-time guard (the real enforcement):** `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines ~57–77). Installed as `http.Transport.DialContext` on the shared `safeClient`, it resolves the hostname on every dial and rejects any resolved IP in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP`. Because it checks the actually-resolved IPs at dial time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding/TOCTOU bypasses.
- **Pre-flight input validation:** `ValidateURL` — `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines ~62–83), invoked when a webhook URL is created/updated (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`). Enforces http/https and that the URL doesn't resolve to a reserved IP.

Both layers share `isReservedIP` + `reservedCIDRs` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (blocking loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud-metadata `169.254.0.0/16`, and IPv6 equivalents).

Caveat: a global `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP` to `false`, disabling the protection in both layers when enabled (intended for self-hosted setups).
