**Component** — The S3 presign runner: `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). Its `CheckAndPresign` walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry, writing the fresh URL back to the attachment's `Reference` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`). This is what keeps the stored links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from going stale.

**Schedule** — Every 12 hours, via a `time.Ticker` (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, driven by the loop in `Run` at lines 29-41). It also runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`).

**Wiring site** — Started from `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, which is invoked during `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:129`). The runner is constructed and kicked off there:
- `server/server.go:162` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`
