The background search confirmed my answer and surfaced one additional distinct surface I missed — the raw file-server path. Here's the corrected, complete list:

**Distinct memo-view enforcement sites:**

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` — central read guard** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-67`. Shared gate used by `GetMemo` (memo + its parent) and `ListMemoComments`; enforces auth for non-public and creator-only for private.

2. **`ListMemos` visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-226`. Restricts the store query (`[PUBLIC]` for anon; `creator_id == me || visibility in [PUBLIC,PROTECTED]` otherwise).

3. **`ListMemoComments` comment-listing filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:156-160`. Separate filter restricting which comment memos are returned.

4. **Attachment listing / gRPC GetAttachment** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214-223` and `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422-454` (`checkAttachmentAccess`). Gate on the parent memo's visibility.

5. **FileServer raw-blob serving** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650-704` (`checkAttachmentPermission`). A separate HTTP (echo) enforcement path serving attachment bytes, honoring memo visibility + share tokens (uses `http.StatusForbidden`, not gRPC `PermissionDenied`). *(This is the one I originally missed.)*

6. **Reactions** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-39` (list) and `:83-84` (upsert). Must be able to view the memo to see/add reactions.

7. **RSS feeds** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90,141`. Discovery restricted to `[PUBLIC]`.

8. **Frontend sitemap XML** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150`. Public-only.

The three core distinct mechanisms are the shared `checkMemoReadAccess` helper (site 1), the list-filter construction (sites 2–3), and the per-surface visibility checks for attachments/file-serving (sites 4–5); reactions, RSS, and sitemap (6–8) re-apply the same visibility rule on their respective surfaces.

Not view-gating (noted for completeness): `memo_mention_helpers.go` and `user_service_notifications.go` gate notification *emission/content* by visibility, not read access.
