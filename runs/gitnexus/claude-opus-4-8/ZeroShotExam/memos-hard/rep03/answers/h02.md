**Delivery function** — `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (executes the actual outbound `safeClient.Do(req)` on line 152). It is invoked either synchronously or via the async worker pool draining `asyncPostQueue` in `init()`/`PostAsync` (same file).

**Protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext` (lines 32–37). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` → `reservedNetworks` defined in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44` (CIDR list at `validate.go:14`). Because the check runs at connection time on the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding.

Notes:
- A second, earlier check exists at webhook create/update time: `ValidateURL` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`, which also calls `isReservedIP`. But the dial-time `safeDialContext` is the guard that actually protects the outbound delivery itself.
- Both checks are bypassable by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`), intended for self-hosted deployments targeting local services.
