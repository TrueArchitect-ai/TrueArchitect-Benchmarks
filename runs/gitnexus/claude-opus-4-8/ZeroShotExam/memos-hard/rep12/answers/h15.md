- **User stats aggregation** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:84` (`ListAllUserStats`) and `:218` (`GetUserStats`): same anonymous→`[Public]`, other-user→`[Public, Protected]` restriction as ListMemos.
- **List attachments (API)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214` (`ListMemoAttachments`): gates on parent memo visibility (distinct from `checkAttachmentAccess`).
- **Reactions (list)** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30` (`ListMemoReactions`), alongside the upsert gate at `:83` I already listed.
- **Notification/email viewer-access predicates** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302` (`canViewerAccessMemo`), `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62` (`canUserAccessMentionContext`), and the duplicate in `server/notification/email.go:306`: decide whether a mentioned/commented user may be notified based on the memo's visibility.

Load-bearing helper (not itself a site): `isSuperUser` — `server/router/api/v1/common.go:77`, the admin bypass most PRIVATE checks call. Visibility constants live in `store/memo.go:16`; the gRPC↔store mapping in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go` is conversion only, not enforcement.

The three central sites remain: `checkMemoReadAccess`/GetMemo (memo_service.go:39), the ListMemos `VisibilityList` filter backed by the store SQL builders (memo_service.go:218 → `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`), and the attachment gates (attachment_service.go:422, fileserver.go:650).
