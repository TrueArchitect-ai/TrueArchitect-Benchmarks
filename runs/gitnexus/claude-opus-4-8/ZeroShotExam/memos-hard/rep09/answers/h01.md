The **S3 presign runner** keeps external attachment links from going stale. S3-stored attachments are served via presigned URLs that expire (set to 5 days). This runner re-presigns them before expiry, refreshing each attachment's `Reference` (the external link) and `LastPresignedTime`.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (the `Run`/`CheckAndPresign` loop; it re-presigns any S3 attachment whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry).
- **Schedule:** every **12 hours** — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (lines 29–41). It also runs once immediately at startup via `RunOnce`.
- **Wiring site:** started in `server.go` — the runner is created and kicked off once at `server/server.go:162-163`, then launched as a continuous background goroutine at `server/server.go:169`.

**Answer:** `s3presign.Runner` (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired at `server/server.go:162` (created + `RunOnce`) and `server/server.go:169` (continuous `Run` goroutine).
