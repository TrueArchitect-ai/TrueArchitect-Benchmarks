**No.** The MySQL driver is never constructed directly anywhere outside the driver-selection factory.

Evidence — a whole-repo grep (including `*_test.go`) for `mysql.NewDB`, `&mysql.DB{}`, `mysql.DB{`, `mysql.NewDriver`, and imports of `store/db/mysql` yields exactly two hits, both in `store/db/db.go`:
- `store/db/db.go:8` — the import of the `store/db/mysql` package
- `store/db/db.go:22` — `driver, err = mysql.NewDB(profile)`, inside the factory `NewDBDriver` (`store/db/db.go:14`), in the switch case on `profile.Driver`

The constructor is `mysql.NewDB` at `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20`; its internal struct literal `DB{profile: profile}` (`mysql.go:29`) is the constructor's own body, not an external construction. No other package — production, test, or tooling — imports the mysql package or constructs the driver, so it is obtained exclusively through the `NewDBDriver` factory.
