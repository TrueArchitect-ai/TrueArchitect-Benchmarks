Counting distinct packages that reference each type from **outside** the data layer (excluding the entire `store/` tree — its `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` drivers, `store/cache`, `store/test`, etc.):

- **`store.User` — 7 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo` — 6 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

The remaining entity types trail well behind (`Attachment` ~4, `Inbox`/`MemoShare`/`MemoRelation` ~3, `Reaction` ~2). Counts are approximate — they depend on whether you treat `server/router/api/v1/test` as distinct from `server/router/api/v1`; either way the ranking (User first, Memo second) is unchanged.
