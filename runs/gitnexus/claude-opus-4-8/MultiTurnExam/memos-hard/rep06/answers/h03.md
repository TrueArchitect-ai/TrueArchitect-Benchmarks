**Abstraction package: `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp` (`internal/idp`); implementation package: `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/idp/oauth2` (`internal/idp/oauth2`)**

- The abstraction lives in `internal/idp/idp.go`, which defines the shared normalized `IdentityProviderUserInfo` type (Identifier, DisplayName, Email, AvatarURL) that concrete providers return. (Note: there is no `plugin/idp` directory; the abstraction is under `internal/idp`.)
- The one concrete protocol that ships is OAuth2, in `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` — with `NewIdentityProvider(*storepb.OAuth2Config)`, `ExchangeToken`, and `UserInfo(...)` returning `idp.IdentityProviderUserInfo`. No separate OIDC or SAML packages exist under `internal/idp`.
