**Protection mechanism:** `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, installed as the `DialContext` of the shared `safeClient`'s transport (`webhook.go:32-37`). At dial time it resolves the target hostname and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (defined in `internal/webhook/validate.go`). Because the check runs on the *resolved* IP at connect time rather than on the URL string, it also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks.
