**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — a ConnectRPC `Interceptor` that sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>` (`setAuthorizationHeader`, line 97-99), with reactive 401 refresh; access-token state lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts` and the transport uses `credentials: "include"` (line 60) so the refresh cookie is sent.

**Token kinds:** Two JWTs — a short-lived **access token** (~15 min), held in the frontend and attached as the `Bearer` header, and a long-lived **refresh token** (~30 days), carried in an HTTP-only cookie and exchanged via the `AuthService.RefreshToken` RPC (`doRefreshAccessToken`, `web/src/connect.ts:74-82`). Defined in `server/auth/token.go:8-9`.

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` — JWTs are parsed/validated with `golang-jwt/jwt/v5` in `server/auth/token.go` and enforced by `server/auth/authenticator.go`.
