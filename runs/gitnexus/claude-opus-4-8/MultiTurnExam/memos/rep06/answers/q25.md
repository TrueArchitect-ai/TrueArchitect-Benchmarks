**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, living in `internal/filter/` — the environment/parser is in `internal/filter/engine.go` (built on `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`), with the SQL renderer in `internal/filter/render.go`.

**Mechanism (one sentence):** Each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`) obtains `filter.DefaultEngine()` and calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.DialectSQLite/DialectPostgres/DialectMySQL, &where, &args)`, which parses the CEL filter expression into an IR and the renderer emits dialect-specific SQL fragments plus bind args (choosing per-dialect column qualification, placeholders, and date/time functions like `strftime` vs Postgres/MySQL equivalents) that are appended to the query's `WHERE` clause.
