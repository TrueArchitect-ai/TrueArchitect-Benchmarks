**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` (the Connect auth `Interceptor` that sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>` and reactively refreshes on 401; token state helpers live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`).

**Token kinds:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT, sent in the `Authorization: Bearer` header) and a **refresh token** (delivered via HttpOnly cookie with `credentials: "include"`, used by the `refreshToken` RPC to mint new access tokens).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — the `Authenticator` (`server/auth/authenticator.go`) validates access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`) and refresh tokens (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), with header/cookie extraction in `server/auth/extract.go`.
