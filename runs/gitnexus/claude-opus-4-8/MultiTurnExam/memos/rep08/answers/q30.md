**Events → dispatch functions** (in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`):
- created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

**Convergence for delivery:** The first three funnel through the private helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (same file), while `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` builds its payload inline; all four ultimately call **`webhook.PostAsync`** in package `internal/webhook` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`) to perform the actual HTTP delivery.
