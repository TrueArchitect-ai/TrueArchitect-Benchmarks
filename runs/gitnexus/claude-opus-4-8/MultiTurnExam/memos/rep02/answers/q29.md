- **Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (line 156) sets the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header and reactively refreshes on `Unauthenticated`.
- **Token kinds it manages:** short-lived **JWT access tokens** (sent as `Bearer`, 15 min) and long-lived **JWT refresh tokens** (30 days, sent with credentials/cookie via the `refreshToken` call) — plus the backend also supports **Personal Access Tokens (PAT)** for programmatic use.
- **Backend validation package:** `server/auth` (JWT validation/claims in `server/auth/token.go` and `authenticator.go`), invoked from the Connect auth interceptor `NewAuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`.
