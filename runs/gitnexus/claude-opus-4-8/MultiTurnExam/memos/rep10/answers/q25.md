**Engine:** Google CEL (`cel-go`) — `internal/filter/` (the `Engine` in `internal/filter/engine.go`, which builds a `cel.NewEnv` from the memo `Schema` in `internal/filter/schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** The filter string is parsed/compiled by CEL into a dialect-agnostic condition tree/IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`, `parser.go`), then `Program.Render`/`CompileToStatement` (`engine.go`) invokes the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`), which emits dialect-specific SQL fragments plus bind args — switching on `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, and `DialectMySQL` for placeholder style and JSON/timestamp handling — that each dialect driver (`store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`) splices into its `WHERE` clause.
