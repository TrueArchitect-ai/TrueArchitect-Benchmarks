**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (defined at line 156, wired into the main transport's `interceptors: [authInterceptor]` at line 188). It sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>` on each request and reactively refreshes on 401; helper token state lives in `web/src/auth-state.ts`.

**Token kinds:** a short-lived **JWT access token** (Bearer, held client-side, ~15 min) and a **refresh token** (kept in an HTTP-only cookie, sent via `credentials: "include"` and rotated through the `refreshToken` RPC on a separate no-interceptor transport, `connect.ts:60-82`).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` — `authenticator.go` (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` validates the JWT access token; `AuthenticateByRefreshToken` validates the refresh token against the DB), with helpers in `server/auth/extract.go` (`ExtractBearerToken`, `ExtractRefreshTokenFromCookie`) and `server/auth/token.go`.
