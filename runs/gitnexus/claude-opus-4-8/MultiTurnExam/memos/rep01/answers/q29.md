**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — a ConnectRPC `Interceptor` that attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` (line 99) and reactively refreshes on 401; token storage helpers live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`.

**Token kinds:** Two JWTs — a short-lived **access token** (~15 min, sent as an `Authorization: Bearer` header) and a long-lived **refresh token** (~30 days, delivered via an HTTP-only cookie, used by `refreshToken` to mint new access tokens) — see `server/auth/token.go:8-9` and `server/auth/extract.go` (`ExtractBearerToken`, `ExtractRefreshTokenFromCookie`).

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` (`server/auth/authenticator.go`, using `golang-jwt/jwt/v5` in `server/auth/token.go`; header/cookie extraction in `server/auth/extract.go`).
