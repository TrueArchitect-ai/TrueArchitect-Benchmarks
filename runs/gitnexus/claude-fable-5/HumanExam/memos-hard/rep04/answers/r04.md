— False premise: there is no message broker; neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor AMQP/Kafka) is used. Webhook delivery is handled in-process by a bounded Go channel `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 worker goroutines (`init`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-51`) that call `Post`.
