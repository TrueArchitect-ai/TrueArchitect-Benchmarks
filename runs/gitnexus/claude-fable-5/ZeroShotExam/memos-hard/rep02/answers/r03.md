- The actual background jobs the server runs live in `server/runner/` and don't use the cron scheduler: the S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) runs on a plain `time.Ticker` every 12 hours, and the memo payload runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) runs once at startup.
