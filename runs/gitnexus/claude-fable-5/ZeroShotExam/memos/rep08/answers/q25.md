**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `cel-go`. **Path:** `internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`).

**Mechanism:** The filter string from `ListMemos` (validated in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` and appended to `memoFind.Filters`) goes through a three-phase pipeline — cel-go parses the expression against a memo-specific environment (`internal/filter/schema.go`), `internal/filter/parser.go` converts the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args per dialect (`?` for SQLite/MySQL, `$n` for Postgres, with dialect-specific JSON access and tag predicates like `LIKE` vs `JSON_CONTAINS` vs `@>`).

Each store driver invokes it via `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect: `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`.
