**Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`**

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` is registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, which sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data:` frames, and sends `: heartbeat` comments every 30 seconds. The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` is implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`). Each SSE connection subscribes to the hub in `handleSSE` (`sse_handler.go:60`).
- **Handlers that broadcast through it:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `memo_service.go:163` (`SSEEventMemoCreated`; suppressed when invoked from CreateMemoComment)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `memo_service.go:585` (`SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) all broadcast indirectly via the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go:71`
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` (`reaction_service.go:106`) and `DeleteMemoReaction` (`reaction_service.go:161`) broadcast reaction events tied to a memo
