The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and exports the clients for all services — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
