The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It builds a Connect transport with `createConnectTransport` (plus an auth interceptor) and then calls `createClient(...)` from `@connectrpc/connect` to export all the service clients — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
