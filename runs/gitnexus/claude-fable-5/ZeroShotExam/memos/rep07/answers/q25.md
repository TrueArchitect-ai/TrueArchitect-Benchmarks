**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language), via `github.com/google/cel-go`. The engine lives in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`, `helpers.go`).

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`) passes the filter strings into `MemoFind.Filters`; each store driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, and the mysql/postgres counterparts) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectSQLite/MySQL/Postgres, &where, &args)`, where the engine compiles the string against a schema-restricted CEL environment (`Engine.Compile` in `internal/filter/engine.go`), converts the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR tree (`parser.go`/`ir.go`), and a per-dialect renderer (`render.go`) then emits parameterized SQL WHERE fragments with dialect-specific placeholders and operators (e.g. `ILIKE`/`~` and `$N` for Postgres, `LIKE`/`REGEXP` with `?` for SQLite/MySQL) that are appended to the driver's query.
