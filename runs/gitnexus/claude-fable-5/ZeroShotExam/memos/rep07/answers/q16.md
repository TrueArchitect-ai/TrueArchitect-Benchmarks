When `Store.DeleteMemo` runs (called from the API's memo service), it deletes these records before removing the memo row itself (`store/memo.go:140-159`):

- **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation`) — two passes: relations where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and relations where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`), via `driver.DeleteMemoRelation` (`store/memo.go:142-147`, defined in `store/memo_relation.go`)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — it lists attachments with `FindAttachment{MemoID}` and deletes each one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`)

Only after that does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row itself; the driver-level delete is a plain `DELETE FROM memo` with no further cascades (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:237`).
