q18.

The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface, with their entry files:

- SQLite — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- MySQL — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- PostgreSQL — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them at startup is **`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go` (lines 14–32). It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) and calls the corresponding package's `NewDB(profile)`, returning an error for any unknown driver.
