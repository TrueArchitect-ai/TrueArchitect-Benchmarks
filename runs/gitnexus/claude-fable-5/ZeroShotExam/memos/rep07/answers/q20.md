Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting memo handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`), `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`), and via the shared `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`) also `SetMemoAttachments` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`) and `SetMemoRelations` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`).

Details: the HTTP endpoint that streams to browsers sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48`, and handlers push through `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(&SSEEvent{...})`. Reaction handlers (`UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`) also broadcast memo-scoped events, if you count them among the memo-related handlers.
