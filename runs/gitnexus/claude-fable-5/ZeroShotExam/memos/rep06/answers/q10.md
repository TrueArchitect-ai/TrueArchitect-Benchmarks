`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

For an uninitialized database, the migrator applies the full schema from `LATEST.sql` instead of running incremental migrations (see `store/migrator.go:27` and `store/migrator.go:56`, confirmed by `store/test/migrator_test.go`). The SQLite variant of that file is `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`.
