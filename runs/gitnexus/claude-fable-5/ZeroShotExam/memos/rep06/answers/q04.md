The SignIn RPC is handled by **`APIV1Service.SignIn`** — `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go` (lines 50–107).

This is the actual business-logic implementation on the backend. The surrounding plumbing is generated code: `_AuthService_SignIn_Handler` in `proto/gen/api/v1/auth_service_grpc.pb.go` is the gRPC dispatch stub, and `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/auth_service.connect.go` holds the Connect handler interface, both of which route the call to `APIV1Service.SignIn`.
