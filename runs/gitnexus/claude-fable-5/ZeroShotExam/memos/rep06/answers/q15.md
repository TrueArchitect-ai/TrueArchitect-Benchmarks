The Go structs in the `store` package are the domain models scanned from those tables (the per-driver code in `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}` runs `SELECT ... FROM user/attachment/reaction` and scans rows into them):

- `user`: `User` — `store/user.go` (declared at `store/user.go:29`)
- `attachment`: `Attachment` — `store/attachment.go` (declared at `store/attachment.go:16`)
- `reaction`: `Reaction` — `store/reaction.go` (declared at `store/reaction.go:7`)

For example, `store/db/sqlite/user.go:164` selects `FROM user` into `store.User`, and `store/db/sqlite/reaction.go:60` selects `FROM reaction` into `store.Reaction`; the same pattern holds for `attachment` in `store/db/sqlite/attachment.go` and the MySQL/Postgres drivers.
