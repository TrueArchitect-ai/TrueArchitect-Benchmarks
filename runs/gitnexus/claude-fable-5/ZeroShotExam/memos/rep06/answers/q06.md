**`memo_relation`**

The table is defined in the schema files for all three supported databases — `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`, and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46` — and was introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`.
