**SSE (Server-Sent Events); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction)**

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data:` frames, and sends heartbeat comments every 30 seconds. The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` is implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast`). Each SSE connection subscribes a client to the hub in `sse_handler.go`.
- **Handlers that broadcast:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo` (memo_service.go:513), `SetMemoRelations` (memo_relation_service.go:48), and `SetMemoAttachments` (memo_attachment_service.go:48) broadcast indirectly through the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`
  - Reaction handlers also broadcast memo events: `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161`
