The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface live under `store/db/`, each with an entry file defining its `DB` type and `NewDB` constructor:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them at startup is **`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go` (`store/db/db.go:14`). It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) and calls the corresponding package's `NewDB`, returning an error for any unknown driver.
