The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface and the factory that picks one:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver (`NewDB`)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver (`NewDB`)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL driver (`NewDB`)

Factory: `NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (line 14), which switches on the profile's configured driver at startup and returns the matching `store.Driver` implementation.
