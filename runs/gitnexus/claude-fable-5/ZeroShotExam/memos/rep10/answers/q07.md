The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It calls `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` to build and export all the service clients (e.g., `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`) over a shared transport.
