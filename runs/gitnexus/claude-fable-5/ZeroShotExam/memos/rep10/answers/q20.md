**Mechanism; hub path; handler names:** Server-Sent Events (SSE, `text/event-stream` over HTTP); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`.

Details:

- **Mechanism** — Server-Sent Events. The endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes`/`handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, and the browser consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (which handles `SSEChangeEvent`s to trigger live memo refresh).
- **Hub** — `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (created by `NewSSEHub`, with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`), instantiated in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` and wired into the server in `server/server.go`.
- **Broadcasters** (all on `APIV1Service`):
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (`SSEEventMemoCreated`, suppressed when invoked from comment creation)
  - `UpdateMemo` — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoUpdated` through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (`SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (`SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`)
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` / `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161` (`SSEEventReactionUpserted` / `SSEEventReactionDeleted`)
