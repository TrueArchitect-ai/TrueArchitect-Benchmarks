MemoService is exposed over two protocols:

1. **REST (HTTP/JSON via gRPC-Gateway)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers it on the gateway mux served under `/api/v1/*`).
2. **Connect RPC** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`; the Connect request/response wrapping of the underlying service methods lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`).

Both routes share the same underlying `APIV1Service` implementation — the Connect handler is a thin adapter over the gRPC-style service methods, as the comment in `connect_handler.go` notes ("Support both native gRPC and Connect protocols").
