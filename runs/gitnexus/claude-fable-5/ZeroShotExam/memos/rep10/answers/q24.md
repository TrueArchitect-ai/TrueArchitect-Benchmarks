- `store/memo.go` — `(s *Store) CreateMemo` (line 109) validates the UID and delegates to `s.driver.CreateMemo`
- `store/db/sqlite/memo.go` — `(d *DB) CreateMemo` (line 16) builds and executes the `INSERT INTO memo ... RETURNING ...` statement (line 41)
