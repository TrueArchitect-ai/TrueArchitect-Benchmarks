**Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`; it lives in `internal/filter/`** (`engine.go`, `schema.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`).

Mechanism: the ListMemos handler (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-215`) validates the filter string and passes it into the store's `Filters`; each dialect driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, and the mysql/postgres counterparts) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` + `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect. Inside the package, a three-phase pipeline runs: `cel-go` parses the expression against a memo-specific CEL environment declared in `schema.go`, `parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`ir.go`), and `render.go` walks that IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args tailored to SQLite, MySQL, or Postgres — encapsulating dialect differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, JSON access (`json_extract` / `JSON_EXTRACT` / `->>`), boolean coercion, and tag-array predicates (`LIKE` on SQLite, `JSON_CONTAINS` on MySQL, `@>` on Postgres), per the package's own `internal/filter/README.md`.
