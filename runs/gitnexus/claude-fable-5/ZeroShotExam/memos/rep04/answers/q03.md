**DB — store/db/postgres/postgres.go**

The PostgreSQL driver is the `DB` struct defined at `store/db/postgres/postgres.go:16` (in package `postgres`). Its constructor `NewDB` returns it as a `store.Driver` (`store/db/postgres/postgres.go:33`), which is the database driver interface.
