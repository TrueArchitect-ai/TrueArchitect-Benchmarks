MemoService is served over **gRPC-Gateway (RESTful HTTP/JSON)** and **Connect RPC** (the browser-facing protocol that replaced grpc-web). There is no standalone gRPC server registration — the gateway registers the service implementation directly.

- **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/REST)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, mounted under `/api/v1/*`)
- **Connect RPC** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))`, mounted under `/memos.api.v1.*`; the Connect-side service adapter methods live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`)
