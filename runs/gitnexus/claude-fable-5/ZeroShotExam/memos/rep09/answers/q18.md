The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface live under `store/db/`, each with an entry file defining its `NewDB` constructor:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go` (line 14). It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) at startup and calls the matching package's `NewDB` to construct the driver.
