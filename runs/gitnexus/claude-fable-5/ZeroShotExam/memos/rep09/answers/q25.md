**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`. **Path:** the filter engine lives in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` validates the filter and appends it to `MemoFind.Filters` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-223`); each store driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, plus the mysql/postgres equivalents) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect — the engine compiles the CEL expression against a schema-defined CEL environment (`Engine.Compile` in `internal/filter/engine.go`), converts the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (IR in `internal/filter/ir.go`/`parser.go`), and a per-dialect `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`, switching on `DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`) turns that tree into a parameterized SQL `WHERE` fragment with placeholder args for each backend.
