The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It builds a Connect transport with `createConnectTransport` and then exports the clients via `createClient` — for example `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:184-203`).
