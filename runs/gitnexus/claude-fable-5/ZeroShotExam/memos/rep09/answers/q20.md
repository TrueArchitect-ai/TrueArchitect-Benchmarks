Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting memo handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, plus `SetMemoAttachments`/`SetMemoRelations` (also via the helper) and the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction`.

Details:

- **Mechanism** — Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data:` frames, and sends `: heartbeat` comments every 30 s. The browser side connects from `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
- **Hub** — `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast`, `Close`). It is created in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` and wired into the server in `server/server.go`; routes are registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` in `sse_handler.go`.
- **Handlers that call `SSEHub.Broadcast`**:
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations` — indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`)
  - `UpsertMemoReaction` / `DeleteMemoReaction` — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106` and `:161` (memo-scoped reaction events)
