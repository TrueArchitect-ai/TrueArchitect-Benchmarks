The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface, each with its entry file defining the driver struct and its `NewDB` constructor:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them at startup is **`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go` (line 14). It switches on `profile.Driver` ("sqlite" / "mysql" / "postgres") and calls the matching package's `NewDB(profile)`, returning an error for any unknown driver.
