In `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`), before calling the driver's `DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row itself, the store layer deletes:

- **Memo relation records** — two passes over the `memo_relation` table: first where the memo is the source (`MemoID`), then where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142-147`)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — it lists attachments via `ListAttachments({MemoID})` and deletes each one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`)

The driver-level `DeleteMemo` (e.g., `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:237`) then just deletes the single `memo` row with no further cascades.
