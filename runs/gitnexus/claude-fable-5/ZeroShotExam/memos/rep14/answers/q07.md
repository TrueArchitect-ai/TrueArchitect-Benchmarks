The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It builds a Connect-Web transport with `createConnectTransport` and then creates all the service clients via `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (lines 184–203), plus a separate `refreshAuthClient` for token refresh (line 72).
