**Engine:** Google CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by memos' own filter engine at `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** The `ListMemos` handler passes the request's filter string through to the store's `FindMemo.Filters`; each dialect driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, and the equivalents in `store/db/mysql` and `store/db/postgres`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect<X>, &where, &args)` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`). Inside the engine, `Engine.Compile` (`internal/filter/engine.go`) compiles the CEL expression against a schema-defined CEL environment, converts it to a parsed AST, and the parser (`internal/filter/parser.go`) lowers it into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (IR in `internal/filter/ir.go`). A renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) then walks that tree with a `DialectName` (SQLite/MySQL/Postgres) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments — switching on the dialect for things like placeholder style, JSON-extraction expressions, and string-length functions — plus bound arguments that get appended to the query's WHERE clause.
