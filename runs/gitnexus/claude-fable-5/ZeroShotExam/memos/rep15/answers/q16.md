In `Store.DeleteMemo` (/bench/home/work/store/memo.go:140-159), before deleting the memo row itself the store layer removes:

- **Memo relation records** — two passes over the `memo_relation` table: rows where the memo is the source (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: ...}`) and rows where it is the target of a relation (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: ...}`).
- **Attachment records linked to the memo** — it lists attachments with `FindAttachment{MemoID: ...}` and deletes each via `DeleteAttachment` (see /bench/home/work/store/attachment.go for that logic).

Only after those cleanups does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row.
