**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). The browser subscribes via `EventSource` (consumed by `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`), and the server streams events from the endpoint registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct with its `Broadcast` method (plus `Close`, wired into server shutdown in `server/server.go`).

**Memo handlers that broadcast through it:**
- `CreateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`, emits `SSEEventMemoCreated`)
- `UpdateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:513` via the helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`, emits `SSEEventMemoUpdated`)
- `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`, emits `SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`, emits `SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`)
- `SetMemoRelations` and `SetMemoAttachments` also broadcast memo-updated events through the same `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper (`memo_relation_service.go:48`, `memo_attachment_service.go:48`)
- Additionally, the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (lines 106, 161) broadcast memo reaction events through the hub.

**Format summary:** Server-Sent Events; `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment` (plus `SetMemoRelations`/`SetMemoAttachments` and the reaction handlers).
