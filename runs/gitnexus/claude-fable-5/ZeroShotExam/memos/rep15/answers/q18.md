The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface, each with its entry file defining `NewDB`:

- SQLite — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- MySQL — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- PostgreSQL — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory: **NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (line 14). It switches on the profile's driver setting and calls the matching package's `NewDB` (`sqlite.NewDB`, `mysql.NewDB`, or `postgres.NewDB`) at startup.
