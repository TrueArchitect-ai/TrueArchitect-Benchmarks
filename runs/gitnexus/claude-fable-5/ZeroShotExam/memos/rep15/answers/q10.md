`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

On a fresh (uninitialized) database, the migrator applies this single file rather than replaying incremental migrations — `store/migrator.go` documents it as "Full schema for new installations" and applies it in the preMigrate step (see `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` at `store/migrator.go:56`, verified by `TestFreshInstall` in `store/test/migrator_test.go`). The versioned directories under `store/migration/sqlite/0.x/` are only for upgrading existing databases.
