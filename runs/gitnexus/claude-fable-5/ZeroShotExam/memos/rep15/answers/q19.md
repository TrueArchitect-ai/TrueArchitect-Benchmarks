MemoService is served over the **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP-JSON)** protocol and the **Connect RPC** protocol. (There is no standalone native gRPC listener — the Connect handlers replaced grpc-web, and the gateway calls the service implementation in-process.)

- **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/JSON REST under `/api/v1/*`)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 108: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)`, mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*`)
- **Connect RPC (under `/memos.api.v1.*`)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go` (line 40: `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), with the per-method Connect wrappers that adapt the gRPC implementation living in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService section around line 304). The Connect mux is mounted on Echo in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (lines 139–144).

Both paths delegate to the same `APIV1Service` implementation, whose generated protocol plumbing is in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go` (gateway) and `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/` (Connect).
