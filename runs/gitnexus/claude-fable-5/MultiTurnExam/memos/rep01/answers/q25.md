Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`) — internal/filter

Mechanism: the filter engine runs a three-phase pipeline — `parser.go` parses and validates the CEL expression against the memo schema declared in `schema.go`, converts it to a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation defined in `ir.go`, and then `render.go` walks that IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args tailored to the target dialect (handling differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>` JSON access, and `LIKE` vs `JSON_CONTAINS` vs `@>` for tags). Each dialect driver's `ListMemos` calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its own dialect constant — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` passes `filter.DialectSQLite`, with equivalents in `store/db/mysql/memo.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo.go` — appending the rendered conditions to the query's WHERE clause. The pipeline is documented in `internal/filter/README.md`, with the glue entry points in `internal/filter/engine.go` and `internal/filter/helpers.go`.
