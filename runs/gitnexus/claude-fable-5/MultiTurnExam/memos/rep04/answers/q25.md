**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), via `github.com/google/cel-go` (`go.mod:13`), wrapped in the project's filter engine at **`internal/filter/`** — `internal/filter/engine.go` builds a `cel.Env` against a memo schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`) and compiles filter strings to a CEL AST.

**Mechanism:** the engine parses the filter into a CEL AST, `internal/filter/parser.go` lowers that AST into a dialect-neutral condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` renders the IR into parameterized SQL WHERE fragments with per-dialect variations (`DialectSQLite` / `DialectPostgres` / `DialectMySQL` — e.g. different timestamp and string-length expressions); each driver's `ListMemos` calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectSQLite, &where, &args)` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, with mysql/postgres equivalents in `store/db/mysql/memo.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`) to append the rendered conditions to its query.
