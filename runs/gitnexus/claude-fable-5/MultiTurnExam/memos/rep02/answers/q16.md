Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes:

1. **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation` table) — both where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`).
2. **Attachments** linked to the memo — it lists attachments with `MemoID` and deletes each via `Store.DeleteAttachment` (`store/attachment.go`).

Only after those cleanups does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row.
