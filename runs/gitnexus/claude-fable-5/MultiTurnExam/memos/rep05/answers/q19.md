MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **Connect (ConnectRPC) — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`**: `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, ...)` on the HTTP mux (line 40). The Connect wrapper methods that adapt the service live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`.

2. **REST via gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (line 108) registers the service on a grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux`, exposed under `/api/v1/*` per the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

Both protocols share a single implementation, `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` — the Connect handler is just a thin wrapper over it.
