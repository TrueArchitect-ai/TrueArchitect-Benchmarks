**Delivery function: `Post` (with `PostAsync` enqueuing to a bounded worker queue that calls it) — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism: dial-time SSRF guard `safeDialContext` + `isReservedIP` on the shared `safeClient` transport (plus creation-time `ValidateURL`) — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` and `internal/webhook/validate.go`.**

Details:

- Outbound delivery executes in `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`, which marshals the payload, optionally adds a Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signature, and sends via `safeClient.Do(req)` (`webhook.go:152`). Callers usually go through `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`). Memo-change events fan out to it from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`.
- The SSRF protection lives in the HTTP client's transport: `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`) re-resolves the hostname at dial time and refuses to connect if any resolved IP falls in a reserved range — loopback, RFC-1918 private, link-local/cloud IMDS (169.254.0.0/16), and IPv6 equivalents, per `isReservedIP` and `reservedCIDRs` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`. Checking at dial time (not just at URL-save time) also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks.
- As a first line of defense, `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects non-http(s) schemes and URLs resolving to reserved IPs when a webhook is created/updated. The guard can be deliberately disabled for self-hosted LAN setups via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`validate.go:41`).
