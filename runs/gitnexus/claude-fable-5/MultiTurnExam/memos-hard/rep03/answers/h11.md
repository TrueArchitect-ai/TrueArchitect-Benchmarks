**1st: `store.User` (~7 outside packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~6 outside packages).**

Method: the persistent entities are the structs in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). I grepped for references to each `store.<Type>` across all Go code and counted distinct packages outside the data layer (excluding `store/`, `store/db/*`, `store/test`):

- **`store.User` — 7 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo` — 6 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

(If you exclude the test package `server/router/api/v1/test`, the counts are 6 vs 5 — the ranking is unchanged.)

Runners-up for context: `store.Attachment` ~4 outside packages (`server/router/api/v1`, its test package, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/runner/s3presign`), and other entities like `MemoRelation`, `Inbox`, and `Reaction` are essentially confined to `server/router/api/v1` (+ tests). So `User` is the most widely shared data-layer entity, with `Memo` second.
