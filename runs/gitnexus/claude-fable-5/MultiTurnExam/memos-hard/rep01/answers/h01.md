**s3presign background runner (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate run at startup — wired in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171` (called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`)**

Details: memos can store attachment blobs in S3 (external storage, `AttachmentStorageType_S3`). Access to those blobs is via presigned URLs, which expire. The runner's `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-generates their presigned URLs so the external links never go stale. On startup, `startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner, calls `RunOnce` immediately, then launches `Run` in a goroutine, which ticks every 12 hours until the server's shutdown context cancels it.
