**No.** The MySQL driver is constructed only inside the driver-selection factory.

- The factory is `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go`, which switches on `profile.Driver` and calls `mysql.NewDB(profile)` at `store/db/db.go:22` for the `"mysql"` case.
- A repo-wide grep for calls to the constructor (`mysql.NewDB`) finds exactly two occurrences in Go code: the call site in the factory (`store/db/db.go:22`) and the definition itself (`store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20`).
