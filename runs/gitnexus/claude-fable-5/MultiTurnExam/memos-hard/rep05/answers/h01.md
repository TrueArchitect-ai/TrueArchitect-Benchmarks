The S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps attachment links fresh: for attachments stored in S3, it re-generates presigned URLs before they expire (`CheckAndPresign` walks S3-type attachments in batches of 100 and refreshes them).

**Component** — `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
**Schedule** — every 12 hours via a ticker (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-27`), plus one immediate `RunOnce` at startup
**Wiring site** — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:161-170` (runner created at line 162, run once at 163, and launched as a background goroutine at 166-170), which is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`
