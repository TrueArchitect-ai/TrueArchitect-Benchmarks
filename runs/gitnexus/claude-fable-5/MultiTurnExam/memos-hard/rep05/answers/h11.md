**1st: `store.User` — ~9 packages; 2nd: `store.Memo` — ~7 packages.**

Method: I enumerated the persistent entity structs defined in the data layer (`store/*.go` — `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `IdentityProvider`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `UserIdentity`, `InstanceSetting`, `UserSetting`, …), then counted the distinct Go packages outside `store/` itself whose non-test files reference each type as `store.<Type>`.

Results (distinct external packages):

- **`User` — 9**: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, plus the three driver packages `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}`. (Type defined in `store/user.go`.)
- **`Memo` — 7**: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`, plus the three driver packages. (Defined in `store/memo.go`.)
- Next tier: `Attachment` — 6 (`store/attachment.go`), `Inbox` and `MemoShare` — 5 each; everything else 4 or fewer.

The ranking is robust to how you treat the `store/db/*` driver packages: if you consider them part of the data layer and exclude them, it's still `User` first (6 packages) and `Memo` second (4 packages, with `Attachment` at 3).
