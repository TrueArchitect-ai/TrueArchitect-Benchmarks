Memo visibility is enforced independently at (at least) five distinct server surfaces:

- **gRPC/API memo read access** — `APIV1Service.checkMemoReadAccess` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39` (public passes; otherwise requires auth; `Private` requires creator) — used by `GetMemo`; list endpoints additionally inject a visibility filter into the query (`creator_id == X || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`) at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:222` and `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:160`.
- **File/attachment serving** — `FileServerService.checkAttachmentPermission` at `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650` (public + anonymous-allowed passes at line 677; share-token fallback; otherwise auth required, and `Private` restricted to creator or admin at line 699) — gates raw attachment downloads for a memo.
- **RSS feeds** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` and `:141`: feed queries hard-restrict to `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`, so protected/private memos never enter a feed.
- **SSE real-time delivery** — `SSEClient.canReceive` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134`: before pushing a memo event to a connected client, `Private` events are delivered only to the creator or an admin (unknown visibility is denied); event visibility is attached in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go:29-37`.
- **Reactions and comments on memos** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:38` and `:83` (can't list/create reactions on a private memo unless creator or superuser) and `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41` (can't comment on a private memo unless creator or superuser).

Each surface re-checks visibility itself rather than relying on a single shared gate, which is why the rule appears in all five places.
