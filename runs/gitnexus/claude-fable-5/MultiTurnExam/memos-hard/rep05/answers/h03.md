**Abstraction package** — `internal/idp` (`internal/idp/idp.go`): defines the provider-neutral types, notably `IdentityProviderUserInfo` (identifier, display name, email, avatar), which is what any concrete provider must produce after authenticating a user.

**Implementation package** — `internal/idp/oauth2` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`): the one concrete protocol implementation that ships, an OAuth 2.0 provider. Its `IdentityProvider` type is constructed from a `storepb.OAuth2Config` via `NewIdentityProvider` and handles the token exchange and user-info retrieval, returning the shared `idp.IdentityProviderUserInfo`. It's consumed by the sign-in flow in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service.go` and provider management in `server/router/api/v1/idp_service.go`.
