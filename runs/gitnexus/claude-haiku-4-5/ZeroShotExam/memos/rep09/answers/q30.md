**Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks:**

- Created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- Updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- Deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- Comment Created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

All dispatch functions are in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`.

**Convergence point for delivery:**

All webhook events converge through `webhook.PostAsync()` (defined in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:183-196`), which enqueues payloads into a buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`, capacity 128). Four worker goroutines initialized at package startup (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`) consume from this queue and call `Post()` (lines 119-180) to deliver webhooks to the configured URLs with HMAC-SHA256 signing.
