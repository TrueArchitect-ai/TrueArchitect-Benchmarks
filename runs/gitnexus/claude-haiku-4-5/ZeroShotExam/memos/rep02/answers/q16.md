When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following records **before** the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the source) — `store/memo.go:142`
2. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the target) — `store/memo.go:145`
3. **Attachments** linked to the memo — `store/memo.go:149-157`

The memo row itself is deleted last (line 158).
