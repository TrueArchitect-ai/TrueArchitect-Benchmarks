**web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; MemoService**

The memo CRUD mutations and queries are contained in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`, which exports:
- `useCreateMemo()` (mutation)
- `useUpdateMemo()` (mutation)
- `useDeleteMemo()` (mutation)
- `useMemos()` / `useInfiniteMemos()` (queries)
- `useMemo()` (query)

It uses the `memoServiceClient` which is created from the generated `MemoService` service definition (imported from `./types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb` at line 10 of connect.ts).
