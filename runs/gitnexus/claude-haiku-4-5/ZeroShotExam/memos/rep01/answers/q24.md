The complete call chain for creating a memo from the frontend mutation hook to the SQL INSERT:

1. web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts - Frontend mutation hook `useCreateMemo`
2. web/src/connect.ts - Client creation with `memoServiceClient`
3. proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.go - Proto RPC definition (generated from .proto)
4. server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go - Handler method `APIV1Service.CreateMemo`
5. store/memo.go - Store method `Store.CreateMemo`
6. store/db/sqlite/memo.go - SQLite dialect driver (example driver; also postgres/mysql variants exist)
