The ConnectRPC service clients are created in the frontend source file:

**web/src/connect.ts**

This file uses `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` to instantiate service clients for various services including `InstanceService`, `AuthService`, `UserService`, `MemoService`, `AttachmentService`, `AIService`, `ShortcutService`, and `IdentityProviderService`.
