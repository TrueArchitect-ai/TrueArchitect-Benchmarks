When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records **before** the memo row itself (store/memo.go:140-159):

1. **MemoRelation** records (both where the memo is the source and where it's the target/related memo)
2. **Attachment** records (all attachments linked to the memo)

These are deleted before calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` which deletes the memo row itself.
