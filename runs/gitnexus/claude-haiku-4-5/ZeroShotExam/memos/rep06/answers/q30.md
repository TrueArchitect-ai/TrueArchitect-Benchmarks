**Delivery convergence:**

All four dispatch functions call either `webhook.PostAsync()` (memo_service_webhooks.go:71, 43) or directly enqueue payloads to `asyncPostQueue` channel. The `asyncPostQueue` is initialized with 4 worker goroutines in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54` (init function), each reading from the channel and calling `Post()` for actual HTTP delivery. The convergence point is `Post()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`, which constructs and executes the HTTP request with HMAC-SHA256 signing and SSRF protection.
