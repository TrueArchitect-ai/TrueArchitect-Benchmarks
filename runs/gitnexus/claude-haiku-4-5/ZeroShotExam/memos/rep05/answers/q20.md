**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) via HTTP endpoint `/api/v1/sse`

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (SSEHub struct)

**Handler names that broadcast through the hub:**
- `CreateMemo` — broadcasts SSEEventMemoCreated
- `DeleteMemo` — broadcasts SSEEventMemoDeleted
- `CreateMemoComment` — broadcasts SSEEventMemoCommentCreated
- `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`) — broadcasts SSEEventMemoUpdated
- `UpsertMemoReaction` — broadcasts SSEEventReactionUpserted
- `DeleteMemoReaction` — broadcasts SSEEventReactionDeleted

All handlers use `s.SSEHub.Broadcast()` to send events to connected clients. The SSEHub manages client subscriptions, filters events by visibility/permissions, and delivers them through buffered channels (32-event buffer per client).
