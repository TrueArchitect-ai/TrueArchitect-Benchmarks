Based on my analysis of the code, here are the database tables affected when DeleteMemo runs:

**Tables affected by DeleteMemo (directly or via explicit cascade code):**

1. **memo** - The main memo record is deleted (store/db/mysql/memo.go:259-270)
2. **memo_relation** - All memo relations where the deleted memo is either source or target are deleted (store/memo.go:142-146, store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go:126-137)
3. **attachment** - All attachments linked to the memo are deleted (store/memo.go:149-156, store/db/mysql/attachment.go:230-241)

The cascade flow in DeleteMemo (server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518-592) is:
- Comments (which are memos with MemoRelationComment type) are explicitly deleted first (lines 573-576)
- Then store.DeleteMemo is called, which:
  - Deletes memo_relation records (both as source and target)
  - Deletes all attachments
  - Deletes the memo itself
