When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the source) - via `DeleteMemoRelation` with `MemoID`
2. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the target) - via `DeleteMemoRelation` with `RelatedMemoID`
3. **Attachments** - via iterating through all attachments linked to the memo and calling `DeleteAttachment` for each
