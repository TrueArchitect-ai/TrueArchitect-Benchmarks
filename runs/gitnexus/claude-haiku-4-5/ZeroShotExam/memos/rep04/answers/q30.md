**Convergence and delivery path:**

All dispatch functions converge at `s.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook()` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`, which enqueues webhooks via `webhook.PostAsync()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`.
