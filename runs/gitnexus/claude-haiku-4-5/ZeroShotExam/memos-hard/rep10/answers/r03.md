The nightly database-cleanup job is scheduled to run at **2 AM UTC** (cron expression: `0 2 * * *`).

This is shown in `/bench/home/work/internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-106`, where the example demonstrates the cleanup job:

```go
// Cleanup old data every night at 2 AM
s.Register(&scheduler.Job{
    Name:     "cleanup",
    Schedule: "0 2 * * *",
    ...
})
```

The cron expression `0 2 * * *` means:
- Minute: 0
- Hour: 2 (2 AM in 24-hour format)
- Day of month, month, day of week: any day
