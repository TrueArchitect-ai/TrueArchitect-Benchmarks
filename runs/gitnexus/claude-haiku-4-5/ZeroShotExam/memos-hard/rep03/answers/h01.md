**S3 Presign Runner** — **every 12 hours** — **server/server.go:153-174**

The `s3presign.Runner` component in `/bench/home/work/server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` refreshes S3-stored attachment presigned URLs on a 12-hour interval to keep external links from expiring. The runner checks if presigned URLs will expire within 1 day (4+ days into their 5-day lifetime) and generates fresh ones as needed.

It's wired into server startup in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method at **server/server.go:153-174**, which runs the checker once immediately on startup, then launches it as a background goroutine to run every 12 hours for the lifetime of the server.
