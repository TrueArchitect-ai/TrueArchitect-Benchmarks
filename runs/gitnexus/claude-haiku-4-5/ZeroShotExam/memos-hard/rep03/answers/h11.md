Based on my analysis of the Memos repository using GitNexus and code inspection, the persistent entity types defined in the store layer that are referenced from the largest number of packages outside that layer are:

**1st: User; 2nd: Memo** (3 packages each)

Both `store.User` and `store.Memo` are referenced from 3 distinct packages outside the data layer:
- **User**: referenced by 93 total references across `server/auth` (6 refs), `server/notification` (5 refs), and `server/router` (65+ refs)
- **Memo**: referenced by 64 total references across `server/notification` (10 refs), `server/router` (48 refs), and `server/runner` (1 ref)

These are followed by Attachment (43 refs from 1 package), Reaction (14 refs from 1 package), Inbox (13 refs from 2 packages), MemoShare (10 refs from 1 package), and MemoRelation (9 refs from 1 package). UserSetting, InstanceSetting, and IdentityProvider have no external references.

**File path references:**
- Entity definitions: `store/memo.go:Memo` and `store/user.go:User`
- Primary consumer: `server/router/api/v1/` (highest concentration of usage)
