h01. **S3 Presign Runner** — **every 12 hours** — **server/server.go:162**

The `s3presign.Runner` component keeps S3 attachment presigned URLs fresh by regenerating them before they expire. It runs every 12 hours (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), checking S3 attachments and re-presigning any URLs that will expire within the next day (they're valid for 5 days total, so it refreshes at the 4-day mark). The runner is instantiated and wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162` within the `startBackgroundRunners()` method, which is called during server initialization.
