**Premise correction**: The webhook delivery workers do not consume from either NATS or RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism**: The webhook delivery system uses **in-memory Go channels** for its queue. The queue is declared in `/bench/home/work/internal/webhook/webhook.go` at lines 39–40 as a buffered channel with capacity 128:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

A fixed pool of 4 goroutine workers is spawned in the `init()` function (lines 42–55) that consume from this queue and dispatch webhooks asynchronously. Messages enqueued via `PostAsync()` are dropped with a warning if the queue reaches capacity.
