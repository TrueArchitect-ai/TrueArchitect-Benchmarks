The component that keeps links to externally-stored attachment content from going stale is the **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`). It operates on a **12-hour schedule** (line 27 in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` defines `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). It is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:162** where `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is instantiated and started as a background runner.

The runner checks S3-stored attachments every 12 hours and re-presigns their URLs before expiration (which is set to 5 days, with refresh triggered when URLs are within 4 days of expiry per lines 85-90 in `runner.go`).
