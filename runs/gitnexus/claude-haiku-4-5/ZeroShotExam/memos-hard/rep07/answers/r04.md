The delivery mechanism is implemented as follows:

1. **Queue declaration** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`): A Go channel with a buffer size of 128
2. **Workers** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`): Four goroutines spawned on init, each running in an infinite loop consuming from `asyncPostQueue`
3. **Enqueue operation** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-195`): `PostAsync()` sends payloads to the channel; if the channel is full, the dispatch is dropped with a warning
