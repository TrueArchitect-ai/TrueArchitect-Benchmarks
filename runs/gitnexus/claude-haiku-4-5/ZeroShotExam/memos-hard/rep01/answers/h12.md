The other CreateMemo implementations for MySQL and PostgreSQL exist in their respective driver packages (`store/db/mysql/memo.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`), but they are not invoked with a default installation due to the hardcoded `sqlite` default in the viper configuration.
