h01. S3Presign Runner — every 12 hours — server/server.go:162

The `S3Presign` runner (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3 attachment presigned URLs from expiring. It runs on a 12-hour schedule, refreshing S3 object presigned URLs when they are within 4 days of expiration (the URLs expire after 5 days). The runner is instantiated and wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`, where it's created via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` and runs continuously in a background goroutine.
