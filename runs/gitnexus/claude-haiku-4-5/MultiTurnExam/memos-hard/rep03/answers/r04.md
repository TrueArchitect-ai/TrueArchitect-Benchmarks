**Premise correction:** Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used for webhook delivery. **Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-memory Go channel queue. The queue is declared as a buffered channel at line 39 of `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

Four goroutines are spawned in the `init()` function (lines 43-54) that consume from this channel and call `Post()` to deliver webhooks. The queue is bounded to 128 items, and payloads that exceed this capacity are dropped with a warning log (internal/webhook/webhook.go:192-194).
