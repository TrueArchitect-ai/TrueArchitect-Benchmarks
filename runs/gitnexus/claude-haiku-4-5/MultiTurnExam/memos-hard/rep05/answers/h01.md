The s3presign runner keeps S3 attachment links from going stale by periodically refreshing their presigned URLs. It runs on a 12-hour schedule (time.Hour * 12), checking if presigned URLs will expire within the next 4 days and refreshing them if needed. It's wired into server startup in server/server.go:162-169, where it's instantiated with NewRunner, initially run once with RunOnce(), then started as a continuous background goroutine with s3presignRunner.Run().
