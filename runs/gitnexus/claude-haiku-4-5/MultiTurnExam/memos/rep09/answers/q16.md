memo_relation records (both as source and target), attachments

When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes:
1. memo_relation records where the deleted memo is the source (line 142)
2. memo_relation records where the deleted memo is the target (line 145)
3. attachments linked to the memo (lines 149-157)

These are all deleted before the memo row itself (line 158).
