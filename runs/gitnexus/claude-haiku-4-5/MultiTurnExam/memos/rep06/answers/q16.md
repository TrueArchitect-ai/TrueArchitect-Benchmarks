Based on my analysis of the code, when a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer (in store/memo.go) deletes the following records before the memo row itself:

1. memo_relation records (where the memo is either the source or target)
2. attachments

The API layer also handles deleting memo comments (which are themselves memos), and the store layer handles their relations and attachments recursively.
