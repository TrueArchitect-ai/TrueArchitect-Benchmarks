Attachment link staleness management
The **s3presign.Runner** component keeps S3 attachment presigned links from going stale. It runs on a **12-hour schedule** (every 12 hours). It is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:162** in the `startBackgroundRunners` method, where it's instantiated and started as a background goroutine. The runner calls `CheckAndPresign()` periodically to refresh presigned URLs that will expire within the next 4 days (since they expire after 5 days).
