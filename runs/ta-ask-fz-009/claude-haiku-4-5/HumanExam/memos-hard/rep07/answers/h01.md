Attachment Link Staleness Prevention
**Component:** `s3presign.Runner`  
**Schedule:** Every 12 hours (const `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`)  
**Wiring Site:** `server/server.go:162-169` (in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`)

The S3 presign runner checks S3-stored attachments and re-presigns URLs when they're within 4 days of expiration (5-day total expiration).
