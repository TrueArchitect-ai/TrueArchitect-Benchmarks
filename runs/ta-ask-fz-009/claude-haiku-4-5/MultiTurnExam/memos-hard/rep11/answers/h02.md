The protection mechanism against internal network addresses is implemented in two layers:

1. **Runtime protection during dial**: The `safeDialContext` function (internal/webhook/webhook.go:59) validates resolved IPs against reserved/private ranges before establishing connections.

2. **Validation protection at webhook creation/update**: The `isReservedIP` function (internal/webhook/validate.go:44) checks whether IPs fall within reserved CIDR ranges (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local, IPv6 equivalents). The reserved ranges are defined at **internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23**.

Both mechanisms can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (internal/webhook/validate.go:41) for self-hosted deployments that intentionally allow webhooks to internal services.
