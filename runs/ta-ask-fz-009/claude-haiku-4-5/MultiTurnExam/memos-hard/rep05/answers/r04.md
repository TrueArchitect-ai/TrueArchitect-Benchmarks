**Premise correction:** The premise is incorrect. Webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses **in-memory Go channels**, not a message broker.

**Details:**

1. **Queue declaration:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`
   - An in-memory buffered channel with capacity 128: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`

2. **Worker pool initialization:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54` (in the `init()` function)
   - 4 concurrent worker goroutines are spawned at package initialization
   - Each worker consumes from `asyncPostQueue` and calls `Post()` to deliver webhooks

3. **Async enqueue:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` (`PostAsync` function)
   - Payloads are sent to the channel with a non-blocking select, dropping overflows if the queue is full

The webhook delivery is entirely in-process and in-memory, using Go's native channel mechanism rather than an external message broker. This means webhooks are lost if the process crashes before delivery completes.
