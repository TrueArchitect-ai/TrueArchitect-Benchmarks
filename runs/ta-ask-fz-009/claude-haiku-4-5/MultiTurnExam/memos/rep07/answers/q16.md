q16. memo_relation records, attachments

**Explanation:** When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`store.Store.DeleteMemo` in `store/memo.go:140-159`), the following records are deleted before the memo row itself:
1. **memo_relation records** (lines 142-147) - both where the memo is the source (MemoID) and where it is the target (RelatedMemoID)
2. **Attachments** (lines 149-157) - all attachments linked to this memo are deleted before the memo itself

Only after these cleanup operations are complete does the memo row itself get deleted (line 158).
