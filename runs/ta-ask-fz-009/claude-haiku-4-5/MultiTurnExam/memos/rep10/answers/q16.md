When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following records before the memo row itself (store/memo.go:140-159):

1. **memo_relation** records — all relations where the memo is either the source (MemoID) or target (RelatedMemoID) are deleted (lines 142-147)
2. **attachment** records — all attachments linked to the memo are deleted (lines 148-157)

After these cleanup operations, the memo row itself is deleted (line 158).
