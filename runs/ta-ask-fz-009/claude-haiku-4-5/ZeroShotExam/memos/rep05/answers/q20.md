**Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP with `text/event-stream` content type.

**Hub path**: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct implements subscription management and broadcasting with visibility-aware filtering for client channels.

**Handler names (memo handlers broadcasting through SSEHub)**:
1. `CreateMemo` — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoCreated`
2. `DeleteMemo` — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoDeleted`
3. `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (called from `UpdateMemo`) — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoUpdated`
4. `CreateMemoComment` — broadcasts `SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`
5. `UpsertMemoReaction` — broadcasts `SSEEventReactionUpserted`
6. `DeleteMemoReaction` — broadcasts `SSEEventReactionDeleted`

All are in `server/router/api/v1/` and call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast()` with `SSEEvent` objects. The hub maintains a map of connected clients with buffered event channels (32-event buffer) and filters events by visibility and user permissions before dispatch.
