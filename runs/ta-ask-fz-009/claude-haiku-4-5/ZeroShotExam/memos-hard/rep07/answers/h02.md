The protection works in two layers:

1. **At dial time** (line 59-77 in webhook.go): The `safeDialContext()` function intercepts every outbound connection attempt. It resolves the hostname to IP addresses and rejects any connection if the resolved IP falls in reserved/private ranges using `isReservedIP()`.
