**Actual mechanism:**

Webhook delivery workers use an **in-memory buffered Go channel** to consume webhook payloads. The queue is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` as:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```
