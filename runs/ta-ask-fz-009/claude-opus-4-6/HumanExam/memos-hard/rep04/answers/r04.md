False premise. There is no message broker (NATS, RabbitMQ, or otherwise). Webhook delivery is synchronous via an in-process Go channel (`asyncPostQueue`, a buffered `chan` of size 128 at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) consumed by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–55`). Each goroutine calls `webhook.Post` directly over HTTP.
