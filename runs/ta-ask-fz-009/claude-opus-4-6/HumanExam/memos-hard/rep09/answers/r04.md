False premise. Webhook delivery does not use any message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). Webhooks are dispatched via a simple in-process Go channel (`asyncPostQueue`, a buffered `chan` of size 128 at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by 4 goroutines launched in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42`). Delivery is a direct HTTP POST.
