False premise. Webhook delivery does not use any message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). Webhooks are delivered via a simple in-process Go channel (`asyncPostQueue`, a buffered channel of size 128 at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) consumed by 4 goroutines spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Delivery is direct HTTP POST via `webhook.Post`.
