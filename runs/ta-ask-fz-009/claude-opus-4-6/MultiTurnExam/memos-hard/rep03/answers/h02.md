**Protection mechanism — SSRF guard via `safeDialContext` + `isReservedIP`:**

1. **At dial time (delivery):** The `safeClient` HTTP client uses a custom `Transport` with `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:35-36`). This also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks, as noted in the comment on line 31.

2. **At URL registration time (validation):** `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) performs the same DNS-resolve-then-check at webhook creation/update, rejecting URLs that resolve to private IPs before they're ever stored.

3. **The blocked ranges** are defined in `reservedCIDRs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`): IPv4 loopback, all three RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud IMDS (`169.254.0.0/16`), and their IPv6 equivalents (`::1`, `fc00::/7`, `fe80::/10`). These are parsed into `reservedNetworks` once at init (`internal/webhook/validate.go:28-36`) and checked by `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`). An `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (line 41) lets self-hosted deployments opt out of this protection.
