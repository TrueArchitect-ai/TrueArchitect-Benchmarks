**`s3presign.Runner`** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at **`server/server.go:162`**.

The runner iterates all S3-stored attachments in batches of 100, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-presigns any whose presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; the runner skips attachments presigned less than 4 days ago). It writes the fresh presigned URL back as the attachment's `Reference`. On startup it also runs once synchronously (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)`, `server/server.go:163`) before entering the ticker loop in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:169`).
