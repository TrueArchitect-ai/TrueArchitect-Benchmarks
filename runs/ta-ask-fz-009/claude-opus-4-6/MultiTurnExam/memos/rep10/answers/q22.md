**Dispatch function:** `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (which delegates to `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook`) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`

**Delivery package:** `internal/webhook` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`) — performs the HTTP POST via `webhook.PostAsync` / `webhook.Post`

**Signing mechanism:** HMAC-SHA256 following the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) convention. The per-webhook signing secret (stored as `whsec_<base64>`) is decoded to raw bytes, then used as the key for HMAC-SHA256 over `msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body`. The resulting signature is sent in the `webhook-signature` header as `v1,<base64>`, alongside `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` headers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`, lines 133–149).
