**Signing mechanism:** HMAC-SHA256 following the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) spec. The signing secret (stored as `whsec_<base64>`) is decoded, then an HMAC is computed over `msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body`. The signature is sent as `v1,<base64(hmac)>` in the `webhook-signature` header, alongside `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` headers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`, lines 133–149).
