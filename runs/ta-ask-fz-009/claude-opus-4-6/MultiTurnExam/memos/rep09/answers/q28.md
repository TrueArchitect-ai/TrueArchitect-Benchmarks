The server starts **one** background runner:

- **s3presign.Runner** — scheduled via a `time.Ticker` every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval`), plus an initial `RunOnce` at startup. It refreshes pre-signed S3 URLs for attachments. Started in `server/server.go` (line 162, `startBackgroundRunners`). Runner implementation: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`.

**Unused infrastructure:** The **memopayload.Runner** (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) exists in the codebase with a `RunOnce` method that rebuilds memo payloads, but it is **not started** by `startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go` — it is never instantiated or invoked by the server's startup sequence.
