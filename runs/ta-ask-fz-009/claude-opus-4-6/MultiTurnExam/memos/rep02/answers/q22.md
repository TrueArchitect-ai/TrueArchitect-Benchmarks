**Dispatch function:** `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` (which delegates to `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` in the same file, calling `webhook.PostAsync`)

**Delivery package:** `internal/webhook` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go`) — performs the HTTP POST via `Post()`, dispatched asynchronously through a buffered queue by `PostAsync()`

**Signing mechanism:** HMAC-SHA256 following the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) convention. When a webhook has a `SigningSecret` (in `whsec_<base64>` format or raw string), the payload is signed by computing `HMAC-SHA256(key, msgID + "." + timestamp + "." + body)` and the signature is sent as `v1,<base64>` in the `webhook-signature` header, alongside `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` headers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:133–149`).
