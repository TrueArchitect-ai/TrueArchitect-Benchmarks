When `DeleteMemo` runs, the following tables are affected:

1. **`memo_relation`** — deleted first in `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:142–147`), removing rows where the memo is either source (`memo_id`) or target (`related_memo_id`)
2. **`attachment`** — deleted next in `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:149–157`), removing all attachments linked to the memo
3. **`memo`** — the comment memos (child memos related via `COMMENT` relation) are deleted first at the handler level (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:573–577`), each triggering their own cascade through `Store.DeleteMemo`; finally the memo itself is deleted (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:580`, `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:239`)

In summary: **`memo_relation`**, **`attachment`**, and **`memo`** (including comment memos).
