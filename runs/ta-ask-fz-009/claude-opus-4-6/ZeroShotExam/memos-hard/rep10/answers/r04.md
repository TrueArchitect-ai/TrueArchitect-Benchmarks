**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **in-process Go buffered channel**, not a broker queue. The channel is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:

asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)

Four goroutine workers are spawned in the package's `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–55`). There are no references to NATS, RabbitMQ, or any other message broker in this codebase's webhook path — it's a self-contained, single-process fan-out over a bounded channel.
