### Convergence function

**`APIV1Service.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`

This is the single function where five of the six paths converge before delivery. It looks up the memo creator's configured webhooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, builds a `WebhookRequestPayload` (including the user-configured `.URL`), and hands it to `webhook.PostAsync` for async dispatch.
