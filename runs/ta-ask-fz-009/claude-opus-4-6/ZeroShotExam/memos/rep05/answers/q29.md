**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (line 156) is a ConnectRPC `Interceptor` that attaches a `Bearer` token to every outgoing request's `Authorization` header and reactively refreshes on 401.

**Token kinds:** Two token kinds are managed:
1. **Access tokens** (short-lived JWTs, ~15 min) — stored in-memory and localStorage (`web/src/auth-state.ts`), attached as `Authorization: Bearer <token>` by the interceptor, and refreshed via `AuthService.refreshToken`.
2. **Personal Access Tokens (PATs)** — also sent as Bearer tokens; the backend `Authenticator.resolveBearer` discriminates between JWTs and PATs at validation time (`server/auth/authenticator.go:26`), with dedicated methods `AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` and `AuthenticateByPAT`.

A **refresh token** is also involved (sent via cookie with `credentials: "include"` on the refresh-only transport, line 60), used to obtain new access tokens.

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth` (package `auth`, anchored at `server/auth/authenticator.go`). The `auth.Authenticator` struct is the core validator, wrapped by `v1.Authorizer` (`server/router/api/v1/authz.go`) which adds method-level access policy. The server-side Connect interceptor `v1.AuthInterceptor` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go:208`) delegates to `Authorizer.Authenticate` → `auth.Authenticator.Authenticate`.

**Compact format:**  
`web/src/connect.ts`; access tokens (JWT) + personal access tokens (PAT) + refresh tokens (cookie); `server/auth`
