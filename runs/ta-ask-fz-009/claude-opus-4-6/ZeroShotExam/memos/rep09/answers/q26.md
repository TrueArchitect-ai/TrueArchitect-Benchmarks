1. **`memo_relation`** — Explicit cascade code in `store.Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:142–146`) deletes rows where the memo is either `memo_id` or `related_memo_id`, via `Driver.DeleteMemoRelation` (SQL: `DELETE FROM memo_relation`, e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go:148–150`).
