**Token kinds managed (three):**
1. **Access token (JWT, short-lived, ~15 min)** — stored in-memory and localStorage (`web/src/auth-state.ts`, key `memos_access_token`). Sent as a `Bearer` token in the `Authorization` header. Stateless validation on the backend.
