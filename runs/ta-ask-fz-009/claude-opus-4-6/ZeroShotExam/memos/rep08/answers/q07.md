The ConnectRPC service clients are created in **`web/src/connect.ts`**. This file imports `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` and `createConnectTransport` from `@connectrpc/connect-web`, sets up the transport, and exports all service clients (e.g., `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, etc.) starting at line 192.
