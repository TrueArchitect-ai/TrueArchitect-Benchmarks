**Token kinds managed (two):**

1. **Access token (short-lived JWT)** — stored in-memory and in `localStorage` (`web/src/auth-state.ts`). The interceptor retrieves it via `getAccessToken()`, sets `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, and proactively refreshes it when it's within 30 s of expiry (`REQUEST_TOKEN_EXPIRY_BUFFER_MS`).
