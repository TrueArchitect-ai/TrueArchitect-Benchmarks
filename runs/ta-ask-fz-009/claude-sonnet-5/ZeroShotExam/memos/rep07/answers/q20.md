q20. **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events (SSE) — a persistent one-way HTTP stream, not WebSockets. The hub is `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`, with the `SSEHub` struct (`sse_hub.go:56`) managing client subscriptions (`Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`, `sse_hub.go:71,89`) and pushing events via `Broadcast` (`sse_hub.go:115`). Clients connect through `handleSSE` / `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`.

**Handlers that broadcast through it** (all in `server/router/api/v1`, calling `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
- `CreateMemo` — `memo_service.go:163`
- `DeleteMemo` — `memo_service.go:585`
- `CreateMemoComment` — `memo_service_comments.go:126`
- `UpsertMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go:106`
- `DeleteMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go:161`
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` — `memo_update_helpers.go:71`, a shared helper that itself broadcasts and is invoked from `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) — so those three handlers broadcast transitively through it.
