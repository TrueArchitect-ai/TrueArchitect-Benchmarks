q19. MemoService is served over two protocols: **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** and **Connect** (used for browser clients, replacing grpc-web).

- **gRPC-Gateway** — registration happens via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, mounted onto the Echo group at `/api/v1/*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:128`).
- **Connect** — wrapping/registration happens via `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, invoked through `RegisterConnectHandlers` and mounted onto the Echo group at `/memos.api.v1.*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:141,144`).

Both wrap the same underlying `*APIV1Service` (which embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer`, `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:26`), so the business logic is shared — only the transport/registration differs.
