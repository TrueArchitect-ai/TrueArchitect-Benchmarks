Now let's look at the connect_handler.go for the Connect-RPC wrapping.

q19. MemoService is served over two protocols, both wired up in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON transcoding of gRPC)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` inside `RegisterGateway`, `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`. This mux is then mounted at `/api/v1/*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:128`).

2. **Connect-RPC (used for browser clients, replacing grpc-web)** — wrapped via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`, `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`. That handler is invoked from `v1.go:141` (`connectHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers(...)`) and mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:144`).

So:
- gRPC-Gateway — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`
- Connect-RPC — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`
