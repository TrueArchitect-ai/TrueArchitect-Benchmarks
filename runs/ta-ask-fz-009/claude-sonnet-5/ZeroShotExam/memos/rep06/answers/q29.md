It reads the current access token via `getAccessToken()` (backed by `web/src/auth-state.ts`), sets it as a `Bearer` token on the `Authorization` header (`setAuthorizationHeader`, `web/src/connect.ts:97`), and is wired into the Connect-web transport's `interceptors` array (`web/src/connect.ts:184-189`). On the backend these Bearer tokens (short-lived JWT access tokens) plus a long-lived refresh-token cookie/PAT are validated by the `auth` package: `server/auth/authenticator.go`, specifically `auth.Authenticator.AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2` (short-lived JWT, ~15 min), `AuthenticateByRefreshToken`, and `AuthenticateByPAT` (Personal Access Tokens), unified through `Authenticator.Authenticate` (`server/auth/authenticator.go:213`).
