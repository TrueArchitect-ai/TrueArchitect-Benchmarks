r04. Premise correction: neither. There's no NATS or RabbitMQ broker involved — webhook delivery isn't consumed off an external message queue at all.

Actual mechanism: webhook dispatch is an in-process, in-memory Go channel. `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` declares `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, and `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` spins up 4 fixed goroutine workers that range over that channel and call `Post(payload)` for each item (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:44-53`). Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a warning log) if the buffered channel of 128 is full — there's no persistence, no broker, and no durable queue backing this; it's purely an in-memory buffered channel scoped to the single server process.
