Actual mechanism: webhook dispatch is an in-process, in-memory Go channel, not an external message broker. A package `init()` (webhook.go:42-55) spins up 4 goroutine workers that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post(payload)` for each item. Producers enqueue via a non-blocking `select`/`case asyncPostQueue <- requestPayload` at webhook.go:190, dropping the payload with a warning log if the channel is full (webhook.go:192). There's no AMQP/NATS client, connection, or subscription anywhere in the repo — confirmed by a repo-wide search for `nats`/`rabbitmq`/`amqp` returning no relevant hits (only unrelated locale-file matches in `web/src/locales/ca.json`).
