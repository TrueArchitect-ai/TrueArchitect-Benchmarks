r03. Premise correction — there is no nightly "database-cleanup" job scheduled in this repo's cron scheduler.

What actually exists:
- `internal/scheduler` (see `internal/scheduler/doc.go`, `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`) is a standalone, GitHub-Actions-inspired cron scheduler library. Its only callers are its own tests/examples (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`) — a `find_references` on `scheduler`/`internal/scheduler` across `server/` and the rest of the tree turns up nothing outside that package itself. It is not wired into the running server anywhere.
- The server's actual background jobs are started in `server/server.go` via `startBackgroundRunners` (server/server.go:153), which launches only two runners: `server/runner/memopayload` (a one-shot run at startup, `RunOnce`) and `server/runner/s3presign` (a ticker-based loop firing every 12 hours per `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, not a cron expression, and not related to cleanup).
- No job named/labeled "database-cleanup" (or similar — cache/session/attachment purge) exists anywhere in the Go codebase; grep for `vacuum|purge|database cleanup|db cleanup` and for a wired `CleanupJob`/`StartCron` returned nothing.
- The only cache-eviction logic (`store/cache/cache.go:200` `cleanupLoop`, `:218` `cleanup`, `:253` `cleanupOldest`) is an in-memory LRU cache sweep, not a database job, and isn't cron-scheduled either — it runs on its own internal loop, unrelated to `internal/scheduler`.

So there's no time to report: no nightly database-cleanup job is registered in `internal/scheduler` or anywhere else in the codebase.
