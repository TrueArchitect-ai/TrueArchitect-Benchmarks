- **Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (invoked synchronously, and dequeued/dispatched asynchronously by the worker goroutines started in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` via `PostAsync`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`). - **SSRF protection** — `internal/webhook/validate.go`: the custom dialer `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`) resolves the target host and rejects any IP that `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`) flags as within a reserved/private CIDR (`reservedCIDRs`, `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23` — loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud metadata `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents). This dialer is installed as the `Transport.DialContext` on `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`), so it runs at connect time and also defeats DNS-rebinding. `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`) performs the same reserved-IP check earlier, at webhook-registration time, for defense in depth. A deployment-level escape hatch, `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`), lets self-hosted instances disable the check.
