Premise correction: neither. This codebase (usememos/memos) has no NATS or RabbitMQ broker at all — there is no message-broker dependency in the module graph, and the webhook "queue" isn't a broker-backed queue.

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process. `internal/webhook/webhook.go` declares `asyncPostQueue` as a buffered Go channel (`chan *WebhookRequestPayload`, capacity 128) at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. On package init, 4 worker goroutines are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over this channel and call `Post()` for each payload. `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues payloads via a non-blocking `select`, dropping and logging a warning if the channel is full rather than blocking. There's an analogous pattern for email (`email.asyncEmailQueue` in `internal/email/email.go:14`), same in-process channel design — no external broker involved anywhere in delivery.
