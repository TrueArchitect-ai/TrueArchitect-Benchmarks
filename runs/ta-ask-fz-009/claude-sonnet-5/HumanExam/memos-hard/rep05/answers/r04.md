— Premise is false: memos uses neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. Webhook delivery is dispatched through an in-process, unbuffered-consumer Go channel: `asyncPostQueue := make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (webhook.go:42-55) that call `webhook.Post` (webhook.go:120).
