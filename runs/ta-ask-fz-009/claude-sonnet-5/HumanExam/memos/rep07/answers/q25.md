Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped in `internal/filter.Engine` — internal/filter/engine.go:14-34. Mechanism: the CEL filter string is compiled into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR (internal/filter/ir.go), then a per-request `renderer` (internal/filter/render.go) walks that IR and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments/args — e.g. `DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL` branches inside functions like `renderFunctionComparison`, `jsonArrayLengthExpr`, and the `strftime`/`EXTRACT`/`MySQL date-fn` branches in `timestampAccessorExpr` (render.go:222-248) — and each store driver (store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go) calls `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectX, ...)` to splice the resulting WHERE clause and args into its query.
