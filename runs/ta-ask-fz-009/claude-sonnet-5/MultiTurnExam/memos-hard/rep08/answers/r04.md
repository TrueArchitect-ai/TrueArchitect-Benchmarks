**Actual mechanism:** webhook dispatch uses a plain in-memory Go channel as its "queue." In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` declares a buffered channel (capacity 128) that acts as the work queue. Four worker goroutines are started in the package's `init()` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55), each running `for payload := range asyncPostQueue { ... Post(payload) ... }` — i.e., consuming from that channel and calling `Post` (webhook.go:120) to actually deliver the HTTP request. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (webhook.go:184-196), which does a non-blocking send (`select`/`default`) and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full rather than blocking — there's no persistence, retry, or cross-process durability, consistent with an in-memory queue rather than a broker-backed one.
