Memo visibility is enforced independently at each of these surfaces:

- **`v1.APIV1Service.GetMemo`** — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:55-63 (rejects non-public memos unless the caller is authenticated and, for private memos, is the creator).
- **`v1.APIV1Service.ListMemos`** — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:219-225 (restricts `memoFind.VisibilityList` to `Public` for anonymous callers, `Public`+`Protected` for authenticated non-owners).
- **`v1.APIV1Service` attachment access** — server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:445-451 and server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214-222 (denies access to a memo's attachments unless the memo is public, or the caller is its creator/a superuser).
- **`v1.APIV1Service` reactions** — server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-38 and :83 (same public/private/creator-or-superuser check before letting a caller view or react to a memo's reactions).
- **`v1.APIV1Service` comments** — server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41 and :100 (a comment's containing memo's visibility gates who may see/create the comment).
- **`v1.APIV1Service` mention notifications** — server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62-84 (checks memo/relatedMemo visibility before notifying a mentioned user).
- **File server (HTTP attachment/file serving)** — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:677-699 (`memo.Visibility == store.Public && AllowAnonymous()` permits anonymous fetches; private memos require the requester to be the creator or an admin).
- **SSE event delivery** — `SSEClient.canReceive`, server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-141 (filters real-time events per connected client: private events only delivered to the creator or an admin, public/protected delivered to all).
- **Notification email composer** — server/notification/email.go:313-316 (visibility gates what memo content/snippet is embedded into notification emails, e.g. suppressing private/protected content for the wrong recipient).
