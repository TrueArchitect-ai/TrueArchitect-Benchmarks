Actual mechanism: the cache layer is an **in-process, in-memory cache** (`store/cache/cache.go`), not backed by any external store. It's a thread-safe `sync.Map`-based structure (`Cache`, `cache.go:67-73`) with a background goroutine (`cleanupLoop`, `cache.go:200-215`) that periodically evicts expired entries.

For user entries specifically: `Store.New` (`store/store.go:38-61`) constructs a shared `cacheConfig` (`store/store.go:40-45`) with `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, and creates `userCache` via `cache.New(cacheConfig)` at `store/store.go:52`. Since user entries are cached with `Cache.Set` (not `SetWithTTL`), they use that default TTL — **10 minutes** — after which `Cache.Get` treats them as expired (`cache.go:135-144`), and the periodic cleanup loop (running every `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`) also proactively purges expired entries.
