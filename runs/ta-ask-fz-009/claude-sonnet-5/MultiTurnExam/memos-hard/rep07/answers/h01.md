h01. The **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) keeps externally-stored (S3) attachment links from going stale.

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner.CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) scans attachments whose storage type is S3, and for any whose presigned URL is older than 4 days (URLs are issued with a 5‑day expiry, per the comment at `runner.go:86-90`), it regenerates the presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and persists it with `Store.UpdateAttachment` (`runner.go:108-123`).
