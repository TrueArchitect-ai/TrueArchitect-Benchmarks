Memo visibility is enforced independently at each of these surfaces:

- **GetMemo / read-access gate (RPC handler)** — `checkMemoReadAccess` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68` (guards `GetMemo` and comment/parent-memo lookups, e.g. lines 356, 367)
- **ListMemos query filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:219-225` (restricts `VisibilityList` to `Public`/`Protected` for anonymous or non-owner listing)
- **Attachment access on a memo** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:445-451` and `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214-222` (checks memo visibility before exposing/serving attachments)
- **Reactions** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-38,83` (checks memo visibility before allowing reaction read/create)
- **Comments** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41,148` (checks related memo visibility before allowing/reading comments)
- **Mention/notification dispatch** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62,67,84` (suppresses mention-based inbox notifications when the mentioned memo is private and the target isn't the creator)
- **Email notification dispatch** — `canViewerAccessMemo` — `server/notification/email.go:306-318` (guards comment/mention email notifications, lines 155/187)
- **SSE event delivery** — `SSEClient.canReceive` — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` (filters real-time events by visibility/creator/role before pushing to a connected client)
- **RSS feed generation** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90,141` (hard-restricts `VisibilityList` to `store.Public` when building the feed)
