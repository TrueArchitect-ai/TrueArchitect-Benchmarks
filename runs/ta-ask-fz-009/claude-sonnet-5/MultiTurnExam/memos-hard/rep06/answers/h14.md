**Computation site:** `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-88`. It calls `markdownService.ExtractAll([]byte(memo.Content))` (backed by the goldmark-based `markdown.service.ExtractAll`, `internal/markdown/markdown.go:356`) and writes the results into `memo.Payload.Tags` and `memo.Payload.Property` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:85-86`).

**Triggers (recomputation call sites):**
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — every new memo, at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`.
- `APIV1Service.UpdateMemo` — only when the update mask includes the `content` path (i.e., the memo's content is actually being changed), at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:447`.
- (Also, the standalone `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` batch job at `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27-72` would recompute payloads for *all* memos, but per h08 this runner is never constructed/wired at startup, so in practice recomputation only happens on create/update.)

**Extracted properties:** per `ExtractedData` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:21-25`) and what `RebuildMemoPayload` persists — `Tags` (all `#tag` occurrences) and `Property` (a `*storepb.MemoPayload_Property` of boolean flags computed from content, e.g. things like has-link/has-code/has-task-list, used for list/preview rendering). Note `ExtractedData.Mentions` is also produced by the same `ExtractAll` call but is consumed separately for mention-notification resolution (`resolveMentionTargets`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:26-38`) rather than stored on `memo.Payload` by this function.
