There is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL public API in this repository. The public API contract is protobuf-defined gRPC services with `google.api.http` annotations under `proto/api/v1/`—for example, `MemoService` maps RPCs to `/api/v1/memos` routes in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-49`, and `UserService` maps to `/api/v1/users` in `proto/api/v1/user_service.proto:17-59`.

At runtime, `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` registers those generated service handlers with gRPC-Gateway and mounts them under `/api/v1/*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:95-129`); it additionally registers Connect RPC handlers (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:131-141`).
