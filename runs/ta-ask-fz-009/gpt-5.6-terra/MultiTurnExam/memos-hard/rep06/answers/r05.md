There is no GraphQL schema in this repository. The public API contract is protobuf/gRPC service definitions under `proto/api/v1/`—for example, `MemoService` and its HTTP mappings are defined in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-64`.

At runtime, `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` registers those protobuf services on the gRPC-Gateway REST surface at `/api/v1/*` and on Connect RPC handlers at `/memos.api.v1.*`: `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64-144`.
