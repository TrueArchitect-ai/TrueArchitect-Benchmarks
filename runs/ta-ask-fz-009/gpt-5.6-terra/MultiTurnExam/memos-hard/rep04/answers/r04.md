Premise correction: webhook workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ; this repository has no broker-backed webhook queue. The actual mechanism is the in-process buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128), declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutine workers started in `init` consume that channel and invoke `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`); `PostAsync` enqueues payloads non-blockingly (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-196`).
