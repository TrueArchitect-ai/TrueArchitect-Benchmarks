CEL (`cel-go`) filter engine — `internal/filter/engine.go` (with its schema/parser/renderer under `internal/filter/`).

`ListMemos` validates and passes the CEL expression to `filter.DefaultEngine`; each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions` with `DialectSQLite`, `DialectMySQL`, or `DialectPostgres`, and the shared renderer produces that dialect’s SQL fragment and bound arguments (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`; rendering in `internal/filter/render.go`).
