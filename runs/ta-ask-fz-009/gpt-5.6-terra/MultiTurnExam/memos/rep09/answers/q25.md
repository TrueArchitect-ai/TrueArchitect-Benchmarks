CEL filter engine — `internal/filter/engine.go`

`ListMemos` validates and stores the CEL expression (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`), then each dialect driver invokes `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`) to compile the expression into a dialect-specific, parameterized SQL predicate via the renderer in `internal/filter/render.go`.
