`server/runner/memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` computes the persisted derived payload by parsing `memo.Content` through `MarkdownService.ExtractAll`, then writes the resulting tags and properties to `memo.Payload` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-87`). The extraction implementation is `internal/markdown.(*service).ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:355-421`).

Triggers: memo creation (`v1.APIV1Service.CreateMemo`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70`), memo update (`v1.APIV1Service.UpdateMemo`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:401`), and the payload runner’s full rebuild over every memo (`memopayload.Runner.RunOnce`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:26-72`).

Extracted properties: unique tags; mentions (extracted but not persisted by `RebuildMemoPayload`); and payload properties—title from an initial H1, whether the content has links, code, a task list, or incomplete tasks (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:376-404`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:85-86`).
