**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. There is no external message broker in this delivery path.

**Actual mechanism:** the webhook package declares an in-process, buffered Go channel:

- Queue declaration: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.
- Four goroutine workers are started in `init` and consume with `for payload := range asyncPostQueue` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`.
- `PostAsync` enqueues payloads using a non-blocking send; when the buffer is full, it drops the dispatch and logs a warning (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
