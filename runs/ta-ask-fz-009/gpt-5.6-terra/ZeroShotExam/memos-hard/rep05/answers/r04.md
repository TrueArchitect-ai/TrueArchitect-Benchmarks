**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from **neither NATS nor RabbitMQ**. They consume from an in-process, buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue`.

**Actual mechanism:** the queue is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutine workers that range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). `PostAsync` enqueues deliveries non-blockingly and drops them if the 128-item buffer is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
