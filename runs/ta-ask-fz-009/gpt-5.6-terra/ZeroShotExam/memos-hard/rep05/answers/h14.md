`server/runner/memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` computes the stored derived payload by parsing `memo.Content` through `markdown.Service.ExtractAll`, then writes `Payload.Tags` and `Payload.Property` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-87`); **triggers:** memo creation (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:84-115`), an update whose field mask includes `content` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:435-451`), and the payload runner’s full rebuild of every memo (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:26-64`); **extracted properties:** deduplicated tags plus markdown properties—title from a first-block H1, and booleans for links, code, task lists, and incomplete tasks (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:355-421`). The parser also extracts deduplicated lowercase mentions, but `RebuildMemoPayload` does not store them in the memo payload (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:379-380`, `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:85-86`).
