**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism:** the process uses an in-memory Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as a bounded `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with capacity 128 in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutines that consume that channel and call `Post` for each payload (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). `PostAsync` enqueues work non-blockingly and drops it when the buffer is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
