**Premise correction:** Redis is not configured for this cache layer—there is no Redis client/dependency or connection configuration in the repository.

**Actual mechanism:** `Store.New` constructs a process-local, `sync.Map`-backed cache for users (`userCache`) in `store/store.go:37-53`.
