**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The delivery boundary is an in-process Go channel, not an external message broker.

**Actual mechanism:** `asyncPostQueue` is declared as a buffered channel of 128 webhook payloads in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutine workers that range over that channel and invoke `Post` for each payload (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). Producers enqueue with non-blocking `PostAsync`; if the channel is full, the delivery is dropped and logged (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
