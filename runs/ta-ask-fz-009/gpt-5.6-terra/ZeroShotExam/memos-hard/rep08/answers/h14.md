**computation site:** `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` parses `memo.Content` through `MarkdownService.ExtractAll` and writes the derived values into `memo.Payload.Tags` and `memo.Payload.Property` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-87`). The Markdown implementation performs the single AST walk in `ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:355-421`).

**triggers:** It runs when a memo is **created** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108`) and when an update includes the **`content`** field (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:436-451`). The payload runner can also rebuild every existing memo in batches, persisting each rebuilt payload (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:26-72`).

**extracted properties:** Unique tags (original case preserved); mentions (lower-cased and deduplicated by exact resulting value); and payload properties: the first top-level H1 as `Title`, plus `HasLink`, `HasCode`, `HasTaskList`, and `HasIncompleteTasks` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:376-405`, `417-421`). Mentions are extracted by the parser but are not assigned to the persisted memo payload in `RebuildMemoPayload` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:85-86`).
