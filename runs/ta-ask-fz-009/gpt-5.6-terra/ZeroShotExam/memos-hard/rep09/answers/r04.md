**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. They consume an in-process Go channel.

**Actual mechanism:** `asyncPostQueue` is declared as a buffered `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` (capacity 128) in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). `PostAsync` enqueues payloads non-blockingly and drops them when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
