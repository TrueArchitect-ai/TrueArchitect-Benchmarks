**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from **neither NATS nor RabbitMQ**.

**Actual mechanism:** the process owns an in-memory buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutines that range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). Producers enqueue through `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-196`); when the channel is full, the delivery is dropped rather than brokered or persisted.
