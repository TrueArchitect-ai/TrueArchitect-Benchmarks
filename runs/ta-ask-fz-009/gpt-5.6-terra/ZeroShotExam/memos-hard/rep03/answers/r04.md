**Premise correction:** webhook delivery workers consume from **neither NATS nor RabbitMQ**. The delivery mechanism is an in-process Go channel, so it is not a broker-backed or durable queue.

**Actual mechanism:** `asyncPostQueue` is declared as a buffered channel of 128 `*WebhookRequestPayload` values in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Package initialization starts four goroutine workers that range over that channel and invoke `Post(payload)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). `PostAsync` enqueues deliveries with a non-blocking send; when the buffer is full, it drops the delivery and logs a warning (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
