`web/src/connect.ts`; access token (sent as `Authorization: Bearer …`), refreshed via the refresh-token credential/cookie; `server/auth`

The `authInterceptor` attaches the current access token, refreshes it before expiry or after one unauthenticated retry, and all Connect clients use that interceptor (`web/src/connect.ts:156-188`). Backend Connect auth delegates through `server/router/api/v1`’s `Authorizer` to `server/auth.Authenticator` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go:217-229`, `server/router/api/v1/authz.go:42-46`); the authenticator validates Access Token V2 or PAT bearer tokens (`server/auth/authenticator.go:210-224`).
