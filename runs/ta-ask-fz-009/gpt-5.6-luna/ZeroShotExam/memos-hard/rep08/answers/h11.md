1st: **User**; 2nd: **Memo** (roughly **6–7 packages** and **5–6 packages**, respectively)

Both are persistent entities defined in `store/user.go` and `store/memo.go`. `store.User` is referenced across authentication, notifications, API v1, fileserver, RSS, and frontend/test packages; `store.Memo` is referenced across notifications, API v1, RSS, memo-payload processing, and frontend/test packages. Examples: `server/auth/authenticator.go`, `server/notification/email.go`, `server/router/api/v1/`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`, `server/router/rss/rss.go`, and `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`.
