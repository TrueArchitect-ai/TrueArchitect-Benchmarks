**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go` declares an in-process buffered Go channel:

- Queue declaration: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`)
- Four goroutines consume from it in `init()`, ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`)
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-196`)

So the queue is an in-memory channel, not an external broker queue.
