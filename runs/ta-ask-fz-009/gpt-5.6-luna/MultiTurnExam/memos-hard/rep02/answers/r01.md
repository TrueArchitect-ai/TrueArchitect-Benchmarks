Premise correction: there is no Redis connection—the cache is an in-memory `sync.Map`-based cache (`store/cache/cache.go:66-84`), instantiated by `store.New` (`store/store.go:37-53`). User entries use the default TTL of **10 minutes**, configured in `store/store.go:39-45` and applied by `userCache.Set` through `store/cache/cache.go:92-114`.
