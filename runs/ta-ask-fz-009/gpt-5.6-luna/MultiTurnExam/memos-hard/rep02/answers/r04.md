Premise correction: webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The package starts four in-process goroutines in `init()` that consume a buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, and call `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`). The queue is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`; `PostAsync` enqueues onto it at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`.
