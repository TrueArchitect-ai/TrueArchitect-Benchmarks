In `Store.DeleteMemo` (store/memo.go:140–159), before deleting the memo row via `s.driver.DeleteMemo`, the store layer deletes:

- **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation`) — two passes: where the memo is the source (`MemoID`, store/memo.go:142) and where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`, store/memo.go:145)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — listed via `ListAttachments{MemoID}` then deleted one-by-one with `DeleteAttachment` (store/memo.go:149–157)

Only after those cleanups does it delete the memo row itself (store/memo.go:158).
