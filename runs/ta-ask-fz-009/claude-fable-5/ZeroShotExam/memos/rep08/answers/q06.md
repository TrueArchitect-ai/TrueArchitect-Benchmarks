**`memo_relation`**

Defined in all three dialect schemas — e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47` (also `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46` and `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`). It links memos to each other via `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` columns plus a `type`, with a uniqueness constraint on `(memo_id, related_memo_id, type)`.
