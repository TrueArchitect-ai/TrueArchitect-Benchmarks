**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`), in the `internal/filter` package.

**Mechanism:** The `ListMemos` handler (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`) passes the request's filter string down to the store, where each dialect driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its own dialect constant — the engine compiles the CEL expression against a memo schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`), converts the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (`buildCondition` in `internal/filter/parser.go`, invoked from `Engine.Compile` at `internal/filter/engine.go:48-71`), and then a per-dialect renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`, switching on `DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`) turns that tree into a parameterized SQL fragment (`Statement{SQL, Args}`) appended to the query's WHERE clause.
