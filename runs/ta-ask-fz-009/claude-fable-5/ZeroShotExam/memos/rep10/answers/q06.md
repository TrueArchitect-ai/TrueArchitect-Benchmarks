**`memo_relation`**

Defined in all three schema dialects — e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47` (also `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46` and `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`). It links one memo to another via `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` columns, with a `type` column describing the relation, and a uniqueness constraint on `(memo_id, related_memo_id, type)`.
