`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

For a fresh (uninitialized) SQLite database, the migrator applies the full schema from `LATEST.sql` instead of replaying incremental migrations — see `store/migrator.go:42` ("LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations") and `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` at `store/migrator.go:56`. The SQLite-dialect copy lives alongside the versioned migration folders in `store/migration/sqlite/`, and `store/test/migrator_test.go:18` verifies it applies cleanly on a fresh install.
