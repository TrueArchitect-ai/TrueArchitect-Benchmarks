When `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518) reaches the store layer, `Store.DeleteMemo` (store/memo.go:140-159) cleans up two other kinds of records before deleting the memo row itself:

- **Memo relations** (`memo_relation` records) — deleted twice, once where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and once where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (store/memo.go:142-147)
- **Attachments** linked to the memo — listed via `ListAttachments` with the memo's ID, then each deleted with `DeleteAttachment` (store/memo.go:149-157)

Only after both cleanups does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row (store/memo.go:158).
