**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`) — the engine lives in `internal/filter/` (`internal/filter/engine.go:13-14` — "Engine parses CEL filters into a dialect-agnostic condition tree").

**Mechanism:** The engine compiles the CEL expression against a schema into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (`Engine.Compile`, `internal/filter/engine.go:48`; IR in `internal/filter/ir.go`), then a per-dialect renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) turns that tree into parameterized SQL for `DialectSQLite`, `DialectMySQL`, or `DialectPostgres` — each dialect driver calls `engine.CompileToStatement(..., RenderOptions{Dialect: ...})` inside `ListMemos` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54`), producing dialect-specific constructs like `ILIKE`/`~` on Postgres vs `LIKE`/`REGEXP` on MySQL/SQLite.
