MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **ConnectRPC** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`: `RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` on the HTTP mux, wrapping `APIV1Service` in `ConnectServiceHandler` (defined in the same file, with the adapter methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`).

2. **REST via gRPC-Gateway** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the service on a `runtime.NewServeMux` gateway, exposing the `google.api.http` bindings from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (e.g. `POST /api/v1/memos`).

Both protocols delegate to the same `APIV1Service` implementation in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
