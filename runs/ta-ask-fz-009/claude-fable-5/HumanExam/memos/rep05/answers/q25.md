— Engine: CEL (Google Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, in `internal/filter` (internal/filter/engine.go:13–27). Mechanism: the engine parses CEL into a dialect-agnostic IR/condition tree (internal/filter/parser.go, ir.go) and renders it to dialect-specific SQL (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`, internal/filter/schema.go:14–16); each driver's ListMemos calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect to append WHERE clauses + args (e.g. store/db/mysql/memo.go:65–69, store/db/postgres/memo.go:54)
