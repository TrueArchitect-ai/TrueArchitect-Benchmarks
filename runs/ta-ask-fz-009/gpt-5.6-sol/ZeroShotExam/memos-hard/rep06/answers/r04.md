Premise correction: the webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

Actual mechanism: they consume from an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:25-40`. Four goroutines are started in `init()` and range over that channel (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). `PostAsync` enqueues payloads non-blockingly and drops them when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
