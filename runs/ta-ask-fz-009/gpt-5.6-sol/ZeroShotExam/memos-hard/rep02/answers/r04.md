**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism:** They consume from an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutines are started in `init()` and range over that channel (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`). Producers enqueue non-blockingly; deliveries are dropped when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:189-192`). There is no external broker queue declaration.
