Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery runs through an in-process buffered Go channel.

Actual mechanism: `asyncPostQueue` is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutine workers are started in `init()` and consume from that channel (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-53`). `PostAsync` enqueues payloads non-blockingly and drops them when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
