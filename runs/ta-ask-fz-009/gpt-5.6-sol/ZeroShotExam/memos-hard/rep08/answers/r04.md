Premise correction: the webhook workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

Actual mechanism: they consume from an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared with capacity 128 in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutines are started in `init()` and range over that channel (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-53`); `PostAsync` enqueues payloads non-blockingly and drops them when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
