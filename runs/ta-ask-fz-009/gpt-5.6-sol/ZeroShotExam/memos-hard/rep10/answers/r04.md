Premise correction: the webhook workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. They read from an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.

Actual mechanism: package initialization starts workers that range over that channel and call `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-46`). `PostAsync` enqueues non-blockingly and drops delivery when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-194`). There is no externally declared broker queue.
