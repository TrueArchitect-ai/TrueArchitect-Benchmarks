CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go`; engine: `internal/filter/engine.go`. The engine compiles CEL into a dialect-neutral condition tree, then `AppendConditions` renders parameterized SQL using SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL dialect rules and appends it to each driver’s `WHERE` clause (`internal/filter/helpers.go`, `internal/filter/render.go`; `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`).
