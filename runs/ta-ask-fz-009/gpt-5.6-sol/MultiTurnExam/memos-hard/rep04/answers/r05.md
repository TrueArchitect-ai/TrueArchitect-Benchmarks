Premise correction: there is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API. The public API contract is defined in Protocol Buffer service files under `proto/api/v1/`—for example, `MemoService` and its HTTP mappings are in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-49`. These contracts are exposed through gRPC-Gateway REST routes under `/api/v1/*` and Connect RPC handlers under `/memos.api.v1.*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:95-144`). An OpenAPI document is generated at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml`.
