There is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL endpoint. The public API contract is defined as Protocol Buffer services under `proto/api/v1/`, with `google.api.http` annotations exposing REST-style `/api/v1/*` routes—for example, `MemoService` at `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-49`. Runtime wiring registers those services through gRPC-Gateway and also exposes Connect RPC handlers (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64-144`).
