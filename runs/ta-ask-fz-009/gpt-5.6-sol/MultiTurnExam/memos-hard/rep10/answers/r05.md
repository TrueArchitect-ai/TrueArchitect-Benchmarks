Premise correction: there is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API. The public API contract is defined as protobuf service schemas under `proto/api/v1/`—for example, `MemoService` and its HTTP mappings are in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17`. Those RPCs are exposed as REST-style `/api/v1` endpoints through the gRPC-Gateway registered at `server/server.go:87-89`; additional public surfaces include SSE, file-serving, and RSS routes.
