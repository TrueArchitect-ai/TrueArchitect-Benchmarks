Premise correction: there is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API. The public API contract is defined with Protocol Buffers under `proto/api/v1/*.proto`; for example, `MemoService` and its HTTP mappings are at `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-80`. Those contracts are exposed through gRPC-Gateway REST routes under `/api/v1/*` and Connect RPC routes under `/memos.api.v1.*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:95-144`), plus SSE at `/api/v1/sse` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:123-125`).
