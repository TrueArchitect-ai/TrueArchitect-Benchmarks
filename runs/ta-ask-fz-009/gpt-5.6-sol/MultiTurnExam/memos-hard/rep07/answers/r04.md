Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared with capacity 128 at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:25-40`. Four goroutine workers are started by package initialization and consume directly from that channel, calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`). `PostAsync` performs a non-blocking enqueue and drops delivery when the channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195`).
