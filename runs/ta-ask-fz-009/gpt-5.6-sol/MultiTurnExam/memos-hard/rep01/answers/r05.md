There is no GraphQL schema or GraphQL API. The public API contract is defined as protobuf services with `google.api.http` annotations under `proto/api/v1/`—for example, `MemoService` in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-49`. These contracts are exposed as REST through gRPC-Gateway and as Connect RPC handlers in `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64-134`).
