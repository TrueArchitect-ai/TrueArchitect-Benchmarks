There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is protobuf-defined gRPC with HTTP/REST annotations—for example `MemoService` (`proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17-49`) and `UserService` (`proto/api/v1/user_service.proto:17-44`)—registered through gRPC-Gateway and Connect (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64-99`).
