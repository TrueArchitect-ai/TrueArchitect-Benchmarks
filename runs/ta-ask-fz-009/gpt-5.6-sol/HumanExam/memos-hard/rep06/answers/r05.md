There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is defined as protobuf RPC services with `google.api.http` routes, for example `MemoService` (`proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto:17`), and exposed through gRPC-Gateway/Connect plus native Echo routes (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:120`).
