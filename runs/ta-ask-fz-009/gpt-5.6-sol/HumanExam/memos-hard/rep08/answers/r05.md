There is no GraphQL schema. The public API is protobuf-defined gRPC/Connect plus gRPC-Gateway HTTP endpoints; handlers are registered in `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:64`–`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:99`), with generated OpenAPI at `proto/gen/openapi.yaml` and native routes such as attachments/avatar registered by the fileserver (`server/server.go:80`–`server/server.go:85`).
