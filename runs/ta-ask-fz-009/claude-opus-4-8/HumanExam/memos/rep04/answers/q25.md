— Engine: Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`) — internal/filter/engine.go (env in internal/filter/schema.go). Mechanism: the CEL expression is parsed to an AST/IR, then internal/filter/render.go's dialect-aware `renderer` (with `DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL`) emits a parameterized SQL WHERE clause; each dialect's memo store (store/db/*/memo.go) calls `filter.AppendConditions(..., DialectX, &where, &args)` to append that SQL.
