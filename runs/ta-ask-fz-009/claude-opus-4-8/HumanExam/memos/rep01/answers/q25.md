Engine: CEL (Google Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, in internal/filter (engine.go:13–25, `Engine` with `cel.Env`; entry `DefaultEngine`). Mechanism: the CEL filter is compiled to a dialect-agnostic condition tree, then a `renderer` (internal/filter/render.go) emits dialect-specific SQL — switching on `DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL` (render.go:230+) for column qualification, placeholders, and date functions — which the dialect memo.go drivers splice into their queries.
