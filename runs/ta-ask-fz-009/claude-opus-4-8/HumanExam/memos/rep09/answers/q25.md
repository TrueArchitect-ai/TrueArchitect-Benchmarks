Engine: Google CEL (`cel-go`) — `internal/filter/engine.go` (parses via `cel.NewEnv`/`Compile` → `AstToParsedExpr`, builds a dialect-agnostic condition tree). Mechanism: the CEL AST is compiled into a `Condition` IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`, `parser.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL by `internal/filter/render.go` (per-dialect tokens, e.g. `accessorSpec`), producing a `Statement` the store layer splices into the memo query for SQLite/Postgres/MySQL.
