Memo lifecycle events → dispatch functions (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`):
- memo created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (:15)
- memo updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (:20)
- memo deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (:25)
- memo comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (:30)
