At dial time it resolves the target hostname (`net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost`) and rejects the connection if any resolved IP is reserved/private, via `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44` (checked against `reservedNetworks`). Because the check runs on the *resolved* address at dial time rather than on the URL string, it also defeats DNS-rebinding.
