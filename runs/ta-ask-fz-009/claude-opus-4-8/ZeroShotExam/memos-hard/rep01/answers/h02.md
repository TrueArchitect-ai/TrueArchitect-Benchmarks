Two notes worth flagging:
- The dial-time check is the load-bearing guard (it defeats DNS rebinding because it runs at connection time, not just at save time). There's also an up-front `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) that rejects reserved-IP URLs when a webhook is created/updated, but that alone would be TOCTOU-vulnerable — the transport-level dial guard is what actually protects delivery.
- The protection is bypassable by design: setting `AllowPrivateIPs = true` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) makes `isReservedIP` always return `false`, disabling both checks for self-hosted deployments targeting local networks.
