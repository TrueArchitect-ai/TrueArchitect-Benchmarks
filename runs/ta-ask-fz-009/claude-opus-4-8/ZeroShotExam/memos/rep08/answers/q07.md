The ConnectRPC service clients are created in **`web/src/connect.ts`**.

That file sets up a `createConnectTransport` (web/src/connect.ts:184) and exports each service client via `createClient(...)` — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (web/src/connect.ts:192–203).
