Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140–159`) removes:

- **`memo_relation` records where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`)
- **`memo_relation` records where the memo is the target/related memo** — `DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:145`)
- **Attachments linked to the memo** — listed via `ListAttachments` and deleted one by one with `DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:148–157`)

Only after these does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row (`store/memo.go:158`, e.g. the `DELETE FROM memo` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:237`).
