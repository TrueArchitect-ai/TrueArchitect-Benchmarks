The ConnectRPC service clients are created there — the transport is built via `createConnectTransport` (web/src/connect.ts:184), and each service client is instantiated with `createClient(...)` and exported: `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (web/src/connect.ts:192–203).
