**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` streams `Content-Type: text/event-stream` with `data: <json>\n\n` frames (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:27,48,93`). Clients subscribe to an in-process `SSEHub`, and each broadcast is fan-out-filtered per client visibility/role via `canReceive`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` type (`clients map[*SSEClient]struct{}` guarded by a `sync.RWMutex`) with `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Close`, and `Broadcast`. The route/connection side lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`).

**Memo handlers that broadcast through it** (`SSEHub.Broadcast` callers, per the map):
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70` (emits `memo.created`, unless SSE is suppressed)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:20` (emits `memo.comment.created`)
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518` (emits `memo.deleted`)
