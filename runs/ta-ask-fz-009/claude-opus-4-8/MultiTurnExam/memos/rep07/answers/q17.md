web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient

The memo CRUD mutations/queries live in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` — it contains the query hooks (`useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo`) and mutation hooks (create/update/delete memo). It imports and uses the generated `memoServiceClient` from `web/src/connect.ts` (calling `.listMemos`, `.getMemo`, `.createMemo`, `.updateMemo`, `.deleteMemo`).
