**Computation site:** `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74`. It runs the memo's content through the goldmark markdown service (`markdownService.ExtractAll([]byte(memo.Content))`, `runner.go:80`) and writes the result onto `memo.Payload` (`runner.go:85-86`). This is the derived metadata stored in the `payload` column and used for lists/previews.

**Triggers (operations that cause recomputation):** the call-graph shows `RebuildMemoPayload` has exactly three callers —
- `v1.APIV1Service.CreateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70`, invoking it at `memo_service.go:108`) — recomputed on memo creation.
- `v1.APIV1Service.UpdateMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:401`, invoking it at `memo_service.go:447`) — recomputed when a memo's content is edited.
- `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27`) — a batch backfill that rebuilds the payload for *all* memos (though, per h08, this runner's `RunOnce` is never actually invoked from production wiring, so in practice recomputation happens on create and update).

**Extracted properties:** `ExtractAll` returns `markdown.ExtractedData` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:21`), and `RebuildMemoPayload` copies two of its fields onto the payload:
- **`Tags`** — the `#tags` found in the content (`memo.Payload.Tags = data.Tags`, `runner.go:85`).
- **`Property`** — a `MemoPayload_Property` of boolean/flag facts computed from the content (`memo.Payload.Property = data.Property`, `runner.go:86`), namely: `HasLink`, `HasTaskList`, `HasCode`, `HasIncompleteTasks` (`proto/gen/store/memo.pb.go:87-90`), plus the title extracted from the first H1 heading (`memo.pb.go:91`+).

(The `ExtractedData.Mentions` field also exists but is not persisted into the payload by `RebuildMemoPayload`; mentions are handled separately.) So the derived metadata is the memo's tag list and its content-flag/title property block, recomputed on every create and update.
