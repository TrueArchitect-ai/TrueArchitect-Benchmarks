**Computation site — `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` at `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74.**

**Triggers (recomputation happens on):**
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:70` (memo created)
- `APIV1Service.UpdateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:401` (memo content updated)
- `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27` (the batch backfill that rebuilds payloads for all memos)

**What the computation extracts:** it feeds the memo's `Content` through the markdown service's `ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:33`), then writes two derived fields onto `memo.Payload` (runner.go:85-86):
- **`Tags`** — the `#tags` parsed out of the content
- **`Property`** — a `MemoPayload_Property` derived from the content (structural flags/properties surfaced by parsing)

These land in `store.Memo.Payload` (a `storepb.MemoPayload`), which is what lists and previews read — i.e., metadata computed from content, not set by the user. (`ExtractAll` also yields `Mentions` in its `ExtractedData` struct, but `RebuildMemoPayload` only persists `Tags` and `Property` into the payload; mentions are handled on a separate path.)
