Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39); 4 worker goroutines are started in `init()` and call `Post` per payload (webhook.go:42-55); producers enqueue non-blockingly via `PostAsync`, dropping with a warning when the buffer is full (webhook.go:184-196).
