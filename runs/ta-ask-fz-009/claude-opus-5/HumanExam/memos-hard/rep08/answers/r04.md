Webhook delivery is in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue` of capacity **128** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) drained by **4 goroutines** started in `package init` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which **drops** the event with a warning when the queue is full (`:189-195`). No queue is "declared" anywhere — the channel literal at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` is the whole mechanism, and it is not durable across restarts.
