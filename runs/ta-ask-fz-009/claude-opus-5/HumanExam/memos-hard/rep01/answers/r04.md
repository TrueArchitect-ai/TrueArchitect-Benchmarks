— Premise wrong: there is no message broker at all — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (no such dependencies in go.mod; no amqp/nats references in the Go sources), and therefore no queue declaration. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–55) that call `Post` (:120); enqueue is non-blocking and **drops** on overflow (`PostAsync`, :184–196). Deliveries are lost on restart; there is no retry or persistence.
