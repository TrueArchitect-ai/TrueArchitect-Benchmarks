— Premise wrong: neither. No NATS, RabbitMQ/AMQP, or any broker is used (no such dependency or import in the repo). Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39), drained by 4 goroutines started in the package `init()` (webhook.go:42‑55), each calling `webhook.Post` (webhook.go:120). Producers enqueue non-blockingly via `PostAsync`, dropping with a warning when the queue is full (webhook.go:189‑195). Nothing is durable: queued webhooks are lost on restart.
