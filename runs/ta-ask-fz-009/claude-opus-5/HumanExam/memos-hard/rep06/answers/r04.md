— Premise wrong: neither — there is no message broker and no queue declaration (no NATS/RabbitMQ/AMQP identifier in the Go tree; nothing in go.mod). Webhook delivery is in-process: a package-level buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39) drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55), each calling `Post` (webhook.go:120). Enqueue is non-blocking and drops on overflow with a warning (`PostAsync`, webhook.go:184-196) — so deliveries are best-effort and lost on process restart.
