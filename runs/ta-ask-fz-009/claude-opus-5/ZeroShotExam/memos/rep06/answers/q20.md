### Mechanism
SSE, not WebSockets. The transport endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` and served by `handleSSE`, which sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25`, `:27`, `:34`, `:48`). Connections are authenticated at subscribe time — `Subscribe(userID, role)` captures the identity on the client record (`sse_hub.go:71`).

### Hub
`SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`) is an in-process, mutex-guarded fan-out registry: `clients map[*SSEClient]struct{}` behind a `sync.RWMutex`, with `NewSSEHub` (:63), `Subscribe` (:71), `Unsubscribe` (:89), `Close` (:99), `Broadcast` (:115).

Two invariants worth naming:
- **Non-blocking fan-out.** Each client has a 32-event buffered channel; `Broadcast` does a `select` with a `default` that *drops* the event for a slow consumer rather than stalling the broadcaster (`sse_hub.go:74`, `:126-130`). Delivery is therefore best-effort — clients must tolerate gaps.
- **Authorization at the edge of delivery, not at emit.** `canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:134`) filters per-client: `Private` memos reach only the creator or an admin; public/protected/empty visibility pass. `Visibility` and `CreatorID` are `json:"-"` (`sse_hub.go:32-33`), so filtering metadata never crosses the wire.

Event contract: six types — `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (`sse_hub.go:14-21`), payload `{type, name, parent?}` (`SSEEvent`, :24).
