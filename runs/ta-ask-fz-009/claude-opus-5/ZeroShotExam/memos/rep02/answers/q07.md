All ConnectRPC service clients are created there via `createClient(...)` over a shared `createConnectTransport` instance — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:184–203`), plus a separate `refreshAuthClient` on its own transport for token refresh (`web/src/connect.ts:65–72`).
