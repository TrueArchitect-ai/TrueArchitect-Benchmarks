**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (HTTP `text/event-stream`) over a single `GET /api/v1/sse` endpoint — Bearer-token authenticated, `data: {json}\n\n` frames plus a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:15-53`, route registered at `sse_handler.go:25-30`). No WebSockets.

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (struct at :56) holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`; `Subscribe` (:71) gives each client a 32-slot buffered channel, `Broadcast` (:115) fans out non-blockingly and drops events for slow clients, `Close` (:99) tears everything down at shutdown. Per-client authorization filtering lives in `SSEClient.canReceive` (:134): private memos only reach the creator or an admin. Event vocabulary is the six `SSEEventType` constants at `sse_hub.go:14-21` (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`).
