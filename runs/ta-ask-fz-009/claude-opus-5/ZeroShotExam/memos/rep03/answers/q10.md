**q10.**

`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

That's the full-schema bootstrap file for fresh installs. The migrator embeds the `migration` FS and resolves the path per driver as `migration/{driver}/LATEST.sql`, with `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` declared at `store/migrator.go:56`; the documented flow at `store/migrator.go:27` and `:42` states that `preMigrate` applies `LATEST.sql` when the DB is uninitialized, instead of replaying the incremental `store/migration/sqlite/{version}/NN__*.sql` files. Confirmed by `TestFreshInstall` in `store/test/migrator_test.go:18-26`.
