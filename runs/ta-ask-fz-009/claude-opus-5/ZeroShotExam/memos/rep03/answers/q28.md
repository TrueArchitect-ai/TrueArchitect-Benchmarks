**Runners the server actually starts** — all wiring lives in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`), called from `Server.Start` (`server/server.go:129`):

- **s3presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)** — started twice-over: one synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at boot (`server/server.go:163`), then a long-lived goroutine on `Run(s3Context)` driven by a `time.NewTicker` of `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,30`). Each tick re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (`runner.go:85-91`). Cancelled via its own `context.CancelFunc` registered in `backgroundRunnerCancels` and awaited through `backgroundRunnerWG` on shutdown (`server/server.go:156-171`, `176-201`).

That is the **only** entry in `startBackgroundRunners` — no other runner is launched there.

Adjacent periodic goroutines that exist but are not "runners" started by `startBackgroundRunners`:

Request-path infrastructure, not scheduled work.
