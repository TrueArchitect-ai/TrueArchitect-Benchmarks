| `memo_share` | Not deleted by code — relies on the schema FK `memo_id REFERENCES memo(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` | `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:109`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:108`, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:108` |

**Explicitly *not* touched:** `reaction`. `DeleteMemo` calls `ListReactions` only, to build the webhook payload (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:545-565`); no reaction rows are deleted, and the `reaction` table has no FK to `memo` in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:92`. Reactions of a deleted memo are orphaned.

**Caveat on the `memo_share` cascade:** SQLite opens with foreign keys *disabled* — `_pragma=foreign_keys(0)` at `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go:53` — so on SQLite deployments the `ON DELETE CASCADE` does not fire and `memo_share` rows are also orphaned. On MySQL/Postgres the cascade is enforced by the engine.

So: **`memo`, `memo_relation`, `attachment`** (code-driven), plus **`memo_share`** via DB-level cascade on MySQL/Postgres only.
