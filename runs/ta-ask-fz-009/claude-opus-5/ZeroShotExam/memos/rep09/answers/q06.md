**`memo_relation`**

It stores the link as `(memo_id, related_memo_id, type)` — e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (also mysql/postgres LATEST.sql), with the store layer in `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go:14`, and `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go:13`. The `type` column distinguishes relation kinds such as `COMMENT`, which `store/db/postgres/memo.go:135-136` joins on to resolve a memo's parent.
