**Runners the server actually starts**

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — one immediate synchronous `RunOnce` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a `time.NewTicker` loop on a hard-coded 12-hour `runnerInterval` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,29-41`); the goroutine is registered on `backgroundRunnerWG` and killed by its own cancel func on shutdown (`server/server.go:156-171`, `176-201`). - **HTTP serve goroutine** (`server/server.go:124-128`) — event-driven, not scheduled; `http.Server.Serve` on the TCP/unix listener.
- **Store cache janitors ×3** (`store/cache/cache.go:83` → `cleanupLoop`, `store/cache/cache.go:200-215`) — started transitively when `store.New` constructs `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (`store/store.go:51-53`, called from `cmd/memos/main.go:64`), each on a `CleanupInterval` ticker of 5 minutes (`store/store.go:40-45`); stopped via `Store.Close()` in `Server.Shutdown` (`store/store.go:72-79`, `server/server.go:146`).
- **SSE heartbeat ticker** (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`) — per-connection and request-scoped, 30s `sseHeartbeatInterval` (`:17`); not a background runner, but it is a live timer the server drives; connections are force-closed via `sseHub.Close()` (`server/server.go:203-208`).
