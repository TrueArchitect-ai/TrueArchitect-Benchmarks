**Server-Sent Events (SSE) over `GET /api/v1/sse`; `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (transport handler in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`); broadcasting handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo` (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`**

**Mechanism.** Plain SSE, not WebSockets. `handleSSE` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` (`sse_handler.go:48`), authenticates via Bearer token, then `hub.Subscribe(userID, role)` (`sse_handler.go:60`), with a 30s heartbeat to keep the connection alive (`sse_handler.go:17`). The route is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` at `sse_handler.go:25-30`.

**Hub.** `SSEHub` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56` — an `RWMutex`-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`/`Broadcast`. Two invariants worth knowing:
- **Non-blocking fan-out:** each client has a 32-event buffer; a full buffer means the event is *dropped*, not queued (`sse_hub.go:126-130`). Live refresh is best-effort, not a guaranteed delivery channel.
- **Server-side visibility filtering:** `SSEClient.canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:134`) gates `Private` events to the creator or an admin; `Visibility`/`CreatorID` are `json:"-"` so they never reach the wire (`sse_hub.go:32-33`).
