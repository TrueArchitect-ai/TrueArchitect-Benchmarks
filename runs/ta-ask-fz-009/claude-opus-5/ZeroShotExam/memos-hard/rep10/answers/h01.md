**`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`) — every 12 hours, plus one immediate pass at startup — wired in `server/server.go:162-171` (invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`).**

How it works:

- **What goes stale:** attachments stored in S3 keep a presigned GET URL in their `reference` column. Presigned URLs expire, so the link rots unless refreshed. `CheckAndPresign` pages through `store.ListAttachments` filtered to `AttachmentStorageType_S3` in batches of 100, re-presigns via `s3.NewClient(...).PresignGetObject`, and writes back the new URL plus `LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).
- **Schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` drives a ticker in `Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`). Within each pass, an attachment is skipped if its `LastPresignedTime` is under 4 days old — the presign expiry is 5 days, so there's a 1-day safety margin (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).
- **Wiring:** `startBackgroundRunners` builds the runner with the shared `*store.Store`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously so links are fresh before serving traffic, then launches `Run(s3Context)` on a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` with its cancel func stored in `backgroundRunnerCancels` (`server/server.go:153-174`), so `Shutdown` can stop and drain it (`server/server.go:134-151`).

The sibling runner directory `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` exists but is not started here — `s3presign` is the only background runner registered in `startBackgroundRunners`.
