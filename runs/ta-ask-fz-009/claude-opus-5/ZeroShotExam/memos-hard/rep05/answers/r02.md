**The only per-request quota that exists is a size cap, not a rate cap:** `maxAPIRequestBytes = 256 << 20` (256 MiB) at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:20`, enforced via `http.MaxBytesHandler` on the grpc-gateway routes (`:126`), the Connect mux (`:144`), and as `connect.WithReadMaxBytes` on the Connect handlers (`:141`).
