**delivery function — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`** (the actual `safeClient.Do(req)` call is at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`). Callers normally go through `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`); each worker calls `Post`.

**protection mechanism — two layers, both keyed on `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`:**

1. **Dial-time SSRF guard (the one that protects delivery itself):** `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, installed as the `Transport.DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). It resolves the host and refuses the connection if any resolved address falls in a reserved range, which is what defeats DNS rebinding — validation-time resolution alone could be re-pointed before dispatch.
2. **Admission-time validation:** `ValidateURL` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:62` — enforces absolute URL, `http`/`https` scheme only, and non-reserved resolution. It runs when webhooks are registered, from `CreateUserWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:53`) and `UpdateUserWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:106`).

The blocked ranges are the `reservedCIDRs` list at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23` — IPv4 loopback, all three RFC-1918 blocks, link-local incl. cloud IMDS `169.254.0.0/16`, plus IPv6 loopback, unique-local, and link-local.

**Documented escape hatch:** the package-level `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP` to `false`, disabling *both* layers at once. It is wired from the `--allow-private-webhooks` CLI flag (`cmd/memos/main.go:133`, bound at `cmd/memos/main.go:160`, assigned at `cmd/memos/main.go:49`) for self-hosted deployments targeting LAN services.
