**delivery function — `Post()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`** (with `PostAsync()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` enqueuing onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 background workers, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`); **protection mechanism — dial-time reserved-IP rejection via `safeDialContext` / `isReservedIP` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59` and `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`, with the CIDR deny-list at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`.**

How the boundary is actually enforced:

- `Post` marshals the payload, optionally attaches Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers (`webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature`), and issues the request through the package-level `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`). That client is the only egress path — its `Transport.DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`).
- `safeDialContext` re-resolves the host at connect time and refuses the dial if *any* resolved address falls in a reserved range (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:65-74`). Placing the check at dial time rather than at URL-parse time is what closes the DNS-rebinding hole — a name that validated as public earlier cannot flip to `127.0.0.1` before the connection is made.
- There is a second, earlier check at the API boundary: `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects non-http(s) schemes and reserved-resolving hostnames with a gRPC `InvalidArgument`, so bad webhook URLs fail at create/update time instead of silently failing later.
- The deny-list covers IPv4 loopback, all three RFC-1918 blocks, link-local including the cloud IMDS address `169.254.169.254`, plus IPv6 loopback, unique-local, and link-local (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`).
- The escape hatch is a single global: `webhook.AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`), short-circuiting `isReservedIP`. It's wired to the `--allow-private-webhooks` flag in `cmd/memos/main.go:49` (declared at `cmd/memos/main.go:133`), which disables *both* layers at once since they share `isReservedIP`.
