h02.

**delivery function — `webhook.Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`** (marshals the payload, signs it, and issues the actual `POST` via `safeClient.Do` at line 152). Callers reach it through `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 background workers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`); the dispatch sites are `APIV1Service.DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` and `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:30,48`.

**protection mechanism — two layers, both keyed on `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`:**

1. **Dial-time enforcement (the one that actually protects delivery):** `safeDialContext` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`. It is installed as the `Transport.DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`), re-resolves the host on every connection, and refuses the dial if any resolved address falls in a reserved range (line 71-73). Because the check happens at dial time rather than at URL-registration time, it also defeats DNS rebinding — the comment at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:30-31` states this intent explicitly.
2. **Admission-time validation:** `webhook.ValidateURL` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`. Enforces absolute URL, `http`/`https` scheme only, and no reserved-IP resolution, returning a gRPC `InvalidArgument`. Called from `CreateUserWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:53`) and `UpdateUserWebhook` (`server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:106`).

The blocked ranges are the `reservedCIDRs` list at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`: IPv4/IPv6 loopback, all three RFC-1918 blocks, link-local `169.254.0.0/16` (explicitly covering the cloud IMDS address `169.254.169.254`), IPv6 unique-local `fc00::/7`, and `fe80::/10`.

One caveat worth flagging: the whole guard is globally defeatable. `webhook.AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP` to `false`, disabling *both* layers at once, and it is wired to the `--allow-private-webhooks` CLI flag in `cmd/memos/main.go:49,133,160`. That is a deliberate self-hosting escape hatch, but it means SSRF protection is a startup-configurable property of the deployment, not an invariant of the delivery path.
