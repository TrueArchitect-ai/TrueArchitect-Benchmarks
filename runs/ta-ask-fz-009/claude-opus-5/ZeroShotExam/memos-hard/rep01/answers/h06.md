**Convergence function — `webhook.Post` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`**

The funnel is two-staged: every dispatcher path ends at `webhook.PostAsync` (only two call sites: `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`), which just enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`). Four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`) drain that queue and call `Post`, which is the sole place the outbound request is built and executed — `http.NewRequest("POST", requestPayload.URL, ...)` at `webhook.go:126` and `safeClient.Do(req)` at `webhook.go:152`.

The user-configured URL enters via `payload.URL = hook.Url`, read from `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:41`, `:67`) — the webhooks users register through `UserService.CreateUserWebhook`/`UpdateUserWebhook`. Egress is constrained at dial time by `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go:59`), which rejects reserved/private IPs to blunt SSRF and DNS rebinding.
