### The shutdown chain

All the storage-side closes hang off one call. `Server.Shutdown` (`server/server.go:134`) invokes `s.Store.Close()` at line 146, and `Store.Close` (`store/store.go:72-79`) fans out:

1. Three cache shutdowns — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (lines 74–76) — each stopping a cleanup goroutine.
2. Then `s.driver.Close()` (line 78), which is **interface dispatch** through `store.Driver.Close` (`store/driver.go:12`) to whichever concrete `DB.Close` the factory selected in `db.NewDBDriver` (`store/db/db.go:14`).

That's why the three per-dialect `DB.Close` methods have no direct textual caller — exactly one of them runs per process, chosen at startup.

`SSEHub.Close` is on a separate branch: `Server.Shutdown` calls `closeLongLivedConnections` (`server/server.go:206`), which closes the hub *before* `httpServer.Shutdown` at line 214. The comment at line 204 explains the ordering — long-lived SSE requests would otherwise never finish on their own and would hang graceful shutdown.
