### Architectural reading

The concentration in `server/router/api/v1` — 114 of `User`'s references, 47 of `Memo`'s — is the significant signal. Store structs are used directly as the working type in the API layer rather than being mapped to domain types at the boundary; the `convert*ToProto` helpers (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/user_service_converters.go`) translate only at the response edge. That's a pragmatic choice, but it means a change to `store.User`'s shape propagates straight into the RPC layer with no insulating seam.
