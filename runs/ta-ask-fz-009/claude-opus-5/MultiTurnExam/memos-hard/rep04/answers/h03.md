**`internal/idp` — `internal/idp/idp.go`; `internal/idp/oauth2` — `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`.**

- **Abstraction**: `internal/idp` is deliberately thin — it defines only the shared result contract `IdentityProviderUserInfo{Identifier, DisplayName, Email, AvatarURL}` (`internal/idp/idp.go:3-8`). There is no Go `interface` here; the boundary is the normalized user-info struct that every provider must produce, not a method set.
- **Implementation**: OAuth2 is the only concrete protocol that ships. `internal/idp/oauth2` declares itself "the OAuth2 identity provider integration" (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go:1`) with `IdentityProvider` (`:20`), `NewIdentityProvider` (`:27`), `ExchangeToken` including PKCE support (`:47`), and `UserInfo` returning `*idp.IdentityProviderUserInfo` (`:83`). `oauth2` is the sole subdirectory under `internal/idp`.
- Configuration comes from the store as `storepb.OAuth2Config` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go:21`), with required fields — clientId, clientSecret, tokenUrl, userInfoUrl, fieldMapping.identifier — enforced at construction (`:28-38`). The `fieldMapping` indirection is what lets one OAuth2 implementation cover arbitrary providers instead of needing per-vendor packages.
