Details:

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner`, constructed by `NewRunner(store)` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:20`). Its work loop `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:47`) pages through S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 (`runner.go:55-65`), re-presigns any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (`runner.go:85-91`) via `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:71`), and writes the fresh URL back into `Attachment.Reference` plus a new `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:114-123`).
- **Why it's needed**: presigned GET URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77`), so the stored `reference` would otherwise go dead. The 4-day refresh threshold leaves a day of margin.
- **Schedule**: `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` with a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:27-41`); startup also does a synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` before the goroutine starts (`server/server.go:163`).
- **Wiring/lifecycle**: `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) gives it its own cancellable context and registers the cancel + WaitGroup so `Shutdown` → `stopBackgroundRunners`/`waitBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:140-143`) can drain it cleanly.
