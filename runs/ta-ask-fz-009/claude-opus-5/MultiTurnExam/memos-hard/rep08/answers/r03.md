**No such job exists. There is no nightly database-cleanup job, and the cron scheduler is never used by the server at all** — so there is no schedule to report.

Two separate premise failures:

**1. The cron scheduler is dead code.** `internal/scheduler/` is a complete, self-contained cron library (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, plus README and doc.go), but nothing in the application wires it up:
- `[ArchMap query]` returns `0/0` with an explicit **certified zero** (widening probes across view, depth, and kinds found nothing).
- Every occurrence of `scheduler.New` / `.Register(` is inside the package's own tests — `internal/scheduler/example_test.go` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go` — or its documentation. The import `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` appears in exactly two files, both `_test.go`.

**2. The `"0 2 * * *"` schedule you may be thinking of is documentation, not configuration.** A `daily-cleanup` job at `Schedule: "0 2 * * *", // 2 AM daily` appears in the package's usage examples — `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-35` and `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` — as an illustration of the API. Likewise `"stale-session-cleanup"` at `"0 3 * * *"` (`internal/scheduler/README.md:241-245`). These are sample snippets in comments and Markdown; no handler behind them exists.

**What actually runs on a timer.** Background work is not cron-driven. `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153-174`) starts exactly one recurring runner — `s3presign.Runner`, on a plain `time.Ticker` at 12-hour intervals (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27, 30`) — plus one immediate pass at startup (`server/server.go:163`). No wall-clock time-of-day scheduling is involved anywhere; intervals are relative to process start.

**Cleanup that does exist is event-driven, not scheduled.** Data removal happens synchronously inside the delete-user transaction — `deleteUserTargetsTx` (`store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go`, and the mysql/postgres equivalents) removes reactions, attachments, memo shares, inboxes, identities, settings, relations, and memos when a user is deleted. In-memory cache expiry is likewise interval-based, a 5-minute `CleanupInterval` sweep per cache (`store/store.go:42`, `store/cache/cache.go:200-215`), not a nightly job.
