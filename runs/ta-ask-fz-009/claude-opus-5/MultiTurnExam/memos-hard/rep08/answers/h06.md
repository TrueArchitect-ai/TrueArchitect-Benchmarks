Two structural points about the convergence:

- **The intermediate layer is nearly, but not fully, a single funnel.** `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` / `Updated` / `Deleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15, 20, 25`) are thin wrappers that all delegate to `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`:48`), differing only in activity-type string. But `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`:30`) bypasses that helper and calls `webhook.PostAsync` directly at `:43` — it targets the *related memo owner's* hooks rather than the actor's. So `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` is not the common point; `PostAsync` is.
- **The user-configured URL enters at the loop.** Both dispatch sites iterate the creator's stored hooks and assign `payload.URL = hook.Url` plus `payload.SigningSecret = hook.SigningSecret` (`:41-42` and `:67-68`) before enqueuing — one dispatch per registered hook.

Delivery is decoupled from the handler: `PostAsync` only enqueues onto the bounded 128-slot `asyncPostQueue`, drained by four worker goroutines that invoke `Post`, where `safeClient.Do(req)` runs (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`). That means the outbound request happens *after* the RPC returns, and a full queue silently drops it (`:191-195`).
