**Why the confusion is understandable**

Both GraphQL and this stack are schema-first with generated clients and typed contracts — but the mechanics differ: one schema language (protobuf, not SDL), no single query endpoint, no field-selection language. The closest analogue to GraphQL's selective queries here is the CEL-based `filter` parameter on list endpoints (`internal/filter/`) plus protobuf `FieldMask` on update RPCs.
