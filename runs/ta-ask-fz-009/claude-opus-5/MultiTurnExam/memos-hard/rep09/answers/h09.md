**How the single shutdown chain reaches everything**

`Server.Shutdown` (`server/server.go:134`) drives two branches. One calls `closeLongLivedConnections` (`:203`), which closes the `SSEHub`. The other calls `Store.Close` (`server/server.go:146`), which fans out inside `store/store.go:72-79`: three `Cache.Close` calls for the instance-setting, user, and user-setting caches (`:74-76`) to stop their cleanup goroutines, then `s.driver.Close()` (`:78`).

That last line is the interface hop — it dispatches through `store.Driver.Close` (`store/driver.go:12`) to whichever dialect the factory selected.
