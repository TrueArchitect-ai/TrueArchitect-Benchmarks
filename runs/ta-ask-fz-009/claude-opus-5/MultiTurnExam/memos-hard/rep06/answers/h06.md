Each also has a Connect-transport wrapper that delegates to it — `connect_services.go:306` (CreateMemo), `:330` (UpdateMemo), `:338` (DeleteMemo), `:346` (SetMemoAttachments), `:362` (SetMemoRelations), `:378` (CreateMemoComment) — so those are the same six reachable paths seen from the wire rather than six additional ones.

**How they converge.** Two distinct hop counts, and the difference is architecturally meaningful:

- *Direct dispatch* — `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` call `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` / `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:15,25`), which funnel into `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`:48`).
- *Side-effect dispatch* — `UpdateMemo`, `SetMemoAttachments`, and `SetMemoRelations` are one hop further out because they all route through the shared `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:66`) → `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`. That's why mutating attachments or relations fires a webhook at all: they're modelled as memo updates, which is easy to miss reading the handlers in isolation.
- `CreateMemoComment` is the outlier — it calls `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`:30`) directly, and notably resolves webhooks for the *related memo's owner* (`GetUserWebhooks(ctx, relatedMemoCreatorID)`, line 31), not the commenter. So a commenter's action triggers delivery to someone else's configured URL.

**The convergence point.** All six paths reach `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) at `memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`, where `payload.URL = hook.Url` is the user-configured target (lines 41, 67). But `PostAsync` only enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel; the actual outbound HTTP request is issued by **`webhook.Post`** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), called by the four worker goroutines started in `init()` (lines 42–55), at `safeClient.Do(req)` (line 152).

So `Post` is the single funnel where every path becomes real network traffic — which is exactly why the SSRF guard is installed on that one client's transport (`safeDialContext`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`) rather than at each of the six handlers. One chokepoint, one place to enforce the boundary.
