One boundary detail worth knowing: there are two layers with a `SignIn` method. `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:78` is the Connect/gRPC transport adapter — it only unwraps `req.Msg` and delegates to `s.APIV1Service.SignIn`, wrapped in `connectWithHeaderCarrier` so the service can set response headers (i.e. the session cookie) protocol-agnostically.
