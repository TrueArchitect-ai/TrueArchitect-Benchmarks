**web/src/connect.ts**

All service clients are instantiated there as module-level exports off a single shared transport (`createConnectTransport` at `web/src/connect.ts:184`):

- `instanceServiceClient` (:192), `authServiceClient` (:193), `userServiceClient` (:194), `memoServiceClient` (:197), `attachmentServiceClient` (:198), `aiServiceClient` (:199), `shortcutServiceClient` (:200), `identityProviderServiceClient` (:203)

That's one client per backend service — the same 8 services defined under `proto/api/v1`.

Note there's a second, deliberately separate transport in the same file: `refreshTransport` (:65) with its own `refreshAuthClient` (:72). It's isolated from the main transport so token-refresh calls don't recurse through the auth interceptor that triggers them.
