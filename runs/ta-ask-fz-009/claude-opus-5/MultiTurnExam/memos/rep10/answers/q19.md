How each is wired:

- **Connect** — `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is constructed and passed through a local `wrap` into the path/handler table in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:33-45` (mounted alongside the seven sibling services). The `s` satisfying `apiv1connect.MemoServiceHandler` is `ConnectServiceHandler`, whose per-RPC adapter methods live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` :306, `ListMemos` :314) and delegate to `APIV1Service`.
- **REST** — `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` registers `APIV1Service` directly against the gateway mux, using the in-process `HandlerServer` variant (no gRPC dial). The verb/path table it enforces comes from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`, generated into `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

Note the consequence: `APIV1Service` methods are the single implementation reached by both protocols — Connect goes through the `ConnectServiceHandler` adapter layer, REST binds to `APIV1Service` directly. Handler-level behavior that lives in the Connect wrapper (e.g. header/cookie carrying via `connectWithHeaderCarrier`) is therefore not on the REST path.
