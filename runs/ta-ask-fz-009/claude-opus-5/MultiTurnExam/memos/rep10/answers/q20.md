**Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE)** — not WebSockets. Confirmed at `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48` (`Content-Type: text/event-stream`) with explicit `http.Flusher` flushing per event (lines 55–56, 76–77, 96–97, 105–106).

**Hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`** — `SSEHub.Broadcast` at line 115. The HTTP edge is separate: `RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25,34`, with event-construction helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

One design detail worth knowing: broadcasts are context-gated to avoid duplicates. `CreateMemo` only fires if `!isSSESuppressed(ctx)` (`memo_service.go:162`) — `CreateMemoComment` calls `CreateMemo` with SSE suppressed (`memo_service_comments.go:56`) and emits its own parent-scoped comment event instead. That invariant is covered by `server/router/api/v1/sse_service_test.go:71,116`.

Events also carry `Visibility` and `CreatorID` (`resolveSSECreatorID`), so fan-out can be filtered per subscriber rather than blasted to every connection.
