**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, CreateMemoComment, DeleteMemo, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction, dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects**

Mechanism and wiring:
- The browser-facing door is `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25` — a plain HTTP streaming endpoint, not part of the proto/Connect surface.
- The hub is `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`, with `Subscribe(userID, role)` / `Unsubscribe(client)` per-connection lifecycle (used in tests at `server/router/api/v1/sse_service_test.go:136–137`) and `Broadcast(*SSEEvent)` as the fan-out entry point.
- Event construction helpers sit beside it in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

Two design points worth noting. `UpdateMemo` does not broadcast directly — it routes through the private `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper (called at `memo_service.go:513`), which keeps update-side effects in one place. And the comment path deliberately suppresses the generic event: `CreateMemoComment` calls `CreateMemo` under `withSuppressSSE(ctx)` (`memo_service_comments.go:56`) so subscribers receive `memo.comment.created` for the parent instead of a duplicate `memo.created`. Events carry `Visibility` and `CreatorID` (`memo_service.go:585–589`), which is how the hub filters delivery per subscriber.
