q06. **memo_relation**

Defined identically across all three dialects — `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47`, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:46`, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:46`.

The schema is a pure join table: `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, and `type`, with `UNIQUE(memo_id, related_memo_id, type)` — so a memo pair can carry more than one relation kind but not a duplicate of the same kind (`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:47-52`). Store-layer access lives in `store/db/*/memo_relation.go` (e.g. `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go`).
