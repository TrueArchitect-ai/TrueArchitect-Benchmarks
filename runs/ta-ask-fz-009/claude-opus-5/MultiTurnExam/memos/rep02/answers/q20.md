q20. **Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP; hub: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`**

**Mechanism** — SSE on a single endpoint, `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25`, wired at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`) and served by `handleSSE` (`sse_handler.go:34`). No WebSockets involved.

**Hub** — `SSEHub` struct at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`, built by `NewSSEHub()` (line 63) and held on `APIV1Service.SSEHub` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:36`, initialized line 57; also referenced by `server.sseHub`, `server/server.go:38,77`). Its contract is `Subscribe` (line 71) / `Unsubscribe` (line 89) / `Broadcast` (line 115) / `Close` (line 99). `Subscribe` takes `userID` and `store.Role`, and broadcast is visibility- and creator-scoped — private events don't fan out to other users, and slow clients get events dropped rather than blocking the hub (behavior pinned by `sse_hub_test.go:119`, `:169`).

**Memo handlers that broadcast:**

- `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — `SSEEventMemoCreated` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`), guarded by `!isSSESuppressed(ctx)` so comment creation doesn't double-fire
- `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` — `SSEEventMemoDeleted` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`)
- `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` — `SSEEventMemoUpdated` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`), the shared tail of the update paths
- `UpsertMemoReaction` / `DeleteMemoReaction` — `SSEEventReactionUpserted` / `SSEEventReactionDeleted` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`, `:161`)

Event construction helpers (`resolveSSECreatorID`, `buildMemoReactionSSEEvent`, the suppression context flags) live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
