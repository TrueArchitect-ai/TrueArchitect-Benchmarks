q07. **web/src/connect.ts**

All service clients are constructed there off a single shared transport (`createConnectTransport` at `web/src/connect.ts:184`) and exported as module-level singletons — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
