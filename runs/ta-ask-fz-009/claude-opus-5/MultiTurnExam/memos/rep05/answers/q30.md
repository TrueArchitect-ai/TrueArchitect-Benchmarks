Structure: the first three are one-liners over a shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook(ctx, memo, activityType)` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo's creator, loads that user's hooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks` (line 57), and fans out one payload per hook (lines 61-72). The comment case is deliberately *not* routed through that helper — it addresses hooks belonging to the **parent** memo's owner (`relatedMemoCreatorID`, line 31), since the notification target is the person being commented on, not the comment author.

All four converge on `webhook.PostAsync(payload)` — lines 43 and 71 respectively — after setting `ActivityType`, `URL`, and `SigningSecret` from the hook record.

Two delivery properties worth knowing:

- **`PostAsync` is a bounded queue, not a goroutine-per-call**: it does a non-blocking send into `asyncPostQueue` and *drops* the event with a `slog.Warn` when the queue is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:189-194`). So webhook delivery is best-effort by design, at two levels — dropped here under load, and callers already log-and-continue on error (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:157-158`).
- **`Post` does the real work**: JSON-marshals the payload, builds the POST, applies HMAC-SHA256 signing headers when a secret is present, and additionally treats a non-zero `code` in the *response body* as a failure (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:175-177`).
