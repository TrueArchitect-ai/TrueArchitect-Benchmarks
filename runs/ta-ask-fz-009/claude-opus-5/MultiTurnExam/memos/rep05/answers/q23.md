The chain has one more layer worth knowing about, since it's where the two shapes are reconciled: **generated Go** at `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.go` (struct `apiv1.Memo`), with the store↔proto translation in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`. That converter is the seam — the SQL/store shape (`creator_id`, `row_status`, `payload`, per `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:33-44`) is deliberately not the wire shape (resource `name`, `visibility` enum), so a field added to the table doesn't reach clients until the proto and converter agree.

Two propagation notes: the SQL layer is triplicated per dialect (`store/migration/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/LATEST.sql`) plus incremental migrations under the same directories, so a column change is four files minimum; and the frontend types are generated from the proto, not hand-written — edit `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` and regenerate rather than touching `memo_service_pb.ts`.
