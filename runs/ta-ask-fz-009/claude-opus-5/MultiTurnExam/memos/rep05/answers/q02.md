q02. **sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, one directory per dialect, each with versioned `.sql` files plus a `LATEST.sql` baseline:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` — oldest history (from `0.2/` through `0.30/`), `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — from `0.17/` onward, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — from `0.19/` onward, `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`

This matches the driver layer: `store/db/` contains exactly `sqlite/`, `mysql/`, and `postgres/` implementations alongside `store/db/db.go`.
