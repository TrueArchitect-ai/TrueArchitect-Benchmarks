**Two convergence points.** The first three are thin wrappers over the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook(ctx, memo, activityType)` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo's creator, loads their hooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks` (`:57`), and enqueues one payload per hook (`:61-71`). `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` bypasses that helper because it targets a *different* user — the **parent memo's** owner, taking `relatedMemoCreatorID` explicitly (`:30-31`) — but builds an identical payload.

Both paths then converge on `webhook.PostAsync` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71` → `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines calling `Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-54`, `:120`). Delivery is fire-and-forget: overflow drops with a warning (`:191`), and every call site logs rather than propagates failure (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:158`), so webhooks never block or fail a memo operation.
