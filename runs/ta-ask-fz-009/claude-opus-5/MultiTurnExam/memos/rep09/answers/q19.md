Both front the *same* business logic — `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` — so this is two transports over one implementation, not two implementations.

**REST/gateway path:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers `APIV1Service` directly onto a grpc-gateway `runtime.ServeMux` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:96-98`, with `gatewayAuthMiddleware`). The mux is size-capped and mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:126-129`). Routes come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`, via generated code in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

**Connect path:** `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` is registered on a plain `http.ServeMux` inside `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:31-50`) — this is what the browser client uses (`web/src/connect.ts:197`).

The seam between them: `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:21-23`), and its per-RPC methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService block starts :304, `CreateMemo` at :306) unwrap `connect.Request`, delegate, then translate errors via `convertGRPCError` (:65) so gRPC status codes keep their semantics on the Connect wire.
