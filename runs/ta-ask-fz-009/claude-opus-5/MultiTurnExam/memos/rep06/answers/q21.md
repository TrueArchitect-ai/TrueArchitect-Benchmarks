Two clarifications that matter for scoping the change:

- **`fetchCurrentUser` is the amplifier.** It's called from nearly every authenticated handler, so a signature change there (if you propagate it) has a far wider blast radius than the 12 rows above. Changing `Store.GetUser` alone stops at these call sites.
- **Name collisions to *not* touch:** `APIV1Service.GetUser` (the RPC handler, reached via `connect_services.go:115` and the generated gateway files under `proto/gen/api/v1/`) is a different method — it's the API-layer `GetUser`, not the store's. Likewise `store/db/mysql/user.go:77` calls the *driver's* `GetUser`, not `Store.GetUser`; it only needs review if you also change the `Driver` interface in `store/driver.go`.
