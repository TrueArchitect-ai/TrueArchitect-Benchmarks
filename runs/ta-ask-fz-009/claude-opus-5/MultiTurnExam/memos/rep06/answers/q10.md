q10. **store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

The migrator embeds the whole `migration` tree (`//go:embed migration`, `store/migrator.go:44`) and on `preMigrate`, if the DB reports uninitialized, applies the per-driver `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` (`store/migrator.go:56`) from `store/migration/{driver}/` — the documented shortcut over replaying incremental migrations (`store/migrator.go:27,42`). For the `sqlite` driver that resolves to `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, which contains the full current table set (memo, memo_relation at line 47, attachment, etc.). Covered by `TestFreshInstall` in `store/test/migrator_test.go:20`.
