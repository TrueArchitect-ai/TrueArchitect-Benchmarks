q02. **sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migration sets live under `store/migration/`, one directory per dialect — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, each with versioned folders plus a `LATEST.sql` baseline. This matches the driver implementations in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/` (dispatched from `store/db/db.go`).
