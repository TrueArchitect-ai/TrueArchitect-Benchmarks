CEL via `cel-go` + [`internal/filter/`]( /bench/repo/internal/filter/README.md ).

Mechanism: CEL is type-checked, converted to an IR, rendered by [`render.go`](/bench/repo/internal/filter/render.go) into dialect-specific SQL, then appended by `AppendConditions` for SQLite, MySQL, or PostgreSQL in [`store/db/sqlite/memo.go`](/bench/repo/store/db/sqlite/memo.go), [`store/db/mysql/memo.go`](/bench/repo/store/db/mysql/memo.go), and [`store/db/postgres/memo.go`](/bench/repo/store/db/postgres/memo.go), using `?` versus `$n` placeholders.
