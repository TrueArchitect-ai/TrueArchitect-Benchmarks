Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used. Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared in [internal/webhook/webhook.go:39](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:39).

Actual mechanism: `PostAsync` enqueues payloads onto that channel ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-190](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:182)), and four goroutines consume it and call `Post` ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-53](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:42)).
