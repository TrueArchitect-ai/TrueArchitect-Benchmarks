Premise correction: webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The mechanism is an in-process buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, declared in [internal/webhook/webhook.go:39](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:39). Four goroutines consume from that channel in `init()` ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:42)), while `PostAsync` enqueues deliveries ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-190](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:182)).
