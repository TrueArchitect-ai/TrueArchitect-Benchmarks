1st: User; 2nd: Memo (roughly 7 and 6 external packages, respectively).

The persistent types are defined in [store/user.go](/bench/repo/store/user.go:29) and [store/memo.go](/bench/repo/store/memo.go:35). `store.User` is referenced across packages including `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/rss` (for example, [server/auth/context.go](/bench/repo/server/auth/context.go), [server/notification/email.go](/bench/repo/server/notification/email.go:136), and [server/router/api/v1/user_service.go](/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/user_service.go)). `store.Memo` appears in those API/test, notification, frontend, and RSS packages plus `server/runner/memopayload` (for example, [server/router/rss/rss.go](/bench/repo/server/router/rss/rss.go:162) and [server/runner/memopayload/runner.go](/bench/repo/server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74)).
