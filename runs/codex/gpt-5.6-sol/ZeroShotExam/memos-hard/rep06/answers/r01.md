Premise correction: The cache layer does not use Redis; no Redis connection is configured in this repository.

Actual mechanism: `store.New` configures process-local in-memory caches via `store/cache.New`, including `userCache`, with `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute` and cleanup every 5 minutes ([store/store.go:37-53](/bench/repo/store/store.go:37)). User records are inserted/read through that cache in [store/user.go:88-167](/bench/repo/store/user.go:88), so user entries expire after 10 minutes unless refreshed or deleted.
