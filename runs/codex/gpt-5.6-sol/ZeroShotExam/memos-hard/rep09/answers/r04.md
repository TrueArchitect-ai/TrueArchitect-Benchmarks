Premise correction: the webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The application uses an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue`, declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in [internal/webhook/webhook.go:39](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:39).

Actual mechanism: `init()` starts four goroutines that range over this channel and call `Post` ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-53](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:42)); webhook dispatchers enqueue payloads via `PostAsync` ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:182)).
