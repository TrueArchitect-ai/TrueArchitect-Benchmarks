Premise correction: the webhook delivery workers consume from neither NATS nor RabbitMQ. The mechanism is an in-process buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue` is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` in [internal/webhook/webhook.go:39](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:39). Four goroutines started in `init()` range over that channel and call `Post` for each payload ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:42)). Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` ([internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-195](/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:182)).
