CEL via `cel-go` + [internal/filter/engine.go](/bench/repo/internal/filter/engine.go).

The engine parses and normalizes CEL into an intermediate condition tree, then `CompileToStatement` renders dialect-specific SQL (SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL) using the renderer selected by `RenderOptions.Dialect`; database drivers append those generated conditions to their queries via [internal/filter/helpers.go](/bench/repo/internal/filter/helpers.go).
