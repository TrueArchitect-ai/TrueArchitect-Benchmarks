**Actual mechanism:** Caching is done by an in-process, thread-safe in-memory cache: `Cache` — `store/cache/cache.go:66-72` (backed by `sync.Map` with TTL-based expiration and periodic cleanup, defined in the same file). The `userCache` instance is constructed in `(*Store) New` at `store/store.go:37-52`, using a `cache.Config` with `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute` (`store/store.go:40-45`, `userCache: cache.New(cacheConfig)` at `store/store.go:52`). So user entries cached via `userCache.Set(...)` (e.g. in `(*Store).GetUser`/`ListUsers`, `store/user.go:132,143,167`) expire after **10 minutes**, with a background cleanup sweep every 5 minutes (`CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`).
