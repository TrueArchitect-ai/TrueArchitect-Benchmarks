h11.

Ranking of persistent entity types (structs in `store/`, e.g. `store.User`, `store.Memo`) by number of distinct packages outside the data layer (`store/` and its `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` driver implementations) that reference them:

**1st: `User`** (`store/user.go`) — referenced from ~5–6 outside packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1` (+ its `/test` subpackage), `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.

**2nd: `Memo`** (`store/memo.go`) — referenced from ~4 outside packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

For comparison, the next closest were `Attachment` (~3 packages: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`) and `MemoShare`/`Inbox` (~2 packages each).

Methodology note: I grepped for qualified references (`store.User`, `store.Memo`, etc.) across all non-test, non-generated Go files, excluded `store/` itself and its `db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` subpackages (since those implement the persistence layer), and counted distinct containing directories. This is a static-reference approximation, not an exhaustive call-graph analysis.
