**Delivery function:** `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180` (invoked directly, or asynchronously via `PostAsync`/`asyncPostQueue` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` and `39-55`) — it marshals the payload, optionally HMAC-signs it, and executes the outbound POST via the shared `safeClient.Do(req)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`).

**Protection mechanism:** The SSRF guard lives in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77` (`safeDialContext`), a custom `net.Dialer.DialContext` installed on `safeClient`'s `http.Transport` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`) that resolves the target hostname and rejects any resolved IP falling in a reserved/private range — checked at dial time (defeating DNS-rebinding TOCTOU). The reserved-range logic itself (`isReservedIP`, `reservedCIDRs`/`reservedNetworks` covering loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS, and IPv6 equivalents) is defined in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`, and the same check is applied pre-flight to user-submitted webhook URLs via `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62-83`).
