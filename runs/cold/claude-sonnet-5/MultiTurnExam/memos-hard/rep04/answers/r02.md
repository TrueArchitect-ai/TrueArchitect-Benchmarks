**Premise correction:** No per-user API rate-limiting middleware exists in this codebase. A repo-wide search for rate-limiting/throttling terms (`ratelimit`, `rate limit`, `throttle`, `limiter`) turns up exactly one hit — a plain-English aspirational comment in `internal/email/doc.go:86` ("Rate limit email sending to prevent abuse") that describes a best practice for *callers of the email package*; it is not backed by any implementation. The actual middleware/interceptor chain wired at startup is limited to CORS and panic recovery on the Echo side (`server/server.go:51-52`, `middleware.Recover()` / `newCORSMiddleware`) and metadata conversion, logging, recovery, and auth on the Connect/gRPC side (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:133-135`, plus `AuthInterceptor` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go:205`) — none of which impose request-count limits per user. There is no configuration surface (env var, flag, or config struct field) for request limits either.
