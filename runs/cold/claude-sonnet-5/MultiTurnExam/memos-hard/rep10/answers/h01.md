**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs before they expire. It calls `CheckAndPresign`, which lists attachments with `StorageType == S3`, skips any whose `LastPresignedTime` is still within 4 days of the (5-day) expiration window, and otherwise generates a fresh presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and updates the attachment's stored `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately, then on a repeating `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-40`). Since it only refreshes URLs older than 4 of their 5-day validity, the 12-hour cadence comfortably re-signs them before they go stale.
