**Premise correction:** There is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled anywhere in this codebase, and the "cron scheduler" you're likely thinking of (`internal/scheduler`) is not wired into the running server at all. A repo-wide search confirms `internal/scheduler` is imported only by its own test files (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`) — no file under `server/`, `store/`, or `cmd/` imports or registers any job with it.

The `"0 2 * * *"` / `"daily-cleanup"` example you may have seen is just illustrative documentation in the package's doc comment (`internal/scheduler/doc.go:14-21`), showing how a consumer *could* register a job — it is not an actual registered job and never executes.

**Actual scheduled background work:** The only real recurring background job in the server is the S3 presign refresh runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), which runs every 12 hours via a `time.Ticker` (not the cron scheduler) — see h01 for details. The in-memory `Cache` also runs an internal cleanup goroutine every 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go:200-215`), but that's cache-eviction housekeeping, not a database-cleanup job. Neither is a "nightly" job with a specific time-of-day, and there is no database-cleanup job among either of them.
