**memo_relation**

The `memo_relation` table (columns `memo_id`, `related_memo_id`, `type`) stores links between one memo and another related memo. Defined in `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (also postgres/mysql equivalents), originally introduced in `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`, with the corresponding Go model in `store/memo_relation.go`.
