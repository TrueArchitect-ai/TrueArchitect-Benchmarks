Chain detail:
- `useCreateMemo()` in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` (react-query `useMutation`) calls `memoServiceClient.createMemo(...)`.
- `memoServiceClient` is built in `web/src/connect.ts` (`createClient(MemoService, transport)`), a Connect-RPC client generated from the proto.
- `rpc CreateMemo(CreateMemoRequest) returns (Memo)` is defined in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.
- `ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` receives the RPC and delegates to `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, which builds a `store.Memo` and calls `s.Store.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
- `Store.CreateMemo` in `store/memo.go` validates the UID and delegates to `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`.
- The dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, with equivalents in `store/db/postgres/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`) executes the literal `INSERT INTO memo (...) VALUES (...)` SQL statement.
