q20. mechanism; hub path; handler names

**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) — `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` and writes `data: %s\n\n` frames with periodic `: heartbeat\n\n` comments; the frontend (`web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`) consumes it as an `EventSource`-style stream from `GET /api/v1/sse`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — the `SSEHub` struct (client registry with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`) and its `Broadcast(event *SSEEvent)` method that fans out JSON events to subscribed clients (filtered by `canReceive` visibility checks). Event payloads are built in the companion file `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

**Memo handlers that broadcast through it** (all in `server/router/api/v1/`, calling `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`):
- `CreateMemo` — `memo_service.go:70` (broadcast at line 163, `SSEEventMemoCreated`, suppressed when called internally from `CreateMemoComment`)
- `UpdateMemo` — `memo_service.go:401`, via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `memo_update_helpers.go:66` (broadcast at line 71, `SSEEventMemoUpdated`)
- `DeleteMemo` — `memo_service.go:518` (broadcast at line 585, `SSEEventMemoDeleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `memo_service_comments.go:20` (broadcast at line 126, `SSEEventMemoCommentCreated`)

- `DeleteMemoReaction` — `reaction_service.go:111` (broadcast at line 161, `SSEEventReactionDeleted`)
