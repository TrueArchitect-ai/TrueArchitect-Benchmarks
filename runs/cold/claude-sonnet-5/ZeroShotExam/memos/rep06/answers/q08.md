`Driver` — `store/driver.go`

The `Driver` interface (store/driver.go:10-79) declares all methods a database driver must implement (attachments, memos, users, settings, etc.). It's implemented by the MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite backends in `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`.
