q25 (confirmed, with additional detail from the deep-dive):

**Engine**: CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`), living in `internal/filter/` — `engine.go` (Engine/Program wrapping `cel.Env`), `parser.go` (walks the CEL AST into IR), `ir.go` (IR types: `Condition`/`ValueExpr`), `schema.go` (field schema + `DialectName` constants), `render.go` (SQL renderer), `helpers.go` (`AppendConditions`).

**Mechanism**: `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178`) validates and appends the raw filter string to `store.FindMemo.Filters`; `filter.Engine.Compile` parses it with `cel-go`'s parser/checker against a fixed memo `Schema`, then `internal/filter/parser.go`'s `buildCondition` walks the CEL AST once into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR tree; `internal/filter/render.go`'s `renderer` then walks that IR a second time, switching on `DialectName` (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`) at each node — placeholders (`?` vs `$N`), timestamp extraction (`strftime` vs `EXTRACT...AT TIME ZONE` vs `MONTH()/YEAR()`), string length (`CHAR_LENGTH` vs `LENGTH`), JSON/tag membership, regex/LIKE, and boolean literals — to emit a `Statement{SQL, Args}`; each driver's `ListMemos` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:62-69`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:51-58`) calls the shared `filter.AppendConditions` helper to splice that fragment into its hand-built `WHERE` clause.
