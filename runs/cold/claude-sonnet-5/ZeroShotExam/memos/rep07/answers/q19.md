MemoService is exposed over **two protocols**:

1. **gRPC-Gateway (REST/JSON over HTTP)** — registered via `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` (inside `RegisterGateway`), which mounts the gRPC-Gateway mux under `/api/v1/*`.
2. **Connect protocol** — wrapped via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` in `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`), registered on the Connect `http.ServeMux`.

(Native gRPC itself is served through the same Connect handler, since `connectrpc.com/connect` handlers speak gRPC, gRPC-Web, and Connect on one endpoint — but the two distinct registration/wrapping call sites are the gRPC-Gateway one and the Connect one listed above.)
