**Engine + path:** CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`), wrapped by an in-repo filter engine at `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`, `schema.go`).

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` passes `request.Filter` into `MemoFind.Filters` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:211-215`), and each store backend (`store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/memo.go`) calls `filter.AppendConditions`, which uses the process-wide `filter.DefaultEngine()` to CEL-compile the expression into an AST, converts that AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition`/IR tree (`internal/filter/parser.go` → `ir.go`), and then a `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR to emit dialect-specific SQL/placeholders (branching on `DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`) plus bound args that get appended to the query's WHERE clause.
