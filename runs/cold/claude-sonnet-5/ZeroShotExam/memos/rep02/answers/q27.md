Mechanism: the compiled React build output (`web/dist`) is embedded into the Go binary at compile time via Go's `embed` directive, then served with Echo's static-file middleware (with a SPA fallback to `index.html` for unmatched routes). Path: `server/router/frontend/frontend.go` (specifically the `//go:embed dist/*` directive at line 20 and the `Serve` method at lines 41-61, which registers `middleware.StaticWithConfig` and `spaFallbackMiddleware`).
