**Premise correction:** Neither. This repo (usememos/memos) has no external message broker in its dependency tree — no NATS, RabbitMQ/AMQP, Kafka, Redis, or SQS client exists in `go.mod` or anywhere in the codebase.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process, in-memory buffered Go channel, not a broker-backed queue.

- Declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:
  ```go
  asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
  ```
- Consumed by 4 goroutines spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)`.
- Enqueued via the non-blocking `PostAsync` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:182-196`), which drops payloads (log-only) if the channel is full.
- Producers are request-handling goroutines in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:31-43,57-71`, which call `webhook.PostAsync(...)` directly on memo create/update/delete/comment events — there's no separate consumer process or persisted job table.

Since it's an unbuffered-to-disk, capacity-128 channel, a process restart or a full queue silently drops pending webhook deliveries (no retry/persistence).
