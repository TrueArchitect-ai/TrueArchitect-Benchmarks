**Addendum to my h14 answer:** In addition to what I reported, there's a second, ephemeral category of derived metadata computed **on read/render** (not persisted):

- **Computation site:** `GenerateSnippet(content, maxLength)` in `/bench/repo/internal/markdown/markdown.go:264-346`, wrapped by `getMemoContentSnippet()` in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:83-90`.
- **Trigger:** every time a memo (or a related memo) is converted to its API response — `convertMemoFromStoreWithCreators` in `memo_service_converter.go:76-80`, and for `MemoRelation` previews (`memo_service_converter.go:225,248,291`) — and when building notification payloads (`user_service_notifications.go:318-391`). So it recomputes on every list/preview render, not just on save.
- **Extracted property:** `Snippet` — a plain-text excerpt (code blocks skipped, tags rendered as `#tag`, autolinks resolved to URL) truncated to a max length at a word boundary.

So the full picture: `Tags` and `Property.{Title,HasLink,HasCode,HasTaskList,HasIncompleteTasks}` are computed once on create/content-update (or via the batch `RunOnce` backfill) and persisted to `memo.Payload`; `Snippet` is computed fresh on every read/render for lists and previews and is never stored.
