Premise correction: neither. This repo (usememos/memos) has no NATS or RabbitMQ integration anywhere — there's no external message broker at all for webhook delivery.

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery uses an in-process, bounded Go channel as the "queue," not a broker:
- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` is the queue declaration (a buffered Go channel, capacity 128).
- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` — `init()` spawns 4 worker goroutines that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` to perform the actual outbound HTTP webhook request.
- `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` — `PostAsync()` is the producer: a non-blocking `select` send into the channel, dropping the payload (with a logged warning) if it's full — no persistence, no redelivery, no external broker.

There's no `docker-compose`, no `amqp.Dial`/`QueueDeclare`, no `nats.Connect`/JetStream, and no Kafka/SQS/Redis anywhere in the codebase to back this queue.
