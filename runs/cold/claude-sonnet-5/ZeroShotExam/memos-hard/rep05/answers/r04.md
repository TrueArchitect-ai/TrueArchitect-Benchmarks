**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process using a buffered Go channel, not an external broker. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `asyncPostQueue` is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — a 128-capacity in-memory channel. An `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over this channel and call `Post()` to deliver each webhook via HTTP. `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues payloads with a non-blocking `select`/`default`, dropping and logging a warning if the queue is full rather than blocking.
