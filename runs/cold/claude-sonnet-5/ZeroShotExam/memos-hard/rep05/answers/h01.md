Wiring — instantiated and started in `server/server.go`: created and run once at `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` / `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (server/server.go:162-163), then launched as a continuous goroutine with `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` (server/server.go:167-171), as part of the server's background-runner startup routine.
