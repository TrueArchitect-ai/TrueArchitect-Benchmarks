The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with an entry file that defines its `NewDB` constructor:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL

The factory that selects between them at startup based on the profile's `Driver` setting is:

**NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver` for `"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, or `"postgres"` and calls the corresponding package's `NewDB`).
