**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language, via `github.com/google/cel-go`), housed in the dedicated filter engine package at `internal/filter/`.

**Mechanism:** The `ListMemos` filter string is compiled through a three-phase pipeline — `cel-go` parses and validates the expression against the memo field schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`, `internal/filter/engine.go`), `internal/filter/parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a SQL fragment plus placeholder args per dialect (`?` for SQLite/MySQL, `$n` for Postgres, plus dialect-specific JSON access like `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`/`@>`). Each store driver calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect to splice the result into the `WHERE` clause — see `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65`, and `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54`. (The design is documented in `internal/filter/README.md`.)
