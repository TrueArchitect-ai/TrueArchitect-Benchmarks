The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It builds a Connect transport with `createConnectTransport` and then creates all the service clients with `createClient` — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
