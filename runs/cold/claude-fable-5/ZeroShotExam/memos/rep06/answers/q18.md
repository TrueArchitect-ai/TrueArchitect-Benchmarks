The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with its own package under `store/db/`:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver (`sqlite.NewDB`)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver (`mysql.NewDB`)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL driver (`postgres.NewDB`)

**NewDBDriver — `store/db/db.go`** (line 14) is the factory that selects between them at startup, switching on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) and returning an error for any other value.
