MemoService is served over the **gRPC-Gateway (RESTful HTTP/JSON)** protocol and the **Connect RPC** protocol. There is no native gRPC server — both are HTTP-mounted on the same Echo server.

- **gRPC-Gateway (HTTP/JSON REST)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `RegisterGateway` calls `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (line 108) and mounts the gateway mux at `/api/v1/*` (generated gateway code lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`).

- **Connect (connectrpc)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `RegisterConnectHandlers` wraps the service with `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` (line 40); the handler is mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go` (line 144), with the generated Connect bindings in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`.

Both protocols share one implementation: `APIV1Service` embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:26`), and `ConnectServiceHandler` adapts it to Connect's request/response wrappers (with `MemoService` method adapters around `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304`).
