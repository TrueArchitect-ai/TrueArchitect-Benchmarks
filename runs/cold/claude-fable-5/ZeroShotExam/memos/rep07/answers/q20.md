Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The hub (`SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) manages subscribed clients and broadcasts JSON-encoded `SSEEvent`s (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`), with visibility-based delivery filtering. The HTTP endpoint lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, and the browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.
