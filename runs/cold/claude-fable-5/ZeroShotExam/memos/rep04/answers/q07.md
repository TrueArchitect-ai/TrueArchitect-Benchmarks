The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. It calls `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` to instantiate and export clients for each service — e.g. `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` (see `web/src/connect.ts:192-203`).
