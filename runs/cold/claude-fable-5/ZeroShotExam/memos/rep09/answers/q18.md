The database driver interface is `store.Driver`, defined in `store/driver.go`. Its three implementations (each a `DB` type in its own package) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — Postgres driver

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

The factory switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) and is called at startup from `cmd/memos/main.go`, which passes the resulting driver to `store.New`.
