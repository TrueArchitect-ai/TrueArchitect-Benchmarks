**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoAttachments and SetMemoRelations via the shared update helper)**

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The hub (`SSEHub`) fans out `SSEEvent` payloads to subscribed clients, with the HTTP endpoint in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` and event-construction helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
- **Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — implements `NewSSEHub`, `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast` (line 115), and per-client visibility filtering via `canReceive`.
- **Memo handlers that call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`:**
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
  - `UpdateMemo` (also `SetMemoAttachments` and `SetMemoRelations`) — these broadcast indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`
  - Additionally, the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` broadcast memo-reaction events (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161`).
