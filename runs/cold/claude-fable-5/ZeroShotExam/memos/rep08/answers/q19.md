MemoService is served over two protocols, both wired up in `server/router/api/v1`:

1. **REST/HTTP-JSON via gRPC-Gateway** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`. In `RegisterGateway`, `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` (line 108) registers the service on the gateway mux, which is mounted on Echo under `/api/v1/*`.

2. **Connect (Connect RPC, replacing grpc-web for browser clients)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`. `RegisterConnectHandlers` calls `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` (line 40) and mounts it under `/memos.api.v1.*`. The per-method wrapping that adapts the gRPC-style implementation to Connect's request/response types lives in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService section starts at line 304).

Note there is no standalone native gRPC server: `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` binds the gateway directly to the in-process service implementation (generated in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`, with Connect codegen in `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`).
