**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `github.com/google/cel-go` (v0.28.1 in `go.mod`); the filter engine lives in `internal/filter/`.

**Mechanism:** `ListMemos` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, ~line 213) validates the filter string and passes it through `MemoFind.Filters` to the store layer; each dialect driver (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54`) calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(...)` with its dialect. The engine runs a three-phase pipeline: (1) parse the CEL expression against a memo-specific schema/environment (`internal/filter/schema.go`, `parser.go`), (2) normalize the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and (3) render the IR into a SQL fragment plus placeholder args per dialect (`internal/filter/render.go`) — handling differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, JSON access (`JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->`/`->>`), boolean coercion, and tag-membership predicates (SQLite `LIKE`, MySQL `JSON_CONTAINS`, Postgres `@>`). The resulting WHERE conditions are appended to each driver's query. This is all documented in `internal/filter/README.md`, with the glue in `internal/filter/engine.go` and `helpers.go`.
