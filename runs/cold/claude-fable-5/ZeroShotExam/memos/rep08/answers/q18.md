The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with a `NewDB` entry file:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver` at startup to select sqlite, mysql, or postgres)
