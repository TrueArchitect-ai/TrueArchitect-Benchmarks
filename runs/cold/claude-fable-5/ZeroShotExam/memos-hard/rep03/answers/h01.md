The component is the **S3 presign background runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). It periodically re-generates presigned URLs for attachments stored in S3, so the externally-stored content links don't expire.

**Component — schedule — wiring site:** `s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), refreshing only URLs whose last presign is older than 4 days (`runner.go:88`) — wired in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, which creates the runner, does an immediate `RunOnce`, then launches the ticker loop in a goroutine: `server/server.go:162-169` (called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`).
