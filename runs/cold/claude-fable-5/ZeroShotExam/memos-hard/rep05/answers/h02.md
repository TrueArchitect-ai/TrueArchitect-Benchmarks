Outbound webhook delivery executes in `Post` (with `PostAsync` enqueueing to a worker pool that calls `Post`) in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` — the request is sent at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152` via the shared `safeClient`.

The SSRF protection has two layers, both in `internal/webhook/`:

- **At dispatch time (the real guard):** `safeClient`'s transport uses `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`), which resolves the hostname at dial time and refuses connections to any reserved/private IP via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`) — this also defeats DNS-rebinding, since the check happens at connection time, not just registration time.
- **At registration time:** `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects webhook URLs that aren't http/https or that resolve to a reserved range when webhooks are created/updated (called from `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go`).

The blocked ranges are the `reservedCIDRs` list in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14` (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS 169.254.0.0/16, and IPv6 equivalents), with an `AllowPrivateIPs` escape hatch for self-hosted deployments (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`).
