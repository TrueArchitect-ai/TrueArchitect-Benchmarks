— False premise: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor any broker) exists — `go.mod` has no broker dependency (the `segmentio/*` entries are JSON/asm libraries). Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: `PostAsync` does a non-blocking send into a 128-slot buffered Go channel (`asyncPostQueue`, `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39,184`), drained by 4 worker goroutines started in the package `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that call `Post` directly over HTTP; payloads are dropped if the buffer is full or the process restarts.
