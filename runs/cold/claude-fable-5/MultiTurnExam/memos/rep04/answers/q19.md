MemoService is served over two protocols:

- **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `RegisterConnectHandlers` registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` (line 40) on the HTTP mux, with the `ConnectServiceHandler` wrapper (whose per-RPC adapters live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`).
- **HTTP/JSON via gRPC-Gateway (REST)** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` (line 108) registers the service on the gateway mux, which is mounted under `/api/v1/*`.

Both protocols delegate to the same `APIV1Service` implementation in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
