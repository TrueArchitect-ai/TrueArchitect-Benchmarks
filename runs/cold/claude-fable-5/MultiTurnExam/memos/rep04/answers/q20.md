**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — defines `SSEEvent`, the event types (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`), and the hub with its `Broadcast` method. The HTTP endpoint is registered in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (mounted via `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`).

**Memo handlers that broadcast:**
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`
- `UpdateMemo` — via the `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` helper (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`, called at `memo_service.go:513`)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`
- Also through the same helper: `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`); reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` broadcast too (`reaction_service.go:106,161`).
