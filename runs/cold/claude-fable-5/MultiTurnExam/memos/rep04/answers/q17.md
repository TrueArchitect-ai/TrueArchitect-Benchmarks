**web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient**

`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` contains the memo queries and CRUD mutations (`useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo`, `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo`, `useMemoComments`, …). They all call `memoServiceClient`, the ConnectRPC client created in `web/src/connect.ts` from the generated `MemoService` definition (`web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb`).
