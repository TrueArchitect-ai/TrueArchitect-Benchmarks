Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes two other kinds of records:

- **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` rows where the memo is either the source (`MemoID`) or the target (`RelatedMemoID`), via two `DeleteMemoRelation` calls (lines 142–147).
- **Attachments** — every attachment linked to the memo, listed with `ListAttachments` and removed one by one with `DeleteAttachment` (lines 148–157).

Only then does it call `driver.DeleteMemo` (line 158), which issues a plain `DELETE FROM memo` (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:237`).
