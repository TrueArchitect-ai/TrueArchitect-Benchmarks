**web/src/connect.ts**

The ConnectRPC service clients are all created in `web/src/connect.ts` using `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect` over a `createConnectTransport` transport (lines 184–203). It exports clients for all eight services — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient` — plus an interceptor-free `refreshAuthClient` used for token refresh (line 72).
