**Signing mechanism:** Standard Webhooks-style **HMAC-SHA256** signing. When a `SigningSecret` is set, `Post` computes `HMAC-SHA256(key, "<msgID>.<timestamp>." + body)`, base64-encodes it, and sends it in the `webhook-signature` header as `"v1,<signature>"` alongside `webhook-id` and `webhook-timestamp` headers (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-150`). Webhooks without a secret are sent unsigned.
