The memo entity is defined at each layer in:

- **SQL schema:** `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (the `memo` CREATE TABLE; equivalents exist in `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql` and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`)
- **Go store model:** `store/memo.go` (the `Memo` struct)
- **API proto message:** `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (the `Memo` message)
- **Generated frontend types:** `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts` (the generated `Memo` type/schema used by the ConnectRPC clients)
