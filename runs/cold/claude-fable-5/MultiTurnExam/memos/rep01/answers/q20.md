**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — defines the `SSEHub`, `SSEEvent`, and event types (`memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`).
