**Premise correction: neither — there is no message broker in this codebase.** No NATS, RabbitMQ, Kafka, or any other broker client appears in `go.mod` or the Go sources.

**Actual mechanism:** webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process with a plain buffered Go channel, declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

- The "queue" is `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.
- The "workers" are 4 goroutines started in the package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each ranging over the channel and calling `Post` (`webhook.go:120`) to execute the HTTP delivery, logging a warning on failure.
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184-196`) using a non-blocking `select`; if the 128-slot buffer is full, the event is **dropped** with a warning rather than persisted or retried.

Consequences of this design worth noting: delivery is best-effort with no durability — pending webhooks are lost on process restart, there are no acknowledgments, redelivery, or dead-letter handling, and backpressure is handled by dropping. Everything lives in the single `internal/webhook` package; there is no external queue declaration anywhere.
