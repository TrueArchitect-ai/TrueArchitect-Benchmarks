**1st: `store.User` (~7 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~6 packages)**

Method: for each persistent entity type defined in the data layer (the `store` package — `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, `store/reaction.go`, `store/inbox.go`, etc.), I counted the distinct packages outside `store/` whose files reference `store.<Type>`.

- **`store.User` — 7 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo` — 6 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

The rest trail well behind: `store.Attachment` (4 packages), then `Inbox`, `MemoRelation`, and `MemoShare` (3 each), `Reaction` and `UserIdentity` (2), with types like `IdentityProvider`, `Shortcut`, `Webhook`, and `Activity` at 1 or 0.
