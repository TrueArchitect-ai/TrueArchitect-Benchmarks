**Computation site:** `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74`, which calls `MarkdownService.ExtractAll` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:356`) to parse the memo's markdown content in a single goldmark pass and store the results into `memo.Payload` (`storepb.MemoPayload`).

**Triggers (operations that recompute it):**

- **Memo creation** — `CreateMemo` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` (this also covers `CreateMemoComment`, which delegates to `CreateMemo` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:56`).
- **Content update** — `UpdateMemo` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:447`, only when the update mask includes the `content` path.
- **Bulk rebuild** — `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` at `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:27,52`, which batch-recomputes payloads for all memos (defined but not wired to any production caller currently).

**Extracted properties:** `ExtractAll` returns tags, mentions, and a property struct; `RebuildMemoPayload` persists two of these into the payload (`runner.go:85-86`):

- `Payload.Tags` — deduplicated `#tag` occurrences from the content.
- `Payload.Property` (`storepb.MemoPayload_Property`) — `Title` (text of a leading H1 heading), `HasLink`, `HasCode` (code blocks or spans), `HasTaskList`, and `HasIncompleteTasks` (unchecked task checkboxes) — computed in `internal/markdown/markdown.go:356-422`.

(The extracted mentions aren't stored in the payload; they drive mention notifications separately.) This payload is what list views and filters use — e.g., tag filtering and the `has_link`/`has_task_list` style predicates — without reparsing content per request.
