**1st: `store.User` (~7 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~6 packages)**

Method: the persistent entities are the structs in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). I grepped for `store.<Type>` references in all Go code outside the data layer (`server/`, `cmd/`, `internal/`) and counted distinct referencing packages:

- **`store.User` — 7 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
- **`store.Memo` — 6 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

(Excluding the test-helper package `server/router/api/v1/test`, the ranking holds at 6 vs 5.) The rest trail well behind: `store.Attachment` in 4 packages; `store.Inbox`, `store.MemoRelation`, and `store.MemoShare` in 3 each; `store.Reaction` and `store.UserIdentity` in 2.
