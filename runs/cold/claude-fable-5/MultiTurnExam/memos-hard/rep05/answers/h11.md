**1st: `store.User` — referenced from ~6 packages outside the data layer.**
**2nd: `store.Memo` — referenced from ~4 packages outside the data layer.**

The persistent entity types live in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.), with per-database persistence under `store/db/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}`. Counting distinct non-test packages outside that layer (`server/`, `internal/`, etc.) that reference each type:

- `store.User` (6): `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test` (non-test helper file), `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`
- `store.Memo` (4): `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`
- Next behind: `store.Attachment` (3: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`), then `store.MemoShare` and `store.Inbox` (2 each); `Reaction`, `MemoRelation`, `IdentityProvider`, etc. trail at 1.

(If you exclude the `server/router/api/v1/test` helper package, `User` still leads 5 to 4.)
