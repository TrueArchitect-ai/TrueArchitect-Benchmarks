SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL

The project ships migrations for exactly three SQL dialects, organized into per-dialect subdirectories under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`:

- **SQLite** — `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.2–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- **PostgreSQL** — `store/migration/postgres/` (versions 0.19–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- **MySQL** — `store/migration/mysql/` (versions 0.17–0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)

These are embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go`, and the corresponding driver implementations live in `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/mysql/`. No other dialects are present.
