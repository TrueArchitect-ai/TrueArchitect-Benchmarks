This is the Memos app (Go, Echo web framework, `store` abstraction over a SQLite/other DB driver). A profile image is stored as a base64 data-URI in the `user.avatar_url` column; viewing another user's avatar hits `GET /file/users/:identifier/avatar`. The chain from serving handler to stored data:

1. `RegisterRoutes` (route `/file/users/:identifier/avatar`) — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:124
2. `(*FileServerService).serveUserAvatar` — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:168 (serving handler; later decodes the data-URI via `parseDataURI` at fileserver.go:196/737 and writes the bytes with `c.Blob`)
3. `(*FileServerService).getUserByUsername` — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:715
4. `(*Store).GetUser` — store/user.go:148
5. `(*Store).ListUsers` — store/user.go:136 (delegates to `s.driver.ListUsers`)
6. `(*DB).ListUsers` — store/db/sqlite/user.go:88 (builds and runs `SELECT ... avatar_url ... FROM user`, `d.db.QueryContext` at store/db/sqlite/user.go:173, scanning into `user.AvatarURL` at :189)

The stored data is the `avatar_url` column of the `user` table (the avatar itself is a `data:image/...;base64,...` URI held in that column, not a separate blob/file).
