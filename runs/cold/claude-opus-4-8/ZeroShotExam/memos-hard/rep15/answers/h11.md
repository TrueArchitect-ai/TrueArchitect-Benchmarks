h11.

**1st: User; 2nd: Memo**

Of the persistent entity types defined in the data layer (`store/` package — e.g. `Memo`, `User`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `UserSetting`, `UserIdentity`, `IdentityProvider`, `InstanceSetting`), the two most widely referenced from packages outside `store/` are:

- **1st: `store.User`** (`store/user.go:29`) — ~7 outside packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
- **2nd: `store.Memo`** (`store/memo.go:35`) — ~6 outside packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

`User` outranks `Memo` under either counting convention. If the store's own sub-packages (`store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/sqlite`, `store/test`) are included, the totals become 11 for `User` and 10 for `Memo` — the ranking is unchanged.

(Method: counted distinct directories containing `store.<Type>` references via `grep -rlE "\bstore\.<Type>\b" --include=*.go`, excluding the `store/` tree.)
