**Component — schedule — wiring site**

`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — every **12 hours** — wired into startup at **`server/server.go:162-169`** (inside `startBackgroundRunners`).

### What it does
The S3-presign runner re-signs the presigned GET URLs that Memos stores for attachments kept in external S3 storage. These presigned links expire (5-day lifetime), so if they aren't refreshed the stored `Reference` URL goes stale and the attachment content becomes unreachable. On each pass (`CheckAndPresign`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) it pages through all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments in batches of 100, and for any whose last presign was more than ~1 day ago (i.e. inside the final 4-day window of the 5-day expiry), it calls `PresignGetObject`, updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:85-123`).

### Schedule
- Interval constant `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`
- `Run` uses a `time.Ticker` firing every 12h and calls `RunOnce` (`runner.go:29-45`)
