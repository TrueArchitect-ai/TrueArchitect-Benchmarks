**Path of convergence:** Each dispatch helper (`memo_service_webhooks.go`) builds a payload and calls `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto the shared `asyncPostQueue`. Worker goroutines drain that queue and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which is where the actual outbound `POST` is issued (`safeClient.Do(req)` to `requestPayload.URL`, `:152`/`:33`).
