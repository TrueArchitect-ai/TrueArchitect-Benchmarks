**S3 presign Runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (fixed `time.Ticker`, `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired at `server/server.go:162` (created/launched inside `startBackgroundRunners`, which `Server.Start` calls at `server/server.go:129`).**

The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (runner.go:47) loads only attachments with `AttachmentStorageType_S3` (externally-stored content), and re-generates presigned GET URLs before they expire — presigned links are minted with a 5-day validity and refreshed once they fall within ~1 day of expiring, writing the fresh URL and `LastPresignedTime` back via `UpdateAttachment`. This is what keeps the external-storage attachment links from going stale.

The interval is defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-27` and driven by a `time.Ticker` in the `Run` method — it does *not* use the repo's `internal/scheduler` cron package. At startup it's also kicked once immediately via `RunOnce(ctx)` (`server/server.go:163`) before the continuous 12-hour goroutine loop starts (`server/server.go:166-171`).
