**Mechanism: explicit Go code.** When a user is deleted, their emoji reactions are removed by explicit `DELETE FROM reaction` statements in the deletion transaction — there is no database-level `ON DELETE CASCADE` rule to do it.

**Evidence locations:**

1. **The deletion orchestration** — `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:92-127` (`deleteUserTargetsTx`) runs, inside a single transaction, two explicit reaction deletions:
