The outbound webhook delivery executes in `Post`, which builds and sends the POST request via the shared `safeClient.Do(req)` call. SSRF protection is enforced at dial time: `safeClient`'s transport uses `safeDialContext`, which resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (checked against `reservedCIDRs` — loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS, and IPv6 equivalents). Resolving at dial time also defeats DNS-rebinding. There's a matching pre-flight check in `ValidateURL` used when webhooks are configured, and the whole guard can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` toggle for self-hosted setups.
