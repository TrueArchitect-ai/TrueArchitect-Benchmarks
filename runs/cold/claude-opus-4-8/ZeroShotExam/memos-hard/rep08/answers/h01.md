Memos stores attachments in S3 by saving a *presigned* GET URL in each attachment's `Reference` field, and those presigned URLs expire (5-day expiry). The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps those links from going stale:

- **Wiring:** in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — constructed at `server/server.go:162`, run once immediately at `:163`, and the recurring loop launched in a goroutine at `:169` (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`).
