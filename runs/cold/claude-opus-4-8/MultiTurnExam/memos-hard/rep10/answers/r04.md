**Premise correction:** Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is involved — there is no external message broker in this codebase. A repo-wide search finds no NATS, RabbitMQ/AMQP, or any broker client dependency in `go.mod`, and none is imported by the webhook code.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery is handled entirely in-process via a buffered Go channel and a fixed pool of goroutine workers, in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:
- The "queue" is a package-level buffered channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` (capacity 128).
- The workers are started in `init()` — a loop spawning **4 goroutines** that range over the channel and call `Post` for each payload — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`.
- Producers enqueue with `PostAsync`, a non-blocking `select` send that drops the payload (with a warning) if the buffer is full — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`.
- Actual delivery (the outbound HTTP POST) happens in `Post` via `safeClient.Do` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`/`:152`.
