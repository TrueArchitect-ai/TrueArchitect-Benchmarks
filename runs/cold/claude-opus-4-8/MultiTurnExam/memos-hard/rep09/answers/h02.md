**Delivery function — `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (the actual HTTP send is `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`); protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` (custom `http.Transport.DialContext`) at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, backed by `isReservedIP` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`.**

How it fits together:

- `Post` (`webhook.go:120`) marshals the payload, optionally adds an HMAC-SHA256 signature, and dispatches the outbound POST via the shared `safeClient` (`webhook.go:152`). `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`) just enqueues onto a bounded channel drained by worker goroutines that call `Post`.

- The SSRF protection is the `safeClient`'s custom transport (`webhook.go:32-37`), whose `DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go:59-77`). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects the connection if any resolved IP falls in a reserved/private range (`isReservedIP`, `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`). Because the check happens on the *resolved* IPs at dial time rather than on the URL string, it also defeats DNS-rebinding attempts to reach internal/private network addresses.
