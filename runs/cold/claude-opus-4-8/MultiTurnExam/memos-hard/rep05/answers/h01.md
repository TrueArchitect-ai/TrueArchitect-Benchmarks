**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:169`**

Externally-stored attachments (S3-backed) are served via presigned URLs that expire after 5 days. A background runner regenerates these presigned URLs before they go stale so links keep working.

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:16`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method lists attachments, skips any whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (`runner.go:85-88`), and re-presigns the rest via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, updating `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:108-124`).
- **Schedule**: every 12 hours, set by `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a ticker in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`).
- **Wiring into startup**: in `server/server.go`, the runner is created and given an initial synchronous pass with `RunOnce` (`server.go:162-163`), then the continuous loop is started in a goroutine at **`server/server.go:169`** (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`).
