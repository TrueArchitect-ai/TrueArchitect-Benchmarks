**1st: `User` (≈6 packages); 2nd: `Memo` (≈4 packages)**

Counting Go packages *outside* the data layer (i.e. excluding the whole `store/` tree, including the `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` drivers) that reference each persistent entity type defined in `store/*.go`:

- **`store.User` — 6 external packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo` — 4 external packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

Next was `store.Attachment` at 3, then `MemoShare`/`Inbox` at 2, and the remaining entity types (`Reaction`, `MemoRelation`, `UserSetting`, `UserIdentity`, `InstanceSetting`, `IdentityProvider`) at 1 each. So `User` has the widest fan-out across packages, followed by `Memo`. (Method: `grep -rlE "\bstore\.<Type>\b"` across `*.go`, dropping `store/…` paths and `_test.go` files, then reducing to distinct directories.)
