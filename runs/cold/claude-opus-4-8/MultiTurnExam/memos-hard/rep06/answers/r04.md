**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers do not consume from any external message broker — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka/AMQP). A repo-wide grep for `nats|rabbitmq|amqp|kafka|broker` across the Go sources returns nothing; no such dependency exists.

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery is handled entirely in-process using a buffered Go channel.
- The "queue" is a package-level buffered channel declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:37`: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (capacity 128).
- The workers are 4 goroutines started in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:41-53`, each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)` (the actual HTTP delivery at `webhook.go:120`/`:152`).
- Payloads are enqueued by `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which sends onto `asyncPostQueue`.

So the "message broker" is just an internal channel with a fixed 128-slot buffer and a fixed pool of 4 in-process consumer goroutines — nothing is declared in or consumed from an external broker.
