**`s3presign.Runner` (the S3 presign runner) — every 12 hours (plus once at startup) — `server/server.go:162-169`**

- **Component:** The `Runner` in the `s3presign` package, at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (`runner.go:47`) lists S3-backed attachments and regenerates each one's pre-signed S3 GET URL via `PresignGetObject`, writing the fresh URL back into the attachment's `Reference` and updating `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:108-123`). URLs are minted with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:77`), and it re-signs any whose last-presigned time won't stay valid for the next 4 days — so links to externally-stored (S3) content never go stale.

- **Schedule:** A plain `time.Ticker` at `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), looped in `Runner.Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). It also runs once immediately via `RunOnce` at startup.

- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162` constructs it (`s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), `server.go:163` runs it once, and `server.go:166-169` launches the continuous 12-hour loop as a tracked background goroutine (import at `server.go:25`).

Note: this only covers the S3 backend; there's no equivalent scheduled refresher for other external storage types.
