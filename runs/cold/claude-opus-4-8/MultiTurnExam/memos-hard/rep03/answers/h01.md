**Component** — the S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). Its `CheckAndPresign` walks all S3-stored attachments in batches and re-presigns any whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry, writing the fresh URL back via `UpdateAttachment` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`). This is what keeps links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from going stale.

**Schedule** — every 12 hours. `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` drives a `time.NewTicker` loop in `Runner.Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`). It also does one immediate pass at startup via `RunOnce`.

**Wiring site** — `server/server.go:162-171`: `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` → `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (initial pass, line 163) → then launched as a background goroutine `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` (line 169).

So: `s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (plus one run at startup) — `server/server.go:162` (registered), started at `server/server.go:169`.
