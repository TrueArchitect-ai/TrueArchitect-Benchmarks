**Protection mechanism** — the SSRF guard is `safeDialContext`, the `DialContext` on `safeClient`'s transport — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`. At dial time it resolves the target hostname and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` — `internal/webhook/validate.go:44` (ranges in `reservedCIDRs`, `internal/webhook/validate.go:14`). Because the check happens at connection time on the actual resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding, and it backstops the earlier admission-time `ValidateURL` check (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) used when webhooks are created/updated. (Note: both can be disabled via the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag, `internal/webhook/validate.go:41`.)
