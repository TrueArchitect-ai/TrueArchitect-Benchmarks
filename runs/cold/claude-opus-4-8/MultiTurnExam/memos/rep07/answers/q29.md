**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — the `authInterceptor` (defined at line 156, registered in the transport's `interceptors` at line 188). Its helpers/token state live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`.

**Token kinds it manages:**
- **Access token** — short-lived JWT held in memory, attached as `Authorization: Bearer <token>` on each request (`connect.ts:97-99`).
- **Refresh token** — long-lived, stored in an HTTP-only cookie sent via `credentials: "include"`; used by the `refreshToken` RPC to mint new access tokens (`connect.ts:60,74-82`).
- (Personal Access Tokens are also supported server-side as Bearer tokens.)

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — the `Authenticator` validates access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`), refresh tokens (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), and PATs (`AuthenticateByPAT`) in `server/auth/authenticator.go` (parsing logic in `server/auth/token.go`).
