**Engine:** Google CEL (`cel-go`), wrapped by the project's own filter engine in **internal/filter** (`internal/filter/engine.go`, using `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** Each dialect driver's `ListMemos` builds a `filter.DefaultEngine()` and calls `filter.AppendConditions(...)` passing its `DialectName` (`filter.DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`) — the engine compiles the CEL filter string to an AST/intermediate representation (`engine.go`, `ir.go`) and `render.go` walks that IR to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments and placeholder args (per-dialect expression templates defined in `internal/filter/schema.go`), which are appended to the query's WHERE clause (see `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`).
