Mechanism: The CEL filter expression is compiled/parsed by the filter engine (`filter.DefaultEngine()`), and each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`) calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect{SQLite|MySQL|Postgres}, &where, &args)`, which renders the parsed expression into dialect-specific SQL `WHERE` clauses plus bound args via the per-dialect switch in `internal/filter/render.go`.
