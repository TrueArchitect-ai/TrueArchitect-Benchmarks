- **Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — a ConnectRPC `Interceptor` sets the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header (`setAuthorizationHeader`) and handles proactive/reactive token refresh; a separate no-interceptor transport calls `AuthService.refreshToken` with `credentials: "include"`. Token state helpers live in `web/src/auth-state.ts`.
- **Token kinds:** a short-lived **access token** (JWT sent as a `Bearer` Authorization header) and a **refresh token** (delivered/sent via httpOnly cookie, used by the `RefreshToken` RPC to mint new access tokens).
- **Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` (the `Authenticator` in `server/auth/authenticator.go`, with JWT parsing/validation in `server/auth/token.go`).
