MemoService is served over two protocols:

- **gRPC-Gateway (REST/HTTP JSON)** — registered via `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`
- **Connect** (for browser clients) — registered via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` in `RegisterConnectHandlers` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40` (invoked from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:141`)

Both are backed by the same `APIV1Service` implementation, with the Connect wrappers defined in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`.
