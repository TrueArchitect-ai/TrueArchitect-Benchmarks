1st: User (~7 packages); 2nd: Memo (~7 packages)

Both the `User` and `Memo` entity types tie for the highest cross-package reference count at approximately 7 distinct packages outside the store/ directory. `User` is referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/rss`. `Memo` is referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, and `server/runner/memopayload`.
