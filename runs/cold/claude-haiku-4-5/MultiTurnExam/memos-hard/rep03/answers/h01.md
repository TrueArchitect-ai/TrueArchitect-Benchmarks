**Component**: S3 presign runner (`github.com/usememos/memos/server/runner/s3presign.Runner`)

**Schedule**: Every 12 hours (`time.Hour * 12`)

**Wiring site**: `/bench/repo/server/server.go:162`

The S3 presign runner refreshes externally-stored S3 attachment URLs (presigned URLs) every 12 hours. It checks which S3 attachments have presigned URLs that will expire within 4 days and regenerates fresh 5-day presigned URLs to keep the links from going stale. This is instantiated and launched in the server startup at line 162 of server.go.
