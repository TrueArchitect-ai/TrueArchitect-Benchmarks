**S3 Presign Runner** — **every 12 hours** — **/bench/repo/server/server.go:153** (in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method where it's registered and started)

The S3 Presign Runner is a background task that periodically refreshes presigned URLs for S3-stored attachments before they expire, preventing external links from going stale. It runs on a 12-hour schedule (defined in `/bench/repo/server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), processing attachments in batches and updating their presigned URLs when they're within 4 days of expiration (5-day validity window). The runner is wired into server startup at `/bench/repo/server/server.go:153-174` in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method, which is called from the `Start()` method.
