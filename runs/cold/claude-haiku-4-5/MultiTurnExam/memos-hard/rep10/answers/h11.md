1st: **User** (7 packages); 2nd: **Memo** (6 packages)

The `User` entity type defined in `/bench/repo/store/user.go` is referenced from 7 distinct packages outside the data layer: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/rss`. The `Memo` entity type, defined in `/bench/repo/store/memo.go`, is referenced from 6 distinct packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, and `server/runner/memopayload`. Both are core to the application's business logic, with User being slightly more pervasive due to its role in authentication and authorization across the system.
