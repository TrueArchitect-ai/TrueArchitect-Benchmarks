Server-Sent Events (SSE); /bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, CreateMemoComment

The server uses **Server-Sent Events (SSE)** to push live events to browsers. The SSE hub is implemented in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (the `SSEHub` type and `Broadcast` method).

The following memo handlers broadcast through the SSE hub:
- **CreateMemo** (line 163 of memo_service.go) — broadcasts SSEEventMemoCreated
- **UpdateMemo** (via dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects in memo_update_helpers.go line 71) — broadcasts SSEEventMemoUpdated
- **DeleteMemo** (line 585 of memo_service.go) — broadcasts SSEEventMemoDeleted
- **CreateMemoComment** (line 126 of memo_service_comments.go) — broadcasts SSEEventMemoCommentCreated
