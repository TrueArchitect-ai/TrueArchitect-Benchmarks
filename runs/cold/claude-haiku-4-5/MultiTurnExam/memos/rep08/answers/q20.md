Server-Sent Events (SSE); `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo

The server uses Server-Sent Events (SSE) to push live events to browsers. The SSE hub is implemented in sse_hub.go. The memo handlers that broadcast through the SSEHub are:
- CreateMemo (broadcasts SSEEventMemoCreated)
- UpdateMemo (broadcasts SSEEventMemoUpdated via dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects in memo_update_helpers.go)
- DeleteMemo (broadcasts SSEEventMemoDeleted)
