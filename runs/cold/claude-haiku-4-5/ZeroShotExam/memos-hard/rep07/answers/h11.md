**1st: User (7 packages); 2nd: Memo (7 packages)**

Both User and Memo are tied as the most widely referenced entity types from the store data layer. User is referenced across 7 packages including server/auth, server/notification, server/router/api/v1, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/rss, and server/router/api/v1/test. Memo is also referenced in 7 packages with similar distribution (server/notification, server/router/api/v1, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/rss, server/router/api/v1/test, and server/runner/memopayload).
