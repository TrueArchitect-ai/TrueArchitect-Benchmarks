**S3 Presign Runner** — **Every 12 hours** — **server/server.go:156-163**

The `s3presign.Runner` component prevents S3 attachment presigned URLs from going stale by regenerating them before expiration (checking if <4 days remain on 5-day URLs). It's wired into server startup at `server/server.go:156-163` in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method, where it calls `RunOnce()` immediately on startup and then launches a goroutine that runs every 12 hours (defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`).
