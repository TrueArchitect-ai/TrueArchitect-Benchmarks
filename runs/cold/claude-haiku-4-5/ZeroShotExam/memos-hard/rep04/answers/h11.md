Looking at the persistent entity types defined in the data layer (store package), I analyzed references from all packages outside of the store directory and proto-generated files.

The top two persistent entity types referenced from the most packages outside the data layer are:

**1st: Memo (8 packages); 2nd: User (8 packages)**

Both are referenced from 8 distinct packages outside the store layer:
- **Memo**: internal/webhook, server/notification, server/router/api, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/mcp, server/router/rss, server/runner/memopayload
- **User**: server/auth (3 sub-packages), server/notification, server/router/api, server/router/fileserver, server/router/frontend, server/router/rss

The third-place entity type (Attachment) is only referenced from 4 packages, confirming these two as the clear leaders.
