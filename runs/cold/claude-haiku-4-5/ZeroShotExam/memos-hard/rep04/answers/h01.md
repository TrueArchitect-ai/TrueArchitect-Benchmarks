h01. **S3 Presign Runner** — every 12 hours (refreshes URLs approaching 5-day expiration if within 4-day threshold) — `/bench/repo/server/server.go:158-171`

The component (`s3presign.Runner`) is initialized and wired into startup in `server.go`, where it runs once immediately (`RunOnce`) and then starts a continuous 12-hour interval loop. The runner regenerates S3 presigned URLs for attachments that are approaching staleness, checked against a 4-day freshness window.
