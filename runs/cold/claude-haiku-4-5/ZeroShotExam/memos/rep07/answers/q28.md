Based on my analysis of the codebase, here are the background workers/runners the server actually starts:

**S3 Presign Runner** — Scheduled every 12 hours (re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before expiry)
**Webhook Async Queue** — 4 concurrent workers, triggered by `PostAsync()` calls
**Email Async Queue** — 2 concurrent workers, triggered by `SendAsync()` calls  
**Cache Cleanup Loop** — Periodic ticker every 5 minutes (configurable, removes expired cache items)
**PAT Usage Recording** — Fire-and-forget goroutine on PAT authentication
**HTTP Server** — Continuous goroutine serving Echo HTTP requests

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** The Scheduler framework at `/bench/repo/internal/scheduler/scheduler.go` implements a full cron-like job scheduler with middleware and timezone support, but is currently not integrated into the server startup flow.
