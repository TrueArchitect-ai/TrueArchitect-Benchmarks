When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records **before** the memo row itself (in this order):

1. **memo_relation records** (where the memo is the source MemoID)
2. **memo_relation records** (where the memo is the related memo)
3. **attachments** (and their associated storage)

**Source:** `/bench/repo/store/memo.go:140-159` - The `DeleteMemo` function performs cascading deletes in this sequence before calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` which deletes the memo row itself.
