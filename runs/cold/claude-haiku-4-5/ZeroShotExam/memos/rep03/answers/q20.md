q20. Server-Sent Events (SSE); `/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; memo.created, memo.updated, memo.deleted, memo.comment.created, reaction.upserted, reaction.deleted

The mechanism is **Server-Sent Events (SSE)** as the real-time communication protocol. The **hub is implemented in `/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`**, which manages client subscriptions and broadcasts events through buffered channels. The **memo handlers that broadcast through it** are:
1. `CreateMemo` (SSEEventMemoCreated)
2. `UpdateMemo` (SSEEventMemoUpdated)
3. `DeleteMemo` (SSEEventMemoDeleted)
4. `CreateMemoComment` (SSEEventMemoCommentCreated)
5. `UpsertReaction` (SSEEventReactionUpserted)
6. `DeleteReaction` (SSEEventReactionDeleted)

These are implemented across `/server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, `/server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`, and `/server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`.
