The `store.Driver` interface is defined in `store/driver.go`. Its three implementations (entry file where `NewDB` constructs the driver struct):

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Selector: **NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver` over `"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`, erroring with "unknown db driver" otherwise; called at startup from `cmd/` paths and from test helpers such as `store/test/store.go:28`).
