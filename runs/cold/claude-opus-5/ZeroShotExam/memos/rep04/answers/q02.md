**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migration files live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect directories:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (same structure, e.g. `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`

These mirror the three driver implementations in `store/db/` (`store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`), selected by `store/db/db.go`.
