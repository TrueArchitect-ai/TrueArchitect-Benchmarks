**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The project ships migrations for three SQL dialects, each with its own directory tree under `store/migration/`:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs (0.17 … 0.30) plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versioned dirs (0.19 …) plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

These mirror the three database drivers in `store/db/` (`store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, `store/db/sqlite/`), selected in `store/db/db.go`.
