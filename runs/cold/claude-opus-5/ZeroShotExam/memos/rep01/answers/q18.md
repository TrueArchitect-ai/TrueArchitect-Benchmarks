The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with a `NewDB(profile) (store.Driver, error)` constructor:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

Factory that selects between them:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver`: `"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`, else `errors.New("unknown db driver")`).
