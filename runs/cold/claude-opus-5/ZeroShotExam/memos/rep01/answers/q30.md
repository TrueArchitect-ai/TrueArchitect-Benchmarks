**Convergence for delivery:** `webhook.PostAsync` → `webhook.Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`

- The three plain lifecycle events (created/updated/deleted) are thin wrappers over the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync(payload)` per hook (`:71`).
- `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` does not go through that helper — it looks up webhooks for the *related memo's* creator instead of the comment author, but still ends at `webhook.PostAsync(payload)` (`:43`).
- `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, dropping with a warning when full); four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`:42-55`) drain it and call `Post` (`:120`), which marshals the payload, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC headers when a signing secret is set, and sends via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.

Call sites: `memo_service.go:157` (create), `memo_update_helpers.go:67` (update), `memo_service.go:562` (delete), `memo_service_comments.go:119` (comment).
