**`web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`; `memoServiceClient`**

Details:

- `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts` is the TanStack Query module holding the memo CRUD surface: `useMemos` / `useInfiniteMemos` (list), `useMemo` (get), `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo`, plus `useLinkMetadata` and `useMemoComments` / `useInfiniteMemoComments`. It also owns the `memoKeys` query-key factory and cache invalidation.
- It imports `memoServiceClient` from `web/src/connect.ts:5` and calls `memoServiceClient.listMemos`, `.getMemo`, `.createMemo`, `.updateMemo`, `.deleteMemo`, `.listMemoComments`, `.getLinkMetadata`.
- `memoServiceClient` is defined in `web/src/connect.ts` as `createClient(MemoService, transport)` over a Connect-Web transport, where `MemoService` is the generated client stub from `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb` (generated from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`).

Related memo-adjacent clients using the same generated service: `web/src/hooks/useMemoShareQueries.ts`, `web/src/components/MemoEditor/services/memoService.ts`, and `web/src/components/MemoReactionListView/hooks.ts`.
