**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over a plain HTTP `GET /api/v1/sse` endpoint — `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, Bearer-token auth in the `Authorization` header, `: heartbeat` comments every 30s, and per-client buffered channels (events dropped for slow clients rather than blocking the broadcaster). It's not WebSockets or gRPC streaming. The browser consumes it via `fetch` (not `EventSource`, since a header is needed) in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203`.

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`, plus `SSEClient.canReceive` for visibility filtering (private memos only reach the creator or an admin). Supporting files: `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (route registration + connection loop) and `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go` (reaction-event construction). It's instantiated in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`, wired in at `v1.go:125`, and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205-206`.
