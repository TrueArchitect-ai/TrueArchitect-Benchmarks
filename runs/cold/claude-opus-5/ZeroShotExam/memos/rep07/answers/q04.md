It handles two credential types: password (bcrypt compare + `DisallowPasswordAuth` check) and SSO OAuth2 (`resolveSSOIdentity` / `resolveSSOUser` in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go`), then issues the token via `s.doSignIn` (`server/router/api/v1/auth_service_session.go:24`).

Note there's a thin Connect-protocol wrapper, `ConnectServiceHandler.SignIn` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:78`, which just unwraps the request and delegates to `APIV1Service.SignIn` — the real logic lives in `auth_service.go`.
