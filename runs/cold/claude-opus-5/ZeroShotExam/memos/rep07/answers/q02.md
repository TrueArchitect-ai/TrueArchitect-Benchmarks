**SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` (plus versioned dirs and `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/mysql/`

They're embedded into the binary at `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`), and the same three dialects appear in `store/db/` (`store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/mysql`). Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is SQLite-only.
