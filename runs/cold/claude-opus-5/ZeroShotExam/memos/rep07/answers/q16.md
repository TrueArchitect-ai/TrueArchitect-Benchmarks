`store.DeleteMemo` (`/bench/repo/store/memo.go:140-159`) removes the following before calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo` for the memo row:

1. **Memo relation records where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`).
2. **Memo relation records where the memo is the target** — `DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:145`).
3. **Attachments linked to the memo** — it lists them with `FindAttachment{MemoID: &delete.ID}` and deletes each via `store.DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:148-157`), which also removes the underlying stored file/blob before deleting the attachment row (`store/attachment.go:132-148`).

Note on scope: comments (child memos) are handled one level up in the API, not in the store — `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` lists comment-type relations and deletes those memos first, then calls `store.DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518` onward). Reactions and memo shares are not deleted in `store.DeleteMemo`.
