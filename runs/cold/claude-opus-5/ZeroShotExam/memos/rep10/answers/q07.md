`web/src/connect.ts`

All ConnectRPC service clients are created in this single file. It builds the `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) with an auth interceptor, plus a separate interceptor-free `refreshTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:65`) used only for token refresh, then exports the clients via `createClient(...)`:

- `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-194`)
- `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:197-200`)
- `identityProviderServiceClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:203`)
- `refreshAuthClient`, internal-only (`web/src/connect.ts:72`)
