**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subdirectories:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — e.g. `LATEST.sql`, `0.17/00__inbox.sql` … `0.30/`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

This matches the three driver packages in `store/db/` (`store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, `store/db/sqlite/`), with `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:22` noting `multiStatements=true` is required for running migrations.
