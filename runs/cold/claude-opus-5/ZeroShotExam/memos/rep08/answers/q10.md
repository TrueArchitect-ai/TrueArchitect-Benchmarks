`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

This is the full current schema applied to uninitialized (fresh) databases, rather than replaying the incremental per-version migrations.

- `store/migrator.go:56` defines `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"`, and the comments at `store/migrator.go:27` and `store/migrator.go:42` describe it as the "Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)," applied by `preMigrate` when the DB isn't initialized.
- `store/test/migrator_test.go:18` (`TestFreshInstall`) verifies that `LATEST.sql` applies correctly on a fresh database.

(The sibling per-version files under `store/migration/sqlite/0.x/` are incremental upgrades for existing installs; `store/seed/sqlite/01__dump.sql` is seed data, not schema.)
