**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over a plain HTTP `GET /api/v1/sse` endpoint (`Content-Type: text/event-stream`), Bearer-token authenticated, with a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s and `X-Accel-Buffering: no` to defeat proxy buffering. It is not WebSockets. Browser side is `EventSource`-style consumption in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (mounted once in `web/src/main.tsx:49`), which invalidates react-query memo/user caches on each event.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, per-client buffered channel of 32, drops events for slow clients, `canReceive` filters private memos to the creator or admins). Constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` and wired to the route in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (`RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE`), registered at `v1.go:125`, held by `server/server.go:38`. Event types and payload live in the same hub file; helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
