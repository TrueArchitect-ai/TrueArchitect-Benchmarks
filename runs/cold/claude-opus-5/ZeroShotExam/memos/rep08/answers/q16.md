The store-level `Store.DeleteMemo` (`/bench/repo/store/memo.go:140-159`) cleans up these records before calling `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row:

1. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`)
2. **`memo_relation` rows where the memo is the target/related memo** — `DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:145`)
3. **Attachments linked to the memo** — listed via `ListAttachments{MemoID: &delete.ID}` and removed one by one with `Store.DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:148-157`), which also deletes the underlying stored file/blob before the `attachment` row (`store/attachment.go:132-149`)

Then the memo row itself is deleted by the driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:237`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:244`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:259`).

Note: reactions and comment memos are *not* handled by the store's `DeleteMemo` — the API layer (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518` onward) deletes comment memos and reactions separately around that call.
