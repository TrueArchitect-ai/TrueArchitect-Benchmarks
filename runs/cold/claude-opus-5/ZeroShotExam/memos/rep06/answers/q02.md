**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Evidence:
- `store/migration/` contains exactly three dialect subdirectories: `sqlite/`, `mysql/`, `postgres/` — each with versioned migration folders (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`) plus a `LATEST.sql` schema file.
- These are embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`.
- The matching drivers are selected in `store/db/db.go:19-24` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`).

SQLite has the deepest history (migrations back to `0.2`), while MySQL starts at `0.17` and Postgres at `0.19`.
