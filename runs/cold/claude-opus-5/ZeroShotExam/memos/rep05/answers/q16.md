Notes on the surrounding layer: the API handler `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (`/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518`) additionally deletes the memo's comment memos first (each recursing through `store.DeleteMemo`) before deleting the memo itself; that is handler-level, not part of the store's `DeleteMemo`.
