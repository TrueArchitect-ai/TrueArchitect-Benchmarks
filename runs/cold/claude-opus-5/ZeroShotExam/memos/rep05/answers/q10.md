`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

On a fresh install, `preMigrate` in `store/migrator.go:211-235` detects that the database is not initialized and applies `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` in a single transaction — for SQLite that resolves to `migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (constant defined at `store/migrator.go:56`). The incremental files under the versioned directories (`store/migration/sqlite/0.x/*.sql`) are only used for upgrading existing databases.
