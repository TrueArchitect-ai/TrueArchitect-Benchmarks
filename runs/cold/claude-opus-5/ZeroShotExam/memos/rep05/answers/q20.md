**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP — a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse` stream with `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, Bearer-token auth, 30s heartbeat comments, and `X-Accel-Buffering: no` to defeat proxy buffering. The browser side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (fetch-based stream with `Accept: text/event-stream`).

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`, a mutex-guarded client set, per-client 32-event buffered channels (events dropped for slow clients rather than blocking), and visibility filtering via `SSEClient.canReceive` (private memos only reach the creator or an admin). Constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`; the endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` (wired at `v1.go:125`). Event construction helpers live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
