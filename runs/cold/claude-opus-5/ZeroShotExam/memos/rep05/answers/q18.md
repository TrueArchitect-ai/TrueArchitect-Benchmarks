The `store.Driver` interface is defined in `store/driver.go`. Three implementations (entry file each):

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite driver (`sqlite.DB`, constructed by `sqlite.NewDB`)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL driver (`mysql.DB`, constructed by `mysql.NewDB`)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — Postgres driver (`postgres.DB`, constructed by `postgres.NewDB`)

Factory that selects between them at startup:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go` (switches on `profile.Driver` over `"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`, erroring with "unknown db driver" otherwise).
