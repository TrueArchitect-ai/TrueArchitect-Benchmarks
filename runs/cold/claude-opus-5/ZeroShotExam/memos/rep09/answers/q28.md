**Runners the server actually starts**

- **s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — started in `server/server.go:153-174` (`startBackgroundRunners`). Trigger: one synchronous `RunOnce(ctx)` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a `time.NewTicker` loop on a fixed 12-hour interval (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`). It re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose presign is older than 4 days. It's the only entry in `backgroundRunnerCancels`/`backgroundRunnerWG`, cancelled via context on shutdown (`server/server.go:176-201`).
- **Cache cleanup loops ×3** (`store/cache/cache.go:75-84`, `cleanupLoop` at :199) — started implicitly by `cache.New` when the store is constructed (`store/store.go:39-53`: `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`). Trigger: `time.NewTicker(CleanupInterval)` = 5 minutes; stops on `Close()`.
- **Webhook dispatch workers ×4** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`) — started by a package `init()`, so they exist as soon as the package is linked in. Trigger: not time-based — they block on the `asyncPostQueue` channel (buffer 128) and fire per enqueued webhook event.
- **Email send workers ×2** (`internal/email/email.go:15-30`) — also package `init()`. Trigger: channel-driven off `asyncEmailQueue` (buffer 128), per queued message.
- **HTTP serve goroutine** (`server/server.go:124-128`) and **signal/shutdown goroutine** (`cmd/memos/main.go:99-103`) — event-driven (incoming connections; SIGINT/SIGTERM), not scheduled work.
- Per-request/per-connection goroutines and tickers, not background workers: SSE heartbeat ticker per client connection (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`) and the fire-and-forget PAT last-used update (`server/auth/authenticator.go:178-184`).

**Unused scheduling infrastructure**

- `internal/scheduler` — a full GitHub-Actions-style cron scheduler (cron parser, timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown; `internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, plus `README.md`/`doc.go`) is **never wired into the server**. The only references to `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` in the whole repo are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`). No production code registers a job or calls `scheduler.New()`/`Start()`; the one real periodic job (s3presign) uses a hand-rolled ticker instead.
- Related naming trap: `server/runner/memopayload` is *not* a background runner despite living under `server/runner/`. `RebuildMemoPayload` is called synchronously from the memo service on create/update (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and `:447`); nothing schedules it.
