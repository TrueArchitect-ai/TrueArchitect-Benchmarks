**Server-Sent Events (SSE) over `GET /api/v1/sse`; hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub`, wired in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:36,57,125`, streamed by `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`); handlers: `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoRelations`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`.**

### Mechanism
- Plain SSE, not WebSockets. `handleSSE` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:34`) authenticates a Bearer token, sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `: connected`, then loops writing `data: %s\n\n` frames with a 30s `: heartbeat` comment (`sseHeartbeatInterval`).
- Route registered via `RegisterSSERoutes` on the gateway group (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`).
- Browser side: `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` (fetch with `Accept: text/event-stream`, line 205), mounted in `web/src/main.tsx:49`.

### Hub
`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`; `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Close`/`Broadcast`. Each client has a 32-buffered channel and events are dropped rather than blocking on a slow client. `canReceive` filters private-visibility events to the creator or admins. Event types: `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. The hub is closed on shutdown (`server/server.go:205-206`).
