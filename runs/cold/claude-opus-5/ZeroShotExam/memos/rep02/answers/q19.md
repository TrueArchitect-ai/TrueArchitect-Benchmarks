**MemoService is exposed over (1) REST/HTTP+JSON via grpc-gateway and (2) Connect RPC** (connect/gRPC-web-style, at `/memos.api.v1.*`). There is no native gRPC listener — no `grpc.NewServer()`/`RegisterMemoServiceServer` call exists in the repo; both paths call the same `*APIV1Service` methods.

- **REST / HTTP+JSON (grpc-gateway) — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`**
  `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, with the mux mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:126-127`). Generated glue lives in `proto/gen/api/v1/memo_service.pb.gw.go`.

- **Connect RPC — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`**
  `wrap(apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...))` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, registered on the Connect mux by `RegisterConnectHandlers` and mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:295-299`. The per-method wrapping of the gRPC impls into Connect request/response types is in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (MemoService block starts at line 304). Generated glue: `proto/gen/api/v1/apiv1connect/memo_service.connect.go`.
