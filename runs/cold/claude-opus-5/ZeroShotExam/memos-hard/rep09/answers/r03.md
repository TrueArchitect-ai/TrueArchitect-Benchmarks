**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.** The question assumes a scheduled production job that doesn't exist.

What actually exists:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a **standalone, generic cron-scheduler library** (`scheduler.New()`, `Register(*Job)`, cron parser, middleware). Per `internal/scheduler/doc.go:1-9`, it's a "GitHub Actions-inspired cron job scheduler" with zero external dependencies.
- **Nothing in the application uses it.** The only files importing `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own tests: `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10` and `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`. No job is registered anywhere in `server/`, `store/`, or `cmd/` — the scheduler is never even instantiated in production code.
- There is no database-cleanup job of any kind. The closest real periodic work is the in-memory cache eviction loop in `store/cache/cache.go:199-218`, which runs on a 5-minute `time.Ticker` (`store/store.go:42`, `store/cache/cache.go:60`), not on a cron schedule and not against the database.

The "2 AM" figure the question may be reaching for appears only in **documentation and example code**, never in a live registration:

- `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` — package doc example: `Name: "daily-cleanup"`, `Schedule: "0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily)
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-106` — `Example_multipleJobs` registers a job named `"cleanup"` at `"0 2 * * *"` with a handler that just prints "Cleaning up old data..."
- `internal/scheduler/README.md:35`, `:321` — same illustrative snippet
- `internal/scheduler/README.md:240-245` — a *different* example, `"stale-session-cleanup"` at `"0 3 * * *"` (3 AM)
- `internal/scheduler/parser_test.go:82` — `"0 2 * * *"` as a parser test input

Note also that `internal/scheduler/README.md:264-269` explicitly advises *against* `0 2 * * *`, recommending staggered times like `"5 2 * * *"` — so even the docs treat 2 AM as a counter-example rather than a project convention.
