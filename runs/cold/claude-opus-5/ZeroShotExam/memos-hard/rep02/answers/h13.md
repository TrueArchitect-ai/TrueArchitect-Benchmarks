**Implementation** — exactly one: the unexported `service` struct wrapping a `goldmark.Markdown` (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:57-60`). Test-only constructions: `server/router/api/v1/test/test_helper.go:44`, `server/router/rss/rss_test.go:54`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver_test.go:427`.
