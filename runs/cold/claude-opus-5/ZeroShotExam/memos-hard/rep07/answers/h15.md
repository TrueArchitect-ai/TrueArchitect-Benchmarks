**Memo-visibility enforcement sites (server side)**

Single-memo read gate (Connect/gRPC API v1) — `checkMemoReadAccess` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68`; called by `GetMemo` (`memo_service.go:356`), by the parent-memo check for comments (`memo_service.go:367`), and by `ListMemoComments` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:148`)

List-query visibility predicate pushed into the store (`ListMemos`) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:217-227` (anonymous → `VisibilityList=[PUBLIC]`; authenticated → CEL filter `creator_id == N || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]`)

Comment-list and relation-list filters (same CEL predicate, separate copies) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:156-162`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:107-112`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go:181-205` (`batchConvertMemoRelations`)

User/instance memo stats — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:82-92` (`ListAllUserStats`) and `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:216-221` (`GetUserStats`)

Memo sub-resource RPCs, each with its own inline copy of the check — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:213-226` (`ListMemoAttachments`), `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:29-41` (`ListMemoReactions`) and `:82-85` (`UpsertMemoReaction`), `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41-43` (`CreateMemoComment`)

Attachment metadata access via API v1 — `checkAttachmentAccess` at `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:422-455` (falls through to the linked memo's visibility)

Raw attachment/file HTTP surface (not gRPC) — `checkAttachmentPermission` at `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650-703`, including the anonymous-instance rule (`:677`), the `share_token` bypass (`:684-689`) and the private-memo rule (`:699`)

Share-token surface (deliberate bypass of visibility, gated on token validity/expiry instead) — `GetMemoByShare` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:152-190` plus `getActiveMemoShare`/`isMemoShareExpired` at `:243-260`; creation/listing/revocation are creator-or-admin gated at `memo_share_service.go:43-45, 89-91, 130-140`

SSE live-event delivery filter — `SSEClient.canReceive` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-143` (private events only to creator or admin), fed by `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go:29-38`

Anonymous public-feed surfaces, hardcoded to PUBLIC only — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90` (explore feed) and `:141` (user feed); `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150` (sitemap.xml)

Notification/mention fan-out (who may see memo content in an inbox item or email) — `canUserAccessMentionContext`/`shouldSkipMentionInbox` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:55-88`; `canViewerAccessMemo` at `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302-317` (used at `:337, :342, :370, :385`); duplicate implementation for email at `server/notification/email.go:306-321` (used at `:155, :187`)

Note: the MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) is a proxy over the same API v1 handlers (`server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-25`), so it inherits enforcement rather than adding its own; the store layer (`store/memo.go`) only builds the `VisibilityList`/`Filters` predicates it is handed and enforces nothing on its own.
