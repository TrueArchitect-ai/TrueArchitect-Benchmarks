**Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP; server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo (via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`), DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism.** Plain SSE — not WebSockets. `SSEHub` (`sse_hub.go:57`) keeps a `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` guarded by an `sync.RWMutex`, each `SSEClient` holding a buffered `events chan []byte` plus the connection's `userID` and `role` for delivery filtering. `Broadcast` is at `sse_hub.go:115`. The HTTP endpoint lives in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` from `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:124` so it shares the CORS setup of the rest of `/api/v1`.

**Event types** (`sse_hub.go:14-21`): `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. `SSEEvent.Visibility` and `CreatorID` are `json:"-"` — server-side filtering only, never sent to clients.
