**Driver — store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` is declared at `store/driver.go:10`. It covers connection lifecycle (`GetDB`, `Close`), initialization/size introspection (`IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`), and per-model CRUD methods for attachments, memos, users, and the rest.

The three backends each implement it with a struct named `DB` — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — and `store/db/db.go` selects one based on the configured driver.
