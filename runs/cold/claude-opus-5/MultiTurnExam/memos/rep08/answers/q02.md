**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

Migrations ship under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subtrees — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` — each with versioned directories (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.30/`) plus a `LATEST.sql` schema dump. This matches the three driver implementations in `store/db/` (`store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`), selected by `store/db/db.go`.
