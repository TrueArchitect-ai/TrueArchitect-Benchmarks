**Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `cel-go` — /bench/repo/internal/filter/** (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`; see `internal/filter/README.md`).

**Mechanism:** A three-phase pipeline — `cel-go` parses/validates the expression against the memo-specific CEL environment declared in `internal/filter/schema.go`, `internal/filter/parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a dialect-specific SQL fragment plus placeholder args, so each driver just calls `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect{SQLite,Postgres,MySQL}, &where, &args)` and appends the result to its `WHERE` clause (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, with the same pattern in `store/db/postgres/memo.go` and `store/db/mysql/memo.go`).

The filter string arrives via the `filter` field on `ListMemosRequest` (`proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`) and is handled API-side in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_filter.go` before reaching the store.
