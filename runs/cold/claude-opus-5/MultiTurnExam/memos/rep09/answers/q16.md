`Store.DeleteMemo` (`/bench/repo/store/memo.go:140-158`) removes, in order, before issuing the `DELETE FROM memo`:

1. **memo_relation rows where the memo is the source** — `DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}` (`store/memo.go:142`)

3. **attachments linked to the memo** — listed via `FindAttachment{MemoID}` and deleted one by one with `Store.DeleteAttachment` (`store/memo.go:149-157`), which also removes the underlying stored file/blob before the DB row (`store/attachment.go:132-148`)
