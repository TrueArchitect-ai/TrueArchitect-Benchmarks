**store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

On a fresh install the migrator detects an uninitialized DB and applies `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` — i.e. `migration/<driver>/LATEST.sql` from the embedded FS — at `/bench/repo/store/migrator.go:218-219` (constant defined at line 56, `//go:embed migration` at line 44). For SQLite that resolves to `/bench/repo/store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`, which contains the full current schema (`CREATE TABLE` statements for `user`, `memo`, `memo_relation`, `attachment`, etc.).
