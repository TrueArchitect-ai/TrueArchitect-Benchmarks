**gRPC-Gateway REST** — `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` binds `APIV1Service` into the gateway mux, which is mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (`v1.go:126-129`). The URL shapes come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`. Note this registers the *server* directly (no in-process gRPC socket), with auth applied via `gatewayAuthMiddleware` (`v1.go:96`).

**Connect** — `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`, inside `RegisterConnectHandlers`. It's wired up in `v1.go:140-144`, mounted at `/memos.api.v1.*` with the interceptor chain (metadata, logging, recovery, auth). The per-RPC wrappers that unwrap `connect.Request` and delegate to `APIV1Service` live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at :306). This path replaced grpc-web and is what the browser client in `web/src/connect.ts` talks to.
