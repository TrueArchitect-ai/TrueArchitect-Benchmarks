**gRPC-Gateway / REST-JSON.** `APIV1Service.RegisterGateway` (`v1.go:65`) builds a `runtime.NewServeMux` with auth middleware and calls `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `v1.go:108`, mounting it on Echo under `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (lines ~125-127). Routes come from the `google.api.http` annotations in `/bench/repo/proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (e.g. `POST /api/v1/memos`).

**ConnectRPC.** `ConnectServiceHandler` (`connect_handler.go:21`) embeds `*APIV1Service`, and `RegisterConnectHandlers` registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` at `connect_handler.go:40`. The per-method wrappers that unwrap `connect.Request`/rewrap `connect.Response` and translate gRPC status codes are in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` at line 307); the interceptor chain and mux mounting happen back in `v1.go` at the end of `RegisterGateway`. This is the protocol the web client uses via `/bench/repo/web/src/connect.ts`.

Both paths delegate to the same `APIV1Service` methods in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, so business logic has one implementation.
