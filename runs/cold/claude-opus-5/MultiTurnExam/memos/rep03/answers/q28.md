Details:

- **`s3presign.Runner`** — the only runner `Server.startBackgroundRunners` explicitly launches (`/bench/repo/server/server.go:152-172`): `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately, then `Run(s3Context)` in a tracked goroutine. `Run` ticks every `runnerInterval = 12h` (`/bench/repo/server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`) and re-presigns S3 attachment URLs whose last presign is older than 4 days. It has its own cancel func and `WaitGroup` entry, so shutdown is orderly.
- **Webhook workers** — started by a package `init()` in `/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`, not by the server: 4 goroutines consuming `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128, line 39). Triggered by `PostAsync` calls from the memo handlers; when the queue is full, dispatches are dropped with a warning.
- **Cache cleanup loops** — `cleanupLoop` at `/bench/repo/store/cache/cache.go:200`, one goroutine per `cache.New`; the store creates three (`instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`) at `/bench/repo/store/store.go:51-53`.
- **SSE heartbeat** — `time.NewTicker(sseHeartbeatInterval)` at `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`, alive for the duration of each client stream.
- **HTTP serve goroutine** — `/bench/repo/server/server.go:124`.

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** `memopayload.Runner` in `/bench/repo/server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` (`NewRunner` line 19, `RunOnce` line 27) is **never started** — nothing in the repo calls `memopayload.NewRunner`, and `startBackgroundRunners` only wires up s3presign. Only the package's standalone helper `RebuildMemoPayload` (line 74) is used, called inline from `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` and `UpdateMemo` (`/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108, 447`). So the payload-rebuild runner is dead scheduling infra; its work happens synchronously on the request path instead.
