**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, which has exactly three dialect subdirectories — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` — each containing a `LATEST.sql` plus versioned migration folders (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.28/00__user_identity.sql`).
