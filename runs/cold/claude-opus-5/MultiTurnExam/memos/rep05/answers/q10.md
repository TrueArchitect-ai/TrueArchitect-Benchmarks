**store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql**

`preMigrate` checks `IsInitialized`; for an uninitialized DB it reads `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName` — i.e. `migration/<driver>/LATEST.sql`, which resolves to `migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` in the embedded FS (`store/migrator.go:211-222`, `store/migrator.go:254-256`, constant at `store/migrator.go:56`). Fresh installs apply that full schema instead of replaying the incremental `sqlite/<version>/*.sql` files.
