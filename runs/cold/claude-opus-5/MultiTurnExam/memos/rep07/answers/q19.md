**Connect (Connect-RPC/gRPC-style POST at `/memos.api.v1.MemoService/*`)** — server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go, server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go

**REST/JSON via grpc-gateway (`/api/v1/memos...`)** — server/router/api/v1/v1.go

How each is wired:

- **Connect:** `ConnectServiceHandler` (`connect_handler.go:21`) embeds `*APIV1Service`; `RegisterConnectHandlers` registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, ...)` on its mux at `connect_handler.go:40`. The per-RPC wrapper methods that unwrap `connect.Request` and delegate to `APIV1Service` live in `connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at `:306`). Mounted onto the Echo server at `v1.go:139-144` under the `/memos.api.v1.*` path prefix.

- **REST:** the grpc-gateway mux is built at `v1.go:96` and `MemoService` is registered directly against the same `APIV1Service` via `RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` at `v1.go:108` (in-process, no network hop). It's mounted at `v1.go:128-129` for `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*`, using the `google.api.http` annotations from `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

Both protocols share one implementation — the `APIV1Service` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
