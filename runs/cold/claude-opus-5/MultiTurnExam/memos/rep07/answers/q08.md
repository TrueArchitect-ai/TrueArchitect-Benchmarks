**Driver — store/driver.go**

Declared as `store.Driver` at `store/driver.go:10`. It covers connection lifecycle (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus the CRUD methods for every model (attachment, memo, user, etc.).

The three implementations are the `DB` structs in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, each returned as a `store.Driver` from its `NewDB` constructor and selected by driver name in `store/db/db.go`.
