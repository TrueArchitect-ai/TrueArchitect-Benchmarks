**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/{driver}/...` — there are exactly three driver directories: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/`, each with versioned subdirectories plus a `LATEST.sql`.

These are embedded and selected by driver at runtime in `store/migrator.go:44-45` (`//go:embed migration`) and `store/migrator.go:255` (`migration/%s/` keyed on `s.profile.Driver`). The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`.

Note: seed data is SQLite-only (`store/seed/sqlite` is the sole seed directory).
