**Server-Sent Events (SSE); server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go (endpoint in server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go); CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism.** SSE over HTTP. The hub is `SSEHub` at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`, with `Subscribe` (:71), `Unsubscribe` (:89), `Broadcast` (:115), and `Close` (:99). Event types are declared at `sse_hub.go:15-20`: `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. The browser-facing endpoint is registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, wired into the Echo group at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`.
