- **Connect:** `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` on the `http.ServeMux` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`). The per-RPC method wrappers that adapt `connect.Request`/`connect.Response` to the plain gRPC signatures live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `CreateMemo` at line 306, `ListMemos`). This is the protocol the web client uses (`web/src/connect.ts`).
- **REST:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` registers the service on the grpc-gateway mux (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`), which is then mounted on Echo at `/api/v1/*` (line 128) — that's what makes the `google.api.http` annotations in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto` (e.g. `POST /api/v1/memos`) reachable.

Both protocols dispatch to the same `APIV1Service` methods in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`.
