**Mechanism:** the built Vite bundle is compiled into the Go binary with `//go:embed` and served as static files by Echo middleware, with an SPA fallback to `index.html`.

**Path:** `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`

Specifics: `//go:embed dist/*` captures `server/router/frontend/dist` into `embeddedFiles` (line 20). `FrontendService.Serve` (line 40) turns it into an `fs.FS` via `getFileSystem("dist")` (line 115) and installs `middleware.StaticWithConfig` over it (line 52), followed by `spaFallbackMiddleware(frontendFS)` (line 58) so client-side routes resolve to the SPA shell.
