**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subdirectories — `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/mysql/`, and `store/migration/postgres/` — each with its own `LATEST.sql` plus versioned migration folders (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/0.30/00__user_tag_setting.sql`).
