Method: enumerated the entity structs declared in the `store` package (the data layer) and counted the distinct Go packages referencing each, excluding `store/...` itself (including its `store/db/*` driver subpackages), the generated `proto/gen/store` tree, and `_test.go` files.

- `server/router/fileserver` — `/bench/repo/server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:708,715`
