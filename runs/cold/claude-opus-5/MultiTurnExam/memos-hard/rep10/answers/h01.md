How it works:

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. `CheckAndPresign` (line 47) pages through attachments with `StorageType = S3` in batches of 100, calls `s3.Client.PresignGetObject` for each S3 object, and writes the fresh URL back via `store.UpdateAttachment` as the attachment's `Reference`, stamping `LastPresignedTime` (lines 108–123). So the stored link to the externally-hosted (S3) blob never expires out from under clients.
- **Schedule**: `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (lines 29–41). Each pass skips any attachment whose presigned URL is still valid for at least the next 4 days — presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry (`runner.go:85-91`).
- **Wiring**: `server/server.go:162` constructs it, `server/server.go:163` runs one immediate pass (`RunOnce`) so links are refreshed at boot, and `server/server.go:166-171` starts the ticker loop in a goroutine tracked by `backgroundRunnerWG` under a cancellable `s3Context` so `Shutdown` (`server/server.go:140,143`) can stop and wait for it.
