**How they converge.** The four `Dispatch*Webhook` wrappers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:15`, `:20`, `:25`, `:30`) all funnel into either `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`) or the comment variant, which look up the memo owner's configured webhooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks` and call `webhook.PostAsync` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`). The three `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` callers share a single hop through `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:67`.

`PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) only enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel; the 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (`webhook.go:42-55`) are what call **`webhook.Post`** — the single function where every path converges and where the outbound request is actually built, signed, and executed at `safeClient.Do(req)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`).

The target URL is fully user-configured (`payload.URL = hook.Url` from the user's stored webhooks), which is why delivery goes through the SSRF-guarded `safeClient` (`webhook.go:32-37`).
