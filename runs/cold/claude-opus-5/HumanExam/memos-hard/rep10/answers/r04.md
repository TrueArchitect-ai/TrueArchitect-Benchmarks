False premise: neither — there is no message broker at all (no NATS, RabbitMQ/AMQP, or any broker client in `go.mod`), and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:38`) drained by 4 goroutines spawned in `init()` (`:42-56`), each calling `Post` (`:120`). Producers call `PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:190-196`) — so delivery is best-effort and non-durable, lost on restart.
