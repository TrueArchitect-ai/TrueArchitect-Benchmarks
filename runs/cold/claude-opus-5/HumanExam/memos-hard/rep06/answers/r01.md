— **False premise: there is no Redis anywhere in the codebase** (no `redis` import in `go.mod`, no match for "redis" in any Go/config file). The cache layer is a process-local in-memory cache: `store/cache/cache.go` (`Cache` backed by `sync.Map`, `Close` at `:187`). The `Store` creates three of them — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — with `cache.Config{DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute, CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute, MaxItems: 1000}` (`store/store.go:38-52`). So user entries get the **default 10-minute TTL** (`Set` → `SetWithTTL(..., c.config.DefaultTTL)`, `store/cache/cache.go:93-95`), plus eviction when >1000 items; entries are also written/invalidated explicitly in `store/user.go:88, 121, 148-168` and `store/user_delete.go:38-46`. Nothing is configurable via connection string or flag.
