— **False premise: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used; there is no message broker and no queue declaration.** Neither appears in `go.mod` or any source file. Webhook delivery is in-process: `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) does a non-blocking send into a package-level buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`:42-55`) that call `Post` (`:120`). If the channel is full the event is dropped with a warning (`:191-195`) — there is no persistence, retry, or external broker.
