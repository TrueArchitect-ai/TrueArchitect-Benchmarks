— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker at all — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ (nor Kafka/AMQP) appears in `go.mod` or any source file, and no queue is declared. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel, `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`); producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the buffer is full. Delivery is `webhook.Post` (`:120`). The queue is not persistent — pending deliveries are lost on restart.
