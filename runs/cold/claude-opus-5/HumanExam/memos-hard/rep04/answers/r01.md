Three instances are created in `store.New` — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — `store/store.go:47-58`, with config `DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute`, `MaxItems: 1000` (`store/store.go:40-45`). Caches are shut down in `Store.Close` (`store/store.go:72-77`).
