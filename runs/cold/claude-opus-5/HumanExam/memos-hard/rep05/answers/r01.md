— **Premise is wrong: there is no Redis anywhere in this codebase** (no `redis` import in `go.mod`, no match repo-wide outside `web/`). Caching is an in-process, sharded-free `sync.Map` cache: `store/cache/cache.go` (`Cache`, `New` at `:74`, TTL expiry in `SetWithTTL` `:94` and `cleanupLoop`). The `Store` creates three of them — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` — with `cache.Config{DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute, CleanupInterval: 5 * time.Minute, MaxItems: 1000}` at `store/store.go:38-51`. So **user entries expire 10 minutes after being set** (`store/user.go:132`, `:145`, `:160` call `Set`, which applies `DefaultTTL`); they are also invalidated explicitly on update/delete (`store/user.go:170` → `deleteUserCache`). Nothing is configurable via flags or env.
