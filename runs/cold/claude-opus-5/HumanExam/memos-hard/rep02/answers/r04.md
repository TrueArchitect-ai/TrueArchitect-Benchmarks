— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker at all — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ appears in `go.mod` or any source file, and no queue is declared anywhere. Webhook delivery uses an in-process buffered Go channel: `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that call `Post` (`:120`). Overflow is dropped with a warning rather than persisted (`PostAsync`, `:189-195`), so the "queue" is non-durable and dies with the process.
