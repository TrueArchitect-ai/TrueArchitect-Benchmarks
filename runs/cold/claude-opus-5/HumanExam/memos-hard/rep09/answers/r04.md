— Premise is wrong: there is no message broker at all — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ appears in go.mod or any source file, and no queue is declared in any external system. Actual mechanism: an in-process buffered Go channel. `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:39), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54), each calling `Post` (:120). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (:184), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the delivery with a warning when the buffer is full (:192-195). Delivery is therefore best-effort, in-memory, with no retries and no persistence across restarts.
