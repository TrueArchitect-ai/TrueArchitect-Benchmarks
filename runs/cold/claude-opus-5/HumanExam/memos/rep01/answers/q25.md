— Engine: Google CEL (`github.com/google/cel-go`), wrapped by the in-repo `internal/filter` package — `engine.go` (CEL env/compile), `schema.go` (exposed fields/functions), `parser.go` (CEL AST → dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR in `ir.go`), `render.go` (IR → SQL), `helpers.go` (`AppendConditions`). Mechanism: each driver's `ListMemos` calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` then `filter.AppendConditions(ctx, engine, find.Filters, filter.Dialect{SQLite|Postgres|MySQL}, &where, &args)` (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:54-58`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:65-69`), and `render.go` switches on the `DialectName` to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments plus bound args that are appended to the WHERE clause.
