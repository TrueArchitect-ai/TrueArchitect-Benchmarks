**Background workers/runners the server actually starts, and their scheduling:**

- **S3 Presign Runner** — `time.NewTicker` every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`); launched by `Server.startBackgroundRunners()` in `server/server.go:153–173`, with one immediate `RunOnce` at startup then a goroutine loop
- **Webhook Async Dispatch (4 goroutines)** — event-driven via buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`, capacity 128); started by package-level `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–55`
- **Email Async Dispatch (2 goroutines)** — event-driven via buffered channel (`asyncEmailQueue`, capacity 128); started by package-level `init()` in `internal/email/email.go:14–36`
- **Cache Cleanup (3 goroutines)** — `time.NewTicker` every 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go:199–215`); one goroutine per cache (`instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`), launched when `store.New()` creates them in `store/store.go:51–53`

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:** The `internal/scheduler/` package is a fully-featured cron job framework (cron expression parser, middleware chain with `Recovery`/`Logging`/`Timeout`, timezone support, graceful shutdown) that is **never imported by any production code** — only by its own tests. The S3 presign runner uses a simple `time.NewTicker` loop instead of this scheduler.
