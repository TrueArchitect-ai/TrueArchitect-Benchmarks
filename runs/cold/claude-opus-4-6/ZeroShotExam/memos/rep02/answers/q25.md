**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with the CEL environment into an AST, then `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) walks the CEL AST to produce a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `renderer.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits dialect-specific SQL (SQLite, MySQL, or Postgres) by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` for each condition node.
