**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`

**Handler names that broadcast through the hub:** `CreateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`, and `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`

The `SSEHub` struct in `sse_hub.go` manages connected clients and provides a `Broadcast()` method that pushes events (e.g., `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`) to browser clients via `text/event-stream`. The six handlers listed above call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)` from across `memo_service.go`, `memo_service_comments.go`, `memo_update_helpers.go`, and `reaction_service.go`. On the client side, `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts` consumes the SSE stream and invalidates React Query caches accordingly.
