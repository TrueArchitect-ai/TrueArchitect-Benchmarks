**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, living in `internal/filter/` (engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`, parser at `internal/filter/parser.go`, renderer at `internal/filter/render.go`).

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with `cel-go` into a CEL AST, converts it to a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and then `renderer.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that condition tree and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) for each condition type — generating the appropriate placeholders, JSON operators, date-part extraction, and case-folding functions per dialect.
