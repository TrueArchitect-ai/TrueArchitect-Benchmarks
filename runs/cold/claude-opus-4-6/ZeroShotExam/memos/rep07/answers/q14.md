The RPCs of `ShortcutService` (defined in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`) are:

1. **ListShortcuts**
2. **GetShortcut**
3. **CreateShortcut**
4. **UpdateShortcut**
5. **DeleteShortcut**

They are implemented in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** (the core logic on `APIV1Service`), with Connect/gRPC-gateway wrappers that delegate to those methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`.
