When a memo is deleted through the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`), the following kinds of records are deleted before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the **source** (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}`)
2. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the **target** (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}`)
3. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo (`FindAttachment{MemoID: &delete.ID}`, then each is deleted individually)
