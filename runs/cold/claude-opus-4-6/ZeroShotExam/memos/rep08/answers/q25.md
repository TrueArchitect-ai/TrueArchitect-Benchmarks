The filter engine uses **CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go`, implemented in `internal/filter/engine.go`. The `Engine.Compile` method parses the CEL expression into a parsed AST, then `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) converts it into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `Program.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments (with per-dialect branches for `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, and `DialectMySQL`) using parameterized placeholders.
