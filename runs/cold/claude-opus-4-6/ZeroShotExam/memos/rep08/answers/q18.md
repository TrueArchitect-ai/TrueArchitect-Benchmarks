The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. **SQLite** — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. **MySQL** — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. **PostgreSQL** — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory that selects between them at startup is:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (sourced from the `--driver` CLI flag or `MEMOS_DRIVER` env var) to instantiate the appropriate backend.
