The three implementations of the `Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` (SQLite)
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` (MySQL)
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` (PostgreSQL)

The factory that selects between them at startup:

**`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`

This function switches on `profile.Driver` to instantiate the appropriate backend, and is called from `cmd/memos/main.go` during startup.
