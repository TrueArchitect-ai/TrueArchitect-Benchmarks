When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) (`store/memo.go:142`)
2. **Memo relations** — where the memo is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) (`store/memo.go:145`)
3. **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo (`store/memo.go:149–157`)
