q07. `web/src/connect.ts`

This file creates all the ConnectRPC service clients (lines 192–203) using `createClient` from `@connectrpc/connect`, including clients for `InstanceService`, `AuthService`, `UserService`, `MemoService`, `AttachmentService`, `AIService`, `ShortcutService`, and `IdentityProviderService`.
