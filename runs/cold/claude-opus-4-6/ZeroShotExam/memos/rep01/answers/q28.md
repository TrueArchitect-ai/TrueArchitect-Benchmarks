**Unused scheduling infrastructure:**

The `internal/scheduler/` package is a fully-implemented cron-based job scheduler (~500 lines across `scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`, etc.) supporting cron expressions, middleware (recovery, logging, timeout), and graceful shutdown. It is **entirely dead code** — nothing outside its own package and tests imports it. The actual background runners (like the S3 presign runner) use plain `time.NewTicker` loops instead.
