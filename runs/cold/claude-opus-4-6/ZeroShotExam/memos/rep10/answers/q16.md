When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes the following kinds of records before the memo row itself:

- **Memo relations** — deleted via `DeleteMemoRelation`, called twice: once where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and once where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
- **Attachments** — all attachments linked to the memo are listed via `ListAttachments` and each is deleted via `DeleteAttachment`
