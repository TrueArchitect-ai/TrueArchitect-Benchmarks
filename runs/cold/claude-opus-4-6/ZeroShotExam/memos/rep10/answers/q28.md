**Background workers/runners the server actually starts, and their scheduling triggers:**

- **S3 Presign Runner** — `time.NewTicker` every 12 hours (plus one synchronous `RunOnce` at startup). Started in `server/server.go:startBackgroundRunners` (~line 153). Refreshes presigned URLs expiring within 4 days (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`).
- **Webhook Async Dispatch Workers (×4)** — channel-driven (`asyncPostQueue`, buffered 128). Goroutines launched at package `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`; work enqueued by `PostAsync()` on memo create/update.
- **Email Async Dispatch Workers (×2)** — channel-driven (`asyncEmailQueue`, buffered 128). Goroutines launched at package `init()` in `internal/email/email.go:16-36`; work enqueued by `SendAsync()` from the notification email dispatcher.
- **Cache Cleanup Goroutines (×3)** — `time.NewTicker` every 5 minutes. One per cache instance (`instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`), started in `store/cache/cache.go:cleanupLoop` when `store.New()` creates them (`store/store.go:51-53`).

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:**

- **`internal/scheduler/` package** — A fully-featured cron-expression job scheduler (with 5/6-field cron parsing, timezone support, middleware for recovery/logging/timeout, graceful shutdown). It is **never imported by any production code** — only by its own test files (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`). None of the active runners above use it; they all rely on simple `time.NewTicker` loops or channels instead.
- **`server/runner/memopayload/` Runner struct** — has a `RunOnce()` method but no continuous `Run()` loop, and `NewRunner()` is never called outside tests. Only the standalone `RebuildMemoPayload()` function is used (inline in memo create/update), not the Runner itself.
