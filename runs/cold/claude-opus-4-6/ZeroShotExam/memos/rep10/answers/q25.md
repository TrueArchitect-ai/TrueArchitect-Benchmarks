CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; the `Engine.Compile` method uses `cel-go` (`github.com/google/cel-go/cel`) to parse the filter string into a CEL AST, then `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) converts that AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree), and `renderer.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks the IR and emits SQL fragments with dialect-specific branches (switching on `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, `DialectMySQL`) for placeholders, JSON operators, case-folding, and date-part extraction.
