The following database tables are affected when `DeleteMemo` runs:

1. **`memo`** — the memo row itself is deleted (`store/db/sqlite/memo.go:239`), and recursively for any comment memos (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:574`)
2. **`memo_relation`** — relations where the memo is either `memo_id` or `related_memo_id` are deleted (`store/memo.go:142-145`, executed via `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go:148-150`)
3. **`attachment`** — all attachments linked to the memo are deleted (`store/memo.go:149-156`, executed via `store/db/sqlite/attachment.go:212`)
