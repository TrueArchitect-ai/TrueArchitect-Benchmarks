**Interceptor path:** `web/src/connect.ts` — defines `authInterceptor` (line ~156) as a ConnectRPC `Interceptor` that attaches `Authorization: Bearer <token>` to every outgoing RPC request, handles 401 retries with token refresh, and coalesces concurrent refresh calls through a `tokenRefreshManager` singleton.

**Token kinds:**
1. **Access token** — a short-lived JWT (15 min), stored in-memory and `localStorage` (`memos_access_token`), sent as `Bearer` in the `Authorization` header.
2. **Refresh token** — a long-lived JWT (30 days), stored server-side and delivered as an `HttpOnly` cookie named `memos_refresh`; never directly accessible to frontend JS; sent automatically via `credentials: "include"`.
3. **Personal Access Token (PAT)** — long-lived, prefixed `memos_pat_`, stored as a SHA-256 hash in the database; the backend distinguishes it from a JWT by the prefix.

**Backend validation package:** `server/auth/` — specifically `server/auth/authenticator.go` (`resolveBearer()` dispatches to either JWT or PAT validation), `server/auth/token.go` (JWT parsing/verification via `golang-jwt/jwt/v5`), and `server/auth/extract.go` (extracts bearer tokens and refresh cookies from requests). The Connect-layer interceptor that calls into this package lives at `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go` (`AuthInterceptor`).
