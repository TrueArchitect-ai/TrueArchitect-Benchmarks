**Workers the server actually starts:**

- **S3 Presign Runner** — `time.NewTicker` every 12 hours (plus one synchronous `RunOnce` at startup); re-signs expiring S3 attachment URLs. Started by `server.startBackgroundRunners()` in `server/server.go:153-174`, implemented in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`.
- **Cache Cleanup (×3 goroutines)** — `time.NewTicker` every 5 minutes; evicts expired entries from `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache`. Spawned by `cache.New()` → `go c.cleanupLoop()` in `store/cache/cache.go:83`, instantiated in `store/store.go:38-61`.
- **Webhook Dispatch (×4 goroutines)** — channel-driven (`asyncPostQueue`, buffered 128); consumes webhook payloads enqueued by `PostAsync()`. Started in package-level `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`.
- **Email Send (×2 goroutines)** — channel-driven (`asyncEmailQueue`, buffered 128); consumes email requests enqueued by `SendAsync()`. Started in package-level `init()` at `internal/email/email.go:14-36`.

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:**

- **`internal/scheduler/` package** — a fully-featured cron-expression-based job scheduler (with timezone support, middleware, graceful shutdown) that is **never imported by any production code**. It is only referenced by its own test files (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`). The S3 presign runner, which would be a natural consumer, uses a plain `time.NewTicker` loop instead.
- **`server/runner/memopayload/` runner** — has a `RunOnce()` batch-rebuild method but no ticker-based `Run()` loop and is **never started** by `startBackgroundRunners()`. Its `RebuildMemoPayload` function is only called synchronously inline from memo create/update handlers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, not as a background worker.
