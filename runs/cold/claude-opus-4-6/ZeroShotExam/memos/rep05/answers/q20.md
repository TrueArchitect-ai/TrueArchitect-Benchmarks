**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`

The `SSEHub` struct manages client connections with `Subscribe`, `Unsubscribe`, `Broadcast`, and `Close` methods. Each `SSEClient` has a buffered event channel, and `Broadcast` fans out events filtered by visibility via `canReceive()`. The HTTP endpoint is registered at `GET /api/v1/sse` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`.
