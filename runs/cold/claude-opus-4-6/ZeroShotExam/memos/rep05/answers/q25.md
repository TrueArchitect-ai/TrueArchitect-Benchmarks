**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/` (entry point `internal/filter/engine.go`). The `Engine.Compile` method parses the CEL filter string into an AST, converts it to a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/parser.go`), and then `Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) walks that IR and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (SQLite, Postgres, or MySQL) for every node type — producing the appropriate SQL functions, operators, placeholders, and JSON accessors for each dialect.
