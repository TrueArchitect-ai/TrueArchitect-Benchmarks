Four memo lifecycle events dispatch webhooks, with dispatch functions defined in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`:

- **memo created**: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- **memo updated**: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- **memo deleted**: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- **memo comment created**: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

The first three delegate to a shared private helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (same file), while `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` iterates webhooks directly.

All of them converge for delivery at **`webhook.PostAsync`** in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`, which enqueues payloads onto a buffered channel. Worker goroutines drain that channel and call **`webhook.Post`** (same file) to perform the actual HTTP POST delivery.
