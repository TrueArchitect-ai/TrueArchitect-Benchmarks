**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, living in `internal/filter/` (`engine.go`, `parser.go`, `render.go`, etc.).

The `Engine.Compile` method (`internal/filter/engine.go:48`) parses the filter string with the CEL environment, converts the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic `Condition` IR tree (via `buildCondition` in `internal/filter/parser.go`), and then `Program.Render` (`internal/filter/engine.go:96`) walks that IR with a `renderer` (`internal/filter/render.go`) that switches on `RenderOptions.Dialect` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments with proper placeholders, JSON operators, and case-folding for each backend.
