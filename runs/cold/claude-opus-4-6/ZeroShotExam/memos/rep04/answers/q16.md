When a memo is deleted, the **store layer** (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes the following records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations (where the memo is the source)** — deletes all `memo_relation` rows where `memo_id` matches the memo being deleted (line 142)
2. **Memo relations (where the memo is the target)** — deletes all `memo_relation` rows where `related_memo_id` matches the memo being deleted (line 145)
3. **Attachments** — lists all attachments linked to the memo, then deletes each one individually (lines 149–157)
