The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/*.go` and its sub-packages `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/postgres`, `store/db/mysql`). It defines 11 persistent entity types: `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `IdentityProvider`, `UserSetting`, `InstanceSetting`, and `UserIdentity`.

**1st: `User`** — referenced from ~7 packages outside the store layer (`proto/gen/store`, `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`)

**2nd: `Memo`** — referenced from ~6 packages outside the store layer (`proto/gen/store`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)
