Memo visibility is enforced across many server surfaces. Here are all distinct enforcement sites:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (central gate for GetMemo/ListMemoComments)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
2. **`ListMemos` query-level visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
4. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
5. **`CreateMemoComment` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
6. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filter on comment memos** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
7. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
8. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (gRPC attachment path)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
9. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (HTTP file-server path)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
10. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
11. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
12. **SSE hub `canReceive` delivery filter** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
13. **RSS feed (global + per-user) restricted to PUBLIC** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
14. **Sitemap restricted to PUBLIC** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
15. **`canViewerAccessMemo` for email notifications** — `server/notification/email.go`
16. **`GetUserStats` / `ListAllUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
17. **`UpdateMemo` forces comment visibility inheritance from parent** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
18. **ACL config + Authorizer (method-level anonymous access gating)** — `server/router/api/v1/acl_config.go` and `server/router/api/v1/authz.go`
