**Computation site:** `RebuildMemoPayload` in `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74-88` calls `markdownService.ExtractAll()` (defined at `internal/markdown/markdown.go:356-422`) which does a single-pass goldmark AST walk over the memo's Markdown content. **Triggers:** Three operations trigger recomputation:

2. **UpdateMemo** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:447` calls `RebuildMemoPayload` when memo content is updated.
