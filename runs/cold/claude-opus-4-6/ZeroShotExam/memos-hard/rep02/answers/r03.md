The premise of this question is incorrect. There is no "nightly database-cleanup job" configured in this repository's cron scheduler. The scheduler (`internal/scheduler/`) is a general-purpose cron scheduling library — it does not come with any pre-configured jobs, including a database-cleanup job.

The closest thing in the codebase is an **example** in `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-105`, which shows a `"cleanup"` job (described as "Cleanup old data every night at 2 AM") with schedule `"0 2 * * *"`. However, this is purely illustrative test/example code, not an actual scheduled job that runs in production. No real database-cleanup job is registered anywhere in the application.
