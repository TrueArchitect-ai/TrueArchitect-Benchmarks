**Determination method:** The default driver is hard-coded to `"sqlite"` in two places in `cmd/memos/main.go`: `viper.SetDefault("driver", "sqlite")` (line 123) and the flag default `rootCmd.PersistentFlags().String("driver", "sqlite", ...)` (line 131). At startup, the profile's `Driver` field flows into `store/db/db.go:NewDBDriver` (line 14), which switches on the driver name and selects `sqlite.NewDB`. The resulting driver is stored in `Store.driver` (`store/store.go`), and `Store.CreateMemo` (`store/memo.go:109`) delegates directly to `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`. Since there are three competing implementations — `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/mysql/` — the one that actually runs is determined entirely by the `"driver"` config value, which defaults to `"sqlite"` when no `--driver` flag is passed.
