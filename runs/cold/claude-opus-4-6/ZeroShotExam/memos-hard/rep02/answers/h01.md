**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired in at `server/server.go:153`

The `s3presign.Runner` (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically refreshes S3 presigned URLs stored in attachment `Reference` fields. It runs on a 12-hour ticker (`runnerInterval` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), re-signing any URL whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days, per `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`).

It is wired into server startup in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`), which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`. There it is instantiated, run once immediately (line 163), and then launched in a goroutine on the ticker (lines 166-171).
