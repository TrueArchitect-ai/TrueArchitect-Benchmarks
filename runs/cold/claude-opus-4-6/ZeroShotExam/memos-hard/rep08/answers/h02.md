**Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (with `PostAsync` feeding a buffered channel drained by worker goroutines that call `Post`).

**Protection mechanism** — SSRF protection via two layers, both in `internal/webhook/`:

1. **URL validation at registration time** — `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` parses the URL, enforces `http`/`https` scheme, DNS-resolves the hostname, and checks every resolved IP against `isReservedIP` (which blocks RFC-1918, loopback, link-local/IMDS, and IPv6 private ranges defined in `reservedCIDRs`).

2. **Dial-time enforcement** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` re-resolves the hostname and re-checks resolved IPs against `isReservedIP` at the moment the TCP connection is made, preventing DNS rebinding attacks that could bypass the registration-time check.

**Answer:** delivery function — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `isReservedIP` / `reservedCIDRs` checks in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (registration-time) and `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (dial-time).
