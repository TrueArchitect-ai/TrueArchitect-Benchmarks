**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner refreshes S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire. Presigned URLs are created with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), and the runner skips any attachment presigned less than 4 days ago (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:88`), effectively renewing URLs ~1 day before they go stale. At startup, `RunOnce` is called immediately (line 163), then `Run` is launched in a goroutine (lines 166–170) to tick every 12 hours.
