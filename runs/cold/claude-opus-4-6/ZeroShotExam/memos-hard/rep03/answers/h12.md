**Concrete method:** `(*DB) CreateMemo` — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:16`

This method builds and executes `INSERT INTO memo (...) VALUES (...) RETURNING id, created_ts, updated_ts, row_status` against a local SQLite file.

**Determination method:** The `--driver` flag defaults to `"sqlite"` (set in two places in `cmd/memos/main.go`: `viper.SetDefault("driver", "sqlite")` at line 122 and the flag definition at line 130). The factory function `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go:14` switches on `profile.Driver` — when it's `"sqlite"`, it instantiates `sqlite.NewDB(profile)` (line 20). The resulting driver is injected into the store at `cmd/memos/main.go:64`, and `store.CreateMemo` (`store/memo.go:113`) delegates directly to `s.driver.CreateMemo(ctx, create)`. Since the `Driver` interface (`store/driver.go`) has three implementations — `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/mysql/` — but the default flag value selects SQLite, it is the SQLite implementation's `CreateMemo` that runs on a default installation.
