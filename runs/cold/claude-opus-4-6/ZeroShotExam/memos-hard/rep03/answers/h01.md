**S3 presign runner** — every **12 hours** — wired at `server/server.go:153`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically re-signs presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It ticks every 12 hours (`runnerInterval`, line 27) and refreshes any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (the URLs themselves expire after 5 days, giving 1 day of headroom).

It is wired into server startup in `server/server.go` at the `startBackgroundRunners` method (line 153). That method is called from `Server.Start()` (line 129). It instantiates the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` (line 162), runs an immediate pass with `RunOnce` (line 163), and then launches the continuous 12-hour loop in a goroutine (line 169).
