The data layer lives in `store/` (Go package `github.com/usememos/memos/store`), which defines all persistent entity structs.

**1st: User** (`store/user.go`) — referenced from ~6 packages outside `store/` (`server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`)

**2nd: Memo** (`store/memo.go`) — referenced from ~5–6 packages outside `store/` (`server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`)

These two are the core domain objects and are consumed far more broadly than any other entity. The next closest is **Attachment** at ~4 external packages.
