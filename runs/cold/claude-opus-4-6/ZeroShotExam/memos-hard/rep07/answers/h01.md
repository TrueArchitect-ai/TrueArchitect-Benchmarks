The runner lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It periodically re-signs presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments so they don't expire. At startup, `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) creates the runner (line 162), runs an immediate pass via `RunOnce` (line 163), then launches a goroutine (lines 166-170) that ticks every 12 hours via `Run`.
