The data layer is the `store` package (`store/`), which defines 11 persistent entity types.

1. **User** — referenced from **7 packages** outside `store/`: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`
2. **Memo** — referenced from **6 packages** outside `store/`: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

(`Attachment` comes third at ~4 packages.)

Both are defined in `store/user.go` and `store/memo.go` respectively.
