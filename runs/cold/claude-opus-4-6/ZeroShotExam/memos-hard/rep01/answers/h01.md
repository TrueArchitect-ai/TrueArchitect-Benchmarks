**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` (`startBackgroundRunners`), which is called from `Server.Start` (line 129).

The runner refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It re-signs any URL whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, generating a new presigned URL valid for 5 days, ensuring at least a 1-day buffer before expiration. It runs once immediately at startup (`server/server.go:163`) and then on a 12-hour ticker via a background goroutine (`server/server.go:169`).
