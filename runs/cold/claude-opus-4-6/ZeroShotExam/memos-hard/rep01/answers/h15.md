Here are the distinct memo visibility enforcement sites:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (shared gate for GetMemo / ListMemoComments)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
2. **`ListMemos` query-time visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
4. **`ListMemoComments` query-time visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
5. **`ListMemoReactions` inline visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
6. **`UpsertMemoReaction` inline visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
7. **`ListMemoAttachments` inline visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
8. **`ListMemoRelations` query-time visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
9. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (memo-linked attachment gate)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
10. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (file-server HTTP serving)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
11. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` bulk relation filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
12. **RSS feed (system & user), public-only filter** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
13. **Sitemap generation, public-only filter** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
14. **SSE delivery filter (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
15. **`ListAllUserStats` query-time visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
16. **`GetUserStats` query-time visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
17. **`canUserAccessMentionContext` (mention notification gating)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go`
18. **`canViewerAccessMemo` (API notification payload filter)** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go`
19. **`canViewerAccessMemo` (email notification dispatch filter)** — `server/notification/email.go`
20. **Store/SQL layer `VisibilityList` WHERE clause** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
21. **Store/SQL layer memo-relation JOIN filter** — `store/db/sqlite/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo_relation.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo_relation.go`
