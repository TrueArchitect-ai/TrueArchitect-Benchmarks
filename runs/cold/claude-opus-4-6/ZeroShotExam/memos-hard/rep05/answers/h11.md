The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/*.go`), which defines 11 persistent entity types: User, Memo, Attachment, MemoRelation, MemoShare, Inbox, Reaction, IdentityProvider, UserSetting, UserIdentity, and InstanceSetting.

**1st: Memo** — referenced from **7 packages** outside the store layer: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`

**2nd: User** — referenced from **7 packages** outside the store layer: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`

These two are effectively tied at ~7 packages each. Memo edges out slightly when counting companion types like `Visibility` and `FindMemoPayload` which pull in additional runner packages, while User's reach is broader across auth-related packages. The next closest is **Attachment** at ~5 packages.
