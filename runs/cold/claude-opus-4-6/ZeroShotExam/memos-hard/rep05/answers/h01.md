**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (with a freshness threshold of 4 days against a 5-day presigned URL lifetime) — wired in at `server/server.go:162–163` inside `startBackgroundRunners`, where the runner is created via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, run once immediately, then launched in a background goroutine (`server/server.go:166–171`).

The runner itself lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches, checks each attachment's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-signs any URL that was last signed more than 4 days ago by calling `PresignGetObject` (which issues a new presigned GET URL with a 5-day expiry, defined in `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`). The `startBackgroundRunners` function is called from `server/server.go:129` during `Start()`.
