Here are the distinct memo visibility enforcement sites:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` (core gatekeeper for single-memo reads)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
2. **`ListMemos` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`
3. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
4. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`
5. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go`
6. **`batchConvertMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go`
7. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
8. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`
9. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go`
10. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (gRPC attachment access)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go`
11. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (file server)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go`
12. **`GetUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
13. **`ListAllUserStats` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`
14. **SSE hub `canReceive` delivery filter** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`
15. **RSS feed (explore + user) public-only filter** — `server/router/rss/rss.go`
16. **Sitemap public-only filter** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go`
17. **Email notification `canViewerAccessMemo`** — `server/notification/email.go`
18. **User notification `canViewerAccessMemo`** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go`
19. **Mention helper `canUserAccessMentionContext`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go`
20. **Store-level SQL `WHERE visibility IN (…)` clause** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`
