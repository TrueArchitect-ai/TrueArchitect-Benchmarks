The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ). The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`), declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go`. The channel has a capacity of 128 and is initialized at package level. Four goroutines are launched in `init()` (lines 42–55 of that file) to drain the channel and call `Post` for each payload. `PostAsync` (line 184) enqueues payloads onto this channel with a non-blocking send that drops the message if the buffer is full. The entire mechanism is in-process with no external dependencies.
