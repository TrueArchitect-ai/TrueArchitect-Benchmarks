The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. The only scheduled background runners are the S3 presign URL refresher (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the in-memory cache cleanup (every 5 minutes, `store/cache/cache.go:60`). The `internal/scheduler/` package exists but is used for user-configured notification scheduling, not database maintenance. No database vacuuming, purging, or cleanup is scheduled anywhere.
