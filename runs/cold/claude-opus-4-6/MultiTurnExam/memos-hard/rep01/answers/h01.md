**S3 Presign Runner** — every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup via `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:153`

The runner refreshes presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire (URLs are valid for 5 days; the runner re-signs any that are within 1 day of expiring). It is instantiated and kicked off in `startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:162–169`), which runs `RunOnce` immediately at startup, then launches the ticker-based `Run` loop in a goroutine on a 12-hour interval.
