**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired in at `server/server.go:163–169`

The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It periodically re-signs S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments so they don't expire (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; the runner refreshes any that were last signed more than 4 days ago).

It is wired into server startup in `server/server.go` inside `startBackgroundRunners` (line ~153), which is called from `Server.Start()`. At startup it runs once immediately (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce`, line 163), then launches the recurring 12-hour ticker loop in a goroutine (`s3presignRunner.Run`, line 169).
