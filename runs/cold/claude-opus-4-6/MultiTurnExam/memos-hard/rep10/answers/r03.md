The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. While there is a scheduler package (`internal/scheduler/`), and background runners exist (such as the S3 presign runner in `server/runner/s3presign/` which runs every 12 hours), none of them perform database cleanup, and none are scheduled as nightly cron jobs. There is no scheduled task that purges old rows, vacuums tables, or performs any other form of database maintenance.
