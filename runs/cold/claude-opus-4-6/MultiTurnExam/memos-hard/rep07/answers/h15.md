The major distinct enforcement sites for memo visibility:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess` helper** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68` (central per-memo check; used by `GetMemo` at line 356 and `ListMemoComments` at line 148)

2. **`ListMemos` query-level filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-227` (sets `VisibilityList` or CEL filter based on auth state)

3. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:157-161` (CEL-based `MemoFilter` on comment visibility)

4. **`CreateMemoComment` parent visibility gate** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41` (PRIVATE parent restricted to creator/admin)

5. **`ListMemoReactions` inline check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-41`

6. **`UpsertMemoReaction` inline check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:83`

7. **`ListMemoAttachments` inline check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:214-225`

8. **`checkAttachmentAccess` (gRPC attachment service)** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:419-455` (enforces memo visibility for linked attachments)

9. **`checkAttachmentPermission` (HTTP file server)** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:649-703` (with share-token fallback)

10. **RSS feed handlers** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90,141` (query restricted to `VisibilityList=[Public]`)

11. **Sitemap generation** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150` (only PUBLIC memos)

12. **`ListMemoRelations` handler** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:108-112` (CEL `MemoFilter` on visibility)

13. **User stats handlers** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:83-91` (`ListAllUserStats`) and `:217-221` (`GetUserStats`)

14. **SSE delivery filter (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` (PRIVATE events only delivered to creator/admin)

15. **Notification visibility gates** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302-316` and `server/notification/email.go:306-320` (`canViewerAccessMemo`); plus `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:56-72` (`canUserAccessMentionContext`)

16. **SQL-level enforcement** — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:96-102`, `store/db/postgres/memo.go:91-95`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go:106-110` (translate `VisibilityList` into `WHERE visibility IN (...)`)
