The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. While an `internal/scheduler` package exists, it is not wired into the server startup (`server/server.go`, `cmd/memos/main.go`). The only background runners registered at startup are the S3 presign URL refresher (`server/runner/s3presign`). The only "cleanup" that occurs is in-memory cache expiration (`store/cache/cache.go`, every 5 minutes) and on-demand resource cleanup during user deletion (`server/router/api/v1/user_service.go`). No scheduled database maintenance or purge job exists.
