The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (NATS, RabbitMQ, or otherwise) involved in webhook delivery. The webhook workers consume from a plain **in-memory Go buffered channel**.

The channel is declared as `asyncPostQueue` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` with a capacity of 128. Four goroutines are started in the package-level `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:43`) to drain this channel and call `Post` for each payload. `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) simply sends the payload into this channel. There is no external queue or broker anywhere in the webhook system.
