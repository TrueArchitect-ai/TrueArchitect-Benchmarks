**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup via `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:153`.

The runner re-signs S3 presigned URLs for attachments before they expire. URLs are given a 5-day lifetime; the runner skips any whose `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old, so it refreshes URLs within roughly 1 day of expiry. At startup, `RunOnce` is called synchronously (`server/server.go:163`), then the continuous ticker loop is launched in a goroutine (`server/server.go:166–170`).
