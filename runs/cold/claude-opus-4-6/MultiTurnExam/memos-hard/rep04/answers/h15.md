Memo visibility is enforced at many distinct sites across the server. Here are the key enforcement locations:

1. **`checkMemoReadAccess`** (central single-memo gatekeeper) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39–67`
2. **`ListMemos` query-time visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218–227`
3. **`CreateMemoComment` parent-memo visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:33–43`
4. **`ListMemoComments` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:148–161`
5. **`ListMemoReactions` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:29–41`
6. **`UpsertMemoReaction` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:82–85`
7. **`checkAttachmentAccess` via memo visibility** — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go:419–455`
8. **`ListMemoAttachments` visibility check** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go:213–224`
9. **`ListMemoRelations` visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go:107–112`
10. **Memo converter relation-loading filter** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go:182–187`
11. **SSE `canReceive` delivery filter** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134–144`
12. **RSS feed PUBLIC-only filtering** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90,141`
13. **File server attachment access** — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:674–701`
14. **Frontend sitemap PUBLIC-only** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150`
15. **User stats visibility filtering** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go:83–91,217–221`
16. **Mention notification gating (`canUserAccessMentionContext`)** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:56–89`
17. **Notification payload conversion (`canViewerAccessMemo`)** — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:302–316`
18. **Email notification (`canViewerAccessMemo`)** — `server/notification/email.go:306–320`
