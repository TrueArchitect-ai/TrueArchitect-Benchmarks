The question's premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) in this codebase. Webhook delivery uses a plain **Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`) declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. Four goroutines are started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42–54`) that range over this channel and call `Post()` for each payload. The entire mechanism is in-process with no external queue or broker involved.
