Protection mechanism — two-layer SSRF guard:

2. **Dial-time re-check (DNS rebinding defense):** `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) re-resolves the hostname at connection time and re-checks against `isReservedIP`, so a DNS rebinding between registration and delivery is still blocked.
