**Protection mechanism** — two-layer SSRF guard in `internal/webhook/validate.go` and `internal/webhook/webhook.go`:

1. **Registration-time URL validation**: `ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) resolves the hostname and checks every IP against `isReservedIP` (`validate.go:44`), which blocks RFC-1918 private ranges, loopback, link-local (including the cloud metadata endpoint `169.254.0.0/16`), and IPv6 equivalents (defined in `reservedCIDRs`, `validate.go:14-23`). Called when webhooks are created/updated in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74,161`.

2. **Connection-time dial guard** (defeats DNS rebinding): `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) re-resolves the hostname at dial time and re-checks against the same reserved-IP list, so a hostname that rebinds from a public to a private IP between registration and delivery is still blocked. This is wired into the `safeClient`'s transport at `webhook.go:35`.
