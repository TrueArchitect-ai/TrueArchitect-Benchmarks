The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) involved in webhook delivery. The mechanism is a plain Go buffered channel.

In `internal/webhook/webhook.go`, `asyncPostQueue` is declared as a `chan *WebhookRequestPayload` with a buffer size of 128 (line ~33). Four goroutines are spawned in `init()` (lines ~42-55) to drain this channel and call `Post()` for each payload. `PostAsync` (line ~184) simply sends the payload into this channel. There is no external queue or broker — it is entirely in-process.
