**S3 Presign Runner** — every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`) — wired in at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically regenerates presigned S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments before they expire. S3 presigned URLs are created with a 5-day lifetime (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), and the runner re-signs any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, giving a 1-day safety margin.

It is wired into server startup in `server/server.go` inside `startBackgroundRunners()` (line ~162): a new `s3presign.Runner` is created, `RunOnce` is called immediately to catch anything that went stale while the server was down, and then the runner is launched in a goroutine to tick every 12 hours.
