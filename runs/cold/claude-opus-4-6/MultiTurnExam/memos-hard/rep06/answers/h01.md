The **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps externally-stored attachment presigned URLs from going stale by periodically re-signing them.

- **Component**: `s3presign.Runner` — it queries all S3-type attachments in batches, checks each one's `LastPresignedTime`, and re-signs any URL older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days, giving a 1-day safety margin).
- **Schedule**: Every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`).
- **Wiring site**: `server/server.go:162–169` — inside `startBackgroundRunners`, the runner is created with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, run once immediately via `RunOnce(ctx)`, then launched as a background goroutine via `Run(s3Context)`.
