The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) in this codebase. The webhook delivery workers consume from a plain **Go buffered channel** (`asyncPostQueue`), declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`. Four goroutines are spawned in `init()` (lines 42–55 of the same file) that drain this channel and call `Post()` directly. `PostAsync` (line 183) sends payloads into the channel, dropping them with a warning if the buffer is full. Everything is in-process with no external messaging infrastructure.
