The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. The only scheduled background runners are the S3 presign runner (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the memo payload runner (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`), neither of which performs database cleanup. The `internal/scheduler` package exists but is not used for any database maintenance task.
