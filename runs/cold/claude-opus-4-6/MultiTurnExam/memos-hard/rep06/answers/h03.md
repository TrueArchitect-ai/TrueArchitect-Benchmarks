**Abstraction package**: `internal/idp` (`internal/idp/idp.go`) — defines the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct that every provider must return.

**Implementation package**: `internal/idp/oauth2` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`) — the sole concrete implementation, providing a standard OAuth2 authorization-code flow with `ExchangeToken` and `UserInfo` methods. No other protocol (SAML, LDAP, OIDC, etc.) ships.
