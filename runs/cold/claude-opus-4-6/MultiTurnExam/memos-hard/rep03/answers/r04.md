The premise is incorrect — there is no message broker (neither NATS nor RabbitMQ) in this codebase. Webhook delivery uses a simple in-memory Go buffered channel, not an external queue.

In `internal/webhook/webhook.go`, an `asyncPostQueue` channel (capacity 128) is declared, and 4 goroutines are launched in `init()` (lines ~42–55) to drain it. `PostAsync` (line ~184) sends payloads onto this channel, and the goroutines call `Post` to execute the HTTP delivery. There is no external broker, no durable queue, and no queue declaration beyond the channel itself.
