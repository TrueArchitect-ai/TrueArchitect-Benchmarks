**Mechanism: explicit Go code** — there is no database-level `FOREIGN KEY ... ON DELETE CASCADE` on the `reaction` table; its schema has no foreign key constraints at all (see `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql:92–99`, and the equivalent in `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql:91` and `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:91`).

Instead, reactions are deleted in two explicit steps within the user-deletion transaction, present in all three drivers:

1. **Reactions on the user's memos** (by `content_id`): `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:380–388`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:383`, `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:420`.
2. **Reactions created by the user** (by `creator_id`): `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:400–403`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:401`, `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:438`.

The test `TestDeleteUserCleansRelatedData` in `store/test/user_delete_test.go` exercises this path.
