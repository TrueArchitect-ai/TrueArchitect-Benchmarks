**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired at **`server/server.go:162`**

The runner regenerates expiring S3 presigned URLs for attachments. Presigned URLs are valid for 5 days (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`), and the runner refreshes any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days, giving a 1-day buffer before expiration.

It is wired into startup in `server/server.go` inside `startBackgroundRunners`: a `s3presign.NewRunner` is created (line 162), `RunOnce` is called immediately for an initial sweep (line 163), and then `Run` is launched in a goroutine on the 12-hour ticker (lines 167–170). `startBackgroundRunners` itself is called from `Server.Start` at line 129.
