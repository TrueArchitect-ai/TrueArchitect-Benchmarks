The premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. The repository does include a cron expression parser (`internal/scheduler/`) for user-facing scheduled tasks, and background runners (S3 presign refresh, memo payload rebuild), but none of them perform database cleanup on a nightly schedule. The only periodic cleanup is the in-memory cache eviction loop in `store/cache/cache.go` (every 5 minutes), which is not a database operation. Attachment storage cleanup happens synchronously during user/attachment deletion, not on a schedule.
