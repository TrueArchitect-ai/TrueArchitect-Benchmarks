**`s3presign.Runner`** — runs every **12 hours** (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup at **`server/server.go:162`** inside `startBackgroundRunners`, where it is instantiated with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, given an immediate `RunOnce` call (line 163), and then launched in a goroutine via `s3presignRunner.Run()` (line 169).

The runner re-signs S3 pre-signed URLs for externally-stored attachments. Pre-signed URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry (`internal/storage/s3/s3.go`), and the runner refreshes any attachment whose last presign is more than 4 days old, giving a ~1-day safety margin before links go stale.
