**Abstraction package:** `internal/idp` (`internal/idp/idp.go`) — defines the shared `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct that all providers must return.

**Implementation package:** `internal/idp/oauth2` (`internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go`) — the only concrete implementation that ships, providing an **OAuth2** identity provider with `ExchangeToken` and `UserInfo` methods.

The two are wired together in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go`, where `resolveSSOIdentity` checks for `IdentityProvider_OAUTH2` and instantiates the `oauth2.IdentityProvider` to perform the token exchange and user-info lookup.
