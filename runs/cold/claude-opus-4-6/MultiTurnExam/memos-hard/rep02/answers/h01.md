**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`) — wired into server startup via `startBackgroundRunners()` at **`server/server.go:153`**

The runner refreshes S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments. It re-signs any attachment whose URL was presigned more than 4 days ago (URLs expire after 5 days, giving a 1-day margin). At startup, `startBackgroundRunners()` creates the runner (`server/server.go:162`), runs it once immediately (`:163`), then launches it as a background goroutine on a 12-hour ticker (`:166-171`).
