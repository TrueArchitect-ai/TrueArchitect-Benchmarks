When a memo is deleted, the store layer (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes these records before the memo row itself:

1. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the source, i.e. `memo_id`)
2. **Memo relations** (where the memo is the target, i.e. `related_memo_id`)
3. **Attachments** linked to the memo

Additionally, at the API layer (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:567-577`), **comment memos** (child memos related via the `Comment` relation type) are recursively deleted before the parent memo, and each of those comment deletions triggers the same store-level cleanup of relations and attachments.
