Engine: **CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, implemented in `internal/filter/engine.go` (the `Engine` struct and `DefaultEngine()` function).

The filter string is parsed into a CEL AST by the engine, then converted into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`). The IR is rendered into dialect-specific SQL WHERE clauses by `internal/filter/render.go`, which switches on `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, and `DialectMySQL` to emit the correct SQL syntax. Each database driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect (e.g., `filter.DialectSQLite` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`) to append the generated conditions and bind args to the query.
