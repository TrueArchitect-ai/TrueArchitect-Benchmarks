**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, engine in `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The filter string is parsed into a CEL AST by the `Engine` in `internal/filter/engine.go`, then compiled into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`). The `AppendConditions` function (`internal/filter/helpers.go`) passes each filter through the engine and then the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`), which walks the IR and emits dialect-specific SQL fragments by switching on `DialectSQLite`, `DialectPostgres`, or `DialectMySQL` — each dialect driver (e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`) calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its dialect constant to get the appropriate SQL WHERE clauses and bound arguments.
