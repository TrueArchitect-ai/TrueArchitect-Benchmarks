Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel` — `internal/filter/engine.go`

The `Engine` parses the filter string into a CEL AST, which is then lowered into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`); the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) translates that IR into SQL WHERE clauses with dialect-specific branches for SQLite, PostgreSQL, and MySQL. Each dialect driver calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (e.g., `filter.DialectSQLite` in `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `filter.DialectPostgres` in `store/db/postgres/memo.go`, `filter.DialectMySQL` in `store/db/mysql/memo.go`) to append the resulting SQL conditions and arguments to the query.
