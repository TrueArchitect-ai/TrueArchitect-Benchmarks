Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language), implemented in `internal/filter/` — `engine.go` compiles CEL expressions via `cel.Env`, `parser.go` converts the AST to a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree), and `render.go` walks the IR emitting dialect-specific SQL fragments. Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect constant (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL`), which compiles the filter and appends SQL WHERE clauses with dialect-appropriate functions (e.g., `strftime` for SQLite vs `EXTRACT` for Postgres).
