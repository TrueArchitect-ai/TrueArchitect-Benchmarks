The filter uses **CEL (Common Expression Language)** parsed by `cel-go`. The engine lives at `internal/filter/` — `engine.go` compiles CEL to AST, `parser.go` converts it to an intermediate representation (`ir.go`), and `render.go` renders the IR to dialect-specific SQL (SQLite/PostgreSQL/MySQL) with parameterized placeholders. Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` from `internal/filter/helpers.go` to inject filter SQL into the WHERE clause.
