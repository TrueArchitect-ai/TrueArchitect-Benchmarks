Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `google/cel-go`, in `internal/filter/` (main entry: `internal/filter/engine.go`). The `Engine.Compile` method parses the CEL filter into a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree), then `Render` in `internal/filter/render.go` translates the IR into SQL with dialect-specific placeholders and syntax for each of SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL. Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions` to compile filters and append SQL WHERE clauses and args to the query.
