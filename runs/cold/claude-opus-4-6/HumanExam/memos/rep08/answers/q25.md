Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`, implemented in `internal/filter/engine.go`. The engine parses the CEL filter expression into a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree), then `internal/filter/render.go` renders it to SQL with per-dialect placeholders and functions (DialectSQLite, DialectPostgres, DialectMySQL). Each db driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` or `engine.CompileToStatement()` with its dialect, producing dialect-specific SQL WHERE clauses and parameterized args.
