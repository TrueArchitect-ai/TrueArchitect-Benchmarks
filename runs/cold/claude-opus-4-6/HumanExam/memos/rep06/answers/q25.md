CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; the CEL expression is compiled into an AST, walked by `internal/filter/parser.go` into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree, then rendered to dialect-specific SQL by `internal/filter/render.go` which switches on DialectSQLite/DialectPostgres/DialectMySQL. Each DB driver calls `engine.CompileToStatement()` passing its own dialect via `filter.RenderOptions`.
