- Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, in `internal/filter/engine.go`
- The `Engine` compiles a CEL filter string into a parsed AST, then `internal/filter/parser.go` converts the AST into a dialect-agnostic condition IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` renders that IR into dialect-specific SQL (SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL) with parameterized placeholders.
