Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language) via `cel-go`, in `internal/filter/` (entry point: `DefaultEngine()` in `engine.go`)
Mechanism: CEL expressions are parsed and type-checked against a memo-specific schema (`schema.go`), normalized to a dialect-agnostic IR (`parser.go`/`ir.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL fragments with appropriate placeholders, JSON access patterns, and text-matching operators (`render.go`) for SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL.
