Before deleting the memo row itself, the store layer deletes:
1. Memo relations (where memo is source: `memo_relation.memo_id`)
2. Memo relations (where memo is target: `memo_relation.related_memo_id`)
3. Attachments linked to the memo

Additionally, at the handler level (`memo_service.go:567-577`), comment memos (child memos related via COMMENT type) are recursively deleted first. And `memo_share` rows are cascade-deleted at the database level via `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
