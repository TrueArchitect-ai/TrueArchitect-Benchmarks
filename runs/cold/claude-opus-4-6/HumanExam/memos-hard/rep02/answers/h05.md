When a user is deleted, reactions are removed in two steps within the delete-user transaction: (1) `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` deletes reactions on the user's memos (e.g., `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:383`), and (2) `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` deletes reactions created by the user (e.g., `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:401`). The reaction table has no foreign keys or CASCADE constraints (verified in `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql:91-98` and equivalent sqlite/postgres schemas).
