**Abstraction package:** `internal/idp` — `internal/idp/idp.go` defines the provider-neutral abstraction, the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct (identifier, display name, email, avatar URL) that any provider implementation must produce.

**Implementation package:** `internal/idp/oauth2` — `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` ships the one concrete protocol implementation, an OAuth2 `IdentityProvider` built from a `storepb.OAuth2Config`, with `ExchangeToken` (including PKCE support) and user-info retrieval that maps provider fields into `idp.IdentityProviderUserInfo`.

Both are consumed by the SSO auth flow in `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go:12-13`.

**Answer:** `internal/idp` (abstraction); `internal/idp/oauth2` (OAuth2 implementation).
