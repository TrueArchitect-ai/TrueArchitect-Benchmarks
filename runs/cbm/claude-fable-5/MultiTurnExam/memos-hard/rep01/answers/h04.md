**No.** The MySQL driver is only ever constructed through the factory.

Evidence:

- The constructor is `NewDB` in `store/db/mysql/mysql.go:20`, and the only call to it in the entire codebase is inside the driver-selection factory `NewDBDriver` at `store/db/db.go:22`.
- A repo-wide grep shows `github.com/usememos/memos/store/db/mysql` is imported exactly once — by `store/db/db.go:8`. No other file can construct the driver without importing that package.
- The other `mysql.*` hits are unrelated packages: `store/test/containers.go` uses the testcontainers-go MySQL *container* module (`github.com/testcontainers/testcontainers-go/modules/mysql`) and the low-level `github.com/go-sql-driver/mysql` for connection checks — not the repo's driver type. Tests that need a real store go through the factory as well (`store/test/store.go:28,50` and `server/router/api/v1/test/memo_service_benchmark_test.go:70` all call `db.NewDBDriver`).

**Answer:** no; `mysql.NewDB` is called only from the factory `store/db/db.go:22`, and `store/db/db.go:8` is the sole import of `store/db/mysql` — all other construction paths (including tests) go through `db.NewDBDriver`.
