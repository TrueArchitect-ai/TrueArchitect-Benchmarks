The nearest "cleanup" behavior is the in-memory cache's expired-entry sweep every 5 minutes (`store/cache/cache.go`, `CleanupInterval` set in `store/store.go:42`), which is also not a database job.

It's currently dead/unused infrastructure, with nothing scheduled at any time.
