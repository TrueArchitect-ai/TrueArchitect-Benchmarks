**1st: `store.User` (~6 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~4 packages)**

Counting distinct non-test packages outside the data layer (i.e., excluding everything under `store/`) that reference each persistent entity struct defined in the `store` package:

- **`store.User` — 6 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test` (test-helper package), `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`. (5 if you discount the test-helper package.)
- **`store.Memo` — 4 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.
- Runners-up for context: `store.Attachment` — 3 packages (`server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`); `store.Inbox` — 2; `store.Reaction`, `store.IdentityProvider`, `store.UserSetting`, `store.MemoRelation` — 1 each; `store.Activity`, `store.Shortcut` — 0.

So `User` is the most widely referenced entity outside the data layer, with `Memo` second — regardless of whether the test-helper package is counted.
