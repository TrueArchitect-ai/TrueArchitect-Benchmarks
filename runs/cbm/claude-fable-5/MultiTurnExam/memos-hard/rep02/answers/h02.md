**Delivery function: `Post` (with `PostAsync` enqueueing to a worker pool) — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism: dial-time SSRF guard `safeDialContext`/`isReservedIP` against reserved/private CIDRs — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` and `internal/webhook/validate.go`**

Details:

- **Delivery:** `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` builds the JSON payload, optionally adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signature headers, and executes the HTTP POST via `safeClient.Do` (`webhook.go:152`). Callers typically go through `PostAsync` (`webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto a bounded channel drained by 4 background workers (`webhook.go:42-55`); those workers call `Post`. It's triggered from memo change paths, e.g. `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`.
- **Protection:** the shared `safeClient` uses a custom `http.Transport` whose `DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`webhook.go:32-37`, `59-77`). At dial time it resolves the hostname and refuses to connect if any resulting IP falls in a reserved/private range (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS 169.254.0.0/16, and IPv6 equivalents) defined in `reservedCIDRs` and checked by `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54`. Doing the check at dial time also defeats DNS-rebinding. There's an additional first line of defense at registration time — `ValidateURL` (`validate.go:62`) rejects non-http(s) schemes and URLs resolving to reserved IPs — and an escape hatch `AllowPrivateIPs` (`validate.go:41`) for self-hosted deployments.
