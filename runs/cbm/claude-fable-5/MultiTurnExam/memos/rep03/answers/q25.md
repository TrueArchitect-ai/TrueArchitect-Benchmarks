**Engine: CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `github.com/google/cel-go` — the engine lives in `internal/filter/`** (`engine.go`, `schema.go`, `parser.go`, `ir.go`, `render.go`; documented in `internal/filter/README.md`).

**Mechanism:** The filter string is compiled in a three-phase pipeline — cel-go parses and validates the expression against the memo field schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`), `internal/filter/parser.go` normalizes the CEL AST into a dialect-agnostic IR (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and `internal/filter/render.go` walks that IR to emit a SQL WHERE fragment plus placeholder args tailored to the target dialect (`DialectSQLite`/`DialectMySQL`/`DialectPostgres`, handling differences like `?` vs `$n` placeholders, JSON access via `JSON_EXTRACT` vs `->>`, and boolean coercion); each dialect driver invokes this from its `ListMemos` — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectSQLite, &where, &args)` to splice the rendered conditions into its query.
