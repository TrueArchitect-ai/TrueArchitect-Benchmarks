web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts; memoServiceClient

The memo CRUD queries and mutations (`useMemos`, `useInfiniteMemos`, `useMemo`, `useCreateMemo`, `useUpdateMemo`, `useDeleteMemo`, `useMemoComments`, etc.) live in `web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts`. They use the `memoServiceClient`, which is created in `web/src/connect.ts` via `createClient(MemoService, ...)` from the generated `MemoService` definition in `web/src/types/proto/api/v1/memo_service_pb.ts`.
