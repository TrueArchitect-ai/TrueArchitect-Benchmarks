Before deleting the memo row, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes:

- **Memo relations** — `memo_relation` records where the memo is either the source (`MemoID`) or the target (`RelatedMemoID`), via two `DeleteMemoRelation` calls
- **Attachments** — every attachment linked to the memo, listed with `ListAttachments` and removed one by one via `DeleteAttachment`

Only then does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row itself.
