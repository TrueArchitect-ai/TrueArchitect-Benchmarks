Before deleting the memo row itself, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes:

1. **Memo relation records** (`memo_relation` table) — both where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) and where it is the target (`RelatedMemoID`), via two `DeleteMemoRelation` calls.
2. **Attachments** linked to the memo — it lists attachments with `FindAttachment{MemoID: ...}` and deletes each via `DeleteAttachment`.

Only after that does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo` to remove the memo row.
