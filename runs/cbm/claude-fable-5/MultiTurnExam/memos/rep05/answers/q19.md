MemoService is served over two protocols:

1. **Connect (ConnectRPC)** — `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go`: `RegisterConnectHandlers` wraps `APIV1Service` in `ConnectServiceHandler` and registers `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler` (line 40) on an `http.ServeMux`.

2. **REST via gRPC-Gateway** — `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`: `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer` (line 108) registers the service on a grpc-gateway `runtime.NewServeMux`, which is mounted at `/api/v1/*` through Echo.

Both protocols share the same underlying implementation, `APIV1Service` (the Connect layer's per-RPC wrappers live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`).
