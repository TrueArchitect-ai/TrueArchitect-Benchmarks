**Engine:** CEL (Common Expression Language), parsed with `github.com/google/cel-go`, wrapped by the project's own filter engine in `internal/filter/` (entry point `filter.DefaultEngine()` in `internal/filter/engine.go`).

**Mechanism:** The engine runs a three-phase pipeline — the CEL expression is parsed with cel-go and validated against a memo-specific schema (`internal/filter/schema.go`, `internal/filter/parser.go`), normalized into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (`internal/filter/ir.go`), and then rendered into a SQL fragment plus placeholder arguments by `internal/filter/render.go`, which encapsulates per-dialect differences (JSON access, placeholders, `LIKE` vs `ILIKE`, boolean semantics) for `DialectSQLite`, `DialectMySQL`, and `DialectPostgres`. Each dialect driver invokes it when building the ListMemos query — e.g. `store/db/sqlite/memo.go:57-61` calls `filter.DefaultEngine()` and `filter.AppendConditions(..., filter.DialectSQLite, &where, &args)` to append the compiled WHERE conditions (likewise in `store/db/mysql/memo.go` and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`).
