**`memo_relation`**

This table links one memo to another via `memo_id` and `related_memo_id` columns (plus a relation `type`). It's defined in the schema migrations, e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql` and `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql` (also in the postgres and mysql `LATEST.sql` files), and accessed in Go via `store/memo_relation.go`.
