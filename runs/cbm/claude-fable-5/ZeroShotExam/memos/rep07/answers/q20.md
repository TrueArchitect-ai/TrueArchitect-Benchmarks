**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE). The hub is implemented at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (`SSEHub` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast` and visibility-based delivery filtering), with the HTTP endpoint in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` and event-building helpers in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.

**Memo handlers that broadcast through it:**
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (emits `memo.created`)
- `UpdateMemo` — via the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` at `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71` (emits `memo.updated`), called from `memo_service.go:513`
- `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (emits `memo.deleted`)
- `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (emits `memo.comment.created`)
- `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) also broadcast `memo.updated` indirectly through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`

(For completeness, the reaction handlers `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161` also broadcast reaction events through the same hub, though they are reaction rather than memo handlers.)
