**Mechanism; hub path; handler names:** Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment (plus SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction).

Details:

- **Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events. The endpoint `GET /api/v1/sse` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, streams `data:` frames, and sends `: heartbeat` comments every 30s (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`).
- **Hub:** `SSEHub` is implemented in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`, defining `SSEEvent` and event types like `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`. Clients subscribe via `hub.Subscribe(userID, role)` in the handler.
- **Broadcasting memo handlers** (all on `APIV1Service`):
  - `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163` (broadcasts `memo.created`)
  - `DeleteMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585` (`memo.deleted`)
  - `CreateMemoComment` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126` (`memo.comment.created`)
  - `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`), `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`), and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`) all broadcast `memo.updated` indirectly through the shared helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`.
  - Related: `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161` broadcast reaction events through the same hub.
