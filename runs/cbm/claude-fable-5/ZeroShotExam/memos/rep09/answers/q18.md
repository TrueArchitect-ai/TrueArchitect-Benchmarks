The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with its entry file:

- `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` — SQLite (`sqlite.NewDB`)
- `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — MySQL (`mysql.NewDB`)
- `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` — PostgreSQL (`postgres.NewDB`)

The factory that selects between them at startup is **`NewDBDriver`** — `store/db/db.go`. It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) and returns the matching driver, erroring on anything else.
