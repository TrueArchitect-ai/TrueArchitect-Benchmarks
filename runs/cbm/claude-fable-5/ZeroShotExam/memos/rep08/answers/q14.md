`ShortcutService` (defined in `proto/api/v1/shortcut_service.proto`) has five RPCs:

1. `ListShortcuts`
2. `GetShortcut`
3. `CreateShortcut`
4. `UpdateShortcut`
5. `DeleteShortcut`

All five are implemented on `APIV1Service` in **`server/router/api/v1/shortcut_service.go`** (at lines 49, 95, 135, 211, and 284 respectively). There are also thin Connect-RPC wrappers for these methods in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go`, which just delegate to the `APIV1Service` implementations.
