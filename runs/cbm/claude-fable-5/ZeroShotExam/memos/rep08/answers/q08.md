**Driver — store/driver.go**

The `Driver` interface, defined in `store/driver.go:10`, is the interface all database backends implement. The three backends — SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL — live in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, and `store/db/postgres`, and are instantiated via `store/db/db.go`. The interface covers connection management (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`) plus CRUD methods for all the store models (memos, attachments, users, inboxes, reactions, etc.).
