Server-Sent Events (SSE); hub at `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; broadcasting memo handlers: `CreateMemo`, `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163,585`), `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`), and `UpdateMemo` — plus `SetMemoRelations` and `SetMemoAttachments` — via the shared side-effect helper `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`).

Details:

- **Mechanism**: Server-Sent Events. The hub (`SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) manages subscribers and serializes `SSEEvent` payloads (typed `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted`); the HTTP endpoint that streams to browsers is in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, and `server/server.go` holds a reference and calls `sseHub.Close()` on shutdown since SSE requests are long-lived.
- Related but reaction-scoped: `UpsertMemoReaction` and `DeleteMemoReaction` in `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106,161` also broadcast memo-targeted events through the same hub.
