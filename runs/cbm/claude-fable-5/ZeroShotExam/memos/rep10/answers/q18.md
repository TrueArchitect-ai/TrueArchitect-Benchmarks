The `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) has three implementations, each with its entry file:

- SQLite — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` (constructor `sqlite.NewDB`)
- MySQL — `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` (constructor `mysql.NewDB`)
- PostgreSQL — `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` (constructor `postgres.NewDB`)

Factory: **NewDBDriver** — `store/db/db.go` (lines 14–32). It switches on `profile.Driver` (`"sqlite"` / `"mysql"` / `"postgres"`) at startup and returns the matching `store.Driver`, erroring on an unknown driver name.
