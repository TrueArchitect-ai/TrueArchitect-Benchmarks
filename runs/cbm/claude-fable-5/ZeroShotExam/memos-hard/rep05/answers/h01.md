- **Component:** the background runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` method pages through all attachments with storage type S3 and regenerates presigned GET URLs for their content, updating each attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`. This is what keeps links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from expiring/going stale.
- **Schedule:** a ticker firing every 12 hours (`const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-27`). It skips attachments whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiry, per the comment at `runner.go:86-88`).
