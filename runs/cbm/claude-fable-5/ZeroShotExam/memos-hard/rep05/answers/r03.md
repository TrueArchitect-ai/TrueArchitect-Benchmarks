The premise doesn't hold: there is no nightly database-cleanup job scheduled in this repository. The cron scheduler package exists at `internal/scheduler/`, but it is never imported or used by any production code — a repo-wide grep shows no imports of `internal/scheduler` outside the package itself.

The "cleanup" jobs you may have seen are purely illustrative examples in the package's own documentation, not registered jobs:

- `internal/scheduler/doc.go` (lines 14–21) shows a hypothetical `"daily-cleanup"` job with schedule `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily)
- `internal/scheduler/example_test.go` (around line 97) registers a `"cleanup"` example job at `"0 2 * * *"` inside a Go example test
- `internal/scheduler/README.md` has similar sample snippets

The actual background maintenance work in the server is done by runners started in `server/server.go` (lines 154–173), not by the cron scheduler: the S3 presign runner in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` runs on a plain `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (line 27), and there's also a `server/runner/memopayload` runner. Neither is a database-cleanup job with a cron schedule.

So: no nightly database-cleanup job exists; the 2 AM (`0 2 * * *`) time appears only in the scheduler package's example documentation.
