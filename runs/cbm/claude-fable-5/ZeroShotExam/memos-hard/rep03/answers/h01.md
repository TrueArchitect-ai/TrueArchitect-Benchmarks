**`s3presign` background runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — **every 12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), plus one immediate pass at startup — **wired in `startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:162-171`** (called from `Start` at `server/server.go:129`).

Details: attachments stored in S3 keep a presigned GET URL in their `Reference`. The runner's `CheckAndPresign` pages through S3-type attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose URL was last signed more than 4 days ago (URLs expire after 5 days), updating the attachment's reference and `LastPresignedTime` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`). At startup, `startBackgroundRunners` runs `RunOnce` synchronously once (`server/server.go:163`), then launches `Run` in a goroutine with a cancellable context tied into the server's shutdown wait group.
