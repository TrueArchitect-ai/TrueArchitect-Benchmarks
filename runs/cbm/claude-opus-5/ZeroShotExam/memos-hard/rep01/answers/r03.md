**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.** The cron scheduler exists, but nothing registers a cleanup job with it — the only "2 AM cleanup" you'll find is illustrative documentation, not a live schedule.

What's actually there:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a standalone, GitHub-Actions-inspired cron library (`scheduler.go`, `parser.go`, `job.go`, `middleware.go`). Nothing in `server/`, `store/`, or `cmd/` imports it — the only importers are its own `example_test.go` and `integration_test.go`.
- The `"0 2 * * *"` (2 AM daily) cleanup schedule appears solely in docs and examples: `internal/scheduler/doc.go:16`, `internal/scheduler/README.md:35`, and `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-100` (job named `cleanup`). `internal/scheduler/README.md:244` shows a different example, a `stale-session-cleanup` at `"0 3 * * *"`, and `README.md:268` even warns that 2:00 AM is "often overloaded." `internal/scheduler/parser_test.go:82` uses `"0 2 * * *"` purely as a parser test fixture.

The only real background job the server runs isn't cron-scheduled and isn't a cleanup: `server/runner/s3presign` re-signs S3 object URLs on a plain ticker every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), started from `server/server.go:153-173`.
