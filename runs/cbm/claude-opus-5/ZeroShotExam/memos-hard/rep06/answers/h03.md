**`internal/idp`; `internal/idp/oauth2`**

- **Abstraction package: `internal/idp`** — `internal/idp/idp.go` defines the provider-neutral contract, the `IdentityProviderUserInfo` struct (`Identifier`, `DisplayName`, `Email`, `AvatarURL`) that every provider normalizes its user data into.
- **Implementation package: `internal/idp/oauth2`** — `internal/idp/oauth2/oauth2.go` ("Package oauth2 implements the OAuth2 identity provider integration") provides the only concrete provider: `IdentityProvider` wrapping `storepb.OAuth2Config`, with `NewIdentityProvider`, `ExchangeToken` (including PKCE `code_verifier` support), and user-info fetching.

Supporting evidence that OAuth2 is the *only* shipped protocol:
- `proto/store/idp.proto:13` — the `IdentityProvider.Type` enum has just `TYPE_UNSPECIFIED = 0` and `OAUTH2 = 1`, and `IdentityProviderConfig` is a `oneof` with a single `oauth2_config` member. No SAML/OIDC/LDAP variants.
- `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go:12-13` is the sole consumer, importing both packages to resolve/link SSO users.
