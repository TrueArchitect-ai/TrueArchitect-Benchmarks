**delivery function — `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (`safeDialContext`) backed by `internal/webhook/validate.go`**

**Where delivery executes**

- `webhook.Post(*WebhookRequestPayload)` — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` — marshals the payload, optionally adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers (`webhook-id` / `webhook-timestamp` / `webhook-signature`), and issues the actual `POST` via `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`.
- Callers don't invoke `Post` directly: `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`), which is where `Post` actually runs. Content-change triggers are in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43,71` (memo created/updated/deleted and comment-created).

**What protects it from internal addresses**

Two layers, both keyed on `isReservedIP`:

1. **Dial-time block (the real enforcement).** The shared `safeClient`'s `Transport` uses `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`), which resolves the host itself and refuses to dial if any resolved IP is reserved. Because the check happens at dial time on the addresses actually being connected to, it also defeats DNS rebinding (TOCTOU between validation and request).
2. **Admission-time validation.** `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) requires an absolute `http`/`https` URL and rejects hostnames resolving to reserved ranges; it's called when webhooks are created/updated in `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74,161,182`.

The blocklist is `reservedCIDRs` in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23` — IPv4 loopback, all three RFC-1918 ranges, link-local `169.254.0.0/16` (cloud IMDS), plus IPv6 `::1/128`, `fc00::/7`, `fe80::/10` — parsed once at init.

One caveat worth flagging: `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP` and thus disables *both* layers, but nothing in the non-test codebase ever sets it — no flag, env var, or profile wiring exists (only `internal/webhook/webhook_test.go:90-92,141-143` toggle it). So today the protection is always on, and the documented "self-hosted local network" escape hatch is effectively unreachable.
