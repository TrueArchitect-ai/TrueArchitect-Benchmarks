**Premise correction: there is no nightly database-cleanup job in this repository.** The scheduler exists, but nothing registers a database-cleanup job with it — so there is no scheduled run time to report.

What's actually there:

- `internal/scheduler/` is a standalone, generic cron library (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`). It is **not wired into the application** — the only Go files importing `github.com/usememos/memos/internal/scheduler` are its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go:10`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go:13`). No production code under `server/`, `cmd/`, or `store/` registers any job.
- The "2 AM cleanup" you may be thinking of appears only in **documentation and example code**, and it's a generic placeholder, not a database job:
  - `internal/scheduler/doc.go:15-16` — `Name: "daily-cleanup", Schedule: "0 2 * * *", // 2 AM daily`
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:34-35` — same snippet in the quick-start
  - `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:97-100` — `Name: "cleanup", Schedule: "0 2 * * *"` with a handler that just prints "Cleaning up old data..."
  - `internal/scheduler/README.md:241-244` — a *best-practices* illustration of a `stale-session-cleanup` job at `0 3 * * *` (no such job exists in code)

The real periodic work in the app doesn't use cron expressions or nightly timing at all — it's interval-based tickers:

- `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-30` — every 12 hours, re-presigns S3 attachment links
- `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` — one-shot payload rebuild
- `store/cache/cache.go:47,60,201` — in-memory cache expiry sweep every 5 minutes (not a database cleanup)
