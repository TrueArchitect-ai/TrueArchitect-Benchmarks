**delivery function** — `webhook.Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (it marshals the payload, signs it, and calls `safeClient.Do`). It is normally reached asynchronously: `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues onto a bounded 128-slot channel drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39-55`). The callers are the memo dispatch helpers in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43,71`.

**protection mechanism** — two layers:

1. **Dial-time SSRF guard (the real enforcement):** `safeClient` uses a custom `Transport.DialContext` = `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`, `:59-77`). It resolves the host itself and refuses the connection if *any* returned address is reserved/private, so the check happens on the actual connection attempt — which is what defeats DNS rebinding (TOCTOU between validation and delivery). The range list and predicate live in `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-54` (`reservedCIDRs` / `isReservedIP`): IPv4 loopback, RFC-1918, link-local incl. 169.254.169.254 IMDS, plus IPv6 `::1/128`, `fc00::/7`, `fe80::/10`.

2. **Registration-time validation:** `webhook.ValidateURL` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) rejects non-http/https schemes and hostnames resolving to reserved IPs when a user creates/updates a webhook — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_webhooks.go:74,161,182`.

Both layers are bypassable by the operator: the `AllowPrivateIPs` global (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) short-circuits `isReservedIP`, and it's set from the `--allow-private-webhooks` flag in `cmd/memos/main.go:49,133,160` (default `false`) for self-hosted setups targeting LAN services.
