**Premise correction:** Neither. Memos has no external message broker at all — there is no NATS, RabbitMQ, Kafka, or Redis dependency anywhere in the project (`go.mod` contains no such client library; the only repo-wide match for "nats"/"amqp"/"rabbit" is an unrelated word in `web/src/locales/ca.json`). There is no broker connection, no exchange/queue declaration, and no separate worker process.

**Actual mechanism — an in-process Go channel with a fixed goroutine pool:**

- **Queue declaration:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` — `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, a package-level buffered channel holding at most 128 pending payloads. It lives entirely in the memos server process's memory.
- **"Workers":** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` — an `init()` function spawns exactly 4 goroutines, each ranging over `asyncPostQueue` and calling `Post(payload)`; failures are only logged via `slog.Warn`, never retried or dead-lettered.
- **Producer:** `PostAsync` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` does a non-blocking `select` send. If the buffer is full, the `default` branch **drops the webhook** and logs a warning — no backpressure, no persistence.
- **Callers:** `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`, invoked from the memo create/update/delete/comment paths.
- **Delivery itself:** `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180` does a plain `net/http` POST through `safeClient` (30s timeout, SSRF-guarded dialer at `webhook.go:59-77`), with optional Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signing.

Practical consequence of this design: because the queue is in-memory and unbounded-drop rather than durable, webhooks pending at process shutdown or during a burst past 128 queued items are lost silently (beyond a log line). The same pattern is used for email at `internal/email/email.go:14-19`.
