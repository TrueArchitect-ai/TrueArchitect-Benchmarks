## Concrete implementation

Exactly one: the unexported `service` struct (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:57-60`), wrapping a `goldmark.Markdown`. There are no mocks, fakes, or alternate implementations anywhere in the repo — tests use the real thing (`internal/markdown/markdown_test.go`, `server/router/rss/rss_test.go:54`, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver_test.go:427`).

Its behavior varies by construction options rather than by type: `WithTagExtension()` / `WithMentionExtension()` (`markdown.go:71-82`) add the custom `#tag` / `@mention` goldmark extenders on top of `extension.GFM` (`markdown.go:91-108`). Internally `RenderMarkdown` and `RenameTag` delegate to a separate concrete helper, `renderer.NewMarkdownRenderer()` (`internal/markdown/renderer/markdown_renderer.go:20`), which is not behind an interface.
