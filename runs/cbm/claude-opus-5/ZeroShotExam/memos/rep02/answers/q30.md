**Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks** — all four dispatch functions live in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`:

- `memos.memo.created`: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:15`, called from `memo_service.go:157` in `CreateMemo`)
- `memos.memo.updated`: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:20`, called from `memo_update_helpers.go:67`)
- `memos.memo.deleted`: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:25`, called from `memo_service.go:562` in `DeleteMemo`)
- `memos.memo.comment.created`: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:30`, called from `memo_service_comments.go:119`)

**Where they converge**

Two convergence layers:

1. The three plain lifecycle events (created/updated/deleted) are thin wrappers over the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook(ctx, memo, activityType)` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `s.Store.GetUserWebhooks`, and builds the payload with `convertMemoToWebhookPayload`. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` does *not* go through this helper — it targets the *related memo owner* (`relatedMemoCreatorID`) rather than the comment's own creator, so it inlines its own equivalent loop.

2. All four converge for actual delivery at `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), called at `memo_service_webhooks.go:43` and `:71`. `PostAsync` enqueues onto the buffered `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, dropping with a warning when full); four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) drain it and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which marshals the JSON body, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers when a signing secret is set, and POSTs via the SSRF-guarded shared `safeClient`.
