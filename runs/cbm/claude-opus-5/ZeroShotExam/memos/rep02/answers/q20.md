**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP — a `GET /api/v1/sse` endpoint that emits `text/event-stream` with 30s heartbeat pings, Bearer-token authenticated. The browser consumes it with `EventSource` in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, 32-event buffered per-client channel, drops events for slow clients). Constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`, route registered at `v1.go:125` via `RegisterSSERoutes` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25`, and closed on shutdown in `server/server.go:206`. Per-client visibility filtering lives in `SSEHub.Broadcast` / `SSEClient.canReceive` (`sse_hub.go:115`, `sse_hub.go:133`).
