**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subtrees:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versions 0.2 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — versions 0.17 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versions 0.19 through 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`

The whole tree is embedded by `store/migrator.go:44` (`//go:embed migration`), and the per-driver path convention is documented at `store/migrator.go:39` as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`. This matches the three driver implementations in `store/db/` (`sqlite`, `mysql`, `postgres`).
