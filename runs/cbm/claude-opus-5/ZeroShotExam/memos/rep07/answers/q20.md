**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, UpdateMemo, DeleteMemo, CreateMemoComment, SetMemoRelations, SetMemoAttachments, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction**

**Mechanism** — SSE over a plain HTTP GET, not WebSockets. `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25` registers `GET /api/v1/sse` on the gateway group (wired at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`). The handler authenticates a Bearer token, sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream` plus `Cache-Control: no-cache` and `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `: connected`, then loops writing `data: %s\n\n` frames with a 30s `: heartbeat` comment (`sse_handler.go:15`, `:48-107`). The browser side is `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`.

**Hub** — `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`: a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Each client gets a 32-event buffered channel; a full buffer drops the event rather than blocking the broadcaster (`sse_hub.go:115-132`). `canReceive` (`:134`) filters private memos to the creator or an admin. It's constructed at `v1.go:57` and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205`.
