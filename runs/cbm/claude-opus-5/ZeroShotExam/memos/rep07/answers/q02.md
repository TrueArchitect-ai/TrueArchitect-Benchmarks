**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs (`0.17` … `0.30`) plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

`store/migrator.go:39` documents the layout as `store/migration/{driver}/{version}/NN__description.sql`, and `store/migrator.go:44` embeds the whole `migration` tree via `//go:embed`. The matching driver implementations are in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`.
