**Convergence for delivery**

The first three funnel through the shared helper `(*APIV1Service).dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator and loops over `Store.GetUserWebhooks`. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` skips that helper (it targets the *related* memo's owner, not the comment's creator) and does its own webhook lookup/loop.

All four then converge on `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues the payload onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, dropped with a warning if full). Four worker goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) drain the queue and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the single delivery function that marshals the payload, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC headers when a signing secret is set, and POSTs via the SSRF-guarded `safeClient`.
