**SQLite, MySQL, PostgreSQL**

Migrations live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versioned dirs `0.2` through `0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/` — `0.17` through `0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — `0.19` through `0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`

The same three dialects have corresponding driver packages at `store/db/sqlite/`, `store/db/mysql/`, and `store/db/postgres/`. Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is SQLite-only.
