**Server-Sent Events (SSE); `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; `CreateMemo`, `UpdateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `SetMemoAttachments`, `SetMemoRelations`, `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`**

**Mechanism** — plain HTTP SSE, not WebSockets. `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48-52` sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, writes `data: <json>\n\n` frames, and sends a `: heartbeat` comment every 30s (`sseHeartbeatInterval`, line 17). The endpoint is `GET /api/v1/sse` (line 27), authenticated by Bearer token in the `Authorization` header (lines 36-44) — which is why the browser side uses `fetch` with `Accept: text/event-stream` rather than `EventSource` (`web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:205`).

**Hub** — `SSEHub` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:56`: a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}` with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Each client gets a 32-buffered channel and events are dropped rather than blocking on a slow consumer (lines 126-130). `canReceive` (line 134) filters delivery: `Private` memos only reach the creator or an admin. Wired up in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` (`NewSSEHub()`) and `:125` (`RegisterSSERoutes`), and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205-206`. Event types and payload shape are at `sse_hub.go:14-34`.

The three update-path handlers don't call `Broadcast` directly — they funnel through `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`memo_service.go:513`, `memo_attachment_service.go:48`, `memo_relation_service.go:48`), which also fires the webhook.
