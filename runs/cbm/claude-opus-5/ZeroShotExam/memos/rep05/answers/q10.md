`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`

On a fresh install, `preMigrate` in `store/migrator.go:210-235` detects an uninitialized database and executes the single file at `getMigrationBasePath() + LatestSchemaFileName`, where `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` (`store/migrator.go:56`) and the base path resolves to `migration/sqlite/` for the SQLite driver. The header comment at `store/migrator.go:42` states it directly: "LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)".

The versioned files under `store/migration/sqlite/0.x/` are incremental upgrade steps for existing databases and are not applied to a fresh install; `store/seed/sqlite/01__dump.sql` is demo seed data, not schema.
