Notes on how these break down:

- `store/driver.go:29-32` is the interface contract (`CreateMemo`/`ListMemos` take/return `*Memo`), so all three drivers under `store/db/` must change together — they do the column scanning and INSERT/UPDATE field lists.
- If a *field* is added/renamed (not just a method), the SQL schema is coupled too: `store/migration/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/LATEST.sql` plus a new versioned migration directory, and `store/seed/sqlite/01__dump.sql`.
- The heaviest consumer is `server/router/api/v1/` — `memo_service_converter.go` is the store→proto boundary and is where most field-level breakage will surface.
- `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go`, `memo_service_comments.go`, `attachment_service.go`, `fileserver/fileserver.go`, and `frontend/frontend.go` don't name `store.Memo` explicitly — they get it back from `ListMemos`/`GetMemo` and read fields (`memo.Visibility`, `memo.CreatorID`, `memo.CreatedTs`, …), so a grep for the literal type name misses them.
- `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/user_delete.go` reads `memo.ID`/`memo.UID` but on a local `deleteUserMemoRef` struct, not `store.Memo` — not affected.
