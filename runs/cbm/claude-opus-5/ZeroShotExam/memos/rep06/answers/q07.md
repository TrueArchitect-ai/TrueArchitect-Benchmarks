**`web/src/connect.ts`**

This is the single file where all ConnectRPC service clients are created. It:

- Builds the transport with `createConnectTransport` and an auth interceptor (`web/src/connect.ts:184-189`), plus a separate `refreshTransport` without the interceptor to avoid recursion (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`).
- Exports the clients via `createClient(...)` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`.
