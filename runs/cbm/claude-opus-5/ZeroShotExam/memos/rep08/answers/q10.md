**`store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`**

For a fresh (uninitialized) SQLite database, `store/migrator.go` builds the path from the driver-specific migration base path plus `LatestSchemaFileName = "LATEST.sql"` (`store/migrator.go:56`, `:218-219`) and applies that single embedded file rather than replaying the incremental versioned migrations under `store/migration/sqlite/0.x/`. The comment at `store/migrator.go:42` states it directly: "LATEST.sql: Full schema for new installations (faster than incremental migrations)", and `store/test/migrator_test.go:18` (`TestFreshInstall`) verifies it applies cleanly on a fresh DB.
