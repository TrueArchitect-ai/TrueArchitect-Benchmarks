`web/src/connect.ts`

This is the single file where all ConnectRPC service clients are created (`/bench/repo/web/src/connect.ts`):

- It builds the transport with `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`), pointing at `window.location.origin`, binary format, credentialed fetch, and an auth interceptor.
- It then exports the clients via `createClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient`.
- It also creates a separate interceptor-free transport and `refreshAuthClient` (`web/src/connect.ts:65-72`) used solely for token refresh, to avoid recursion through the auth interceptor.
