**Convergence:**

1. The first three funnel through the shared helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their webhooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, builds a payload with `convertMemoToWebhookPayload`, and sets `ActivityType`/`URL`/`SigningSecret`. 2. All four then call `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which enqueues onto the bounded `asyncPostQueue` (cap 128, drops with a warning when full).
3. Four background workers started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) drain that queue and call `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`) — the single delivery point that marshals JSON, adds Standard Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 headers (`webhook-id`/`webhook-timestamp`/`webhook-signature`) when a signing secret is set, and POSTs via `safeClient`, whose `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`) blocks reserved/private IPs for SSRF protection.
