**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:
- `store/migration/mysql/` (versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/`
- `store/migration/sqlite/`

These are embedded into the binary via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44-45`, and the corresponding drivers live in `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.
