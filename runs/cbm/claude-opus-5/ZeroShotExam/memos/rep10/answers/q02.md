**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `/bench/repo/store/migration/`, with one subdirectory per dialect:

- `store/migration/mysql/` — versioned dirs `0.17`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/postgres/` — versioned dirs `0.19`–`0.30` plus `LATEST.sql`
- `store/migration/sqlite/` — versioned dirs starting at `0.10` plus `LATEST.sql`

These match the driver implementations in `store/db/mysql`, `store/db/postgres`, and `store/db/sqlite`. Note that seed data (`store/seed/`) is shipped for SQLite only.
