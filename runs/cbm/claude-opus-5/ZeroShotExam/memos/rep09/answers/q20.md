**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over a plain HTTP `GET /api/v1/sse` stream (`Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`), with `: heartbeat` comments every 30s and Bearer-token auth on the request. The browser side consumes it via `fetch("/api/v1/sse")` with reconnect backoff in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts:203`, invalidating react-query caches on each event.

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, 32-buffered per-client channel, drops events for slow clients) with `NewSSEHub`/`Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`. Constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57`, wired to the HTTP route by `RegisterSSERoutes`/`handleSSE` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, and closed on shutdown at `server/server.go:205`. Per-client filtering is by visibility/creator (`canReceive`, `sse_hub.go:135`); event payload helpers live in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
