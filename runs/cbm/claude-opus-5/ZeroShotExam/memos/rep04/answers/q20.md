**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`, with `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast`/`Close`; 32-event per-client buffer, drops on overflow; `canReceive` filters private memos to the creator or admins). It's constructed in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:57` and its route registered at `v1.go:125`.
