Interceptor: `authInterceptor` in `web/src/connect.ts:150-176` (installed on the transport at `:182`). Tokens: a JWT **access token** kept in `localStorage`/memory and sent as `Authorization: Bearer` (`web/src/auth-state.ts`), and an HttpOnly **refresh token** cookie sent via `credentials: "include"` and exchanged through `AuthService.RefreshToken` (`web/src/connect.ts:68-80`); the backend also accepts **personal access tokens** on the same header. Backend validation package: `server/auth` (`authenticator.go`, `token.go`, `extract.go`), driven by `Authorizer` in `server/router/api/v1/authz.go` for both the gateway middleware and the Connect auth interceptor.
