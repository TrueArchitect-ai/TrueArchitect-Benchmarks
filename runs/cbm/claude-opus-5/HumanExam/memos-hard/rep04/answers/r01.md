The cache layer is a **process-local in-memory cache**: `store/cache/cache.go` (`Cache` struct backed by `sync.Map`, `:65-72`; `New` at `:74`; background eviction goroutine and `Close` at `:187`). `Store` holds three such caches — `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (`store/store.go:26-29`), all created in `store.New` with the same config: **DefaultTTL = 10 minutes**, CleanupInterval 5 minutes, MaxItems 1000 (`store/store.go:38-43`; identical defaults in `cache.DefaultConfig`, `store/cache/cache.go:56-63`). So user entries expire after 10 minutes, and are also written/invalidated explicitly on `CreateUser`/`UpdateUser`/`ListUsers`/`GetUser` (`store/user.go:96,131,142,155`) and on delete via `deleteUserCache` (`store/user_delete.go:38-47`).
