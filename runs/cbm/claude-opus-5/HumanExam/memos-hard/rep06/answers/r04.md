— Premise is wrong: **there is no message broker — neither NATS nor RabbitMQ appears in the codebase, and no queue is declared in any broker.** Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: a package-level **buffered Go channel** `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`) with **4 worker goroutines** started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `Post` (`:46`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`:184`), which does a non-blocking send and **drops** the payload with a warning when the buffer is full (`:189-195`). Consequences worth noting: the queue is unpersisted, so pending deliveries are lost on restart, and there are no retries.
