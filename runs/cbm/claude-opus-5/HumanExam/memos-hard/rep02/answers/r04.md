— Premise is wrong on both counts: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used, and there is no external broker or declared queue anywhere (`go.mod` contains no `nats`, `amqp`, `rabbitmq`, or `kafka` dependency). Webhook delivery is entirely in-process: an unbuffered-overflow-dropping Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`), drained by 4 goroutines started in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`), each calling `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`). Producers call `webhook.PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`), which drops the payload with a warning when the channel is full (`:189-195`). There is no persistence, no retry, and no redelivery — an in-flight webhook is lost on restart.
