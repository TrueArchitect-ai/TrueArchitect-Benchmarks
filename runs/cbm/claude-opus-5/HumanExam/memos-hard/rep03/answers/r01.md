Three instances are created in `store.New`: `instanceSettingCache`, `userCache`, `userSettingCache` (`store/store.go:47-52`). User entries are written by `Store.CreateUser`/`UpdateUser`/`ListUsers`/`GetUser` (`store/user.go:91,132,142,152,166`) and invalidated on delete (`store/user_delete.go:40`).
