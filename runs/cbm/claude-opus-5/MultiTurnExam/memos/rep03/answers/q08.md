**Driver — /bench/repo/store/driver.go**

`store.Driver` is declared at `store/driver.go:10`. It bundles connection/lifecycle methods (`GetDB`, `Close`, `IsInitialized`, `GetDatabaseSize`) plus the full set of per-model CRUD methods (attachment, memo, user, etc.). The three backends each implement it with a `DB` struct — `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` — and the concrete one is picked by DSN driver in `store/db/db.go`.
