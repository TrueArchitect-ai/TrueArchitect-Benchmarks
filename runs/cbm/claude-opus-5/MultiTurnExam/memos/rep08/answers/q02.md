**sqlite, postgres, mysql**

The migrations live under `store/migration/`, which contains exactly three dialect subdirectories: `store/migration/sqlite/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/mysql/` (each with per-version folders plus a `LATEST.sql`).

These are embedded and selected at runtime in `store/migrator.go`: `//go:embed migration` (line 44) and `getMigrationBasePath()` at line 254-255, which builds the path as `migration/<profile.Driver>/`.
