**Runners actually started by the server** (`Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:153-174`):

```
s3presign.Runner — RunOnce immediately at startup, then a 12-hour time.Ticker loop until its context is cancelled
```

`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`; the synchronous first pass at `:163`; the goroutine running `Run(s3Context)` at `:166-171`. SSE heartbeat — per-connection time.Ticker, one per subscribed browser (server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64)
```
Cache janitor — per-Cache time.Ticker on CleanupInterval, started when a cache is constructed (store/cache/cache.go:200-215)
```
