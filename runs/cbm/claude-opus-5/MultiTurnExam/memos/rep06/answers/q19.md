REST / HTTP+JSON via grpc-gateway     — server/router/api/v1/v1.go

- **ConnectRPC:** `RegisterConnectHandlers` mounts `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` on the mux at `server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`. The `s` there is `ConnectServiceHandler` (`connect_handler.go:21`), whose per-RPC wrappers unwrap `connect.Request`/`Response` and delegate to `APIV1Service` — the MemoService block starts at `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go:304`.

- **REST gateway:** `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108` registers the generated gateway against `APIV1Service` directly (in-process, no gRPC dial); the mux is served at `/api/v1/*` via Echo at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:123-129`. The URL paths come from the `google.api.http` options in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.

Both protocols share one implementation: `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`. (`APIV1Service` also embeds `v1pb.UnimplementedMemoServiceServer` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:26`, so it satisfies the generated gRPC server interface, but the registration points actually wired up are the two above.)
