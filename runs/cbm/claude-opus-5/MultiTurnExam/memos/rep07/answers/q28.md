**Started by the server**

```
s3presign.Runner — one immediate RunOnce at startup, then a 12-hour time.Ticker loop
internal/webhook async dispatch workers — 4 goroutines draining a buffered channel (event-driven, not scheduled)
apiv1.SSEHub — long-lived goroutine per subscribed browser client (event-driven)
```

- **s3presign** — `Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:150-171`) constructs `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously, then launches `Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine under a `WaitGroup` with its own cancel func for shutdown. `Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:29`) is a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` loop with `runnerInterval = 12 * time.Hour` (`:26`), refreshing expiring S3 presigned URLs.
- **webhook workers** — started by a package `init()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`: 4 goroutines consuming `asyncPostQueue` (capacity 128). They're triggered by `webhook.PostAsync` calls from the memo handlers, not by a timer, and are not registered with the server's shutdown `WaitGroup`.
- **SSE hub** — created with the API service in `server/server.go:76` and closed on shutdown via `closeLongLivedConnections` (`server/server.go:196-201`); it fans out per-client goroutines in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`, driven by handler broadcasts rather than a schedule.

**Unused infrastructure**

`server/runner/memopayload` ships a full `Runner` (`NewRunner` at `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:19`, `RunOnce` at `:27`) that rebuilds memo payloads across the whole table, but **nothing constructs or starts it** — `startBackgroundRunners` only wires s3presign, and the package isn't imported by `server/server.go`. The only live use of that package is the standalone helper `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload`, called inline from the memo handlers at `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` (create) and `:447` (update).

There is also no cron/scheduler dependency in `go.mod` — all periodic work is plain `time.Ticker`.
