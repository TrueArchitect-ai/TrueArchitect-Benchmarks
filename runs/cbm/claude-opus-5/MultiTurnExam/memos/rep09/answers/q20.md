**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP — a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse` stream, Bearer-token authenticated, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` / `handleSSE` in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25,34` and mounted alongside the rest of `/api/v1` in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`.

**Hub:** `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` (line 54) holds a mutex-guarded `map[*SSEClient]struct{}`; `Broadcast` is at line 115. Each `SSEClient` carries `userID` and `role` so the hub can filter delivery by the event's `Visibility`/`CreatorID` (which are `json:"-"` and never sent to the browser). Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (lines 15-20). Helpers for building events live in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
