**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) — `text/event-stream` over a long-lived `GET /api/v1/sse`, registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:25-27` (mounted from `v1.go:125`). Not WebSockets.

**Hub:** `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` with `NewSSEHub` (63), `Subscribe` (71), `Unsubscribe` (89), `Close` (99), `Broadcast` (115), and per-client visibility filtering in `canReceive` (134). Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (lines 15-20). Helpers for building events are in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
