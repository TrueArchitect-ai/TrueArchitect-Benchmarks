All eight clients are created there (`web/src/connect.ts:192-203`) — `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, `identityProviderServiceClient` — over a shared `createConnectTransport` (`web/src/connect.ts:184`) that uses the binary format and an auth interceptor for bearer tokens plus 401 refresh-and-retry.
