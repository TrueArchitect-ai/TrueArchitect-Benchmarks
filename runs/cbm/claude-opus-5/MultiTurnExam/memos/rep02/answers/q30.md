**Convergence:** `webhook.PostAsync` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` → the 128-slot `asyncPostQueue` drained by 4 worker goroutines started in that package's `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`) → `webhook.Post` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:120`), which does the signed HTTP POST.

Two structural details:

- The first three share the helper `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo's creator and loads *that user's* webhooks. `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` is separate precisely because it must notify the **parent** memo's owner (`GetUserWebhooks(ctx, relatedMemoCreatorID)`, L31), not the comment author.
- `memos.memo.updated` fires from a shared side-effect helper, so it's also emitted by `SetMemoRelations` (`memo_relation_service.go:48`) and `SetMemoAttachments` (`memo_attachment_service.go:48`), not just `UpdateMemo` (`memo_service.go:513`).

Every call site logs a warning on error rather than failing the RPC, and delivery is fire-and-forget — `PostAsync` drops the payload with a warning if the queue is full.
