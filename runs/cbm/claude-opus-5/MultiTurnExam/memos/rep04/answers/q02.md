**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (each holding versioned `NN__description.sql` files plus a `LATEST.sql`). The whole tree is embedded in `store/migrator.go:44-45` and selected at runtime by driver name (`store/migrator.go:255`). Matching driver implementations exist at `store/db/mysql/`, `store/db/postgres/`, and `store/db/sqlite/`.

Note that seed data is SQLite-only (`store/seed/sqlite/`).
