**Runners the server actually starts** (all wired from `Server.startBackgroundRunners`, `server/server.go:153-174`, unless noted):

s3presign runner — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`: one immediate `RunOnce` at startup (`server/server.go:163`), then a `time.Ticker` loop every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-41`); stops on its own cancellable context.
Webhook async dispatch workers — `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-54`: 4 goroutines started by package `init()`, event-driven off a buffered channel (`asyncPostQueue`, capacity 128) fed by `PostAsync`; not ticker-based.
Email async dispatch workers — `internal/email/email.go:16-28`: 2 goroutines started by package `init()`, consuming the `asyncEmailQueue` channel (capacity 128).
In-memory cache janitor — `store/cache/cache.go:83` (`go c.cleanupLoop()`, loop at line 200): started per `Cache` instance when the store builds its caches, ticking every 5 minutes (`store/store.go:42`).
SSE heartbeat — `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:64`: a per-connection ticker inside `handleSSE`, not a global runner; the hub itself (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`) is closed at shutdown via `closeLongLivedConnections` (`server/server.go:203`).
PAT last-used updates — `server/auth/authenticator.go:180`: a fire-and-forget goroutine per authenticated request, not a scheduled worker.

**Unused infrastructure:** `internal/scheduler/` — a full cron scheduler (5/6-field cron parsing, timezone handling, middleware, graceful shutdown; `scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, plus a README) is present but **not referenced anywhere outside its own package and tests** — nothing in `server/` or `cmd/` imports it, so no jobs are registered or run.

Also note `server/runner/memopayload/` is not a background runner despite its location: `RebuildMemoPayload` is called synchronously from the memo handlers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108,447`).
