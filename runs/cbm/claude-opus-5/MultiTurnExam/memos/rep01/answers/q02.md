**MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite**

Migrations live under `store/migration/`, with one directory per dialect: `store/migration/mysql/`, `store/migration/postgres/`, and `store/migration/sqlite/` (29, 23, and 58 `.sql` files respectively). The whole tree is embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`, and the same three dialects have driver implementations under `store/db/` (`mysql`, `postgres`, `sqlite`).
