**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE) over HTTP — `Content-Type: text/event-stream` (`server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go:48`), with the endpoint registered by `RegisterSSERoutes` (`sse_handler.go:25`, wired into the `/api/v1` group at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:125`) and served by `handleSSE` (`sse_handler.go:34`).

**Hub:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` — `SSEHub` struct (line 56), `NewSSEHub` (63), `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe` (71, 89), `Broadcast` (115), and per-client visibility filtering in `SSEClient.canReceive` (134). Event types are `memo.created`, `memo.updated`, `memo.deleted`, `memo.comment.created`, `reaction.upserted`, `reaction.deleted` (lines 15-20). Payload helpers are in `server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go`.
