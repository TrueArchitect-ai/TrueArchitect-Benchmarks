**Convergence:** created/updated/deleted all funnel through the shared `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook` (`memo_service_webhooks.go:48`), which resolves the memo creator, loads their hooks via `Store.GetUserWebhooks`, and calls `webhook.PostAsync` per hook (line 71). `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook` skips that helper — it targets the *parent* memo's owner rather than the comment's creator — but converges at the same `webhook.PostAsync` (line 44).

From there everything lands in `internal/webhook`: `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) enqueues onto a 128-slot channel drained by 4 worker goroutines started in `init()` (line 42), each calling `Post` (line 120) to send the signed JSON POST over the SSRF-hardened `safeClient`.
