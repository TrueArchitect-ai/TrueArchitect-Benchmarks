`Server.startBackgroundRunners` (`server/server.go:153`) starts exactly **one** long-lived runner: it calls `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by a `WaitGroup` with its own cancel func for shutdown. `Run` ticks every `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-30`) and re-presigns S3 attachment URLs older than 4 days.

Fire-and-forget goroutines (not scheduled workers): webhook delivery `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:44`), async email send (`internal/email/email.go:18`), and PAT last-used recording (`server/auth/authenticator.go:180`).

**Unused infrastructure — two items:**

1. **`internal/scheduler`** — a full cron-style scheduler package (`scheduler.go`, `job.go`, `parser.go`, `middleware.go`, plus a README and docs) is **not referenced anywhere in production code**. The only importers are its own `example_test.go` and `integration_test.go`.
2. **`server/runner/memopayload`** — despite living under `server/runner/`, it is not a runner at all: `RebuildMemoPayload` is called inline from the API handlers (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` and `:447`), never started as a background loop.
