**sqlite, mysql, postgres**

The project ships migrations for three SQL dialects, one directory each under `store/migration/`:

- `store/migration/sqlite/` (versions 0.10 → 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (0.17 → 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (0.19 → 0.30 plus `LATEST.sql`)

These are embedded via `//go:embed migration` in `store/migrator.go:44`, and the same three dialects have driver implementations in `store/db/sqlite`, `store/db/mysql`, and `store/db/postgres`.
