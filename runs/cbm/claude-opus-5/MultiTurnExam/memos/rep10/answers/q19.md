- **REST / gRPC-Gateway:** `RegisterGateway` (`server/router/api/v1/v1.go:65`) builds a `runtime.NewServeMux` with an auth middleware and calls `v1pb.RegisterMemoServiceHandlerServer(ctx, gwMux, s)` at `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:108`, mounting it on Echo under `/api/v1/*` and `/file/*` (lines 128-129). Paths come from the `google.api.http` options in `proto/api/v1/memo_service.proto`.
- **ConnectRPC:** the same `RegisterGateway` then builds a Connect mux (`v1.go:139-144`); registration happens in `ConnectServiceHandler.RegisterConnectHandlers` via `apiv1connect.NewMemoServiceHandler(s, opts...)` (`server/router/api/v1/connect_handler.go:40`), mounted on Echo at `/memos.api.v1.*`. The per-RPC wrappers that delegate to `APIV1Service` live in `server/router/api/v1/connect_services.go` (e.g. `ConnectServiceHandler.CreateMemo` at line 307).

Both protocols share one implementation — `ConnectServiceHandler` embeds `*APIV1Service` (`connect_handler.go:21-23`), so `APIV1Service.ListMemos`/`CreateMemo` etc. in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` back both.
