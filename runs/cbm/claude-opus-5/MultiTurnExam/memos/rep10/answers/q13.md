Notes on where the risk concentrates: the three dialect files (`store/db/*/memo.go`) do the actual column scanning/insertion, so any field add/remove/rename breaks there first; `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_converter.go` maps `store.Memo` to the proto `Memo`; and `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go` rebuilds the memo payload. `store/driver.go` isn't a "reference" so much as the interface whose `CreateMemo`/`ListMemos`/`UpdateMemo` signatures carry the type. If the change touches persisted columns rather than just in-memory fields, add a migration under `store/migration/{sqlite,mysql,postgres}/` plus the three `LATEST.sql` files.
