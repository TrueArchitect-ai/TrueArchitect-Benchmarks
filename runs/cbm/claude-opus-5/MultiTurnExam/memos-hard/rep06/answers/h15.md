Two structural notes. The gates split into two idioms: *post-fetch checks* on a single memo (`checkMemoReadAccess`, the reaction/comment/attachment checks) versus *pre-query scoping* that never loads invisible rows (`ListMemos`, the stats endpoints, RSS, sitemap) — the latter are enforcement too, since they're the only thing keeping non-public memos out of those responses. Separately, `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:686-691` is the one place visibility can be satisfied by a share token instead of identity.

Not enforcement sites, though they read `Visibility`: the notification-suppression logic (`server/notification/email.go:313-316`, `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:309-312`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62-84`) decides whether to *send* a notification rather than whether a viewer may read a memo. MCP adds no separate check — `server/router/mcp/catalog.go:12-14` merely whitelists the existing `MemoService_ListMemos`/`GetMemo` methods, so it inherits the v1 gates above.
