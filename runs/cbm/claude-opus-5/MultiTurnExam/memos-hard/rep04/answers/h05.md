What I checked:

**1. The delete path.** `store.DeleteUser` (`store/user.go:171`) delegates straight to `s.driver.DeleteUser`. Each driver implements it in its own `user_delete.go`: `store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:29`, `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:29`, `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:29`. **2. Reaction deletion is explicit, and happens twice for different reasons** (`store/db/sqlite/user_delete.go:92-127`):

- `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx` (line 96, defined at line 380) issues `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)` over the deleted user's whole memo tree — this removes *other* users' reactions on the departing user's memos. Content IDs are built as `"memos/"+memo.UID` (`memoContentIDsFromRefs`, line 512), matching how `reaction.content_id` is stored as a resource name.
- `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx` (line 102, defined at line 400) issues `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?` — the deleted user's own reactions, wherever they were left.

Both are batched at 500 rows (`deleteUserBatchSize`, line 14) where they take an `IN` list. The same two calls exist in the other drivers at `store/db/postgres/user_delete.go:96,102` (definitions at `:380`, `:400`) and `store/db/mysql/user_delete.go:96,102` (definitions at `:417`, `:437`).
