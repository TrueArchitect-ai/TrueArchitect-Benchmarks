**Premise correction: there is no per-user API rate-limiting middleware in this repository.** Nothing registers one, and there are no request limits to report.

Evidence:

- A case-insensitive grep across all `.go` files for `ratelimit`, `rate_limit`, `rate limit`, `throttl`, `limiter`, and `golang.org/x/time/rate` returns exactly one hit — a comment in `internal/email/doc.go:86` listing "Rate limit email sending to prevent abuse" as a *future* consideration, not an implementation.
- `go.mod` declares no rate-limiting dependency (no `golang.org/x/time`, no `echo` rate-limiter module, no token-bucket library).
- The complete Echo middleware chain is two entries, registered at `server/server.go:51-52`: `middleware.Recover()` and `newCORSMiddleware(profile)` (defined in `server/cors.go:14`). The frontend adds only static-file and SPA-fallback middleware (`server/router/frontend/frontend.go:54,59`).
- The API layer's interceptors are authentication/authorization only — the gRPC-Gateway auth middleware and Connect interceptor built around `NewAuthorizer` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:66+`. Neither counts or throttles requests.

What does exist, and is easy to mistake for rate limiting, are **concurrency caps on CPU-heavy media work** — not per-user request quotas. They are global semaphores shared across all callers, they block rather than reject, and they are unrelated to identity:

| Guard | Limit | Registered | Acquired |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FileServerService.thumbnailSemaphore` | 3 concurrent | `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:116` (const `maxConcurrentThumbnails` at `:45`) | `fileserver.go:448` |
| `APIV1Service.thumbnailSemaphore` | 3 concurrent | `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:59` | — |
| `APIV1Service.imageProcessingSemaphore` | 2 concurrent | `server/router/api/v1/v1.go:60` | `attachment_service_image.go:71` |

Two other bounded-resource mechanisms, also not rate limits: the webhook dispatch queue drops events when its 128-slot channel is full (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:39,189-195`), and the SSE hub drops events for slow clients (`server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:126-130`).
