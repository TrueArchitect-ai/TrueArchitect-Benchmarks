**1st: `store.User` (~7 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~6 packages)**

Both are declared in the data layer — `store.User` in `store/user.go`, `store.Memo` in `store/memo.go` — alongside the other persistent entities (`Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `UserIdentity`, `UserSetting`, `InstanceSetting`, `IdentityProvider`).

Method: I listed the exported struct types declared in `store/*.go`, then counted the distinct package directories referencing each as `store.<Type>` while excluding everything under `store/` itself (the data layer, including the `store/db/*` drivers and `store/test` helpers). Word boundaries keep `store.User` from matching `store.UserSetting`/`store.UserIdentity`, and `store.Memo` from matching `store.MemoRelation`/`store.MemoShare`.
