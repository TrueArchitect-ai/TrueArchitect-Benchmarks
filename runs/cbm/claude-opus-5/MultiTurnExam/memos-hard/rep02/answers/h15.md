Notes that matter for reading these:

- **Two enforcement styles.** Single-memo handlers check an already-loaded memo and return `NotFound`/`Unauthenticated`/`PermissionDenied`; list handlers instead constrain the *query* (`memoFind.VisibilityList`, or a CEL filter `creator_id == N || visibility in ["PUBLIC","PROTECTED"]` at `memo_service.go:222`) so restricted rows are never fetched.
- **`checkMemoReadAccess` is the only shared helper**, called from `GetMemo` (`memo_service.go:356`), the parent-memo check (`:367`), and `ListMemoComments` (`memo_service_comments.go:148`). Every other site above re-implements the `PUBLIC` → `PRIVATE`-owner-or-admin ladder inline, which is why they count as separate enforcement sites rather than callers of one gate.
- **Share tokens are a deliberate bypass**, not a gap: `GetMemoByShare` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_share_service.go:151`) resolves a memo with no auth, and `checkAttachmentPermission` honors a matching non-expired `share_token` query param (`server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:683-689`).
- **Adjacent but not view-access enforcement** (excluded above): visibility-based redaction of memo snippets in notification/email payloads (`server/notification/email.go:313-316`, `server/router/api/v1/user_service_notifications.go:309-312`) and mention-notification targeting (`server/router/api/v1/memo_mention_helpers.go:62-84`).
- The MCP surface (`server/router/mcp/`) contains no visibility logic of its own — it proxies the v1 HTTP API and inherits its checks.
