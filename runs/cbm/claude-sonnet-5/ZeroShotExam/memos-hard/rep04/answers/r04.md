Premise correction: neither. This repo (usememos/memos) doesn't use NATS or RabbitMQ for webhook delivery at all — there's no message-broker dependency anywhere in the codebase (`go.mod`/`go.sum` and a full-repo grep for `nats`/`rabbitmq`/`amqp` turn up nothing).

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery is an in-process, in-memory Go channel worker pool, not a broker-backed queue.
- The "queue" is a buffered Go channel `asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)`, declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`.
- 4 goroutine workers are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` to actually deliver the HTTP request.
- Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking channel send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no external broker, persistence, or redelivery involved.
