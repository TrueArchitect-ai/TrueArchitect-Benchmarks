**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that lists S3-backed attachments and re-presigns their `GetObject` URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, storing the new URL and a `LastPresignedTime` back on the attachment (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27,30`). Within each run it skips any attachment whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days, refreshing URLs before their 5-day expiration (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:161-171` constructs the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately at `server/server.go:163`, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine at `server/server.go:169` with its own cancellable context for graceful shutdown.
