- Delivery function — `Post` (and its async wrapper `PostAsync`) in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (actual HTTP dispatch happens via `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`).
- Protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, which is wired as the `http.Transport.DialContext` for `safeClient` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:34-36`). On every dial it resolves the hostname and rejects any resolved IP for which `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`) returns true, blocking loopback, RFC-1918 private ranges, and link-local/cloud-metadata addresses (`169.254.0.0/16`) as well as their IPv6 equivalents (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`) — checking at dial time (not just at the earlier `ValidateURL` pre-check in `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`) is what defeats DNS-rebinding SSRF attempts. Note `AllowPrivateIPs` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`) can disable this protection for self-hosted deployments.
