Premise correction: neither NATS nor RabbitMQ is used — this repo (usememos/memos) has no message-broker dependency at all. `go.mod` contains no `nats-io` or `amqp`/RabbitMQ client library.

Actual mechanism: webhook delivery is handled by an in-process, in-memory Go channel, not an external broker. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `asyncPostQueue` is declared as `make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` — a buffered channel acting as the "queue." Its `init()` (webhook.go:42-55) spawns 4 goroutine workers that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post()` to deliver each webhook via HTTP. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync()` (webhook.go:184-196), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no persistence or external broker backing this queue.
