**Component:** `server/runner/s3presign.Runner` — a background runner that re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each pass only refreshes attachments whose presigned URL is within 4 days of the 5‑day S3 expiration (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`), i.e. it re-presigns roughly once per day for any given attachment before the link goes stale.

**Wiring into server startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` creates the runner, does an immediate `RunOnce`, then launches `Run` in a goroutine — `server/server.go:162-171`. `startBackgroundRunners` itself is invoked from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
