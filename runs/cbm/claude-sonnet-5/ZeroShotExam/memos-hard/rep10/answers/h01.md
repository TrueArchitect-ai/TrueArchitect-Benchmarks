**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background runner that re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each pass re-signs any S3 attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration), skipping ones still valid (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:161-171` constructs the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously once (`server/server.go:163`), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine (`server/server.go:169`) tracked via `s.backgroundRunnerWG`/`backgroundRunnerCancels` for graceful shutdown.
