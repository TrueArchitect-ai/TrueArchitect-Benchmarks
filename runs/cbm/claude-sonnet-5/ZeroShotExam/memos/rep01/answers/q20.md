Mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over an in-process pub/sub hub (`SSEHub` with per-client buffered channels), not WebSockets; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (registered/served via `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`, wired into `APIV1Service` in `server/router/api/v1/v1.go`); handlers that call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`: `CreateMemo` and `DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`), `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go`, invoked from `UpdateMemo` in `memo_service.go` and also from `memo_attachment_service.go` and `memo_relation_service.go`), `CreateMemoComment` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go`), and `UpsertMemoReaction`/`DeleteMemoReaction` (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go`).
