q29. The frontend auth interceptor lives at `web/src/connect.ts` (the `authInterceptor` Connect-RPC interceptor, backed by token storage/refresh helpers in `web/src/auth-state.ts`). It manages two token kinds: a short-lived JWT **access token** (attached as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, stored in-memory/localStorage, auto-refreshed on expiry or 401) and a **refresh token** (an HttpOnly cookie sent via `credentials: "include"`, used by `refreshToken()` to mint new access tokens). On the backend, these are validated by the `server/auth` package (`server/auth/authenticator.go`, along with `token.go`, `extract.go`, `context.go`), whose `Authenticator` validates JWT access tokens (`AuthenticateByAccessTokenV2`), refresh tokens (`AuthenticateByRefreshToken`), and Personal Access Tokens (`AuthenticateByPAT`), and is invoked from the Connect/gRPC interceptors in `server/router/api/v1/connect_interceptors.go`.
