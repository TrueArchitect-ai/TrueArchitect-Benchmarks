q20. mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE) over a long-lived HTTP stream at `GET /api/v1/sse` (client side consumes it in `web/src/hooks/useLiveMemoRefresh.ts`); hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (the `SSEHub` type, registered/served via `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`); handler names that broadcast through it (all in `server/router/api/v1/`):
- `CreateMemo` (memo_service.go) — broadcasts `memo.created`
- `DeleteMemo` (memo_service.go) — broadcasts `memo.deleted`
- `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (memo_update_helpers.go) — broadcasts `memo.updated`; called from `UpdateMemo` (memo_service.go), and from the attachment (`memo_attachment_service.go`) and relation (`memo_relation_service.go`) update handlers
- `CreateMemoComment` (memo_service_comments.go) — broadcasts `memo.comment.created`
- `UpsertMemoReaction` (reaction_service.go) — broadcasts `reaction.upserted`
- `DeleteMemoReaction` (reaction_service.go) — broadcasts `reaction.deleted`
