Before deleting the memo row, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140`) deletes, in this order:
- **`memo_relation` records** where the memo is the source (`DeleteMemoRelation{MemoID: &delete.ID}`) — `store/memo.go:142`
- **`memo_relation` records** where the memo is the target/related memo (`DeleteMemoRelation{RelatedMemoID: &delete.ID}`) — `store/memo.go:145`
- **Attachments** linked to the memo — fetched via `ListAttachments` and deleted one-by-one with `DeleteAttachment` — `store/memo.go:149-157`

Only after these are removed does it call `s.driver.DeleteMemo(ctx, delete)` (`store/memo.go:158`) to delete the memo row itself.
