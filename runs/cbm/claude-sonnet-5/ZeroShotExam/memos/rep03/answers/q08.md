`Driver` — `store/driver.go` (defined at store/driver.go:10)

This interface declares all the methods a database backend must implement (attachments, memos, users, settings, identity providers, etc.). It's implemented by the SQLite, MySQL, and Postgres backends, per their `var _ store.Driver = (*DB)(nil)`-style usage in `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`, `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`, and `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`, and selected/constructed in `store/db/db.go`.
