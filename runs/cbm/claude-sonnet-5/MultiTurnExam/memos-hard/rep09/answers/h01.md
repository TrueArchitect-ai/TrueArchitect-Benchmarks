**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background goroutine that re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs before their AWS presigned-URL expiration.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`), then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Within each run, it skips any attachment whose `LastPresignedTime` is less than 4 days old (presigned URLs are valid 5 days), re-signing only those close to expiry (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring into startup:** `server/server.go:153-174`, in `startBackgroundRunners` — it constructs the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` at `server/server.go:162`), calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately (`server/server.go:163`), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine (`server/server.go:167-171`) using a cancellable context tracked for graceful shutdown.
