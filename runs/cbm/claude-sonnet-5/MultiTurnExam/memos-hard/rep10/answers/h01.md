**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs. It lists attachments with `StorageType == S3` and, for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs expire after 5 days), generates a fresh presigned GET URL via `s3.NewClient(...).PresignGetObject` and writes it back to the attachment's `Reference`/`Payload` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-131`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately on startup, then on a `time.NewTicker(runnerInterval)` where `runnerInterval = 12 * time.Hour` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:26-41`).

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `Server.startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` runs once synchronously (`server.go:163`) then is launched as a goroutine calling `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` (`server.go:169`), which itself is invoked from `Server` startup (`startBackgroundRunners` is called during server start).
