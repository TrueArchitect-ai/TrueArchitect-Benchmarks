**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-process Go channel, not an external broker. In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`, `asyncPostQueue := make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)` is a buffered channel (capacity 128). Four goroutines are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over this channel and call `Post` for each payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select` send and simply drops (with a warning log) if the channel is full — there's no persistence, no external process, and nothing survives a restart.
