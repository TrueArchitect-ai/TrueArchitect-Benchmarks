**Premise correction:** There is no nightly database-cleanup job wired into a cron scheduler in this codebase. There *is* an `internal/scheduler` package that implements a cron-style scheduler (`internal/scheduler/scheduler.go`), but a repo-wide search shows it is only referenced by its own tests (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`) and its doc comment (`internal/scheduler/doc.go`) — it is never imported or used by `server/server.go` or any other production code, so no job (nightly or otherwise) is actually registered with it.

The only recurring background jobs actually wired into server startup are the two "runners" discussed earlier: the S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`, every 12 hours, wired at `server/server.go:161-171`) and the never-invoked `memopayload.Runner`. Neither performs general "database cleanup," and neither runs on a fixed clock time (e.g. "2am nightly") — the s3presign runner just runs once at startup and then every 12 hours from that point, not pinned to a specific time of day. There is no scheduled database-cleanup job to report a time for.
