**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background goroutine that re-presigns S3-backed attachment URLs before they expire. Its `CheckAndPresign` method (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132`) lists all attachments with `StorageType == S3` and, for each, refreshes the presigned URL if its `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs are issued with a 5-day expiration, per the comment at `runner.go:86-87`).

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-40`).

**Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:153-174`, in `Server.startBackgroundRunners`:
- `runner.go:162` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`
- `runner.go:163` — `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (immediate run on boot)
- `runner.go:167-171` — spawned as a goroutine calling `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` for the recurring 12-hour loop, added to `s.backgroundRunnerWG`/tracked via `s3Cancel` for graceful shutdown.

This is called from `Server.Start` as part of general server bootstrap (`startBackgroundRunners` is invoked during startup alongside the other background runners).
