**Premise correction:** There is no nightly (or any) database-cleanup job scheduled anywhere in this repository. The codebase does contain a generic cron-style scheduler package, `internal/scheduler` (with `New`, `Register`, `ParseCronExpression`, etc.), but a repo-wide search shows it's only referenced from its own test files (`internal/scheduler/example_test.go`, `internal/scheduler/integration_test.go`) — it is never imported or instantiated by `cmd/` or `server/` startup code, so no job is actually registered with it in production.

**Actual mechanism:** The only background jobs that do run in production are the two runners wired into `startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:153-174`: the S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`, ticking every 12 hours — see [[h01]]) and the (dead-code, never invoked) `memopayload.Runner`. Neither performs database cleanup, and neither is a "nightly" job — the s3presign runner's 12-hour interval is not clock-aligned to a specific time of day. Every "cleanup" hit in the codebase (e.g. `server/router/api/v1/user_service.go:421-450`, `server/router/api/v1/auth_service_sso.go:76`, `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:579`) is inline best-effort cleanup executed synchronously during a request (e.g. deleting orphaned attachments after a failed step), not a scheduled task.
