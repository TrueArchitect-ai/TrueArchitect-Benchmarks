**Component:** The `s3presign.Runner` background job (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), which re-presigns S3-stored attachment URLs. It lists all attachments with `StorageType = S3` and, for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs expire after 5 days), calls `PresignGetObject` to generate a fresh signed URL and updates the attachment's `Reference`/`LastPresignedTime` in the store.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately on startup, then on a `time.Ticker` every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), refreshing any URL that's within 4 days of its 5-day expiration.

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162-169` inside `startBackgroundRunners` — `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` followed by an immediate `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` and a goroutine running `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` for the recurring 12-hour loop.
