Excellent — a 6th distinct enforcement site. This gives us a solid list of at least 6 distinct enforcement points across gRPC handlers, RSS, sitemap, file server, and SSE.

h15.

- **`checkMemoReadAccess`** (gRPC `GetMemo`/related-memo lookups) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68` — denies non-public memos to anonymous/unauthenticated users and private memos to non-creators.
- **`ListMemos`** visibility filtering — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:216-226` — restricts `VisibilityList`/filter expression to `PUBLIC` (anonymous) or `PUBLIC`+`PROTECTED` (authenticated non-owner) so private memos never appear in listings.
- **`CreateMemoComment`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:41` — blocks commenting on a private memo unless the requester is its creator or a super user.
- **`ListMemoReactions`** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:30-38` — same private/creator/admin check before returning reactions on a memo.
- **`UpsertMemoReaction`** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:83` — same check before allowing a reaction to be added.
- **`checkAttachmentPermission`** (file server) — `server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650-704` — gates attachment downloads by the parent memo's visibility (public+anonymous-allowed, valid share token, or creator/admin for private).
- **`SSEClient.canReceive`** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go:134-144` (fed by `buildMemoSSEEvent`/`server/router/api/v1/sse_event_helpers.go:29-39`) — filters live SSE broadcast delivery so private-memo events only reach the creator or an admin.
- **RSS feed generation** — `server/router/rss/rss.go:90,141` — feeds are built with `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}`, excluding private/protected memos entirely.
- **Sitemap generation** — `server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150` — same `VisibilityList: []store.Visibility{store.Public}` restriction when listing memos for the public sitemap.
