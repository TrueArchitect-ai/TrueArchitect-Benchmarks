**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — its `CheckAndPresign` method scans S3-backed attachments and re-generates presigned GET URLs for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs expire at 5 days), storing the new URL and timestamp via `store.UpdateAttachment`.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately on startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27-44`).

**Wiring site:** `server/server.go:153-171` (`startBackgroundRunners`) — creates the runner with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously once, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tracked by `s.backgroundRunnerWG`/`s.backgroundRunnerCancels` for graceful shutdown.
