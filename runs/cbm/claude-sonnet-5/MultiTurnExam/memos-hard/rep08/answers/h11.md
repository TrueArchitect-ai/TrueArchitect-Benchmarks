**Data-layer entity types** are the persistent structs defined in `store/*.go` (e.g. `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Reaction`, `Inbox`, `MemoShare`, `MemoRelation`, `UserSetting`, `UserIdentity`, `IdentityProvider`, `InstanceSetting`), with `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` as the driver implementations of that same layer.

Counting distinct packages outside `store/` and `store/db/*` that reference each type (`store.<Type>`):

**1st: `store.User`** — referenced from ~7 outside packages: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.

**2nd: `store.Memo`** — referenced from ~6 outside packages: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

(Next closest was `store.Attachment` at ~4 packages: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.)
