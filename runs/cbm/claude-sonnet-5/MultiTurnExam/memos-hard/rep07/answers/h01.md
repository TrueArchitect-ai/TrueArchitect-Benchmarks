**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background job that periodically re-presigns S3 attachment URLs before they expire.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately on startup, then every 12 hours via a `time.Ticker` (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Within each run, it refreshes any S3 attachment whose presigned URL is within 4 days of expiring (5-day presign lifetime), see `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`.

**Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:161-171`, inside `startBackgroundRunners`: `s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` (line 162), immediately calls `s3presignRunner.RunOnce(ctx)` (line 163), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine (line 169) to keep it running on the 12-hour ticker for the life of the server.
