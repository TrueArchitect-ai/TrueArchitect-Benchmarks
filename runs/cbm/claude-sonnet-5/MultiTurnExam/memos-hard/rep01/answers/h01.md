**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background goroutine that scans S3-backed attachments and re-presigns their `GetObject` URLs before they expire.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a `time.Ticker` every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). Each run only actually re-presigns attachments whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (URLs expire at 5 days), so it refreshes each link with a 1-day safety margin (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-91`).

**Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:161-171` — it creates the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), calls `RunOnce(ctx)` synchronously once, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine tied to a cancellable sub-context of the server's startup context (`server/server.go:162-170`).
