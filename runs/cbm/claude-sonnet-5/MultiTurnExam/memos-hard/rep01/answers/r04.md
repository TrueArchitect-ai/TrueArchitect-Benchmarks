**Premise correction:** Neither. The webhook delivery workers don't consume from any external message broker — I already traced this in h02/h06: there's no NATS or RabbitMQ dependency in this codebase (confirmed by `go.mod` and the fact that `internal/webhook/webhook.go` has no such imports).

**Actual mechanism:** Delivery uses an in-process, in-memory Go channel as the "queue." In `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

This buffered channel (capacity 128) is declared as a package-level variable. Four consumer goroutines are spawned in `init()` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over `asyncPostQueue` and call `Post` for each payload. Producers enqueue via `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`), which does a non-blocking `select` send and drops (with a logged warning) if the channel is full — there's no persistence, no external broker, and no durability across process restarts.
