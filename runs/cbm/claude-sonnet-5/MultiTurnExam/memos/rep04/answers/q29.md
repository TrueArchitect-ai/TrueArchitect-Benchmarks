q29. **Interceptor:** web/src/connect.ts (`authInterceptor`, lines 156-178)

**Token kinds:** 
- **Access token** — short-lived JWT, held in memory/state (web/src/auth-state.ts) and attached as `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header by the interceptor
- **Refresh token** — a separate, longer-lived JWT sent as an HttpOnly cookie (`memos_refresh`); the interceptor transparently calls `AuthService.RefreshToken` (server/router/api/v1/auth_service_session.go) to mint a new access token when the current one is expired or a request returns `Unauthenticated`

**Backend validation package:** server/auth (`Authenticator` in server/auth/authenticator.go, which parses/validates the JWT and refresh-token claims)
