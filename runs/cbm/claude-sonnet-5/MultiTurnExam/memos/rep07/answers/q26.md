q26. memo, memo_relation, attachment

Source: `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) explicitly deletes `memo_relation` rows (both as source and target) and iterates+deletes linked `attachment` rows before deleting the `memo` row itself. The handler `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:518-582`) additionally recurses `Store.DeleteMemo` for comment memos (rows in the same `memo`/`memo_relation`/`attachment` tables), but does not touch `reaction` or `memo_share` tables — those rows are listed (e.g. for the webhook payload) but not deleted, leaving no explicit cascade to those tables.
