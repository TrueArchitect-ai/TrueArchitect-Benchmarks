q20. mechanism: Server-Sent Events (SSE), via an in-process `SSEHub` broadcast

- Hub implementation: server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go (`SSEHub` type, `Subscribe`/`Unsubscribe`/`Broadcast` methods; served via `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`)
- Handlers that broadcast through it:
  - `APIV1Service.CreateMemo` — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go
  - `APIV1Service.DeleteMemo` — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go
  - `APIV1Service.dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (used by `UpdateMemo`) — server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go
  - `APIV1Service.CreateMemoComment` — server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go
  - `APIV1Service.UpsertMemoReaction` — server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go
  - `APIV1Service.DeleteMemoReaction` — server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go
