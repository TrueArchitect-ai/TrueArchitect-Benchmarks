**Delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line 120). This function marshals the payload as JSON, constructs an HTTP POST request, optionally signs it with HMAC-SHA256, and sends it via the `safeClient`. **Protection mechanism** — SSRF protection via `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (line 59) combined with `isReservedIP` and `ValidateURL` in `internal/webhook/validate.go`.

Two layers defend against internal-network targeting:

1. **At dial time** (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59–77`): The `safeClient`'s transport uses `safeDialContext`, which resolves the target hostname via DNS and checks every resolved IP against `isReservedIP` before opening a connection. This also defeats DNS rebinding attacks because the check happens at connection time, not just at registration time.

2. **At URL registration time** (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62–83`): `ValidateURL` performs an upfront check — it parses the URL, enforces `http`/`https` schemes only, resolves the hostname, and rejects any address falling within reserved CIDR ranges.

The reserved ranges (`internal/webhook/validate.go:14–23`) cover IPv4 loopback (`127.0.0.0/8`), all three RFC-1918 private ranges, link-local/cloud IMDS (`169.254.0.0/16`), and their IPv6 equivalents (`::1/128`, `fc00::/7`, `fe80::/10`).

**Answer:** delivery function — `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go`; protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` + `isReservedIP`/`ValidateURL` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go` and `internal/webhook/validate.go`.
