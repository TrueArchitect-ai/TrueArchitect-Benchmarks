**S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — **every 12 hours** — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`.

The runner lives in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It periodically iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; it re-signs if fewer than 4 days remain), and updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the store. The schedule is defined by `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`).

At startup, `Server.Start()` calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` (`server/server.go:129`), which constructs the runner via `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, runs it once synchronously (`RunOnce`), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` in a goroutine (`server/server.go:162–169`).
