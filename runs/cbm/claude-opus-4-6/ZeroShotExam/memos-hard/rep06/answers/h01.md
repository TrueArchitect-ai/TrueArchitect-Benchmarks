**S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — every **12 hours** — wired into server startup at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`.

The runner lives at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. It periodically iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs are valid for 5 days; it re-signs if fewer than 4 days remain), and writes fresh presigned URLs back to the store. The schedule is defined by `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`).

At startup, `Server.Start` calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)` (`server/server.go:129`), which creates the runner, runs it once synchronously (`s3presignRunner.RunOnce`), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run` in a goroutine with a cancelable context (`server/server.go:162-170`).
