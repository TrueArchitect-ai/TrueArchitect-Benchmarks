Every distinct server-side enforcement site for memo visibility:

1. **GetMemo / checkMemoReadAccess** (single-memo read guard) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (lines 40–68, `checkMemoReadAccess` checks `Visibility` for non-Public memos and denies Private memos to non-creators)
2. **ListMemos** (list-level query filtering) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (lines 218–227, restricts `VisibilityList`/filters to Public-only for anonymous users, excludes Private memos of other creators for authenticated users)
3. **CreateMemoComment** (comment creation on a memo) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go` (line 41, blocks commenting on a Private memo unless you are the creator or a super-user)
4. **ListMemoComments** (listing comments, filter by visibility) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go` (lines 157–161, applies `visibility == "PUBLIC"` or `creator_id == …` filter to the comment query)
5. **ListMemoReactions** (reading reactions on a memo) — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (lines 29–41, checks memo visibility before returning reactions)
6. **CreateMemoReaction** (adding a reaction to a memo) — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (lines 82–85, denies reaction on a Private memo to non-creators)
7. **ListMemoAttachments** (listing a memo's attachments) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_attachment_service.go` (lines 213–225, checks memo visibility before listing attachments)
8. **checkAttachmentAccess** (downloading/viewing an attachment) — `server/router/api/v1/attachment_service.go` (lines 422–455, enforces memo visibility rules for linked attachments)
9. **ListMemoRelations** (related-memo filtering) — `server/router/api/v1/memo_relation_service.go` (lines 108–112, applies a visibility filter on related memos so only Public/Protected/own memos appear)
10. **ListAllUserStats** (stats queries) — `server/router/api/v1/user_service_stats.go` (lines 83–91, restricts memo visibility when counting stats for users)
11. **SSE delivery filtering (`canReceive`)** — `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` (lines 134–143, Private memo events are only delivered to the creator or admins)
12. **RSS feed** — `server/router/rss/rss.go` (lines 90 and 141, queries only `store.Public` memos, filtering out Protected and Private)
