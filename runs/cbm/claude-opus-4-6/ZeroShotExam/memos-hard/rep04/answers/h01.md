**S3 Presign Runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — **every 12 hours** — wired at `server/server.go:153` in `startBackgroundRunners`, which is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.

The runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) periodically iterates over all S3-stored attachments, checks whether each presigned URL is about to expire (presigned URLs last 5 days; it re-signs once the URL is older than 4 days), and writes the refreshed presigned URL back to the database. On startup it runs once immediately (`RunOnce` at line 163), then a goroutine ticks every 12 hours via `time.NewTicker` (const `runnerInterval` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`).
