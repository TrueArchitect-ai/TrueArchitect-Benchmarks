The data layer is the `store/` package (`store/*.go`), which defines persistent entity types such as `User`, `Memo`, `Attachment`, `Inbox`, `Reaction`, `MemoRelation`, `MemoShare`, `UserIdentity`, `UserSetting`, `IdentityProvider`, and `InstanceSetting`.

**1st: User; 2nd: Memo** (~7 packages each)

Both `User` and `Memo` (including their associated types like `FindUser`/`UpdateUser`/`DeleteUser` and `FindMemo`/`UpdateMemo`/`DeleteMemo`/`MemoRelation`/`MemoShare`) are referenced from 7 distinct packages outside `store/`. However, `User` edges ahead: it appears in 41 files with 169 references vs. Memo's 31 files and 155 references. The packages referencing `User` include `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, and `server/router/rss`. Memo's reach spans `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`, and `server/runner/memopayload`.
