The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
2. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`
3. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`

The factory function that selects between them at startup is:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (values `"sqlite"`, `"mysql"`, `"postgres"`) and delegates to the corresponding `NewDB` constructor in each package.
