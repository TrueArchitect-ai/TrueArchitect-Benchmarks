**Runners/workers the server actually starts:**

- **S3 Presign Runner** — 12-hour `time.Ticker` (runs once synchronously at startup, then every 12 hours via goroutine); started in `server/server.go:153` via `startBackgroundRunners()`, defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`
- **Webhook dispatch workers (×4)** — channel-driven (`asyncPostQueue`, buffered 128); launched in `init()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-43`, consume webhook POST requests enqueued by `PostAsync()`
- **Email send workers (×2)** — channel-driven (`asyncEmailQueue`, buffered 128); launched in `init()` at `internal/email/email.go:16-17`, consume emails enqueued by `SendAsync()`
- **Cache cleanup goroutines (×3)** — 5-minute `time.Ticker` per cache instance (instanceSettingCache, userCache, userSettingCache); launched by `cache.New()` at `store/cache/cache.go:83`, instantiated in `store/store.go:51-53`

**Unused scheduling infrastructure:**

- The **`internal/scheduler/` package** — a fully-featured cron-expression job scheduler with middleware support (recovery, logging, timeout) — is **never imported or started** by any production code. It exists only alongside its own tests.
- The **Memo Payload Runner** (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go`) has a `RunOnce()` batch method, but the server's `startBackgroundRunners()` only starts the S3 presign runner; the memo payload runner is never instantiated or scheduled. (Its per-memo `RebuildMemoPayload` function *is* called inline during memo create/update in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go`, but not as a background runner.)
