**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`

The `SSEHub` struct manages a set of `SSEClient` connections with thread-safe broadcast. The SSE endpoint is registered at `GET /api/v1/sse` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go`.

**Memo handlers that broadcast through the hub:**

- **`CreateMemo`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (broadcasts `memo.created`)
- **`CreateMemoComment`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go` (broadcasts `memo.comment.created`)
- **`UpdateMemo`** — via `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` in `server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go` (broadcasts `memo.updated`), also called from **`SetMemoAttachments`** (`memo_attachment_service.go`) and **`SetMemoRelations`** (`memo_relation_service.go`)
- **`DeleteMemo`** — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go` (broadcasts `memo.deleted`)
- **`UpsertMemoReaction`** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (broadcasts `reaction.upserted`)
- **`DeleteMemoReaction`** — `server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go` (broadcasts `reaction.deleted`)

**Answer:** SSE; `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; CreateMemo, CreateMemoComment, UpdateMemo (via dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects), DeleteMemo, UpsertMemoReaction, DeleteMemoReaction
