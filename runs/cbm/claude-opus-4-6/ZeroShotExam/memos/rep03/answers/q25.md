**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `google/cel-go`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go`. The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with `cel-go` into a CEL AST, then `buildCondition` (`internal/filter/parser.go`) walks that AST to produce a dialect-agnostic IR (condition tree defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`), and `renderer.Render` (`internal/filter/render.go`) converts that IR into SQL by switching on a `DialectName` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) to emit dialect-specific SQL fragments, placeholders, and JSON/date-part expressions for each condition node.
