**Server-Sent Events (SSE)**; hub path: `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`; handler names: `CreateMemo`, `DeleteMemo`, `CreateMemoComment`, `dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects` (called by `UpdateMemo`), `UpsertMemoReaction`, `DeleteMemoReaction`

The `SSEHub` struct in `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go` manages client connections and broadcasts events via its `Broadcast` method. The SSE HTTP endpoint is registered at `/api/v1/sse` in `server/router/api/v1/sse_handler.go` using `Content-Type: text/event-stream`. The following handlers call `s.SSEHub.Broadcast(...)`:

- **`CreateMemo`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:163`) — broadcasts `memo.created`
- **`DeleteMemo`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:585`) — broadcasts `memo.deleted`
- **`CreateMemoComment`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_service_comments.go:126`) — broadcasts `memo.comment.created`
- **`dispatchMemoUpdatedSideEffects`** (`server/router/api/v1/memo_update_helpers.go:71`) — broadcasts `memo.updated` (invoked from `UpdateMemo`)
- **`UpsertMemoReaction`** (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:106`) — broadcasts `reaction.upserted`
- **`DeleteMemoReaction`** (`server/router/api/v1/reaction_service.go:161`) — broadcasts `reaction.deleted`
