**CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go/cel`; the engine lives at `internal/filter/engine.go`.

The `Engine.Compile` method parses the filter string with `cel.Env.Compile` to get a CEL AST, then walks the protobuf AST (`exprv1.Expr`) in `internal/filter/parser.go` to build a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (defined in `internal/filter/ir.go`), which `Program.Render` passes to the `renderer` in `internal/filter/render.go` that switches on a `DialectName` (SQLite, Postgres, MySQL) to emit the appropriate SQL fragment with dialect-specific placeholders, JSON operators, and date-part extraction for each condition node.
