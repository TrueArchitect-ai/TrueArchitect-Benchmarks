The three implementations of the `store.Driver` interface (defined in `store/driver.go`) are:

1. `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go` (SQLite)
2. `store/db/postgres/postgres.go` (PostgreSQL)
3. `store/db/mysql/mysql.go` (MySQL)

The factory function that selects between them at startup is:

`NewDBDriver` — `store/db/db.go`

It switches on `profile.Driver` (set via the `--driver` CLI flag or `MEMOS_DRIVER` env var) and delegates to the corresponding `NewDB` constructor in each driver package.
