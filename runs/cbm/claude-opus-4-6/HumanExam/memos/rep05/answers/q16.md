Before deleting the memo row, the store layer deletes:
1. `memo_relation` records (where memo is source, `store/memo.go:142`)
2. `memo_relation` records (where memo is target, `store/memo.go:145`)
3. `attachment` records linked to the memo (`store/memo.go:149-157`)
