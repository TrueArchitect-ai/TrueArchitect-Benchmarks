CEL (Common Expression Language) engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; the engine compiles CEL filter expressions into a dialect-agnostic IR condition tree (`internal/filter/parser.go`), then renders it to dialect-specific SQL (SQLite/Postgres/MySQL) via `internal/filter/render.go`, with each store driver calling `filter.AppendConditions()` with its dialect name to inject the WHERE clause fragment.
