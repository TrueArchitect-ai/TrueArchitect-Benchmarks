The ListMemos RPC accepts a filter expression. What syntax/engine parses it, where does that engine live, and how does the filter become SQL for each dialect?

CEL (Common Expression Language) via cel-go, engine at internal/filter/engine.go; the CEL expression is compiled to an intermediate condition tree (internal/filter/parser.go), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL fragments by internal/filter/render.go, selecting SQLite/MySQL/Postgres dialect at render time.
