What background workers/runners does the server actually START, and what drives each one's scheduling?

s3presign.Runner — 12-hour ticker (time.NewTicker), plus RunOnce at startup (server/runner/s3presign/runner.go)
webhook async workers — 4 goroutines draining a buffered channel, started in init() (internal/webhook/webhook.go:43)

Unused infrastructure: `internal/scheduler` — a full cron-based scheduler framework that is never imported or used outside its own package. The `server/runner/memopayload` Runner exists but is never started as a background runner; only its `RebuildMemoPayload` function is called inline during memo create/update.
