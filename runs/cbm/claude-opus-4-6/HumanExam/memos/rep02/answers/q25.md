The filter uses **CEL (Common Expression Language)** via `github.com/google/cel-go`. The engine lives in `internal/filter/` (`internal/filter/engine.go`). The CEL expression is parsed and type-checked, converted to a dialect-agnostic IR tree (`internal/filter/parser.go` + `internal/filter/ir.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific SQL (`internal/filter/render.go`) for SQLite, Postgres, or MySQL, with the resulting WHERE clause fragments appended by `filter.AppendConditions()` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`) in each dialect's `ListMemos` implementation.
