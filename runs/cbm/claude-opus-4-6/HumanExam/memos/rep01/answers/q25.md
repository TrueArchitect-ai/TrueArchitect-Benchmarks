CEL (Common Expression Language) engine via `cel-go`, at internal/filter/engine.go; the CEL expression is compiled into a dialect-agnostic condition tree (`internal/filter/parser.go`), then rendered to dialect-specific parameterized SQL by `internal/filter/render.go` — each DB driver calls `filter.AppendConditions` with `DialectSQLite`/`DialectPostgres`/`DialectMySQL` to produce WHERE-clause fragments.
