CEL (Common Expression Language) via `github.com/google/cel-go` — `internal/filter/engine.go`

The `Engine` parses filter strings into a CEL AST, converts them into a dialect-agnostic IR (`Condition` tree in `internal/filter/ir.go`), and then `internal/filter/render.go` renders that IR into dialect-specific SQL `WHERE` clauses (with switch branches for SQLite, PostgreSQL, and MySQL). Each dialect driver calls `filter.AppendConditions()` (`internal/filter/helpers.go`) passing its dialect name, which selects the correct SQL rendering.
