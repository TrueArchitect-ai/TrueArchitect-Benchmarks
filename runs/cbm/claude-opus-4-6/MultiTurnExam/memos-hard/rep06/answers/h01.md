**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired in at `server/server.go:153`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) refreshes pre-signed S3 URLs for externally-stored attachments. It iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches, checks whether each attachment's `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), and re-presigns any that are close to expiring (`runner.go:27` sets `runnerInterval = 12 hours`).

It is wired into server startup in `Server.startBackgroundRunners` at `server/server.go:153`. That method is called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`. It runs `RunOnce` immediately on startup (line 163), then launches the ticker-based `Run` loop in a goroutine (line 169).
