**S3 Presign Runner** — every **12 hours** — wired at `server/server.go:153`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps S3 presigned URLs for externally-stored attachments from going stale. It iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; it re-signs if fewer than 4 days remain), and updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` with a fresh presigned URL.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule:** Every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`)
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:153` — the `startBackgroundRunners` method creates the runner, calls `RunOnce` immediately, then launches `Run` in a goroutine. `startBackgroundRunners` is itself called from `Server.Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
