**S3 Presign Runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — **every 12 hours** — wired at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) refreshes presigned URLs for S3-stored attachments. It iterates over all attachments with S3 storage type in batches of 100, re-presigns any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (presigned URLs expire after 5 days), and updates the attachment's `Reference` field with the fresh URL.

The runner interval is set to 12 hours (`runner.go:27`). At startup, it runs once immediately (`server/server.go:163` via `RunOnce`), then launches a background goroutine (`server/server.go:169`) that ticks every 12 hours via `Run()`. This is all wired in the `startBackgroundRunners` method of `Server`.
