The **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) keeps S3 presigned URLs from going stale by periodically re-signing them. It runs on a **12-hour schedule** (defined as `runnerInterval` at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). On each tick it iterates over all S3-stored attachments in batches, checks whether each presigned URL will expire within 4 days (URLs are valid for 5 days), and regenerates any that are close to expiring.

It is wired into server startup at **`server/server.go:162`**, inside `startBackgroundRunners()`. That function creates the runner, runs it once immediately (line 163), then launches `s3presignRunner.Run()` in a background goroutine (line 169). `startBackgroundRunners` itself is called from `Server.Start()` at `server/server.go:129`.
