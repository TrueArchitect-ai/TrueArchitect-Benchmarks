**S3 Presign Runner** — every 12 hours — wired at `server/server.go:162`

The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) refreshes pre-signed URLs for S3-stored attachments. It re-signs any attachment whose presigned URL will expire within the next day (presigned URLs last 5 days; re-signing triggers when 4 days have elapsed). The runner ticks every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, line 27 of `runner.go`).

It is wired into server startup in `server/server.go:162–171`: the `startBackgroundRunners` method creates the runner with `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`, calls `RunOnce` immediately for an initial pass, then launches `s3presignRunner.Run()` in a background goroutine under a cancellable context.
