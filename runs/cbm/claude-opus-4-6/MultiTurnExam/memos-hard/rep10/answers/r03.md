There is no nightly database-cleanup cron job. The `internal/scheduler` package exists but is used for user-facing scheduled memo publishing, not database maintenance. The only periodic background tasks are the S3 presign runner (every 12 hours, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) and the in-memory cache cleanup (every 5 minutes, `store/store.go:42`), neither of which is a database-cleanup job. Attachment and relation cleanup happens inline during delete operations, not on a schedule.
