**S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`) — keeps S3 pre-signed attachment URLs from going stale by re-signing them before they expire.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (defined in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)
- **Schedule:** Every **12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). It skips any attachment whose pre-signed URL is less than 4 days old (URLs expire after 5 days).
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162` — the runner is created and invoked once immediately (`RunOnce`), then launched in a background goroutine via `Run` at `server/server.go:169`.
