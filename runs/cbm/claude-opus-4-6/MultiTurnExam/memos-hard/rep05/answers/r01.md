There is no Redis. The cache is a process-local in-memory `sync.Map`-based store (`store/cache/cache.go`), configured in `store/store.go:40-41` with a shared `cache.Config`. The TTL for user entries (and all other cached entities) is **10 minutes** (`DefaultTTL: 10 * time.Minute`, `store/store.go:41`).
