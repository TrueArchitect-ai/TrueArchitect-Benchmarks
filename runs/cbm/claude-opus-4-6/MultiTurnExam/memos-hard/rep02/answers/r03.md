The question's premise is incorrect — there is no nightly database-cleanup cron job in this codebase. The `internal/scheduler` package exists but is not used for any database cleanup task. The only scheduled background runner is the S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`), which refreshes presigned URLs every 12 hours. No cron-based scheduler, database purge, vacuum, or nightly cleanup job of any kind is implemented.
