The migration directory `store/migration/` contains three subdirectories, one per dialect.

q02.
sqlite, postgres, mysql

The project ships migrations for three SQL dialects, evidenced by the subdirectories under `store/migration/`:
- `store/migration/sqlite/` (e.g. `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/postgres/` (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`)
- `store/migration/mysql/` (e.g. `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`)
