q02.

The project ships migrations for three SQL dialects, organized under `store/migration/`:

- **SQLite** — `store/migration/sqlite/`
- **PostgreSQL** — `store/migration/postgres/`
- **MySQL** — `store/migration/mysql/`

Each has its own `LATEST.sql` plus versioned migration subdirectories (e.g. `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`, `store/migration/sqlite/LATEST.sql`).

Answer: SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL
