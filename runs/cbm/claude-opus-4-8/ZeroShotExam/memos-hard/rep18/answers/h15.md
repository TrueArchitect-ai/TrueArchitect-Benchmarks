Memo view-visibility is enforced at several independent server surfaces (all keyed off `store.Memo.Visibility` = Public/Protected/Private plus creator/role checks):

Single-memo read gate (`checkMemoReadAccess`, used by GetMemo / ListMemoComments) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39-68 (called at memo_service.go:356, 367 and memo_service_comments.go:148)
List-query visibility scoping (`ListMemos` builds `VisibilityList`/creator filter) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:218-227
Attachment/file access gate (`checkAttachmentPermission`, resolves the linked memo's visibility) — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:649-704
RSS feed (restricts feed items to `store.Public`) — server/router/rss/rss.go:90, 141
Sitemap XML (lists only `store.Public` memos) — server/router/frontend/frontend.go:149-151

The first three are the substantive "who may view this memo" gates; the RSS and sitemap surfaces enforce the same rule by hard-limiting their queries to public memos. (Note: the comment-create check at memo_service.go / memo_service_comments.go:41 and the share-management checks in memo_share_service.go are write/ownership gates, not view gates.)
