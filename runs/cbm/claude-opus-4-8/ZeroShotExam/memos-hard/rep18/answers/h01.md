**s3presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — **every 12 hours** — wired into startup via `startBackgroundRunners` in `server/server.go:162-169`.

Details:

- **Component**: The `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` (`runner.go:47`) walks all S3-backed attachments in batches of 100 and re-generates presigned S3 GET URLs, updating each attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`. This is what keeps links to externally-stored (S3) attachment content from going stale: presigned URLs expire after 5 days, and it refreshes any that fall within 4 days of expiry (`runner.go:85-91`).

- **Schedule**: `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.NewTicker` in `Run` (`runner.go:29-41`). Additionally, one immediate `RunOnce` is fired at startup.

- **Wiring site**: `server/server.go:162` constructs the runner, `server/server.go:163` calls `RunOnce(ctx)` once at boot, and `server/server.go:169` launches the recurring `Run(s3Context)` in a background goroutine. `startBackgroundRunners` is itself invoked from server `Start` at `server/server.go:129`.
