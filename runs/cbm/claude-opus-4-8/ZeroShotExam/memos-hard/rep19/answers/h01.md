**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (plus one immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162`**

Details:

- **What it does:** The runner keeps S3-stored attachments' `Reference` links from going stale. Attachments stored in S3 use presigned GET URLs that expire (set to 5 days). `CheckAndPresign` pages through all S3-type attachments and, for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is within 4 days of expiry, generates a fresh presigned URL and updates the attachment's `Reference`. See `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47-132` (expiry check at lines 85-91, re-presign at 108-123).

- **Schedule:** A `time.Ticker` fires every `runnerInterval = 12h` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, loop in `Run` at lines 29-41). In addition, `RunOnce` is invoked once synchronously at startup before the periodic loop begins.

- **Wiring into startup:** `Server.startBackgroundRunners` constructs the runner and does the initial `RunOnce`, then launches the periodic `Run` in a goroutine under a cancelable context — `server/server.go:162` (construction), `:163` (initial run), `:169` (periodic loop). `startBackgroundRunners` itself is called during server start at `server/server.go:129`.
