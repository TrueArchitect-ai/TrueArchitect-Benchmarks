**`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours (with an immediate `RunOnce` at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162-171`**

Attachments backed by S3 store their `reference` as a presigned GET URL, which memos expires after 5 days. If left alone, those links go stale. The `s3presign` runner walks all S3-stored attachments in batches and re-presigns any whose URL is within 4 days of expiring, updating the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`.

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.Run` drives a `time.Ticker`; `CheckAndPresign` does the actual re-presigning (`runner.go:29-45`, logic at `:85-123`).
- **Schedule:** `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` — every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`). It only refreshes URLs older than ~1 day before their 5-day expiry (`runner.go:86-90`).
- **Wiring into startup:** `server/server.go:162` constructs the runner, `server.go:163` runs it once immediately, and `server.go:167-171` launches the recurring `Run` loop in a background goroutine (cancelable via the context set up just above at `server.go:158-159`).
