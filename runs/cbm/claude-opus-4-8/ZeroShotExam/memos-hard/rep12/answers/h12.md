There are three implementations of `CreateMemo` — `store/db/sqlite/memo.go`, `store/db/mysql/memo.go`, and `store/db/postgres/memo.go`. The one that actually executes is selected at startup by the configured driver:

2. **Driver selection.** `db.NewDBDriver` (`store/db/db.go:13`) switches on `profile.Driver`: `"sqlite"` → `sqlite.NewDB`, `"mysql"` → `mysql.NewDB`, `"postgres"` → `postgres.NewDB`.

3. **Default value of `profile.Driver`.** In `cmd/memos/main.go` the driver flag/config defaults to `"sqlite"` — `viper.SetDefault("driver", "sqlite")` (line 122) and `rootCmd.PersistentFlags().String("driver", "sqlite", ...)` (line 130). With no database flags, `profile.Driver == "sqlite"`, and `internal/profile/profile.go:110` even auto-fills the SQLite DSN (`memos_prod.db`) in that case.
