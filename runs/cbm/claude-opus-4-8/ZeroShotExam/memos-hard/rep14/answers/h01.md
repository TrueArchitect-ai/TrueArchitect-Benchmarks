## What it does
The `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) keeps the stored `Reference` (the pre-signed GET URL) for S3-backed attachments from expiring. Memos stores externally-hosted attachment content in S3, and the pre-signed URLs it hands out expire after 5 days (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:87`). The runner walks all `AttachmentStorageType_S3` attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry (`runner.go:85-91`, `runner.go:108`), then updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:114-123`). This prevents the externally-stored links from going stale.

## Schedule
- `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` → ticks every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, loop at `runner.go:29-41`).
