**S3 presign runner — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:169` (started at `server/server.go:162-171`)**

The component is the `s3presign.Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Memos stores attachments either as blobs or in external S3 storage; for S3-backed attachments the attachment's `Reference` is a presigned GET URL that expires (set to 5 days). If left alone, those links would go stale and stop resolving.

- **What it does:** `CheckAndPresign` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) pages through all S3-storage-type attachments in batches of 100. For any whose presigned URL isn't still valid for at least the next 4 days (expiry is 5 days), it re-presigns via `s3Client.PresignGetObject`, then writes the fresh URL back into the attachment's `Reference` plus a new `LastPresignedTime` (`runner.go:85-123`).
- **Schedule:** a `time.Ticker` fires every 12 hours (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `runner.go:27`; loop in `Run`, `runner.go:29-41`).
- **Wiring at startup:** in `server.go`, `s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)` is constructed at `server/server.go:162`, run once synchronously (`RunOnce`) at line 163, then started as a background goroutine calling `Run(s3Context)` at `server/server.go:169`.
