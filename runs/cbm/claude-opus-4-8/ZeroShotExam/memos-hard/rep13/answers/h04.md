Tests go through the profile → `NewDBDriver` path rather than constructing the driver directly.

One caveat to avoid confusion: `store/test/containers.go:16` imports `github.com/go-sql-driver/mysql`, but that is the third-party SQL *client library* used to configure a test-container DSN — not memos' own `store.Driver` MySQL implementation.
