**protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines 59–77), the custom `DialContext` on `safeClient`'s transport. At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` — `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines 44–54), backed by the `reservedCIDRs` list (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents). Because the check runs at the dialer against the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding.

Notes worth flagging:
- There's a second, earlier line of defense at configuration time: `ValidateURL` — `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines 62–83) — which validates the scheme and rejects private-resolving hostnames when a webhook URL is set. The dial-time check is the one that actually protects each delivery.
- Both checks can be globally disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41`, honored in `isReservedIP`), intended for self-hosted setups targeting local services.
