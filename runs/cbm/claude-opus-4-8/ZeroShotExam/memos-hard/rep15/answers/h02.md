delivery function — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines 119–180). It marshals the payload, builds the POST request, optionally attaches the HMAC signature, and actually executes the outbound call via `safeClient.Do(req)` (line 152). Content-change events reach it through `PostAsync` (line 184), which feeds a bounded worker queue whose goroutines call `Post`.

protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go` (lines 59–77), the `DialContext` of the shared `safeClient` transport (lines 32–37). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address in a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` in `internal/webhook/validate.go` (lines 44–54), backed by the `reservedCIDRs` list (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/IMDS `169.254.0.0/16`, and IPv6 equivalents) at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14–23`. Because the check happens at connection time on the resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding attacks that would slip past the earlier configuration-time `ValidateURL` check (`internal/webhook/validate.go:62–83`). Note the guard can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag (`internal/webhook/validate.go:41,45`).
