Memo visibility is enforced at several distinct server surfaces:

gRPC/REST single-memo read access (`checkMemoReadAccess`, called by `GetMemo`, parent-memo lookups, and comment listing) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:39 (checks at :55–:65)

gRPC/REST memo listing (`ListMemos` builds the visibility filter: anonymous → PUBLIC only; authenticated → own memos + PUBLIC/PROTECTED of others) — server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:178 (filter logic at :217–:227)

Attachment/file serving (`checkAttachmentPermission` gates a resource by its owning memo's visibility, with anonymous/share-token/private+admin rules) — server/router/fileserver/fileserver.go:650 (checks at :677 and :699)

RSS feed generation (restricts the feed's `FindMemo` to `store.Public`) — server/router/rss/rss.go:90 and rss.go:141

Frontend sitemap/public-memo listing (restricts `ListMemos` to `store.Public`) — server/router/frontend/frontend.go:150

The first three are the active permission-check sites; the RSS and frontend surfaces enforce visibility by hard-scoping their store queries to `Public`.
