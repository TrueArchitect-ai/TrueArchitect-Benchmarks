**delivery function** — `Post` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (the actual outbound HTTP request fires at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152` via `safeClient.Do(req)`; `PostAsync` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184` just enqueues onto a bounded channel whose worker goroutines call `Post`).

**protection mechanism** — `safeDialContext` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, wired into the shared `safeClient`'s `http.Transport.DialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). At dial time it resolves the target host and rejects any address matching a reserved/private range via `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`), whose CIDR list (loopback, RFC-1918, link-local/cloud-IMDS `169.254.169.254`, and IPv6 equivalents) lives at `internal/webhook/validate.go:14-23`. Because the check happens at connection time on the actually-resolved IP, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. (A best-effort pre-check, `ValidateURL` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:62`, runs at webhook-creation time, and the whole SSRF guard can be disabled by the `AllowPrivateIPs` flag at `internal/webhook/validate.go:41`.)
