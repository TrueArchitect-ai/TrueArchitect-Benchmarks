**Component** — the **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`, in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`). It walks all attachments whose storage type is S3 and re-presigns their `GetObject` URLs, updating each attachment's `Reference` (the externally-facing link) and `LastPresignedTime`. It only re-presigns a link once it's within ~1 day of expiry — presigned URLs are issued with a 5-day life and refreshed when less than 4 days remain (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-115`). This keeps the stored links to S3-hosted attachment content from expiring/going stale.

**Schedule** — every **12 hours** via a `time.Ticker` (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`; loop at `29-41`). It also runs once immediately at startup (`RunOnce`).

**Wiring site** — started in the server's background-runner startup: the runner is constructed and kicked off once synchronously, then launched as a long-lived goroutine at `server/server.go:162-169` (immediate `RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163`, continuous `Run` goroutine at `server/server.go:169`).
