**memo, memo_relation, attachment**

`(*Store).DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go`) explicitly deletes:
- `memo_relation` rows (both `MemoID` and `RelatedMemoID` directions)
- `attachment` rows linked to the memo (via `DeleteAttachment`; this also removes the underlying stored file, but that's object/local storage, not a DB table)
- the `memo` row itself (via `driver.DeleteMemo`)

No other tables are touched by explicit cascade code in that path.
