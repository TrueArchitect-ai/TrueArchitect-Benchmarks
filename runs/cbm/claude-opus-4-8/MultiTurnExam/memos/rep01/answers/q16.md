Before deleting the memo row itself, `(*Store).DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go`) deletes:

- **memo_relation records where the memo is the source** (`MemoID` = memo)
- **memo_relation records where the memo is the target** (`RelatedMemoID` = memo)
- **attachments linked to the memo** (each attachment found via `ListAttachments` with `MemoID`, deleted via `DeleteAttachment`)

Then finally it calls `driver.DeleteMemo` to delete the memo row.
