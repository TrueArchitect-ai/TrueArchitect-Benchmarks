In the store layer, `Store.DeleteMemo` (`store/memo.go:140-159`) deletes the following before deleting the memo row itself:

- **Memo relations** where the memo is the source (`MemoID`) — `store/memo.go:142`
- **Memo relations** where the memo is the target (`RelatedMemoID`) — `store/memo.go:145`
- **Attachments** linked to the memo — `store/memo.go:149-157`

(At the API-handler level in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:567-577`, comment memos are also deleted first, each recursively going through `Store.DeleteMemo` and thus cleaning up their own relations and attachments. Reactions are listed there for the webhook payload but are not deleted by the store's `DeleteMemo`.)
