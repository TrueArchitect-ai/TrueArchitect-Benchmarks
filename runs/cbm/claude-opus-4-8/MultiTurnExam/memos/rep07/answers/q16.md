Before deleting the memo row, `Store.DeleteMemo` deletes:

- **memo_relation** records where the memo is the source (`MemoID`)
- **memo_relation** records where the memo is the target (`RelatedMemoID`)
- **attachment** records linked to the memo (each attachment found via `ListAttachments(MemoID)` is deleted)

Then it deletes the memo itself. Source: `store/memo.go:140-159`.
