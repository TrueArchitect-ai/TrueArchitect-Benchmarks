Engine: Google CEL (google/cel-go) — `internal/filter` (engine at `internal/filter/engine.go`; parsing/IR in parser.go & ir.go; SQL rendering in render.go)

Mechanism: The filter string is a CEL expression that the filter engine compiles to an intermediate condition tree, and each dialect driver (`store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}/memo.go`) calls `filter.AppendConditions` with its `DialectName` (DialectSQLite/DialectPostgres/DialectMySQL), so the renderer (`internal/filter/render.go`) emits dialect-specific SQL WHERE fragments and bound placeholder args.
