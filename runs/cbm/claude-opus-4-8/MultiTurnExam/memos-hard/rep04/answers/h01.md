The **S3 presign runner** keeps external attachment links fresh. Attachments stored in S3 are referenced by presigned URLs that expire (set to 5 days), so this component periodically re-presigns them before they go stale.

**Component — schedule — wiring site:**

`s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — **every 12 hours** (`runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`, runner.go:27), skipping any attachment whose presigned URL is still valid for the next 4 days (runner.go:85–91) — wired in at **`server/server.go:162`** (`s3presignRunner := s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), with an immediate `RunOnce` at `server/server.go:163` and the recurring loop launched in a background goroutine at `server/server.go:169` (`s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)`).

The runner's job (`CheckAndPresign`, runner.go:47) batches through S3-type attachments, generates a fresh presigned GET URL via `PresignGetObject`, and updates each attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime` in the store (runner.go:108–123).
