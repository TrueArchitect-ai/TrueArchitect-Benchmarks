**1st: `store.User` (≈6 packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (≈4 packages)**

Persistent entity types are the structs declared in the `store` package (`store/user.go`, `store/memo.go`, `store/attachment.go`, etc.). Counting distinct packages *outside* the data layer (excluding everything under `store/`, i.e. the store package and its `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` drivers) that reference each via `store.<Type>`:

- **`store.User` — 6 packages:** `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`.
- **`store.Memo` — 4 packages:** `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

Next after those: `store.Attachment` (≈3 packages), then `store.MemoShare` / `store.Inbox` (≈2 each). So `User` is the most widely-referenced entity outside the data layer, with `Memo` second.
