**computation site:** `memopayload.RebuildMemoPayload` — `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:74`. It calls `markdownService.ExtractAll([]byte(memo.Content))` (the goldmark-backed `service.ExtractAll`, `internal/markdown/markdown.go`) and writes the results into `memo.Payload.Tags` and `memo.Payload.Property` (`server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:85–86`). So the derived metadata lives on the memo's `Payload` and is parsed from `Content`, not user-set.

**triggers (production):**
- `CreateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:108` (on every memo creation).
- `UpdateMemo` — `server/router/api/v1/memo_service.go:447`, inside the branch that handles a `content` field change (guarded by the update mask; it reassigns `memo.Content` then rebuilds).
- (There is also a batch `memopayload.Runner.RunOnce` at `server/runner/memopayload/runner.go:52` that recomputes payloads for all memos, but as noted the `Runner` type is never constructed in production — the two RPC paths above are the live triggers.)

**extracted properties:** `ExtractAll` walks the markdown AST in a single pass (`internal/markdown/markdown.go:ExtractAll`) and produces:
- `Tags` — the list of `#tag`s in the content.
- `Property` (`storepb.MemoPayload_Property`): `Title` (text of a leading level-1 `#` heading), `HasLink` (any link), `HasCode` (code span/block/fenced), `HasTaskList` (task-list checkbox present), and `HasIncompleteTasks` (an unchecked task checkbox).

(`ExtractAll` additionally collects `Mentions`, but `RebuildMemoPayload` only persists `Tags` and `Property` onto the memo payload.)
