**Component — schedule — wiring site**

`s3presign.Runner` (the S3 presign runner) — every 12 hours, plus one immediate run at boot — wired in `server/server.go:162`–`169`.

### Details

External attachment content stored in S3 is served via **presigned URLs**, which expire (set to a 5-day expiration). The `s3presign.Runner` refreshes these before they go stale so the stored `Reference` link stays valid.

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — `Runner.CheckAndPresign` iterates over all S3-type attachments in batches of 100 and re-presigns any whose presigned URL isn't valid for at least the next 4 days (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`–`132`, skip logic at `:85`–`91`), then updates the attachment's `Reference` and `LastPresignedTime`.

- **Schedule:** every 12 hours — `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` driving a ticker in `Runner.Run` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`, `:29`–`41`).

- **Wiring into startup:** `server/server.go:162` constructs the runner, `server/server.go:163` calls `RunOnce(ctx)` immediately at startup, and `server/server.go:166`–`169` launches `Run(s3Context)` in a background goroutine (with its own cancelable context registered at `:156`–`159` for graceful shutdown).
