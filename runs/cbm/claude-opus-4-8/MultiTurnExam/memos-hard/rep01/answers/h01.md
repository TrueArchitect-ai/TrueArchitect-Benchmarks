**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (with an immediate run at startup) — wired in `server/server.go:162-169`.**

- **Component:** The `Runner` in `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Its `CheckAndPresign` (line 47) lists attachments whose content lives in external S3 storage and regenerates presigned GET URLs before they expire — refreshing any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than ~8 days (it re-presigns unless still valid for the next 4 days; `runner.go:85-88`) and storing the fresh URL back on the attachment (`runner.go:115-124`). This keeps the stored links to externally-stored content from going stale.
- **Schedule:** Every 12 hours, driven by a `time.Ticker` in `Run` (`runner.go:26-41`, `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). It also does one synchronous `RunOnce` pass immediately at startup.
- **Wiring into server startup:** `server/server.go:162` constructs the runner (`s3presign.NewRunner(s.Store)`), `server/server.go:163` runs it once inline, and `server/server.go:166-169` launches the continuous `Run` loop in a background goroutine (cancelable via `s3Context`).
