h11.

Ranking persistent `store` entity types by the number of distinct non-data-layer packages that reference them (excluding the `store/...` tree — i.e. `store`, `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}`, `store/test` — and excluding test files):

- **1st: `store.User` — ~6 packages.** Referenced from `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss`.
- **2nd: `store.Memo` — ~4 packages.** Referenced from `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/frontend`, `server/router/rss` (plus memo-payload handling).

(`store.Attachment` is next at ~3 packages, then `store.Inbox` at ~2.) The entity structs live in the data layer — e.g. `store/user.go`, `store/memo.go` — and `User` is the most widely depended-on across the server's subsystems, with `Memo` second.
