**S3 presign runner (`s3presign.Runner`) — every 12 hours (re-signs any S3 attachment whose presigned URL is within 4 days of its 5-day expiry) — wired at `server/server.go:162-171`.**

- **Component:** `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`. Externally-stored attachments are kept in S3 (`AttachmentStorageType_S3`), and their `Reference` links are time-limited presigned URLs. `CheckAndPresign` (runner.go:47) walks all S3 attachments in batches of 100, and for any whose `LastPresignedTime` is older than 4 days (URLs expire at 5 days — runner.go:85-91), it regenerates a fresh presigned URL via `s3Client.PresignGetObject` and writes it back with `UpdateAttachment` (runner.go:108-123). This is what stops the external links from going stale/expiring.
- **Schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (runner.go:27), driven by a ticker in `Run` (runner.go:29-41). There's also an immediate `RunOnce` at startup.
- **Wiring site:** `server/server.go:162` creates the runner, `server/server.go:163` runs it once synchronously at startup, and `server/server.go:167-171` launches the recurring `Run` loop in a background goroutine (import at `server/server.go:25`).
