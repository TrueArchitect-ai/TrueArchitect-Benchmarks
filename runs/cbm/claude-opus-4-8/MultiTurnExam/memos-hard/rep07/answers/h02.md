**Delivery function — `Post` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120` (the actual outbound send is `safeClient.Do(req)` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`); protection mechanism — `safeDialContext` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:59`, backed by `isReservedIP` at `internal/webhook/validate.go:44`.**

Details:

- **Where delivery executes**: `Post` marshals the payload, builds the POST request (optionally adding Standard-Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signature headers), and fires it via the shared `safeClient` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:152`. `PostAsync` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`) just enqueues onto a bounded channel drained by 4 worker goroutines that call `Post`.

- **SSRF protection**: All webhook dispatches use `safeClient`, whose `Transport.DialContext` is `safeDialContext` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:32-37`). At dial time, `safeDialContext` resolves the target hostname with `net.DefaultResolver.LookupHost` and rejects the connection if *any* resolved IP satisfies `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:59-77`). Because the check happens on the post-resolution IPs at connect time, it also defeats DNS-rebinding. `isReservedIP` (`internal/webhook/validate.go:44`) is what classifies loopback/private/link-local/other reserved ranges as blocked.
