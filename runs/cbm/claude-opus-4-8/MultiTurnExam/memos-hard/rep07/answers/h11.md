**1st: `store.User` (~6 external packages); 2nd: `store.Memo` (~4 external packages).**

The persistent entity types live in the `store` data layer (`store/*.go`), and the driver subpackages `store/db/{sqlite,postgres,mysql}` are part of that layer, so I excluded everything under `store/` and counted distinct non-test packages referencing each `store.<Type>`.

- **`store.User`** — referenced from ~6 packages outside the data layer: `server/auth`, `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/api/v1/test`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`. (5 if you also drop the `.../v1/test` helper package.)
- **`store.Memo`** — referenced from ~4: `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`.

Next closest is `store.Attachment` at ~3 packages, so `User` and `Memo` are clearly the top two.
