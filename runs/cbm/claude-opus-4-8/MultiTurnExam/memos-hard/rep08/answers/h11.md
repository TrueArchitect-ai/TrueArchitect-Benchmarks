  - **`store.Memo`** — `server/notification`, `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/rss`, `server/runner/memopayload`. ≈4 outside-layer packages.
- Next-closest is `store.Attachment` (≈3 non-store packages: `server/router/api/v1`, `server/router/fileserver`, `server/router/rss`), so `User` and `Memo` are clearly the top two.

(If the three `store/db/*` driver packages are counted as "outside" the core store type-definitions, the raw totals become User ≈9 and Memo ≈7 — the ranking is unchanged.)
