**S3 presign runner — every 12 hours (with one immediate run at startup) — `server/server.go:162-171`**

Externally-stored attachments (`AttachmentStorageType_S3`) are referenced by S3 presigned URLs, which expire (set to a 5-day expiry). The `s3presign.Runner` walks all S3-backed attachments in batches and re-presigns any URL that isn't valid for at least the next 4 days, updating the attachment's `Reference` (the link) and `LastPresignedTime`. This keeps the stored links from going stale.

- **Component:** `s3presign.Runner` — `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` (loop `Run` at :29, work in `CheckAndPresign` at :47).
- **Schedule:** `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27`), driven by a `time.Ticker`. It refreshes any attachment whose presigned URL isn't good for the next 4 days (`runner.go:85-91`).
- **Wiring into startup:** `server/server.go`. `startBackgroundRunners` (called from startup at `server.go:129`) constructs the runner and does an immediate `RunOnce(ctx)` at `server/server.go:163`, then launches the recurring `Run` loop in a goroutine at `server/server.go:169`.
