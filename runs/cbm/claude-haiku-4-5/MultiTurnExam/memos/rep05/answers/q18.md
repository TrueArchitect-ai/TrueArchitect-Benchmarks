The three implementations of the database driver interface are:
- SQLite: `store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go`
- PostgreSQL: `store/db/postgres/postgres.go`
- MySQL: `store/db/mysql/mysql.go`

The factory function `NewDBDriver` in `store/db/db.go` (lines 14-32) selects between them at startup based on the profile.Driver configuration.
