When a memo is deleted through the API, the store layer deletes records in this order before the memo row itself:
1. **memo_relation** records (both where the memo is the source and where it's the target, as shown in lines 142-147 of store/memo.go)
2. **attachment** records linked to the memo (lines 148-157 of store/memo.go)

Then the memo row itself is deleted (line 158 of store/memo.go).
