web/src/connect.ts

The ConnectRPC service clients are created in `web/src/connect.ts`. This file imports the `createClient` function from `@connectrpc/connect` (line 2) and uses it to instantiate service clients for all eight services (lines 192-203): `instanceServiceClient`, `authServiceClient`, `userServiceClient`, `memoServiceClient`, `attachmentServiceClient`, `aiServiceClient`, `shortcutServiceClient`, and `identityProviderServiceClient`.
