Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks and their dispatch functions:

- memo created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- memo updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- memo deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- memo comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

Delivery convergence: All dispatch functions converge at `dispatchMemoRelatedWebhook()` which iterates over the memo creator's webhooks and calls `webhook.PostAsync()` (in `/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go` line 184) for asynchronous delivery.
