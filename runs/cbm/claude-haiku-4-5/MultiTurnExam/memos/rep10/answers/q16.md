Perfect! Now I have the complete answer. The Store.DeleteMemo method deletes (in order, before deleting the memo row itself):

1. memo_relation records where this memo is the source (MemoID)
2. memo_relation records where this memo is the target (RelatedMemoID)  
3. attachments linked to this memo (by iterating and calling DeleteAttachment for each)

Then finally it deletes the memo row itself.
