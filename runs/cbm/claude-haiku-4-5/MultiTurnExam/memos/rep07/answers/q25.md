The ListMemos RPC filter uses **CEL (Common Expression Language)** parsed by **google/cel-go** in the **internal/filter** package. The filter engine converts the CEL expression into a dialect-agnostic intermediate representation (IR), and the renderer in **internal/filter/render.go** produces dialect-specific SQL fragments by walking the IR and applying dialect-specific rules (JSON access syntax, placeholder formats, boolean semantics, etc.) for SQLite, MySQL, and PostgreSQL.
