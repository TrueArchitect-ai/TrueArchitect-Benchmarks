The memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks and their dispatch functions are:

memo.created: DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook
memo.updated: DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook
memo.deleted: DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook
memo.comment.created: DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook

All of them converge at the delivery function:

**webhook.PostAsync — internal/webhook/webhook.go (line 184)**

This function enqueues webhook requests for asynchronous delivery through a bounded channel with 4 goroutines processing the queue.
