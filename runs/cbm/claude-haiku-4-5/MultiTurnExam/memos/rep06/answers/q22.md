When a memo is created, user webhooks are notified through:
1. **Dispatcher function**: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook` in `/bench/repo/server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` (called from CreateMemo)
2. **Delivery package**: `internal/webhook` 
3. **Signing mechanism**: HMAC-SHA256 with Standard Webhooks format (messages signed with `whsec_<base64>` prefixed secrets, using headers: `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, and `webhook-signature`)
