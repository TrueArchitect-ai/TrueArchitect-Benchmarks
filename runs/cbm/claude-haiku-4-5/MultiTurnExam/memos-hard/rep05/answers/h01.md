The component is **S3 presign runner** (`server/runner/s3presign.Runner`), it runs on a schedule of **every 12 hours**, and it is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:169** (where `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` is called from within `startBackgroundRunners`).

The runner's `CheckAndPresign` method refreshes presigned URLs for S3-stored attachments, regenerating them when they're within 4 days of their 5-day expiration window, keeping the links from going stale.
