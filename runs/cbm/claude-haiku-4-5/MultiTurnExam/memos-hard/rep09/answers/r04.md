**Premise correction:** The question assumes webhook delivery uses either NATS or RabbitMQ message brokers. Neither is used.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery workers consume from a **simple in-process Go channel**, not a message broker.

**Details:**
- **Queue declaration:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`
  ```go
  asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
  ```

- **Worker goroutines:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55` — four concurrent goroutines spawn during `init()` and range over `asyncPostQueue`, consuming payloads
  
- **Enqueue mechanism:** `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196` — `PostAsync()` sends requests to the channel; drops if queue is full (bounded at 128 items)

- **Usage sites:** Called from `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go` when memo events occur

No NATS, RabbitMQ, or external message broker exists anywhere in the codebase for webhooks. Delivery is entirely in-process, bounded, and non-persistent.
