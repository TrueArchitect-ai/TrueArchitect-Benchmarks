h01.

The component that keeps links to externally-stored attachment content from going stale is the **S3 presign runner** (`s3presign.Runner`). It runs on a schedule of every **12 hours**, and it is wired into server startup at **server/server.go:162** in the `startBackgroundRunners` method, which is called from the server's `Start` method at **server/server.go:132**.

The runner automatically refreshes presigned S3 URLs for external attachment content. It checks all S3-stored attachments in batches and re-presigns URLs that are approaching expiration (more than 4 days into their 5-day lifespan) to ensure they remain valid.

**Component — Schedule — Wiring site:**
`s3presign.Runner` — every 12 hours — `server/server.go:162`
