**Premise correction**: Neither NATS nor RabbitMQ.

**Actual mechanism**: The webhook delivery workers consume from an in-memory Go channel. The queue is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39` as:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

Four worker goroutines are spawned in the `init()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that range over this channel and process webhook payloads. The `PostAsync()` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues payloads into this channel.
