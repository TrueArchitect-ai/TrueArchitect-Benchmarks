**s3presign.Runner** — **12 hours** — **server/server.go:162**

The `s3presign.Runner` component keeps S3-stored attachment links from going stale by periodically refreshing presigned URLs. It runs on a 12-hour schedule (defined at `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` as `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). The runner is wired into server startup at `server/server.go:162`, where it's instantiated and started as a background runner in the `startBackgroundRunners()` method. It runs an initial refresh synchronously (`RunOnce`) at startup, then continuously runs on the 12-hour schedule.
