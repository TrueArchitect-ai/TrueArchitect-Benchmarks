**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery workers do not consume from NATS or RabbitMQ — neither message broker is used for webhook delivery.

**Actual mechanism:** The webhook delivery workers consume from an **in-memory Go channel queue**. The queue is declared at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

In the `init()` function (lines 42-55), 4 concurrent worker goroutines are spawned that continuously read from this channel and process webhook dispatches. When `PostAsync()` is called, webhook payloads are enqueued to this channel (lines 189-190). If the queue is full (bounded at 128 messages), new requests are dropped with a warning log.
