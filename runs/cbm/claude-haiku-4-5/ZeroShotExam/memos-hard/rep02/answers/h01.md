The component that keeps links to externally-stored attachment content from going stale is the **S3 presign runner** (in the `s3presign` package).

**Component** — S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`)  
**Schedule** — Every 12 hours (defined as `runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12` at line 27)  
**Wiring site** — `server/server.go:129` (in the `Start()` method, which calls `s.startBackgroundRunners(ctx)`)

The runner works by checking S3 attachments and re-presigning their URLs when they're within 4 days of expiration (since the expiration is set to 5 days), keeping the links fresh.
