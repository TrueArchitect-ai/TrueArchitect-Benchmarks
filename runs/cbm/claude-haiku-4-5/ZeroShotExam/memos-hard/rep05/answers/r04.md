**Premise correction:** The webhook delivery system does not use NATS or RabbitMQ at all.

**Actual mechanism:** Webhook delivery uses an in-memory Go channel queue declared in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:39`. The queue (`asyncPostQueue`) is a buffered channel with capacity 128:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

Four worker goroutines are spawned at initialization (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) that consume payloads from this channel and dispatch webhooks via HTTP. The `PostAsync` function (`internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`) enqueues webhook payloads to this queue, and if the queue is full, payloads are dropped with a warning log.
