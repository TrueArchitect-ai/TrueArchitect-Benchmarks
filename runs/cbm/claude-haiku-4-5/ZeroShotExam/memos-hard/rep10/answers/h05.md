**Explicit Go code; emoji reactions are removed by explicit Go code in two ways:**

1. **Reactions on deleted user's memos are removed** — when a user is deleted, all their memos (and memos they own) are collected and their content_ids are used to delete reactions: `deleteReactionsByContentIDsTx()` at lines 417-425 in MySQL (and equivalent in PostgreSQL and SQLite) runs `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE content_id IN (...)` where content_ids are `"memos/{memo_uid}"`.

2. **Reactions created by the deleted user are removed** — `deleteReactionsByCreatorTx()` at lines 437-440 in MySQL (and equivalent in PostgreSQL and SQLite) runs `DELETE FROM reaction WHERE creator_id = ?` where `?` is the deleted user's ID.
