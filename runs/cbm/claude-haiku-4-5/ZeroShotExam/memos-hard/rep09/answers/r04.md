**Premise correction:** The question assumes the webhook delivery workers consume from either NATS or RabbitMQ, but neither message broker is used.

**Actual mechanism:** The webhook delivery workers consume from an **in-process Go channel queue**. The queue is declared as `asyncPostQueue` in `/bench/repo/internal/webhook/webhook.go` at line 39 with a capacity of 128:

```go
asyncPostQueue = make(chan *WebhookRequestPayload, 128)
```

Four worker goroutines are spawned in the `init()` function (lines 42-55) that consume from this queue and dispatch webhooks by calling the `Post()` function.
