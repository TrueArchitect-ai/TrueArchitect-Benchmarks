**S3 Presign Runner** — **every 12 hours** — **server/server.go:169**

The S3 presign runner (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) is the component that keeps S3 attachment links from going stale by refreshing their presigned URLs every 12 hours. It:
1. Runs continuously with a 12-hour interval (`time.Hour * 12`)
2. Checks S3 attachments and regenerates presigned URLs if they're within 4 days of expiration (since URLs expire after 5 days)
3. Gets wired into server startup at `server/server.go:169` where `s3presignRunner.Run(s3Context)` is started as a background goroutine
