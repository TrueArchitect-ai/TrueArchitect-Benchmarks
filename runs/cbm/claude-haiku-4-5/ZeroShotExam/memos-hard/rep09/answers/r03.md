The nightly database-cleanup job is scheduled to run at **2:00 AM** (midnight plus 2 hours). 

This is defined in `internal/scheduler/example_test.go:100` where the job's schedule is set to `"0 2 * * *"`, which in standard 5-field cron format means:
- 0 = minute (0th minute)
- 2 = hour (2 AM in 24-hour format)
- \* = every day of month
- \* = every month
- \* = every day of week
