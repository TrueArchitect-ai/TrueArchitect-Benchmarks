This function enqueues webhook payloads to an asynchronous queue (`asyncPostQueue`), where 4 worker goroutines (initialized in webhook.go:42-55) consume from the queue and call `webhook.Post()` (internal/webhook/webhook.go:120) to perform the actual HTTP delivery with HMAC-SHA256 signing.
