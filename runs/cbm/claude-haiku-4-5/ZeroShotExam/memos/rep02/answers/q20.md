**Mechanism:** Server-Sent Events (SSE)

**Hub path:** `server/router/api/v1/sse_hub.go`

**Memo handlers that broadcast through the hub:**
1. CreateMemo
2. UpdateMemo
3. DeleteMemo
4. CreateMemoComment
5. UpsertMemoReaction
6. DeleteMemoReaction

These handlers are methods on the `APIV1Service` type and call the `Broadcast()` method on the SSE hub to push live events to connected browser clients. The hub manages client subscriptions via the `/api/v1/sse` endpoint and filters event visibility based on user permissions (public/protected/private memo visibility).
