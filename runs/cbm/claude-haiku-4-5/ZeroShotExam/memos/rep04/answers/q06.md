The database table that stores links between one memo and another related memo is **`memo_relation`** (lines 45-51 in `/bench/repo/store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql`).

The table has the following structure:
- `memo_id` INT - the primary memo
- `related_memo_id` INT - the related/linked memo  
- `type` VARCHAR(256) - the type of relationship
- A unique constraint on all three columns together
