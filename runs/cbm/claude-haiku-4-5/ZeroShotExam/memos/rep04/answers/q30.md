Looking at the memo lifecycle webhook dispatch functions in `server/router/api/v1/memo_service_webhooks.go`:

**Memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks:**

- memos.memo.created: DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook
- memos.memo.updated: DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook
- memos.memo.deleted: DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook
- memos.memo.comment.created: DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook

**Delivery convergence:**

All webhook dispatches converge at `webhook.PostAsync()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184`. This function enqueues the payload into an `asyncPostQueue` channel. The queue is serviced by 4 goroutines (spawned in `init()` at lines 42-55 of `webhook.go`) that consume from the queue and call `Post()` (line 120 of `webhook.go`) for actual HTTP delivery.
