The memo lifecycle events that dispatch webhooks are:

**Lifecycle events and dispatch functions:**
- memo created: `DispatchMemoCreatedWebhook`
- memo updated: `DispatchMemoUpdatedWebhook`
- memo deleted: `DispatchMemoDeletedWebhook`
- memo comment created: `DispatchMemoCommentCreatedWebhook`

**Convergence point for delivery:**
All webhooks converge at `webhook.PostAsync()` in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:184-196`. This function enqueues webhook payloads into a bounded async channel (`asyncPostQueue`) where 4 goroutines (initialized in `internal/webhook/webhook.go:42-55`) consume and deliver the requests via `Post()` at `internal/webhook/webhook.go:120-180`.
