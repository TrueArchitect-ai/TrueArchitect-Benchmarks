The three implementations of the database driver interface are:
1. store/db/sqlite/sqlite.go
2. store/db/mysql/mysql.go
3. store/db/postgres/postgres.go

The factory function is **NewDBDriver** in **store/db/db.go**, which selects between the three implementations based on the profile.Driver value at startup (lines 18-24).
