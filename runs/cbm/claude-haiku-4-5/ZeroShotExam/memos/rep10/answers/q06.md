The database table that stores links between one memo and another related memo is **memo_relation**.

This table is defined in:
- `store/migration/sqlite/0.13/00__memo_relation.sql`
- `store/migration/mysql/LATEST.sql` (lines 45-51)
- `store/migration/postgres/LATEST.sql`

The table has three columns:
- `memo_id` - the source memo
- `related_memo_id` - the related memo it links to
- `type` - the type of relationship
- A UNIQUE constraint on (memo_id, related_memo_id, type)
